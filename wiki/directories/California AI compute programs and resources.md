## Regions

The California region column uses the 13 Jobs First regions, spelled as page 7 of [CJF Investment Playbook 2025 (web)](../sources/CJF%20Investment%20Playbook%202025%20%28web%29.md) prints them:

- North State
- Redwood
- Capital
- Sierra
- Bay Area
- North San Joaquin
- Central San Joaquin
- Central Coast
- Kern County
- Los Angeles County
- Orange County
- Inland SoCal
- Southern Border

The rule:

- A row gets a region name only when a cited source gives a county, city or campus that the Playbook places in that region. The row then cites the Playbook too.
- Page 7 of the Playbook lists the counties of each region. A city counts through its county.
- The region pages of the Playbook place these campuses and laboratories:
  - Bay Area (p. 47): UC San Francisco, UC Berkeley, Stanford, Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Sandia National Laboratories.
  - Central Coast (p. 53): UC Santa Cruz, Cal Poly San Luis Obispo, UC Santa Barbara.
  - Los Angeles County (p. 56): USC, UCLA, Caltech.
  - Orange County (p. 59): UC Irvine, CSU Fullerton.
  - Inland SoCal (p. 61): UC Riverside, CSU San Bernardino.
- A record can have more than one region, for example a program with sites in several regions.
- `Statewide` marks a record that its source describes as statewide, or as open to all of state government or a whole public system.
- `National` marks a national resource that Californians can use.
- A blank cell means that no cited source gives a place that the Playbook puts in a region. The Playbook does not place UC San Diego, UC Davis or UC Merced. A row for one of these campuses has a region only when a cited source gives the city.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: [CJF Investment Playbook 2025 (web)](../sources/CJF%20Investment%20Playbook%202025%20%28web%29.md) names a region "North San Joaquin" (p. 7). [California Jobs First regions](../sources/California%20Jobs%20First%20regions.md) names it "Northern San Joaquin". This page uses the Playbook spelling.

## Serves, Offers, Domains and Cost

- Serves gives the groups that a cited source names as users or as eligible: UC, CSU, Community colleges, Government, Nonprofits, Industry, Public.
- Offers gives what a user gets. It uses one fixed list of eight values:
  - GPU compute
  - CPU compute
  - Cloud credits
  - Data access
  - Network
  - Training
  - Funding
  - Advice and support
- Domains uses one fixed list of nine values:
  - Compute
  - Data and networks
  - Workforce and learning
  - Health and life sciences
  - Energy and physical sciences
  - Agriculture and environment
  - Government services
  - Governance and policy
  - Funding
- Cost is one of `Free`, `Fee`, `Credits` or `Not stated`. A short note can follow in parentheses. `Credits` means that the user receives credits to spend with a provider.

## Access, Get access and Link

- Access says who can use the record.
- Get access gives the route as a source states it: where to apply or sign in, what to submit, and the review time or deadline. It says so when a source reports a paused or closed route.
- Link is one public address of the record: its own program, access or apply page. A cited source page carries each address, in its `url`, in its text or in its raw or cached copy. A blank Link means that no cited source gives one.
