# Texas A&M System VISION supercomputer TOP500 (web)

## Citation

Texas A&M University System. "Texas A&M System Home to Nation’s Fastest University Supercomputer." News release, June 25, 2026. https://news.tamus.edu/stories/texas-am-system-home-to-nations-fastest-university-supercomputer/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The Texas A&M University System's VISION supercomputer is No. 66 on the June 2026 TOP500 list, at 34.82 petaflops. The System calls it the most powerful university supercomputer in the United States.
- VISION is an NVIDIA DGX H200 SuperPOD with 760 H200 GPUs, a $45 million investment. It serves 12 universities and eight state agencies.

## Background

The Texas A&M University System is a Texas public university system. This release reports the TOP500 ranking of VISION and quotes the chair of the Board of Regents, the Texas A&M University President and the Chancellor. The [Texas A&M System triples supercomputing capacity (web)](Texas%20A%26M%20System%20triples%20supercomputing%20capacity%20%28web%29.md) release of February 2025 announced the purchase.

## Key claims

- "The Texas A&M University System’s new VISION supercomputer is the most powerful university supercomputer in the United States, according to the June 2026 TOP500 list, which ranks VISION No. 66 among the world’s fastest computing systems." (lead)
- "VISION gives our 12 universities and eight state agencies access to computing power that most institutions could never build alone." (Chancellor Glenn Hegar)
- "VISION posted 34.82 petaflops of measured performance." (TOP500 paragraph)
- "The ranking validates the A&M System’s $45 million investment in advanced computing infrastructure, approved by the Board of Regents." (body)
- "The supercomputer features 95 NVIDIA DGX nodes, 760 NVIDIA H200 graphics processing units and fast storage built for data-intensive work." (body)
- "VISION is an NVIDIA DGX H200 SuperPOD acquired through World Wide Technology, an NVIDIA channel partner. The system is housed at the West Campus Data Center at Texas A&M University" (body)

## Evidence and method

An institutional news release. It gives the TOP500 rank, the benchmark result, the GPU count, the node count and the price. The ranking comes from the TOP500 list, which the release does not link.

## Limits

The release does not give operating costs, power use, allocation rules or user fees. It does not say how state agencies get access.
