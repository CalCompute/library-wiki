# CalHR MOU Unit 7 CSLEA 2023-2026 (web)

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the California Statewide Law Enforcement Association (CSLEA) for State Bargaining Unit 7. Its term is July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026.
- The State keeps all rights that the MOU does not abridge. It gives CSLEA 30 days notice of a change in an area that the MOU does not cover.
- The State reimburses training that a department requires. The MOU has no article on contracting out and does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 7 holds peace officers, fire fighters, lifeguards, investigators and other public safety classes in many departments, by the articles of the MOU. The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "Unless a specific provision provides for a different effective date, the terms of the Agreement shall be effective upon ratification by both the Legislature and the Union and remain in full force from July 1, 2023, through June 30, 2026." (Article 20, 20.2.1)
- The rights of the State include the right "to determine the methods, means and personnel by which State operations are to be conducted". (Article 4, 4.1.2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify CSLEA of the proposed change thirty (30) days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 20, 20.1)
- "Provides for counseling and training programs for employees whose positions are to be eliminated by automation, technological or management-initiated changes." (Government Code section 19995.2, in the MOU's list of superseded and incorporated sections)
- "The State agrees to reimburse Unit 7 employees for expenses incurred as a result of satisfactorily completing training or education courses required by a department to assure adequate performance." (Article 14, 14.1.1)
- "This section shall apply to Unit 7 employees whose main function is the daily operation of a computer on a continuous basis [at least five (5) hours per day]." (Article 13, 13.4.1)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, many pay differentials, training rules by department, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The term ended on June 30, 2026. The library holds no successor agreement for Unit 7.
- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence, on technological change or on contracting out.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
