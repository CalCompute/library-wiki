# Tech Talent Project homepage (web)

## Citation

Tech Talent Project. "We transform how government serves people" (homepage). techtalentproject.org, date not stated; its latest blog entry is of Nov. 19, 2026. https://techtalentproject.org/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Tech Talent Project is a nonprofit that places technology leaders in government roles. It says it has matched "200+" technical leaders with government roles since 2017 and supported "30+" states.
- It lists five programs: the Medicaid Delivery Innovation Initiative, State transitions, Federal transitions, Tech to Gov and the Tech Executive Leadership Initiative.

## Key claims

- "We help government work better for the American people by getting the right tech leaders into the right roles at the right time." (Our approach)
- "We don’t just fill positions. We look for opportunities where technical leadership can **fundamentally improve how government serves people** and ensure government is accountable to taxpayers." (Our approach)
- State transitions: "Practical playbooks and proven tech talent for state administrations entering their first 100 days". (Current programs & initiatives)
- Tech to Gov: "Connecting skilled technologists with the government agencies that need them most". (Current programs & initiatives)
- "Technical leaders matched with government roles since 2017": "200+". "States supported": "30+". (Our proven impact)
