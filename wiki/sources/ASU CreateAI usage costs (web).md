# ASU CreateAI usage costs (web)

## Citation

Faith Timoh, Arizona State University (ASU). "Is There a Cost to Using CreateAI?" Article on the ASU Artificial Intelligence site, September 24, 2026. https://ai.asu.edu/articles-and-documentation/there-cost-using-createai. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- ASU's Enterprise Technology pays for standard use of CreateAI, the university's generative AI platform. Faculty, staff and departments get no automatic bill.
- Each project has a monthly limit. Cost goes to the builder who owns the project, and a project can pause at its limit until the next month.

## Background

CreateAI is ASU's platform for building and using AI projects, with model choice in each project. The article answers faculty and staff questions about who pays for model use. A second article covers students. The page is part of ASU's articles and documentation for CreateAI.

## Key claims

- "AI usage has a real cost, but faculty, staff, and departments are not currently billed automatically for standard CreateAI usage." (A: For Faculty and Staff)
- "Enterprise Technology currently funds standard CreateAI infrastructure and AI model usage within established project allocations." (Who Pays for CreateAI Today?)
- "Usage and cost are attributed to the builder who owns the project. This includes activity generated by students, colleagues, and other app viewers." (Who Is Responsible for Project Usage?)
- "AI spend is calculated based on the tokens processed when a project receives a request and generates a response." (How Is AI Spend Calculated?)
- "When a project reaches its monthly limit, it may be temporarily paused until its allocation resets at the beginning of the next month." (What Happens When a Project Reaches Its Limit?)
- "Requests may be reviewed based on the project's purpose, expected audience, model selection, anticipated usage, and available funding." (What Happens When a Project Reaches Its Limit?)
- "ASU may evaluate additional funding, allocation, top-up, or departmental chargeback approaches to keep the service sustainable." (Could the Funding Model Change?)

## Evidence and method

A help article from the university's AI unit. It describes the cost rules in words. It gives no prices, no limit amounts and no total spend.

## Limits

The article does not give the size of a monthly project limit, the total budget of Enterprise Technology for CreateAI, or the model prices. The student rules are on a separate page that the library does not have.
