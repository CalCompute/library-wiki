# Google Cloud HIPAA Business Associate Addendum (web)

## Citation

Google LLC. "Google Cloud Platform HIPAA Business Associate Addendum." Google Cloud Terms Directory, last modified September 22, 2025. https://cloud.google.com/terms/hipaa-baa. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The addendum is Google's online contract for Google Cloud customers that hold protected health information (PHI). PHI is a term of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). The customer accepts the addendum with a click.
- It covers only the Google services on a stated list. Google uses safeguards, follows the HIPAA Security Rule, reports incidents and breaches, and returns or destroys PHI at the end.
- The customer must use the service controls in Google's HIPAA Implementation Guide. It must not ask Google for a use of PHI that HIPAA does not permit.

## Background

A business associate agreement is the contract that HIPAA requires between a covered entity and a service provider that handles PHI. Google publishes this one in its Cloud Terms Directory. It supplements the customer's existing Services Agreement. It does not apply to customers of a reseller or to customers with a signed offline version. See [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (web)](Google%20Cloud%20Platform%20Terms%20of%20Service%20%28web%29.md) and [Google Cloud Assured Workloads overview (web)](Google%20Cloud%20Assured%20Workloads%20overview%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "This BAA will be effective when Customer clicks to accept this BAA (the "BAA Effective Date")." (Preamble)
- "Customer must have an existing Services Agreement in place for this BAA to be valid and effective." (Preamble)
- "This BAA does not apply to any Google product, service, or feature that is not a Covered Service." (Section 2)
- "Google will implement all requirements of the HIPAA Security Rule with regard to electronic PHI." (Section 4(a))
- "Google will promptly notify Customer of (i) any Security Incident of which Google becomes aware, subject to Section 4(b)(iii); and (ii) any Breach that Google discovers" (Section 4(b)(i))
- "On termination of the Agreement, Google will return or destroy all PHI received from Customer, or created or received by Google on behalf of Customer" (Section 4(g))
- "Customer will use controls available within the Services, including those detailed in the HIPAA Implementation Guide, to ensure its use of PHI is limited to the Covered Services." (Section 5(b))

## Evidence and method

A standard online contract from a cloud provider, of about 2,200 words of terms. It states obligations and definitions with citations to 45 CFR parts 160 and 164. It gives no data and no audit result.

## Limits

The list of Covered Services is on another Google page, which the library has not read. The addendum sets no fixed time for breach notice beyond "promptly and without unreasonable delay". It does not cover state health privacy law, such as California's Confidentiality of Medical Information Act, or PHI held outside the Covered Services. The copy also holds the site's navigation text, which is not part of the terms.
