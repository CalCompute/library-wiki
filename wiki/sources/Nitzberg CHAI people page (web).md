## Citation

## Bottom line

- Mark Nitzberg is Executive Director of CHAI and Head of Strategic Outreach for Berkeley AI Research (BAIR).
- He wrote his Ph.D. at Harvard University in 1991. He was founding CEO of The Blindsight Corporation and later Director of Computer Vision Products at A9/Amazon.
