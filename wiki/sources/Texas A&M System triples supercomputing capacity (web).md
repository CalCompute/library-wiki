# Texas A&M System triples supercomputing capacity (web)

## Citation

Texas A&M University. "Texas A&M System Triples Supercomputing Capacity." Texas A&M Stories, February 10, 2025. https://stories.tamu.edu/news/2025/02/10/texas-am-system-triples-supercomputing-capacity/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The Texas A&M University System buys an NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with DGX H200 systems for $45 million, through World Wide Technology.
- The system has 760 NVIDIA Hopper GPUs. The Chancellor says it will "triple our computing capacity".

## Background

Texas A&M University publishes this story on its news site. It announces the purchase that became the VISION supercomputer. See [Texas A&M System VISION supercomputer TOP500 (web)](Texas%20A%26M%20System%20VISION%20supercomputer%20TOP500%20%28web%29.md) for its later ranking.

## Key claims

- "Under an agreement with World Wide Technologies Inc., an NVIDIA channel partner, the A&M System will acquire the NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with DGX H200 systems for $45 million." (second paragraph)
- "The A&M System also will make the necessary upgrades, equipment and installation costs at the West Campus Data Center on the Texas A&M University campus." (third paragraph)
- "Upon completion, the DGX SuperPOD will feature a total of 760 NVIDIA Hopper GPUs scaled with NVIDIA Quantum-2 InfiniBand networking." (third paragraph)
- "This investment will triple our computing capacity, which will support the A&M System’s growing research initiatives, particularly in areas such as machine learning, generative AI applications, graphics rendering and scientific simulations," (Chancellor John Sharp)

## Evidence and method

An institutional news story with the price, the vendor and the GPU count. It gives no cost for the data center upgrades.

## Limits

The story does not give the operating budget, the allocation rules or the cost of the data center work. It names the vendor as "World Wide Technologies Inc."; the 2026 release names it "World Wide Technology".
