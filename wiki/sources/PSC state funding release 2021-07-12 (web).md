# PSC state funding release 2021-07-12 (web)

## Citation

Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (PSC). Megan Carroll. "PSC Receives Another Year of State Funding." Press release, July 12, 2021. https://www.psc.edu/funding-from-commonwealth-of-pennsylvania/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The release says that the 2021-22 Pennsylvania budget gives PSC $500,000, in the Department of Community and Economic Development budget.
- The money supports PSC training events, educator workshops and outreach.
- PSC says that its state funding over six years helped it and its partners bring "over $200 million of Federal funding into the State."

## Background

PSC published this release on July 12, 2021. Governor Tom Wolf signed the budget in late June 2021. The release follows the January 2021 release on the 2020-21 award ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- Amount: "The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania has granted PSC $500,000 in its annual budget, signed by Governor Tom Wolf in late June." (paragraph 1)
- Agency: "This inclusion within the Department of Community and Economic Development budget demonstrates the Commonwealth’s continued support of the research computing community, and PSC in particular." (paragraph 2)
- Uses: "The funding PSC receives from the Commonwealth supports our workforce development efforts such as training events, educator workshops, and other outreach activities." (paragraph 3)
- Federal grants: "The funding PSC has received over the past six years has enabled PSC and our partners to bring over $200 million of Federal funding into the State." (paragraph 3)

## Evidence and method

This is a short press release from the center that received the money. It states one amount, the budget that holds it and its uses. The $200 million figure is PSC's own claim. The release gives no method or list of grants behind it.

## Limits

- The release does not cite the budget line.
- It does not support the $200 million figure with a list of grants.
- It does not say whether the state funding pays for any computing hardware.
