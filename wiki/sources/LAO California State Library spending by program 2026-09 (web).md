## Bottom line

- The Legislative Analyst's Office (LAO) table gives the California State Library $100.5 million in the 2026-27 enacted budget, up from $77.5 million in 2025-26.
- The Statewide Library Broadband Services Program stays at $4.1 million each year. The library's Information Technology Services are $3.8 million in 2026-27.

## Background

The LAO is the Legislature's nonpartisan fiscal and policy advisor. Its EdBudget figures give education budget tables by agency. This table splits the State Library's spending into local assistance and state operations, and gives the fund sources. Related figures show funding by source and General Fund changes.
