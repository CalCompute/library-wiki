# California Jobs First Economic Blueprint

## Citation

California Jobs First (GO-Biz). "California Jobs First State Economic Blueprint." Web page, no date. https://jobsfirst.ca.gov/blueprint/. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-29 California Jobs First Economic Blueprint.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-29%20California%20Jobs%20First%20Economic%20Blueprint.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-10-29.

## Bottom line

- The Blueprint is the state's 10-year economic roadmap, built from the 13 regional plans.
- It sorts strategic sectors into four groups: Strengthen, Accelerate, Bet and Anchor. High-Tech is in Accelerate and Bet. Education & Skilled Workforce is an Anchor sector.
- GO-Biz develops sector strategies for life sciences, semiconductors and microelectronics, aerospace and defense, agtech and farm equipment, quantum and the clean economy.

## Background

Taken because the [California Jobs First homepage](California%20Jobs%20First%20homepage.md) is thin.

## Key claims

- "Built on the foundation of the thirteen regional plans, the Blueprint is California’s economic roadmap to create, attract, and ensure access to good-paying jobs for all Californians." (page head)
- "High-Tech: California’s high-tech sector is a key driver of the state’s economy, known for its influential role in innovation and powered by top research universities and major venture capital investment." (Accelerate; Bet)
- "Education plays a crucial role in developing a skilled workforce that meets industry needs. As the economy evolves, the state’s education and workforce systems must adapt to meet the demands of the future of work." (Anchor, Education & Skilled Workforce)
- "GO-Biz is currently developing sector-specific strategies as part of the California Jobs First Initiative the following sectors: life sciences, semiconductors & microelectronics, aerospace & defense, agtech & farm equipment, quantum, and clean economy." (California Jobs First Sector Strategies)
- GO-Biz asks for input to the sector strategies. (California Jobs First Sector Strategies)
