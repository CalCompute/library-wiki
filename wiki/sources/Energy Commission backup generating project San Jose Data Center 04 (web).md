# Energy Commission backup generating project San Jose Data Center 04 (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "San José Data Center 04." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". ## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for San José Data Center 04 (SJDC 04), a Microsoft data center campus. Its maximum electrical demand is 97.8 megawatts (MW). Its status is "Under Review".
- The campus has two four-story data center buildings and Tier 4 diesel backup generators. PG&E is the local utility.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. Microsoft Corporation is the project owner. The docket number is 22-SPPE-02, the capacity is 97.8 MW and the location is San José, California. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "This proceeding is for the potential exemption of a data center project in the City of San José , California." (page text)
- "The project would include two four-story data center buildings, each approximately 315,639 gross square feet" (Project Description)
- "The total maximum electrical demand of the project would be 97.8 megawatts (MW)." (Project Description)
- "The Tier 4 diesel backup generating facilities with selective catalytic reduction and diesel particulate filters would consist of thirty-two 3 MW primary emergency generators, two 0.5 MW administrative emergency generators, and two 0.8 MW storage tank area emergency generators." (Project Description)
- "The sole purpose of the backup generating facilities is to provide electrical power to support the data center campus operations in the event of loss of electrical service from the local electric utility provider, Pacific Gas and Electric Company." (Project Description)
- "The Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants with facilities between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)
