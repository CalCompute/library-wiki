# UCOP Privacy Statement (web)

## Citation

The Regents of the University of California, University of California Office of the President (UCOP). "Privacy Statement." ucop.edu. Effective date January 9, 2025. https://www.ucop.edu/privacy-statement/index.html. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The statement covers the www.ucop.edu website and the sites that link to it. It does not cover UC campuses, medical centers or student records, which have their own statements.
- UCOP says that the Information Practices Act limits what it does with data from its sites. It will not share Electronically Collected Personal Information under Government Code section 11015.5 without the user's permission, except as the statement sets out.

## Background

UCOP is the systemwide headquarters of the University of California (UC). The Regents of the University of California is the controller of the data. Student data falls under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and a separate statement. See [University of California Office of the President](../entities/University%20of%20California%20Office%20of%20the%20President.md).

## Key claims

- "This Statement does not address UC campus or medical center activities." (introduction)
- "This Statement also does not address our processing of Personal Information ... that is part of a student applicant's or student's educational record ("Student Data"), which is done in accordance with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA") and other laws, and is covered by a separate privacy statement." (introduction)
- "Any information acquired by UC through the Sites is subject to the limitations set forth in the Information Practices Act and any other applicable laws." (Personal Information we share)
- "The Office of the President will not distribute or share Electronically Collected Personal Information (as defined in subdivision (d) of California Government Code Section 11015.5) about users to any third-party without the permission of the user, except in the circumstances set forth in this Statement." (Personal Information we share)
- "We keep your Personal Information as long as needed or legally permitted for the purposes for which it was obtained, unless otherwise provided in agreements between you and the Office of the President or as required by law." (Retention of Personal Information)
- "We may use your personal data for automated individual decision-making, in which case we will inform you of such processing activities and any potential impact of such processing activities." (Statement for EEA, UK and Switzerland Residents, Profiling and Automated Decision-making)

## Evidence and method

A website privacy statement of about 4,000 words, with an effective date. It lists the data that UCOP collects, its uses and the parties that receive it. It also gives the rights of residents of the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. It cites California law by section.

## Limits

The statement covers UCOP's websites only. It does not cover the research data of UC computing systems or the privacy rules of each campus. It gives no fixed retention period.
