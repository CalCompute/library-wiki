# NVIDIA A100 specifications (web)

## Citation

NVIDIA. "NVIDIA A100 Tensor Core GPU." Product web page with specifications, date not stated. https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/data-center/a100/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The A100 is NVIDIA's Ampere-generation data center GPU. The 80GB SXM version has 80GB of HBM2e memory, 2,039 GB/s of memory bandwidth and a 400W maximum power.
- One A100 can be split into up to seven Multi-Instance GPU (MIG) instances for different users.

## Background

NVIDIA makes GPUs for AI and high-performance computing (HPC). This is its product page for the A100, with performance claims and a specifications table for the 80GB PCIe and 80GB SXM versions. The page gives no price.

## Key claims

- "Powered by the NVIDIA Ampere Architecture, A100 is the engine of the NVIDIA data center platform." (Accelerating the Most Important Work of Our Time)
- Specifications, PCIe then SXM: "| GPU Memory | 80GB HBM2e | 80GB HBM2e |", "| GPU Memory Bandwidth | 1,935 GB/s | 2,039 GB/s |", "| Max Thermal Design Power (TDP) | 300W | 400W \*\*\* |" (Specifications).
- Specifications: "| FP64 | 9.7 TFLOPS |", "| FP16 Tensor Core | 312 TFLOPS | 624 TFLOPS\* |", where "\* With sparsity" (Specifications).
- "With MIG, an A100 GPU can be partitioned into as many as seven independent instances, giving multiple users access to GPU acceleration." (Enterprise-Ready Utilization)
- "Server Options ... NVIDIA DGX™ A100 with 8 GPUs" (Specifications, SXM column).

## Evidence and method

A vendor's product page. The performance claims are NVIDIA's own benchmarks, with test settings in footnotes. The table gives rated figures, not measured ones.

## Limits

- No price or cost of ownership.
- The A100 is an older generation. See [NVIDIA H100 specifications (web)](NVIDIA%20H100%20specifications%20%28web%29.md) and [NVIDIA H200 specifications (web)](NVIDIA%20H200%20specifications%20%28web%29.md).
