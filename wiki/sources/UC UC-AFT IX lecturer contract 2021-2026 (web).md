## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and the University Council-American Federation of Teachers (UC-AFT) for the Non-Senate Instructional (IX) unit. Article 40 gives its term as Dec. 3, 2021 to June 30, 2026.
- UC cannot lay off a qualified lecturer only because the lecturer needs training to teach a course online. An online course generally has the same workload value as the same in-person course.
- Management rights include the right to alter, extend or discontinue existing equipment and facilities. The agreement has no subcontracting article.

## Background

UC-AFT represents UC's lecturers, called Unit 18 faculty in the agreement. UC posts the agreement on its [UC IX contract page (web)](UC%20IX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md). A side letter on instruction during COVID-19, which applied no later than June 30, 2021, is in the file.

## Key claims

- "Except as otherwise limited by this Agreement, the UC-AFT agrees that the University has the right to establish, plan, direct, and control the University's missions, programs, objectives, activities, resources, and priorities" (Article 28, A)
- "to alter, extend, or discontinue existing equipment, facilities, and location of operations;" (Article 28, A)
- "The University shall not lay off or reduce the appointment of a Unit 18 faculty member who is qualified to teach a course solely because the Unit 18 faculty member requires training to teach the course in an online format." (Article 42, C)
- "An online course shall generally be valued at the same IWC as the same in-person course." (Article 42, D.1)
- "University copyright policies that apply to all faculty, including Senate Faculty, in the areas of intellectual property and distance education shall apply to Unit 18 faculty." (Article 9, D.1)
- "Third party vendors shall not have any ownership of course materials in a learning management system (LMS)." (COVID-19 side letter, C.3)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a faculty union, as UC posts it. Many of its pages are tables made from the PDF layout. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The cover gives Nov. 23, 2021 as the start; Article 40 gives Dec. 3, 2021.
- The term ended on June 30, 2026. The [UC IX contract page (web)](UC%20IX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) reports an extension through June 30, 2027. The library holds no text of the extension.
- The agreement has no article on artificial intelligence.
