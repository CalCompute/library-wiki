# Berkeley Data Science Discovery partner page (web)

## Citation

UC Berkeley, College of Computing, Data Science, and Society. "Research Mentors." Program page for the Data Science Discovery program, date not stated; shows Spring 2027 dates. https://cdss.berkeley.edu/discovery/partner. Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- Data Science Discovery at UC Berkeley pairs teams of three to five students with a mentor. Mentors come from academia, industry, nonprofits and government agencies. A project runs about 12 six-hour weeks a semester.
- The program has "helped energize and accelerate research for over 1,000 projects" since 2015. Students get cloud computing and technical support.

## Background

The page tells a prospective mentor how to host a project. It gives the Spring 2027 timeline, the requirements for a mentor and a project, and the benefits. The program is for credit: students take a Data Discovery course and present posters at a symposium.

## Key claims

- "Since 2015, Data Discovery has helped energize and accelerate research for over 1,000 projects. We connect project mentors with talented student teams using data to solve real-world problems. Projects can be hosted by mentors in academia, industry, nonprofits and government agencies." (top of page)
- "To host a Data Discovery project, you must be able to provide your student team with a dataset and a goal related to a larger project you're working on. We accept proposals hosted by any academic institution, private company, nonprofit or government agency." (Requirements, Eligibility)
- "Data discovery is not an internship, and students should not be used to produce work in the place of regular, paid workers." (Requirements)
- "Cloud computing resources and technical support are available to help students contribute to your project." (Benefits of participating as a mentor)
- "Each Data Discovery project must accommodate a minimum of 3 and a maximum of 5 students." (Requirements, Project team size)

## Evidence and method

The program's own page. The 1,000-project figure has no breakdown by host type.

## Limits

The page does not say who pays for the cloud computing or which provider gives it. It does not list current projects or government hosts.
