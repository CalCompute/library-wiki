## Citation

## Bottom line

- Leilani H. Gilpin is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at UC Santa Cruz. She leads the AI Explainability and Accountability (AIEA) Lab.
- She holds a PhD from MIT (2020). She was a research scientist at Sony AI, and won an Air Force Office of Scientific Research Young Investigator Program (AFOSR YIP) grant in 2023.

## Background

Her own homepage, on a UC Santa Cruz server. It has her bio, news, talks, teaching and projects.

## Key claims

- She is "an affiliate of the Science & Justice Research Center and the Generative AI Center at UC Santa Cruz." (bio)
- "Previously, I was a research scientist at Sony AI working on explainability in AI agents." (bio)
- "My research focuses on the theories and methodologies towards monitoring, designing, and augmenting complex machines that can **explain** themselves for diagnosis, accountability, and liability." (bio)
- Education: PhD, Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, MIT, 2020; M.S., Computational and Mathematical Engineering, Stanford, 2013; BSc, Computer Science and Mathematics, UC San Diego, 2011. (Education)
- "December 2023: I was awarded the Air Force Office of Scientific Research Young Investigator Program (AFOSR YIP) grant." (News)
- She was general chair of the 19th Conference on Neurosymbolic Learning and Reasoning (NeSy 2025). (News, November 2024)
- Projects include "Explaining LLM Failures", "Faithful Reasoning" for "high-stakes use cases", and "NeSy Law" for legal applications. (Projects)
- She taught CSE 246, Responsible Data Science (Fall 2022). (Teaching)
