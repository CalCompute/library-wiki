# Orange County Regional Consortium team (web)

## Citation

Orange County Regional Consortium (OCRC). "Meet the Team." Web page, date not stated. https://ocregionalconsortium.org/team. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The page lists seven staff of the OCRC, with titles and, for most, a biography. Michael Sacoto is the Interim Executive Director.
- The fiscal agent is the Rancho Santiago Community College District (RSCCD). Its Assistant Vice Chancellor states that she administers more than $20 million each year for the region.

## Background

The OCRC is a regional body of Orange County community colleges for career and technical education (CTE) and workforce development. This page is its staff list. The staff work on the Strong Workforce Program (SWP), the K-12 Strong Workforce Program, special programs, labor market research and fiscal administration. Several biographies say that the region was part of the Los Angeles and Orange County Regional Consortium until a restructuring in 2022.

## Key claims

- "Mr. Michael Sacoto serves as the K-14 Technical Assistant Provider (TAP) for the Orange County K-12 Strong Workforce Program (SWP)." (Michael Sacoto, Interim Executive Director)
- "In this role, he oversees the development and implementation of regional strategies to strengthen and expand pathways for students in Orange County." (Jorge Saucedo-Daniel, OCRC Director, Talent Development & Retention)
- "In 2017, Maria joined the Los Angeles and Orange County Regional Consortium, where she collaborated with 28 community colleges under the Doing What Matters initiative, later transitioned into the Strong Workforce Program, managing grants and building strategic partnerships to strengthen regional workforce efforts." (Maria Lepe, Interim Director, Special Programs)
- "Following a consortium restructuring in 2022, Maria shifted her focus exclusively to Orange County" (Maria Lepe)
- "Ms. Santoyo has developed processes and procedures to support grant management for 100-200 grant budgets annually, which prepared me to serve as the fiscal agent administrator since 2016 for over $20 million each year on behalf of the region, and for over $300 million on behalf of the state since 2018." (Sarah Santoyo, OCRC Fiscal Agent, Assistant Vice Chancellor of Educational Services, Rancho Santiago CCD)
- "Dr. Crete’s strengths include utilizing data and labor market information to drive the regional decision-making process." (Dr. Jesse Crete, Director, OC Center of Excellence)

## Evidence and method

A staff page with self-descriptive biographies. It lists these people and titles:

- Michael Sacoto, Interim Executive Director.
- Jorge Saucedo-Daniel, OCRC Director, Talent Development & Retention.
- Maria Lepe, Interim Director, Special Programs.
- Lorena Ruiz, OCRC Administrative Clerk.
- Sarah Santoyo, OCRC Fiscal Agent, Assistant Vice Chancellor of Educational Services, Rancho Santiago CCD.
- Miguel Gonzlez (spelled so on the page), Strong Workforce Program Fiscal Agent Department, Regional Funds, Rancho Santiago CCD.
- Dr. Jesse Crete, Director, OC Center of Excellence.

The biography of Jesse Crete names reports she wrote: the Orange County Sector Analysis Project and the Orange County Resilient Jobs report.

## Limits

The page has no date, and some biographies are out of date. The Sacoto biography refers to the "2021 – 2022 academic year", and the Saucedo-Daniel biography says he "is graduating in May 2023". The Crete biography calls her the "Orange County Regional Chair/Director", but her heading is "Director, OC Center of Excellence". The heading "Lorena Ruiz" sits above text about "Lorena Gonzalez". The page has no biography for Miguel Gonzlez, and it does not mention artificial intelligence or computing.
