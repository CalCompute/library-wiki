# CalHR MOU Unit 2 CASE 2025-2028 (web)

## Citation

California Department of Human Resources (CalHR). "Agreement Between The State of California and California Attorneys, Administrative Law Judges and Hearing Officers in State Employment (CASE) covering Bargaining Unit 2 Attorneys and Hearing Officers, Effective July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2028." ## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and CASE for State Bargaining Unit 2, the State's attorneys and hearing officers. Its term is July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2028.
- The State keeps "complete control and discretion over its organization and the technology of performing its work". It gives CASE 30 days notice of a change in an area that the MOU does not cover.
- Requests to telework "shall not be denied except for operational needs". The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. CASE represents the attorneys, administrative law judges and hearing officers in state employment. The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement. Article 4.4 lists the Government Code sections that the MOU supersedes or incorporates.

## Key claims

- "The State and CASE agree the term of this agreement will be from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2028." (Article 4, 4.5)
- "All State rights and functions, except those which are expressly abridged by this MOU, shall remain vested with the State." (Article 3, 3.1.1)
- The rights of the State include the right to "determine the methods, means and personnel by which State operations are to be conducted" and to "exercise complete control and discretion over its organization and the technology of performing its work." (Article 3, 3.1.2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify CASE of the proposed change thirty (30) days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 4, 4.3)
- "Provides for counseling and training programs for employees whose positions are to be eliminated by automation, technological or management-initiated changes." (Article 4, 4.4, Government Code section 19995.2)
- "Employee requests to telework shall not be denied except for operational needs." (Article 6, 6.4.1)
- "Job-related training is training designed to increase efficiency or effectiveness and improve performance above the acceptable level of competency established for a specific job assignment." Unit 2 employees "may be reimbursed for up to one hundred percent (100%)" of its cost. (Article 12, 12.7.3)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, leaves and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on contracting out or on artificial intelligence. Technological change appears only in the Government Code section 19995.2 entry of Article 4.4.
- The Telework Stipend of Article 5.13 "shall sunset on June 30, 2025", before the term starts. The page does not say if a later side letter changed it.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
