## Bottom line

- Amit K. Roy-Chowdhury is Professor and UC Presidential Chair at UC Riverside. He co-directs the UC Riverside AI Research and Education (RAISE) Institute and a DoD Center of Excellence, and chairs the Robotics program.
- His group works on computer vision, image processing and machine learning. He lists "AI for Science" among his research interests.
