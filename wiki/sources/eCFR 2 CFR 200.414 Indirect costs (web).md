## Bottom line

- Section 200.414 sets the rules for indirect costs on federal awards. All federal agencies must accept negotiated indirect cost rates, with few exceptions.
- A recipient without a negotiated rate can charge a de minimis rate "of up to 15 percent of modified total direct costs (MTDC)".

## Background

Section 200.414 is part of 2 CFR Part 200, the federal Uniform Guidance. The Office of the Federal Register publishes it in the eCFR. This is the current text. The version of Sept. 30, 2024 is [eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 as of 2024-09-30 (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20as%20of%202024-09-30%20%28web%29.md). The definitions are in [eCFR 2 CFR 200.1 Definitions (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.1%20Definitions%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "For major Institutions of Higher Education (IHE) and major nonprofit organizations, indirect costs must be classified within two broad categories: “Facilities” and “Administration.”" (§ 200.414(a))
- "Major nonprofit organizations are those which receive more than $10 million in direct Federal funding." (§ 200.414(a))
- "Negotiated indirect cost rates must be accepted by all Federal agencies." (§ 200.414(c)(1))
- "Pass-through entities are subject to the requirements in § 200.332(b)(4) and must accept all federally negotiated indirect costs rates for subrecipients." (§ 200.414(d))
- "Recipients and subrecipients that do not have a current Federal negotiated indirect cost rate (including provisional rate) may elect to charge a de minimis rate of up to 15 percent of modified total direct costs (MTDC)." (§ 200.414(f))
- "A recipient or subrecipient with a current Federal negotiated indirect cost rate may apply for a one-time extension of that agreement for up to four years." (§ 200.414(g))

## Limits

The copy has no source note, so it does not show when the current text took effect. The section points to Appendices III to VII and IX for rate proposals; the library has not read them. It applies to federal awards, not to state grants. The 2024 version set the de minimis rate at "10%". It also had a paragraph (h) on public rate disclosure, which the current text does not have ([eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 as of 2024-09-30 (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20as%20of%202024-09-30%20%28web%29.md)).
