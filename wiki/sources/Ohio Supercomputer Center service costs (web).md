# Ohio Supercomputer Center service costs (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Service Costs." OSC website, printable export with the FY27 budgets notice and the Service Terms, no date. https://www.osc.edu/book/export/html/4887. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Ohio Supercomputer Center service costs.md`.

## Bottom line

- OSC charges Ohio academic clients $0.003 a core hour and $0.090 a GPU hour. It says that these rates are "about 20%" of its actual costs.
- Each Ohio faculty member gets a $1,000 credit every fiscal year. OSC says that the credit has covered all charges for about 90% of faculty. Classroom use is free.
- Commercial, nonprofit, government and out-of-state academic clients pay set rates. They sign a service agreement and pay a $500 deposit.

## Background

OSC is a state-funded supercomputer center in Ohio. This page is its main statement of fees, with frequently asked questions (FAQs) for each client category. The export also holds a notice about budgets for fiscal year 2027 (FY27), and the terms of its billing system. FY27 is July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027. The page lists a billing contact at each Ohio university under contract; this library does not copy them.

## Key claims

- "Classroom usage is always free and academic researchers in Ohio qualify for credits that largely or completely offset fees." (Client Categories, Ohio academic clients)
- "Commercial/nonprofit clients must sign a service agreement, provide a $500 deposit and pay for resource usage." (Client Categories, Commercial, nonprofit and more)
- "| Standard compute | $0.003 / core hour |" and "| GPU compute | $0.090 / GPU hour |" (Ohio Academic Fees FAQ, Service types and costs for Ohio Academic clients)
- "Historically, the $1,000 credit has covered all charges for approximately 90% of faculty members, who then have no cost for usage of OSC’s services." (Ohio Academic Fees FAQ, Do Ohio-based academic clients receive a subsidy?)
- "In addition to the $1,000 per year credit, the rates listed above represent about 20% of OSC's actual costs to provide these services. The remaining 80% is subsidized by a variety of other sources including state funding and fees from commercial clients." (Ohio Academic Fees FAQ)
- "Clients that are NOT based at Ohio academic institutions account for about 10 percent of usage of OSC resources, and the income derived from their usage helps fund the Center’s operating budget." (General Services FAQ)
- "Since OSC’s establishment more than 30 years ago, state funding comes through a separate line item in the biannual state operating budget, directed through the Department of Higher Education (DHE)." (General Services FAQ, Where does OSC funding come from?)
- "In 2018, OSC implemented certain fees for six Ohio universities that comprised the greatest usage of OSC's resources." (Ohio Academic Fees FAQ, Why does OSC charge Ohio-based academics?)
- "No, project review and approval are not through an allocations committee." (Ohio Academic Fees FAQ)

## Evidence and method

The center's own statement of its fees and funding. OSC gives three shares with no method: costs (20%), faculty covered (90%) and usage by outside clients (10%). Its claim that its costs are "significantly lower" than cloud providers and peer centers cites "extensive cost comparisons" that the page does not give.

## Limits

- The page gives no rates for commercial, nonprofit or out-of-state clients. It asks them to make contact.
- It gives no dollar total of OSC's budget or of its state funding. The state line item is on another source page ([Ohio LSC 2025 catalog of budget line items, Higher Education (web)](Ohio%20LSC%202025%20catalog%20of%20budget%20line%20items%2C%20Higher%20Education%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives no model of the GPU in a "GPU hour".
