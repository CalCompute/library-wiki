# National Compute home page (web)

## Citation

National Compute Labs PBC. "National Compute" home page, date not stated. https://nationalcompute.com/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The home page says that the National Compute Grid is "Now in Public Sector Access". Users sign in with a `.edu`, `.mil` or `.gov` email address; other users join a waitlist.
- It links to the Grid paper and to a planner of compute capacity.

## Background

National Compute Labs PBC runs the National Compute Grid, a platform that pools AI compute. The home page is short. It links to the documentation, the sign-in console, the paper [National Compute Grid paper (web)](National%20Compute%20Grid%20paper%20%28web%29.md), a Trust Center, a privacy notice and terms of service. Its footer calls the company "an AMP frontier systems partner".

## Key claims

- "We are currently experiencing extreme surges in demand. Please be patient." (Banner)
- "Sign in to the Grid with a .edu, .mil or .gov email. If you don't qualify, sign up to join the waitlist." (Now in Public Sector Access)
- "Pooled infrastructure for the frontier research ecosystem." (The National Compute Grid)
- "The Grid is only available to public access partners during preview." (Explore compute capacity)
- "Access the Grid with a .gov or other public institution email." (Explore compute capacity)

## Evidence and method

A company home page. The text is short; the capacity planner and the paper load in the browser and are not in the Markdown copy.

## Limits

The page gives no prices, capacity figures or member names. The capacity planner's "Dated planning estimates" did not load in the copy. The page has no date.
