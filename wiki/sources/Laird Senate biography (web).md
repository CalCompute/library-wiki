## Bottom line

- John Laird is a member of the California State Senate. Voters re-elected him on November 5, 2024.
- In the Senate, he chairs or serves on committees that include the Budget and Fiscal Review Committee and the Joint Legislative Budget Committee.

## Background

The office of the senator publishes this page. It gives his Senate committees, his work as Secretary of the California Natural Resources Agency, his Assembly and local service, and his education.

## Key claims

- "John Laird was re-elected to the State Senate on November 5, 2024, carrying all four counties in the district which encompass Santa Cruz, San Benito, and Monterey Counties, as well as the majority of San Luis Obispo County." (Biography)
- "In the State Senate, Laird chairs and serves on a broad array of committees, including the Budget and Fiscal Review Committee, the Joint Legislative Budget Committee, and the Select Committee on Older LGBTQ+ Californians." (Biography)
- "Prior to his Senate service, Laird served as Secretary of the California Natural Resources Agency under Governor Jerry Brown from January 5, 2011, until January 7, 2019." (Biography)
- He "taught environmental policy at the University of California Santa Cruz" (Biography)
