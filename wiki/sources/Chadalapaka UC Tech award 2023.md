# Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023

## Citation

UC Tech News, University of California Office of the President (UCOP). "Sarvani Chadalapaka UC Merced: UC Tech Award Program DEI Leadership 2023 silver award winner." March 28, 2024. https://uctechnews.ucop.edu/sarvani-chadalapaka-uc-merced-uc-tech-award-program-dei-leadership-2023-silver-award-winner/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Sarvani Chadalapaka won the 2023 UC Tech Awards diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) leadership silver award. The post quotes her nomination by Nick Dugan, UC Merced CIO.
- The nomination says she grew the researchers on UC Merced's core HPC resources "from 40 to 600" in four years.

## Background

UC Tech News is the UC system's information technology (IT) news site. The post is an excerpt from a nomination letter.

## Key claims

- She is "director of Cyberinfrastructure and Research Technologies at UC Merced". (first paragraph)
- "UC Merced serves a highly diverse population – over 70% of our students are first generation, and more than 80% are from historically under-represented groups." (Project Description)
- "In just four years, she was able to increase the number of researchers using core HPC resources from 40 to 600, including a 900% increase during her first year." (Project Description)
- She "created a worldwide HPC mentorship program" with Women in HPC. It "operates across five continents, over 25 countries, and with over 60 volunteers." (Project Description)
- She was DEI Co-Chair of the Practice and Experience in Advanced Research Computing (PEARC) conference, and worked for "on-site childcare and dependent care grants". (Project Description)
- She received the Internet2 Inclusivity Initiative Scholarship Award in 2017, and later co-chaired its steering committee. (Project Description)
- She works to establish "a Data Science and Computing degree in UC Merced's School of Natural Sciences." (Project Description)

## Evidence and method

A nomination letter.

## Limits

- An award nomination praises its subject. Use it for facts and numbers, not for judgments.
- The "40 to 600" figure has no years.
