# GovOps homepage

## Citation

California Government Operations Agency. "Transforming government through innovation." Web page, govops.ca.gov, date not stated. https://www.govops.ca.gov/. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-26 GovOps homepage.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-26%20GovOps%20homepage.md) (original: `.html` beside it), fetched 2026-10-26. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- The Government Operations Agency (GovOps) "works to accelerate innovation, develop a skilled workforce and promote results-oriented practices in partnership with more than 150 departments statewide."
- It has "direct responsibility for 16 state departments and offices", including the departments of Technology, Human Resources and General Services. The California Education Learning Lab, Cal-ICOR's funder, is on its list.
- GovOps now leads the state's work on "independent oversight" of AI and "an AI kill switch".

## Background

GovOps is the agency that § 11546.8(f)(1) names to submit the consortium's Report. See [Government Operations Agency](../entities/Government%20Operations%20Agency.md).

## Key claims

- Secretary: Nick Maduros.
- "GovOps has been tasked with leading state government in accelerating independent oversight and advancing the creation of an AI kill switch." Earlier, "GovOps led efforts in adopting AI technology into state operations."
- "GovOps and its Department of Technology lead the effort to achieve Broadband for All."
- "We are embedding AI strategically and removing bureaucratic bottlenecks".
- The department list includes:
  - CDT, CalHR (the Department of Human Resources) and the State Personnel Board
  - the Office of Data and Innovation and Cradle to Career
  - the California Education Interagency Council and "CaLearningLab.org"
