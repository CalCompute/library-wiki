# Chico State HPC initiative (web)

## Citation

California State University, Chico (Chico State), Information Technology. "High-Performance Computing (HPC): Chico State and the National Research Platform (NRP)." Chico State website, no date. https://www.csuchico.edu/about/office/information-technology/about/strategic/hpc-initiative.shtml. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Chico State HPC initiative.md`.

## Bottom line

- Chico State has a node on Nautilus, the cluster of the National Research Platform (NRP), through CENIC AIR. UC San Diego loaned the first server in June 2024. It went on the NRP on April 1, 2025.
- Four servers that NVIDIA donated followed. The page projects servers with 240 CPUs and 32 GPUs on the NRP by September 30, 2025.
- Chico State offers rack space and its Science DMZ network for more CENIC AIR nodes in the California State University (CSU).

## Background

This is a page of the Information Technology office of Chico State, a CSU campus. It describes the campus part in the NRP and CENIC AIR, and gives a timeline from April 2024 to November 2025. CENIC is the Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California.

## Key claims

The initiative ("Overview of the initiative"):

- "Researchers at UC San Diego, along with over 50 partner institutions including Chico State, have connected a High-Performance Computing (HPC) node to the National Research Platform (NRP) via their Internet Service Provider, CENIC."
- "Supported by awards from the National Science Foundation (NSF), this initiative has deployed over 300 nodes of compute and storage."

CENIC AIR ("About CENIC AIR and the National Research Platform"):

- "The CENIC AIR component connects California's research and education institutions to the platform, enabling: Access to over 1,400 GPUs, 22,000 CPU cores, and 22PB of storage"

The campus role ("Chico State's Role"):

- "Chico State has installed a dedicated Nautilus node, providing the local campus community with direct access to AI-ready computing infrastructure."
- "We are also contributing to broader CSU system collaboration by offering rack space and network infrastructure (Science DMZ) for future expansion of the CENIC AIR network."

Other campuses ("Real-World Impact"):

- "Chico State joins a coalition of forward-thinking CSU campuses (including Fullerton, San Bernardino, Sacramento State, and others) in leveraging this platform."

Timeline ("Timeline"):

- "June 16, 2024 -Agreement finalized; server loaned by UCSD to Chico State."
- "April 1, 2025 - Server officially available on the NRP."
- "April 15, 2025 - Four NVIDIA-donated servers installed and staged."
- "September 30, 2025 - (Projected) New servers with 240 CPUs & 32 GPUs offered on the NRP."

## Evidence and method

This is a campus service page with a dated timeline. It gives NRP and CENIC AIR totals without a source or a date. It gives no costs.

## Limits

- The page does not say if the projected servers of September 30, 2025 went into service.
- It does not give the terms of the UC San Diego loan or of the NVIDIA donation.
- The timeline has a typing error: "May 15, 202 -". The overview writes "NPR's resources" for the NRP.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page says the NRP has "over 50 partner institutions". [NRP about (web)](NRP%20about%20%28web%29.md) says it pools hardware "across more than 70 institutions". Neither page gives the date of its count.
