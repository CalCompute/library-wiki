# NAIRR Pilot deep partnerships (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation, NAIRR Pilot. "NAIRR Pilot Deep Partnerships." NAIRR Pilot portal, no date. https://nairrpilot.org/opportunities/deep-partnerships. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Deep Partnerships track of the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot is for joint projects with one industry partner. The project uses that partner's contributed resources.
- Projects last no more than 12 months. All results must be open and publishable. Independent peer reviewers and the partner review each proposal.

## Background

The National Science Foundation (NSF) leads the NAIRR Pilot with other federal agencies. This portal page describes one of its allocation tracks, beside the Start-Up and research calls ([NAIRR Pilot Start-Up projects (web)](NAIRR%20Pilot%20Start-Up%20projects%20%28web%29.md), [NAIRR Pilot resource allocations (web)](NAIRR%20Pilot%20resource%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)). The page links to a coming partnership with LCCF Horizon. The list of partners on the page did not come through in the text copy.

## Key claims

- "Several Pilot industry partners are interested in engaging interactively and collaboratively on selected research projects that employ their NAIRR contributed resources." (introduction)
- "This call is open to meritorious proposals by US-based researchers and educators from US-based institutions including academic institutions, non-profits, federal agencies or federally funded R&D centers, state, local, or tribal agencies, startups and small businesses with federal grants." (Eligibility)
- "Because of the strong public component of the NAIRR, all project results must be open and publishable." (Expectations of Projects)
- "If a PI believes that a project will result in commercializable intellectual property (IP), the PI should discuss directly with the assigned resource provider to determine if commercialized IP requirements can be accommodated." (Expectations of Projects)
- "NAIRR Pilot Deep Partnerships are intended to be completed during a time period defined by the Deep Partner (not to exceed 12 months)." (Expectations of Projects)
- "Proposals require completing a form and uploading a three-page PDF proposal." (Proposal Instructions)
- "All proposals will be evaluated by independent peer reviewers and representatives from the requested deep partner for scientific and technical appropriateness and feasibility." (Proposal Evaluation)
- "The NAIRR Pilot may share this usage information, typically in aggregate form but potentially unfiltered, with funding agencies and researchers." (Expectations of Projects)

## Evidence and method

A call page on the program portal. It gives eligibility, project rules, the six review criteria and the submission route. It gives no count of projects and no resource amounts.

## Limits

- The page has no date.
- The text copy does not name the partners or their submission windows. The page says each partner sets its own deadline.
- It does not say how many Deep Partnership projects the pilot has funded.
