# Anthropic Commercial Terms of Service (web)

## Citation

Anthropic. "Commercial Terms of Service." Terms web page, effective June 17, 2025. https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- These terms govern business use of Anthropic API keys and other Anthropic services. They are "not for consumer use".
- The customer keeps its Inputs and owns its Outputs. Anthropic "may not train models on Customer Content from Services."
- Use must follow the Usage Policy, the Supported Regions Policy and the Service Specific Terms. Anthropic can suspend access for a breach of them.

## Background

Anthropic publishes these terms on its website, with the audience "Business". For customers in the European Economic Area (EEA), Switzerland or the UK, the contracting party is Anthropic Ireland, Limited. For all other customers, it is Anthropic, PBC. The terms have 13 sections, A to M. The Usage Policy that they incorporate is in the library as [Anthropic Usage Policy 2026-11-12](Anthropic%20Usage%20Policy%202026-11-12.md).

## Key claims

- Scope: "Services under these Terms are not for consumer use." (opening notice)
- Use for others: "Anthropic gives Customer permission to use the Services, including to power products and services Customer makes available to its own customers and end users (“Users”)." (Section A.1)
- Content: "Customer (a) retains all rights to its Inputs, and (b) owns its Outputs." (Section B)
- Training: "Anthropic may not train models on Customer Content from Services." (Section B)
- Policies: "Customer and its Users may only use the Services in compliance with these Terms, including (a) the Usage Policy (“Usage Policy”, which was previously referred to as the Acceptable Use Policy), (b) our policy on the countries and regions Anthropic currently supports (“Supported Regions Policy”) and (c) our Service Specific Terms" (Section D.2)
- Outputs: "It is Customer’s responsibility to evaluate whether Outputs are appropriate for Customer’s use case, including where human review is appropriate, before using or sharing Outputs." (Section D.3)
- Restrictions: Customer may not "access the Services to build a competing product or service, including to train competing AI models or resell the Services except as expressly approved by Anthropic" (Section D.4)
- Fees: "Customer is responsible for fees incurred by its account, at the rates specified on the Model Pricing Page, unless otherwise agreed by the parties." (Section H.1)
- Termination: "Each party may terminate these Terms at any time for convenience with Notice, except Anthropic must provide 30 days prior Notice." (Section I.2.a)
- Indemnity: Anthropic will defend Customer against a third-party claim that "Customer’s paid use of the Services ... or Outputs generated through such authorized use violates any third-party intellectual property right." (Section K.1)
- Governing law: for customers outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, "the laws of the State of California." (Section M.7.a)

## Evidence and method

This is the legal text of a commercial contract. The library read all 13 sections. It gives the rules on content ownership, model training, use restrictions, fees, suspension, termination, disputes and liability. It gives no prices; those are on the Model Pricing Page.

## Limits

- The terms give no prices.
- The Data Processing Addendum, the Supported Regions Policy and the Service Specific Terms are separate documents. The library did not read them for this page.
- Section J sends disputes to arbitration: for customers outside the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, "a sole arbitrator in San Francisco, CA". The terms do not have a separate section for public agencies.
- Liability is "limited to Fees paid by Customer for the Services in the previous 12 months" (Section L.3.a).
