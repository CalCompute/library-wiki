# Chandler Stanford Profiles (web)

## Citation

Stanford University. "Zach Chandler." Stanford Profiles. Date not stated. https://profiles.stanford.edu/zach-chandler. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The Stanford Profiles page of Zach Chandler. It gives his title as "Director of Open Source Strategy, Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI)".
- It says that his role "advances open science practices at Stanford and beyond" and that he represents Stanford at the HELIOS initiative.

## Background

Stanford Profiles is Stanford University's directory of faculty and staff pages. This page gives Zach Chandler's current role, his education, his interests and two publications. See [Stanford University](../entities/Stanford%20University.md).

## Key claims

- "Director of Open Source Strategy, Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI)" (page heading)
- "In collaboration with faculty and research teams, advances open science practices at Stanford and beyond." (Current Role at Stanford)
- "Represents Stanford at the HELIOS initiative and other fora." (Current Role at Stanford)
- "A.M., Duke University, East Asian Studies (2002)" (Education & Certifications)
- "Policy recommendations to ensure that research software is openly accessible and reusable." (All Publications: PLoS Biology, 2023)

## Evidence and method

A staff profile that the person or the university keeps. It lists two publications with their journals and DOI links: one in Clinical and Translational Science (2026) and one in PLoS Biology (2023). It gives no other evidence.

## Limits

The page has no date. [OpenSource at Stanford people (web)](OpenSource%20at%20Stanford%20people%20%28web%29.md) gives a different title. It calls him "Director of Open Scholarship Strategy within the Stanford Center for Open and Reproducible Science (CORES)" and "Executive Director" of OpenSource@Stanford. Neither page has a date, so the library cannot say which title is current. The page does not describe compute resources.
