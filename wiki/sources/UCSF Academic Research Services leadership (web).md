# UCSF Academic Research Services leadership (web)

## Citation

UC San Francisco (UCSF), Academic Research Services (ARS). "Leadership." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://ars.ucsf.edu/leadership. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Academic Research Services runs the research information technology program at UCSF. Mandy Terrill, Associate Chief Information Officer for Research, leads it.
- The page lists eight leaders. They include an HPC Core Manager and an operations manager for the Facility for Advanced Computing (FAC).

## Background

ARS is a unit of UCSF. This page names its leadership team, with a title for each person and a link to that person's team.

## Key claims

- "Mandy Terrill, Associate Chief Information Officer for Research, plays a key role in providing management to the research information technology program for the UCSF research mission in all aspects of their research." (Leadership)
- "The ARS leadership team works closely with their peers and associated teams to meet the current and evolving needs of the UCSF research community." (Leadership)
- Marissa Jules: "HPC Core Manager", with a link to "High-Performance Computing (HPC)". (Leadership)
- Bharat Bhargava: "Research Services Ops Manager", with a link to "Facility for Advanced Computing (FAC)". (Leadership)

## Evidence and method

A staff list. The eight people and titles are:

- Mandy Terrill, Associate CIO for Research.
- Rick Larsen, Director of Research Informatics.
- David Aaronson, Senior Biospecimen Information Systems Manager.
- Ganesh Bhalerao, Sr. Manager of Research Administration and Compliance Systems.
- Sian Shumway, ARS Service Delivery Director/ITOM Program Director.
- Alyssa Tecklenburg, Program Manager of Research Administration and Compliance Systems.
- Marissa Jules, HPC Core Manager.
- Bharat Bhargava, Research Services Ops Manager.

## Limits

The page has no date. It does not give the size, the cost or the access rules of the HPC core or the FAC.
