# Romo AP0110.org homepage

## Citation

AP0110. "An independent internet, built for everyone." Website homepage, publication date unknown (copyright 2026). https://AP0110.org/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- AP0110.org describes a research group that "researches and builds technology people can own and verify". It lists CalCompute as a partnered project.
- It also lists partnered projects with UNA-USA on human rights and with Life Impact International against child trafficking.
- The page does not name Daniel Romo. It is self-published.

## Background

The research site that goes with the AP0110 company site ([Romo AP0110.com homepage](Romo%20AP0110.com%20homepage.md)).

## Key claims

- "AP0110 researches and builds technology people can own and verify — and puts it to work for the communities that have been surveilled, sidelined, or left offline." (top)
- On CalCompute: "Working with California and the University of California toward public AI that's **safe, fair, and built for everyone** — not just those who can afford it." (Partnered Projects)
- "Advising **UNA-USA** on how technology can uphold human rights — and protect them — in the digital age." (Partnered Projects)
- "Partnering with **Life Impact International** to put protective technology in the hands of anti-trafficking groups around the world." (Partnered Projects)

## Evidence and method

A self-published homepage.

## Limits

- Self-published. The CalCompute line is the site's own description. It is not a statement by the consortium.
