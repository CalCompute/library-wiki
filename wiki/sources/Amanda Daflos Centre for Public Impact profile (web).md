## Bottom line

- Amanda Daflos is a member of the CPI Global Board. She is the Deputy Mayor of the Office of Strategic Partnerships (OSP) in the City of Los Angeles.
- Before this post, she worked for more than ten years at Deloitte Consulting, on government transformation, strategy and technology.
