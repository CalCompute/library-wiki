# NSF cooperative agreement terms CA-FATC 2024-10 (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF). "Cooperative Agreement Financial & Administrative Terms and Conditions (CA-FATC)." Effective for new awards and funding amendments made on or after Oct. 1, 2024. https://nsf-gov-resources.nsf.gov/files/cafatc-1024-r.pdf. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The CA-FATC are the standard financial and administrative terms for every NSF cooperative agreement made on or after Oct. 1, 2024. They put into effect the revised Title 2 of the Code of Federal Regulations (2 CFR) of April 22, 2024.
- Title to equipment vests in the recipient on condition. Program income normally stays with the project. Annual and final project reports are due, and continued funding depends on accepted reports and progress.

## Background

The Advanced Computing Systems and Services solicitations name the CA-FATC as an award condition ([NSF 23-518 Advanced Computing Systems and Services solicitation (web)](NSF%2023-518%20Advanced%20Computing%20Systems%20and%20Services%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md)). The document has 62 numbered terms and conditions. It opens with a summary of the changes in this revision. A program can add its own programmatic terms to these.

## Key claims

- "The recipient has overall responsibility for NSF-funded cooperative agreements, including providing award oversight for technical/programmatic, as well as financial and administrative performance." (Term 2.a, p. 8)
- "The cognizant NSF Program Officer may participate in discussions of programmatic, technical, managerial and/or scheduling concerns; provide guidance and/or advice" and "may schedule desk and on-site reviews as necessary". (Term 2.a, p. 8)
- Prior approval is necessary for "Changes in Objectives or Scope", "Change of PI or co-PI" and "Long-Term Disengagement of the PI or co-PI". (Term 3.d, pp. 8 to 9)
- "Unless specifically identified in the award as exempt property, title to equipment purchased or fabricated under the award will conditionally vest upon acquisition in the recipient subject to the conditions identified in 2 CFR § 200.313(a)." (Term 8.a, p. 11)
- "NSF reserves the right to transfer the title to the Federal Government or a third party named by the Federal Government at any time during the award period or in disposition instructions issued no later than 120 days after the end date of the NSF-supported project for which it was acquired." (Term 8.a, p. 11)
- "Continuing funding is contingent on: (1) availability of funds; (2) satisfactory scientific/technical progress; and (3) fulfillment of any special conditions of the award." (Term 13, Cooperative Agreement Increments, p. 16)
- "Program income includes but is not limited to income from fees for services performed, the use or rental of real or personal property acquired under the award, the sale of commodities or items fabricated under the award, license fees, and royalties on patents and copyrights" (Term 27.a, p. 28)
- "Unless otherwise specified in the award, program income received or accruing to the recipient during the period of the award is to be retained by the recipient, added to the funds committed to the project by NSF, and thus used to further project objectives." (Term 27.b.1, p. 28)
- "The recipient also is required to notify NSF when a significant development that could impact the award occurs between project reporting due dates." (Term 14.a, p. 17)
- "NSF, through authorized representatives, has the right, at all reasonable times, to make site visits to review project accomplishments and management control systems" (Term 32, Site Visits, p. 39)

## Evidence and method

Standard federal award terms. The library read the summary of changes and the table of contents. It read these terms in full:

- 1 to 8: responsibilities, prior approval, costs and equipment;
- 13 and 14: increments and project reports;
- 27: program income;
- 30: cost sharing;
- 32: site visits.

It read the definitions in Term 29.a (intangible property) and the start of Term 33 (termination). The library did not read the other terms.

## Limits

- The CA-FATC apply to every NSF cooperative agreement. They do not give the special terms of any one award, such as key personnel or a Project Execution Plan. A solicitation or award notice sets those.
- The document refers to the NSF Prior Approval Matrix and to the Proposal and Award Policies and Procedures Guide (PAPPG) for details. The library has not read the matrix. Chapter II of the PAPPG is at [NSF PAPPG 24-1 Chapter II (web)](NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Chapter%20II%20%28web%29.md).
- Term 27 says that program income "means gross income earned by the recipient that is directly generated by a supported activity". For the federal definition, see [eCFR 2 CFR 200.1 Definitions (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.1%20Definitions%20%28web%29.md).
- Under Term 30 (p. 37), the recipient must cost share "in accordance with any amount specified on Line M of the award budget".
