# Apereo join us (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "For Education, By Education..." Membership web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/join-us. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page gives the Apereo Foundation's six membership types and their costs.
- Community membership has no cost. Foundation and Institutional membership use a sliding scale on the member's annual budget. Commercial membership is $2,500 (Standard) or $10,000 (Platinum). Friends of Apereo pay $100 a year.
- Apereo says that it has "300+ institutions", "Twenty projects and communities" and "2000+ contributors".

## Background

This is the membership overview page on apereo.org. It describes the foundation and each membership type with its cost. It opens with a quotation from a 2024 report by a software security company on open source in education technology. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Scope: "The Apereo Foundation is a global network of academic institutions, educational consortia, government agencies, and corporate affiliates fostering, developing, and sustaining open technologies and innovation to support learning, teaching, and research."
- Size: "**300+ institutions:** research universities, residential and community colleges, technical schools, and educational consortia."
- Model: "There is no one-size-fits-all membership type as we serve institutions large and small, wealthy and underfunded, across six continents." (Apereo Membership Model Overview)
- Community Members: "Cost: available at no cost" (Community Members)
- Foundation Members: "For campuses seeking a home for original, internally developed open source software projects." The cost is a "Sliding scale based on the member organization's annual budget" (Foundation Members)
- Institutional Members: "*OSPO as a Service:* Apereo Foundation provides a “center of excellence” for the institution’s internal open source operations and structure, acting as an Open Source Program Office for the Institutional Member" (Institutional Members)
- Sustaining Members: "Organizations that have implemented an Apereo project wish to directly fund the open source software they rely on." The cost "Varies by project community." (Sustaining Members)
- Commercial Members: "Cost: Standard $2,500.00 Platinum $10,000.00" (Commercial Members)

## Evidence and method

This is a membership page of the foundation. It gives membership types, prices and the foundation's own counts. It does not say how it counts institutions or contributors.

## Limits

- The page gives no total income and no number of members in each type.
- The fee tables for Institutional and Sustaining Members are on [Apereo institutional members (web)](Apereo%20institutional%20members%20%28web%29.md) and [Apereo sustaining members (web)](Apereo%20sustaining%20members%20%28web%29.md).
