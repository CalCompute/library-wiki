# TACC EU GDPR page (web)

## Citation

Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC), The University of Texas at Austin. "European Union General Data Protection Regulations (EU GDPR)." TACC website, Security and Compliance section, date not stated. https://tacc.utexas.edu/about/security-and-compliance/eu-gdpr/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- TACC says that it "is working towards compliance" with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It does not say that it complies now.
- A user can ask TACC to remove their GDPR information with a help ticket or an email.

## Background

TACC is a research computing center in the Office of the Vice President for Research at The University of Texas at Austin. This page is one of the pages in the Security and Compliance section of its website. Its user rules are in [TACC user policies (web)](TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md), and its notice on health information is in [TACC privacy practices (web)](TACC%20privacy%20practices%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) is working towards compliance with the European Union General Data Protection Regulations" (page body)
- "The GDPR applies to the processing of personal data, wholly or partly, by automated means and to the processing other than by automated means of personal data which form part of a filing system or are intended to form part of a filing system." (page body)
- "TACC protects the rights for the protection of personal data." (page body)
- "To remove your GDPR information, submit a ticket at consult.tacc.utexas.edu or email help@tacc.utexas.edu." (page body)

## Evidence and method

A web page of about 100 words of text. It states the center's aim and a removal route. It gives no policy text, audit or date.

## Limits

The page does not say which data TACC holds on users in the European Union. It does not say how long TACC keeps that data, or when it expects to comply. It gives no legal basis for processing and no data protection officer.
