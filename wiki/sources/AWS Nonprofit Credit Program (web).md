# AWS Nonprofit Credit Program (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "The AWS Nonprofit Credit Program." Program web page, date not stated. https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/nonprofit-credit-program/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- AWS gives nonprofits and public libraries up to $5,000 in AWS Promotional Credit.
- TechSoup and its partner NGOs (non-governmental organizations) give out the credit. An organization can request one grant each fiscal year.
- The credit covers on-demand services and lasts at least one year.

## Background

AWS publishes this page for its credit program for nonprofits. The page gives eligibility, the route to request credit, and the services that the credit covers. It also has general questions and answers about AWS.

## Key claims

- Offer: "The AWS Nonprofit Credit Program provides access to up to $5,000 USD in AWS Promotional Credit, helping to offset costs for nonprofits associated with implementing cloud-based solutions." (Get started)
- Eligibility: "The AWS Nonprofit Credit Program is available to nonprofits with 501(c)(1), 501(c)(3), 501(c)(8), 501(c)(9), 501(c)(11), 501(c)(12), 501(c)(14), and 501(c)(15) designations and public libraries with valid 501(c)(3) status or licensed within the Institute of Museum and Library Services database." (FAQ)
- Distribution: "The AWS Nonprofit Credit Program works with TechSoup and its Partner NGOs to distribute AWS Promotional Credit to qualified organizations." (FAQ)
- Frequency: "Organizations may request one grant of AWS Promotional Credit each fiscal year (July 1 to June 30)." (FAQ)
- Coverage: "The AWS Promotional Credit is valid for all on-demand services with pay-as-you-go pricing." The credit does not cover Reserved Instances, AWS Marketplace or "any services for mining for cryptocurrency". (FAQ)
- After the credit: "Once your AWS Promotional Credit is exhausted or expires, your account will be charged for services as you use them." (FAQ)

## Evidence and method

The program's own web page. It states the offer and the rules. The quotes from users do not give counts or totals.

## Limits

- The page does not state how many organizations receive the credit, or the total value.
- The page does not state the TechSoup request steps. It links to TechSoup.
- The general AWS questions on the page give figures, such as "76 Availability Zones across 24 geographic regions", without a date.
