## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and UAW Local 4811 for Academic Student Employees (BX) and Graduate Student Researchers (BR). It ends on Dec. 31, 2029.
- UC keeps the right "to subcontract all or any portion of any operations" and to introduce or eliminate methods, equipment or facilities.
- UC also keeps the right to decide research topics and approaches, and who does the teaching.

## Background

The agreement covers two units on the UC campuses. Academic Student Employees include Teaching Assistants. A Graduate Student Researcher does funded research "under the specific direction of a faculty member or authorized Principal Investigator". The Public Employment Relations Board (PERB) certified the units. The agreement has 40 articles.

## Key claims

- "The University hereby recognizes the International Union, United Automobile, Aerospace and Agricultural Implement Workers of America (UAW), AFL-CIO and its Local Union 4811 as the exclusive representative for matters within the scope of representation for all employees within what was previously defined as the Academic Student Employee Unit" (Article 01A, A)
- "Graduate student who performs research as a condition of receiving financial remuneration from funding generated by the University in an academic department or research unit" (Article 01B, B.1.a)
- "to introduce new or improved methods, programs, equipment, or facilities or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment, or facilities;" (Article 19, A.8)
- "to subcontract all or any portion of any operations;" (Article 19, A.20)
- "to determine the research topics, goals and approaches, and the qualifications of personnel required to perform the research;" (Article 19, A.23)
- "to determine how and by whom instruction is delivered;" (Article 19, A.26)
- "The terms and conditions of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect commencing upon ratification by the parties, and shall terminate at 11:59 p.m. on December 31, 2029." (Article 40, A.1)

## Evidence and method

The full text of a collective bargaining agreement for two units, with settlements and side letters. It is a legal agreement between a public university and a union. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The text has no article on subcontracting beyond the management right in Article 19, and it does not name artificial intelligence.
- The cover gives the term as March 20, 2026 to Dec. 31, 2029. The file does not give a ratification date on its cover.
