# SEIU Local 1000 State MOU 2023-2026 (web)

## Citation

SEIU Local 1000 and the State of California. "Master Agreement 2023–2026. Effective July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026. Covering Bargaining Units 1, 3, 4, 11, 14, 15, 17, 20 & 21." Published 2023-09-13 (row date); file address dated 2025/05. https://www.seiu1000.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/192370_SEIU_MasterAgreement_23-26_wCover_V5.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and SEIU Local 1000 for nine state bargaining units, July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026.
- The State must try to notify the union 180 days before automation or technological changes with a significant impact on unit employees. The minimum is 60 days. On request, the State must negotiate their impact.
- The State "must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain bargaining unit employees before resorting to the use of private contractors". An Information Technology Joint Apprenticeship Committee gives training programs for IT classifications.

## Background

SEIU Local 1000 is the union of nearly 100,000 California state workers, by its own cover letter. The Department of Human Resources (CalHR) negotiates for the State. Unit 1 is the Professional, Administrative, Financial, and Staff Services unit. The file opens with a union cover letter, then the contract, side letters and salary schedules. The union's updates on bargaining for the next contract are [SEIU Local 1000 Master Table Update 2026-06-11 (web)](SEIU%20Local%201000%20Master%20Table%20Update%202026-06-11%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The State shall endeavor to notify the Union one hundred eighty (180) days, but no less than sixty (60) days, prior to implementation of automation or technological changes that will result in a significant impact on bargaining unit employees." (14.5 Automation and New Technology)
- "Upon request of the Union within thirty (30) days of such notification, the State shall negotiate with the Union on the impact of such changes." (14.5)
- "The State shall notify the Union at least thirty (30) calendar days in advance of the effective date, before assigning duties normally performed by employees in the bargaining units covered by this Contract to any employee, group, individual, organization or business enterprise, if such assignment(s) may result in the displacement of employees" (14.7 A)
- "The purpose of this section is to guarantee that the State does not incur unnecessary, additional costs by contracting out work appropriately performed at less expense to the State by bargaining unit employees" (14.8 A)
- "Except in extremely unusual or urgent, time-limited circumstances, or under other circumstances where contracting out is recognized or required by law, Federal mandate, or court decisions/orders, the State must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain bargaining unit employees before resorting to the use of private contractors." (14.8 B)
- "Departments will provide the Union's designated representative with copies of Requests for Proposals (RFPs) and Invitations for Bid (IFBs) for personal services contracts when released for publication if they call for services found in bargaining unit class specifications." (14.8 C.1)
- "the department shall arrange for counseling, education, and training of employees as may be reasonably needed to prepare them for placement in other State civil service positions when the employee's positions have been and are about to be changed substantially or eliminated by automation, technological changes or other management initiated changes." (13.10.1 Education and Training (Unit 1), L)
- "The purpose of the IT JAC is to provide training programs for IT classifications, (e.g., entry-level, career development and project management)." (5.20.1 Information Technology Joint Apprenticeship Committee)
- "Unless a specific provision provides for a different effective date, the term of this Contract shall be July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026." (24.2 A)

## Evidence and method

The full text of a state collective bargaining agreement (a memorandum of understanding), with side letters and salary schedules. It gives rules. The union's cover letter gives its own account of the gains, for example "a 10% general salary increase over the life of this contract".

## Limits

- The term ended on June 30, 2026. The library holds the union's account of bargaining for the next contract, not a successor agreement.
- The text does not name artificial intelligence. Its technology rules are 14.5 and the retraining rules of Article 13.
- Each unit has its own rules in many sections. The quotes from 13.10.1 are for Unit 1.
- The publisher is the union, not the State. The cover letter is the union's text, not part of the agreement.
