# MGHPCC AI Compute Resource page (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC). "MGHPCC AI Computing Resource (AICR)." Project page, date not stated. https://mghpcc.org/project/mghpcc-ai-computing-resource-aicr/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The AI Compute Resource (AICR) is a shared AI platform that MGHPCC operates with its member institutions, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and regional partners.
- It has graphics processing unit (GPU) systems, high-performance networks and large-scale storage. Its users are researchers, educators, startups and public-sector organizations.

## Background

MGHPCC is a computing facility at 100 Bigelow Street in Holyoke, Massachusetts. This short project page on its website introduces AICR in one paragraph and links a longer post. The rest of the page lists other MGHPCC projects and partners. The university partners are Boston University, Harvard, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Northeastern, the University of Massachusetts and Yale. Dell EMC, Cisco and the Commonwealth appear "IN PARTNERSHIP WITH".

## Key claims

- "Delivering the advanced computing and data infrastructure needed to support AI-driven research, innovation, and emerging businesses across the region." (page summary)
- "The AI Compute Resource (AICR) is a shared AI infrastructure platform operated by the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center in partnership with its member institutions, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, and regional partners." (AICR paragraph)
- "Built around advanced GPU systems, high-performance networking, and large-scale storage, AICR provides researchers, educators, startups, and public-sector organizations with the computing resources needed for modern AI applications." (AICR paragraph)
- "By expanding access to advanced computing, AICR supports AI-driven research, innovation, workforce development, and technology commercialization across Massachusetts." (AICR paragraph)

## Evidence and method

An institutional project page. It names the operator, the partners, the hardware types and the user groups. It gives no figures. The page links a longer post on the MGHPCC website. The library has not read it.

## Limits

- The page does not give the number or type of GPUs, the cost, the funding or an allocation process.
- It does not say how a user applies.
- The heading says "AI Computing Resource" and the text says "AI Compute Resource". State releases say "Artificial Intelligence Compute Resources" ([Massachusetts AI Hub release](Massachusetts%20AI%20Hub%20release.md)).
- For the builder and the hardware, see [MassTech AICR builder selection (web)](MassTech%20AICR%20builder%20selection%20%28web%29.md). For the operator of MGHPCC, see [MGHPCC governance (web)](MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md).
