# CSU CFA Unit 3 contract 2022-2026 (web)

## Citation

The Board of Trustees of the California State University and the California Faculty Association. "Unit 3 – Faculty Collective Bargaining Agreement between the Board of Trustees of the California State University and the California Faculty Association. February 3, 2022 – June 30, 2025 (as modified on March 4, 2024)." California State University. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Documents/unit3-cfa/2022-2026-CFA-CBA.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the California State University (CSU) and the California Faculty Association (CFA) for Unit 3, the faculty. Its cover gives the term February 3, 2022 to June 30, 2025, as modified on March 4, 2024.
- In a decision on excessive workload, "the effects of the introduction of new instructional technologies" are one of the primary elements to consider.
- Faculty who teach online have the same evaluation rights. Faculty keep use of materials made without extraordinary University support, software included.

## Background

CFA represents CSU's Unit 3. CSU posts the agreement on its [CSU CFA Unit 3 contract page (web)](CSU%20CFA%20Unit%203%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) as the "Current Unit 3 CBA". A note on each page says that Articles 20, 23, 31, 32, 37 and 41 changed on March 4, 2024.

## Key claims

- "The CSU retains and reserves to itself, whether exercised or not, all powers, rights, authorities, duties, and responsibilities that have not been specifically abridged, delegated, or modified by this Agreement." (Article 5, 5.1)
- "Faculty members teaching online are subject to all the rights and conditions set out in Article 15 and applicable campus evaluation policies. The collection and use of online course quantitative data for evaluation purposes shall only occur when required in campus evaluation policies and procedures." (Article 15, 15.3)
- "In determining what is "excessive" or "unreasonable" under this section, the items listed under 20.3(b), as well as the number of students seeking to take courses in the academic area, the distribution of student enrollment, the level of support provided the program, and the effects of the introduction of new instructional technologies, and the prior practices of the University shall be among the primary elements to be considered." (Article 20, 20.3.c)
- "Unless there is a separate agreement to the contrary, consistent with this Article, neither the CSU nor third party providers are entitled to grant licenses or make assignments with respect to such materials to publishers and publishing agents, or any third party." (Article 39, 39.3)
- "This Agreement does not apply to those materials created with extraordinary University support, which shall be addressed by separate individual agreements at the campus." (Article 39, 39.6)
- "This agreement shall be effective upon its ratification by both parties and remain in full force and effect up to and including June 30, 2025." (Article 41, 41.1)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university system and a faculty union. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The file address names 2022-2026, but the cover and Article 41 give June 30, 2025 as the end of the term. [CSU CFA Unit 3 contract extension to 2026-07-31 (web)](CSU%20CFA%20Unit%203%20contract%20extension%20to%202026-07-31%20%28web%29.md) extends the agreement, "scheduled to expire on June 30, 2026", to July 31, 2026. The library holds no document for the change from June 30, 2025 to June 30, 2026, and no successor agreement.
- The agreement has no article on artificial intelligence and no article on contracting out.
- [CFA PERB hearing on management's AI initiative (web)](CFA%20PERB%20hearing%20on%20management%27s%20AI%20initiative%20%28web%29.md) reports a CFA proposal for AI terms in its contract. It also reports a hearing over CSU's AI Initiative of February 2025.
