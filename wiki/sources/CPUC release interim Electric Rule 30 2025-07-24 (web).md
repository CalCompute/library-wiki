# CPUC release interim Electric Rule 30 2025-07-24 (web)

## Citation

California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC). "CPUC Streamlines Electric Grid Connections for High-Energy Users Like Data Centers and EV Chargers." News release, July 24, 2025. https://www.cpuc.ca.gov/news-and-updates/all-news/cpuc-streamlines-electric-grid-connections-for-high-energy-users-like-data-centers-and-ev-chargers. Read 2026-11-19. ## Bottom line

- The CPUC announces that it approved an interim Electric Rule 30 for the Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E) service territory. The rule speeds up grid connections at transmission level for large users such as artificial intelligence (AI) data centers.
- Only applicants that pay for the transmission work in advance can use the interim rule. A later decision is to set refunds.

## Background

The CPUC regulates utilities in California. This release announces Decision 25-07-039, of the same date: [CPUC Decision 25-07-039 interim Electric Rule 30 (web)](CPUC%20Decision%2025-07-039%20interim%20Electric%20Rule%2030%20%28web%29.md). The release is four paragraphs long.

## Key claims

- The CPUC "today approved an interim electric rule to streamline and accelerate electric grid connections at the transmission level for high-energy users such as artificial intelligence (AI) data centers and electric vehicle (EV) charging stations within the Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E) service territory." (para. 1)
- "To date, PG&E has handled requests from large transmission-level customers on a case-by-case basis, which can be slow and resource-intensive." (para. 2)
- "The interim rule is only available for applicants that agree to pay for necessary transmission infrastructure work upfront. The terms and cost allocation of any refunds will be determined by a future CPUC decision." (para. 3)
- The decision protects "ratepayers from any potential risks associated with serving these large new loads." (para. 4)

## Evidence and method

- A government news release. It gives no figures and no evidence. The decision gives the record.
