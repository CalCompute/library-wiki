# Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). 2025 Annual Report. PDF, date not stated. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/files/publications/annualreports/2025_Annual_Report_Final.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The report describes RCAC's computing at Purdue University in Indiana. It covers the Gautschi campus cluster, the National Science Foundation (NSF) Anvil system and on-premises large language model (LLM) services.
- NSF funds Anvil with "a $10 million acquisition grant". Anvil became a National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot resource provider in May 2024 and added 84 NVIDIA H100 GPUs.
- It says that RCAC serves as an "on-ramp" to ACCESS, NAIRR, Department of Energy (DOE) systems through the Argonne Lighthouse program, and commercial clouds.

## Background

RCAC is the research computing center of Purdue IT. This report covers 2025 and the 2024-25 academic year. It has sections on Gautschi, Anvil, research software engineering, industry partners, the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF) Lighthouse Initiative, external funding, student programs and outreach. The RCAC Executive Director, Preston Smith, signs the welcome.

## Key claims

- Community clusters: "20+ years into our community cluster program, we provide powerful but cost-effective high-performance computing resources that now have pivoted into AI, with our new system “Gautschi”" (Director’s Welcome, p. 01)
- On-ramp: "we’re prepared to serve as your on- ramp to national-scale platforms via the NSF’s ACCESS and NAIRR infrastructures, the Department of Energy’s leadership class systems via our partnership with Argonne National Lab’s Lighthouse program, and commercial clouds." (Director’s Welcome, p. 01)
- Gautschi funding: "The Gautschi cluster was built through a partnership with Dell, AMD, and Nvidia, thanks to support from Purdue Computes and the Institute for Physical AI (IPAI)." (p. 04)
- Gautschi AI partition: "The AI partition, Gautschi-AI, is comprised of 20 Dell PowerEdge XE9680 compute nodes, each with Dual 56-Core Intel Xeon Platinum 8480+ CPUs, 8 Nvidia H100 SXM GPUs with 80 GB of RAM, and 8x non-blocking 400 Gbps NDR links." (p. 04)
- Teaching: "During the 2024-25 Academic Year (Fall 2024 – Summer 2025 semesters), 121 courses used HPC for instructional purposes, impacting a total of over 4700 students." (HPC Impact on Courses, p. 04)
- Anvil: "Thanks to supplemental funding from the NSF’s National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot, the Anvil AI partition was added to the supercomputer and brought online. A total of 84 Nvidia H100 SXM GPUs were procured and added to the system." (Anvil Year Four, p. 05)
- Industry: "The Industry Partnership program allows industry users to utilize the Anvil supercomputer for their business needs, but at a fraction of the cost of private HPC companies." (Industry Partners, p. 11)
- Lighthouse: "Purdue will be given a discretionary allocation for ALCF machines, and the RCAC staff will receive intensive training on the use of these systems." (Lighthouse Initiative, p. 18)
- On-premises LLMs: "Both AnvilGPT and Purdue GenAI Studio are hosted entirely on-premises, meaning no documents or contexts are uploaded into commercial cloud-hosted AI services." (Powerful New LLM Services)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional annual report. It gives system specifications, usage counts, training counts and program descriptions. Its figures come from RCAC. The Markdown copy of the PDF loses the layout of the statistics panels, so some numbers do not match their labels in the copy. This page quotes only running text. The parts read were the welcome, the Gautschi, Anvil, Industry Partners, Lighthouse and LLM services sections, and the start of the student news.

## Limits

- The report does not give the cost of Gautschi or the prices of the community cluster program. For the purchase units, see [Purdue RCAC Gautschi overview (web)](Purdue%20RCAC%20Gautschi%20overview%20%28web%29.md).
- The External Funding page has no text in the Markdown copy.
- It does not state any funding from the State of Indiana.
- The Anvil text gives "more than 12,000" users and "1.8 billion CPU hours" in running text. The statistics panel on the same page shows 14,319 Anvil users and 2.4 billion allocated CPU hours. The report does not explain the difference.
