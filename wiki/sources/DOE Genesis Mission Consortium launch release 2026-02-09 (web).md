# DOE Genesis Mission Consortium launch release 2026-02-09 (web)

## Citation

U.S. Department of Energy (DOE). "Energy Department Launches Genesis Mission Consortium to Accelerate AI-Driven Scientific Discovery and American innovation." Press release, Washington, Feb. 9, 2026. https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-launches-genesis-mission-consortium-accelerate-ai-driven-scientific. ## Bottom line

- DOE announced the Genesis Mission Consortium, "a historic public-private partnership" for the Genesis Mission.
- The release says that the Genesis Mission Consortium finds partnerships, promotes solicitations, executes agreements and tracks projects. TechWerx, a DOE partnership intermediary that RTI International operates, administers it.

## Background

DOE published this release on Feb. 9, 2026. It links the Genesis Mission Consortium to two Executive Orders, including Executive Order 14363 ([Executive Order 14363 Launching the Genesis Mission (web)](Executive%20Order%2014363%20Launching%20the%20Genesis%20Mission%20%28web%29.md)). It quotes Under Secretary for Science Darío Gil, the Genesis Mission lead. It sets out the functions, working groups and events of the Genesis Mission Consortium.

## Key claims

- "the consortium brings together technical capabilities and expertise from the Department of Energy, National Laboratories, private sector leaders, and academic institutions to usher in a new era of science and technology exploration." (paragraph 2)
- "The consortium will help identify high-value partnerships among its members and external stakeholders, strengthening collaborative responses to funding opportunities." (paragraph 4)
- "It will amplify DOE’s outreach by promoting solicitations, executing agreements, and tracking project successes." (paragraph 4)
- "Functioning as a collaborative hub, the consortium will serve as a single, coordinated access point for members and their resources." (paragraph 4)
- "the consortium will facilitate member-driven working groups focused on AI model development and validation, data integration and standards, high-performance computing and cloud infrastructure, and robotics and automation." (paragraph 5)
- "The consortium will be administered by TechWerx, a DOE partnership intermediary operated by RTI International." (closing note)

## Evidence and method

A federal press release. It states the purpose, functions and administrator of the Genesis Mission Consortium. It gives no members, no budget and no membership terms.

## Limits

- The release does not say how an organization joins or what a member contributes. The [Genesis Mission Consortium FAQ (web)](Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md) gives those terms.
- It lists four working groups. Later pages list a fifth, "Workforce Development and Training" ([DOE Genesis Mission Collaboration page (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Collaboration%20page%20%28web%29.md); [Genesis Mission Consortium homepage (web)](Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- The release uses "artificial intelligence (AI)". The later consortium pages use "SI" for the same mission.
- For the partner commitments that DOE reported later, see [DOE Genesis Mission 800 million partner commitments (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20800%20million%20partner%20commitments%20%28web%29.md).
