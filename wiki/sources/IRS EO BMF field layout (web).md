# IRS EO BMF field layout (web)

## Citation

Internal Revenue Service (IRS). "Exempt Organizations Business Master File Extract (EO BMF)." Information sheet (PDF), November 2023. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/eo-info.pdf. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 IRS EO BMF field layout.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20IRS%20EO%20BMF%20field%20layout.md) (original: `.pdf` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The sheet lists the 28 fields of the EO BMF files and explains their codes. One code is the National Taxonomy of Exempt Entities (NTEE).
- The NTEE code classifies an organization "in terms of its primary exempt activity". Major group U is "Science and Technology", with U41 "Computer Science".

## Background

The IRS publishes this information sheet with the EO BMF data files. The download page, [IRS EO BMF extract page (web)](IRS%20EO%20BMF%20extract%20page%20%28web%29.md), calls it the sheet that "explains the codes used in the data files". The sheet has the date November 2023.

## Key claims

- "Exempt organization information is extracted monthly from the Internal Revenue Service’s Business Master File." (Introduction)
- "If an organization's exemption is revoked, an announcement to inform potential donors of the revocation is published in the Internal Revenue Bulletin. In addition, the organization’s name is removed from publicly accessible venues, including this file." (Introduction)
- "The National Taxonomy of Exempt Entities (NTEE) Code is a four-digit code used to classify an exempt organization in terms of its primary exempt activity." (NTEE Code)
- The NTEE table lists "U Science and Technology", "U05 Research Institutes and Public Policy Analysis", "U40 Engineering and Technology" and "U41 Computer Science". (Common Codes)
- Foundation code "15" is "Organization that receives a substantial part of its support from a governmental unit or the general public 170(b)(1)(A)(vi)". (Foundation Code)
- "Income Amount is a computer-generated amount from the most recent Form 990 series return filed by the organization." (Income Amount)

## Evidence and method

A data dictionary. It lists the fields, their positions and the code tables. It gives no counts of organizations.

## Limits

The sheet does not define the fourth digit of the NTEE code. It gives no data on any organization. Its date, November 2023, is older than the data files that the download page lists. Its order of states in each region differs from the download page, but the region members match.
