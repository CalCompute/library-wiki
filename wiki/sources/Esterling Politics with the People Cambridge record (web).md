## Bottom line

- *Politics with the People* is a 2018 book by Michael A. Neblo, Kevin M. Esterling and David M. J. Lazer, from Cambridge University Press.
- It reports experiments in which members of Congress met groups of their constituents in online, deliberative town hall meetings.
