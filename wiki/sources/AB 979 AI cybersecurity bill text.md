## Bottom line

- AB 979 amends Government Code section 8586.5. The California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) must develop a "California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook" on or before Jan. 1, 2027.
- The playbook must include mandatory mechanisms for state AI contractors and vendors to share known threats and vulnerabilities. It can include voluntary mechanisms for other entities.
- Cyber threat information shared under the playbook is confidential.

## Background

Assembly Member Irwin wrote the bill, with Senator McNerney as coauthor. Cal-CSIC is a center that the Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) leads. The bill models the playbook on the federal Joint Cyber Defense Collaborative (JCDC) playbook of Jan. 14, 2025. The library's copy comes from the Legislature's website through the Wayback Machine.

## Key claims

- "On or before January 1, 2027, the California Cybersecurity Integration Center shall develop, in consultation with the Office of Information Security and the Government Operations Agency, a California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook to facilitate information sharing across the cyber and artificial intelligence communities and to strengthen collective cyber defenses against emerging threats, including AI." (Gov. Code § 8586.5(g)(1))
- "The California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook shall include mandatory mechanisms for information sharing on potential threats and vulnerabilities known to state contractors and vendors providing artificial intelligence services regarding those contracted or purchased services, to a state entity identified in the California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook." (§ 8586.5(g)(3))
- "Notwithstanding any other law, any information related to cyber threat indicators or defensive measures for a cybersecurity purpose shared in accordance with the California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook developed under this subdivision is confidential" (§ 8586.5(g)(6))
- The center's members include "(9) The California State University. (10) The University of California. (11) The California Community Colleges." (§ 8586.5(a))
- "On January 14, 2025, the JCDC published the JCDC AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook to facilitate voluntary information sharing across the cyber and artificial intelligence (AI) communities" (Sec. 1(b), findings)
