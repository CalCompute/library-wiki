## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and the California Nurses Association (CNA) for the Registered Nurse (NX) unit, Nov. 22, 2025 to Jan. 2, 2030.
- Article 42, New Technology, says that clinical technology, "including artificial intelligence (AI)", is to complement nursing skills. New technology "shall not be used to replace the Nurse’s role in delivery of care to patients." Nurse committee representatives can take part in the selection, design, building and validation of new technology.
- UC keeps the right to subcontract. For a subcontract of $1,000,000 or more a year in new operations, UC gives CNA at least 90 days notice.

## Background

CNA represents UC's registered nurses. UC posts the complete agreement on its [UC NX contract page (web)](UC%20NX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md). The Professional Practice Committee (PPC) of Article 7 is a committee of unit nurses at each patient care facility. Its scope includes technology.

## Key claims

- "Clinical technology, including artificial intelligence (AI), is intended to complement, not diminish, nursing skills, judgment and decision-making. The use of technology shall not limit the Nurse’s exercise of clinical judgment in assessment, evaluation, planning and implementation of care, nor from acting as a patient advocate." (Article 42, A)
- "New technology may affect nursing duties, but shall not be used to replace the Nurse’s role in delivery of care to patients." (Article 42, A)
- "Representatives of the PPC shall be entitled to participate in the selection, design, building and validation processes whenever new technology, including emerging technologies affecting the delivery of nursing care is being considered." (Article 42, B)
- "A Nurse’s patient care assignment shall be taken into consideration when determining the appropriate method for training on new technology." (Article 42, C)
- "The University acknowledges and generally supports the principle of preserving bargaining unit work whenever possible." (Article 44, A.1)
- "When the subcontract is $1,000,000 or more on an annual basis, the University shall provide CNA with at least ninety (90) calendar days’ notice of its intent to initially contract out newly created operations in which subcontracting may be appropriate that may traditionally involve bargaining unit work at the individual location." (Article 44, B.1)
- "to introduce new or improved methods, programs, equipment, or facilities or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment, or facilities;" (Article 33, B.5)
- "The terms and conditions of this agreement shall remain in full force and effect commencing, November 22, 2025, and shall terminate at 11:59 p.m. on January 2, 2030" (Article 38, A)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a nurses' union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The file address names an initial draft. The [UC NX contract page (web)](UC%20NX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) says that Appendix A, the wage tables, "is being updated".
- It is the only labor agreement in the library with an article that names artificial intelligence.
- Article 44 exempts registry and traveling nurses. It gives no definition of "new technology".
