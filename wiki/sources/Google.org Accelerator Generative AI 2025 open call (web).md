# Google.org Accelerator Generative AI 2025 open call (web)

## Citation

Johnson, Maggie (Global Head and VP, Google.org). "An open call for the next Google.org Accelerator: Generative AI." Google blog (The Keyword), Google.org, Jan. 15, 2025. https://blog.google/company-news/outreach-and-initiatives/google-org/google-org-generative-ai-accelerator-2025/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Google.org opened a $30 million global call for the second cohort of its Generative AI Accelerator.
- Nonprofits, civic entities, academic institutions and social enterprises could apply by Feb. 10, 2025.
- Selected organizations get a six-month accelerator with technical training, Google Cloud credits, pro bono support and a share of the funding.

## Background

Google.org is the philanthropy of Google. This blog post announces the open call. It also describes the first cohort of 2024, which had 21 organizations.

## Key claims

- Call: "Apply now to receive funding and participate in the Google.org Accelerator: Generative AI, a $30 million global open call for organizations using the power of generative AI to unlock potential for everyone, everywhere." (summary line)
- Barrier: "A 2024 Google for Nonprofits survey found that while four in five nonprofits feel that generative AI is applicable to their work, nearly half are not using the technology due to a lack of awareness, training, tools and funding." (body)
- First cohort: Google.org "launched a Generative AI Accelerator in 2024 that supported 21 organizations to build gen AI-powered solutions that collectively aim to serve more than 30 million people by 2028." (body)
- Eligibility: "Nonprofits, civic entities, academic institutions and social enterprises can apply if they are advancing social impact solutions across Google.org's three focus areas." (body)
- Offer: "Selected organizations will participate in a six-month accelerator that includes technical training, Google Cloud credits and pro-bono support from Google employees, along with a share of $30 million in funding." (body)
- Deadline: "Visit g.co/Accelerator/GenAI to apply by February 10, 2025." (body)

## Evidence and method

A company blog post that announces a call. It gives the offer and three examples from the first cohort. The post takes the 2024 survey figure from a separate Google for Nonprofits report.

## Limits

- The post does not state the size of each award or the amount of Google Cloud credits.
- The post does not state the selection criteria or how many organizations it selects.
- The deadline in the post passed on Feb. 10, 2025. The post does not report the result of the call.
- The page shows a summary that Google AI wrote. This page does not quote it.
