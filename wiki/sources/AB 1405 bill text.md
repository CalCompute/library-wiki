# AB 1405 bill text

## Bottom line

- AB 1405 adds Government Code sections 11549.80 and after. The Government Operations Agency (GovOps) must set up an AI Auditor Registry on its website by Jan. 1, 2029.
- From Jan. 1, 2029, a person must not offer, sell or conduct a "covered AI audit" without registration.
- A registered AI auditor must report to the agency, meet standards of independence, keep records for 10 years and not retaliate against whistleblowers. Fees go to a new AI Auditors' Registration Fund.

## Key claims

- "“Covered AI audit” means an audit conducted to assess internal controls, processes, or systems implemented for an AI system or model that are necessary for compliance with state law." (Gov. Code § 11549.80(d))
- "No later than January 1, 2029, the agency shall do all of the following: (1) Establish an AI Auditor Registry on the agency’s internet website allowing AI auditors to register with the agency." (§ 11549.82(a))
- "Beginning January 1, 2029, a person shall not offer, sell, or conduct a covered AI audit unless the person is registered with the agency pursuant to this chapter." (§ 11549.82.5)
- "A statement prominently disclosing that registration by an AI auditor does not constitute recommendation or endorsement of that entity by the State of California." (§ 11549.82(b)(2)(C))
- "A registered AI auditor shall maintain independence from the auditee and shall not conduct a covered AI audit if the auditor has a financial, business, employment, or other interest or relationship that would reasonably be expected to impair the auditor’s independence or objectivity." (§ 11549.83(f)(1)(A))
- "All moneys collected or received by the agency under this chapter shall be deposited into the AI Auditors’ Registration Fund to be available for expenditure by the agency, upon appropriation by the Legislature, to administer this chapter." (§ 11549.81)

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate and no fee amount; fees must not exceed "the reasonable costs of administering this chapter" (§ 11549.82(a)(2)). It does not mention research compute or CalCompute. Accountants licensed by the California Board of Accountancy meet some duties through their own professional standards (§ 11549.83(d)(2) and (f)(2)). The agent did not read the sections after 11549.83 in full.
