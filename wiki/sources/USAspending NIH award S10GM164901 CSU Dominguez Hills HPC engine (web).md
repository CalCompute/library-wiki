## Bottom line

- NIH gives California State University (CSU), Dominguez Hills Foundation $224,992 to buy a shared high-performance computing (HPC) system, a "POWERWULF™ ZXR1".
- The system supports nine major users in five departments and training in five courses. The period of performance is Sept. 1, 2026 to July 31, 2027.

## Background

USAspending.gov publishes federal spending records. This is its API record for one NIH grant. The NIH National Institute of General Medical Sciences (NIGMS) funds and awards it. The Assistance Listing is 93.859, "Biomedical Research and Research Training", and the funding opportunity is PAR-23-138. The recipient is in Carson, Los Angeles County. Its business categories are "Higher Education" and "Higher Education (Public)".

## Key claims

- "FUNDS ARE REQUESTED FOR THE ACQUISITION OF A POWERWULF™ ZXR1 HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTING (HPC) ENGINE TO ENHANCE COMPUTATIONAL SKILL DEVELOPMENT IN BIOMEDICAL EDUCATION AND RESEARCH AT CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY, DOMINGUEZ HILLS." (description)
- "THIS CENTRALIZED SHARED RESOURCE WILL SUPPORT THE RESEARCH OF NINE MAJOR USERS ACROSS FIVE DEPARTMENTS" (description)
- "THIS SHARED RESOURCE WILL ALSO HELP INTEGRATE HANDS-ON TRAINING INTO FIVE CORE COURSES ACROSS FOUR DEPARTMENTS" (description)
- The system is for "OVER 50 STUDENTS ANNUALLY TO DEVELOP PRACTICAL SKILLS IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE–DRIVEN PROTEIN DESIGN, MOLECULAR DYNAMICS, QUANTUM AND SEMI-EMPIRICAL CHEMISTRY, GENOMICS, MATHEMATICAL MODELING, AND DEEP-LEARNING BIOINFORMATICS." (description)
- "THE POWERWULF™ ZXR1 HPC ENGINE WILL BE HOUSED IN THE CSU DOMINGUEZ HILLS DATA CENTER, MANAGED BY THE CSUDH IT DEPARTMENT" (description)
- "AN ADVISORY COMMITTEE WILL ENSURE BROAD ACCESS, WHILE STRUCTURED COURSEWORK AND REGULAR WORKSHOPS WILL SUSTAIN AND EXPAND INTERDISCIPLINARY COLLABORATIONS" (description)

## Evidence and method

A federal award record. It gives the amount obligated, the dates, the funding office, the Assistance Listing and the recipient. The description is the recipient's project summary at the time of the award, not a report on results. The record shows no outlay yet and no subawards. The non-federal funding is $0.

## Limits

- The record gives no specification of the system: no count of cores, GPUs or storage.
- It does not say who can use the system outside the nine major users and the five courses.
- The applicant eligibility text of Assistance Listing 93.859 is about "NIGMS trainees". It is not specific to equipment awards.
- The description names the major users. This page does not repeat their names.
