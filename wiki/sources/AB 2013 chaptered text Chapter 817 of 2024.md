## Bottom line

- AB 2013 adds Title 15.2, "Artificial Intelligence Training Data Transparency", to the Civil Code, from section 3110.
- A developer of a generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) system or service must post documentation of its training data on its website.
- The duty starts on or before Jan. 1, 2026. It covers a system released on or after Jan. 1, 2022, and each later substantial modification.
- "Developer" includes a state or local government agency that makes an AI system for use by members of the public.

## Background

The bill's author is Assembly Member Irwin, as the Digest prints the name. The Governor approved it on Sept. 28, 2024. The text holds the Legislative Counsel's Digest and the new title in two sections: definitions and the documentation duty. A 2025 bill refers to this duty in its Digest ([AB 316 chaptered text Chapter 672 of 2025](AB%20316%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20672%20of%202025.md)). The library's copy comes from the Legislature's website through the Wayback Machine.

## Key claims

- "“Developer” means a person, partnership, state or local government agency, or corporation that designs, codes, produces, or substantially modifies an artificial intelligence system or service for use by members of the public." (Civ. Code § 3110(b))
- "A high-level summary of the datasets used in the development of the generative artificial intelligence system or service" (§ 3111(a), the first required item)
- "Whether the datasets include any data protected by copyright, trademark, or patent, or whether the datasets are entirely in the public domain." (§ 3111(a)(5))
- "Whether the datasets include personal information, as defined in subdivision (v) of Section 1798.140." (§ 3111(a)(7))
- "“Train a generative artificial intelligence system or service” includes testing, validating, or fine tuning by the developer of the artificial intelligence system or service." (§ 3110(f))

## Limits

The bill sets no penalty and names no enforcing agency. It gives no cost estimate. The duty has three exceptions (§ 3111(b)). They are a system whose sole purpose is security and integrity, a system for aircraft operation, and a defense system for federal use only. The summary can give the number of data points "in general ranges". The capture is of Aug. 29, 2026, so a later change does not show.

The bill does not mention research compute or CalCompute.
