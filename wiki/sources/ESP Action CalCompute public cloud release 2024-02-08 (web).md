# ESP Action CalCompute public cloud release 2024-02-08 (web)

## Citation

Economic Security Project Action. "Landmark CA legislation would create 'public cloud' allowing equitable AI access and spurring innovation." Press release, Sacramento, February 8, 2024. https://economicsecurity.us/news/landmark-ca-legislation-would-create-public-cloud-allowing-equitable-ai-access-and-spurring-innovation/. Read 2026-10-20.

## Bottom line

- It announces SB 1047 (2024), not SB 53.
- Economic Security California Action co-sponsored SB 1047 and called CalCompute "a public cloud". The CalCompute idea came from this sponsor before SB 53.
- The release is advocacy. It is a primary source for the sponsor's own position only.

## Background

The bill set safety rules for large AI models and included CalCompute. The Governor vetoed SB 1047 in 2024 ([ESP Governor signs SB-53 release 2025-09-29 (web)](ESP%20Governor%20signs%20SB-53%20release%202025-09-29%20%28web%29.md)). SB 53 (2025) carried CalCompute forward as a consortium that writes a framework. Economic Security California Action is the advocacy affiliate of the [Economic Security Project](../entities/Economic%20Security%20Project.md) (ESP) in California.

## Key claims

- Subtitle: "CA State Sen. Wiener introduces bill co-sponsored by Economic Security California Action to create CalCompute, a public cloud computing cluster."
- SB 1047 "would create a public, state-of-the-art, high-powered computing infrastructure called CalCompute, providing broad access to artificial intelligence (AI) systems to boost innovation, research, development, and disaster response."
- "this bill aims to carve out a space for public good in the field of AI."
- Teri Olle, "Director of Economic Security California": "The AI market is dominated by a handful of corporate actors and this essential legislation takes the first critical step in fostering greater innovation and openness to serve the public interest."
- Olle: "With the development of a public cloud like CalCompute, we can harness the potential of AI for good, accessing data to advance the next breakthroughs in healthcare, technology, and disaster response."
- The bill "would also require fair and transparent pricing."
- The bill "aims to democratize access to cutting-edge technology by creating a public cloud computing cluster".
- Wiener: "SB 1047 will also promote the growth of the AI industry by establishing CalCompute, a public AI research cluster that will allow startups, researchers, and community groups to participate in the development of large-scale AI systems".
- Wiener: "CalCompute will help align large-scale AI systems with the values and needs of California communities."
- Olle: "The vast computing power and money required to develop AI means people who may have brilliant ideas [...] do not have access to the tools they need to pursue them."
- Olle: "AI technology developed with a goal to best serve the public—rather than to achieve market dominance—cannot rely on the traditional business practices that corporations have historically used to amass power and profit".
- "Economic Security California Action is an affiliate of Economic Security Project Action."

## Evidence and method

An organization press release. It gives no data. It quotes the sponsor and the author of the bill. It links the SB 1047 bill text and a PDF copy of the release.

## Limits

- The release is advocacy for a bill. It describes what the sponsor wanted, not what became law.
- SB 1047 did not become law. Its CalCompute text is not the statute that governs the Report.
- The release does not name the companies that dominate the AI market.
- The release does not use the term "public option". That term appears in the [ESP Governor signs SB-53 release 2025-09-29 (web)](ESP%20Governor%20signs%20SB-53%20release%202025-09-29%20%28web%29.md).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The release says SB 1047 "would create a public, state-of-the-art, high-powered computing infrastructure called CalCompute". The law in force, § 11546.8 (a), sets up "a consortium that shall develop, pursuant to this section, a framework for the creation of a public cloud computing cluster to be known as "CalCompute."" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)). The statute governs.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This release calls Teri Olle "Director of Economic Security California". The 2025 release calls her "Director of Economic Security Project California" ([ESP Governor signs SB-53 release 2025-09-29 (web)](ESP%20Governor%20signs%20SB-53%20release%202025-09-29%20%28web%29.md)). The 2024 launch release uses "Director of Economic Security California" ([ESP launches Economic Security California 2024-01-30 (web)](ESP%20launches%20Economic%20Security%20California%202024-01-30%20%28web%29.md)).
