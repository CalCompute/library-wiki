## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the International Union of Operating Engineers (IUOE), Locals 39 and 501, for State Bargaining Unit 13. Unit 13 is the State's stationary engineers. Its term is July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2027.
- The State keeps "complete control and discretion over its organization and the technology of performing its work". It gives IUOE 60 days notice of a change in an area that the MOU does not cover.
- Each appointing power pays $100 a year for each employee into the IUOE Training Fund, when the Legislature approves the funds. The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 13 is the State's stationary engineers. The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement. The terms take effect only with ratification by the Legislature and the union membership, and with funding by the Legislature (Article 17.1).

## Key claims

- "The terms of this Agreement shall go into effect on July 1, 2025, and upon ratification by both the Legislature and the Union's membership and upon approved funding by the Legislature, unless specified elsewhere in the MOU, and remain in full force through June 30, 2027." (Article 17, 17.1.1)
- "All State employer rights and functions, except those which are expressly abridged by this Agreement, shall remain vested with the State." (Article 3, 3.1.1)
- The rights of the State include the right to "determine the methods, means and personnel by which State operations are to be conducted" and to "exercise complete control and discretion over its organization and the technology of performing its work." (Article 3, 3.1.2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, it shall notify IUOE BU 13 of the proposed change sixty (60) days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 4, 4.4)
- "Each appointing power, upon approval of sufficient funds by the Legislature, shall contribute to the IUOE Training Fund to provide a training program for Bargaining Unit 13 employees who wish to improve their skills." (Article 13, 13.2)
- "The contribution, due in January of each year for each employee covered by this Agreement and on the payroll as of September 1 of the previous year, will be one hundred dollars ($100)." (Article 13, 13.2)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, leaves, benefits and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on contracting out and no article on artificial intelligence. Technological change appears only in the Government Code section 19995.2 entry of its list of superseded and incorporated sections.
- The page does not say how much the Legislature funded for the Training Fund.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
