# Gangas Kapor Center profile

## Citation

Kapor Center. "Lili Gangas (She/Her), Chief Technology Community Officer." Team profile page, publication date unknown. https://www.kaporcenter.org/team/lili-gangas/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Lili Gangas is Chief Technology Community Officer at the Kapor Center. She co-leads its Equitable Tech Policy Initiative.
- The initiative's interests include "closing digital divide" and "scaling new tech workforce models".
- Before Kapor she worked at Accenture Technology Lab, Booz Allen and Raytheon. She holds an MBA (New York University Stern) and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering (University of Southern California).

## Background

The Kapor Center is "an operating foundation at the intersection of technology and racial justice". The page is its staff profile.

## Key claims

- "She co-leads the Kapor Center's Equitable Tech Policy Initiative with a focus on inclusive technology policy with special interests in closing digital divide, scaling new tech workforce models, advocating for responsible emerging technology as well as providing foundational support across civic engagement issues and tech enablement of civic organizations." (bio, paragraph 1)
- Fellowships: Omidyar Network's Digital Luminaries, and New America CA. (bio, paragraph 2)
- Working groups: "FCC Diversity & Inclusion WG, Aspen Latinos & Society Digital Equity, Aspen Fair Data Future, Rock Health Latine Digital Health Innovators WG." (bio, paragraph 2)
- She was "a founding member of the Innovation Services team at Booz Allen specializing in crowdsourcing, prize challenges, and open data solutions at the federal level." (bio, paragraph 3)
- She was "a Senior Multi-Disciplined Software Engineer at Raytheon Space and Airborne Systems." (bio, paragraph 3)
