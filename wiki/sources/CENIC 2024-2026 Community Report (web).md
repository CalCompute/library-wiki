# CENIC 2024-2026 Community Report (web)

## Citation

Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California (CENIC). "Community Report 2024-2026." Web page, date not stated. https://cenic.org/publications/2024-2026-community-report. Read 2026-11-17.

## Bottom line

- This is the landing page of CENIC's community report for 2024 to 2026. It gives a short passage from the President's letter and a link to the PDF.
- The report itself is [CENIC 2024-2026 Community Report PDF (web)](CENIC%202024-2026%20Community%20Report%20PDF%20%28web%29.md).

## Background

CENIC operates the California Research and Education Network (CalREN). The page is in the Publications part of CENIC's website. Under the report link, it has a link to the "30@30" anniversary video playlist and links to five recent blog posts.

## Key claims

- "As we kick off CENIC’s 30th anniversary year, this community report gives us a wonderful chance to look back—and more importantly, look forward." (introduction, signed Louis Fox)
- "A strong legacy isn’t a place to rest; it’s the launching pad for our future." (introduction)
- "Thank you for being the driving force behind everything we do. This community report is a celebration of your hard work." (introduction)
- "Download the report (PDF)" (link to https://assets.cenic.org/publications/Final-Community-Report-9-25-1026_med.pdf)

## Evidence and method

A short web page from the publisher, about 150 words of body text. It gives no figures. The blog post titles name CENIC AIR, Pacific Wave and the distributed "Bring-Your-Own-Resources" model; the library has not read those posts.

## Limits

The page has no content of its own beyond the letter passage and the links. For the report's figures, governance and programs, read the PDF.
