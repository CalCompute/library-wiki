# CSULB High Performance Computing article (web)

## Citation

California State University, Long Beach (CSULB), Division of Information Technology (DoIT). "High Performance Computing (HPC)." Knowledge Base article 60914, no date. https://csulb.teamdynamix.com/TDClient/KB/ArticleDet?ID=60914. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 CSULB High Performance Computing article.md`.

## Bottom line

- CSULB runs a Central High Performance Computing (HPC) environment in its campus data center for faculty and principal investigators (PIs).
- It uses a condo model: faculty who buy nodes get priority on them and can use idle resources. The HPC2 cluster has 1,664 cores on 36 nodes and 20 GPUs.

## Background

DoIT publishes this article in the CSULB help portal. It describes the cluster, the users, the support hours and an HPC Steering Committee of faculty. It names six participating condo owners.

## Key claims

The service (section "About the Central HPC"):

- "The Central HPC features a robust computing configuration, Tier 3 data center environment, and professional system administration for Faculty and Principal Investigator (PI) research."
- "Unlike legacy practices, individual computing environments paid for by Faculty or PI and housed locally in unauthorized locations, the Central HPC is purchased and maintained in the campus data center and managed by DoIT technical staff."
- "While this system is available for all Faculty and PI research, Faculty or PIs who invest in the HPC will receive priority to resources they purchased in addition to idle resources that may be available. This is what's known as an Institution/Condo Model."

Hardware (section "CSULB HPC Configuration"):

- "Cores – 1664 distributed over 36 nodes"; "GPU Nodes – 5 (total of 20 GPUs)"; "Total RAM – 19 TB".

Users and support (sections "Target Users", "Getting Started" and "Support"):

- "Research Faculty, Principal Investigators (PI)"
- "To request service, please review the article regarding the Institution/Condo Model to decide on how you'd like to participate."
- "Assistance with formulating computation, code support, or using applications/software is not available from DoIT. We suggest peer support from other Condo Owners."

Governance (section "HPC Steering Committee"):

- The committee has seven faculty and associate deans. It "Meets each semester, with the option to meet more if needed."

## Evidence and method

This is a help article. It gives the hardware configuration, the access model and names. It gives no usage or cost data.

## Limits

- The article does not give the price of a condo share. The linked Condo Model and Service Level Agreement articles may give it. This library has not read them.
- It does not say if students can use the cluster.
- It has no date.
