# NY Economic Development Law 361 Empire AI (web)

## Citation

New York Legislature. N.Y. Economic Development Law § 361, "New York state empire artificial intelligence research program." Text as shown by Public.Law, last modified May 3, 2024, verified by Public.Law Nov. 10, 2026. https://newyork.public.law/laws/n.y._economic_development_law_section_361. Public.Law gives its source as https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/COM/361. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Section 361 sets up the Empire AI research institute, "a state-owned research and computing facility" at the State University of New York at Buffalo.
- The Empire AI consortium, a not-for-profit corporation, operates and manages the institute.
- The section also requires a project labor agreement for construction and sets energy and environmental duties.

## Background

Section 361 is in Article 18* of the New York Economic Development Law, on the Division of Science, Technology and Innovation. Public.Law is a private website that shows state law text. It links to the New York State Senate's copy as the original source. The section has four subdivisions: definitions, the institute, labor standards and energy efficiency.

## Key claims

- Consortium: "“Empire AI consortium” or “the consortium” shall be the not-for-profit corporation created to construct and manage the institute." (subd. 1(b))
- Institute: "A state-owned research and computing facility at the state university of New York at Buffalo shall be established, to be known as the empire AI research institute, to promote responsible research and development to advance the ethical and public interest uses of artificial intelligence technology in the state." (subd. 2)
- Operator: "The institute shall be operated and managed by the consortium." (subd. 2)
- Construction: "Construction of the institute shall be completed by the university at Buffalo, its affiliates or related entities at the direction of the consortium, or the consortium." (subd. 2)
- Labor: any construction project under the section, or with state money for its purposes, "shall require the use of a project labor agreement" for all contractors and subcontractors. (subd. 3)
- Energy: the division, with the urban development corporation and the consortium, shall work with state energy and environment bodies "to ensure a reliable and sufficient clean energy supply for the institute". (subd. 4)

## Evidence and method

This is the text of a state statute, as a private law website shows it. It defines the bodies and gives their duties. It has no figures and no findings.

## Limits

- The section does not name the members of the consortium, its board or how members join.
- It does not give an appropriation or a dollar amount.
- It does not give access rules for researchers.
- Public.Law is not the official publisher. The official text is at the New York State Senate address in the Citation.
