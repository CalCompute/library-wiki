# Cryst World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era (web)

## Citation

Daniel Zhang, Russell Wald, Ehsan Adeli, Elena Cryst, Daniel E. Ho, Caroline Meinhardt, Jiajun Wu, Amy Zegart and Fei-Fei Li. "The World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era: Governing AI Beyond Language." Issue brief, Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI), July 27, 2026. https://hai.stanford.edu/policy/the-world-model-and-spatial-intelligence-era-governing-ai-beyond-language. Read 2026-11-06.

## Bottom line

- A Stanford HAI issue brief of July 27, 2026, by nine authors, with Elena Cryst among them.
- It sets out three governance priorities for world models: broad access, safeguards matched to use, and public capacity to evaluate the systems.

## Background

Stanford HAI publishes this brief on its policy pages. The web page gives the abstract, the key takeaways and the introduction, and links the full paper as a PDF. This is a profile page. Elena Cryst's entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "This brief highlights the emergence of world models and outlines a first-of-its-kind governance and policy agenda for the technology." (abstract)
- "No existing benchmark gives policymakers an adequate basis to evaluate a world model for safety-critical deployment. Closing that gap requires public investment in measurement science." (Key Takeaways)
- "Public datasets should be an explicit target of federally funded research." (Key Takeaways)
- "This brief sets out three governance priorities: broad access to the technology, safeguards matched to how and where a system is used, and the public capacity to evaluate these systems independently." (Introduction)
