# IRS EO BMF extract page (web)

## Citation

Internal Revenue Service (IRS). "Exempt Organizations Business Master File Extract (EO BMF)." Web page, last reviewed or updated 15-Oct-2026. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/exempt-organizations-business-master-file-extract-eo-bmf. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 IRS EO BMF extract page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20IRS%20EO%20BMF%20extract%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The IRS publishes the Exempt Organizations Business Master File Extract (EO BMF) as CSV files, one for each state and four for regions. California has its own file.
- The posting of 10/13/2026 has a record count of 1,975,975. The state of a record is the filing address, which "may or may not represent" where an organization operates.

## Background

The EO BMF is the IRS's public extract of data on tax-exempt organizations from its Business Master File. This page is the download page. It links to the field layout, [IRS EO BMF field layout (web)](IRS%20EO%20BMF%20field%20layout%20%28web%29.md), and to [IRS TEOS bulk data downloads (web)](IRS%20TEOS%20bulk%20data%20downloads%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The Exempt Organizations Business Master File Extract provides information about an organization from the Internal Revenue Service's Business Master File. This is a cumulative file, and the data are the most recent information the IRS has for these organizations." (introduction)
- "States and regions are determined from the filing address and represent the location of an organization's headquarters, which may or may not represent the state(s) in which an organization has operations." (introduction)
- "Updated data posting date: **10/13/2026**" and "Record count: **1,975,975**" (Exempt organizations by state and region)
- Region 3 is "Gulf Coast and Pacific Coast areas (CSV)", which "Includes" California among 24 states. (CSV files by region)

## Evidence and method

An official download page for a government dataset. It states the file format, the sort order, the geography rule, the posting date and the record count. The library did not download the CSV files.

## Limits

The page gives no count for California alone. It does not explain the codes; the field layout does. The library has not read the data files.
