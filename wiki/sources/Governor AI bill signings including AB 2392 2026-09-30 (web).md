## Bottom line

- On Sept. 30, 2026, the Governor signed 13 AI bills, among them AB 2392 on GenAI training and procurement in public higher education. He also signed Executive Order N-10-26 on the term "Artificial Intelligence".
- The bills cover workplace AI and surveillance, AI in health care, AI transparency and watermarks, digital replicas and gene synthesis.

## Background

The release lists the bills signed that day with their authors and subjects. It groups them under workers, doctors, transparency, exploitation and safety. It then lists the Governor's earlier AI actions, among them SB 53, SB 813, AB 1405 and Executive Order N-9-26.

## Key claims

- "Requiring California public higher education institutions to create training for students, faculty, and staff on AI training and procurement." (Protecting workers)
- "AB 2392 by Assemblymember Mike Fong (D-Alhambra) — Public postsecondary education: generative artificial intelligence systems: procurement standards: training." (list of bills signed)
- "Ensuring real people review automated employment actions by prohibiting employers from only relying on AI when making a disciplinary action or termination decision." (Protecting workers)
- "Providing more transparency by requiring employers to provide if a mass layoff, relocation, or termination is caused by an AI system." (Protecting workers)
- "In 2025, Governor Newsom signed SB 53, the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act, the nation’s first AI safety law. It requires frontier AI developers to publicly disclose safety frameworks, report specified critical safety incidents to the state, and protect whistleblowers who report serious risks." (California's nation-leading framework)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It names bills and gives one-line summaries, without bill text. It quotes the Governor and the California Federation of Labor Unions.

## Limits

The release does not name CalCompute or the consortium, and its summary of SB 53 leaves out § 11546.8. It gives no bill text or chapter numbers.
