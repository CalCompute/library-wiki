# Esterling TECD lab homepage

## Citation

Laboratory for Technology, Communication and Democracy (TeCD-Lab), UC Riverside. "The Laboratory for Technology, Communication and Democracy (TeCD-Lab)." Website homepage, publication date unknown. https://tecd-lab.ucr.edu/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Kevin Michael Esterling's lab at UC Riverside, the TeCD-Lab, started in 2019. It builds "communication technology that makes democracy more robust, responsive and resilient."
- Its main project is Prytaneum, an open source webinar platform for large public meetings.

## Background

The homepage describes the lab, its main project and news items. The lab joins the UCR School of Public Policy and the UCR Department of Computer Science and Engineering.

## Key claims

- "Founded in 2019, the TeCD-Lab aims to design and develop communication technology that makes democracy more robust, responsive and resilient." (top)
- Prytaneum is "an open source webinar platform designed to make synchronous, speaker-led meetings more constructive, more inclusive, and more informative." (Prytaneum section)
- Prytaneum "uniquely solves the problem of "many-to-one" communication, which occurs whenever a speaker wishes to understand the perspectives an audience during a synchronous meeting." (top)
- News lists the Nature article "Post-January 6th deplatforming reduced the reach of misinformation on Twitter" and three Brookings TechTank pieces on technology in Congress, 2020. (News)

## Evidence and method

A lab homepage. It describes the lab's own work.

## Limits

- The page does not say where Prytaneum is in use. The expertise map says it was "piloted with a California commission". This page does not confirm that.
