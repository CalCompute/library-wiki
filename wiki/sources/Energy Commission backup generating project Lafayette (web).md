# Energy Commission backup generating project Lafayette (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Lafayette Backup Generating Facility." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". ## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 99.8 megawatts (MW) for the Lafayette Data Center. Its status is "Exempted".
- The data center is designed for 66 MW of power to information technology (IT) equipment. Silicon Valley Power supplies its normal power, and 44 diesel generators give backup.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. The docket number is 20-SPPE-02 and the capacity is 99.8 MW. The page names no project owner and has no location field. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The Lafayette Data Center project (LDC) would include demolition of existing improvements on a 13.04-acre site to construct a three-story 576,120 square foot data center building, utility substation, generator equipment yard, surface parking, and landscaping." (Project Description)
- "The data center building will house computer servers for private clients in a secure and environmentally controlled structure and would be designed to provide 66 megawatts (MW) of power to information technology (Critical IT) equipment." (Project Description)
- "Power to run the LDC would be provided by Silicon Valley Power." (Project Description)
- "The LBGF would consist of 44 diesel-fired backup generators arranged in a generation yard located on the south side of the LDC." (Project Description)
- "Order Certifying the Final Environmental Impact Report and Granting the Small Powerplant Exemption" (Key Dates, 11/9/2023)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)
