# Packard Foundation history (web)

## Citation

David and Lucile Packard Foundation. "Our History." Web page with a 60-year timeline, date not stated. https://www.packard.org/about/our-history/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- David and Lucile Packard set up the foundation in 1964. David Packard was one of the founders of Hewlett-Packard.
- The timeline lists its programs from 1964 to 2024. They include the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute (1987) and the Packard Fellowships for Science and Engineering (1988).
- In 2024 the foundation set a minimum indirect cost rate of 15 to 25 percent on all project grants.

## Background

The foundation's history page has a short history and a timeline of its programs and grants for its 60th year. The family's children and grandchildren take part in its work with trustees and staff.

## Key claims

- "They formalized their passion for philanthropy in 1964 when they established the David and Lucile Packard Foundation." (Our History)
- "In 1971, the Foundation has five defined areas of grantmaking: Conservation and Ecology; Cultural; Education; Health; and Youth and Minority." (1971)
- "The Foundation creates the Monterey Bay Aquarium Research Institute, a leading center for research in ocean science." (1987)
- "The Packard Fellowships for Science and Engineering is created to support the nation’s most promising early-career scientists and engineers with flexible funding to take risks and explore new frontiers in their fields of study." (1988)
- "Over ten years, the Packard Foundation invests $20.6 million in colleges established by Native American Tribes, supporting science, mathematics, and technology education at these schools." (1994)
- "the Foundation updates its approach to provide a minimum indirect cost rate of 15-25% on all project grants, allowing organizations to adequately pursue their critical, mission-driven work." (2024)

## Evidence and method

A timeline written by the foundation, from 1964 to 2024. Most entries give a program and some give an amount.

## Limits

The page gives no current grant total and no assets. It does not describe an AI or compute program. The timeline is not in strict date order: the 1979 entry comes before two 1977 entries.
