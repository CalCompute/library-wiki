# CSU CFA Unit 3 contract page (web)

## Citation

California State University, Labor and Employee Relations. "Bargaining Agreement: Unit 3." Web page, no date. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Pages/unit3-cfa.aspx. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The California State University (CSU) lists the articles of its contract with the California Faculty Association (CFA) for Unit 3. The page gives the term "February 3, 2022 – June 30, 2025", "(as modified on March 4, 2024)".
- It links the complete agreement as the "Current Unit 3 CBA", 41 articles, appendices A to H, three memoranda of understanding and a grievance form.

## Background

The page is one of CSU's Labor and Employee Relations pages for each bargaining unit. The library holds the complete agreement as [CSU CFA Unit 3 contract 2022-2026 (web)](CSU%20CFA%20Unit%203%20contract%202022-2026%20%28web%29.md). The page also links the previous agreement for 2022 to 2024.

## Key claims

- "California Faculty Association (CFA)"
- "February 3, 2022 – June 30, 2025" and "(as modified on March 4, 2024)"
- "Download Current Unit 3 CBA"
- "Article 25: Professional Development" and "Article 39: Intellectual Property Rights" (list of articles)
- "There are no items to show in this view of the “Updates and Notices” list." (Bargaining Updates)

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page gives no date of its last update.
- It shows no bargaining update and gives no term after June 30, 2025.
