# UCSF Wynton HPC home (web)

## Citation

UCSF Wynton HPC. "Wynton HPC - UCSF Research Computing at Scale." Wynton HPC website, date not stated (news items to 2026-10-22). https://wynton.ucsf.edu/hpc/. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 UCSF Wynton HPC home.md`.

## Bottom line

- Wynton HPC is the shared high performance computing (HPC) cluster of UC San Francisco (UCSF). Campus IT and research groups fund and run it together, and it is free to all UCSF researchers.
- Groups that contribute hardware get higher priority than groups that do not, under a fair-share "co-op" model.
- UCSF is retiring Wynton. It takes no new labs from Oct. 22, 2026, the last day to submit jobs is Jan. 31, 2027, and it retires at the end of February 2027.

## Background

The Wynton HPC team publishes this home page. It describes the cluster and its co-op model, and lists news items from 2025 and 2026. It says that UCSF moves research to the Facility for Advanced Computing (FAC) and CoreHPC. It links to a UCSF transition page that this library does not hold.

## Key claims

- "Funded and administered cooperatively by UCSF campus IT and key research groups, it is available to all UCSF researchers" (Wynton HPC - UCSF Research Computing at Scale)
- "Each contributing member brings more resources and compute power for everyone based on a fair-share model where contributors get higher access priority than non-contributing members." (Wynton HPC - UCSF Research Computing at Scale)
- "The Wynton HPC environment is available for free to all UCSF researchers." (Wynton HPC - UCSF Research Computing at Scale)
- "Please note that the Wynton HPC service is being phased out as part of the Wynton Transition project and is scheduled to retire in end of February, 2027. The last day to submit jobs is January 31, 2027" (Banner)
- "2026-10-22: Wynton no longer accepts new labs, and from October 31 no accounts are created for new members of existing labs." (News)
- "2026-01-30: Protected (P3 and P4) data and accounts have been decommissioned. Only Wynton Regular (P1-P2) accounts can use the cluster from now on." (News)
- "Wynton HPC is being gradually phased out to align with UCSF’s evolving research infrastructure strategy." (Wynton's Future)

## Evidence and method

A program home page of about 800 words, most of it site navigation. It states the access rules, the co-op model and the dates of the retirement. It gives no size of the cluster, no costs and no count of users. For those figures, see [UCSF Wynton cluster specifications (web)](UCSF%20Wynton%20cluster%20specifications%20%28web%29.md).

## Limits

The page does not say why UCSF retires Wynton, beyond its research infrastructure strategy. It does not say what FAC and CoreHPC offer or cost. One news item has the date "2025-09-31", which does not exist. The page still calls Wynton "available to all UCSF researchers", although its news item of Oct. 22, 2026, says that it accepts no new labs.
