## Bottom line

- Sarvani Chadalapaka is the principal investigator (PI) of NSF award 2346744, CENVAL-ARC, at UC Merced. The award is $1,000,000, from June 1, 2024 to May 31, 2027.
- CENVAL-ARC is a regional computing hub for the Central Valley. UC Merced leads it with three California State University (CSU) campuses.
- It is a working California model of a UC campus that shares compute with CSU campuses.

## Key claims

- PI: Sarvani Chadalapaka. Co-PIs: Anna Klimaszewski-Patterson (CSU Sacramento), Suzanne S Sindi and Emily Jane McTavish (UC Merced). (record, coPDPI)
- Amount: "FY 2024 = $1,000,000.00". Start June 1, 2024. End May 31, 2027. (record)
- "The project is led by the University of California, Merced (UCM), in collaboration with California State Universities (CSUs) - CSU Sacramento, CSU Stanislaus, and CSU Fresno." (abstract)
- The initiative serves "the historically underserved Central Valley region of California" and "Hispanic-Serving Central Valley institutions". (abstract)
- "The hardware seamlessly integrates into UCM's existing Pinnacles High Performance Computing (HPC) cluster." It has GPU and CPU nodes, a Science DMZ, RedHat Linux and the Slurm scheduler. (abstract)
- "The project supports yearly research symposia that engage dozens of regional participants". (abstract)
