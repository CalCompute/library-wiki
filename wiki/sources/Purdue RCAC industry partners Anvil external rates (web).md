# Purdue RCAC industry partners Anvil external rates (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). "Industry Partners." RCAC website, no date. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/industry. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Purdue RCAC industry partners Anvil external rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- RCAC sells time on its Anvil supercomputer to companies in four packages, from $500 to $25,000. Each package is a year of service with a free 30-day trial.
- Extra GPU time costs $1.76 a GPU hour. Persistent storage costs $73 a terabyte (TB) a year.
- RCAC says that all external rates include Facilities and Administration (F&A) overhead costs.

## Background

RCAC is Purdue University's research computing center in West Lafayette, Indiana. This is its page for industry partners: companies that use its computers, sponsor research or sponsor events. It gives the external rates of Anvil, a system that the National Science Foundation (NSF) funds ([Purdue RCAC Anvil (web)](Purdue%20RCAC%20Anvil%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Our industry partners include users of our computing resources, benefactors for sponsored research projects, and corporate sponsors for our events and programs." (Industry Partners)
- "| Upfront Allocation | Up to 100k CPU hours or 295 GPU hours | Up to 200k CPU hours or 591 GPU hours | Up to 1.01M CPU hours or 2,988 GPU hours | Up to 5.25M CPU hours or 15,532 GPU hours |" (Pricing table: Nano, Micro, Startup and Enterprise packages)
- "| Package Cost | $500 | $1,000 | $5,000 | $25,000 |" and "| Effective Discount | 3% off spot rate | 4% off spot rate | 5% off spot rate | 9% off spot rate |" (Pricing table)
- "**GPU:** $1.76 per GPU hour" (Pricing, rate schedule for additional resources)
- "**Data Storage** (up to 200 TB Scratch, 1 TB Project)" and "1-year Service Agreement" (Pricing, included in all packages)
- "All external rates include Facilities & Administration (F&A) overhead costs." (Pricing)

## Evidence and method

The operator's own price list. It gives package prices and one hourly GPU rate. It gives no method for the rates.

## Limits

- The page says that it offers "two convenient packages", but its table gives four.
- It does not give the "spot rate" that the discounts refer to, or the model of the GPU.
- It does not say how many hours of Anvil RCAC can sell to companies, or how industry use fits within the NSF award.
