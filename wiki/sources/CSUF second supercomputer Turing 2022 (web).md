## Bottom line

- CSUF's College of Natural Sciences and Mathematics opened its second HPC cluster, "Turing", in 2022. It is part of the Center for Computational and Applied Mathematics (CCAM).
- The first cluster, "Kepler", opened in 2015. A federal grant paid for the expansion.

## Background

This is a campus news story about the opening of a research computing cluster at CSUF, a California State University (CSU) campus. It quotes the director of CCAM and the dean of the college.

## Key claims

The cluster (opening paragraphs):

- "The new high-performance computing cluster, nicknamed "Turing" after English mathematician Alan Turing, will be part of the Center for Computational and Applied Mathematics."
- "CSUF's first high-performance computing cluster, named "Kepler," opened in 2015, but in the name of advancement and new technology, Sam Behseta, professor of mathematics and director of CCAM, said that Turing is a crucial upgrade."

Students (middle of story):

- Behseta: "The new cluster will provide a historic opportunity for our students to directly participate in cutting-edge computational research programs of the faculty affiliated with CCAM"

Funding (closing paragraphs):

- Marie Johnson, dean: "The federal grant that Dr. Behseta and his team secured will significantly expand the high-performance computing resources of our campus facilitating the high level research of our faculty members and current and future students."
