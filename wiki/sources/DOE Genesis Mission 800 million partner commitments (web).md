# DOE Genesis Mission 800 million partner commitments (web)

## Citation

U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), Office of the Under Secretary for Science. "U.S. Department of Energy Announces More Than $800 Million in Partner Commitments to the Genesis Mission." Press release, July 22, 2026. https://www.energy.gov/undersecretaryforscience/articles/us-department-energy-announces-more-800-million-partner. ## Bottom line

- DOE reports "more than $800 million in committed partner support" to the Genesis Mission through the Genesis Mission Consortium.
- The commitments include compute resources and credits, model access, cloud infrastructure, expertise and funding. The Genesis Mission Consortium has all 17 DOE National Laboratories, five NNSA plants and sites, and 41 other organizations.

## Background

DOE announced the commitments at the first Genesis Mission Summit, on the day it announced the first selected projects. The President launched the Genesis Mission by executive order in November 2025. The Under Secretary for Science is the Genesis Mission Director.

## Key claims

- "the Genesis Mission has secured more than $800 million in committed partner support through the Genesis Mission Consortium." (first paragraph)
- "The reported commitments by consortium members include compute resources and credits, access to foundational AI models, cloud infrastructure, scientific expertise, research partnerships, and direct funding." (second paragraph)
- "The Genesis Mission aims to integrate artificial intelligence into transformative scientific discovery, with the goal of doubling the productivity and impact of American science and engineering within a decade." (fifth paragraph)
- "The Genesis Mission Consortium serves as the central mechanism for engaging organizations committed to advancing that vision." (fifth paragraph)
- "The consortium currently includes all 17 DOE National Laboratories, five NNSA plants and sites, and 41 industry, nonprofit, and philanthropic organizations." (sixth paragraph)
- "DOE is now expanding engagement to academic institutions to further strengthen the mission with additional research capacity, capabilities, and talent." (sixth paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A federal press release. It gives one total for the commitments and the count of members of the Genesis Mission Consortium. It does not list the members or the amount from each.

## Limits

The release does not name the partners or say how much each committed. It does not say how much of the $800 million is compute, credits or cash. [White House science initiatives fact sheet 2026-10 (web)](White%20House%20science%20initiatives%20fact%20sheet%202026-10%20%28web%29.md) later reports "$2.4B" from "eleven industry partners", with amounts for each.
