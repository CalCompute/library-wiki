# CSU CFA Unit 3 contract extension to 2026-07-31 (web)

## Citation

The California State University and the California Faculty Association. "Memorandum of Understanding: CFA/CSU Contract Extension." Signature dates 6/30/26 and 06/30/2026. California State University. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Documents/unit3-cfa/cfa-cba-ext-07-31-2026.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- A one-page memorandum extends the Unit 3 faculty agreement of the California State University (CSU) and the California Faculty Association (CFA) to July 31, 2026.
- The extension includes "all supplemental Memoranda of Understanding contained in Appendix H".

## Background

Unit 3 is CSU's faculty. The agreement that this memorandum extends is [CSU CFA Unit 3 contract 2022-2026 (web)](CSU%20CFA%20Unit%203%20contract%202022-2026%20%28web%29.md). The memorandum has signature lines for CFA's Director of Representation and Bargaining and for CSU's Associate Vice Chancellor of Labor Relations and Collective Bargaining. Both sides signed on June 30, 2026.

## Key claims

- "The California State University (CSU) and the California Faculty Association (CFA) hereby agree as follows:"
- "The Collective Bargaining Agreement between the Parties covering employees in Bargaining Unit 3, which is scheduled to expire on June 30, 2026, including all supplemental Memoranda of Understanding contained in Appendix H, shall be extended up to and including July 31, 2026."
- "CFA/CSU Contract Extension" (title)

## Evidence and method

A short signed agreement between a public employer and a union. It changes only the end date. The library's Markdown copy comes from a text layer made by optical character recognition (OCR) of the scanned page. The signature names and titles in that copy are partly unreadable.
