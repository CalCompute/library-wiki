# CASC member directory (web)

## Citation

Coalition for Academic Scientific Computation (CASC). "Our Members." Member directory web page, date not stated. https://casc.org/members/. Read 2026-11-10.

## Bottom line

- CASC lists 113 member organizations: university research computing centers, national laboratories, supercomputing centers and research institutes.
- 10 members are in California. Six are University of California (UC) campuses. The others are Stanford University, the University of Southern California (USC), Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory and the Chan Zuckerberg Biohub.

## Background

CASC is a membership organization of academic research computing centers. Its address is in Washington, DC. The directory gives each member's name and city. The counts on this page are the library's counts of the directory entries.

## Key claims

- "At CASC, we are collectively advancing the frontiers of science and technology." (page heading "Our Members")
- "University of California, San Diego San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC)", "San Diego, CA". (directory entry)
- "University of California, Berkeley Berkeley Research Computing", "Berkeley, CA". (directory entry)
- "University of California, Merced Cyberinfrastructure and Research Technologies", "Merced, CA". (directory entry)
- "Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC)", "Columbus, OH". (directory entry)

## Evidence and method

A membership list on the organization's own site. It gives names and cities only.

The California members, as the directory names them:

- Chan Zuckerberg Biohub CZ BioHub Scientific Computing, San Francisco
- Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Berkeley
- Stanford University, Stanford
- University of California, Berkeley Berkeley Research Computing
- University of California, Davis HPC Core Facility
- University of California, Los Angeles Institute for Digital Research and Education
- University of California, Merced Cyberinfrastructure and Research Technologies
- University of California, San Diego San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC)
- University of California, San Francisco Academic Research Services
- University of Southern California Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC), Los Angeles

## Limits

The directory gives no member's size, budget, systems or date of joining. It does not give CASC's governance or the cost of membership; those are on other CASC pages, which the library has not read. No California State University campus is in the list.
