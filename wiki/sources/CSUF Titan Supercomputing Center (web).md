## Citation

## Bottom line

- The Titan Supercomputing Center (TSC) is the high-performance computing (HPC) center of CSUF, a California State University (CSU) campus. Its motto is "Empowering Innovation Through Computation."
- The page gives the center's vision, its mission and four pillars: Community, Research, Education and Workforce (CREW). It links to an Access Request Form.
- It gives no hardware, rates or user rules.

## Key claims

Purpose (opening section):

- "We empower student and faculty to push the boundaries of what’s possible through computation."

Vision ("Our Vision"):

- "To build a vibrant computational center that serves as a collaborative space for all, where knowledge is shared, educational innovation thrives, and real-world research drives solutions."

Mission ("Our Mission"):

- "The center is committed to providing high-performance computing infrastructure for creating a dynamic and inclusive environment that unites our community, drives innovative research, fosters computational education, and prepares a skilled workforce for the Southern California region by fostering student organizations, faculty engagement, industry partnerships, and institutional collaborations to inspire growth and collaboration."

Pillars ("CREW: Community, Research, Education, Workforce"):

- Education: "Bringing high-performance computing into the classroom and supporting next-gen learning."
- Workforce: "Preparing students with the skills, experiences, and connections to succeed in industry and academia."

## Evidence and method
