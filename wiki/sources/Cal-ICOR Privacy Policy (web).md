# Cal-ICOR Privacy Policy (web)

## Citation

Cal-ICOR team. "Cal-ICOR Privacy Policy." Cal-ICOR Documentation, docs.cal-icor.org, date not stated. https://docs.cal-icor.org/privacy. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- Cal-ICOR says it does not keep personally identifiable information about its partners or users. Its staff have access to each partner's hub for maintenance.
- When a partnership ends, Cal-ICOR deletes all user data, accounts and cloud infrastructure within 30 days. The institution can ask for a transfer to a new partner or cloud provider first.

## Background

Cal-ICOR is a JupyterHub teaching service led by UC Berkeley. The page is one of two user policies in its documentation, with the [Cal-ICOR Acceptable Use Policy (web)](Cal-ICOR%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md). See [Cal-ICOR](../entities/Cal-ICOR.md).

## Key claims

- "Cal-ICOR does not store or retain any personally identifiable information about its partners or users, or the information that is created or placed on the Infrastructure we run." (Summary)
- "This access and information will be deleted within 30 days of the end of the partnership." (Summary)
- "Cal-ICOR will require access to the Cal-ICOR Managed Infrastructure of a partnered Institution, including but not limited to all filesystems and servers for general maintenance and debugging." (While partnered with an Institution)
- "Some data will be consumed by our monitoring and metrics for the purpose of alerting staff to potential issues with a deployment as well as usage statistics." (While partnered with an Institution)
- "If the Institution has a new partner or Cloud Provider, the data can potentially be transferred to the new partner or Cloud Provider." (After a partnership with an Institution ends)

## Evidence and method

A privacy policy of about 640 words, from the program's own documentation. It states practices. It cites no audit or law.

## Limits

The policy does not name a law, such as FERPA, that it follows. It does not say which metrics Cal-ICOR collects or how long it keeps them. It gives no date or version.
