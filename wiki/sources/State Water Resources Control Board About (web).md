## Bottom line

- The State Water Resources Control Board (State Water Board) and nine Regional Water Quality Control Boards together are the California Water Boards. They act under the federal Clean Water Act and the state Porter-Cologne Water Quality Control Act.
- The State Water Board is "one of six environmental entities operating under the authority of the California Environmental Protection Agency". The page names a five-member board with a Chair and Vice Chair.

## Background

An about page for a state regulatory board. It gives the mission, vision, values, the board members, the relation to the regional boards, and the difference from the Department of Water Resources. It is an example of a state board with regional boards under a cabinet agency.

## Key claims

- "The State Water Resources Control Board (State Water Board) and the nine Regional Water Quality Control Boards (Regional Water Boards), collectively known as the California Water Boards (Water Boards), are dedicated to a single vision: abundant clean water for human uses and environmental protection to sustain California's future." (Principles & Values)
- "Under the federal Clean Water Act (CWA) and the state's pioneering Porter-Cologne Water Quality Control Act, the State and Regional Water Boards have regulatory responsibility for protecting the water quality of nearly 1.6 million acres of lakes, 1.3 million acres of bays and estuaries, 211,000 miles of rivers and streams, and about 1,100 miles of exquisite California coastline." (Principles & Values)
- "The State Water Resources Control Board (State Water Board) and the nine Regional Water Quality Control Boards (Regional Boards) protects water quality and allocates surface water rights. The Department of Water Resources is responsible for the management of water usage including the delivery of water to two-thirds of California's population through the State Water Project." (Water Boards or Water Resources?)
- "The State Water Board is one of six environmental entities operating under the authority of the California Environmental Protection Agency" (footer)
- Board: "Board Members Jared Blumenfeld and Nichole Morgan, Board Chair E. Joaquin Esquivel, Vice Chair Dorene D'Adamo, and Board Member Sean Maguire" (photo caption)

## Evidence and method

A state agency's own page. It cites the two statutes by name but not by section.

## Limits

The page does not say who appoints board members, their terms, or the board's budget. It is not about computing.
