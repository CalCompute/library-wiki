# UCSC CruzCon 2026 event page (web)

## Citation

Lieggi, Stephanie. "CruzCon: Open Source for Research & Innovation." ## Bottom line

- CruzCon was a one-day event on open source in research and innovation at the Seymour Center, UC Santa Cruz, on Oct. 22, 2026.
- The UCSC OSPO, the Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) and CITRIS and the Banatao Institute hosted it. The program had two panels, two keynotes and a directed discussion on "Institutional Infrastructure, Research & OSPOs".

## Background

The UCSC OSPO publishes the page. Stephanie Lieggi, Executive Director of the OSPO and of CROSS, is its author. The page gives the program, the logistics and a link to slides. It says a final report is "coming soon".

## Key claims

Purpose (top of page):

- "CruzCon brings together UC researchers, faculty, students, campus leadership, and industry partners for a day exploring open source in research and innovation — hosted by the UCSC OSPO, Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) and CITRIS and the Banatao Institute at the Seymour Center, UC Santa Cruz."
- "THANKS TO EVERYONE FOR PARTICIPATING – FINAL REPORT COMING SOON"

Program (section "Event Program"):

- "Panel 1: Open Source Ecosystems as a Pathway to University Innovation"
- "Keynote - Research software engineering and research software engineers: Successes and challenges. Daniel S. Katz, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign"
- "Panel 2: Campus Infrastructure Supporting Open Source and Research Impact"
- "Keynote - Everything old is new again: the deepened importance of humans learning together in the age of AI. Kirstie Whitaker, Berkeley Institute for Data Science (BIDS)"
- "Directed Discussion: Institutional Infrastructure, Research & OSPOs"

This is an event page. It gives the program and the names of speakers and moderators. It does not report what the speakers said.
