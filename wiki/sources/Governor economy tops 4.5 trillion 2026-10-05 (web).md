# Governor economy tops 4.5 trillion 2026-10-05 (web)

## Citation

Office of Governor Gavin Newsom. "California's economy continues to dominate — topping $4.5 T in economic output, and remains the world's fourth largest economy." Press release, Sacramento, October 5, 2026. https://www.gov.ca.gov/2026/10/05/californias-economy-continues-to-dominate-topping-4-5-t-in-economic-output-and-remains-the-worlds-fourth-largest-economy/. Read 2026-10-20.

## Bottom line

- This is the newest figure for the state economy: "$4.5 trillion in economic output", from Bureau of Economic Analysis (BEA) data for 2025.
- The Governor's office still calls California "the world's fourth largest economy". The release gives no international data for the rank.
- The release does not mention CalCompute or AI.

## Background

The Office of Governor [Gavin Newsom](../entities/Gov.%20Gavin%20Newsom.md) published this release after the BEA released state GDP data. The fourth rank first appears in [Governor California 4th largest economy 2025-04-23 (web)](Governor%20California%204th%20largest%20economy%202025-04-23%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- California is "the first U.S. state to surpass $4.5 trillion in economic output."
- "Since 2019, California's economy has increased by over half, at $1.58 trillion."
- "California's private sector alone has now crossed the $4 trillion mark for the first time."
- BEA data "shows that California ranks first in economic output across almost every sector in 2025, including agriculture, manufacturing, technology, entertainment, healthcare, and wholesale trade."
- "California's economy grew by nearly $300 billion in the past year alone".
- "total wages and salaries grew by 5.2%".
- California grew "3.5% after inflation" in 2025.
- California "accounted for over 40% of total U.S. computer and electronics manufacturing" in 2025.
- "Since 2021, California has invested over $215 million in over 675 contracts with employers to train over 194,000 workers in the manufacturing sector."
- California "represents only 11.5% of the U.S. population but is responsible for nearly 14% of U.S. GDP".
- "As the world's fourth-largest economy, California continues to lead the nation".
- The release lists California as the "#1 state for" new business starts, high-tech business, agriculture, workers and venture capital.

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It links the BEA release for the GDP figures. It cites a Bloomberg analysis for growth against other nations. It links an earlier Governor's release for the venture capital (VC) rank, with no amount.
