# Groome USC Viterbi CTO profile (web)

## Citation

University of Southern California (USC) Viterbi School of Engineering. "Rob Groome." Leadership profile, published August 19, 2026. https://viterbischool.usc.edu/leadership/rob-groome/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- Rob Groome is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) of the USC Viterbi School of Engineering and the USC Stevens School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence.
- In this post he leads technology strategy and operations, research computing included. He worked at USC from 2001, apart from 2021 to 2023 in private industry.

## Background

The USC Viterbi School of Engineering publishes this profile in its leadership section. It gives his work history. He worked at the USC Institute for Creative Technologies (ICT), in USC Information Technology Services and at a private artificial intelligence (AI) company. It then gives the scope of his CTO post. The page gives August 19, 2026 as its publication date and its last update.

## Key claims

- "Rob Groome is a transformational technology executive with more than 25 years of experience leading information technology, cybersecurity, and research computing at the intersection of academia, defense, and innovation." (profile)
- "He joined the USC Institute for Creative Technologies (ICT) as IT Manager in 2001, where he architected the institute's technology infrastructure and pioneered the implementation of NIST 800-171 controls for defense research contracts establishing ICT as a national model for secure academic research environments." (profile)
- "From 2018 to 2021, he was selected to lead USC's university-wide Security Operations Team within Information Technology Services" (profile)
- "In 2021, Rob brought his expertise to the private sector as Head of IT and Information Security Officer at BlueOcean, a San Francisco–based brand tech AI company" (profile)
- "Under his leadership, ICT became one of the first 366 organizations nationwide, and first University Affiliated Research Center (UARC), to earn early-adopter CMMC Level 2 certification through C3PAO assessment in October 2025" (profile)
- "He will oversee research computing, enterprise infrastructure, cybersecurity and compliance, academic and desktop support, the Distance Education Network (DEN), and the responsible adoption of emerging technologies" (profile)

## Evidence and method

A dated institutional profile, written in the third person by the school that employs him. It gives years for each post. It describes the CTO scope in the future tense ("will lead", "will oversee"), as for a new appointment.

## Limits

The page does not give his education. It does not give the size, budget or hardware of the research computing that the CTO oversees. It does not give the date he started as CTO. "NIST", "CMMC" and "C3PAO" are not written in full on the page.
