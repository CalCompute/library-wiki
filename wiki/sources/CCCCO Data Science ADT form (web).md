# CCCCO Data Science ADT form (web)

California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office. "ADT Submission Form for Data Science." ## Bottom line

- The form sets the core of a community college Associate in Science for Transfer (AS-T) in Data Science: 25 to 27 semester units.
- The core is Introduction to Data Science, a calculus sequence, multivariable calculus, linear algebra and two programming courses. The degree can have up to 66 units.

## Background

A college fills in this form to submit its Data Science Associate Degree for Transfer (ADT) to the Chancellor's Office. It maps the college's courses to the Transfer Model Curriculum (TMC) and to C-ID course descriptors. The memo that released it is [CCCCO ESS 26-71 Data Science TMC memo (web)](CCCCO%20ESS%2026-71%20Data%20Science%20TMC%20memo%20%28web%29.md). The faculty group's notes are at the end of the form.

## Key claims

- "CCC Major or Area of Emphasis: Data Science"; "CSU Majors deemed similar: Data Science"; "Degree Type: AS-T" (form header)
- "REQUIRED CORE: (25-27 units)"; "Introduction to Data Science (4 units)" with C-ID descriptor "DATA 100" (Transfer Model Curriculum table)
- "Programming Concepts and Methodology I (units 3)" "COMP 122"; "Programming Concepts and Methodology II (units 3)" "COMP 132" (Transfer Model Curriculum table)
- "General Education (Cal-GETC) Units 34"; "Total Degree Units (maximum) 60-66**" (unit totals)
- "FDRG received 86 individual feedback entries from a broadly representative group of faculty from the field. CCC’s, CSU’s, and UC’s were represented." (Notes and History)
- "FDRG decided to keep DATA 100 as a required part of the core of the degree. This is consistent with the FDRG's decision that this degree should require a commitment on behalf of the colleges to develop data science curriculum, and provide appropriate supports and professional development to students and faculty." (Notes and History)

## Evidence and method

A curriculum template with the faculty review notes. The notes give the number of feedback entries, the meeting dates (March 17 and April 6, 2026) and the votes, which "were both unanimous".

## Limits

The text copy stops in the middle of the faculty notes. The original PDF is the authority for the rest. The form does not say which colleges offer the degree. It does not link the degree to compute access or to any computing resource.
