# CSU CSUEU tentative agreement 2026-10-31 (web)

## Citation

The California State University and the California State University Employees Union. "CSU/ CSUEU Tentative Agreement." Dated 10/31/2026, with signatures dated Oct. 31 to Nov. 2, 2026. California State University. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Documents/unit2-5-7-9-csueu/CSUEU-Full-TA-Document-10-31-2026-signed.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-22. Read 2026-11-22.

## Bottom line

- The California State University (CSU) and the California State University Employees Union (CSUEU) signed this tentative agreement. It is for a successor contract for staff Units 2, 5, 7 and 9. Article 29 sets a term to June 30, 2029.
- A new memorandum of understanding (MOU) on artificial intelligence (AI) systems keeps CSU's right "to introduce, pilot, modify, or discontinue the use of AI systems". CSU meets and confers on the impacts when the union asks. The MOU expires on June 30, 2029.
- The salary article gives a General Salary Increase (GSI) of 4 percent on July 1, 2026 and on July 1, 2027. It has a reopener for 2028-29. A separate MOU gives a one-time bonus of $3,575 for fiscal year 2025/26.

## Background

The prior contract is [CSU CSUEU contract Units 2 5 7 9 2023-2026 (web)](CSU%20CSUEU%20contract%20Units%202%205%207%209%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md), with a term to June 30, 2026. A table of the 29 articles and seven MOUs gives CSU's position on each: "Status Quo", "TA" or a numbered CSU proposal. The signature pages follow, then the texts of the changed articles and of the MOUs.

The seven MOUs are on shared services, AI, classification review, job posting, temporary employees, uniforms and boots, and the 2025/26 wage and benefits reopener.

## Key claims

- "Article 3: Management Rights Status Quo" and "Article 22: Professional Development TA Status Quo" (table of articles)
- "Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems mean software, applications, or computational tools that use machine learning, statistical models, or other algorithmic techniques to support or assist tasks such as generating content, analyzing data, and recognizing patterns." (MOU, Artificial Intelligence Systems)
- "Confidential, restricted, or otherwise sensitive information may not be entered into generative AI systems in any manner that could compromise data privacy, security, or compliance obligations." (MOU, Artificial Intelligence Systems)
- "The Parties recognize that the University retains all management rights under HEERA, including the right to introduce, pilot, modify, or discontinue the use of AI systems." (MOU, Artificial Intelligence Systems)
- "the University will meet and confer upon request of the Union regarding the identifiable, foreseeable impacts of the University’s decision to implement AI technologies on matters within the scope of representation." (MOU, Artificial Intelligence Systems)
- "This Memorandum of Understanding shall expire on June 30, 2029 in recognition of the rapidly evolving nature of artificial intelligence technologies and the need for periodic reassessment." (MOU, Artificial Intelligence Systems)
- "If the CSU establishes or expands a shared services arrangement in which employees assigned to more than one campus work together to provide services to multiple campuses, the CSU and the Union shall meet regarding the impacts of the arrangement." (MOU, Shared Services)
- "For fiscal year 2026/2027, effective July 1, 2026, all bargaining unit employees in active pay status (or on leave) in the classifications set forth in Appendix F as of November 13, 2026, shall receive a GSI of four percent (4%)." (Article 20, 20.4 a)
- "all eligible CSUEU employees shall receive a single one-time bonus in the gross amount of $3,575, prorated based upon time base." (MOU, 25/26 Salary & Benefits Reopener)
- The classification review MOU lists the "Technology Support Series" among ten classifications. "The review shall commence in January 2027, and the Parties shall make reasonable efforts to complete the review by April 30, 2028." (MOU, Classification Review)
- "Pursuant to Government Code 3572(b) (HEERA) any term of this Agreement which is deemed by the Employer to carry an economic cost shall not be implemented until the Employer determines that the amount required therefore has been appropriated" (Article 29, 29.3)

## Evidence and method

A tentative agreement between a public employer and a union, signed by the bargaining teams of both sides. Article 29.1 makes the agreement effective "upon ratification by both parties". The Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations Act (HEERA) and the Public Employment Relations Board (PERB) precedent set the limits of the duty to meet and confer.

## Limits

- The document does not say whether the members or the CSU Board of Trustees ratified it.
- The Markdown copy keeps the text that the agreement strikes out. Article 29.1 reads "up to and including June 30, 20296": the old year 2026 and the new year 2029 run together. Article 20.4 keeps the old GSI lines for 2023/2024. Read the original for the marked changes.
- The AI MOU gives no rule for training, layoffs or job changes from AI. It refers to "University and Campus policies" and the CSU Information Security Policy, which this library does not hold.
- The library holds no extension for the months from June 30, 2026 to the date of this agreement.
