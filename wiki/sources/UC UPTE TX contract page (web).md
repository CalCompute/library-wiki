# UC UPTE TX contract page (web)

## Citation

University of California, UCnet. "TX — Technical: Contract." Web page, no date. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/tx/contract.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-20 UC UPTE TX contract page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-20%20UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) lists the articles of its contract with University Professional and Technical Employees (UPTE) for the Technical (TX) unit. The page gives a new contract "Effective November 1, 2025 – September 30, 2028".
- The page says that the articles it posts "do not reflect the most recent negotiated language". The posted files carry 2013-2017 or 2019-2024 in their addresses.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the TX unit's pages, under "Bargaining units, contracts and local agreements". It links one file for each of 47 articles, seven appendices, side letters and a memo of negotiators. The library holds two of the articles: [UC UPTE TX contract Article 19 Management Rights (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2019%20Management%20Rights%20%28web%29.md) and [UC UPTE TX contract Article 40 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2040%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Contract for the Technical Unit (TX) between the University of California and the University Professional and Technical Employees (UPTE)"
- "Effective November 1, 2025 – September 30, 2028"
- "New contract ratified for contract period 11/1/2025 to 9/30/2028."
- "The University and UPTE are currently reviewing and finalizing updates to the HX collective bargaining agreement articles. Until that process is complete, the articles currently posted on this website do not reflect the most recent negotiated language."
- "The draft collective bargaining agreement (CBA) is available to location labor managers."
- "Article 8 – Development and Training" and "Article 16 – Layoff and Reduction in Time" (list of articles)

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page is the TX contract page, but its notice names "the HX collective bargaining agreement articles". It does not say if the notice is for TX too.
- The page gives no date of its last update.
- The library holds only Articles 19 and 40 of the posted files. It does not hold the draft agreement for 2025 to 2028. It holds the signed memorandum of the successor agreement: [UPTE UC successor tentative agreement 2025-11-08 (web)](UPTE%20UC%20successor%20tentative%20agreement%202025-11-08%20%28web%29.md).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page gives "Effective November 1, 2025 – September 30, 2028". The [UPTE UC successor tentative agreement 2025-11-08 (web)](UPTE%20UC%20successor%20tentative%20agreement%202025-11-08%20%28web%29.md) gives "November 7, 2025 - September 30, 2028".
