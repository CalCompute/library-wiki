## Bottom line

- Kim Hazelwood describes herself as "an engineering and applied research executive" in AI and computer systems. She holds a PhD in Computer Science from Harvard.
- She served on the Computing Research Association board from 2017 to 2025.
- Her papers include "Sustainable AI" (2022) and "Applied machine learning at Facebook: A datacenter infrastructure perspective" (2018).
