# SDSC F3i Supercomputing Center release 2026-07-16 (web)

## Citation

Jarrett Haley, San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC). "Supercomputer Center launches at Fresno State." News release, July 16, 2026. https://www.sdsc.edu/news/2026/PR20260716-F3i-supercomputing-center.html. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- F3 Innovate (F3i), Fresno State and SDSC at UC San Diego launched the F3i Supercomputing Center for California's food and agriculture supply chain.
- Fresno State is the home of the center. It gives three services: shared high-performance computing, applied AI services, and workforce and talent development.

## Background

SDSC published this release on its news site. It reports a launch event on July 9, 2026. It quotes the chief executive officer of F3i, the SDSC Director and the Fresno State chief information officer. It describes the center as one part of a wider F3 Innovate strategy for the Central Valley.

## Key claims

- "F3 Innovate (F3i), Fresno State and the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at the University of California San Diego have launched the F3i Supercomputing Center, a regional innovation platform providing the computing power, technical support and talent pathways needed to accelerate innovation across California's food and agriculture supply chain." (first paragraph)
- "The center represents a first-of-its-kind partnership between industry and higher education to expand access to the advanced computing infrastructure needed to compete in an AI-driven economy." (first paragraph)
- "Instead of requiring businesses, researchers and educators to build their own computing systems, the F3i Supercomputing Center provides shared access to powerful computing resources and technical support" (body)
- "Fresno State will serve as the home of the center, expanding opportunities for applied research, workforce development and industry collaboration" (body)
- "**Affordable High-Performance Computing** The center offers shared access to advanced computing resources that enable growers, startups, students and researchers to train AI models, process large datasets and test emerging technologies without making significant infrastructure investments." (three integrated services)
- "**Workforce and Talent Development** Through F3i’s Data Challenges, AI Launchpad program with Microsoft, and other talent development programs, the center prepares students, educators, researchers and working professionals to use advanced computing and artificial intelligence in careers advancing agriculture’s next era." (three integrated services)
- "The Central Valley produces a significant share of the nation's food, supports more than 14,000 farming and food-processing operations" (body)

## Evidence and method

An institutional news release. It names the three partners, the host campus, the user groups and the three services. It gives one count: more than 14,000 farming and food-processing operations in the Central Valley. It gives no source for that count. The rest is description and quotations.

## Limits

The release does not give the hardware, the number of graphics processing units (GPUs), the cost, the funding source or an allocation process. It does not say what "Affordable" means in prices. It names an AI Launchpad program with Microsoft but does not describe it. [F3i Supercomputing Center (web)](F3i%20Supercomputing%20Center%20%28web%29.md) is F3 Innovate's own page on the center. It gives the role of each partner and the priority user groups.
