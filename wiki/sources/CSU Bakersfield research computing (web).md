# CSU Bakersfield research computing (web)

## Citation

California State University, Bakersfield (CSUB). "Research: Welcome to Research Hub." CSUB website, no date. https://www.csub.edu/research/. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 CSU Bakersfield research computing.md`.

## Bottom line

- CSUB's Research Hub sends researchers to three shared platforms: the National Research Platform (NRP), Cal-ICOR and a campus HPC cluster that is in beta.
- The campus cluster is open only to CSUB Computer Science and Mathematics students and faculty.
- CSUB is adding an HPC node to the NRP's Nautilus cluster. The node gives CSUB research priority access.

## Background

This is the research computing page of CSUB, a California State University (CSU) campus. It tells researchers how to ask the Research Support Team for help with a ServiceNow ticket. It lists training links, three campus projects on Nautilus and the services of the Grants, Research and Sponsored Programs (GRASP) office.

## Key claims

How CSUB helps ("Typical ways we help"):

- "Data management planning; connecting to shared platforms (NRP, Cal-ICOR)"
- "Right-sizing resources across NRP, Cal-ICOR, and CSUB HPC (beta)"

The campus cluster ("Resources Links"):

- "CSUB HPC Cluster (In beta) Internal access for CSUB Computer Science & Mathematics students and faculty. Accounts are required and limited to the campus community."

The platforms ("Research Resources Overview"):

- Cal-ICOR: "Free, browser-based computing for California higher-ed, supporting JupyterLab, VS Code, Shiny, and RStudio—excellent for teaching, prototyping, and reproducible workflows."
- CSUB HPC: "On-premises high-performance computing for research and classroom education, available to current CSUB students and faculty in CS and Math."

The campus node ("How CSUB is Contributing"):

- "CSUB is adding an HPC node to the Nautilus ecosystem, contributing resources for community use while providing CSUB research priority access when deploying apps through the platform."

Project examples ("Project Spotlights"):

- "Mathematics project training compact local LLMs (e.g., Mistral 7B, DeepSeek 7B) on course materials. Runs on Nautilus/Kubernetes with persistent storage."

## Evidence and method

This is a campus service page. It describes services, platforms and three projects. It gives no hardware figures, user counts, costs or dates.
