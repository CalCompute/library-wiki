# UC OSPO Network education resources

## Citation

UC OSPO Network. "Open source learning for research and teaching." Web page, no date. https://ucospo.net/education/. Read 2026-10-29.

## Bottom line

- The UC OSPO Network curates 43 free lessons on open source for researchers, developers, librarians and instructors.
- The network describes the lessons. Other organizations host them.
- Find partners who already teach, and make their material available. CalCompute does not build the course material itself.

## Background

The UC OSPO Network is the collaboration of the open source program offices of six UC campuses.

## Key claims

- "Explore 43 free lessons curated by the UC OSPO Network for researchers, developers, maintainers, project leads, librarians, and instructors." (page head)
- "43 curated resources · 14 standards-informed topics · Six UC campuses · Workshops, courses, and practical guides" (page head)
- "We curate and describe these resources — the original organizations host and maintain the lessons themselves." (page head)
- Topics include "version control, licensing and reuse, testing and automation, security and supply chains, governance, sustainability, research software". (page body)

## Evidence and method

A catalog page. It gives counts and topics. It gives no use data or outcomes.
