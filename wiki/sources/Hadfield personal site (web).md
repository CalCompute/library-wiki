# Hadfield personal site (web)

## Citation

Gillian K. Hadfield. "Gillian K. Hadfield: Building AI That Understands Human Systems." Personal website, gillianhadfield.org. Date not stated; it lists talks to February 2027. https://gillianhadfield.org/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The personal website of Gillian K. Hadfield. It gives her title as "Bloomberg Distinguished Professor of AI Alignment and Governance at Johns Hopkins University".
- It says that her research on "regulatory markets" "has helped shape the Independent Verification Organization (IVO) model". It says that state and federal bills include the IVO model.

## Background

The homepage gives her current research focus, three research areas, four selected publications, an upcoming talk and recent media coverage. Its research areas are normativity and AI alignment, cooperative AI, and AI governance.

## Key claims

- "Bloomberg Distinguished Professor of AI Alignment and Governance at Johns Hopkins University, developing the legal, regulatory, and technical infrastructure for artificial intelligence that operates within human normative frameworks." (page heading)
- "I lead a research group of computer scientists, cognitive scientists, and legal scholars at Johns Hopkins University that develops training environments and architectures that enable AI to become "normatively competent."" (Current Focuses)
- "I also develop practical, evidence-based policy proposals anchored in my foundational research on "regulatory markets"—private regulators competing to provide oversight while themselves subject to government regulation—to address the challenge of governing rapidly evolving AI systems." (Current Focuses)
- "This research has helped shape the Independent Verification Organization (IVO) model, a concrete instantiation of that approach that has been introduced in state and federal legislation." (Current Focuses)
- "Proposes regulatory markets where private regulators compete to provide oversight while being regulated by government to achieve public policy goals." (Select Publications: Regulatory markets: The future of AI governance, Jurimetrics, 2025, with Jack Clark)

## Evidence and method

A personal homepage that the person writes about her own work. It lists publications with their venues and years, such as Science (2024) and Jurimetrics (2025). It does not cite the bills that it says use the IVO model.

## Limits

The page does not name the state or federal bills on the IVO model. The library has [SB 813 bill text](SB%20813%20bill%20text.md), a California bill on independent verification organizations; the page does not name it. The page does not discuss compute resources.
