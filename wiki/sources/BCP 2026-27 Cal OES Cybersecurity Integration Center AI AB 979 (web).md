## Bottom line

- Cal OES asks for two positions and $717,000 General Fund in 2026-27 and $463,000 in 2027-28, to write the California Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook.
- AB 979 requires the playbook under Government Code section 8586.5 before January 1, 2027.
- The positions are an analyst and an Information Technology Specialist II, for two years only.

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. The California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) is in Cal OES. Four bodies staff it: Cal OES, the California Department of Technology, the California Military Department and the California Highway Patrol. The bill text is in [AB 979 AI cybersecurity bill text](AB%20979%20AI%20cybersecurity%20bill%20text.md). The Department of Technology's proposal for the same bill is [BCP 2026-27 Department of Technology Cybersecurity Integration Center AI AB 979 (web)](BCP%202026-27%20Department%20of%20Technology%20Cybersecurity%20Integration%20Center%20AI%20AB%20979%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The Office of Emergency Services requests two positions and $717,000 General Fund in FY 2026-27 and $463,000 in FY 2027-28 to create the California Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook pursuant to Government Code Section 8586.5." (Budget Request Summary)
- "Government Code Section 8586.5(g)(1) requires Cal OES, namely Cal-CSIC, to develop prior to January 1, 2027, in consultation with the Office of Information Security and the Government Operations Agency, a California Artificial Intelligence (AI) Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook to facilitate information sharing across cyber and AI communities, strengthening cyber defenses against emerging threats." (A. Problem Statement)
- "The playbook must include mandatory mechanisms for information sharing of potential threats and vulnerability known to state contractors and vendors providing AI services regarding those contracted or purchased services, to a state entity identified in the playbook." (A. Problem Statement)
- "At its current funding level, the Cal-CSIC does not have the resources to support the creation of the playbook." (B. Justification)
- "Cal-CSIC will comply with the statutory requirements and the successful completion of the playbook by January 1, 2027." (D. Outcomes and Accountability)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form, with a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. The positions are 1.0 Associate Governmental Program Analyst and 1.0 Information Technology Specialist II from July 1, 2026. The fiscal sheet gives them for 2026-27 and 2027-28 and gives no money after that. The proposal names the federal Joint Cyber Defense Collaborative AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook as a model for staff to consider.

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- The 2026-27 floor report lists $717,000 in 2026-27 and $463,000 in 2027-28 for the playbook ([Assembly Budget floor report 2026-27 budget 2026-06-27 (web)](Assembly%20Budget%20floor%20report%202026-27%20budget%202026-06-27%20%28web%29.md), p. 128).
- It says nothing about CalCompute.
- It does not give the cost of the AI training that it says staff need.
