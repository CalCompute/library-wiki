# Wiener SB-53 introduction release 2025-02-28 (web)

## Citation

Office of Senator Scott Wiener (D-San Francisco). "Senator Wiener Introduces Legislation to Protect AI Whistleblowers & Boost Responsible AI Development." Press release, San Francisco, February 28, 2025. <https://sd11.senate.ca.gov/news/senator-wiener-introduces-legislation-protect-ai-whistleblowers-boost-responsible-ai>. Read 2026-10-20.

## Bottom line

- The author's office says SB 53 "takes the first step toward establishing CalCompute". This agrees with the statute's framework approach.
- It promises "low-cost compute to researchers and start-ups". The statute sets no price and names no users.
- It compares CalCompute with New York's AI consortium and its "over $90 million in new funding".

## Background

At introduction, SB 53 had two parts: whistleblower protections and CalCompute. The Senate Governmental Organization Committee heard it on March 25, 2025 ([SB-53 Senate GO analysis 2025-03-25 (web)](SB-53%20Senate%20GO%20analysis%202025-03-25%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- **First step.** SB 53 "also takes the first step toward establishing CalCompute, a research cluster to support startups and researchers developing large-scale AI."
- **New York.** "The bill helps California secure its global leadership as states like New York establish their own AI research clusters." Governor Hochul announced "over $90 million in new funding for their state AI consortium".
- **Senator Wiener.** "SB 53 supports the development of large-scale AI systems by providing low-cost compute to researchers and start-ups through CalCompute." He adds: "this bill may evolve as the process continues."
- **Cost barrier.**
  > Developing today's advanced AI models requires the use of expensive computing resources, which can put advanced AI development out of reach for many startups and researchers. SB 53 would help rectify that problem by providing low-cost compute and other resources to aid responsible AI development.
- **Concentration.** "California is home to the world's largest concentration of leading AI companies and researchers."
- **Teri Olle, "Director of Economic Security for California Action".**
  > California has a history of making important public investments where it counts: from stem cell research to our stellar higher education system, we have led the way in using public dollars to foster the American entrepreneurial spirit [...] creating the infrastructure for a public option for cloud computing needed in AI development. If we want to see AI used to promote the public good and make life better and easier for people, then we must broaden access to the computing power required to fuel innovation.
- **Sunny Gandhi, Encode.** "With CalCompute, we're democratizing access to the computational resources that power AI innovation".
- **Process.** SB 53 works by "Establishing a process to create a public cloud-computing cluster (CalCompute) that will conduct research into the safe and secure deployment of large-scale artificial intelligence (AI) models."
- **Sponsors.** "SB 53 is sponsored by Encode, Economic Security Action CA, and Secure AI."

## Evidence and method

An advocacy statement by the author and sponsors. The New York figure links to StateScoop, a trade outlet. The release gives no CalCompute cost or size.

## Limits

- The text changed before enactment.
- The New York funding figure is second-hand.
- The release names the co-sponsor two ways: "Economic Security for California Action" and "Economic Security Action CA". Later sources use "Economic Security California Action".

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The release promises "low-cost compute to researchers and start-ups". Enacted § 11546.8 sets no price. Subd. (f)(2)(B) asks the Report for "An analysis of the cost to the state to build and maintain CalCompute and recommendations for potential funding sources", and subd. (f)(2)(D) asks for "a process for determining which users and projects will be supported" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The release says CalCompute "will conduct research". Subd. (d) describes "A fully owned and hosted cloud platform" with staff to run it and support its users ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
