# AWS Imagine Grant program (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS Imagine Grant." Program web page, date not stated; the page gives the 2026-2027 U.S. grant cycle. https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/aws-imagine-grant-program/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- AWS gives U.S. 501(c) nonprofits three grant awards: Pathfinder, Go Further, Faster, and Momentum to Modernize.
- An award gives up to $200,000 of "unrestricted financial support" and up to $100,000 in AWS Promotional Credit, with training and support.
- Educational institutions cannot apply. The page reports that AWS closed the 2026 application cycle.

## Background

AWS publishes this page for its grant program for nonprofits. The page gives the award categories, the eligibility rules, the timeline and the selection criteria. It also links to versions of the grant for the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "A public grant opportunity open to registered nonprofit organizations who are using cloud technology to accelerate their missions" (page header)
- Pathfinder award: "Up to $200,000 USD of unrestricted financial support" and "Up to $100,000 USD in AWS Promotional Credit" (The Pathfinder award)
- Go Further, Faster award: "Up to $150,000 USD of unrestricted financial support" and "Up to $100,000 USD in AWS Promotional Credit" (The Go Further, Faster award)
- Momentum to Modernize award: "Up to $50,000 USD of unrestricted financial support" and "Up to $20,000 USD in AWS Promotional Credit" (The Momentum to Modernize award)
- Status: "The application cycle for the 2026 AWS Imagine Grant is now closed" (section heading)
- Eligibility: "Nonprofit organizations with a registered 501(c) status based in the United States are eligible to apply. This includes 501(c)3 organizations, as well as credit unions, public utility organizations, and other 501(c)-designated entities." (FAQ, Eligibility)
- Exclusion: "We are not accepting applications from education institutions, including primary or secondary schools, and colleges and universities as they are currently outside of the AWS Imagine Grant priority funding areas." (FAQ)
- Cycle: "We accept proposals once annually." Round One opens March 19 and closes June 5; "December 1 – Imagine Grant winners publicly announced" (FAQ, Program timeline and criteria)
- Review: "An internal AWS adjudication panel will review project proposals." (FAQ)
- AWS use: "The use of AWS is not a requirement to apply for an AWS Imagine Grant." (FAQ)

## Evidence and method

The program's own web page. It states the offer, the rules and the timeline. It gives quotes from past recipients but no count of awards or total funding.

## Limits

- The page does not state how many grants AWS gives: "The number of grantees in each award category varies each year".
- The page states that "AWS reserves the right to determine actual awarded amounts at its sole discretion."
- The page gives no date of publication. The timeline lists days without a year.
