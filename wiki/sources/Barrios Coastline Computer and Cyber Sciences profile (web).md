# Barrios Coastline Computer and Cyber Sciences profile (web)

## Citation

Coastline College. "Computer & Cyber Sciences." Academic department web page, date not stated. https://www.coastline.edu/academics/academic-departments/computer-and-cyber-sciences.php. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- This is the web page of the Computer & Cyber Sciences Department at Coastline College, a community college in Orange County. It is not a personal biography.
- The department teaches cybersecurity, artificial intelligence (AI), information technology, computer information systems and data, mostly online. Its faculty list names Andrea Barrios with the credentials "CISSP, GCFE".

## Background

Coastline College has campuses in Fountain Valley, Garden Grove, Newport Beach and Westminster. The page describes the department, its five program areas and its outreach camps. It states that the National Security Agency (NSA) has designated the college a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD) since 2014. It ends with a list of 18 department faculty. Tobi West is the department chair.

## Key claims

- "Coastline College's Computer & Cyber Sciences Department offers flexible, career-focused education in cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, information technology, computer information systems, data, and other rapidly evolving technology fields." (Unlock Your Potential with a Career in Computer & Cyber Sciences)
- "With flexible online courses and access to hands-on labs and learning environments, Coastline makes it possible to develop new technology skills, advance your expertise, or prepare for your next career opportunity on a schedule that works for you." (Unlock Your Potential with a Career in Computer & Cyber Sciences)
- "Coastline College has been designated a National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense (CAE-CD) since 2014 through the National Security Agency as part of the National Centers of Academic Excellence in Cybersecurity (NCAE-C) program." (Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense)
- "The camp concluded with participants presenting their newly developed lessons, which were expected to reach more than 3,200 K–12 students during the following academic year." (GenCyber Teachers Camp)
- "Andrea Barrios, CISSP, GCFE" (Department Faculty)

## Evidence and method

An institutional department page. It lists the program areas: Business Computing, Computer Information Systems, Cybersecurity, Information and Computer Sciences, and Information Technology. It describes two GenCyber summer camps, one for teachers and one for girls, the second dated 2021. It gives one student quote and one camp participant quote. The faculty list gives names, credentials and email addresses only.

## Limits

The page gives no biography of Andrea Barrios: it does not state her title, courses, start date or work history. It does not give enrollment numbers, course counts or the AI courses by name. It does not describe the hands-on labs or any computing equipment. The page has no date. The camp text describes past events, and the footer shows "© 2020".
