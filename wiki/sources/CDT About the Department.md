# CDT About the Department

## Citation

California Department of Technology. "About." Web page, cdt.ca.gov, date not stated. https://www.cdt.ca.gov/about/. ## Bottom line

- CDT oversees "statewide IT strategic planning, project delivery, procurement, policy and standards, and enterprise architecture". It is "Home to the State Data Center".
- "The Director of CDT is also the State Chief Information Officer (CIO)". Its mission names "educational entities" as partners.
- CDT is the state body that sets IT policy and oversees IT projects. A state role in CalCompute passes through it.

## Key claims

- "CDT is tasked with securing statewide information assets by providing oversight and infrastructure for many state departments and serves as the custodian of information for mission-critical and essential business applications."
- "Home to the State Data Center, CDT provides infrastructure services for government customers that include on-premises and cloud-based services."
- Mission: "Partner with state, local government, and educational entities to advance California's technology and ensure secure, equitable, and reliable solutions through effective policy and oversight, statewide strategies, and innovative services."
- Strategic plan "Bridge to the Future", 2023 to 2026: "broad responsibility and authority over all aspects of technology in California state government", including "Policy formation", "Inter-agency coordination", "IT project oversight, information security", "Technology service delivery" and "Advocacy".
- Links: an organization chart (August 2026), offices, and a Disability Advisory Committee.
