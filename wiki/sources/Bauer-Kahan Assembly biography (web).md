# Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)

## Citation

Office of Assemblymember Rebecca Bauer-Kahan. "Biography." Website of Assembly District 16, California State Assembly Democratic Caucus, date not stated. https://bauer-kahan.asmdc.org/biography. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Rebecca Bauer-Kahan represents Assembly District 16 in the California State Assembly. Voters first elected her in 2018.
- She is Chair of the Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection. The page says that in this role she helps "California lead the way on regulating artificial intelligence tools".

## Background

The California State Assembly Democratic Caucus hosts the websites of its members. This page is the biography of the member for District 16. It gives her work before the Assembly, her committee roles and her education.

## Key claims

- "Assemblymember Rebecca Bauer-Kahan was first elected to represent Assembly District 16 in the California State Assembly in 2018, which includes Alamo, Danville, Dublin, Lafayette, Livermore, Moraga, Orinda, Pleasanton, San Ramon, and Walnut Creek." (Biography)
- "Now, as Chair of the Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection, she is helping California lead the way on regulating artificial intelligence tools to ensure that the state is proactive on protecting users without stifling innovation." (Biography)
- "She also worked with major tech companies on intellectual property cases involving such topics as copyright, trademark, and trade secret theft." (Biography)
- "Rebecca is a product of Bay Area public schools and a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania and Georgetown University Law Center." (Biography)

## Evidence and method

An official biography from the member's office. It gives no sources. It names three past bills, AB 624, AB 988 and AB 1666, but does not describe them.

## Limits

The page has no date. It names no AI bill and does not mention compute, CalCompute or SB 53. It does not give the dates of her committee roles.
