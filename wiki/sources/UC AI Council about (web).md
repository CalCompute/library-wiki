## Citation

About page of the University of California Artificial Intelligence website, date not stated. https://ai.universityofcalifornia.edu/about/. ## Bottom line

- The page introduces the University of California (UC) AI website. The site has four parts: AI Communities, Governance and Transparency, Tools and Resources, and News and Events.
- It says UC was the first university system to publish guiding principles for its use of AI. The page is short and gives no data.

## Background

This is the About page of the UC AI website. The site names the UC AI Council as one of the AI communities in the UC system. The page lists what each part of the site holds. It links to the UC Responsible AI Principles on the UCOP website.

## Key claims

- "UC was the first university system to publish a set of guiding principles for the use of AI within its system" (Join us as we explore the frontiers of AI at the UC)
- "Learn about the various AI communities within the UC system, including the UC AI Council." (AI Communities)
- "This includes an introductory AI 101 training, a glossary of common AI terms, and a model risk assessment framework." (Tools and Resources)
- "Review the UC AI Transparency Report to see how AI is currently being used across the system" (Governance and Transparency)

## Evidence and method

An institutional landing page. It describes the website and states one claim about UC's principles. It gives no evidence for that claim, no dates and no figures.

## Limits

The page is thin. It does not say who sits on the UC AI Council, what it decides or how it reports. It does not mention compute, funding or CalCompute. The [UC AI Council roster (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20roster%20%28web%29.md) and [UC AI Council subcommittees (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20subcommittees%20%28web%29.md) pages give the council's members and parts.
