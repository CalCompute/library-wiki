# CSU GenAI FAQ (web)

## Citation

California State University (CSU). "Frequently Asked Questions." CSU AI Commons web page, date not stated. https://genai.calstate.edu/about/frequently-asked-questions. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The CSU gives ChatGPT Edu to students, faculty and staff at its 22 universities at no personal cost. The current agreement "costs $1.60 per user".
- The renewed agreement expands the service "from 500,000 to 675,000 users". OpenAI does not use CSU workspace data to train its models.

## Background

The AI Commons is the CSU's website for its systemwide AI initiative, which started in 2025. This page answers questions about the initiative, the OpenAI contract, ChatGPT Edu features, data and privacy, and next steps. The CSU Academic Technology Services and the Division of Academic Affairs publish it.

## Key claims

- "The current agreement with OpenAI costs $1.60 per user. This cost is covered with systemwide resources and offered free to our students, faculty and staff. The closest provider offered $8.00 per user, per month and would need to be supported by campus resources or a user fee." (What specific criteria did the CSU use in selecting OpenAI for contract renewal?)
- "Finally, the new agreement anticipates expanding the service from 500,000 to 675,000 users, including a new benefit for free service to students up to one year after graduation in their efforts to enter the workforce." (same section)
- "The CSU Chancellor’s Office has signed an 18-month (February 2025 - July 2026) contract for ChatGPT with an option to extend the agreement." (How long can I access ChatGPT?)
- "There are 500,000 user licenses available systemwide. These licenses were purchased to cover employees and students only." (How many licenses are available for CSU campuses?)
- "OpenAI does not use CSU workspace data to train its large language models." (How is the CSU’s data that is used when CSU community members use ChatGPT being protected?)
- "Since launching the initiative, the CSU has provided AI tools and training to nearly half a million students and 63,000 faculty and staff, creating one of the largest AI deployments in higher education." (What has the CSU accomplished since launching the AI initiative?)

## Evidence and method

An FAQ page from the institution. It gives a price per user, license counts and contract dates. It does not say whether "$1.60 per user" is a monthly price. The comparison price of $8.00 is "per user, per month".

## Limits

The page does not give the total contract value or the vendors it compared. It does not describe any CSU-owned compute. The 18-month contract dates and the renewal answer can disagree in time: the page does not date the renewal.
