# Purdue RCAC About Us (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). "About Us." Web page, date not stated. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/about. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- RCAC operates all centrally maintained research computing resources at Purdue University in West Lafayette, Indiana. It is a division of Purdue IT.
- It supports Purdue faculty and staff researchers with systems, training, consultation and help with grant proposals.

## Background

This is the "About Us" page of the RCAC website. It gives the origin of the center's name and its mission. It lists four activities that serve the mission. It links a research computing cyberinfrastructure (CI) plan and facilities text for grant submissions.

## Key claims

- "The Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC) operates all centrally-maintained research computing resources at Purdue University in West Lafayette, Indiana. A division of Purdue IT" (About Us)
- "RCAC provides advanced computational resources and services to support Purdue faculty and staff researchers. We also conduct our own research and development to enhance the capabilities of these resources." (About Us)
- "promote the effective use of our research computing systems and application software through training and education, consultation, and documentation" (Mission)
- "partner with researchers to develop grant proposals by providing expertise in the assessment of hardware and software requirements." (Mission)
- "Facilities boilerplate text about RCAC facilities for grant submissions is available at Purdue E-Pubs." (Mission)

## Evidence and method

An institutional web page. It gives the mission and the position of RCAC inside Purdue IT. It gives no figures for systems, users, staff or budget. The page also lists videos and a virtual data center tour; the Markdown copy has no text for them.

## Limits

The page does not name the clusters or say how users pay.
