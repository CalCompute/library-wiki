# EuroHPC JU access policy and FAQ (web)

## Citation

European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU). "Supercomputers Access Policy and FAQ." EuroHPC JU website, date not stated (the page shows a 2026 call timeline). https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/access-our-supercomputers/access-policy-and-faq_en. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- EuroHPC JU manages from 35 percent (petascale systems) to 50 percent (pre-exascale and exascale systems) of the time on the supercomputers that it bought.
- Academia, research institutes, public authorities and industry in the European Union (EU) and associated countries can apply through open access calls. Access is free of charge at this time.
- Users must acknowledge the resources, contribute to dissemination events and submit a report after their allocation.

## Background

EuroHPC JU is "a joint initiative between the EU, European countries and private partners to develop a World Class Supercomputing Ecosystem" (page footer). Supercomputing centres, the Hosting Entities, operate its systems. This page summarises who can get access, how to apply, and the history of peer review with PRACE. See also [EuroHPC JU AI Factories (web)](EuroHPC%20JU%20AI%20Factories%20%28web%29.md) and [EuroHPC JU governance (web)](EuroHPC%20JU%20governance%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "EuroHPC JU manage the access time (from 35% for petascale systems up to 50% of pre-exascale and exascale systems total capacity) of EuroHPC supercomputers." (Access to EuroHPC Systems)
- "Researchers from academia, research institutes, public authorities, and industry established or located in an EU Member State or in a Participating State or in a third country associated to the Digital Europe Programme or to Horizon Europe shall be granted the Union’s share of access time to EuroHPC supercomputers acquired after 2020." (Eligibility)
- "Any European organisation is eligible for access to perform Open Science research (the results of the work are made available for open access)." (FAQ)
- "Currently access is free of charge." (FAQ: What is the cost?)
- Users "acknowledge the use of the resources in their related publications", "contribute to dissemination events" and "produce and submit a report after completion of a resource allocation." (FAQ: participation conditions)
- "Currently no privileged access is foreseen." (FAQ: large EU projects)
- "At the end of 2022, the EuroHPC JU, as owner of an increasing number of world-class European supercomputers, took over from PRACE the management of this peer-review and support process." (FAQ: EuroHPC JU and PRACE)

## Evidence and method

An official access policy and FAQ page of the joint undertaking. It cites Council Regulation (EU) 2021/1173 and its amendments of 2024 and 2026, and the JU's Access Policy document, which this library does not hold. It gives no allocation counts or acceptance rates.

## Limits

The page does not give the review criteria or the documents for each call. Those are in the call pages. It does not say who allocates the rest of the time on each system.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the Eligibility section applies the eligibility rule to supercomputers "acquired after 2020", and the FAQ applies a similar rule, with Horizon 2020-associated countries, to supercomputers "acquired before 2020" ([EuroHPC JU access policy and FAQ (web)](EuroHPC%20JU%20access%20policy%20and%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md), Eligibility and FAQ). The page does not say which rule covers which system.
