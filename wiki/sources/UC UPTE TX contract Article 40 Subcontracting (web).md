## Bottom line

- Article 40 of the agreement between the University of California (UC) and UPTE keeps UC's right to subcontract. UC will not contract out services that cause the layoff of non-probationary career unit employees, except in three listed cases.
- The cases are special services and equipment not available internally, special expertise or efficiencies, and financial necessity. For financial necessity in work paid from state funds, UC must first seek funding from the Legislature.
- A career employee laid off because of a contract gets the offer of another unit position at the same base rate of pay.

## Background

The agreement is between UC and UPTE for the Technical (TX) unit. UC posts each article as a separate file on its [TX contract page](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The University retains the right to subcontract all or any portion of operations." (Article 40, A.1)
- "In the event no RFP is issued and the subcontract will result in bargaining unit employee layoffs, the University will give at least sixty (60) calendar days notice prior to the commencement of work by the contractor." (Article 40, A.1)
- "Except as provided below, the University of California will not contract out services that result in the layoff of non-probationary career bargaining unit employees." (Article 40, B.1)
- "The need to obtain special services and equipment that are not available internally;" (Article 40, B.2.a)
- "The need to obtain special expertise or efficiencies that are better provided through an outside contractor than by the University; and" (Article 40, B.2.b)
- "Where financial necessity is the reason for the exception, before contracting for work which is fully or partially supported from state funds, including those at the teaching hospitals, the University shall first seek funding from the Legislature to address the financial necessity." (Article 40, B.3)
- "the University will make available another bargaining unit position for which the employee is qualified. The position will be at the same campus/hospital/LBNL from which the employee was laid off." (Article 40, C)
- "The Office of the President decision is not grievable or arbitrable." (Article 40, B.4.b)

## Evidence and method

One article of a collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.
