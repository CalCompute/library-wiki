# NJ Governor release 2026-10-20 (web)

## Citation

State of New Jersey, Office of the Governor. "Governor Sherrill Launches NJ Supercomputer Initiative to Drive Innovation, Create Inclusive Economic Opportunity, and Solve Shared Statewide Challenges." Press release, October 20, 2026. https://www.nj.gov/governor/news/2026/20261020a.shtml. Read 2026-11-09. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-09 NJ Governor release 2026-10-20.html` and `.md`.

## Bottom line

- Gov. Mikie Sherrill launches the New Jersey Supercomputer Initiative (NJSCI), a public-private partnership of "more than $125 million". It is a shared supercomputer for the state's researchers, students and businesses.
- An "independent nonprofit organization" administers it from 2027. SHI International hosts and operates the hardware. All eight of the state's research institutions are partners.

## Background

The Governor's office publishes this release. The money has three named parts. Chris and Melody Malachowsky give $75 million and SHI International gives $25 million. The state gives $25 million through the New Jersey Economic Development Authority (NJEDA). Several state agencies support the effort. An AI Higher Education and Workforce Collaborative works on curricula, training and apprenticeships.

## Key claims

- "Governor Mikie Sherrill today announced the launch of the New Jersey Supercomputer Initiative (NJSCI), a groundbreaking, more than $125 million public-private partnership" (lead)
- "That includes a $75 million personal gift from Chris and Melody Malachowsky." (funding paragraph)
- "Through the New Jersey Economic Development Authority, the State is also investing $25 million, which was appropriated for the initiative in the Fiscal Year 2026 budget." (funding paragraph)
- "The NJ Supercomputer Initiative is a partnership between all 8 of New Jersey’s higher education research institutions ... as well as the NJ Council of County Colleges. As part of this partnership, each institution plans to provide annual contributions to the initiative’s budget." (partners paragraph)
- "The supercomputer will be administered starting in 2027 by NJSCI, an independent nonprofit organization, and guided by principles including transparent governance and equitable access." (governance paragraph)
- "SHI’s work spans architecture and design, integration of the partner technology ecosystem, implementation, and the hosting and day-to-day operation of the infrastructure." (funding paragraph)
- "The initiative is expected to double the current supercomputing capacity available in aggregate across all of New Jersey’s research universities" (second paragraph)
- "This includes developing new curricula, hands-on training programs, and apprenticeship pathways ranging from AI literacy and responsible use to advanced computing, data science, and related disciplines." (workforce paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It gives the funding sources and amounts, the partners, the governance form and the operator. It gives no hardware specification.

## Limits
