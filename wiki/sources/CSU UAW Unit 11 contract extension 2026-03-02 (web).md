# CSU UAW Unit 11 contract extension 2026-03-02 (web)

## Citation

United Auto Workers 4123 and the Board of Trustees of the California State University. "Memorandum of Understanding: Extension of the Current Collective Bargaining Agreement between The United Auto Workers and The California State University." Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- A one-page memorandum of understanding extends the Unit 11 agreement between the California State University (CSU) and United Auto Workers (UAW) 4123 to Sept. 30, 2026.
- The extension includes "all side letters and supplemental MOUS".

## Background

Unit 11 holds CSU's Teaching Associates, Graduate Assistants and Instructional Student Assistants. The agreement that this memorandum extends is [CSU UAW Unit 11 contract 2023-2025 (web)](CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%202023-2025%20%28web%29.md). The memorandum has signature lines for the union's president and CSU's senior director of collective bargaining.

## Key claims

- "The United Auto Workers 4123 and the Board of Trustees of the California State University (CSU) hereby agree as follows:"
- "The Collective Bargaining Agreement between the parties covering Bargaining Unit 11, which is set to expire on February 27, 2026, including all side letters and supplemental MOUS, shall be extended up to, and including, September 30, 2026."
- "Extension of the Current Collective Bargaining Agreement between The United Auto Workers and The California State University" (title)

## Evidence and method

A short signed agreement between a public employer and a union. It changes only the end date.

## Limits

- It does not say why the parties extended the agreement, or what happens after Sept. 30, 2026.
- The agreement's own text ends on Oct. 31, 2025, and this memorandum gives Feb. 27, 2026.
