# Orange County Regional Consortium profile (web)

## Citation

Orange County Regional Consortium (OCRC). "Orange County Regional Consortium." Home page, date not stated. https://ocregionalconsortium.org/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The OCRC is a regional body of nine community colleges and one continuing education center in four community college districts in Orange County.
- It coordinates and plans career and technical education (CTE) and workforce and economic development in the region. It invites business, labor, government and industry to work with it.

## Background

This is the home page of the OCRC website, under the name "California Community Colleges - Orange County Regional Consortium". The site has sections on program recommendation, the Strong Workforce Program, governance and a work-based learning resource center. The footer states that the California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office and a Carl D. Perkins IV grant support the OCRC. Its office is in Santa Ana.

## Key claims

- "The Orange County Regional Consortium (OCRC) consists of nine community colleges and one continuing education center across four community college districts, and serves as a regional framework for communicating, coordinating, collaborating, promoting and planning career and technical education, and workforce and economic development initiatives in the Orange County Region." (home page)
- "The goal of the OCRC is to support the connection of individuals and the alignment of programs and curriculum to the needs of business & industry." (home page)
- "The OCRC invites the collaboration of business, non-profits, labor, government agencies and industry leaders in order to improve the decision-making and responsiveness of our educational institutions to reflect changing market and technological trends." (home page)
- "OCRC is supported by the CA Community College Chancellor's Office and Carl D. Perkins IV grant #13-150-003." (footer)
