# Apereo governance (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Governance." Web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/about/governance. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Apereo Foundation has its registration in New Jersey. An elected Board of Directors governs it.
- Board elections are normally once a year. Representatives of member organizations elect the replacements for about three retiring members.
- The page says that the board can appoint up to four more members.

## Background

This is a short summary page on apereo.org. It has one paragraph and a table of contents. The table of contents links to the bylaws, licensing, annual reports, board minutes, public disclosure and vendor policies. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Registration: "Apereo is registered in the State of New Jersey, USA, and governed by an elected Board of Directors."
- Elections: "Board elections normally occur once per year. Typically, three board members retire and their replacements are elected by representatives from Apereo member organizations."
- Nominations: "Representatives from member organizations can nominate any individual or they may nominate themselves."
- Appointed members: "In addition to elected board members, the board has the right to appoint up to four additional board members."

## Evidence and method

This is the foundation's own summary of its governance. It gives no data. The detail is in the linked documents.

## Limits

- The page is a summary of one paragraph. The rules are in [Apereo bylaws (web)](Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md).
- It gives no current board size and no budget.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page says that the board can appoint "up to four additional board members". The bylaws say "up to three (3) additional Directors" ([Apereo bylaws (web)](Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article V, Section 2.6).
