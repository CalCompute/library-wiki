# UCI RCIC resource allocations and rates (web)

## Citation

UC Irvine (UCI), Research Cyberinfrastructure Center (RCIC). "4. Resource Allocations." RCIC documentation, last updated 2026-11-10. https://rcic.uci.edu/about/allocations.html. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 UCI RCIC resource allocations and rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- Central campus funds give UCI faculty a no-cost baseline on the HPC3 cluster. Every six months, it is 100,000 core-hours and 1,000 GPU-hours. Each researcher also gets 1 TB of campus storage.
- Above the baseline, labs buy time at $0.01 a core-hour and $0.32 a GPU-hour. Or they buy nodes: about $15,000 for a CPU node and $47,000 for a node with 4 L40S GPUs.
- The rates are set to recover the cost of hardware over five years at 60 percent use. They apply only to UCI researchers.

## Background

RCIC runs HPC3, the shared research cluster of UC Irvine, and its storage systems. This page of its documentation states the no-cost allocations, the recharge rates and how to buy time, nodes and storage. A principal investigator (PI) asks for each purchase by email. Time and storage purchases go through a memorandum of understanding (MOU).

## Key claims

- "RCIC resources are funded and operated under a shared financial model of no-cost and recharge (pi-funded) allocations." (4.1 Introduction)
- "RCIC is funded through central campus to provide a baseline allocations of centralized, research computing and data services." (4.2 No-cost)
- "It should be noted, that rates apply only to UCI researchers (faculty, research faculty, research staff)." (4.3 Recharge)
- "GPU hours | $0.32/GPU-Hour | Minimum: 250 GPU-hours | Prepaid GPU-hours valid for 1 year after purchase." (Table 4.4)
- "The current HPC3 Computing Related Recharges are computed to recover the cost of hardware over a 5 year period at 60% use." (4.4.1.1 Purchase core-hours)
- "Hardware is commodity-based and subject to market variability, prices as of 1Q 2025: CPU nodes (48 cores) are approximately $15K. GPU-nodes (4 x NVIDIA L40S) are approximately $47K." (4.4.1.2 Purchase hardware)
- "The conversion rate is 95% of the theoretical core hours your hardware could deliver in a year" (4.4.1.2 Purchase hardware)
- "GPU Hours (2 cores) | ... | $0.20/GPU-hour" (Table 4.5, HPC3 Effective Costs for CPU and GPU hours)
- "Purchasing of nodes in HPC3 does NOT give you a “private” queue" (4.4.1.2 Purchase hardware)
- "Proposed rates for CRSP and CPU/GPU hours are in the process of being approved by the UCI recharge rate review process." (4.3 Recharge)

## Evidence and method

A documentation page with five tables and worked examples. It shows how purchased hardware converts to service units (SUs), and the effective cost of an owned node over six years. The effective rates assume that the owner uses all SUs in each six-month cycle. The page states its assumptions. It gives no usage data.

## Limits

- The page gives no count of nodes, GPUs or users of HPC3.
- It does not say what central campus pays RCIC each year.
- It calls the rates proposed rates that wait for approval. It does not say when they took effect.
- Table 4.1 gives the GPU cycle price as "$.32/core-hr". Table 4.4 on the same page gives "$0.32/GPU-Hour".
