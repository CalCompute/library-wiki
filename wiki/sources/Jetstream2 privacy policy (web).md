# Jetstream2 privacy policy (web)

## Citation

Jetstream2, Indiana University. "Privacy Notice." Jetstream2 website, effective 2021-10-15. https://jetstream-cloud.org/privacy/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The notice applies "only to the Jetstream2 website". It does not cover the Jetstream2 cloud or the data of its users.
- The site takes technical data through Indiana University's web hosting, contact data that visitors give, and Google Analytics data. Jetstream2 shares personal data only for legal, investigation or compliance reasons.

## Background

Jetstream2 is a research cloud at Indiana University, funded by the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) under Grant 2005506. The notice follows the Indiana University template for website privacy notices. Its rules for users of the cloud are in [Jetstream2 policies (web)](Jetstream2%20policies%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "This privacy notice applies only to the Jetstream2 website (https://jetstream-cloud.org/) and explains our practices concerning the collection, use, and disclosure of visitor information." (Applicability)
- "We do not share any personally identifiable information with other entities or organizations, except when legally required to do so, at the request of governmental authorities conducting an investigation, to verify or enforce compliance with the policies governing our website and applicable laws, or to protect against misuse or unauthorized use of our website." (Information Sharing)
- "This website uses Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google, Inc. (“Google”)." (Use of Third Party Services)
- "IU cannot guarantee the security of any information users transmit to university sites, and users do so at their own risk." (Security)
- "From time to time, we may use visitor information for new, unanticipated uses not previously disclosed in our privacy notice." (Privacy Notice Changes)

## Evidence and method

A website privacy notice of about 1,000 words, with an effective date. It states practices. It names no law, other than a general promise to follow "all applicable federal, state and local laws".

## Limits

The notice does not cover the accounts, allocations or research data of Jetstream2 users. It says that the site "does not use cookies to store information about your actions or choices". It also says that Google Analytics on the site uses cookies. It does not resolve the two statements.
