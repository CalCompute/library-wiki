# Cal-ICOR hub usage

## Citation

Cal-ICOR. "Cal-ICOR Hub Users." Web page, cal-icor.github.io, "Last updated: 2026-10-25 10:14 PDT". https://cal-icor.github.io/hub-users-page/. Fetched 2026-10-26. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- 13 institutions had "more than 5 users in AY 2026–27", on 20 hubs. Monthly active users were 1,739 in September 2026.
- Users by term grew from 9 (spring 2025) to 1,002 (fall 2025), 1,609 (spring 2026) and 1,907 (fall 2026). Summer use drops to 144 to 179.

## Limits

- No compute hours, cost or capacity.
