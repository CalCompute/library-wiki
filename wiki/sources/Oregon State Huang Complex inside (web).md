# Oregon State Huang Complex inside (web)

## Citation

Oregon State University (OSU). "Inside the Huang Complex." Jen-Hsun Huang and Lori Mills Huang Collaborative Innovation Complex website, date not stated. https://huangcomplex.oregonstate.edu/inside-complex. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page says that the Huang Complex is "under construction through the end of 2026" and becomes "fully operational in 2027".
- Its capabilities come "in phases": laboratories, supercomputing and AI, maker facilities, digital twins, visualization studios and shared work spaces.
- For supercomputing and AI, "No prior computational expertise is required."

## Background

OSU publishes this page on the website of the Huang Complex. It lists eight platforms and capabilities, each with one or two sentences.

## Key claims

- Schedule: "The Huang Complex is under construction through the end of 2026. When the research and education facility becomes fully operational in 2027, it will serve as a hub for discovery" (Platforms and Capabilities)
- Phases: "As the complex comes online, key capabilities will be introduced in phases to researchers, students and partners" (Platforms and Capabilities)
- Computing access: "Infrastructure will be available across disciplines, with consultation to help teams identify where computing can accelerate their work. No prior computational expertise is required." (Supercomputing and AI)
- Digital twins: these environments "Will enable teams to explore ideas, integrate data and evaluate promising concepts before real-world deployment." (Physical and digital twin environments)
- Teams: the complex "Will bring together engineers, scientists, social scientists, business strategists, policy researchers, artists, humanists and designers." (Transdisciplinary collaboration by design)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional web page. It describes planned capabilities in short statements. It gives no hardware, cost or allocation facts.

## Limits

- The page does not give the size or type of the supercomputer.
- It does not say who outside OSU can use the capabilities, or how to apply.
- It does not give the order or dates of the phases.
