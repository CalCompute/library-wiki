# Hochul NY AI science and safety research launch (web)

## Citation

Office of the Governor of New York. "Governor Hochul Declares NY Will Lead the Nation in AI Science and Safety; Launches New Research Institute." Press release, Albany, NY, November 10, 2026. https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-declares-ny-will-lead-nation-ai-science-and-safety-launches-new-research. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Hochul NY AI science and safety research launch.html`.

## Bottom line

- New York launches the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research (NYCAIRR) at SUNY Binghamton University. More than 80 researchers across the state are to use Empire AI to test advanced AI systems for safety and security.
- The state also opens a portal where large frontier AI developers must register under the RAISE Act by January 1, 2027.

## Background

The Office of the Governor of New York publishes this release. It names Greg Morrisett (Cornell Tech) and Jeannette Wing (Columbia University) as interim co-directors. It quotes the Governor, the Empire AI Board Chair, the Department of Financial Services, SUNY and Binghamton University.

## Key claims

- "Governor Kathy Hochul today launched a first-of-its-kind statewide effort to advance artificial intelligence science and safety – the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research based at State University of New York’s (SUNY) Binghamton University." (opening)
- "The Center is supported by the advanced computing power of Empire AI, Governor Hochul’s $500 million public-private initiative to drive AI research for public good." (release text)
- "That mission will be pursued through independent testing of frontier AI systems, foundational scientific research, and the training of a new generation of AI safety experts." (release text)
- "Governor Hochul first announced the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research in January with a historic $30 million philanthropic gift from a group of donors led by Binghamton alumnus and Bloomberg LP co-founder Tom Secunda, paired with an initial $25 million research investment from SUNY and operating capital from New York State." (release text)
- "Large frontier developers are required to register through the newly released RAISE Portal by January 1, 2027." (release text)
- "At the State’s request, NYCAIRR will be able to provide expertise, assessments and evidence that can assist in informing state policy making and oversight of frontier AI Developers." (release text)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It gives the funding and the research areas of the Center. It also describes the Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT), which carries out the RAISE Act.

## Limits

The release does not say how much Empire AI computing time the Center gets. It does not give the Center's yearly budget. The release does not break down the figure of "$500 million" for Empire AI. Other New York releases in this library give separate state and partner amounts ([Hochul Empire AI Beta launch 2025-06-26 (web)](Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20Beta%20launch%202025-06-26%20%28web%29.md)).
