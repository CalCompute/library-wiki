## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and Teamsters Local 2010 for the Clerical and Allied Services (CX) unit. Its term is Oct. 22, 2022 to March 31, 2026.
- UC will not contract out services that cause the layoff of non-probationary career unit employees, except in three listed cases. For financial necessity in work paid from state funds, UC must first seek funding from the Legislature.
- When UC requires a training program, UC pays its costs and the time counts as time worked.

## Background

Teamsters Local 2010 represents UC's clerical staff. UC posts the agreement on its [UC CX contract page (web)](UC%20CX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md). Its subcontracting article has the same three cases as the UPTE agreements ([UC UPTE TX contract Article 40 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2040%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "The terms and conditions of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect commencing at 12:01 midnight on October 22, 2022 (the day after UC received Teamsters Local 2010’s written notice of ratification) and shall terminate at 11:59 p.m. on March 31, 2026" (Article 6, A)
- "To introduce new or improved methods, programs, equipment, or facilities or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment, or facilities;" (Article 15, B.5)
- "The University retains the right to subcontract all or any portion of operations subject to the limitations imposed by this article." (Article 37, A)
- "Except as provided below, the University of California will not contract out services that result in the layoff of non-probationary career bargaining unit employees." (Article 37, B.1)
- "Where financial necessity is the reason for the exception, before contracting for work which is fully or partially supported from state funds, including those at the teaching hospitals, the University shall first seek funding from the Legislature to address the financial necessity." (Article 37, B.3)
- "To the extent this Article is in conflict with legislation regarding subcontracting at the University, the legislation shall control." (Article 37, D)
- "When the University requires attendance at an educational or training program, the University will pay the fees and related costs for materials, travel and per diem, and the employee’s attendance at the actual program shall be considered time worked." (Article 38, A.2)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.
