# GCS Steering Committee (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing e.V. (GCS). "Steering Committee." Web page, date not stated. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us/organization/steering-committee. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The GCS Steering Committee is made of the four ministries that fund GCS: one federal ministry and three state ministries.
- It sets the GCS strategy, the rules for use of GCS computers and the process to award computing time. The federal ministry holds the chair.

## Background

GCS is the registered association that joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)). This page states the tasks of its Steering Committee and names one official for each funding ministry. [GCS funding bodies (web)](GCS%20funding%20bodies%20%28web%29.md) lists the same four ministries as the funders.

## Key claims

- "The GCS steering committee is responsible for forming and guiding the GCS strategy as well as setting the rules and conditions for using GCS HPC resources, setting the process for reviewing and awarding computing time, and reviewing allocations recommended by the scientific steering committee." (Steering Committee)
- "The steering committee is made up of representatives of the agencies funding GCS" (Steering Committee)
- "The chair is held by the BMFTR representative, and the vice-chair rotates every two years." (Steering Committee)
- "Bundesministerium für Forschung, Technologie und Raumfahrt (BMFTR)" "Ministerialrat Dr. Fabian Kohler" "Leiter Referat T12 – Mikroelektronik, Supercomputing, Robotik" (BMFTR). Rendering by the library: Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space; head of unit T12, microelectronics, supercomputing and robotics.

## Evidence and method

A short institutional page. It gives the committee's tasks in one paragraph. It then names one official and a unit for each ministry, in German:

- Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR): Dr. Fabian Kohler;
- Baden-Württemberg Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts: Peter Castellaz, head of unit 42 (digitalisation and information infrastructure);
- Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts: Georg Antretter, unit Z.4 (IT at universities and in the arts);
- North Rhine-Westphalia Ministry of Culture and Science: Anne Kristin Bischoff, head of unit 325 (natural and engineering sciences, Forschungszentrum Jülich).

The library does not copy the email addresses on the page.

## Limits

- The page has no date and gives no budget.
- It does not say how the committee votes or how often it meets.
- This page says the committee reviews allocations that the Scientific Steering Committee recommends. [GCS organization (web)](GCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md) says the committee does "reviewing and approving" of them. This page does not use the word "approving".
