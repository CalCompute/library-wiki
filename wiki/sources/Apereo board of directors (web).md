# Apereo board of directors (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Board of Directors." Web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/about/board-directors. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Apereo Foundation board has four types of directors: organizational member seats, an individual member seat, appointed seats and ex-officio members.
- The page lists 16 people: 14 directors, the Executive Director and the non-voting Treasurer.
- Directors come from universities, companies and partner organizations in several countries.

## Background

The Apereo Foundation publishes this page on apereo.org. It describes the seat types and gives a short profile of each board member. It points to the bylaws for eligibility and elections. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Organizational seats: "Organizational member seat, board members elected by Apereo's Educational Organizational Members and Commercial Affiliate Members."
- Individual seat: "Individual Member Seat, board members elected by Individual Members who are part of the Friends of Apereo program."
- Appointed seats: "Appointed Member Seat, board members nominated by the seated board of directors. Often affiliated with partner organizations such as AXIES or ESUP-Portail."
- Ex-officio: "Ex-officio members such as the non-voting Treasurer and Executive Director of the foundation."
- California members: the list includes "Stephanie Lieggi is the Executive Director of the Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) at the University of California, Santa Cruz". It also includes "Wayne Smith is the Department of Management Faculty and IT Administrator at California State University, Northridge."
- Executive Director: "Patrick joined Apereo as Executive Director in 2023, serving previously as Interim General Manager of the Foundation."

## Evidence and method

This is the foundation's own list of its board. It gives names, titles and employers. It does not say which seat type each director holds.

## Limits

- The page gives no terms of office and no election dates.
- It does not say how each director got the seat.
- The seat rules are in [Apereo bylaws (web)](Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md). The bylaws give the Individual Members three seats; this page names one "Individual Member Seat".
