# DOE Genesis Mission Phase II awards release 2026-10 (web)

## Citation

U.S. Department of Energy (DOE). "Energy Department Announces New Genesis Mission Awards to Advance Super Intelligence for Science." ## Bottom line

- DOE announced "12 Phase II Genesis Mission project awards totaling $159 million" and six new Phase I awards. The release says these cap "a first-year cohort of 297 projects".
- Three of the twelve Phase II leads are in California. They are the University of California San Diego, the University of California Irvine and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.

## Background

The Genesis Mission is a federal effort on artificial intelligence (AI) for science that the President launched in November 2025. DOE selected its first projects in July 2026. This release names the twelve new Phase II projects through the DOE Office of Science. With two earlier awards, there are now 14 Phase II projects.

## Key claims

- "Twelve newly selected Phase II and six Phase I projects to advance the frontiers of Super Intelligence (SI) for science, capping a first-year cohort of 297 projects across the Genesis Mission portfolio." (subtitle)
- "The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) today announced 12 Phase II Genesis Mission project awards totaling $159 million." (first paragraph)
- "Decoding the RNA Structurome to Secure AI Advantage for the Bioeconomy: Led by the University of California San Diego". (Awarded Projects)
- "Multi-agent AI Expert for Subsurface Reasoning and Optimization (MAESTRO): Led by the University of California Irvine, MAESTRO will establish an SI platform to accurately map, stimulate, and manage deep underground reservoirs". (Awarded Projects)
- "The Multi-office Accelerator Team Core (MOAT-Core): Led by Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, MOAT-Core will develop a shared SI platform across National Laboratories". (Awarded Projects)
- "An Iterative Framework of AI-Assisted Scientific Development and Optimization (AI4HPC): Led by Argonne National Laboratory, AI4HPC will create an SI framework to modernize, optimize, and verify supercomputing scientific software." (Awarded Projects)
- "These new selections bring the total number of Phase II projects to 14, alongside previously announced awards for GridFM 2.0 under the Office of Electricity and Prometheus under the Office of Nuclear Energy." (after the list)

## Evidence and method

A federal press release. It gives the total award amount, the twelve Phase II project titles and the lead institution of each. It gives no amount for each project and does not list partners.

## Limits

The release gives one total, not an amount for each project. It does not name the six new Phase I projects. It does not name partner institutions, so it does not show the SLAC role that [SLAC Genesis Mission phase II projects (web)](SLAC%20Genesis%20Mission%20phase%20II%20projects%20%28web%29.md) reports. The AI4HPC title here differs from the SLAC page, which gives "An Iterative Framework for AI-Assisted Scientific Software Development and Optimization".
