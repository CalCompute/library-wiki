# Google TPU Research Cloud (web)

## Citation

Google Research. "TPU Research Cloud." Program page, date not stated. https://sites.research.google/trc/about/. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- The TPU Research Cloud (TRC) gives accepted researchers Cloud Tensor Processing Units (TPUs) "at no charge". The pool has "more than 1,000 Cloud TPU devices".
- The price of free access is openness and feedback. Participants share their research publicly and give "detailed feedback with Google".
- A person applies through an "Express interest" form. The page does not state the review, the allocation size or the duration.

## Background

Google Research runs TRC. The program was earlier called the TensorFlow Research Cloud. The page also points to Google Colab, a notebook tool with free TPU access and no application. The page links to an FAQ.

## Key claims

- Offer: "TRC enables researchers to access a cluster of more than 1,000 Cloud TPU devices. Researchers accepted into the TRC program will have access to Cloud TPUs at no charge and can leverage a variety of frameworks including TensorFlow, PyTorch, Julia and JAX to accelerate the next wave of open research breakthroughs." (Learn more about the TRC program)
- Duties: "Participants in the TRC program will be expected to share their TRC-supported research with the world through peer-reviewed publications, open source code, blog posts, or other means." (Learn more about the TRC program)
- Feedback: "They should also be willing to share detailed feedback with Google to help us improve the TRC program and the underlying Cloud TPU platform over time." (Learn more about the TRC program)
- Terms: "participants accept Google's Terms and Conditions, acknowledge that their information will be used in accordance with our Privacy Policy, and agree to conduct their research in accordance with the Google AI principles." (Learn more about the TRC program)
- Purpose: "We'd like to empower researchers from many different backgrounds to think even bigger and tackle exciting new challenges that would be inaccessible otherwise." (Learn more about the TRC program)
- No-application tier: "If you'd like to get started with Cloud TPUs right away, you can access them for free in your browser using Google Colab." (Use Cloud TPUs for free, right in your browser)
- Application: an "Express interest" link goes to a Google form. (Unlock new breakthroughs with the TPU Research Cloud)

## Evidence and method

The program's own page. It is short. It states the offer and the duties, not the process.

## Limits

- The page does not state eligibility. It does not say that a person must be at a university.
- The page does not state who reviews, the criteria or the time to a decision.
- The page does not state the allocation size for one project or the duration.
- The page does not state renewal.
- The page says TPUs are free.
