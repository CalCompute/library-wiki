# AIST release AIST Solutions established 2023-04-01 (web)

## Citation

National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST). 株式会社AIST Solutionsの設立について (On the establishment of AIST Solutions Co.). News release, April 1, 2023 (2023/04/01). Web page, in Japanese. https://www.aist.go.jp/aist_j/news/pr20230401.html. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- On April 1, 2023, AIST set up AIST Solutions Co. (AISol), a company that AIST owns 100 percent. Its capital is 100 million yen.
- The company is to put AIST research results into use in society, through marketing, open innovation and new business.
- AIST set it up as a corporation under the Act on the Activation of Science, Technology and Innovation Creation.

## Background

AIST is a national research and development agency of Japan. This short release on its Japanese news page announces the new company. It gives the name, offices in Tokyo and Tsukuba, date, representative and capital. On April 1, 2024, AISol took over the AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure (ABCI) service from AIST ([ABCI Agreement of Use 2026 Japanese (web)](ABCI%20Agreement%20of%20Use%202026%20Japanese%20%28web%29.md), Article 8-3). The renderings below are the library's own, not an official translation.

## Key claims

- Founding: AIST "研究成果の社会実装に向けた体制と活動を強化するため、産総研100％出資により、株式会社AIST Solutions（アイストソリューションズ）を2023年4月1日付で設立しました。" (paragraph 1). Library's rendering: "to strengthen its structure and activities for the implementation of research results in society, established AIST Solutions Co. on April 1, 2023, with 100% investment by AIST."
- Role: the company will use AIST's "技術資産と研究資源を活用し、積極的なマーケティング活動を通じて、市場や産業のニーズに即応すべく、オープンイノベーションの強化、エコシステムの構築や新規事業創出を行ってまいります。" (paragraph 2). Library's rendering: "technology assets and research resources, and, through active marketing, will strengthen open innovation, build ecosystems and create new businesses, to respond quickly to market and industry needs."
- Capital: "資本金：１億円" and "国立研究開発法人産業技術総合研究所（100％）" (About AIST Solutions). Library's rendering: "Capital: 100 million yen" and "National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (100%)."
- Legal basis: "※株式会社AIST Solutionsは、科学技術・イノベーション創出の活性化に関する法律に基づく法人として設立されます。" (About AIST Solutions). Library's rendering: "AIST Solutions Co. is established as a corporation based on the Act on the Activation of Science, Technology and Innovation Creation."

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional news release of three paragraphs and a data box. It states the decision, the purpose and the company data. It gives no budget, staff numbers or business plan.

## Limits

- The release does not name ABCI or any computing service. The link between AISol and ABCI comes from other ABCI sources ([ABCI about (web)](ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- It does not describe the governance of AISol, or how AIST oversees it.
- It does not say how AISol earns revenue or what it pays to AIST.
