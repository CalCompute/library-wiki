# ACCESS Executive Council charter (web)

## Citation

ACCESS Executive Council. "Charter of the ACCESS Executive Council." ACCESS Collaboration Portal, ACCESS Coordination Office (ACO). Approved October 8, 2024; page updated July 28, 2025; page author John Towns. https://access-ci.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ACP/pages/57901267/Charter+of+the+ACCESS+Executive+Council. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The charter makes the ACCESS Executive Council (EC) the primary governance body for all ACCESS awards. The award principal investigators (PIs) and the Resource Provider Forum Chair vote.
- A simple majority passes a resolution. The EC meets at least monthly. A change to the charter needs 4 of the 6 voting members. The National Science Foundation (NSF) program officer arbitrates disputes.

## Background

ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services and Support) is the NSF program that gives allocations on national research computing resources. NSF funds it as a set of separate awards ([ACCESS governance (web)](ACCESS%20governance%20%28web%29.md)). This charter is the agreed text that the governance page refers to. The ACO keeps it on the program's public collaboration portal, a Confluence site.

## Key claims

- "The ACCESS Executive Council (EC) is the primary governance body providing the necessary coordination and integration of the day-to-day execution of all ACCESS awards and essential leadership to ensure high operational efficiency and user-centric performance of the collective ACCESS resources for the benefit of the scientific and engineering research and education community." (Definition of Purpose)
- "ACCESS program award PIs serve as *ex-officio,* voting members of the EC." (Membership)
- "The ACCESS Resource Providers Forum (RP Forum) Chair also serves as an *ex-officio* voting member of the EC to facilitate interactions between Resource Providers and the ACCESS program and to represent RP interests to the ACCESS EC." (Membership)
- "NSF ACCESS Cognizant Program Officer(s) participate as ex-officio, non-voting observer(s)." (Membership)
- "Accordingly, any voting member of the EC may request that an item come up for a formal vote. A vote in support of a resolution shall pass with a simple majority of votes (>50%)." (Executive Council Voting)
- "In the case in which the need to take a vote arises during the course of an EC meeting, or as a result of an issue that has arisen past the one week notice period, a vote can only be taken if all voting members are present and they all agree that they are prepared to vote on the issue." (Executive Council Voting)
- "Meetings shall be no less frequent than monthly with the venue and format set by the EC." (Meetings)
- "The EC can establish and discontinue Working Groups and Standing Committees to facilitate carrying out program-wide projects and activities." (Working Groups and Standing Committees)
- "The EC Charter can be amended or altered as the EC sees fit. Any changes to the charter will require agreement by 4 out of 6 voting members of the EC." (Alterations to the Charter)
- "Should the EC fail to come to resolution on an issue or should an ACCESS awardee not behave in a manner consistent with the EC Charter, and all attempts at resolution within the EC and facilitation from the ACO fail, the ACO EC Facilitator will make an appeal to the NSF ACCESS Cognizant Program Officer(s) for arbitration and remediation." (Conflict Resolution and Compliance with the Charter)

## Evidence and method

The full text of a governance charter, in eleven sections. Apart from the quotes above, it sets these rules:

- The ACO PI facilitates EC meetings and gives "impartial recommendations". The External Advisory Board (EAB) Facilitator stands in when the ACO PI is not available.
- A vote known in advance goes on the agenda at least one week before the meeting. An absent voter votes in advance or names a delegate.
- A draft record of each meeting goes to the EC for approval at the next meeting. Approved summaries go to all program staff.
- Working groups have "a well-defined deliverable and time-limited scope of work". Standing committees give "ongoing and consultative guidance".
- The ACO keeps the EAB charter, and the EC approves changes to it.
- PIs tell the EC when a decision can change the scope, schedule or budget of their Cooperative Agreement.

## Limits

- The charter does not name the members or the awards. The figure "4 out of 6 voting members" implies six voting seats, but the page does not list them.
- It does not set a quorum for ordinary meetings or say how the EC decides an item without a formal vote.
- It does not give budgets.
- The page links to a Google Document on the structure of working groups. The library has not read it.
- [ACCESS governance (web)](ACCESS%20governance%20%28web%29.md) says the EC meets "on a regular basis as defined in the Charter". This charter sets the floor at monthly.
