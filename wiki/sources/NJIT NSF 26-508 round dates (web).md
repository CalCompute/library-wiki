# NJIT NSF 26-508 round dates (web)

## Citation

New Jersey Institute of Technology (NJIT), Research. "TechAccess: AI-Ready America." Funding opportunity listing, date not stated. https://research.njit.edu/node/18606. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- A university research office's listing of the National Science Foundation (NSF) program TechAccess: AI-Ready America, NSF 26-508.
- It gives the award sizes, the total funding and the dates of the three rounds for the State/Territory Coordination Hubs.

## Background

NJIT is a university in Newark, New Jersey. Its research office lists funding opportunities for its researchers. This listing restates parts of NSF 26-508 and links to the NSF solicitation. [NSF 26-508 TechAccess solicitation (web)](NSF%2026-508%20TechAccess%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md) is the primary record.

## Key claims

- "State/Territory Coordination Hub Awards: Up to 56 awards; one for every state, the District of Columbia, or territory in the U.S." (Awards)
- "Coordination Hubs will be selected over three rounds: 10 Coordination Hubs in round one, 20 in round two, and the remainder in round three." (Awards)
- "Each Coordination Hub Award will receive support of $1M/year for three years, with the possibility for one additional year of support" (Awards)
- "$168,000,000 to $224,000,000" (Anticipated Funding Amount)
- "Required: State/Territory Coordination Hubs: June 16, 2026 (round 1); December 15, 2027 (round 2); June 1, 2027 (round 3)" (Letter of Intent)
- "State/Territory Coordination Hubs: July 16, 2026 (round 1); January 15, 2027 (round 2); July 1, 2027 (round 3)" (Full Proposal Submission Deadline)
- "Unlike initiatives centered around K – 16 education, AI-Ready America additionally reaches businesses, public-serving organizations, and individuals" (Description)

## Evidence and method

A secondary listing of a federal solicitation. It gives the award type as "Standard Grant or Continuing Grant". It names the three parts of the program: Coordination Hubs, a National Coordination Lead and AI-Ready Catalyst Award Competitions. It says the National Lead "will be funded as an Other Transaction (OT)". The fields for proposal limits, preliminary proposals and contacts are empty.

## Limits

- The listing does not give the hub responsibilities or the review criteria.
- It does not say when NJIT posted or last updated it.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this listing gives the round 2 letter of intent date as "December 15, 2027", after the round 2 full proposal date of January 15, 2027. [NSF 26-508 TechAccess solicitation (web)](NSF%2026-508%20TechAccess%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md) gives Dec. 15, 2026.
