## Bottom line

- Stephanie C. Lieggi is a co-principal investigator (co-PI) of NSF award 2347360, Beginnings, at UC Santa Cruz. Emily Lovell is the PI.
- The award is $999,924, from July 15, 2024 to June 30, 2028. It funds an experiential learning program in open source for undergraduates from historically Black colleges and universities (HBCUs).
