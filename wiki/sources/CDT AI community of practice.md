## Bottom line

- The Artificial Intelligence Community (AIC) brings "state experts together in shaping the future of ethical, transparent, and trustworthy Artificial Intelligence (AI)". It meets about every two months.
- UC researchers already speak there. Speakers include Deirdre Mulligan, Fernando Pérez, Safiya Noble, Brandie Nonnecke (CITRIS Policy Lab) and Hemant Bhargava. UC Berkeley's Chief Technology Officer, Bill Allison, spoke too.
- It is a ready forum to present CalCompute to state AI staff.
