# PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)

## Citation

Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (PSC). Ken Chiacchia. "$1.5 M in State Funding for PSC and Collaborators." Press release, January 21, 2021. https://www.psc.edu/1-5-m-in-state-funding-for-psc-and-collaborators/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The release says that the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania funded PSC and partners with $1.5 million in two awards.
- A $500,000 line item in the 2020-21 state budget pays for PSC workforce development and outreach.
- A $1 million Redevelopment Assistance Capital Program (RACP) grant helps pay for power and cooling upgrades for Bridges-2.

## Background

PSC published this release on January 21, 2021. PSC is a joint computational research center of Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Pittsburgh. The release reports two state awards. One is a budget line item. The other is a capital grant to PSC and the landlord of its machine room building in Monroeville.

## Key claims

- Total: "The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania has funded PSC and collaborators with $1.5 million in two separate awards." (paragraph 1)
- Budget line: "The state’s 2020-21 budget includes a line item for $500,000 to fund activities at PSC not covered by the organization’s research grants." (paragraph 2)
- Uses: "The money will support PSC’s workforce development efforts and other non-technical outreach activities." (paragraph 2)
- Federal grants: "Funding by the state is also important in remaining competitive for larger federal and charitable foundation research grants, such as those that fund PSC’s supercomputers." (paragraph 2)
- Capital: "This grant will in part fund upgrades to the electrical supply and cooling systems at NTC@PE, making PSC’s new Bridges-2 supercomputing platform possible." (paragraph 3)
- RACP: "RACP, administered by the state Office of the Budget, funds acquisition and construction for regional economic, cultural, civic, recreational and historical improvement projects." (paragraph 3)

## Evidence and method

This is a short press release from the center that received the money. It states two amounts, their state programs and their uses. It gives no budget act citation.

## Limits

- The release does not cite the budget act or the RACP grant number.
- It does not say how the $1 million divides between PSC and the landlord.
- It does not say that the state pays for any supercomputer. It says federal and foundation grants fund the supercomputers.
