# Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)

## Citation

The Giving Pledge. "Henry and Susan Samueli." Pledger page with their pledge letter of December 1, 2011. The Giving Pledge, page date not stated. https://givingpledge.org/pledger?pledgerId=278. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The pledger page of Henry and Susan Samueli. It says that they pledged in 2012, and it gives their pledge letter.
- The letter names three areas for their family foundation: education in science, technology, engineering and mathematics (STEM), integrative medicine, and Jewish culture.

## Background

The Giving Pledge describes itself as "a promise by the world's wealthiest philanthropists to give the majority of their wealth to charitable causes in their lifetime or wills." Each pledger has a page with a region, a pledge year and a letter. This page also links news items and events about the Samuelis.

## Key claims

- "We formed our family foundation in 1993 when Broadcom went public." (Pledge letter)
- "We are committed to enhancing engineering education at the university level as well as promoting innovation in STEM education at the kindergarten through high school levels, especially in socially disadvantaged communities." (Pledge letter, area 1)
- "We are committed to promoting wellness and whole body healing by supporting innovative research in the fields of integrative medicine and complementary and alternative modalities." (Pledge letter, area 2)
- "It is also important to our family that perhaps our gifts will encourage others to give as well." (Pledge letter)
- A news item of September 28, 2026 says: "The couple expect to give approximately $185M in 2026 supporting healthcare, education, workforce development, and Jewish organizations in the U.S. and Israel." (In the news)

## Evidence and method

A profile page. It gives the pledgers' own letter, dated December 1, 2011, and the region "North America". The news item links to a magazine story with the headline "Henry and Susan Samueli surpass $1.1B in lifetime giving". The library has not read that story.

## Limits

The page does not name the foundation or give its grants. It does not mention computing or AI. The figure of $1.1 billion is a headline that the page links, not a statement of the Giving Pledge.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the pledge letter dates the Broadcom public offering to 1993: "We formed our family foundation in 1993 when Broadcom went public." UCLA gives a different year: "Since the company’s initial public offering in 1998" ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
