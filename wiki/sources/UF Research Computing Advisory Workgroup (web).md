# UF Research Computing Advisory Workgroup (web)

## Citation

University of Florida (UF), UF Information Technology (UFIT). "Research Computing Advisory Workgroup." Web page, date not stated. https://it.ufl.edu/about/governance/advisory-committees/research-computing-advisory-workgroup/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The Research Computing Advisory Workgroup (RCAW) is one of the topical workgroups in UF's IT governance structure. Its mandate is to advise on a sustainable research computing environment and to help UF researchers compete for allocations.
- A professor of materials science chairs it, and the Director of Research Computing is vice chair. The page lists 25 other members from colleges, institutes and the library.
- A research computing user group (RCUG) of technical experts meets monthly.

## Background

UFIT Research Computing runs the HiPerGator supercomputer ([UF AI HiPerGator page (web)](UF%20AI%20HiPerGator%20page%20%28web%29.md)). This page sits under the UFIT topical advisory workgroups. [UFIT Governance (web)](UFIT%20Governance%20%28web%29.md) lists the RCAW as one of four topical IT advisory workgroups. The page links a vision document and two campus cyberinfrastructure plans, for 2011 to 2015 and for 2015 to 2020.

## Key claims

- "The Research Computing Advisory Workgroup is a topical workgroup in the IT Governance structure." (introduction)
- "The Campus Cyberinfrastructure Plan for 2015-2020 outlines the strategic direction of the Research Computing infrastructure for 2015-2020." (introduction)
- Mandate: "Make recommendations to create a sustainable and appropriate research computing environment." (Mandate)
- Mandate: "Enable UF researchers to compete for resource allocations within UF and from external sponsors." (Mandate)
- Mandate: "Encourage state, national and international partnerships with other research institutions." (Mandate)
- User group: "A research computing user group (RCUG) composed of technical experts in various fields of research who represent the needs and experience from the user community of research computing services meets monthly to ensure that the services meet the needs of the user community." (Subcommittees)
- Link between the two groups: "At least one person with in-depth knowledge of services and technologies shall be a member of both RCAW and RCUG" (Subcommittees)
- Officers: "Richard Hennig, Chair" (Professor, Materials Science and Engineering, College of Engineering) and "Erik Deumens, Vice Chair" (Director, Research Computing). (Membership)

## Evidence and method

An institutional governance page with a mandate, a note on reports and a membership table. The 25 members come from medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, nursing, veterinary medicine, engineering, agriculture (IFAS), physics, public health, the library and AI institutes. Two members are IT directors, at the Interdisciplinary Center for Biotechnology Research and the Clinical and Translational Science Institute (CTSI). The page does not give reports itself. It links a report archive that the library has not read.

## Limits

- The page gives no dates for the membership list or the mandate. The latest plan it names ends in 2020.
- It calls the body both a "workgroup" and the "Research Computing Advisory Committee".
- It says "A new sub-workgroup  has been added", but the chart that shows it is an image, and the page text does not name it.
- One sentence has a missing name: "The  oversees the operation of CTS-IT". The page says that the director of CTS-IT is Christopher Barnes.
- The page does not say who chooses the members, their terms, whether the RCAW has a budget role, or to whom it reports.
- The library has not read the vision document, the two cyberinfrastructure plans or the report archive.
