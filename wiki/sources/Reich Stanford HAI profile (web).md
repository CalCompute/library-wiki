# Reich Stanford HAI profile (web)

## Citation

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI). "Rob Reich." Stanford HAI people page. Date not stated. https://hai.stanford.edu/people/rob-reich. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The Stanford HAI profile of Rob Reich. It gives his titles as "McGregor-Girand Professor of Social Ethics of Science and Technology" and "Associate Director and Senior Fellow, Stanford HAI".
- The page lists a paper by Reich and Deirdre Mulligan for the California Department of Technology. It gives recommendations "on the implementation of its frontier AI transparency requirements".

## Background

Stanford HAI is an institute of Stanford University. The page gives Reich's titles, a biography, his books and a list of HAI items that relate to him. See [Stanford HAI](../entities/Stanford%20HAI.md).

## Key claims

- "McGregor-Girand Professor of Social Ethics of Science and Technology | Associate Director and Senior Fellow, Stanford HAI | Professor, by courtesy, of Education and of Philosophy | Senior Fellow, by courtesy, at Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies" (page heading)
- "He is a co-director of the Center on Philanthropy and Civil Society (publisher of the Stanford Social Innovation Review), and a senior fellow of the Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence." (biography)
- "His scholarship in political theory engages with the work of social scientists and engineers. His newest work is on ethics and AI." (biography)
- "In this paper, HAI Associate Director and Senior Fellow Rob Reich and UC Berkeley School of Information Professor Deirdre K. Mulligan provide concrete recommendations to the California Department of Technology on the implementation of its frontier AI transparency requirements." (Latest Related to Rob Reich: Implementing the Transparency in Frontier AI Act Report for the California Department of Technology Multistakeholder Workshops)
- "This paper examines how nonprofits and frontier AI labs partner to deploy AI for social good and offers recommendations for structuring these partnerships for greater impact." (Latest Related to Rob Reich: Beyond Pilots and PR: What Makes AI-for-Good Partnerships Work)

## Evidence and method

An institutional profile with a biography and a list of related HAI papers, news and events. The list gives dates for most items. The two newest papers show a month and day only, with no year.

## Limits

The page has no date. The biography calls him "professor of political science". The heading gives a named chair in social ethics. The page does not say whether both titles are current. The library has not read the paper for the California Department of Technology.
