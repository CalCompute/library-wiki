# Apache trademark policy (web)

## Citation

The Apache Software Foundation (ASF). "Apache Software Foundation Trademark Policy." Web page, version 1.2, published March 2021. https://www.apache.org/foundation/marks/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The ASF owns the trademarks of all its projects "on behalf of our project communities". Other parties can use the marks only in the ways the policy permits.
- Nominative use, to name ASF software, needs no approval. Use of ASF marks in product branding, company names or domain names generally is not allowed without written approval.
- The VP, Brand Management and a Brand Management Committee set the policy.

## Background

The ASF publishes this policy on apache.org. It gives the rationale for trademark control, the key legal terms, and specific rules for software products, books, domain names and events. The page names the current VP, Brand Management. Version 1.2 is from March 2021.

## Key claims

- Ownership: "The Apache Software Foundation (ASF) owns all ASF-related trademarks, service marks, and graphic logos on behalf of our project communities, and the **names of all ASF projects are trademarks of the ASF**." (Introduction)
- Neutrality: "We are a vendor-neutral organization, and an important part of our brand is that ASF projects are governed independently." (Rationale)
- Nominative use: "Anyone can use ASF trademarks if that use of the trademark is nominative." (Description of Key Trademark Principles)
- Product branding: "In general you may **not** use ASF trademarks in any software product branding" (Using ASF Trademarks in software product branding)
- Probable infringement: the policy lists "Software service offerings that are for anything other than official ASF-distributed software." (list of uses that "are probably infringing")
- Logos: "Unlike ASF's word trademarks (such as "Apache" and "Foo"), our graphic logos are licensed to the public under the Apache License." (Using the Apache Foo or similar project graphic logos)
- Licence link: "This License does not grant permission to use the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the Licensor, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the Work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file." (quoting section 6 of the Apache License)
- Policy body: "The VP, Brand Management and associated Brand Management Committee is the formal body within the ASF responsible for setting policy and answering questions about the use of our logos and trademarks" (Who We Are)

## Evidence and method

This is a policy document of the ASF. It states rules and examples. It gives no data on enforcement or on approval requests.

## Limits

- The policy says that it "is not intended to summarize the complex law of trademarks."
- It links to separate pages for domain names, events, merchandise and a list of marks. This page does not hold those rules.
- It gives no fees or licence terms for an approved commercial use.
