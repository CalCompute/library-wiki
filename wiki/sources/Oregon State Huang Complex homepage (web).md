# Oregon State Huang Complex homepage (web)

## Citation

Oregon State University (OSU). "The Jen-Hsun Huang and Lori Mills Huang Collaborative Innovation Complex." Homepage, date not stated. https://huangcomplex.oregonstate.edu/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The homepage describes the Huang Complex as a place that connects "experts across disciplines with advanced AI, supercomputing and immersive technologies".
- It links pages on the team, purpose, process, governance framework, residency, seed fund projects and partners.
- It quotes Jen-Hsun Huang and Lori Mills Huang on the supercomputer.

## Background

OSU publishes this homepage for the Huang Complex at 2490 NW Monroe Avenue, Corvallis, Oregon. The page is short. Most of its content is a list of links to other pages of the site.

## Key claims

- Heading: "Where Human Expertise and the Real World Shape AI for Impact" (page heading)
- Purpose: "Connecting experts across disciplines with advanced AI, supercomputing and immersive technologies to create sustainable solutions for the most urgent challenges of our time — turning human insight and innovation into trusted tools, public goods and practical solutions." (opening text)
- Donors: "To harness this force, engineering students need access to a supercomputer, a time machine, to accelerate their research." (quotation of Jen-Hsun Huang and Lori Mills Huang)
- Fields: "This new AI supercomputer will enable OSU students and researchers to make very important advances in climate science, oceanography, materials science, robotics and other fields." (same quotation)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional homepage. It states a purpose and lists links. It gives no facts on cost, hardware, schedule or access.

## Limits

- The page gives no numbers.
- The linked pages on governance, residency, seed funds and partners are not part of this source.
