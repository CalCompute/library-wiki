## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the Union of American Physicians and Dentists (UAPD) for State Bargaining Unit 16. Its term is July 1, 2025 to July 1, 2028.
- The State "must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain Unit 16 employees before resorting to the use of private contractors". A joint Contracting Out Committee monitors this rule and recommends contracts to cancel or reduce.
- An employee can grieve "the State’s use of current or future technology" for harassment. Side Letter 5 suspended the return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 for Unit 16 until July 1, 2026. The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 16 is the State's physicians, dentists and podiatrists. Several of its articles apply to California Correctional Health Care Services (CCHCS). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The term of the Agreement shall be July 1, 2025 and remain in full force through July 1, 2028." (Article 17, 17.4.1)
- The rights of the State include the right "to exercise complete control and discretion over its organization and technology of performing its work." (Article 4, paragraph 2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify UAPD of the proposed change prior to its implementation." (Article 17, 17.1.2)
- "Except in extremely unusual or urgent, time-limited circumstances, or under other circumstances where contracting out is recognized or required by law, Federal mandate, or court decisions/orders, the State must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain Unit 16 employees before resorting to the use of private contractors." (Article 20, 20.1.2)
- "The Contracting Out Committee will study and make recommendations in a timely manner regarding which contracts may be cancelled or reduced by the State." (Article 20, 20.2.4)
- "If an employee believes that the State’s use of current or future technology is being used for the purpose of harassment the employee may grieve such action under Article 6." (Article 12, 12.14.1)
- "The State shall not use the log on/off time to the computer or electronic access card entry/exit times of employees as the sole source of attendance reporting or as the sole reason of discipline." (Article 12, 12.14.2)
- "Unit 16 employees who are on-call may be issued and use technologies such as a laptop. The use of such technologies shall be voluntary until such time notice is provided to the union pursuant to Article 17.1." (Article 7, 7.9)
- "For employees in Bargaining Unit (BU) 16, the State and the Union agree that the State’s return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 will be suspended and reinstituted on July 1, 2026." (Side Letter 5)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, leaves, a contracting out committee, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence. Technological change appears only in the Government Code section 19995.2 entry of its list of superseded and incorporated sections, and in Articles 7.9 and 12.14.
- Article 17.1 gives no number of days of notice, unlike the 30 or 60 days in other state MOUs in this library.
- Side Letter 5 "is operative through June 30, 2026". The library holds no later document on the return-to-office requirements for Unit 16.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
