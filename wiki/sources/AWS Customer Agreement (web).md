# AWS Customer Agreement (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS Customer Agreement." Agreement web page, last updated October 29, 2026. https://aws.amazon.com/agreement/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The AWS Customer Agreement is the standard contract for the use of all AWS services. It takes effect when a customer clicks "I Accept" or first uses a service.
- The customer is responsible for its content, its security and backup, and the use of the services by its End Users.
- AWS can suspend use at once for a security risk, a breach or non-payment. Each party can end the agreement for convenience; AWS must give 30 days' notice.

## Background

AWS posts this agreement on its website. The AWS Contracting Party depends on the customer's country. For a country that the table does not list, it is Amazon Web Services, Inc., of Seattle, Washington. The agreement has 12 sections, from AWS Responsibilities to Definitions. It refers to separate documents: Service Terms, Service Level Agreements, the Acceptable Use Policy and the Privacy Notice. The AWS Acceptable Use Policy is in the library as [AWS Acceptable Use Policy 2021-07-01](AWS%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%202021-07-01.md).

## Key claims

- Data location: "You may specify the AWS regions in which Your Content will be stored." (Section 1.4)
- Data access: "We will not access or use Your Content except as necessary to maintain or provide the Services, or as necessary to comply with the law or a binding order of a governmental body." (Section 1.4)
- Notice of changes: "We will provide you at least 12 months’ prior notice before discontinuing a material functionality of a Service that we make generally available to customers and that you are using." (Section 1.5)
- Accounts: "you are responsible for all activities that occur under your account, regardless of whether the activities are authorized by you or undertaken by you, your employees or a third party (including your contractors, agents or End Users)" (Section 2.1)
- End Users: "You are responsible for End Users’ use of Your Content and the Services, and for their compliance with your obligations under this Agreement." (Section 2.5)
- Fees: "We calculate and bill fees and charges monthly." AWS "may increase or add new fees and charges for any existing Services you are using by giving you at least 30 days’ prior notice." (Section 3.1)
- Suspension: AWS may suspend use "immediately upon notice to you" if use "poses a security risk to the Services or any third party". (Section 4.1)
- Termination: "We may terminate this Agreement for any reason by providing you at least 30 days’ advance notice." (Section 5.2(a))
- Retrieval after termination: for 30 days after the Termination Date, AWS "will allow you to retrieve Your Content from the Services only if you have paid all amounts due under this Agreement." (Section 5.3(b))
- Resale: neither the customer nor any End User will "resell the Services or AWS Content." (Section 6.4)
- Liability cap: aggregate liability "WILL NOT EXCEED THE AMOUNTS PAID BY YOU TO AWS UNDER THIS AGREEMENT FOR THE SERVICES THAT GAVE RISE TO THE LIABILITY DURING THE 12 MONTHS BEFORE THE LIABILITY AROSE" (Section 9.2)
- Changes: "We may modify this Agreement (including any Policies) at any time by posting a revised version on the AWS Site or by otherwise notifying you in accordance with Section 11.10." (Section 10)

## Evidence and method

This is the legal text of a commercial contract. It gives the rules on data location, access to content, fees, suspension, termination, resale and liability. It gives no prices. Prices are on the AWS Site. The library read Sections 1 to 10, the dispute and trade compliance parts of Section 11, and the Contracting Party table.

## Limits

- The agreement gives no prices and no service-level figures. Those are in other AWS documents.
- It does not give the terms for public agencies or universities that sign a separate agreement. Section 11.12 covers only use "on behalf of the U.S. Government".
- Section 11.5(a) sends disputes with Amazon Web Services, Inc. to binding arbitration by the American Arbitration Association. The library did not read the Governing Laws table in full.
- The text can change: AWS may modify it "at any time" (Section 10).
