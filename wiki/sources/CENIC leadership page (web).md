# CENIC leadership page (web)

## Citation

CENIC (Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California). "Leadership." Web page, date not stated. https://cenic.org/about/leadership. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- CENIC lists eight executive leaders, with Louis Fox as President and Chief Executive Officer.
- The page also lists its departments and their leaders. One department plans CalREN, which the page calls "CENIC's advanced research and education network".

## Background

CENIC calls itself a nonprofit, and CalREN its research and education network. This page in its About section names its executive staff and describes each department in one or two sentences.

## Key claims

- "CENIC staff are united in our mission to advance education and research statewide by providing a world-class network essential for innovation, collaboration, and economic growth." (Leadership)
- "Louis Fox, President & Chief Executive Officer" (Executive Leaders)
- Network Planning "Leads the strategic design and evolution of CalREN, CENIC's advanced research and education network, and is responsible for enriching and strengthening CENIC's tradition of collaboration with CENIC associates, private industry partners, and research and education partners globally." (Department Leaders)
- Network Operations "manages the Network Operations Center and maintains our technical environment for millions of organizations and individuals across California." (Department Leaders)
- "As a nonprofit, CENIC relies on our accounting and administration teams to minimize our operating costs and pass savings on to our members." (Department Leaders)

## Evidence and method

A staff list. The eight executive leaders are:

- Louis Fox, President and Chief Executive Officer.
- Bill Paik, Treasurer and Chief Financial Officer.
- Tony Nguyen, Executive Vice President and Chief Information Officer.
- Robert Kwon, Vice President and Chief Technology Officer.
- Raul Rincon, Senior Vice President of Strategic Initiatives.
- Kim Lewis, Vice President, Government Relations.
- Sherilyn Shiotsu Evans, Senior Advisor, Access and Equity Initiatives.
- Kao Saefong, Chief of Staff and Corporate Secretary.

The departments are Network Operations, Network Planning, Network Engineering, Security, Platform Engineering, Project Management Office, Administrative Services, Accounting and Finance, Communications, Engagement, Human Resources and Contracting.

## Limits

The page has no date. It does not list the board of directors, the number of staff or the budget. Some departments have no named leader.
