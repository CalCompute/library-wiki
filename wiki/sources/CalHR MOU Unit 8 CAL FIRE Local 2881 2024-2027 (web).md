## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and CAL FIRE Local 2881 for State Bargaining Unit 8, firefighters. Its Duration section gives the term as July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2027.
- The rights of the State and the department include the right "to contract and subcontract existing and future work". All subcontracting stays within the constitution and the Government Code.
- The State indentures new firefighters and fire apparatus engineers as apprentices, under a joint apprenticeship committee. The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 8 is the State's firefighters. Its articles apply mainly to the California Department of Forestry and Fire Protection (CAL FIRE). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The term of this agreement is from July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2027." (Article 16, 16.4.1)
- The State and the department keep the rights to determine "the methods, processes and equipment to be employed" and "to contract and subcontract existing and future work". (Article 4, 4.1.1)
- "All subcontracting will be within existing constitutional provisions, and applicable government code sections." (Article 5, 5.8.2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify CAL FIRE Local 2881 of the proposed change 30 days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 16, 16.1)
- "New permanent full-time appointments to the classes of Fire Apparatus Engineer, Fire Apparatus Engineer (Paramedic), Heavy Fire Equipment Operator, permanent full time Fire Fighter II, and Fire Fighter II (Paramedic) will continue to be indentured as Apprentices under the California Firefighter Joint Apprenticeship Committee" (Article 18, 18.1.1)
- "Prior to implementation of any apprenticeship modifications initiated by Sub-JAC, the Employer will meet and confer with CAL FIRE Local 2881 over impacts." (Article 18, 18.1.11)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, staffing rules, leaves, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the title of the MOU gives "Effective January 1, 2024 through June 30, 2027", and its Duration section (16.4.1) gives "from July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2027". Both are in [this source](CalHR%20MOU%20Unit%208%20CAL%20FIRE%20Local%202881%202024-2027%20%28web%29.md).

- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence or on technological change. It has no list of Government Code sections with section 19995.2.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
