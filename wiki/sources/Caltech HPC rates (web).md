# Caltech HPC rates (web)

## Citation

California Institute of Technology (Caltech), Resnick High Performance Computing Center. "Rates." Center documentation, last updated Nov. 19, 2026. https://www.hpc.caltech.edu/docs/rates.html. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Caltech HPC rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- The Caltech Resnick High Performance Computing Center charges for compute units. The fee for each unit falls from $0.012 to $0.006 as a group's spend in the fiscal year goes up.
- One CPU core-hour is 1 unit. One H100 GPU-hour is 120 units, and one H200 GPU-hour is 156 units.
- The first 20 TB of group storage is free. Each additional TB is $8.00 a month.

## Background

The Resnick High Performance Computing Center runs the shared research cluster of Caltech. This page is its price list. It points to an online cost calculator, which this library does not hold. The tiers reset every fiscal year.

## Key claims

- "The Resnick High Performance Computing Center uses a tiered pricing model that resets every fiscal year. Total rates are broken into compute core hours and additional storage costs." (Rates)
- "The fee per compute unit drops as your aggregate spend rises within the fiscal year" (Compute Pricing)
- The tiers are "≤ $6,400 | $0.012", "$6,401 – $24,000 | $0.007" and "> $24,000 | $0.006" for each compute unit. (Compute Pricing table)
- "1 CPU core-hour | 1 unit", "1 L40S hour | 61 units", "1 H100 hour | 120 units", "1 H200 hour | 156 units" (Compute Unit Definitions)
- "1 P100 hour | 10 units" and "1 V100 hour | 10 units" (Compute Unit Definitions)
- "First 20 TB on `/resnick/groups/<groupname>` | **Free**" and "Additional storage | $8.00/TB/month" (Storage Pricing)

## Evidence and method

A facility price list of about 200 words of content, with long site navigation. It gives the unit fees, the unit value of each resource and the storage price. It does not give a price for each GPU-hour. At the three unit fees, one H100 hour is $1.44, $0.84 or $0.72. These three figures are this page's multiplication of the listed values, not figures that Caltech prints.

## Limits

- The page does not say who can buy time, or whether users outside Caltech can.
- It does not say how the unit fees were set or what costs they recover.
- It gives no count of nodes or GPUs. The center's resources page, which this library does not hold, lists them.
