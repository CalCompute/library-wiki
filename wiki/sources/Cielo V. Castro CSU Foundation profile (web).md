# Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)

## Citation

California State University (CSU). "Cielo V. Castro." CSU Foundation, Meet the Board and Staff, profile page, date not stated. https://www.calstate.edu/impact-of-the-csu/support-the-csu/foundation/meet-the-board-staff/Pages/Cielo-V-Castro.aspx. Read 2026-11-13.

## Bottom line

- Cielo V. Castro is the Chief Impact Officer at the California Community Foundation (CCF), in Los Angeles County. She leads four teams there, Grantmaking and Policy & Advocacy among them.
- The CSU Foundation lists her on its Meet the Board and Staff pages. She is also Board Chair of the Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles.

## Background

The CSU website has a page for each person on the CSU Foundation's board and staff. This is the page for Cielo V. Castro. It gives her current post, her past posts and her degrees.

## Key claims

- "Cielo V. Castro currently serves as the Chief Impact Officer at the California Community Foundation (CCF), where she leads the Foundation's integrated strategy toward A Good Life for All in Los Angeles County." (Page Content)
- "At CCF, Castro was the principal architect of their new 2025–2035 Strategic Framework" (Page Content)
- "She now leads four teams—Community Investment & Engagement, Policy & Advocacy, Impact & Outcomes, and Grantmaking—deploying every tool at CCF's disposal on the region's most urgent challenges" (Page Content)
- "Prior to CCF, Castro led Fairplex's transformation into a county-designated community benefit organization" (Page Content)
- "Castro holds a B.S. from Boston University and an M.P.A. from the Harvard Kennedy School." (Page Content)
- "Castro serves as Board Chair of the Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles" (Page Content)

## Evidence and method

A profile on the CSU Foundation's board and staff pages. It gives no dates of appointment and no publications.

## Limits

The page does not say if she is a board member or a staff member of the CSU Foundation, or when she joined. It has no date. It names past work at the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, NALEO and the National League of Cities, but gives no post titles for them.
