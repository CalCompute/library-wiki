# Maryland Chapter 633 of 2026 SB 597 (web)

## Citation

Maryland General Assembly. Chapter 633 of the Laws of Maryland 2026 (Senate Bill 597), "Higher Education – Maryland Artificial Intelligence Partnership." Enrolled act, approved by the Governor May 26, 2026; effective July 1, 2026. https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/2026RS/Chapters_noln/CH_633_sb0597e.pdf. ## Bottom line

- The Maryland law puts a Maryland Artificial Intelligence Partnership in the University System of Maryland. The Partnership must write a strategic plan by December 1, 2026, and set up technology extension hubs in three regions.
- It also makes an AI Public Service Fellowship that matches students with state agencies, and an AI Incubation Lab that helps state agencies with prototypes. It adds AI to two scholarship programs.

## Background

Maryland's General Assembly passed Senate Bill 597 in its 2026 session. The act changes the Education Article and the State Finance and Procurement Article of the Annotated Code of Maryland. Its preamble cites the federal AI Action Plan. The Center for Equitable Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Systems at Morgan State University becomes a statewide resource for state agencies.

## Key claims

- "THERE IS A MARYLAND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PARTNERSHIP IN THE UNIVERSITY SYSTEM OF MARYLAND." (Education § 12–124(B)(1))
- "THE PURPOSE OF THE PARTNERSHIP IS TO PROVIDE A SINGLE NEXUS FOR STAKEHOLDERS IN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE INITIATIVES TO ADVANCE THE INNOVATION, ECONOMIC GROWTH, WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT, AND PUBLIC GOOD ACROSS THE STATE." (§ 12–124(B)(2))
- "ON OR BEFORE DECEMBER 1, 2026, THE PARTNERSHIP SHALL DEVELOP A STRATEGIC PLAN." (§ 12–124(C)(1)(I))
- Each hub shall "FACILITATE REMOTE, CLOUD–BASED ACCESS TO CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURE, INCLUDING LEVERAGING THE SIGNIFICANT COMPUTING POWER OF COLLEGES AND UNIVERSITIES" (§ 12–124(D)(3)(III))
- "THE PURPOSE OF THE FELLOWSHIP IS TO MATCH STUDENTS ENROLLED IN PUBLIC INSTITUTIONS OF HIGHER EDUCATION WITH STATE AGENCIES TO ADDRESS ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE PROJECTS." (§ 12–125(C))
- "THE PURPOSE OF THE LAB IS TO ASSIST STATE AGENCIES TO ACCELERATE THE RESPONSIBLE USE OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE BY HELPING STATE AGENCIES TO DISCOVER, DEVELOP, AND APPLY PROTOTYPES AND MANAGE RISK OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SOLUTIONS." (§ 12–126(B)(2))
- "WHEREAS, The federal AI Action Plan calls for expanding access to critical computing resources, removing barriers to innovation, and strengthening U.S. leadership in artificial intelligence research and development" (Preamble)

## Evidence and method

A state statute in its enrolled form. It sets up bodies, duties and dates. It gives no appropriation amounts.

## Limits

The enrolled PDF marks amendments with strike-through and underline. The Markdown copy loses those marks, so some passages show the old and the new words side by side, for example two hub dates. Read the PDF for the enacted text. The act does not fund the Partnership, the hubs or the Lab with a stated amount. The Lab exists subject to a memorandum of understanding with the Department of Budget and Management.
