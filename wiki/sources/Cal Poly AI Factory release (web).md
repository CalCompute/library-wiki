# Cal Poly AI Factory release (web)

## Citation

Cal Poly University Communications and Marketing. "Cal Poly to Open Advanced AI Factory with NVIDIA Technology." Press release, November 18, 2025. https://ucm.calpoly.edu/news/cal-poly-open-advanced-ai-factory-nvidia-technology. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 Cal Poly AI Factory release.md`.

## Bottom line

- California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo (Cal Poly) announced an AI Factory with four NVIDIA DGX B200 systems. The Noyce School of Applied Computing funds it with $3 million.
- Cal Poly expected it to be fully operational by January 2026. It is for students and faculty, and regional partners can work with them to use it.

## Background

Cal Poly announced the AI Factory at its first AI Convening. Mark III Systems is the build partner. The release quotes the provost, the Noyce School director and the interim dean of engineering.

## Key claims

The system (opening paragraphs):

- "Powered by an NVIDIA DGX B200 BasePOD with DGX B200 systems and funded through a $3 million investment from The Noyce School of Applied Computing, the system will align Cal Poly with the technology used in NVIDIA's data centers and is expected to be fully operational by January 2026."
- "The Cal Poly AI Factory, to be built in collaboration with Mark III Systems, will feature four NVIDIA DGX B200 systems, along with high-performance storage, networking and the NVIDIA AI software stack."
- "It also gives the university something commercially available AI platforms cannot: full ownership of the data, models and applications developed on it."

Cost to users (later paragraphs):

- "Comparable GPU capacity through cloud services can cost individual users thousands of dollars in cloud fees. By operating on-premises, Cal Poly ensures students across all majors, not only computer science, can gain experience building AI systems instead of relying solely on commercial tools."

Regional partners (later paragraphs):

- "The AI Factory will support collaborations across the Central Coast in areas such as agriculture, aerospace, biotechnology and advanced manufacturing. Regional businesses and organizations may be able to leverage the system's computing power by working with Cal Poly faculty and students"

## Evidence and method

This is a press release at announcement. It gives the hardware, the funder, the build partner and the expected date. Performance claims are the vendor's ("up to three times the training performance").

## Limits

- The release does not say how users get time on the system or what it costs them.
- It does not give operating costs.
- The unveiling release, [Cal Poly AI Factory unveiling release (web)](Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20unveiling%20release%20%28web%29.md), reports a formal unveiling on Oct. 22, 2026, not January 2026.
