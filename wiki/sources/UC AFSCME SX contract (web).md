# UC AFSCME SX contract (web)

## Citation

University of California and American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees (AFSCME) Local 3299. Agreement for the Service (SX) unit, Jan. 31, 2020 to Oct. 31, 2024. University of California, UCnet. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/sx/docs/sx_00_complete-contract.pdf. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-20 UC AFSCME SX contract.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-20%20UC%20AFSCME%20SX%20contract.md) (original: `.pdf` beside it). Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and AFSCME Local 3299 for the Service (SX) unit. Its term ran from Jan. 31, 2020 to no earlier than Oct. 31, 2024. Without timely notice, it stays in effect from year to year.
- Its contracting out article has the same structure as the EX agreement: a general prohibition, six listed cases and equivalent wages and benefits. Its covered services are service work, for example food, laundry, grounds and building maintenance.
- Professional development and educational leave can be used for career-enhancing education such as computer skills.

## Background

AFSCME Local 3299 represents two UC units: SX and the Patient Care Technical (EX) unit ([UC AFSCME EX contract (web)](UC%20AFSCME%20EX%20contract%20%28web%29.md)). UC posts the SX agreement on its [UC SX contract page (web)](UC%20SX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "There shall be a general prohibition on contracting for services and functions that can be performed by University staff." (Article 5, A.1)
- "cleaning, custodial, janitorial, or housekeeping services; food services; laundry services; grounds keeping; building maintenance (excluding skilled crafts); transportation and parking services; and security services." (Article 5, A.6, Covered Services)
- "Contracting out should be used sparingly and treated as an option of last resort to address temporary needs, not as a means to replace employees with contractors." (Article 5, C)
- "Examples of educational opportunities for which Professional Development and Education Leave would be appropriate include, but are not limited to, the following: English as a Second Language, Interviewing Skills, Computer Skills, Resume Writing Skills, and Supervisory/Leadership Skills." (Article 7, B.2)
- "The terms and conditions of this Agreement shall be in full force effective one minute past midnight on January 31, 2020 and terminating at 11:59 p.m. no earlier than October 31, 2024" (Article 9, A)
- "to discontinue, relocate or subcontract all or any portion of any operation;" (Article 18, B.7)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The term ended no earlier than Oct. 31, 2024. The library holds no successor agreement and not the implementation package that the SX contract page links.
- The agreement has no article on artificial intelligence.
