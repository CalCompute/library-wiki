# CA.gov privacy policy (web)

## Citation

California Department of Technology, for the State of California. "Privacy Policy." CA.gov, date not stated. https://www.ca.gov/privacy-policy/. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CA.gov privacy policy.html`.

## Bottom line

- The State's CA.gov website collects no personal information except statistical information, unless a user gives it by email, form or survey.
- The policy follows the Information Practices Act, the Public Records Act and Government Code sections 11015.5 and 11019.9. Information that a user gives can become a public record.
- Users can ask to see, correct or delete their personal information, or to have electronically collected data discarded.

## Background

The California Department of Technology (CDT) runs CA.gov. This policy applies only to the CA.gov website. CDT has its own policy for its own site, the [CDT privacy policy (web)](CDT%20privacy%20policy%20%28web%29.md), which also names CA.gov. See [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md).

## Key claims

- "It is the policy of the State to limit the collection and safeguard the privacy of Personal Information and Electronically Collected Personal Information (as defined below) that may be collected through CA.gov." (Introduction)
- "CA.gov’s information management practices conform to the requirements of the Information Practices Act (Civ. Code §§1798 et seq.), the Public Records Act (Govt. Code §§7920.000 et seq.), Government Code sections 11015.5 and 11019.9, and other applicable state and federal laws" (Introduction)
- "This information may become a public record once it is provided." (Introduction)
- "CA.gov website will not collect any Personal Information about you other than statistical information that can be used to make the website more effective." (Collection and Management of Personal Information)
- "the CA.gov website is anonymizing that information and only part of device IP addresses are being used." (Item 3, Google Analytics)
- "Electronically Collected Personal Information about users will not be distributed or sold to any third party without prior written permission from the user except as set forth in Government Code section 11015.5(b)." (Item 8)

## Evidence and method

A state website privacy policy of about 1,900 words. It gives definitions from the Civil Code and the Government Code, 11 practices, and a list of the data collected on each visit.

## Limits

The policy gives no date and no retention period. It covers the CA.gov website only, not other state systems or services.
