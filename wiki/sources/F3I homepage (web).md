# F3I homepage (web)

## Citation

F3 Innovate (F3i). "F3i: Critical Resources for Agriculture's Next Era." Home page, date not stated. https://f3i.org/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The home page of F3 Innovate, a Fresno nonprofit, says that it builds infrastructure for food production, new technologies and rural economies.
- It has three programs: talent development, a commercialization engine and a catalyst fund. The page is short and has little detail.

## Background

This is the home page of the F3 Innovate website. The page calls the organisation "F3i". The footer says "© 2026 F3 Innovate, a 501c(3) Nonprofit". The page has a headline, four regional figures, three program panels, a list of news items and a subscription link.

## Key claims

- "We’re building the infrastructure to secure food production, scale new technologies, and reinvest in rural economies." (headline)
- "A single stretch of farmland produces one quarter of the nation’s food." (headline)
- "Building the workforce for the next generation of secure food production." (program panels)
- "F3i connects public programs, commercial partners, and operational resources to drive coordinated deployment across the world’s most productive agricultural region." (program panels)

## Evidence and method

A marketing home page. It gives four figures: "5M+", ">350", "$70B" and "13.6k". It gives four labels: "Acres in Production", "Commodities Grown", "Farming Enterprises" and "Annual Value Created". The page gives no source for the figures. The news list names 2025 innovation grants to five companies, a partnership with Queensland’s Department of Primary Industries and a 4-part series on NPR’s *Marketplace*.

## Limits

The Markdown copy separates the figures from their labels, so it does not show which figure goes with which label. The page does not mention computing, apart from a banner link to the F3i Supercomputing Center.
