# GCS Council (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing e.V. (GCS). "GCS Council." Web page, date not stated. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us/organization/gcs-council. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The GCS Council is the deliberative body of the Gauss Centre for Supercomputing. It decides on all matters of the association.
- It has nine members. Three institutions send representatives. The other six are the three centre directors and three scientists with posts on the centres' scientific bodies.

## Background

GCS is a registered association. It joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)). They are the High Performance Computing Center Stuttgart (HLRS), the Jülich Supercomputing Centre (JSC) and the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre (LRZ). This page lists the current Council members with their posts and addresses. [GCS organization (web)](GCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md) describes the Council beside the other GCS bodies.

## Key claims

- "The GCS Council is the deliberative body of GCS and decides on all matters of the association. It consists of nine members in total." (GCS Council)
- "Bayerische Akademie der Wissenschaften, represented by Prof. Dr.-Ing. Arndt Bode" (member 1). Rendering by the library: the Bavarian Academy of Sciences.
- "Forschungszentrum Jülich GmbH, represented by" "Prof. Dr. Astrid Lambrecht, Chairwoman of the Board of Directors" and "Dr. Stephanie Bauer, Vice Chairwoman of the Board of Directors" (member 2)
- "Universität Stuttgart, represented by" "Anna Steiger (Chancellor)" and "Stephan Bissinger (Vice Chancellor)" (member 3)
- "Prof. Dr. Peter Bastian, Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing, Heidelberg University, Chairman of the SuperMUC Steering Committee at LRZ" (member 5)
- "Prof. Dr. Thomas Ludwig" "Chairman of the HLRS Steering Committee, Director of German Climate Computing Centre (DKRZ) and professor at the University Hamburg" (member 8)

## Evidence and method

A membership list on the publisher's own website. It gives nine numbered entries with each member's post and postal address. The other entries are:

- Prof. Dr. Marcus Müller, University of Göttingen, Vice Chairman of the NIC Scientific Council at the John von Neumann Institute for Computing (NIC) (member 4);
- Prof. Dr. Dieter Kranzlmüller, Chairman of the Board of Directors at LRZ (member 6);
- Prof. Dr. Dr. Thomas Lippert, Director of JSC (member 7);
- Prof. Dr.-Ing. Michael M. Resch, Director of HLRS (member 9).

The library does not copy the telephone numbers and email addresses on the page.

## Limits

- The page has no date, so the list can be out of date.
- It does not give the terms of office, the voting rules or how often the Council meets.
- Two of the nine entries name two representatives each, so the page names eleven people for nine seats. It does not say how those pairs vote.
- [GCS organization (web)](GCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md) says the Council "is composed of institutional and private members", and that private members are "representatives of the three GCS HPC centres". This page does not label each member as institutional or private.
