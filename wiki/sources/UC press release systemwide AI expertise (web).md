# UC press release systemwide AI expertise (web)

## Citation

University of California Office of the President (UCOP). "University of California leverages systemwide expertise to advance purposeful, human-centered uses for AI." Press release, September 16, 2026. https://www.universityofcalifornia.edu/press-room/university-california-leverages-systemwide-expertise-advance-purposeful-human-centered. ## Bottom line

- UCOP's release on the first report of the UC AI Steering Committee to the UC Board of Regents in September 2026.
- The committee has five working groups. UC has AI research awards and computing credits from Amazon and Google. The committee seeks systemwide approaches so that UC "is not reliant on a single vendor".

## Background

The release reports the Steering Committee's presentation at the September 2026 Regents meeting. The Regents item is [UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16](UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md). The release quotes UC President James B. Milliken, Co-Chair Van Williams, Professor Giovanni Vigna and Professor Rita Raley. Vigna and Raley lead two of the working groups. Both are at UC Santa Barbara.

## Key claims

- "Formed by President Milliken in May of 2026, the University of California’s AI steering committee was established to leverage the university’s extraordinary scale of 10 campuses, six academic health centers, and three national labs" (paragraph 2)
- The five working groups cover "health and longevity; learning and work; discovery and innovation; protecting humans and humanity; and AI for the public good." (paragraph 2)
- UC was "the first university to address artificial intelligence explicitly in a Digital Risk Appetite statement" (paragraph 5)
- "These include research awards and computing credits from Amazon and Google, the latter of which has granted more than $32.7 million across 61 funded projects" (paragraph 6)
- "The Steering Committee is seeking coordinated, systemwide approaches with AI developers and cloud providers to ensure UC is not reliant on a single vendor." (paragraph 7)
- "The committee is also exploring the creation of a systemwide learning platform to support the responsible adoption of AI tools and use of resources." (paragraph 7)
- Raley's working group "will provide systemwide recommendations on what each student needs to know about AI." (paragraph 10)

## Evidence and method

A university press release. It summarizes a presentation and quotes the people who gave it. It gives one figure, the Google total. It names the risks the report addressed: "security breaches, fragmented regulation, and increased energy and utility consumption". Its example of AI for public good is a brain-computer interface from UC Berkeley and UC San Francisco that restored speech to a stroke patient.

## Limits

- The release gives no figure for the Amazon credits. [UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16](UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md) gives $14 million in AWS credits (p. 6).
- It does not describe the Sovereign AI Cloud that the Regents item proposes.
- In one quotation, President Milliken calls the committee "the task force".
- It does not mention CalCompute or the Report.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this release says President Milliken formed the Steering Committee "in May of 2026". [UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16](UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md) says he formed it in March 2026 (pp. 1 to 2).
