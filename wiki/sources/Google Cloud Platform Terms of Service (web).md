# Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (web)

## Citation

Google. "Google Cloud Terms of Service." Terms web page, last modified October 12, 2026. https://cloud.google.com/terms. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The Google Cloud Terms of Service govern online use of Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Google Workspace, SecOps, Looker (original) and Cloud Identity services. A customer that signed an offline version uses its offline terms.
- Google processes Customer Data only under the Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Google gives 12 months' notice before it discontinues a service.
- For a U.S. city, county or state government entity, the agreement "will be silent regarding governing law and venue."
- For other non-federal entities, California law and the courts of Santa Clara County apply.

## Background

Google posts these terms on its Cloud website, with translations into nine languages. "Google" has the meaning given on a separate Google entity page. The terms have 15 sections, from Provision of the Services to Regional Modifications. They refer to separate URL Terms, among them the Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), the Service Specific Terms and the Cloud Data Processing Addendum. The AUP is in the library as [Google Cloud Acceptable Use Policy 2026-10-12](Google%20Cloud%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%202026-10-12.md).

## Key claims

- Updates to the terms: for GCP Services, "material updates to this Agreement will become effective 30 days after they are posted." (Section 1.4(b)(i))
- Discontinuation: "Google will notify Customer at least 12 months before: (i) discontinuing any Service (or associated material functionality) unless Google replaces such discontinued Service or functionality with a materially similar Service or functionality" (Section 1.4(e))
- Credits: "Google may also, regardless of any such risk, require all customers to purchase credits to use certain Services." (Section 2.1(a))
- Late payment: late payments "may bear interest at the rate of 1.5% per month (or the highest rate permitted by law, if less)" (Section 2.4)
- Restrictions: Customer will not use the Services or any AI Agent "for High Risk Activities", "in violation of the AUP", "to engage in cryptocurrency mining without Google's prior written approval" or "to transmit, store, or process health information subject to United States HIPAA regulations except as permitted by an executed HIPAA BAA." (Section 3.3)
- AUP suspension: "If Customer fails to correct the violation within 24 hours of Google's request, then Google may Suspend all or part of Customer's use of the Services until the violation is corrected." (Section 4.1)
- Generative AI: "Google uses automated safety tools to detect abuse of Generative AI Services." (Section 4.3)
- Customer Data: "Google will only access, use, and otherwise process Customer Data in accordance with the Cloud Data Processing Addendum and will not access, use, or process Customer Data for any other purpose." (Section 5.2)
- Inactivity: Google may end GCP Services to a Project "upon 30 days' advance notice if, for a period of 60 days" the Project has no access or network activity and no Fees. (Section 8.4)
- Liability cap: total liability "is limited to the Fees Customer paid for such Services during the 12 month period before the event giving rise to Liability, except Google’s total aggregate Liability for damages arising out of or related to Services or Software provided free of charge is limited to $5,000." (Section 12.2)
- State and local government: "If Customer is a U.S. city, county, or state government entity, then this Agreement will be silent regarding governing law and venue." (Section 14.12(a))

## Evidence and method

This is the legal text of a commercial contract. The copy is about 14,800 words. The library read the opening, Sections 1 to 5, Section 8, Section 12 and Section 14.12. The library did not read Sections 6, 7, 9 to 11, 13, the rest of Section 14, or Section 15 in full.

## Limits

- The terms give no prices.
- They do not apply to Starter Tier resources, to customers in the exempt categories, or to customers with offline terms.
- The Service Specific Terms, the AUP and the Cloud Data Processing Addendum are separate documents. This page does not cover them.
