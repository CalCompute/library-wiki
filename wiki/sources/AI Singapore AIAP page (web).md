# AI Singapore AIAP page (web)

## Citation

AI Singapore. "AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP)." Program page, date not stated. https://aisingapore.org/innovation/aiap/. Read 2026-10-29.

## Bottom line

- AI Singapore runs a paid AI apprenticeship of six or nine months, with a real industry project.

## Key claims

- "over 90% of graduates placed in AI roles within 6 months."
- "Receive a monthly stipend of SGD 4,000 while you deep-skill in AI".
- Three tracks, including AI Engineer and Forward Deployed Engineer.

## Evidence and method

The program's own page.

## Limits

Marketing text. The placement figure has no method stated.
