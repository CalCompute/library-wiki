# Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)

## Citation

California State Assembly, Chief Clerk. "Appendix to Assembly Journal: Record of State and Local Agency Reports Received Pursuant to Government Code Section 9795, March 2026." California Legislature, 2025–26 Regular Session, pp. 135 to 146. https://clerk.assembly.ca.gov/sites/clerk.assembly.ca.gov/files/2026_agencyreports_mar.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Assembly Journal appendix lists each agency report that the Chief Clerk received in March 2026 under Government Code section 9795.
- Each entry gives the report title, its legal authority, its date, the agency that sent it and the committee that received it. Reports from the California Department of Technology (CDT) and the Office of Data and Innovation (ODI) are among them.

## Background

The Chief Clerk of the Assembly publishes this record each month as an appendix to the Assembly Journal. The Legislative Data Center keeps the same information on agencyreports.ca.gov. The March 2026 record has about 80 entries from state agencies, universities, courts and counties. It does not list a CalCompute report.

## Key claims

- "The following state and local agency reports information website is maintained by the Legislative Data Center: agencyreports.ca.gov" (front matter, before p. 135; spaces restored from the PDF text)
- "An agency report dated March 6, 2026, entitled: Statewide Information Technology Annual Report, 2024–25, was received from the Department of Technology and transmitted to the Committee on Budget." (p. 137)
- "An agency report dated March 6, 2026, entitled: Envision 2026: California’s Technology Future, Year 3 Roadmap, was received from the Department of Technology and transmitted to the Committees on Governmental Organization, Communications and Conveyance, and Privacy and Consumer Protection." (p. 137)
- "An agency report dated March 5, 2026, entitled: Office of Data and Innovation’s Statewide Data Strategy Report, was received from the Office of Data and Innovation and transmitted to the Committee on Budget." (p. 137)
- "An agency report dated March 4, 2026, entitled: California Jobs First: Regional Investment Initiative Annual Report, was received from the Employment Development Department and transmitted to the Committee on Public Employment and Retirement." (p. 135)

## Evidence and method

An official legislative record. Each entry follows one fixed form: title, the statute or budget item that requires the report, date, sending agency and receiving committee. It records receipt only. It gives no content from the reports.

## Limits

The record covers March 2026 only. It does not give the text of any report or a link to it. The PDF text loses many spaces between words; the quotations above restore them. It does not mention CalCompute or § 11546.8.
