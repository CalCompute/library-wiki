## Bottom line

- Sacramento State lists four research tools: EndNote, NVivo, Qualtrics and TIDE.
- TIDE is part of the National Research Platform (NRP) Nautilus cluster. The page gives it 18 GPU nodes, 6 CPU nodes and a managed JupyterHub.

## Background

This is a page of Information Resources and Technology at Sacramento State, a California State University (CSU) campus. It lists software and computing for research, with a short paragraph on each.

## Key claims

TIDE ("TIDE: High Performance Computing (HPC) Project"):

- "Part of the National Research Platform Nautilus hyper-cluster, TIDE provides high-performance computing (HPC) resources including:"
- "18 GPU nodes and 6 CPU nodes to accelerate data-intensive research"
- "A managed JupyterHub instance featuring several pre-built software containers with many common packages common to artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science"
- "TIDE serves as a hub for cutting-edge research, collaboration, and skill development, benefiting both students and faculty."

Software ("EndNote", "Qualtrics"):

- "Students, faculty and staff have access to download and install EndNote."
- "Qualtrics is an online survey tool which is available to support research, instruction, and administration."

## Evidence and method

This is a campus service page. It gives one paragraph and two figures on TIDE. It gives no costs, dates or access route for TIDE.

## Limits

- The page does not say where the TIDE nodes are, who runs them or how to get access.
- It does not give the GPU type or count.
- The page has no date.
