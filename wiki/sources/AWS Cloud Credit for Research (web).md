# AWS Cloud Credit for Research (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS Cloud Credit for Research." Program page, date not stated. https://aws.amazon.com/research-credits/. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- AWS gives promotional credit to faculty, research staff and graduate students. It reviews proposals on a rolling basis, with "Typical review cycles are 90 to 120 days."
- The award size follows the need that the application documents. Student awards stop at $5,000. "Faculty and Staff awards are not capped."
- This page shows only the free credit tier. It does not show a subsidized tier.

## Background

The AWS Public Sector team runs the program. The page links to FAQs, Terms and Conditions and previous recipients.

## Key claims

- Purpose: the program "supports researchers who seek to" (program summary):
  - "Build cloud-hosted publicly available science-as-a-service applications, software, or tools to facilitate their future research and the research of their community"
  - "Perform proof of concept or benchmark tests evaluating the efficacy of moving research workloads or open data sets to the cloud"
  - "Train a broader community on the use of cloud for research workloads via workshops or tutorials"
- Eligibility (Eligibility):
  - "Full-time faculty at an accredited research institution"
  - "Full-time research staff at an accredited research institution"
  - "Graduate, post-graduate, or PhD students enrolled at an accredited research institution"
- Review: "Amazon Web Services (AWS) will evaluate academic research support proposals on a rolling basis throughout the year. Typical review cycles are 90 to 120 days." (Application process)
- "Due to the heavy volume of applications received, expedited reviews are not possible." (Application process)
- Size: "Awarded amounts will vary depending on the research proposal and service usage requirements documented in the application." (Application process)
- "Student awards will be up to a maximum of $5,000.00. Faculty and Staff awards are not capped." (Application process)
- Form: "Awards are in the form of AWS Promotional Credit applicable to AWS services. Amazon EC2 usage supported by AWS Promotional Credit will be for on-demand and spot instances only." (Application process)
- Duration: "the AWS Promotional Credit will be good for the entirety of the one or until the credit has been fully utilized." (Application process; the page text has a word missing after "one".)
- Application: an "Apply now" link goes to a form at pages.awscloud.com. (Page header)

## Evidence and method

The program's own page. It states the rules in short form. It defers detail to an FAQ and to the AWS Promotional Credit terms.

## Limits

- The page does not state the review criteria or who reviews.
- The page does not state the form's questions.
- The page does not state reporting or acknowledgement duties.
- The page does not state renewal. It does not say whether a person can apply twice.
- The page does not state usage rules beyond the EC2 limit. The Terms and Conditions page holds them.
- The page names no subsidized or paid tier for research. The page header links to "AWS Free Tier" and "AWS Offers", which are general AWS offers.
