# Stanford Sherlock facts (web)

## Citation

Stanford Research Computing. "Sherlock facts." Sherlock documentation, "as of November 2026". https://www.sherlock.stanford.edu/docs/tech/facts/. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Stanford Sherlock facts.md`.

## Bottom line

- Sherlock is the shared high performance computing (HPC) cluster of Stanford. In November 2026 it had 9,236 user accounts in 1,364 PI groups, and 228 owner groups.
- It had 1,982 compute nodes, 74,460 CPU cores and 1,248 graphics processing units (GPUs), with 18.72 petaFLOPS at 64-bit precision (FP64).
- It used 1,055.98 kW of power. Its storage was 14.6 PB of scratch and 93.9 PB of long-term research storage.

## Background

Stanford Research Computing publishes the Sherlock documentation. This page is a list of figures in eight groups: users, interfaces, computing, hardware, energy, storage, networking and the scheduler. A note at the top dates the figures to November 2026.

## Key claims

- "9,236 user accounts", "1,364 PI groups from all Stanford's seven Schools, SLAC, Stanford Institutes, etc." and "228 owner groups". (Users)
- "18.72 PFLOPs (FP64) 57.84 (FP32) PFLOPs", "74,460 CPU cores" and "1,248 GPUs 5 GPU generations (14 GPU models)". (Computing)
- "1,982 compute nodes 24 server models (from 3 different manufacturers)" in "57 racks". (Hardware)
- "1,055.98 kW total power usage". (Energy)
- "14.6 PB $SCRATCH parallel, distributed filesystem, delivering over 600 GB/s of I/O bandwidth" and "93.9 PB $OAK long term research data storage". (Storage)
- "139 Infiniband switches across 3 Infiniband fabrics (EDR, HDR, NDR)". (Networking)
- "66,520 CPU.hours/day over 7 years of computing in a single day". (Scheduler)
- "$4,444,657 /month to run the same workload on t2.large on-demand cloud instances". (Scheduler)

## Evidence and method

A documentation page of about 600 words, most of it site navigation. It gives counts with no method. The cloud figure names an instance type, "t2.large", but not the provider, the price or the date of the price.

## Limits

The page gives no cost of operation, no budget and no funding source. It does not give the method of the cloud figure. It does not split the GPU count by model. For the Sherlock condo model and its fees, see [Stanford Research Computing facilities (web)](Stanford%20Research%20Computing%20facilities%20%28web%29.md).
