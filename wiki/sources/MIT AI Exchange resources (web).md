# MIT AI Exchange resources (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) AI Exchange. "Resources." Web page, date not stated. https://aiexchange.mit.edu/resources/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- A list of AI tools, guidance and reading for the MIT community. The one AI tool on the list is MIT Parley, "MIT's generative AI service".
- The list also links Institute guidance on AI use with MIT data, course design guidance and an MIT committee report on AI in teaching.

## Background

The page is part of the MIT AI Exchange site. It groups links by type: AI tools, guidance and policy, teaching and course design, reports, and newsletters. Most links go to other MIT sites.

## Key claims

- "MIT's generative AI service, with access to approved AI models." (AI tools: MIT Parley)
- "Institute guidance on using AI tools responsibly with MIT data and systems." (Guidance and policy: IS&T guidance on AI use)
- "The MIT committee report on AI in teaching, learning and research training." (Reports)
- "No resources for this exchange yet." (Research Exchange filter)

## Evidence and method

A page of links with one-line descriptions. It holds no data.

## Limits

The page does not say which models MIT Parley gives, who can use it, or what it costs. The library has none of the linked documents.
