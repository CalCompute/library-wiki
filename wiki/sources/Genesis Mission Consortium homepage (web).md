# Genesis Mission Consortium homepage (web)

## Citation

Genesis Mission Consortium. Homepage, "Create the Next Era in Science." Web page, date not stated (latest news item Oct. 15, 2026). https://genesismissionconsortium.org/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The homepage says that members of the Genesis Mission Consortium "intend to contribute computing power, SI tokens, technical expertise, or in-kind support". Members get access to computing, data, facilities and expertise "through Project Agreements".
- It announces a Genesis Mission Platform Research and Development (R&D) Partnerships Program of "Approximately $20 million". The page expects its release in the first quarter of fiscal year (FY) 2027.

## Background

The Genesis Mission Consortium is the public-private partnership of the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Genesis Mission. RTI International, operating as TechWerx, administers it for DOE. The homepage describes the purpose of the Genesis Mission Consortium, who can join, how it works, who benefits and its five working groups. It links to news items and to the university membership document ([Genesis Mission Consortium University Membership (web)](Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20University%20Membership%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Consortium members intend to contribute computing power, SI tokens, technical expertise, or in-kind support, to advance the Genesis Mission goals and build the community." (How the Consortium Works)
- "Members will have the opportunity to collaborate and gain access to computing capabilities, data, facilities, and expertise through Project Agreements shared across members and the National Laboratories." (Who Benefits, The Consortium Members)
- "The consortium will identify strategic partnerships between members and non-members for select opportunities." (Who Benefits, The Research Community)
- "Both open and proprietary work will be supported, creating new capabilities for the Genesis Mission Platform, and domestic businesses of all sizes, academic institutions, and other non-Federally-affiliated organizations will be welcome to apply." (Genesis Mission Platform R&D Partnerships Program)
- "**Approximately $20 million** in total funding is anticipated, with release expected in Q1 FY27." (Genesis Mission Platform R&D Partnerships Program)
- The High-Performance Computing and Cloud Infrastructure working group is to "Integrate supercomputing resources with the American Science Cloud (AmSC) to enable scalable, SI-driven research and real-time collaboration." (Working Groups)
- "The Genesis Mission aims to double the productivity and impact of American research within a decade." (Who Benefits, The Public)

## Evidence and method

An institutional homepage. It states the aims, functions and working groups of the Genesis Mission Consortium and announces one funding program. It gives one dollar figure and no member names.

## Limits

- The page does not define "SI tokens" or say what a Project Agreement contains.
- The R&D Partnerships Program is "Coming Soon". The page gives no award sizes, eligibility rules or dates beyond the expected quarter.
- The page has no publication date. The news items on it run to Oct. 15, 2026.
- For DOE's count of members and partner commitments, see [DOE Genesis Mission 800 million partner commitments (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20800%20million%20partner%20commitments%20%28web%29.md).
