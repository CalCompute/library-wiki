# Stanford Research Computing facilities (web)

## Citation

Stanford Research Computing. "Hosting Facilities." Web page, no date. https://srcc.stanford.edu/facilities. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 Stanford Research Computing facilities.md`.

## Bottom line

- The Stanford Research Computing Facility (SRCF) is a Stanford data center on SLAC land. It opened in 2013 and got a second module, SRCF2, in 2023.
- Researchers can host their own racks, or use the shared Sherlock cluster. The Provost funds Sherlock's base. PI "owners" add nodes with grant funds and pay a one-time per-node fee.

## Background

Stanford Research Computing runs the SRCF and a smaller data center in Forsythe Hall (RCF). The page describes the facility, two service models and how to get physical access.

## Key claims

The facility (top of page and "Overview"):

- "A Stanford building located on SLAC's land, the SRCF provides a highly efficient hub for the physical hosting of high density compute and storage equipment, along with systems administration and support services."
- "The SRCF opened for production use in November 2013. A major expansion was completed in 2023."
- "The facility is cooled with ambient air fan systems for 90% of the year."

Hosting model (section "Service Models"):

- "a researcher purchases their own rack, PDUs, network switches, and equipment and works with Research Computing to coordinate installation timing and access. The researcher is responsible for the management and system administration of the equipment."
- "Equipment must be replaced with new equipment, or removed from the facility, before or when the equipment is 5 years old."

Shared cluster (section "Service Models"):

- "The Provost provides Research Computing with capital funding to purchase computing equipment for the shared Research Computing cluster. The cluster purchased with those funds, Sherlock, is available for the use of any Stanford faculty member, and associated research teams, for his/her sponsored or departmental research."
- "Owners have access to the servers they purchased, but they also can use other owners' servers when they are idle."
- "At the present time, system administration and support of all components of the Sherlock cluster - whether base servers or owners' servers is funded by the Dean of Research and Provost. A modest one-time per-node infrastructure fee is charged to PI owners at the time of installation."

Grant support (top of page):

- "Many program announcements for grant proposals require you to provide a description of local compute capabilities and facilities. We can help you out!"

## Evidence and method

This is a service page. It describes the facility, the funding model and the rules. It gives no capacity, usage or price figures.

## Limits

- The page does not give the power capacity, the number of racks or the fee amount.
- It does not describe Sherlock's hardware.
- Sherlock at Stanford is a different service from Sherlock at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC).
