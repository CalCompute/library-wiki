# SDSC service rates (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "Service Rates." SDSC website, no date. https://sdsc.edu/services/service_rates.html. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 SDSC service rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- SDSC lists its recharge rates for computing, cloud, storage, colocation, consulting and other services. Most rows give a UC rate, and many say "Available upon request" for users outside UC.
- An Expanse GPU service unit (SU) is $1.7530 and an Expanse CPU SU is $0.0123, at the UC rate.
- A TSCC Hotel SU is $0.0352 for UC users and $0.0563 for others.

## Background

SDSC at UC San Diego sells services to campus, UC and outside users. This page puts the rates of all its services on one page. The UC San Diego Recharge Rate Committee approves each change. Some services are exempt from UC San Diego indirect costs (IDC), and others follow normal IDC rules.

## Key claims

- "HPC Cycle - Expanse CPU | SU | $0.0123 | Available upon request." (High-Performance Computing table)
- "HPC Cycle - Expanse GPU | SU | $1.7530 | Available upon request." (High-Performance Computing table)
- "TSCC Cycle | SU | $0.0352 | 0.0563" (TSCC Hotel table, UC and non-UC rates)
- "**NIST Hotel purchase** minimum 10K base core-hours or SUs | SU | $0.1402 | $0.2253" (TSCC NIST 800-171 Compliant Environment table)
- "Cloud Compute On-Demand | hour | $0.0314/vCPU $0.0007/GB of RAM" (SDSC Cloud table)
- "Rack space | Rack | $44.76 | $572.60 | Available upon request." (Colocation table, UCSD, UC and non-UC rates)
- "Utilities charges for UC San Diego customers are paid for by campus." (Colocation table, Utilities)
- "**Universal Scale Storage** ... 200 TB minimum for UC customers; 66 TB minimum for non-UC customers. | TB/Month | $5.83" (Universal Scale Storage table)
- "Recharge rates are subject to change with approval from the UC San Diego Recharge Rate Committee." (note under each table)

## Evidence and method

A price list in 20 tables. It gives the unit and the rate of each service. It gives no method for the rates and no usage figures. The TSCC Condo fees are the same as on the program page ([SDSC TSCC condo details (web)](SDSC%20TSCC%20condo%20details%20%28web%29.md)).

## Limits

- The page does not say what an Expanse GPU SU buys. The Expanse user guide gives one SU as "1 GPU using less than 92G of data for 1 hour". Its GPU nodes have V100 GPUs ([SDSC Expanse User Guide (web)](SDSC%20Expanse%20User%20Guide%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives no rate outside UC for most computing services.
- It has no date. The rates are those of the fetch date.
