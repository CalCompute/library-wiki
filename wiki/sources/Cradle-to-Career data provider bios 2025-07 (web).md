# Cradle-to-Career data provider bios 2025-07 (web)

## Citation

California Cradle-to-Career Data System (C2C). Data provider biographies. Data and Tools and Community Engagement Advisory Boards Meeting, Agenda Item 3, 8 pages, upload dated July 2025 by its address. https://c2c.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Data-Provider-QA-Bios-BRD-3-Joint-Advisory-Boards-2.pdf. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Cradle-to-Career data provider bios 2025-07.pdf`.

## Bottom line

- A meeting paper for two C2C advisory boards. It gives short biographies of the data staff of the agencies and segments that supply data to C2C.
- The staff come from 11 bodies. Among them are the California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office and the Employment Development Department (EDD).

## Background

C2C publishes the papers of its advisory boards on its website. This paper was Agenda Item 3 at a joint meeting of its Data and Tools Advisory Board and its Community Engagement Advisory Board. Its file name contains "Data-Provider-QA".

## Key claims

- "Todd Hoig is the Director of Management Information Systems at the California Community Colleges Chancellor’s Office. In his role, he oversees the collection, warehousing, and use of Management Information Systems data." (p. 2)
- "Van Vu is the Chief Data Officer at the Department of Developmental Services and oversees the Enterprise Data Operations Office." (p. 6)
- "My team is responsible for providing CDSS data on program participation to C2C." (Hilary Berwick, California Department of Social Services, p. 7)
- "Juan is Chief of the Labor Market Information Division at the California Employment Development Department." (p. 7)

## Evidence and method

A government meeting paper. Each entry gives a name, a title, the agency and a short career history. The bodies are:

- Association of Independent California Colleges and Universities (AICCU).
- Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education.
- California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office.
- California Health and Human Services, Center for Data Insights and Innovation.
- California Student Aid Commission.
- California State University (CSU) Office of the Chancellor (two people).
- Department of Developmental Services (two people).
- Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Apprenticeship Standards.
- California Department of Social Services.
- Employment Development Department.
- ScholarShare Investment Board.

## Limits

The paper gives no meeting date; the date comes from the upload path. It spells some names in two ways: "Panela Ong" and "Pamela Ong", "Adele Burns" and "Adele Burnes", "Hillary Berwick" and "Hilary Berwick". It does not describe the data that each body supplies, except for the Department of Social Services. It does not mention AI or compute.
