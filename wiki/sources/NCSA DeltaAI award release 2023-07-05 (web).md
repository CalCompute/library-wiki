# NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)

## Citation

Helregel, Andrew. "NCSA Awarded $10 Million For DeltaAI." National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. News release, posted July 5, 2023, updated October 6, 2025. https://www.ncsa.illinois.edu/ncsa-awarded-10-million-for-deltaai/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The National Science Foundation (NSF) gave NCSA a $10 million award to deploy DeltaAI, a companion system to NCSA's Delta.
- NSF manages 90 percent of the computing through ACCESS. The DeltaAI project coordinates the other 10 percent, which the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign allocates.

## Background

NCSA published this news release when NSF made the award. It describes DeltaAI as a graphics processing unit (GPU) system that uses the same interconnect and similar hardware as Delta. It quotes NCSA Director Bill Gropp. It links the project to the recommendations of the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Task Force.

## Key claims

- "NCSA received a $10 million award from the National Science Foundation (NSF) to deploy DeltaAI, an advanced computing and data resource that will be a companion system to NCSA’s Delta." (paragraph 2)
- "DeltaAI will triple NCSA’s AI-focused computing capacity and greatly expand the capacity available within the NSF-funded advanced computing ecosystem." (paragraph 2)
- "This will be achieved by deploying DeltaAI using similar system and storage hardware, and the same high-speed interconnect." (paragraph 4)
- "This project seeks to expand the use of AI methods in research by providing easier access, training offerings and other support to promote a wider demographic of researchers." (Bill Gropp quotation)
- "DeltaAI represents a step in the direction recommended by the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Task Force, which urged an intergovernmental effort to increase funding for machine learning and AI research." (paragraph 7)
- "NSF will manage 90 percent of the available computing resources via ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services & Support) with the DeltaAI project coordinating the remaining 10 percent to be allocated by the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign." (last paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A news release from the center that got the award. It gives the award amount, the planned allocation split and the design approach. It links the NSF award record (award 2320345). It gives no hardware counts.

## Limits

The release comes before the system was built. It does not give the number of GPUs or nodes, or the operating cost. [NCSA DeltaAI launch release 2024-11-18 (web)](NCSA%20DeltaAI%20launch%20release%202024-11-18%20%28web%29.md) gives later facts: "nearly $30 million" in NSF awards and 20 nodes from Illinois Computes.
