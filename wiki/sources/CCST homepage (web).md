# CCST homepage (web)

## Citation

California Council on Science and Technology (CCST). Homepage, "Charting California's Future in AI Governance", date not stated (copyright 2026). https://ccst.us/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- CCST gives science and technology advice to California policymakers through experts at its partner institutions. Its homepage leads with AI governance.
- Its services include expert testimony, briefings, workshops, peer-reviewed reports and the Science and Technology Policy Fellowship.

## Background

CCST is a nonpartisan nonprofit. Its homepage gives its services, recent news, its people and its recent reports.

## Key claims

- "For more than 38 years, CCST has tailored its services to meet the needs of California policymakers, particularly when it comes to providing impartial advice and expertise on sensitive scientific matters." (Science Services)
- "Contact CCST to speak to an expert in a specific scientific or technological field, secure expert testimony for a hearing, arrange for an in-office meeting with a researcher, organize an Expert Briefing, commission a report, and more." (Science Services)
- "CCST convenes trusted experts to produce rigorously reviewed scientific reports that aid state leaders in making critical decisions." (Projects and Publications)
- "We annually recruit a cohort of PhD scientists, engineers, and social scientists to make California's policies stronger with science and technology." (Science & Technology Policy Fellowship)

## Evidence and method

An organization homepage. Its news list includes "CCST and Cal OES Co-Host the California AI Defense & Response Summit" and the annual Legislative Staff Academy on AI. Its recent reports are on fumigants, forest health, energy, pandemics and wildfire costs.

## Limits

The homepage does not list an AI report. It gives no figures on staff, budget or funders.
