# UCLA DTS GenAI available tools (web)

## Citation

UCLA Digital and Technology Solutions (DTS). "AI Tools." Web page, last updated September 2026. https://www.dts.ucla.edu/initiatives/genai/available-tools. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- UCLA lists its campus AI tools: Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT Edu, BruinChat, Zoom AI Companion and others. Claude, Box AI and a Bruin AI Gateway are "Coming Soon".
- Bruin Cloud gives research labs AWS and GCP infrastructure with "managed model gardens (Bedrock, Vertex AI)" and GPU and TPU accelerators.

## Background

UCLA DTS is the campus information technology organisation. The page is a catalog in four groups: productivity and creativity tools, applications and agents, development and engineering (BruinStack), and platforms and infrastructure.

## Key claims

- "Solve complex problems with frontier reasoning, advanced data analysis, and custom GPT builders using ChatGPT Edu, operating under enterprise data privacy." (OpenAI)
- "Gives the campus community direct access to Claude for general-purpose AI assistance. Coming Soon" (Anthropic AI)
- "Access enterprise cloud infrastructure across AWS and GCP, featuring managed model gardens (Bedrock, Vertex AI) and scalable high-performance GPU/TPU accelerators for research labs." (Bruin Cloud)
- "Integrate frontier text, vision, reasoning, and embedding endpoints directly into custom applications and pipelines with enterprise privacy and pay-as-you-go billing." (OpenAI API)
- "Connect your applications and research pipelines to top commercial and open-source models through a single endpoint: making it seamless to switch models, compare outputs, and manage spend in one place. Coming Soon" (Bruin AI Gateway)

## Evidence and method

A service catalog. It names products and their status. It gives no prices, quotas or user numbers.

## Limits

The page does not say who pays for each tool, or the rates of Bruin Cloud and the OpenAI API. It does not describe any campus-owned GPU cluster.
