## Bottom line

- AB 2392 requires the California Community Colleges (CCC) and the California State University (CSU) to form a joint working group. It requests the University of California (UC) to join.
- The working group sets GenAI training and procurement standards. Its recommendations are due by Jan. 1, 2028.
- The bill requires training before students, faculty and staff use a procured GenAI system. The analysis ties the bill to CSU's $16.9 million ChatGPT Edu contract, which had no training requirement.

## Background

The document is the Assembly floor analysis of AB 2392 by Assemblymember Mike Fong, version of April 23, 2026. A Higher Education Committee consultant wrote it. It summarises the bill, gives committee comments and lists the votes. The Governor signed AB 2392 on Sept. 30, 2026 ([Governor AI bill signings including AB 2392 2026-09-30 (web)](Governor%20AI%20bill%20signings%20including%20AB%202392%202026-09-30%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Requires the California Community Colleges (CCC) and the California State University (CSU), and requests the University of California (UC), to convene a joint working group to develop responsible generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) training and procurement standards and present its recommendations, by January 1, 2028, to the respective public higher education system leaders." (Summary, p. 1)
- "Procurement standards ensuring that any procured GenAI system meets specified minimum protections relating to risk assessment, sycophancy, safety monitoring, incident reporting, data governance, data minimization, training data disclosure, vendor contracting, and labor practices" (Major Provisions 1a, p. 1)
- "Requires the CCC and CSU, and requests the UC, to submit a written report to the Legislature and relevant policy committees ... before executing any contract for the procurement of a GenAI system during the period beginning January 1, 2027, and ending upon the adoption of procurement standards by the working group." (Major Provisions 5, pp. 1 to 2)
- "The contract for ChatGPT Edu $16.9 million, and runs from February 2025 through July 2026." (Comments, CSU AI-Empowered Initiative, p. 2)
- "Upon questioning from the Committees, it became clear that there had been no training requirement in place for students, faculty, and staff prior to the deployment of ChatGPT Edu." (Comments, Joint Hearing, p. 3)
- "One-time General Fund costs of an unknown amount, likely minor and absorbable, to the CCC, CSU, and UC to convene the working group and provide recommendations." (Fiscal Comments, p. 5)

## Evidence and method

A legislative staff analysis. It cites the CSU press release, a California State Student Association white paper of Feb. 24, 2026, and a joint oversight hearing of Aug. 26, 2025. It records no opposition and unanimous committee votes (10-0, 15-0 and 14-0-1).

## Limits

The analysis is about GenAI tools that the three systems buy for teaching and work. It does not discuss research compute or CalCompute. It describes the April 23, 2026 version, not the chaptered text. Its cost figure is "unknown".
