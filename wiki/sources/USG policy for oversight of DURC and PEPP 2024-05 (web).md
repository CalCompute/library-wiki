## Bottom line

- The policy is one federal oversight framework for federally funded life sciences research on some biological agents and toxins. When enhanced, these agents can pose risks to public health or national security.
- It sorts research into Category 1 (dual use research of concern) and Category 2 (pathogens with enhanced pandemic potential). It gives duties to investigators, institutions and funding agencies.
- It encourages institutional oversight of in silico research, including AI, "regardless of funding source", when it could enable the design of a dangerous agent.

## Background

OSTP issued the policy under the 2022 National Biodefense Strategy and National Security Memorandum 15, and under 42 U.S.C. § 6627. It replaces the 2012 and 2014 federal dual use research of concern (DURC) policies and the P3CO Framework. It has eight sections: introduction, background and principles, definitions, the two research categories, the oversight framework, research outside its scope, resources, and review. The copy is from an archived White House website.

## Key claims

- "This Policy addresses oversight of research on biological agents and toxins that, when enhanced, have the potential to pose risks to public health, agriculture, food security, economic security, or national security." (1.1, p. 2)
- "This Policy applies to federal departments and agencies that fund or sponsor intramural or extramural research at research institutions in the United States and internationally with biological agents or toxins where the research is within Category 1 or Category 2 under this Policy" (1.2, p. 3)
- "This Policy covers the research proposal stage and the full life cycle of the research." (1.2, p. 3)
- "This Policy encourages institutional oversight of in silico research, regardless of funding source, that could result in the development of potential dual-use computational models directly enabling the design of a PEPP or a novel biological agent or toxin." (6.2.2, p. 28)
- "For PIs and research institutions, failure to follow the research oversight framework under this Policy may result in suspension, limitation, or termination of federal funding and loss of future federal funding opportunities for the research proposal and for other life sciences research at the research institution, as imposed by the federal funding agency." (5.6, p. 26)

## Evidence and method

A federal policy document of about 30 pages. It states definitions, categories and duties. It gives no data on how much research it covers.

## Limits

This page reads the table of contents and sections 1.1, 1.2, 5.6, 5.7 and 6.2.2. It does not read the agent lists, the experimental outcomes of Section 4 or the full duties of Section 5. The rules for in silico and AI research are voluntary guidance, not duties.
