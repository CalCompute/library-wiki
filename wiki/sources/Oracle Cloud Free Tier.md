# Oracle Cloud Free Tier

## Citation

Oracle. "Oracle Cloud Free Tier." Oracle website, product page, publication date unknown (© 2026). https://www.oracle.com/cloud/free/. Read 2026-10-28.

## Bottom line

- Oracle gives a new account two things: a US$300 credit for 30 days, and "Always Free" services with no time limit.
- The Always Free list has CPU (central processing unit) compute, storage and databases. It has no GPU (graphics processing unit). AI services come with a paid "Pay As You Go" account.
- This is vendor marketing. It shows a model for free access rules, not capacity. The page names no region and no California facility.

## Background

The inventory's row for Oracle (section 5, ID 7) says "San Jose region (us-sanjose-1) confirmed; GPU portfolio confirmed".

This page does not support either part. It names no region and no GPU. The inventory also lists Oracle release notes (docs.oracle.com) as a source. Those may support the claim.

## Key claims

- Credit (FAQ, "What is Oracle Cloud Free Tier?"): "a Free Trial with US$300 of free credit to use on all eligible Oracle Cloud Infrastructure services for up to 30 days."
- Always Free (same answer): "The Always Free services are available for an unlimited period of time."
- Trial end (same answer): the trial runs "until your US$300 of free credits are consumed or the 30 days has expired, whichever comes first."
- Always Free services (FAQ, "What are Always Free Services?"): the list includes "AMD-based Compute", "Arm-based Ampere A1 Compute", "Block Volume", "Object Storage" and "Autonomous Data Warehouse". It has no GPU service. The FAQ adds: "Some limitations apply."
- AI (FAQ, "What is the advantage of moving to Pay As You Go accounts after the trial?"): "You will have access to the latest portfolio of OCI services, such as artificial intelligence, and you only pay for what you use."
- Capacity (footnote \*\*): "Availability to Free Tier is subject to capacity limits." The listed limits "are only estimates".
- Eligibility (FAQ, "Who is eligible for Oracle Cloud Free Tier?"): "One Oracle Cloud Free Trial or Always Free account is permitted per person." Multiple free accounts are "prohibited".
- Idle accounts (same answer): "Accounts left idle for 30 days or more may be deemed abandoned and become eligible for suspension or termination."
- Identity (FAQ on card details): Oracle uses "credit/debit card information for account setup and identity verification". It does not accept "virtual, single-use, or prepaid cards".
- Countries (footnote \*): "The US$300 cloud credit is available in select countries."

## Evidence and method

A vendor's product page. It states its own terms. It gives no usage data, no capacity figures and no method.

## Limits

- The copy does not hold the per-service capacity limits. The service filter on the page did not render in the copy.
- The page has no GPU offer, so it does not show free AI compute.
- Oracle can change the terms at any time: "The list of services and capacity estimates are subject to change" (footnote \*\*).
- Vendor material.
