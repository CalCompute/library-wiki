# WSCUC directory of institutions (web)

## Citation

WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC). "Currently Accredited & Candidate Institutions." Directory web page, date not stated. https://www.wscuc.org/directory/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The WSCUC directory lists the colleges and universities that WSCUC accredits, and its candidates. It gives the city, the country, the accreditation status and the financial structure of each.
- The copy that the library read holds the first 50 rows only, alphabetically from "Abu Dhabi University" to "California Western School of Law".
- Of those 50 rows, 46 are "Accredited", three are "Accredited with Notice of Concern" and one is "Accredited on Warning".

## Background

WSCUC is an accreditor of senior colleges and universities. Its directory page has filters by institution, OPEID, accreditation status, financial structure and country. Each row links to a WSCUC page for the institution. WSCUC says these pages carry "the peer review team reports and full commission action letters" ([WSCUC where to find data on institutions (web)](WSCUC%20where%20to%20find%20data%20on%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- Heading: "Currently Accredited & Candidate Institutions". (page heading)
- Filters: "Institution", "OPEID", "Accreditation Status", "Financial Structure" and "Country". (filter bar)
- Financial structure: the 50 rows give "Non-Profit" (23), "Public" (18) and "For-Profit" (9). (rows)
- Public rows: the rows include 18 public institutions, from "California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo" to "California State University, Stanislaus". (rows)
- Status example: "California State University, East Bay", "Hayward", "Accredited with Notice of Concern", "Public". (row)
- Outside the United States: six of the 50 rows are in other countries, for example "American University of Armenia" in "Yerevan". (rows)

## Evidence and method

This is a directory page of an accrediting body. The library counted the rows in the Markdown copy; the page prints no totals. In the copy, the field labels are one place out of line with the values. The label "Accreditation Status" comes before the country. The label "Financial Structure Type" comes before the status. The values on this page follow the order of the values, not the labels.

## Limits

> 🔍 Gap: the copy has only the first page of results. The University of California campuses and most California private universities are not in it.

- The page gives no state, county, coordinates or institution web address.
- It gives no date for each status.
- It does not give the reasons for a notice of concern or a warning.
