# MIT AI Research Exchange (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) AI Exchange. "Research Exchange." Program page, date not stated; lists events to November 16, 2026. https://aiexchange.mit.edu/research/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The program page of the MIT AI Research Exchange. It has "AI + X" research themes and faculty-led communities of practice.
- Its shared support has three parts: tools and infrastructure, training and guidance, and research connections.

## Background

The Research Exchange is one of the two parts of the MIT AI Exchange. Its faculty director is Markus Buehler. The four first themes are AI + Science, AI + Engineering, AI + Management and AI + Society. The example communities are Materials, Biology, Energy, Physics, Design and Creativity.

## Key claims

- "AI + X programs connect MIT's schools and the Schwarzman College of Computing around opportunities at the intersection of AI and disciplinary research." (Research themes)
- "Seminars, workshops, fellowships, and seed grants help researchers exchange ideas, identify shared challenges, and build collaborations that extend beyond individual research groups." (Research themes)
- "Faculty-led communities of practice bring together researchers with relevant expertise around shared research questions." (Research communities)
- "Alongside enabling research discoveries, they will develop reusable tools, datasets, and practices that other research groups can adapt and extend." (Research communities)
- "Access to AI models, computing resources, technical support, and shared research infrastructure helps researchers explore new approaches and build on one another's work." (Shared support: Tools and infrastructure)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It states aims and structure. It gives no figures.

## Limits

The page gives no budget, no seed grant amounts and no compute details.
