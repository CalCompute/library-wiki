## Bottom line

- GovOps strategic goal 1 is "Develop the State Workforce": to "attract and retain a highly skilled workforce that reflects Californians."
- Goal 2 is "Accelerate Innovation". A CalCompute plan for the current public sector workforce fits both goals.

## Background

See [Government Operations Agency](../entities/Government%20Operations%20Agency.md).

## Key claims

- Mission: "to advance effectiveness, efficiency, and equity in state operations."
- Goals: "Develop the State Workforce", "Accelerate Innovation", "Sustain Business Transformation", "Advance Equity".
- Goal 1: "Using people centered approach, develop and support strategies across departments to attract and retain a highly skilled workforce that reflects Californians."
- A value under Collaboration: "Build public confidence in government by creating opportunities for resident input to improve how government works."
