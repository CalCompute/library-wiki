# Apereo bylaws (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Bylaws of Apereo Foundation Incorporated (a New Jersey Nonprofit Corporation)." Web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/about/governance/bylaws. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Apereo Foundation is a New Jersey non-stock, nonprofit corporation, organized for purposes under Section 501(c)(3). Its date of incorporation is Dec. 28, 2012.
- Members pay a Supporting Subscription. Only Organizational Members vote on foundation matters, one vote for each organization. Individual Members vote only for their own directors.
- Software communities govern themselves under the principle of subsidiarity. The board endorses or ends each community.

## Background

The Apereo Foundation publishes its bylaws on apereo.org. They have 16 articles, from name and purpose to amendments. Article IX sets out how the foundation governs its software communities and communities of interest. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Mission: the Foundation assists educational organizations which "collaborate to foster, develop, and sustain open technologies and innovation to support learning, teaching, and research." (Article I, Section 2.A)
- Members: "The Foundation shall have two types of members: **Organizational Members** with voting rights under the Act, and **Individual Members**. Organizational Members and Individual Members are defined by their payment of a Supporting Subscription to the Foundation." (Article III, Section 1.1)
- Eligibility: "Organizational membership in the Foundation is open to educational, academic, research, and commercial organizations and institutions with programs and missions consistent with the purposes of the Foundation" (Article III, Section 1.2)
- Voting: "One (1) vote is allowed per member organization. A quorum of the membership shall be the lesser of twenty (20) members or thirty percent (30%) of the membership." (Article III, Section 3.3)
- Board size: "The number of directors shall be not less than seven (7) and not more than twenty-one (21) as determined by the Board of Directors from time to time. All Directors shall serve three (3) year terms" (Article V, Section 2.1)
- Elected majority: "Primary Representatives of Organizational Members will elect the majority of the Board of Directors." (Article V, Section 2.4)
- Individual seats: "Individual Members, as a group, will elect three members to sit on the Board of Directors." (Article V, Section 2.5)
- Appointed seats: "The Board may appoint, at its discretion, by majority vote of the Directors present at a meeting where a quorum exists, up to three (3) additional Directors who have not been elected by the Membership" (Article V, Section 2.6)
- Subsidiarity: "projects and communities that choose to join the Apereo Foundation are empowered to govern themselves within the policies and principles established by the Foundation" (Article IX, Section 1.2.3)
- Community governance: "Community governance structures will be responsible for strategic, financial and operational oversight of the community, including adherence to overall Foundation licensing and intellectual property policies." (Article IX, Section 4)

## Evidence and method

This is the legal governing document of the foundation, as it publishes it. It states rules for members, directors, officers, committees and communities. It gives no fee amounts. Fees are in a separate "Supporting Subscriptions Schedule" (Article III, Section 2).

## Limits

- The page gives no date of the current version.
- The bylaws do not give the amounts of the Supporting Subscriptions. See [Apereo institutional members (web)](Apereo%20institutional%20members%20%28web%29.md) and [Apereo sustaining members (web)](Apereo%20sustaining%20members%20%28web%29.md).
- The bylaws do not agree with themselves on Individual Member seats. Article III, Section 3.4 says that Individual Members "shall elect an Individual Member Director". Article V, Section 2.5 gives them three directors.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the bylaws allow the board to appoint "up to three (3) additional Directors" (Article V, Section 2.6). The governance page says that "the board has the right to appoint up to four additional board members" ([Apereo governance (web)](Apereo%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
