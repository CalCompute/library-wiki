# FedRAMP 20x (web)

## Citation

Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP), U.S. General Services Administration (GSA). "FedRAMP 20x." Web page, date not stated. https://www.fedramp.gov/20x/. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 FedRAMP 20x.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20FedRAMP%2020x.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- FedRAMP 20x is GSA's new process to assess and authorize cloud services for federal agencies. It uses automated validation and "Key Security Indicators" in place of yearly manual audits.
- The page says the rules are final for Class A, Class B and Class C. Class D (High) is for Phase 4, and FedRAMP "will stop accepting new Rev5 Certifications on June 11, 2027".

## Background

FedRAMP is the federal program for cloud security assessment and authorization. GSA runs it. This page is the program's overview of 20x: its classes, principles, phases and timeline. GSA announced 20x in March 2025, and the page reports two pilots, in 2025 and 2026.

## Key claims

- "A new approach to cloud security assessment and authorization that moves beyond traditional compliance to focus on the security decisions that matter most." (FedRAMP 20x)
- "The full rules for FedRAMP 20x Certification are now finalized for Class A, Class B, and Class C." (Available Now)
- "Class C Certifications are for cloud services that provide common enterprise services that are likely to be used in systems across an entire agency or that provide important government services." (Class C)
- "Continuous evidence of what is happening is stronger than a policy saying it should happen." (Core Principles, Automatic Validation)
- "Requiring every service to meet requirements designed for the highest-risk systems is not fair to agencies or the cloud services they want to use." (Context Matters)
- "FedRAMP received 26 complete packages between May 30 and August 18, 2025." (Phase 1 Recap)
- "Participation was limited to passing Phase 1 providers, AI-prioritized offerings, and critical-need services such as trust centers and GRC tools." (Phase 2 Recap)
- The FedRAMP Authorization Act of December 2022 "Established FedRAMP in law as a government-wide, standardized, reusable approach to cloud security assessment and authorization." (Timeline)

## Evidence and method

An official program page. It gives the certification classes, five principles, pilot counts and dates, lessons from each pilot and a timeline. It gives no costs, no list of certified services and no assessment data.

## Limits

The page is about federal agencies. It does not name state or local governments or universities, and it does not name California. The copy that the library read shows empty quotation boxes where the page quotes OMB Memorandum M-24-15.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: In this page, "Available Now" says the full rules "are now finalized". The Phase 3 section still says the Consolidated Rules for 2026 "are planned for completion by the end of FY26 Q3 (June 2026)" and that the submission pipeline "is planned to open in FY26 Q4". Both statements are in [FedRAMP 20x (web)](FedRAMP%2020x%20%28web%29.md).
