# OSC governance (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Governance and Advisory." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/about/pandc. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The Ohio General Assembly funds OSC through the Ohio Department of Higher Education, and OSC reports to its Chancellor.
- The Ohio State University operates OSC for the State of Ohio, as a unit of its Office of the Chief Information Officer. Ohio State is the fiscal agent. A joint OSC-OARnet Advisory Board recommends policy guidelines.

## Background

This page in the About section of the OSC website describes who funds, governs and advises OSC. It has two parts: Governance and Advisory Board.

## Key claims

- "Funded by the Ohio General Assembly through the Ohio Department of Higher Education, OSC reports to the Chancellor of the Ohio Department of Higher Education." (Governance)
- "All business and legal services are provided by The Ohio State University, with direction from the Deputy Chief Information Officer." (Governance)
- "The Ohio Supercomputer Center operates as a unit within and is operated for the State of Ohio by the Office of the Chief Information Officer at The Ohio State University." (Governance)
- "Ohio State serves as the fiscal agent for the Center and consults with the Ohio Department of Higher Education in appointing an executive director, developing the Center’s budget and designing OSC programs." (Governance)
- "A joint OSC-OARnet Advisory Board recommends broad policy guidelines for OSC governance and periodically reviews its program and operations" (Advisory Board)

## Evidence and method

An institutional description of the governance structure. It names the funder, the reporting line, the operating university and the advisory board. It gives no budget figures and no names of board members.

## Limits

The page does not give the size of the state appropriation or the members of the advisory board. It links to the board list on the Ohio Technology Consortium (OH-TECH) website: [OSC and OARnet Advisory Board (web)](OSC%20and%20OARnet%20Advisory%20Board%20%28web%29.md).
