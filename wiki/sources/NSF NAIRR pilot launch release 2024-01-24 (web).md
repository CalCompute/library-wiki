# NSF NAIRR pilot launch release 2024-01-24 (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF). "Democratizing the future of AI R&D: NSF to launch National AI Research Resource pilot." News release, Jan. 24, 2024. https://www.nsf.gov/news/democratizing-future-ai-rd-nsf-launch-national-ai-research. Read 2026-11-09. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-09 NSF NAIRR pilot launch release 2024-01-24.html`.

## Bottom line

- NSF launched the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) pilot on Jan. 24, 2024. Its partners are 10 other federal agencies and 25 private sector, nonprofit and philanthropic organizations.
- The pilot gives U.S.-based researchers and educators access to computing, datasets, models, software, training and user support, in four focus areas.

## Background

NSF announced the pilot in this release. The launch met a 90-day deadline in Executive Order 14110 of October 2023. The release lists the 11 government partners and the 25 private sector and nonprofit partners, with a quote from most of them. The pilot's later allocation pages are [NAIRR Pilot resource allocations (web)](NAIRR%20Pilot%20resource%20allocations%20%28web%29.md) and [NAIRR Pilot allocation management policy](NAIRR%20Pilot%20allocation%20management%20policy.md).

## Key claims

- "Partnering with 10 other federal agencies as well as 25 private sector, nonprofit and philanthropic organizations, the NAIRR pilot will provide access to advanced computing, datasets, models, software, training and user support to U.S.-based researchers and educators." (paragraph 2)
- "This pilot is a proof of concept to ignite the level of investment needed to realize the full NAIRR vision." (paragraph 2)
- "The collaborative nature of the pilot, bringing together academia, industry, nonprofit and government sectors, is intended to promote cross-sector partnerships." (paragraph 5)
- "NAIRR Secure, co-led by NIH and DOE, will enable AI research requiring privacy and security-preserving resources and will assemble exemplar privacy preserving resources." (focus areas)
- "NAIRR Classroom will reach new communities through education, training, user support and outreach." (focus areas)
- "The launch meets a goal outlined in Executive Order 14110, signed by President Joe Biden in October 2023, directing NSF to launch a pilot for NAIRR within 90 days." (after focus areas)

## Evidence and method

A federal news release. It names the partners and the four focus areas, and quotes officials and partner executives. It gives no budget, no hardware figures and no count of users.

## Limits

The release is from the pilot's first day. It does not say what each partner contributes in amounts, or how allocation works. The release cites Executive Order 14110. [Executive Order 14365 national AI policy framework (web)](Executive%20Order%2014365%20national%20AI%20policy%20framework%20%28web%29.md) says that by Executive Order 14179 the President "revoked my predecessor's attempt to paralyze this industry".
