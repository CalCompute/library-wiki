# OSC supercomputers (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Cluster Computing." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/resources/technical_support/supercomputers. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- OSC's three clusters, Pitzer, Ascend and Cardinal, give a total peak of 30.3 petaflops. OSC has about 26 petabytes of disk storage and more than 14 petabytes of tape backup.
- The GPUs are NVIDIA V100 on Pitzer, A100 on Ascend and 128 H100 on Cardinal. A fully subsidized account is for full-time faculty or research scientists at an Ohio college or university.

## Background

The address serves the page "Cluster Computing" in the Services section of the OSC website. It gives the technical specifications of each cluster, the GPU counts and the account rules.

## Key claims

- "Collectively, OSC supercomputers provide a peak computing performance of 30.3 Petaflops (PF). The center also offers approximately 26 Petabytes (PB) of disk storage capacity distributed over several file systems, plus more than 14 PB of available backup tape storage (with the ability to easily expand to over 23 PB)." (Cluster Computing)
- "OSC’s cluster computing capabilities make it a fully scalable center with mid-range machines to match those found at National Science Foundation centers and other national labs." (Cluster Computing)
- "Ascend Cluster: A 40,448-core, 322 node Dell AMD EPYC™ machine" with "Theoretical system peak performance of 16.0 petaflops" (Technical Specifications)
- "Cardinal: 128 NVIDIA H100s and NVLink (four each on 32 nodes)" (Technical Specifications, GPU Computing)
- "To request a fully subsidized account you must be a full-time faculty member or research scientist at an Ohio college or university. Graduate students, postdoctoral researchers, lab members and other colleagues seeking access to OSC resources must be working with an eligible primary investigator." (Getting Started)
- "OSC is regularly audited for security standards and abides by OSU's digital accessibility standards. OSC also supports export controlled and HIPAA projects." (Security, Accessibility, and Policies)

## Evidence and method

An institutional specification page. It gives the cores, nodes, memory and GPUs of each cluster. The peaks are Pitzer 3.9, Ascend 16.0 and Cardinal 10.5 petaflops. The three add up to 30.4 petaflops; the page gives the total as 30.3.

## Limits

The page does not give the cost of the clusters or the dates they were bought. The peak figures are theoretical. The page title differs from the address: the address names "supercomputers", and the page is "Cluster Computing".
