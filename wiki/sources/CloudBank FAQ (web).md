# CloudBank FAQ (web)

## Citation

CloudBank. "Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)." Web page, date not stated. https://www.cloudbank.org/faq. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- CloudBank "is available to any U.S. researcher or educator through the ACCESS and NAIRR Pilot programs". It gives allocations on Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
- UC San Diego manages CloudBank and does not charge indirect costs on its cloud resources.
- A user portal is the "single point of access" for help, training, allocations and cost monitoring.

## Background

CloudBank is a cloud access service for research and education. The National Science Foundation (NSF) funds it under awards 1925001 and 2505560. The FAQ names four partners. Two are at UC San Diego: the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) and the Information Technology Services Division. The others are the University of Washington eScience Institute and the UC Berkeley Division of Data Science and Information.

## Key claims

- Users: "CloudBank is available to any U.S. researcher or educator through the ACCESS and NAIRR Pilot programs." (question 1)
- Services: "CloudBank provides information, education, training, and allocations for commercial clouds -- currently, Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud, and Microsoft Azure." (question 2)
- Open training: "Information, education, and training resources are available to anyone in the research and education communities." (question 2)
- Partners: "CloudBank is established via an NSF Cooperative Agreement with the San Diego Supercomputer Center and the Information Technology Services Division at UC San Diego, the University of Washington eScience Institute, and UC Berkeley Division of Data Science and Information." (question 5)
- Indirect costs: "No. CloudBank is managed by UC San Diego (UCSD), which does not charge indirect costs (IDC) on cloud resources." (question 7)
- Policy: "UCSD was among the first campuses in the nation—along with the University of Washington—to adopt a policy of not assessing IDC on commercial cloud resources." (question 7)
- Portal: "The CloudBank user portal is the single point of access for users, giving them an on-ramp to cloud providers and access to materials and tools to support their research and education outcomes." (question 10)

## Evidence and method

This is a page of questions and answers. It gives no figures on users, spending or allocations.

## Limits

- The FAQ does not give the size of allocations, the cost to users or the review time. It links to a Get Access page that the library has not read.
- It does not say which award funds which part of CloudBank.
- It lists four cloud providers. The [NAIRR Pilot resource catalog feed (web)](NAIRR%20Pilot%20resource%20catalog%20feed%20%28web%29.md) says that the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot has paused some CloudBank awards. The FAQ does not say so.
- The [CloudBank Acceptable Use Policy (web)](CloudBank%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md) names a narrower community, "the computer science research and education community".
