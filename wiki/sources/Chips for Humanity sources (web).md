# Chips for Humanity sources (web)

## Citation

Chips for Humanity (Hofvarpnir Studios dba Chips for Humanity). "Sources and methods." Web page, 2026; no exact date stated. https://chipsforhumanity.org/sources.html. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The page gives the source and method for each number on the [Chips for Humanity homepage](Chips%20for%20Humanity%20homepage%20%28web%29.md). It calls them estimates, with "likely" ranges as 80 percent intervals.
- In 2025, it estimates frontier lab research compute at about $33 billion and outside safety research compute at about $59 million. That is about $1 for every $558.
- From October 2025 to October 2026, the median B200 rental price on Vast.ai went from $3.98 to $7.72 an hour.

## Background

The page has seven parts: funding, cluster purchase, GPUs per safety researcher, GPUs for outside safety research, prices, timelines and methods. It takes its funding estimates from its own "AI safety allocation" project at slop.ie. It cites Geodesic Research, Epoch AI, Anthropic, Bloomberg and Glass.ai, a ProPublica tax filing record, and the AI Futures Project.

## Key claims

- "They are estimates; “likely” ranges are 80% intervals." (Sources and methods)
- "The frontier labs’ research compute was about $33 billion (likely $27–41 billion)." (Safety research outside the labs is underfunded)
- "Safety research outside the labs used about $59 million of compute (likely $44–90 million), about $1 for every $558 of the labs’ research compute." (Safety research outside the labs is underfunded)
- "Offers disappeared within 24–48 hours, because nodes are not reserved until a contract is signed." (Buying a big cluster is hard)
- "No lab publishes compute per safety researcher, so these are rough, right to within a factor of two or three." (GPUs per safety researcher)
- "Redwood Research’s 2024 tax filing lists $473,000 of GPU rental for about 7.5 researchers, about 2.4 each; it lists only vendors paid over $100,000, so that is a floor." (GPUs per safety researcher)
- "From October 2025 to October 2026, B200 went from $3.98 to $7.72 and H200 from $2.61 to $5.04; A100 stayed around $0.90." (Prices)
- "Both are low-confidence scenarios." (GPUs for safety research outside labs)

## Evidence and method

A methods page with tables. It counts GPUs in H100-equivalents running all year, and it leaves out spending on AI model APIs. The Anthropic figure multiplies Epoch AI's 1.19 million H100-equivalents by a 50 percent research share and a 6 percent safety share. It then divides by 119 to 230 safety staff. The 50 percent research share is an assumption; the page says it is "not public". The outside figures come from grants, tax filings and organizations' own budgets.

## Limits

Most numbers are the publisher's own estimates, and the page says so. Only Anthropic has measured its safety share; the shares for the other labs are the publisher's estimates. The prices come from single machines on an open marketplace, not from large clusters on long contracts. The 2027 paths are scenarios. The page does not name its authors.
