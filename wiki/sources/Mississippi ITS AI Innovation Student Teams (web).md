# Mississippi ITS AI Innovation Student Teams (web)

## Citation

Mississippi Department of Information Technology Services (ITS). "AI Innovation Student Teams." Web page, date not stated. https://www.its.ms.gov/services/ai-innovation-student-teams. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- Mississippi's state IT agency pairs student teams from three state universities with agencies to build AI proofs of concept. A proof of concept usually takes 6 to 8 weeks.
- The work is part of the ITS AI Innovation Hub, a collaboration of ITS, Amazon Web Services (AWS) and the Mississippi Artificial Intelligence Network. The page lists 16 project teams and their members.

## Background

ITS announced the AI Innovation Hub on January 10, 2025. The partner universities are Mississippi State University, the University of Southern Mississippi and the University of Mississippi. Faculty or staff supervise each team. The projects are for ITS and other agencies, for example the Secretary of State and the Board of Nursing. They include chatbots, certificate renewal tools and transcript checks. Most of the page is student biographies.

## Key claims

- "The AI Innovation Hub is a groundbreaking statewide collaboration between the Mississippi Department of Information Technology Services (ITS), Amazon Web Services (AWS) and the Mississippi Artificial Intelligence Network (MAIN)." (AI Innovation Student Teams)
- "The Hub aims to accelerate the deployment of AI-driven solutions to enhance public services, boost economic growth, equip the workforce with cutting-edge AI skills and create a path to keep our best and brightest students working in Mississippi after college." (AI Innovation Student Teams)
- "The innovative student teams are interdisciplinary and supervised by institutional staff/faculty to work on rapid proof of concept (PoC) AI solutions with use cases they submit a proposal for." (AI Innovation Student Teams)
- "Timelines for PoCs vary by use case but are typically expected to be 6-8 weeks each. There are over 20 use cases submitted already and more coming in every day." (AI Innovation Student Teams)
- "This Proof of Concept is developing an internal AI-powered chatbot that assists ITS Procurement staff in efficiently locating and interpreting relevant procurement information." (About the MSU ITS Procurii Project Team)

## Evidence and method

A state agency program page. It describes the program and lists each team's project and members. It gives no costs, results or evaluation.

## Limits

The page gives no budget, no student pay and no terms of the AWS role. It does not say which computing the teams use. It does not report whether agencies adopted any proof of concept.
