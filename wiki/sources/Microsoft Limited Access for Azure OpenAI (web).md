# Microsoft Limited Access for Azure OpenAI (web)

## Citation

Microsoft. "Limited access for Foundry Models sold by Azure." Microsoft Learn, Azure AI Foundry responsible AI documentation. Last updated 2026-06-05. https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ai-foundry/responsible-ai/openai/limited-access. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- All Azure customers can use Foundry Models sold by Azure, including Azure OpenAI, with no registration form. Exceptions are models that Microsoft names as Limited Access Services.
- A customer that wants to turn Guardrails (content filters) partly or fully off, or to change abuse monitoring, must apply. Only customers managed by a Microsoft account team or under an eligible program can get this.
- For models made available on or after Nov. 1, 2026, Modified Abuse Monitoring will end on a date that Microsoft will announce.

## Background

The page is part of Microsoft's responsible AI documentation for Azure AI Foundry. It explains the Limited Access policy for models that Microsoft sells on Azure. The terms of use are the Product Terms, including the Universal License Terms for Microsoft Generative AI Services. See also [Microsoft Enterprise AI Services Code of Conduct 2026-05-01](Microsoft%20Enterprise%20AI%20Services%20Code%20of%20Conduct%202026-05-01.md) and [Microsoft Product Terms For Online Services (web)](Microsoft%20Product%20Terms%20For%20Online%20Services%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "certain Models sold by Azure (or versions of them) are designated as Limited Access Services, and access and use are subject to eligibility criteria determined by Microsoft." (Introduction)
- "Unless otherwise indicated in the service, all Azure customers are eligible for access to Models sold by Azure, and all uses consistent with the Product Terms and Code of Conduct are permitted, so customers are not required to submit a registration form unless they are: (a) accessing a model sold by Azure designated as a Limited Access Service, or (b) requesting approval to modify Guardrails (previously content filters) and/or abuse monitoring for a model sold by Azure." (Introduction)
- "All customers have the ability to configure severity thresholds on Guardrails (previously content filters), however, the modified Guardrails approval process is required to turn the Guardrails partially or fully off." (Registration for modified Guardrails and/or abuse monitoring)
- "At this time, modified Guardrails (previously content filters) and/or modified abuse monitoring for Models sold by Azure are available only to customers and partners managed by a Microsoft account team or under an eligible program, and are subject to additional requirements." (Registration for modified Guardrails and/or abuse monitoring)
- "For models made available on or after November 1, 2026, Modified Abuse Monitoring will no longer be available, beginning on a future date to be announced." (What's changing with Modified Abuse Monitoring)

## Evidence and method

A vendor documentation page of about 860 words. It states Microsoft's access rules. It gives no data on how many customers apply or get approval.

## Limits

The registration forms are links, and the copy does not hold their questions. The page does not list which models are Limited Access Services, or what the eligible programs are. It does not say how long a review takes. The page says that Microsoft's "updated approach to data retention, access, and safety monitoring" will apply, but it does not describe that approach.
