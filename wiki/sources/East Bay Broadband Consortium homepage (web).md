# East Bay Broadband Consortium homepage (web)

## Citation

East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC). "East Bay Broadband Consortium." Homepage, date not stated. https://www.eastbaybroadband.org/. Read 2026-11-13.

## Bottom line

- The East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC) works with community organizations, local governments and other partners to close the digital divide. It works in Alameda, Contra Costa and Solano counties.
- The page lists five lines of work: broadband affordability, device access, regional collaboration, broadband availability and community-informed data.

## Background

This is the homepage of the EBBC website. Its tag line is "Connecting the East Bay for Good." The page is short. It gives the mission and the five lines of work, with no figures.

## Key claims

- "The East Bay Broadband Consortium bridges the digital divide by working with community organizations, local governments, and other partners across Alameda, Contra Costa, and Solano counties to identify the barriers that keep communities from getting connected and transform the systems that shape digital access" (homepage)
- "Improve Broadband Affordability so that cost is not a barrier to getting and staying connected" (Our Work)
- "Facilitate Regional Collaboration to uplift collective needs and collaboratively generate solutions" (Our Work)
- "Increase Broadband Availability in communities with limited connectivity" (Our Work)
- "Collect Community-Informed Data that Is grounded in communities’ lived experiences" (Our Work)

## Evidence and method

An organization's own description of its mission and work. It gives no data, no budget and no list of members. A heading "What the Data Shows" has no text in the downloaded copy.
