# UC Noyce Initiative homepage (web)

## Citation

UC Noyce Initiative. "UC Noyce Initiative" (homepage). Web page, date not stated. The page footer says "Last update: November 5, 2026". https://ucnoyce.org/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) Noyce Initiative supports collaborative research by experts at five UC campuses. Its tagline is "Digital Innovation for the Public Good".
- It has three research areas: Computational Health, Cybersecurity & Privacy, and Quantum Science. A 2020 gift from The Robert N. Noyce Trust to the UC system launched it. The page does not give the amount.

## Background

The homepage introduces the initiative, links to its three research areas and lists five recent articles. It has a section on the legacies of Robert Noyce and Ann Schmeltz Bowers, and an image of the five campus logos. The site shows the UC Davis logo, and its footer gives a copyright of "The Regents of the University of California, Davis campus".

## Key claims

- "The UC Noyce Initiative brings together some of the world's foremost experts across five UC campuses and supports them in pursuing groundbreaking, collaborative research that is driving digital innovation to address some of the most pressing challenges and exciting opportunities of our time." (Harnessing the Power of 5)
- "In 2020, The Robert N. Noyce Trust made a gift to the University of California system to launch the UC Noyce Initiative to honor their legacies." (Legacies of Innovation)
- "Revolutionizing Computation Capabilities to Solve Societies Most Complex Challenges." (Quantum Science)
- "Supporting Innovation at the Intersection of Technology, Health Science and Medicine." (Computational Health)
- "UC logos from Berkeley, Davis, Irvine, San Francisco and Santa Barbara" (The Power of Five, image text)

## Evidence and method

An institutional homepage. It states the mission, the research areas, the funder and the year of the gift. It gives no amounts, no governance details and no data about research output.

## Limits

The page is thin. It does not give the size of the Noyce Trust gift, how the initiative divides funds among the campuses, or who governs it. It does not mention compute infrastructure, state government or CalCompute. [UC Noyce Initiative power of five](UC%20Noyce%20Initiative%20power%20of%20five%20%28web%29.md) describes each campus. [UC Noyce Initiative cybersecurity](UC%20Noyce%20Initiative%20cybersecurity%20%28web%29.md) describes one research area.
