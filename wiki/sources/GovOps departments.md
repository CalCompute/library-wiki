# GovOps departments

## Citation

California Government Operations Agency. "Our Departments, Boards and Programs." Web page, govops.ca.gov, date not stated. https://www.govops.ca.gov/who-we-are/departments/. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-26 GovOps departments.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-26%20GovOps%20departments.md) (original: `.html` beside it), fetched 2026-10-26. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- GovOps "oversees 15 different state departments, boards and programs".
- CalHR "is responsible for all issues related to employee salaries and benefits, job classifications, civil rights, training, exams, recruitment and retention". The State Personnel Board oversees "the merit-based, job-related recruitment and selection process".
- The California Education Learning Lab, "a state-funded grantmaking organization", is on the list. It funds Cal-ICOR.

## Key claims

- CDT "is committed to partnering with state and local government entities to deliver digital services, develop innovative and responsive solutions for business needs and provide quality assurance for Information Technology projects and services."
- The Department of General Services "serves as business manager for the State of California", with "procurement solutions, real estate management".
- ODI "helps departments meet Californians where they are."
- "The California Education Learning Lab is a state-funded grantmaking organization that awards grants to support faculty-led projects across California's public higher education segments."
- The California Education Interagency Council "was established to help California's education and workforce systems work better together."

## Evidence and method

The agency's own page, with one paragraph for each entity.

## Limits

- The page says 15 entities. The homepage says 16 departments and offices ([GovOps homepage](GovOps%20homepage.md)).
