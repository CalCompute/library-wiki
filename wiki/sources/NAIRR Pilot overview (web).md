# NAIRR Pilot overview (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation, NAIRR Pilot. "About NAIRR Pilot." NAIRR Pilot portal, no date. https://nairrpilot.org/about/overview. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page now expands NAIRR as "National Architecture for Innovation, Research, and Resource-sharing" and calls its subject "Super Intelligence (SI)". Earlier sources expand NAIRR as the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource.
- It lists the pilot's operations teams and their members. Members come from universities and research centers, UC San Diego and the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) among them.

## Background

The NAIRR Pilot portal is "brought to you by SGX3" under NSF award 2231406. This About page gives the pilot's purpose, the text that users must put in the acknowledgments of a paper, and the operations teams. The teams cover allocations, coordination, user experience, the portal, metrics, tickets and the newsletter.

## Key claims

- "The National Architecture for Innovation, Research, and Resource-sharing (NAIRR) is a concept for a shared national research infrastructure to bridge this gap by connecting U.S. researchers and educators to SI resources, namely computation, data, software, models, training, and educational materials" (What is the NAIRR?)
- "Super Intelligence (SI) holds the potential to accelerate discovery and innovation and reshape our modern workforce. However, many researchers and educators lack access to the SI resources necessary to fully conduct their research activities and to train the next generation." (What is the NAIRR?)
- "The Allocations Working Group manages the peer review and resource matching processes for NAIRR Pilot resource requests" (NAIRR Pilot Operations Teams)
- "The Coordination Working Group facilitates collaboration across all NAIRR Pilot Operations Teams, with primary goals of establishing and evolving governance structures" (NAIRR Pilot Operations Teams)
- "The team develops and maintains the NAIRR Pilot XDMoD portal, which is an instance of Open XDMoD tailored to support NAIRR Pilot resources." (Metrics Team)
- "This research is supported by the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot and [AWARDED-RESOURCE-ACK]" (How to Acknowledge NAIRR)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It names the members of each operations team with their institution. The acknowledgment table lists nine resources, each with its funding award, for example Delta, Frontera, Jetstream2 and Summit. It gives no budget and no usage figures.

## Limits

- The page has no date.
- The page uses the new expansion of NAIRR, but its own acknowledgment text keeps "National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot".

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page expands NAIRR as "National Architecture for Innovation, Research, and Resource-sharing". [NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)](NSF%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20update%20%28web%29.md), of Sept. 1, 2026, and [NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md) use "National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource".
