# MGHPCC AICR RFP release 2025-03-25 (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC). "MGHPCC Moves Forward with Infrastructure for the Massachusetts AI Hub Initiative: RFP Open for an Initial Phase." Press release (Word document), Holyoke, Massachusetts, March 25, 2025. https://mghpcc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/MGHPCC-AICR-PR-3_25_25.docx. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- MGHPCC opens a request for proposals (RFP) for an AI and machine learning (ML) compute resource for the Massachusetts AI Hub.
- The RFP covers the first of three tranches of funding. Responses are due April 17, 2025, and the first system is to operate in 2025.

## Background

MGHPCC is a data center in Holyoke, Massachusetts. Six research universities, the Commonwealth of Massachusetts and two companies developed it. Governor Maura Healey announced the Massachusetts AI Hub in December 2024. This release from MGHPCC announces the procurement for the AI Compute Resource (AICR) and describes its partners and its expected users.

## Key claims

- "The Massachusetts Green High Performance Center (MGHPCC) is now accepting proposals for an AI/ML compute resource that will support the Massachusetts AI Hub initiative that was announced in December 2024." (first paragraph)
- "The AI Compute Resource (AICR) Infrastructure System RFP covers the first of 3 tranches of funding." (third paragraph)
- "The first deployment in the sequence is expected to be in operation this year. The deadline for responding to the RFP is April 17, 2025." (third paragraph)
- "The initiative is a public-private partnership with sizable investments from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, as well as Boston University, Harvard University, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, the University of Massachusetts system, and Yale University." (fourth paragraph)
- "The MGHPCC was developed through an unprecedented collaboration among the most research-intensive universities in the region (Boston University, Harvard University, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, the University of Massachusetts system, and Yale University); the Commonwealth of Massachusetts; and private industry (Cisco and Dell EMC)." (About)
- "The member universities fund the ongoing operation of the data center." (About)

## Evidence and method

A one-page press release. It gives the RFP deadline, the three-tranche plan and the partners. It lists the expected areas of use: academic research, early-stage startups, robotics, national security, financial innovation, education and health and biotechnology. It names quantum, fusion and new materials as possible future areas.

## Limits

- The release gives no dollar amount for any tranche, and no hardware or capacity.
- It does not give the RFP text. It links an "AI Compute Resource System" page, which is not in the library.
- It does not give an allocation process or the user rules.
- The first line writes "Massachusetts Green High Performance Center". The name of the center is Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center, as the About paragraph prints it.
- Later sources report the $31 million state grant ([Massachusetts AI Hub release](Massachusetts%20AI%20Hub%20release.md)) and the selected builder ([MassTech AICR builder selection (web)](MassTech%20AICR%20builder%20selection%20%28web%29.md)).
