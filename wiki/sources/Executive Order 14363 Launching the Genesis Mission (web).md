# Executive Order 14363 Launching the Genesis Mission (web)

## Citation

The White House. "Launching the Genesis Mission." Executive Order 14363, November 24, 2025. https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/11/launching-the-genesis-mission/. ## Bottom line

- The order establishes the Genesis Mission, "a national effort to accelerate the application of AI for transformative scientific discovery". The Secretary of Energy implements it in the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE).
- The Secretary must operate the American Science and Security Platform. It provides high performance computing, AI frameworks, foundation models, datasets and experimental tools. The order sets deadlines from 60 days to 1 year.

## Background

The President signed the order on November 24, 2025. The Assistant to the President for Science and Technology (APST) gives general leadership of the Genesis Mission. The APST works through the National Science and Technology Council (NSTC). It sets out the Platform, a list of national science and technology challenges, interagency coordination, external partnerships and yearly reports.

## Key claims

- "There is hereby established the Genesis Mission (Mission), a national effort to accelerate the application of AI for transformative scientific discovery focused on pressing national challenges." (Sec. 2(a))
- "The Secretary shall establish and operate the American Science and Security Platform (Platform) to serve as the infrastructure for the Mission". (Sec. 3(a))
- The Platform provides "high-performance computing resources, including DOE national laboratory supercomputers and secure cloud-based AI computing environments, capable of supporting large-scale model training, simulation, and inference". (Sec. 3(a)(i))
- "Within 90 days of the date of this order, the Secretary shall identify Federal computing, storage, and networking resources available to support the Mission, including both DOE on-premises and cloud-based high-performance computing systems, and resources available through industry partners." (Sec. 3(c))
- "Within 270 days of the date of this order, the Secretary shall, consistent with applicable law and subject to available appropriations, seek to demonstrate an initial operating capability of the Platform". (Sec. 3(f))
- The Secretary must "establish clear policies for ownership, licensing, trade-secret protections, and commercialization of intellectual property developed under the Mission". (Sec. 5(c)(ii))
- The Secretary must "establish procedures to ensure the highest standards of vetting and authorization of users and collaborators seeking access to the resources of the Mission". (Sec. 5(c)(iv))
- "This order shall be implemented consistent with applicable law and subject to the availability of appropriations." (Sec. 7(b))

## Evidence and method

A presidential executive order, from the White House website. It is a legal instrument that directs federal agencies. It gives roles, tasks and deadlines. It gives no dollar amounts.

## Limits

The order gives no budget. It does not say who outside the federal government can use the Platform, only that users get vetted. It names six priority domains but leaves the list of challenges to the Secretary and the APST. The White House page is the source here, not the Federal Register text.
