# CSCS organization (web)

## Citation

Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS). "Organization." CSCS website, About section. No date on the page ("© CSCS 2026"). https://www.cscs.ch/about/organization. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- CSCS is a unit of ETH Zurich (the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich). It reports to the ETH Vice President for Research.
- Three bodies advise it: an Advisory Board, a Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC) and a board for the Swiss Platform for Advanced Scientific Computing (PASC).
- The SAC meets twice a year to review user projects and gives recommendations on project allocations.

## Background

This page of the CSCS website describes where CSCS sits in ETH Zurich and its three advisory bodies. It names the five permanent members of the SAC and the five permanent members of the PASC board. All ten work at universities or laboratories outside Switzerland.

## Key claims

- Place in ETH: "CSCS is a unit of the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich (ETH Zurich) reporting to the Vice President for Research." (Organization)
- Advisory Board: "The Board advises the responsible Vice President of ETH Zurich and the CSCS director in advancing the centre and its academic and operational programs." (Advisory Board)
- Terms: "The members of the Advisory Board are appointed for two-year renewable terms." (Advisory Board)
- SAC role: "The Scientific Advisory Committee (SAC) advises the Director on scientific directions relevant to the User Lab Program and gives recommendations on the project allocations." (Scientific Advisory Committee)
- SAC make-up: "The SAC comprises five permanent members to cover the most  important fields of research for CSCS and occasionally includes invited members as well if there are proposals that call  for additional expertise." (Scientific Advisory Committee)
- SAC cycle: "The SAC meets two times a year to review user projects and requested allocations and then have strategic discussions with the director." (Scientific Advisory Committee)
- PASC board: it "advices the Director and Deputy Director for Science on the direction of the PASC Program, including project selection and review. This program adheres to a four-year cycle." (PASC Scientific Advisory Board)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional page on structure. It states reporting lines, the roles of three advisory bodies, one term length and the names of permanent members. The SAC members come from the University of Texas at Austin, the University of Edinburgh, and KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Stockholm University. One comes from the National Centre for Atmospheric Science in Reading. The PASC board members come from Oak Ridge, Sandia, King Abdullah University of Science and Technology, the University of Helsinki and Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory.

## Limits

- The page does not name the Advisory Board members or say who appoints them.
- It does not say whether the SAC's recommendations bind the Director.
- It does not describe funding or the role of the ETH Board. For funding, see [CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md) and [CSCS annual report 2022 (web)](CSCS%20annual%20report%202022%20%28web%29.md).
