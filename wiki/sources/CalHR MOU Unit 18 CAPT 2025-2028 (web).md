# CalHR MOU Unit 18 CAPT 2025-2028 (web)

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the California Association of Psychiatric Technicians (CAPT) for State Bargaining Unit 18. Its term is July 1, 2025 to July 1, 2028.
- The State keeps all rights that the MOU does not abridge. It notifies CAPT of a change in working conditions. It meets and confers before a change that affects a "large number" of Unit 18 employees.
- Three departments meet CAPT once a year on personal services contracts with Unit 18 work. The State supports an apprenticeship program for psychiatric technicians. The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 18 is the State's psychiatric technicians, at departments such as the Department of State Hospitals (DSH) and the Department of Developmental Services (DDS). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The terms of this agreement shall go into effect on July 1, 2025 and shall remain in full force and effect through July 1, 2028." (Article 14, 14.2.1)
- "All the functions, rights, powers and authority which the employer has not specifically abridged, delegated or modified by this Agreement are recognized by the Association as being retained by the State employer." (Article 3, paragraph 1)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify CAPT of the proposed change and meet and confer pursuant to the following paragraph prior to the implementation of the change, except in cases of emergency as provided in Government Code section 3516.5." (Article 14, 14.1)
- CCHCS is California Correctional Health Care Services: "CCHCS, DSH, and DDS agree to meet with CAPT once a year to examine existing personal services contracts that call for services found in Bargaining Unit 18 class specifications." (Article 12, 12.15)
- "It is the policy of the State employer to support the continuation of an apprenticeship program for training Psychiatric Technicians." (Article 11, 11.1.1)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, staffing ratios, leaves, an apprenticeship program and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence or on technological change. Article 1.7 incorporates the Government Code sections of Dills Act section 3517.6 by reference, and does not list them.
- Article 12.15 provides for a yearly meeting only. It sets no limit on contracting out.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
