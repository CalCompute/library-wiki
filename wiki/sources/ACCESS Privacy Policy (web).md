# ACCESS Privacy Policy (web)

## Citation

ACCESS. "Privacy Policy." Version 1.4, December 9, 2025. access-ci.org. https://access-ci.org/privacy-policy/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- ACCESS treats data about the use of its services and allocated resources as public and sharable, with safeguards for private user information. It does not sell user information.
- Allocation requests and reviews stay private. The existence of an award, its size, its resources and its abstract are public.
- ACCESS does not collect or read users' research data. It shares personally identifiable information (PII) with Resource Providers to run accounts, accounting and security.

## Background

ACCESS runs the central allocation, support and accounting services of the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) for research computing. This policy covers what those central services collect, use and share. Resource Providers have their own policies. See [ACCESS](../entities/ACCESS.md), the [ACCESS acceptable use policy (web)](ACCESS%20acceptable%20use%20policy%20%28web%29.md) and the [ACCESS Allocations policy](ACCESS%20Allocations%20policy.md).

## Key claims

- "data collected by ACCESS and Resource Providers about the use of ACCESS infrastructure and ACCESS-allocated resources are presumed public and sharable, with exceptions and appropriate safeguards for private user information." (Section 2)
- "ACCESS does not sell user information to third parties." (Section 2)
- "ACCESS collects Resource Provider–reported aggregate accounting data about the use of storage, but ACCESS does not collect nor read users’ research data." (Section 4.4)
- "The existence of an award and basic information about the principal investigator (PI) and project (e.g., the size and duration of the award, on which resources the award is made, and the project abstract) are public." (Section 6.3)
- "Information included in allocation requests is treated as private communications between the submitters and the ACCESS staff and committees that evaluate the proposals." (Section 6.4)
- "As a project supported by a U.S. federal agency, most usage data about ACCESS services and for ACCESS-allocated resources is made public via the XDMoD Portal." (Section 7)
- "ACCESS may permit access by a third party to PII data for authorized research purposes. Such permission shall be contingent on approval from the third party’s Institutional Review Board." (Section 7.2)
- "ACCESS is subject to Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests and must respond to these as well as any other legitimate legal and regulatory requests." (Section 7.3)

## Evidence and method

A privacy policy of about 2,700 words in nine sections, with definitions. It states the program's own practices. It cites no audit.

## Limits

The policy does not give how long ACCESS keeps each kind of data. It does not cover data that Resource Providers or partners such as science gateways collect by other means. It names no specific privacy law other than the Freedom of Information Act.
