# Mozilla Foundation who we are (web)

## Citation

Mozilla Foundation. "This is who we are." foundation.mozilla.org. Date not stated. https://foundation.mozilla.org/en/who-we-are/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Mozilla Foundation is a non-profit that began with "$2 million in seed funding from Netscape". It calls itself "the philanthropic mission-holder of the Mozilla Project".
- The page gives its history from 1998, its work on AI, and its leadership and board. The board includes "Alondra Nelson" as a "Board Member".

## Background

The page is the Foundation's own description of its mission, history and people. Its history runs from the open source release of the Netscape code in 1998 to its "Trustworthy AI" work in the 2020s. It lists 11 staff leaders and 6 board members.

## Key claims

- "Mozilla Foundation is a non-profit building a better technology future — powered by people, open by design, fueled by imagination." (Mission)
- "Mozilla Foundation was established as a non-profit with $2 million in seed funding from Netscape to kick off an era of open source development." (History: Hello World!)
- "New initiatives include our Technology Fund, the Data Futures Lab, and an expansion of the responsible AI Fellowship." (History: Our Work on AI)
- "Common Voice is our community crowdsourcing project to ensure that tech speaks your language." (History: Our Work on AI)
- "Our Fellowship program supports over 200 technologists." (History: Sharing Knowledge)
- "Mozilla Foundation is a global non-profit and the philanthropic mission-holder of the Mozilla Project." (footer)

## Evidence and method

An organization page of about 900 words. It gives a history in five periods and names its leaders and board. It does not give figures for its budget or grants.

## Limits

The page does not give the year of each event in its history, apart from 1998, 2004 and 2018. It does not describe the relation between the Foundation and the Mozilla Corporation. [Stanford STVP Reid Hoffman profile (web)](Stanford%20STVP%20Reid%20Hoffman%20profile%20%28web%29.md) names a "Mozilla Corporation" board. The page does not discuss compute resources.
