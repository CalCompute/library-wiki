# Google Cloud Assured Workloads overview (web)

## Citation

Google Cloud. "Overview of Assured Workloads." Google Cloud documentation page, last updated Nov. 10, 2026. https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/docs/overview. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Assured Workloads sets data residency, access and personnel controls for regulated workloads on Google's commercial cloud.
- It applies "control packages" to folders. Packages cover regional data boundaries and regulatory frameworks such as FedRAMP Moderate and High, CJIS, HIPAA, IL2 to IL5, ITAR and IRS Publication 1075.
- For FedRAMP High, Google support personnel must be in the U.S. and meet enhanced background checks.

## Background

Google Cloud publishes this page in its product documentation. It describes what Assured Workloads does and lists its control packages. The page says that the names of the control packages changed in June 2025.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "Assured Workloads enables organizations in the private and public sectors to configure a sovereign data and access boundary with residency, access, and personnel controls for sensitive workloads in the cloud." (Overview)
- Commercial cloud: "Assured Workloads lets you run compliant workloads while maintaining the scale, cost, and service availability benefits of commercial cloud infrastructure." (Overview)
- Control packages: "When you create an Assured Workloads folder for a specific control package, the controls within the control package define guardrails for all projects and resources within the folder." (Assured Workloads capabilities)
- Regulatory boundaries: the list includes "Criminal Justice Information Systems (CJIS)", "FedRAMP Moderate and FedRAMP High", "Impact Level 2 (IL2), Impact Level 4 (IL4), and Impact Level 5 (IL5)" and "IRS Publication 1075". (Regulatory data boundaries)
- Personnel: "access controls that support FedRAMP High require all first-level and second-level Google support personnel and subprocessors to be located in the US and to meet enhanced background check requirements." (Control of Google personnel access to your data)
- Monitoring: "Assured Workloads monitors for organization policy constraint violations and resource violations to provide insights into compliance for a deployed control package." (Violation monitoring)
- Product coverage: "Support for Google Cloud products and services varies by control package." (Assured Workloads capabilities)

## Evidence and method

Vendor documentation. It describes the product. It does not give audit reports or authorization letters.

## Limits

- The page does not state prices. It links to a pricing page.
- The page does not list which products each control package supports. It links to another page.
- Assured Support for Assured Workloads needs Enhanced Support or Premium Support, which the page does not price.
