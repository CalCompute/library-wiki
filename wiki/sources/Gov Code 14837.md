## Bottom line

- Section 14837 defines "small business", "microbusiness", "manufacturer" and "disabled veteran business enterprise" for the state's Small Business Procurement and Contract Act.
- A small business has its principal office in California, 100 or fewer employees, and average annual gross receipts of $15,000,000 or less from Jan. 1, 2019.
- A certified small business must perform a "commercially useful function" on the contract. A pass-through role does not count.

## Background

The section is in the Government Code chapter on the Department of General Services (DGS). The Small Business Procurement and Contract Act is Chapter 6.5, sections 14835 to 14847. The library's copy comes from the Legislature's code website through the Wayback Machine.

## Key claims

- "“Small business” means an independently owned and operated business that is not dominant in its field of operation, the principal office of which is located in California, the officers of which are domiciled in California, and which, together with affiliates, has 100 or fewer employees, and average annual gross receipts of ten million dollars ($10,000,000) or less over the previous three years" (subd. (d)(1)(A))
- "Commencing January 1, 2019, the average annual gross receipts threshold shall be fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000)." (subd. (d)(1)(A))
- "Commencing January 1, 2019, the average annual gross receipts threshold shall be five million dollars ($5,000,000)." (subd. (d)(2), microbusiness)
- "A contractor, subcontractor, or supplier will not be considered to perform a commercially useful function if the contractor’s, subcontractor’s, or supplier’s role is limited to that of an extra participant in a transaction, contract, or project through which funds are passed in order to obtain the appearance of small business or microbusiness participation." (subd. (d)(4)(B))
- For public works and engineering contracts, a small business has "200 or fewer employees, and average annual gross receipts of thirty-six million dollars ($36,000,000) or less over the previous three years" (subd. (d)(1)(B))

## Limits

The section gives definitions only. The rules on preferences and participation goals are in other sections of the chapter, which this library does not hold. The thresholds can change: the Director of General Services reviews them every two years (subd. (d)(3)). The capture is from Feb. 13, 2025, and later amendments are not shown.
