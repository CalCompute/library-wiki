# UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)

## Citation

UCLA Samueli School of Engineering. "Henry Samueli, Ph.D." Profile page, page date not stated. https://samueli.ucla.edu/henry-samueli/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The engineering school of the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) gives the life of Henry Samueli, its namesake. He studied and taught at UCLA and co-founded Broadcom Corporation in 1991.
- The foundation of Henry and Susan Samueli "has granted over $500 million in gifts". One of its largest early gifts endowed the UCLA school of engineering.

## Background

The UCLA Samueli School of Engineering publishes this page in its "UCLA Samueli 101" section. The page tells his story from his childhood in Los Angeles to his work at Broadcom. It quotes him at length.

## Key claims

- "Following high school, Samueli attended UCLA’s School of Engineering, where he earned bachelor’s, master’s and Ph.D. degrees." (page text)
- "Breakthroughs in this research eventually led Samueli, and one of his Ph.D. Students, Henry Nicholas, to found Broadcom Corporation in 1991." (page text)
- "Since the company’s initial public offering in 1998, the nonprofit foundation run by Samueli and his wife Susan has granted over $500 million in gifts in the areas of education, health, social services, and the arts." (page text)
- "One of our largest initial gifts was back to UCLA to endow the school of engineering. It was (then) named after me, which was an amazing honor." (Henry Samueli)
- "Samueli maintains his position as a UCLA faculty member, though on leave." (page text)
- "Dr. Samueli serves on the UC President’s Board on Science and Innovation, is Chair of the UCLA Chancellor’s Competitiveness Council and serves on the Dean’s Executive Board at UCLA Samueli." (Recognition)

## Evidence and method

A profile page by the school that carries his name. It gives these honours:

- The University of California Presidential Medal.
- Fellow of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), 2000.
- Member of the National Academy of Engineering, 2003.
- Fellow of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences, 2004.

## Limits

The page has no date, so its figures, for example "$18 billion in annual revenue" for Broadcom, are of an unknown date. It does not give the amount of the gift to UCLA. It does not mention AI.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page dates the Broadcom public offering to 1998: "Since the company’s initial public offering in 1998". The Samuelis' pledge letter gives 1993: "We formed our family foundation in 1993 when Broadcom went public."
