## Bottom line

- Researchers at UC Irvine, UCLA and the University of Michigan received "nearly $9 million" from the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) for an award called NSF BRIDGE. The NSF Integrated Data Systems and Services (IDSS) program funds it.
- NSF BRIDGE helps scientific communities deploy data platforms built on Texera, an open-source system. The platforms can run on public clouds such as Amazon Web Services (AWS) or on research laboratory clusters.

## Background

UC Irvine computer science professor Chen Li leads the three-year project as Principal Investigator. The co-principal investigators are Wei Wang of UCLA, and Ardalan Amiri Sani, Kristin Turney and Rui Chen of UC Irvine. Subawards go to Wei Wang and Naomi Sugie of UCLA, Steve Parker of the University of Michigan, and the UC Irvine Research Cyberinfrastructure Center (RCIC). Texera started in 2016, and the Apache Software Foundation now incubates it.
