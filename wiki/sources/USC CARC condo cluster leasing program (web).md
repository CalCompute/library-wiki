# USC CARC condo cluster leasing program (web)

## Citation

University of Southern California (USC), Center for Advanced Research Computing (CARC). "Condo Cluster Leasing Program." CARC user guide, last updated March 6, 2024. https://www.carc.usc.edu/user-guides/hpc-systems/endeavour/condo-cluster-program/leasing-model. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 USC CARC condo cluster leasing program.md`.

## Bottom line

- USC research groups can lease compute nodes on the Endeavour cluster for one year at a time. CARC buys and maintains the hardware.
- A node costs $2,000 to $8,000 a year. The node with 2 A100 GPUs is $8,000 a year.
- Leases follow the fiscal year from July 1. A lease cannot end early, and CARC bills the PI's department.

## Background

CARC runs the research computing systems of USC. Endeavour is its condo cluster. This user guide gives the lease term, the four node types and their prices. It also tells how to ask for a lease in the CARC user portal. The leased nodes are for the group's own condo job queue.

## Key claims

- "The annual leasing model allows research groups to subscribe to their selected number of compute nodes on a yearly basis." (introduction)
- "All of the hardware is purchased and maintained by CARC throughout the course of the lease term." (introduction)
- "Due to limitations with the administration of annual budgets by USC departments, CARC is unable to offer multi-year lease terms." (1 Lease term)
- "*Leases cannot be terminated early.*" (1 Lease term)
- "Dell R6525 | 64 | 2.6 | 256 GB | - | - | $2,400/year" (2 Compute resources available for lease)
- "Dell R7525 | 32 | 3.0 | 256 GB | 2 A40 | 48 GB | $4,000/year" (2 Compute resources available for lease)
- "Dell R7525 | 64 | 2.6 | 256 GB | 2 A100 | 80 GB | $8,000/year" (2 Compute resources available for lease)
- "Dell R740 | 32 | 2.1 | 192 GB | 2 V100 | 32 GB | $2,000/year" (2 Compute resources available for lease)

## Evidence and method

A user guide page with one price table and the steps to ask for a lease. The request needs a justification and an estimate of service unit hours. A PI must also complete an annual project review. CARC then sets the lease to "Active".

## Limits

- The page was last updated on March 6, 2024. The prices and node types can be different now.
- It does not say whether groups outside USC can lease nodes.
- It does not say what the lease price covers, for example power, staff or a hardware reserve.
- It gives no count of leased nodes or of research groups.
