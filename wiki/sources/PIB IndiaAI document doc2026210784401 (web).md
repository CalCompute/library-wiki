# PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)

## Citation

Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India, PIB Research. *Democratising AI in India: Expanding Access to AI Resources, Skills, and Technology Nationwide.* Backgrounder, February 10, 2026. PDF. https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2026/feb/doc2026210784401.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Under the IndiaAI Mission, more than 38,000 GPUs are available at ₹65 per hour. The document says this is "nearly one third of the global average cost".
- The Government of India approved the Mission in March 2024, with an outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore over five years.
- The document also describes AIKosh (7,541 datasets and 273 models), AI skills programs, and fellowships for PhD, postgraduate and undergraduate students.

## Background

PIB is the Government of India's agency for information to the media. It issued this backgrounder six days before the India–AI Impact Summit 2026 in New Delhi (16 to 20 February 2026). The document has sections on applications, infrastructure (data, compute, chips, data centres, energy), policy, skills and global cooperation. It has no page numbers; the sections below use its headings.

## Key claims

- Compute: "Under the IndiaAI Mission, more than 38,000 high-end GPUs have been onboarded and are available at ₹65 per hour, which is nearly one third of the global average cost. In addition, 1,050 TPUs have also been onboarded". (Access to Compute)
- Outlay: the Mission was "Approved in March 2024 with a financial outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore over five years". (Democratising Access to AI Infrastructure)
- Supercomputing: "Over 40 petaflops of capacity has been deployed across IITs, IISERs and research institutions" through the National Supercomputing Mission. (Access to Compute)
- Datasets: "As of February 2026, AIKosh brings together 7,541 datasets and 273 AI models spanning 20 sectors on a single national platform." (AIKosh)
- Models: "more than 500 proposals were received, and 12 startups were selected across the first two phases" to build models trained on Indian data and languages. (Access to Datasets and Models)
- Government cloud: "as of December 2025, 2,170 Ministries and Departments have already hosted applications on MeghRaj". (Regulatory and Policy Environment)
- Public officials: "an AI Competency Framework has been introduced for public officials." (Education, Skilling and AI Literacy)
- Fellowships: "Support is being provided to 500 PhD scholars, 5000 postgraduate students and 8000 undergraduate students." (Education, Skilling and AI Literacy)
- Labs: "31 labs have already been launched in collaboration with NIELIT and industry partners" in Tier two and Tier three cities. (Education, Skilling and AI Literacy)

## Evidence and method

This is a government backgrounder. It gives program figures with "as of" dates, but no sources for most of them. It gives no method for the comparison with the "global average cost".

## Limits

- The document does not say how the ₹65 per hour price is set, or who pays the difference. The IndiaAI allocation table shows a subsidy for each award ([IndiaAI compute capacity (web)](IndiaAI%20compute%20capacity%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives no source for "nearly one third of the global average cost".
- It is a summit backgrounder. It reports programs, not results.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this document says the Summit brings together "15 to 20 Heads of Government, over 50 international ministers" (Global Cooperation). The PIB document of 17 February 2026 says "over 20 Heads of State, 60 Ministers, and 500 global AI leaders" ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026217795001 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026217795001%20%28web%29.md)).
