# Introducing the MIT AI Research Exchange (web)

## Citation

Markus J. Buehler, Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) AI Exchange. "Introducing the MIT AI Research Exchange." Letter to the MIT research community, November 3, 2026. https://aiexchange.mit.edu/news/introducing-the-mit-ai-research-exchange/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The faculty director of the MIT AI Research Exchange describes its plans. It is one part of the MIT AI Exchange, an Institute-wide effort on AI in research.
- Its main parts are "AI + X" efforts led by the schools, and Communities of Practice in Research (COP-Rs). It also plans workshops and AI training, and holds listening sessions in November 2026.

## Background

The MIT AI Exchange has two parts: the Learning Exchange and the Research Exchange. This letter follows a launch message from MIT's President, Provost and Chancellor. The author is the faculty director of the Research Exchange.

## Key claims

- "Created to help our community expand the scope and ambition of research with AI, the Research Exchange has a three-part mission: to empower researchers to tackle ambitious problems and pursue questions previously beyond reach by connecting expertise across research groups and communities; to develop and share methods and tools; and to strengthen the human creativity, judgment, and expertise that drive discovery." (opening)
- "This is an Institute-wide effort that will connect activities across all five schools and the college, and across departments, labs and centers." (opening)
- "The central focal areas we envision in the Research Exchange are "AI + X" efforts, led by the schools with the college, and Communities of Practice in Research (COP-Rs)." (opening)
- "The Research Exchange will also help identify shared needs, connect researchers with relevant expertise, and disseminate practical experience with AI-enabled research." (Identifying and providing needed resources)
- "We are planning workshops, research showcases and hands-on AI training, alongside support for specialized workshops, working groups and committees." (Identifying and providing needed resources)

## Evidence and method

A letter of announcement from the program's director. It states plans and dates of community meetings. It gives no budget, staff count or compute figures.

## Limits

The letter does not describe computing resources, models or funding. The "AI + X" details are for the deans to share later. See [MIT AI Research Exchange (web)](MIT%20AI%20Research%20Exchange%20%28web%29.md) for the program page.
