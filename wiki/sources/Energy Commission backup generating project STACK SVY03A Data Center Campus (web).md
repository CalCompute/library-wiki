# Energy Commission backup generating project STACK SVY03A Data Center Campus (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "STACK SVY03A Data Center Campus." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/stack-svy03a-data-center-campus. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project STACK SVY03A Data Center Campus.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20STACK%20SVY03A%20Data%20Center%20Campus.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project STACK SVY03A Data Center Campus.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for the SVY03A Data Center Campus of STACK Infrastructure in Hayward. Its backup generators give up to 76.6 megawatts (MW). Its status is "Exempted".
- The campus has two data center buildings and 28 emergency generators in two yards, with redundant generators included.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. STACK Infrastructure is the project owner. The docket number is 23-SPPE-01, the capacity is "Up to 76.6 megawatts" and the location is Hayward. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "STACK Infrastructure is proposing to develop the SVY03A Data Center Campus (campus), which would be located in the City of Hayward at 26203 Production Avenue." (Project Description)
- "The proposed campus would include a three-story data center building, a one-story data center building, backup generators to support both data centers, a security building, an on-site project substation, and a Pacific Gas and Electric Company switching station." (Project Description)
- "The backup generators would have a total generation capacity of up to 76.6 megawatts (MW)." (Project Description)
- "A total of 28 emergency generators would be arranged in two generation yards, with 26 2.75 MW generators and a single 1 MW generator serving the larger data center building, and a single 1.6 MW generator serving the smaller data center building." (Project Description)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Limits

- The page gives no key dates and no date for the exemption.
- It does not say the fuel of the generators. It gives no floor area of the buildings.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
