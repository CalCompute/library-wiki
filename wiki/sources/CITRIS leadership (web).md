# CITRIS leadership (web)

## Citation

CITRIS and the Banatao Institute. "Leadership." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://citris-uc.org/people/leadership/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- Alexandre Bayen is Director and Camille Crittenden is Executive Director of CITRIS and the Banatao Institute.
- The page lists three campus directors (UC Merced, UC Davis, UC Santa Cruz) and the heads of three research initiatives. It also lists the heads of two labs, five operations directors and past directors.

## Background

CITRIS is the Center for Information Technology Research in the Interest of Society. It is a UC institute on four campuses: Berkeley, Davis, Merced and Santa Cruz ([CITRIS About (web)](CITRIS%20About%20%28web%29.md)). This page in its People section lists the institute's leaders by group. [CITRIS advisory board (web)](CITRIS%20advisory%20board%20%28web%29.md) lists the outside advisors.

## Evidence and method

A list page with a name and a title for each person. The groups are:

- Research Initiatives: CITRIS Health (co-directors Nicholas R. Anderson and James Marcin, executive director David Lindeman), CITRIS Climate (director Michele Barbato, executive director Carl Blumstein) and CITRIS Aviation (director Ricardo Sanfelice).
- Labs and Programs: Jill Finlayson (CITRIS Innovation Hub), and Kristofer Pister and Michael Helmbrecht (Marvell NanoLab).
- Operations: development, communications, events, corporate relations, and finance and research support.
- Directors Emeriti: Ruzena Bajcsy, Randy Katz, S. Shankar Sastry, Paul Wright and Costas Spanos, with their years.
- Campus Directors Emeriti: nine people at UC Davis, UC Merced and UC Santa Cruz.
