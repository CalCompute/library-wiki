# NERSC Doudna announcement 2025-05-29 (web)

Ball, Elizabeth. "DOE Announces New Supercomputer Powered by Dell and NVIDIA to Speed Scientific Discovery." National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC), Center News, May 29, 2025. https://www.nersc.gov/news-and-events/news/doe-announces-new-supercomputer-powered-by-dell-and-nvidia-to-speed-scientific-discovery. ## Bottom line

- The Secretary of Energy announced a contract with Dell Technologies for NERSC-10, the next NERSC flagship supercomputer. It is due in 2026. Its name is *Doudna*.
- *Doudna* uses NVIDIA's Vera Rubin platform and Dell liquid-cooled servers. It "will provide more than ten times the performance of Perlmutter".

## Background

NERSC is a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) user facility at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (Berkeley Lab). The Secretary made the announcement during a visit to Berkeley Lab. The news item quotes the Secretary, Dell, NVIDIA and the NERSC Director. "Berkeley Lab is a multiprogram national laboratory managed by the University of California for the U.S. Department of Energy's Office of Science."

## Key claims

- "Secretary of Energy Chris Wright today announced a new contract with Dell Technologies to develop NERSC-10, the next flagship supercomputer at the National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC)". (lead paragraph)
- "The new system, due in 2026, will be named in honor of Jennifer Doudna". (lead paragraph)
- "The Doudna supercomputer will provide more than ten times the performance of Perlmutter, NERSC's current flagship supercomputer." (system description)
- "Doudna will be built using cutting-edge technology such as Dell's most advanced ORv3 direct liquid-cooled server technology and the NVIDIA Vera-Rubin CPU-GPU platform." (system description)
- "We are collaborating with NVIDIA and Dell to prepare our 11,000 users to effectively use this system's exciting new workflow capabilities". (quote of the NERSC Director)
- "Doudna will be connected to DOE experimental and observational facilities through the Energy Sciences Network (ESnet)". (quote of the NERSC Director)
- "the system's powerful GPUs will enable DOE-funded researchers to seamlessly integrate large-scale AI into their simulation and data analysis workflows". (system description)
- NERSC supports "more than 11,000 scientists and 800 projects annually." (boilerplate)

## Evidence and method

A news item from the facility. It describes a contract and the planned system. It quotes officials and the vendors. It gives no contract value, no node count and no peak performance figure.

## Limits

The item does not give the cost of the contract or the system. It gives performance only as a multiple of Perlmutter. It does not give a delivery month. It describes a planned system in May 2025, not one in operation.
