# Microsoft nonprofit Azure grant activation (web)

## Citation

Microsoft. "Activate your Azure grant." Microsoft Learn documentation page, Microsoft for Nonprofits, May 22, 2025 (page metadata). https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/nonprofit/microsoft-for-nonprofits/claim-activate-nonprofit-azure-grant. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Microsoft gives a nonprofit that it approves a $2,000 Azure Sponsorship grant each year.
- The nonprofit activates the grant in the Nonprofit Hub within 90 days. The credit lasts one year, and the nonprofit must renew it each year.
- The credit covers first-party Azure services only. It does not roll over.

## Background

Microsoft publishes this page in its Microsoft Learn documentation for Microsoft for Nonprofits. It tells an approved nonprofit how to activate and manage its Azure grant.

## Key claims

- Offer: "Once your organization is approved for Microsoft’s nonprofit programs, you can access the $2,000 USD annual Azure Sponsorship grant" (introduction)
- Route: "Sign in to the Nonprofit Hub by using your validated Microsoft account." Then go to the Offers tab and activate the "Azure $2,000 Sponsorship" offer. (Activate your Azure Sponsorship grant)
- Deadline: "You must activate your Azure credit within 90 days of offer issuance." (Activation deadline)
- Coverage: "Azure Sponsorship credit applies only to first-party Microsoft Azure services." The nonprofit cannot use it for reserved instances, third-party marketplace offers or support plans. (Important notes about activation)
- Term: "Your Azure credit is active for one year and must be renewed annually." (FAQ)
- No rollover: "Your credit does not roll over from year to year." (FAQ)
- Billing: "A valid credit card might be required during setup, but you won't be charged if you stay within the $2,000 grant limit." (FAQ)

## Evidence and method

Vendor documentation with step-by-step instructions. It states the rules of the grant. It gives no figures on use.

## Limits

- The page does not state the eligibility rules for Microsoft's nonprofit programs. It starts after approval.
- The page does not state how many nonprofits receive the grant.
