# Jupyter standing committees and working groups (web)

## Citation

Project Jupyter. "Standing Committees and Working Groups." Web page in the Jupyter Governance site, date not stated. https://jupyter.org/governance/standing-committees-and-working-groups. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Standing Committees and Working Groups do the work of Project Jupyter that is not software, by delegation from the Executive Council (EC).
- Standing Committees are permanent. The EC and the Software Steering Council (SSC) create and dissolve them together.
- The EC alone creates and dissolves Working Groups.

## Background

Project Jupyter publishes this page in its governance site. It gives examples of the work: code of conduct incident response, diversity and inclusion, operations, legal, fundraising, events, community and marketing. The page links to a separate list of the committees and groups. The site states the content licence "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)".

## Key claims

- Accountability: Standing Committees and Working Groups both "Are managed by, report to, and are accountable to the Executive Council." (Common Points)
- Charters: both "Have charters that define their scope and purpose. Within those charters, they are free to make decisions autonomously." (Common Points)
- Transparency: both "Maintain a public GitHub repository with basic information (council members, charter, public meetings, SSC representative, etc.) for purposes of transparency and consistency." (Common Points)
- Standing Committees: they "are established and dissolved by joint decisions of the SSC and EC." (Standing Committees)
- Resources: "The EC is responsible for ensuring the Standing Committees are appropriately resourced at all times." (Standing Committees)
- Working Groups: they "are established and dissolved by decisions of EC." (Working Groups)
- Representation: "Working Groups, by default, do not have representatives on the SSC." (Working Groups)

## Evidence and method

This is the project's own governance rule. It states how the two kinds of group start, end and report. It gives no list of groups on this page.

## Limits

- The page does not name the current Standing Committees or Working Groups. A separate page lists them.
- It does not say how the EC resources a Standing Committee, for example with money or staff.
