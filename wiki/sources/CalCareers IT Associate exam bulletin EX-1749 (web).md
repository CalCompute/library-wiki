# CalCareers IT Associate exam bulletin EX-1749 (web)

## Citation

California Department of Human Resources (CalHR). "Exam Bulletin: Information Technology Associate." Exam code EX-1749, CalCareers, continuous filing, date not stated. https://www.calcareers.ca.gov/CalHrPublic/Exams/ExamBulletin.aspx?ExamControlId=1749. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CalCareers IT Associate exam bulletin EX-1749.html`.

## Bottom line

- The bulletin is the open, servicewide exam for the state class Information Technology Associate, the working level of state IT work.
- The pay is $4,935 to $8,740 each month in ranges A to D, and $5,084 to $9,004 in ranges L to O. Applicants need 18 months as an IT Technician, two years of IT experience, or 60 semester units with 15 units of IT course work.

## Background

CalHR runs the state civil service exams on CalCareers. The exam is a 75-minute timed online exam. An applicant who passes with 70 percent goes on an eligible list that departments use to fill jobs in the whole state. The list expires after 12 months.

## Key claims

- "INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ASSOCIATE: $4,935.00 - $6,614.00 (A) per Month" and "INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ASSOCIATE: $6,718.00 - $9,004.00 (O) per Month" (Classification Details)
- "Eighteen months as an Information Technology Technician; **or** Two years of general information technology experience performing technical, analytical, or support tasks for computer systems or services in any of the six domains or emerging information technology fields; **or** 60 semester units (or 90 quarter units) from an accredited college or university including at least 15 semester units (or 22.5 quarter units) of information technology or closely related course work; **or** Any equivalent combination of experience and education." (Minimum Qualifications)
- "Under general supervision, incumbents typically perform a variety of recurring, well-defined tasks requiring occasional innovative problem-solving within guidelines and a scope that may encompass one or more units, functions or processes in the Business Technology Management, Client Services, or Software Engineering domains." (Position Description)
- "Note: This is a 75-minute timed examination." (Examination Information)
- "A final score of 70% must be attained to be placed on the eligible list." (Examination Information)
- "Eligibility expires **12 months** after it is established." and "Resulting Eligibility List(s) will be used to fill vacancies throughout the State of California." (Eligible List Information)

## Evidence and method

An official exam bulletin. It gives the eight pay ranges, the minimum qualifications, the duties, the knowledge and abilities the exam tests, the eligible list rules and Veterans' Preference.

## Limits

The bulletin does not say what each range letter requires. It does not name AI, machine learning or research computing among its domains or duties. It does not give how many people hold the class.
