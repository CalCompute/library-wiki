# Anthropic Claude for Nonprofits (web)

## Citation

Anthropic. "Claude for Nonprofits." Anthropic news post, Dec. 2, 2025 (page metadata; modified Oct. 19, 2026). https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-nonprofits. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Anthropic gives nonprofits a discount of up to 75 percent on its Claude Team and Enterprise plans.
- The offer also has connectors to the nonprofit tools Blackbaud, Candid and Benevity, and a free course, AI Fluency for Nonprofits.
- Anthropic launched the offer with GivingTuesday and piloted Claude with more than 60 grantee organizations of three funders.

## Background

Anthropic is an AI company. This post announces its program for nonprofits. The page metadata dates the post Dec. 2, 2025, and shows a change on Oct. 19, 2026. The model names in the text are from the later version.

## Key claims

- Offer: "Claude for Nonprofits includes three things: discounted access of up to 75% to Claude, connectors to new nonprofit tools—Blackbaud, Candid, and Benevity—and a free course, AI Fluency for Nonprofits, designed to help teams use AI more effectively." (introduction)
- Discount: "Nonprofits are now eligible for a discount of up to 75% on Team and Enterprise plans." (Discounted access to Team and Enterprise plans)
- Models: "At the discounted price, Claude for Nonprofits includes access to Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Sonnet 4.5, and Claude Haiku 4.5." (Discounted access to Team and Enterprise plans)
- Training: the course "is designed for those who are new to AI, and requires no technical background." (AI Fluency for Nonprofits)
- Pilot: Anthropic partnered with the Constellation Fund, Robin Hood and Tipping Point Community "to pilot Claude with more than 60 of their grantee organizations." (Learning from our partners and customers)
- Partners: Anthropic is "collaborating with The Bridgespan Group, Idealist Consulting, Vera Solutions, and Slalom, who provide tailored expertise to nonprofits adopting new technologies." (Connecting new services to Claude)

## Evidence and method

A company announcement. It gives the offer and quotes from customers and partners. The figures for customer results, such as "up to 16× faster", come from the customers and have no method.

## Limits

- The post does not state the eligibility rules or the price after the discount.
- The post does not state how many nonprofits use the offer.
- The post links to claude.com/solutions/nonprofits for the route to apply. This library does not have that page.
