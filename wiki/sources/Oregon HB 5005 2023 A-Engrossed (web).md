# Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed (web)

## Citation

Oregon Legislative Assembly. House Bill 5005, A-Engrossed, 82nd Oregon Legislative Assembly, 2023 Regular Session. Ordered by the House June 21, 2023. https://apps.oregonlegislature.gov/liz/2023R1/Downloads/MeasureDocument/HB5005/A-Engrossed. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed.pdf`.

## Bottom line

- House Bill (HB) 5005 sets the amounts of state bonds for the 2023-2025 biennium.
- Section 1 (1)(m) authorizes $72,680,000 in general obligation bonds for the "Oregon State University, Collaborative Innovation Complex".
- Section 9 requires matching funds at least equal to the bonds. Section 11 sets apprentice and benefit rules for the contracts of this project.

## Background

HB 5005 is the 2023 Oregon bond bill. The Oregon Department of Administrative Services requested it. This A-Engrossed version includes the House amendments of June 21, 2023. The bill declares an emergency and takes effect July 1, 2023. The Collaborative Innovation Complex is the building that Oregon State University calls the Huang Complex.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "Establishes amounts authorized for issuance of general obligation bonds, revenue bonds, certif- icates of participation and other financing agreements for biennium." (Summary)
- Amount: item "(m) Oregon State University, Collaborative Innovation Complex" has the amount "$ 72,680,000" (Section 1 (1), under "Higher Education Coordinating Commission (Art. XI-G)", page 2)
- Matching funds: bonds "may not be issued until the constructing authority certifies that the constructing authority has matching funds available for the same or similar purposes as the bonds" (Section 9)
- Match size: the matching funds must be "available to the constructing authority in an amount at least equal to the amount of indebtedness incurred by the state through the is- suance of the bonds." (Section 9)
- Apprentices: each contractor in a contract of $200,000 or more must "Employ apprentices to perform 15 percent of the work hours that workers in apprenticeable occupations perform under the contract" (Section 11 (1)(a))
- Benefits: each such contractor must "Provide health insurance and retirement benefits to workers" (Section 11 (1)(c))
- Qualified project: "(B) The Oregon State University Collaborative Innovation Complex referenced in section 1 (1)(m) of this 2023 Act" (Section 11 (4)(e))

## Evidence and method

This is the text of a bill as amended by the House. It is a primary legal source. It lists bond amounts by agency and project, and conditions on their issue. It gives no description of the building or of its computing systems. The Markdown copy breaks some words across lines, as in "certif- icates" and "is- suance". The quotations keep those breaks.

## Limits

- This is the A-Engrossed version, not the enrolled act. It does not show later amendments or the Governor's signature.
- The bill does not describe the supercomputer or say who may use it.
- It does not say where the matching funds come from. For the private gift that the university reports, see [Oregon State CIC 72 million legislature release (web)](Oregon%20State%20CIC%2072%20million%20legislature%20release%20%28web%29.md).
