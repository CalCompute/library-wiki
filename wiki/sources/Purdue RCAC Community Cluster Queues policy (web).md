# Purdue RCAC Community Cluster Queues policy (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). "Community Cluster Queues." Policy page, date not stated. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/policies/communityclusterqueues. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- On Purdue's community clusters, a research group that buys access gets a priority queue sized to the cores it bought, with very long jobs.
- A "standby" queue is open to anyone and uses idle nodes, with a short run time per job, usually about 4 hours.

## Background

This is a short policy page on the RCAC website. Purdue IT operates the community clusters. The page explains the two kinds of queue on each cluster and why the standby queue has a short time limit.

## Key claims

- "Purdue IT operates a number of Community Clusters which allow researchers to purchase access to a large computational cluster through the Community Cluster Program." (paragraph 1)
- "These systems offer priority access to a portion of the system as paid for by the researcher, but also offer "standby" access to the rest of the system, whenever it would otherwise not be busy." (paragraph 1)
- "This is restricted to the number of cores to which access was purchased, allows for very long jobs, and is usually named after the group or faculty member." (paragraph 2)
- "This queue is open to anyone, but offers only a relatively short run time per job, usually about 4 hours." (paragraph 3)
- "This ensures that if jobs come in to other queues, any running standby jobs will complete soon and their nodes made available to the priority queues." (paragraph 3)
- "The exact names, configurations, and limits on these queues may vary slightly from system to system." (paragraph 4)

## Evidence and method

A policy page that states the scheduling rules in four paragraphs. It gives one number, the standby run time of "about 4 hours". It gives no prices.

## Limits

The page does not say who "anyone" covers for the standby queue, for example only Purdue users. It does not give prices, the long-job limit or rules for each cluster. [Purdue RCAC Gautschi overview (web)](Purdue%20RCAC%20Gautschi%20overview%20%28web%29.md) gives the purchase units for one cluster.
