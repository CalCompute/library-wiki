## Bottom line

- This is the landing page of PTAC's Written Agreement Checklist. It gives a one-sentence description and a link to the PDF.
- The PDF lists the mandatory terms of a written agreement under two FERPA exceptions. See [ED PTAC written agreement checklist PDF (web)](ED%20PTAC%20written%20agreement%20checklist%20PDF%20%28web%29.md).

## Background

The Department of Education's Student Privacy website publishes FERPA guidance from PTAC. This resource page describes the checklist and links to related guides for parents and eligible students.
