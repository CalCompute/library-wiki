# ODI Work with us (web)

## Citation

California Office of Data and Innovation (ODI). "Work with us." Services page, date not stated. https://innovation.ca.gov/work-with-us/. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-09 ODI Work with us.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-09%20ODI%20Work%20with%20us.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- ODI offers six services to state departments. They are the Analytics Accelerator, the Data Advisory Service, the Data Science Accelerator, the Innovation Lab, the Modern Data Stack Accelerator and training.
- The Modern Data Stack Accelerator is an experiment. It tests if departments can adopt cloud data tools in house, faster and cheaper than with contractors.

## Background

ODI is a state office that helps departments with data, design and technology. This page lists the services a department can ask for, with one short paragraph on each. The library's [ODI homepage (web)](ODI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md) describes the office.

## Key claims

- "ODI's services help state departments solve business problems and spend more time serving Californians." (top of page)
- "Save analyst time by streamlining mandatory reporting via adoption of PowerBI"; "Optimize that liberated analyst time via training in modern analytics approaches". (Analytics Accelerator)
- "You'll get advice, not a deliverable, to help you solve complex problems about data engineering and data science." (Data Advisory Service)
- "The Modern Data Stack Accelerator (MDSA) will help teams rapidly learn and adopt modern cloud-based data tools to solve problems like combining data across systems or program areas." (Modern Data Stack Accelerator)
- "Departments often rely on costly and time consuming RFPs or contractors to do this work. This experimental service will test if we can meet this need in house in a way that is faster and cheaper than traditional methods." (Modern Data Stack Accelerator)
- "Our courses cover: Human-centered design, Plain language, Product approach, Working with data. Most are free and have individual and group options." (Training)

## Evidence and method

A state agency services page. It describes each service in one paragraph. It gives no figures on cost, use or results.

## Limits

The page does not name the cloud tools of the Modern Data Stack Accelerator. It does not say what a service costs a department, or how many departments use it. It has no AI or compute service. The linked detail pages were not read.
