# Hewlett Foundation about us (web)

## Citation

William and Flora Hewlett Foundation. "About Us." Web page, date not stated. https://hewlett.org/about-us/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Hewlett Foundation is a nonpartisan philanthropy in the Bay Area, set up in 1966 by Bill and Flora Hewlett.
- In 2024 it awarded $631 million in grants. Its assets were about $13.9 billion at the end of 2024.

## Background

The foundation's About page gives its history, values, programs, grants and people. It says the foundation is independent of HP and Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE).

## Key claims

- "Established in 1966, the Hewlett Foundation is a nonpartisan philanthropy created through the personal generosity of engineer and entrepreneur Bill Hewlett and his wife, Flora." (Our History)
- "The foundation is wholly independent of HP, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), and the HPE Foundation." (Our History)
- "In 2024, the foundation awarded $631 million in grants, with nearly three-fourths of that total awarded as flexible funding." (Our Grants)
- "As of December 31, 2024, the foundation’s assets were approximately $13.9 billion." (Our Grants)
- "Our board of directors always includes four members of the Hewlett family and between five and 11 other leaders drawn from philanthropy, government, business, education, and civil society." (Our People)

## Evidence and method

An organization page. It gives the grant total, the asset total, the number of active grants (about 3,000) and the number of employees (about 130).

## Limits

The page does not describe an AI or compute program. It lists program areas (education, democracy, conservation, climate, reproductive health and others) but no grant amounts by area.
