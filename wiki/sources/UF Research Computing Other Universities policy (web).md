# UF Research Computing Other Universities policy (web)

## Citation

University of Florida (UF), UF Information Technology (UFIT) Research Computing. "Other Universities." Policy page, date not stated. https://it.ufl.edu/rc/documentation/policies/other-universities/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- UF opens HiPerGator to two groups of other universities. Faculty in the State University System of Florida (SUS) can use it for teaching and research, under UF's own policies. Southeastern Conference (SEC) members can use it for teaching only, at no cost.
- Teaching use has no cost. SUS research use needs resources bought at subsidized rates.
- Each partner gets a standing allocation for course preparation and up to three courses each semester. A local unit at the partner gives front-line support.

## Background

UFIT Research Computing runs HiPerGator. The page says UF shares it "As part of the AI Initiative". It sets out the policies for sharing and the steps to get access. The page spells the athletic conference as "Southeast Conference".

## Key claims

- Two classes: "Colleges and universities in the State of Florida State University System (SUS) can use HiPerGator for teaching and research whereas Southeast Conference (SEC) universities can only use HiPerGator for teaching." (Use of HiPerGator by faculty and researchers at other colleges and universities)
- SUS agreement: "The University of Florida has reached an agreement with the Board of Governors of the State University System (SUS) of Florida that all faculty at universities in the State of Florida can use HiPerGator, which includes HiPerGator 3.0, HiPerGator AI, and HiPerGator-RV under the same policies and procedures as faculty at the University of Florida." (Universities in the Florida State University System (SUS))
- Cost: "Note that the teaching use has no cost associated with it, but the use for research, including graduate student research, does require purchasing resources at the subsidized rates published." (same section)
- SEC: "The Provost of the University of Florida has invited all member institutions of the SEC to use HiPerGator for teaching only at no cost." (Universities in the Southeast Conference (SEC))
- Standing allocation: "Each partner institution will receive a standing allocation of 32 NCUs, 2TiB Blue storage, and 4 NGUs." (Standing allocation for course preparation)
- Course limit: "Teaching access to HiPerGator is limited to three courses per partner institution per semester." (Course number limits)
- Support: "The scale of staff is insufficient to support the faculty at all partner universities. The front-line support for faculty at any partner university comes from a local unit supporting research computing or IT." (User Support model)
- Sign-in: "Authentication of students and faculty needing access to HiPerGator works through InCommon Federation using the credentials of each partner institution." (Authentication and authorization)

## Evidence and method

A policy page with rules for access, cost, support, sign-in and training. Other rules:

- The IT point of contact at each partner holds the standing allocation and renews it each year.
- SUS faculty who invest for research set up a group and use the UFIT Research Computing (UFRC) purchase request forms. "Teaching resources cannot be used for research."
- UF requires multi-factor authentication (MFA) at the partner. The partner's InCommon identity provider must pass the MFA confirmation to UF.
- All users must take the HiPerGator Account training.

## Limits

- The page gives no rates. It links the published subsidized rates, which the library has not read.
- It does not say what an NCU or an NGU is. [UF Research Computing Groups and Allocations (web)](UF%20Research%20Computing%20Groups%20and%20Allocations%20%28web%29.md) defines NCUs as Normalized Computing Units.
- It does not give the date of the SUS agreement or the SEC invitation, or how many partners use HiPerGator.
- It does not cover Florida colleges outside the SUS, or private universities.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: [UF AI HiPerGator page (web)](UF%20AI%20HiPerGator%20page%20%28web%29.md) says HiPerGator "can be used by faculty and their students and researchers and their collaborators at all Florida colleges and universities for teaching and research". This policy page gives research use to SUS universities and teaching use to SUS and SEC universities only. It names no other Florida colleges.
