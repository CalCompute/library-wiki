# NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF), Directorate for Computer and Information Science and Engineering (CISE). "NSF establishes operations center for the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource." CISE update, Sept. 1, 2026. https://www.nsf.gov/cise/updates/nsf-establishes-operations-center-national-artificial. Read 2026-11-11. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 NSF NAIRR Operations Center update.html`.

## Bottom line

- NSF is setting up the NSF National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Operations Center (NSF NAIRR-OC). It is to run NAIRR as a long-term national resource after the pilot.
- The San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at UC San Diego leads it. The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) at The University of Texas at Austin is its partner.
- NSF says the pilot has supported over 800 research projects since 2024, with over 25 private-sector partners.

## Background

This is NSF's own notice of the award. SDSC published its release on the same day ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)). The NSF award record gives the amount and dates ([Würthwein NSF award 2616251 (web)](W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202616251%20%28web%29.md)). The update also names three NSF programs that the Operations Center will coordinate with.

## Key claims

- "Working closely with the agency, NSF NAIRR-OC will serve as the operational backbone of NAIRR. It will coordinate participating resource providers; integrate computing, data, models and tools; operate the national NAIRR portal; and provide support and training to users." (paragraph 2)
- "The center will also gather input from the research community to continually improve NAIRR's capabilities and lead the transition of appropriate functions from the NAIRR pilot into the long-term national infrastructure." (paragraph 2)
- "Since its launch in 2024, the pilot has supported over 800 research projects and thousands of innovators and students nationwide. With over 25 private-sector partners, including major technology leaders and nonprofit organizations, this novel public-private partnership has expanded access to resources" (paragraph 3)
- "Led by the San Diego Supercomputer Center at UC San Diego, in collaboration with the Texas Advanced Computing Center at The University of Texas at Austin, the operating center will bring complementary expertise and decades of experience developing and operating national-scale research infrastructure." (paragraph 4)
- "UC San Diego is home to the San Diego Supercomputer Center and leads the NSF-supported National Data Platform and National Research Platform, in addition to supporting advanced computing platforms." (paragraph 4)
- "The NSF State and Regional AI Infrastructure Hubs program will bring together research institutions, government, industry and philanthropy to expand regional access to computing resources and develop the workforce needed to use them." (paragraph 6)
- "The NAIRR-OC will work closely with federal and private-sector resource providers, strengthening the public-private partnership model" (paragraph 7)

## Evidence and method

A federal agency notice of an award. It states the center's tasks, its lead institutions and the pilot's results in round numbers. It gives no amount and no dates for the award.

## Limits

- The update gives no dollar amount. The SDSC release and the NSF award record give "$35 million" and an estimated total of "35000000".
- It does not describe the governance of the Operations Center or who decides allocations.
- The figure of "over 800 research projects" has no list or method in this update.
