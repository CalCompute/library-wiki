# McGovern Foundation Our Grants (web)

## Citation

Patrick J. McGovern Foundation. "Our Grants." Grants database page, updated weekly; page date not stated. https://www.mcgovern.org/grants/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The grants database of the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation. It lists awards from July 2017 to 2026. Many grants are for artificial intelligence (AI) and data projects.
- The foundation says that it "has awarded nearly $500 million in charitable contributions". Many grantees are in California.

## Background

Each grant record gives the grantee, a fiscal sponsor if any, the purpose, the amount, a focus area, a location and the year. The focus areas include "AI Fluency", "Data as a Public Good", "Emergent AI", "Health" and "Climate". The page offers the list as a CSV file.

## Key claims

- "Since its inception, the Foundation has awarded nearly $500 million in charitable contributions." (Our Grants)
- "Our grants database contains awards from July 2017 until now and is updated weekly. Comprehensive details can be found as part of the Foundation’s annual 990-PF filing." (Our Grants)
- A 2026 grant to Skillup Coalition, San Francisco, of $400,000 is "to build shared AI infrastructure for low-income household stability". (Grant list)
- A 2024 grant to the University of California, Berkeley, of $500,000 is "to advance data science capacity and innovation in Togo through the establishment of a Data Lab". (Grant list)
- A 2018 grant to the University of California, Berkeley Foundation, of $250,000 is "To provide support for the Simons Institute's Theory of Computing Programs 2018-19." (Grant list)
- The list has four grants to the Public Interest Technology (PIT) Infrastructure Fund, from 2021 to 2024, for a total of $4.5 million. The 2022 grant of $1,500,000 is "to build private and public infrastructure to develop and deploy technology that is equitable, accountable to the public, and responsive to society’s most pressing problems". (Grant list)

## Evidence and method

A grants database. The page copy that the library read has a table of 890 grant rows, from 2017 to 2026. This count comes from the library's copy of the page on 2026-11-11, not from a statement of the foundation. A location with ", CA" appears in 271 rows. Grantees in California include the University of California, San Francisco Foundation, the Computer History Museum, Code for America Labs and The UCLA Foundation.

## Limits

The page does not give the foundation's total grants for each year. It is a live database, so the rows change each week. The page does not describe grants for shared computing hardware. For full details, the page sends readers to the foundation's 990-PF filing, which the library does not have.
