## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the California Correctional Peace Officers Association (CCPOA) for State Bargaining Unit 6, corrections. Its term is July 3, 2025 to July 2, 2028.
- The State keeps the rights to set "the methods, processes and equipment to be employed". It also keeps the right "to contract and sub-contract existing and future work". Notice of a change outside the MOU is 30, 45 or 60 days, by the number of institutions it affects.
- Surveillance video and body worn cameras "will not be used for routine supervision of staff". The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 6 is the State's correctional officers, parole agents, correctional counselors and institutional fire captains, by the articles of the MOU. Many of its rules name the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation (CDCR). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The term of this MOU shall be July 3, 2025, through July 2, 2028." (Article XXVII, 27.03)
- The State and the departments keep the rights "to determine and, from time to time, to redetermine the number, location, and type of work forces, facilities, operations, and the methods, processes and equipment to be employed". (Article IV, 4.01.1)
- The same rights include the rights "to contract and sub-contract existing and future work" and "to determine whether and to what extent the work required in their operations shall be performed by employees covered by this Agreement". (Article IV, 4.01.1)
- The State notifies CCPOA of a proposed change "At least thirty (30) days prior to a proposed local implementation", "At least forty-five (45) days prior to an implementation involving two (2) institutions" and "At least sixty (60) days prior to a statewide implementation". (Article XXVII, 27.01.2)
- "The State and CCPOA agree that reviewing/viewing of live or recorded video will not be used for routine supervision of staff. Neither the Audio/Video Surveillance System (AVSS) nor Body Worn Cameras (BWC) will be used to monitor staff arrivals/departures from the job site." (Article IX, 9.16.1)
- An institution meets CCPOA "to discuss a local process for providing training to staff who are unable to utilize the Learning Management Systems (LMS) due to technological constraints at their primary worksite." (Article VIII, 8.05)
- "The parties hereto declare their joint purpose and policy to continue an organized, planned system of apprenticeship, conducted as a joint labor and management departmental undertaking." (Article VIII, 8.03.1)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It is the longest of the state MOUs in this library, at about 114,000 words in its Markdown copy. It gives rules, salaries, post and bid rules, leaves, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence, on technological change or on limits to contracting out.
- The source page reads the articles on rights, term, changes, training, video recordings and apprenticeship. It does not summarize the articles on salaries, leaves, posts and the many side letters.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
