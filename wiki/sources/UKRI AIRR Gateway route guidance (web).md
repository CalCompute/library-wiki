# UKRI AIRR Gateway route guidance (web)

## Citation

UK Research and Innovation (UKRI). "AIRR Gateway route (UKRI guidance)." GOV.UK, updated 28 September 2026. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-research-resource/airr-gateway-route-ukri-guidance. Read 2026-11-19. The page states: "This publication is licensed under the terms of the Open Government Licence v3.0 except where otherwise stated."

## Bottom line

- The Gateway route of the UK AI Research Resource (AIRR) gives up to 10,000 GPU hours, for use within 3 months. It is for first-time users and for projects that test or benchmark code.
- UK businesses, charities, not-for-profit organisations and research organisations eligible for UKRI funding can apply. The route gives compute only, not money.
- A UKRI and Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST) team reviews applications each month, with a target of resource access within 2 months.

## Background

AIRR is the UK's set of AI supercomputers, described in [UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md). This UKRI guidance gives the rules of one of its access routes. It covers eligibility, subsidy control, support ratios for businesses, scope, the application, assessment and the award offer. A companion page gives the [UKRI AIRR Rapid Access route guidance (web)](UKRI%20AIRR%20Rapid%20Access%20route%20guidance%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Up to 10,000 GPU hours on AIRR services, to be used within 3 months of project start." (Summary)
- The route supports "first-time users of artificial intelligence (AI) supercomputing resources" and "benchmarking of algorithms, code, and workflows before applying for larger AIRR - opportunities". (Summary)
- "Remember that no funding is available through this route, only compute resource." (Project finances)
- "All AIRR access routes provide awards in line with the Subsidy Control Act 2022." (Subsidy control)
- Support ratios for feasibility studies and industrial research: "up to 70% if you are a micro or small organisation", "up to 60%" for medium and "up to 50%" for large ones. "Academic organisations are not subject to this requirement." (Support ratios)
- "Self-contained applications only: do not include links to external web resources." (Important notes)
- "Reviews will normally take place on a monthly basis, with a target maximum time to resource access of 2 months from the date of submission." (How your project will be assessed)

## Evidence and method

Official application guidance of about 2,000 words from a public research funder. It states rules and assessment criteria. It gives no counts of applications or awards.

## Limits

The guidance does not name the AIRR systems that a Gateway award uses. It does not give a success rate, a closing date or the award terms and conditions. It does not say how GPU hours convert to a cash value for the support ratios.
