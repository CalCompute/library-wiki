# Assembly Budget floor report 2025-26 budget 2025-10 (web)

## Citation

Assembly Budget Committee (Jesse Gabriel, Chair). "Floor Report, 2025-2026 Budget." California State Assembly, October 2025. https://abgt.assembly.ca.gov/media/8017. ## Bottom line

- This is the Assembly Budget Committee's summary of the 2025-26 state budget, with all actions taken in 2025. It gives General Fund spending of an estimated $228 billion.
- It lists the money for AI laws of 2024 at state departments, and "initial budgetary guardrails" for the generative AI projects of state departments.
- It does not name CalCompute or Government Code section 11546.8. The "SB 53" in this report is a firearms storage bill.

## Background

This report has one part for each subject area, for example Health, Higher Education and General Government. It also summarizes each bill of the 2025 budget package. The Governor signed the budget bills on June 27, 2025, and the Legislature adopted SB 105 on Sept. 11, 2025 (Overview, p. 2). The report reads 62,000 words; this page covers its overview and its items on technology, AI, higher education and the state workforce.

## Key claims

- "The 2025-26 California state budget includes an estimated $228 billion of spending from the state’s General Fund in 2025-26, $89 billion of special fund spending, and $4 billion of spending from bond accounts." (Overview, p. 2)
- "With $20 million, funds new efficiency effort for state government, led by Department of Finance, accompanied by some initial budgetary guardrails for state departments’ generative artificial intelligence projects that will need to be revisited in future budgets." (Overview, p. 6)
- "Ratifies various agreements between the administration and state employee unions that include near-term savings provisions." (Overview, p. 6)
- "Allocates $761,000 in 2025-26, $740,000 in 2026-27 and ongoing from the Managed Care Fund to implement SB 1120 (Becker, Chapter 879, Statutes of 2024) requiring plans that use artificial intelligence to comply with specific requirements" (Health, Department of Managed Health Care, p. 19)
- "Provides $672,000 in 2025-26 and ongoing from the Public Health Licensing and Certification Program Fund and 3 positions to implement AB 3030 (Calderon, Chapter 848, Statutes of 2024)" (Health, p. 22)
- "Rejects the May Revision proposal to reduce operational funding by $129.7 million ongoing General Fund, or a 3 percent cut to ongoing state support. Restores the cut but defers payment until July 1, 2026." (Higher Education, University of California, p. 59)
- "Provides $12 million one-time Proposition 98 General Fund to support the Common Cloud Data Platform project with reporting requirements and Department of Technology review." (Higher Education, California Community Colleges, p. 62)
- "Modifies Governor’s May Revision proposal and approves $5.3 million for the California Education Learning Lab in 2025-26, and delays phase out of program by one year." (p. 89)
- "Adds Control Section 3.90 stating that it is the Legislature’s expectation that state employee bargaining units will, through the collective bargaining process, meet and confer in good faith to achieve savings in employee compensation needed to maintain the sound fiscal condition of the state." (General Government, p. 131)

The Department of Justice part lists technical corrections to two budget change proposals on AI laws (Public Safety, p. 143). One is for the California AI Transparency Act (SB 942). The other is for AB 2013, "Public Disclosure of Generative Artificial Intelligence Training Data".

## Evidence and method

A summary of enacted budget actions by a committee of the Legislature. It gives the amounts, fund sources and positions of each action. It gives no analysis of need and no data on outcomes.

## Limits

- The report does not name CalCompute, a public cloud computing cluster or Government Code section 11546.8.
- The "SB 53" in the Department of Justice part is "Firearms: Storage (SB 53)" (Public Safety, p. 143). It is not Chapter 138, Statutes of 2025.
- The report does not say what the "budgetary guardrails" for generative AI projects are.
- The report summarizes actions. For the text of each appropriation, read the budget bills.
- The report has no item for the CDPH request of $8 million for a generative AI tool at inspections ([BCP 2025-26 May Revision Public Health generative AI (web)](BCP%202025-26%20May%20Revision%20Public%20Health%20generative%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).
