## Bottom line

- Percy Liang of Stanford University gave the EECS Colloquium talk "Marin: An Open Lab For Frontier AI" on September 16, 2026.
- The abstract says Marin develops frontier language models "entirely in the open from scratch", with experiments and discussions on GitHub and Discord.
- The page is a short event notice: a title, a bio and a one-paragraph abstract.

## Background

The page is part of the Department Colloquium Series on the UC Berkeley EECS website. It gives the date, the room (Gateway 1220) and the time (4:00 to 5:00 pm). It names Percy Liang as Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University and co-founder of Together AI, Simile AI and Marin. The bio lists his work on SQuAD, HELM, generative agents and prefix tuning, and his awards.

## Key claims

- "I will present Marin, a project that aims to develop frontier language models entirely in the open from scratch." (Abstract)
- "Experiments and discussions all happen in the open on GitHub and Discord, opening up the process knowledge that is missing from open-weight (and even open-source) model releases." (Abstract)
- "I argue that open development is a better way of doing science that improves on preregistration, reproducibility, and peer review." (Abstract)
- "I will then discuss the design decisions around data, architectures, optimizers, and scaling laws for training our latest multi-hundred billion parameter mixture of experts model." (Abstract)
- "Percy Liang is a Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University, co-founder of Together AI, Simile AI, and Marin, which aims to build frontier models fully in the open." (Bio)
