## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the California Association of Highway Patrolmen (CAHP) for State Bargaining Unit 5. Its Duration article runs it from July 1, 2024 to June 30, 2027.
- The State keeps all rights that the MOU does not abridge. For the term, each party waives the right to bargain on any subject, covered or not. The MOU changes only by mutual agreement.
- The MOU has no article on contracting out, technological change or artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 5 is the officers of the California Highway Patrol. The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "Unless a specific provision provides for a different effective date, the terms of this Agreement shall go into effect July 1, 2024, upon ratification by both the Legislature and the Union and remain in full force through June 30, 2027." (Article XIX, 88.1)
- "Except for those rights which are expressly abridged or limited by this Agreement, all rights are reserved to the State." (Article III, 4.1)
- The rights of the State include the right "to determine the methods, means and personnel by which State operations are to be conducted". (Article III, 4.1)
- "Therefore, the State and the CAHP, for the duration of this Agreement, each voluntarily and unqualifiedly waives the right, and each agrees that the other should not be obligated, to bargain collectively with respect to any subject or matter whether or not referred to or covered in this Agreement" (Article XVIII, 87)
- "This Agreement may only be amended by mutual agreement of the parties." (Article XVIII, 87)
- "Employees may request tuition assistance from the Department for approved college/university courses and Out-Service Training." (Article XI, 78.1)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, pay differentials, leaves and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the title of the MOU gives "July 1, 2024 through July 1, 2027", and its Duration article (88.1) gives "through June 30, 2027". Both are in [this source](CalHR%20MOU%20Unit%205%20CAHP%202024-2027%20%28web%29.md).

- The MOU has no clause for notice and impact bargaining on changes that it does not cover.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
