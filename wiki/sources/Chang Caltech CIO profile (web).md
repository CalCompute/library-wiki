# Chang Caltech CIO profile (web)

## Citation

California Institute of Technology (Caltech), Information Management Systems & Services (IMSS). "Chief Information Officer." Web page with a message signed by Jin Chang, date not stated. https://www.imss.caltech.edu/about/cio. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- Jin Chang is the Chief Information Officer (CIO) of Caltech. The photo caption reads "Jin Chang, CIO".
- The message introduces the IMSS annual report. It states that IMSS expanded the Resnick High Performance Computing Center in 2025, with more compute, storage and graphics processing unit (GPU) resources.

## Background

IMSS is the information technology organization of Caltech, in Pasadena. This page is the CIO page in the "About" section of the IMSS website. It has a photo, a "Message from the Chief Information Office" signed by Jin Chang, and an administrative support contact. It gives no biography.

## Key claims

- "Information Management Systems & Services (IMSS) provides the talent, tools, and infrastructure that support Caltech's research and education mission with the freedom and flexibility to pursue innovative paths of inquiry and discovery." (Message from the Chief Information Office)
- "Over the past year, IMSS has continued to evolve as a strategic partner to the Institute, modernizing core platforms, strengthening cybersecurity, expanding research computing capabilities, and delivering reliable, high-quality services to our campus community." (Message from the Chief Information Office)
- "We completed an ERP assessment and selection, supported grants management and the PI dashboard system, strengthened our cybersecurity posture through a new multi-year security roadmap, and expanded endpoint and identity protection." (Message from the Chief Information Office)
- "Our infrastructure and data services teams successfully expanded the Resnick High Performance Computing Center, supporting a growing research community with increased compute, storage, and GPU resources." (Message from the Chief Information Office)
- "With gratitude, Jin Chang Chief Information Officer" (Message from the Chief Information Office, signature)

## Evidence and method

A short message from the head of an institutional IT organization. It lists the work of 2025 by area. It gives no numbers for the computing expansion, no budget and no user counts.

## Limits

The page has no biography of Jin Chang: no start date, education or work history. It does not give the size of the Resnick High Performance Computing Center or the number of GPUs added. The text links the "latest annual report" to a 2025 report, and the sidebar links a "2026 Annual Report". The library has read neither report. The page has no date. The message reports on the year 2025 as a past year.
