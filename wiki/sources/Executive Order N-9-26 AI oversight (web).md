# Executive Order N-9-26 AI oversight (web)

## Bottom line

- Executive Order N-9-26 sets two deadlines for the Government Operations Agency (GovOps): May 1, 2027 and Dec. 1, 2027. They are for Government Code sections 8898.1 and 11549.82.
- It tells GovOps, with the Governor's Office of Emergency Services, to send the Governor recommendations on changes to state AI safety law by Nov. 16, 2026. The topics include onsite verifiers in frontier labs and a "kill switch".

## Background

This is the signed order. The Governor's press release about it is [Governor EO N-9-26 release (web)](Governor%20EO%20N-9-26%20release%20%28web%29.md). The recitals name earlier actions: Executive Order N-12-23, the 2025 frontier AI report, SB 53 and Executive Order N-5-26. Government Code sections 8898.1 and 11549.82 are the sections that the order names.

## Key claims

- "No later than May 1, 2027, the Government Operations Agency shall complete the requirements of Section 8898.1 of the Government Code and develop application requirements, procedures, and criteria for independent verification organizations and publicly post them, as required by law." (Order 1)
- "No later than December 1, 2027, the Government Operations Agency shall complete the requirements of subdivision (a) of Section 11549.82 of the Government Code and begin taking the actions required by subdivision (b) of that Section." (Order 2)
- "The Government Operations Agency, in consultation with the Governor’s Office of Emergency Services, shall, no later than November 16, 2026, submit to my office recommendations, developed in consultation with national experts, addressing the technical feasibility and potential efficacy of amendments to existing state laws regarding AI safety and security" (Order 3)
- "Requiring that all large frontier developers embed designated independent verification organizations onsite in their labs to conduct periodic audits and evaluations." (Order 3a)
- "Requiring the creation of a “kill switch” for frontier models, with the efficacy of the switch verified on an ongoing basis by an independent verification organization." (Order 3c)
- "WHEREAS California dominates AI innovation, with 32 of the top 50 private AI companies in the world based in California" (recitals)
- "This Order is not intended to, and does not, create any rights or benefits, substantive or procedural, enforceable at law or in equity, against the State of California" (closing)

## Evidence and method

The legal text of an executive order: recitals, then numbered orders. The recitals describe recent incidents in general terms, such as "attempts by individuals to use AI products to create bioweapons". They give no source for them.

## Limits

The order does not mention CalCompute or § 11546.8. It does not name the national experts.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: [Governor EO N-9-26 release (web)](Governor%20EO%20N-9-26%20release%20%28web%29.md) says the order will give a guide "within two months" and names "the Hugging Face attack". The order sets Nov. 16, 2026 for the recommendations and does not name Hugging Face. Its item 3d refers to "recently reported incidents from large frontier developers".
