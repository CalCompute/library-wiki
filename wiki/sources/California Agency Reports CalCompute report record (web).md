## Bottom line

- The state's agency reports database has one record for the keyword "CalCompute": the report due under Government Code section 11546.8.
- The record names the Government Operations Agency as the agency and the Legislature as the recipient. The due date is "On or before January 1, 2027". On Nov. 11, 2026, its File Date and Report Title cells are empty.

## Background

The Legislative Data Center keeps agencyreports.ca.gov ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)). The site lists the reports that state and local agencies must send to the Legislature. A search gives a table with the authority, agency, description, recipient and due date of each required report. This page is the search result for "CalCompute" on the day the library read it.

## Key claims

- "Displaying 1 - 1 of 1" (search results)
- Authority: "Section 11546.8 of the Government Code" (results table, Authority column)
- Agency: "Government Operations Agency" (results table, Agency column)
- Description: "Framework for the creation of CalCompute that advances the development and deployment of artificial intelligence that is safe, ethical, equitable, and sustainable" (results table, Description column)
- Recipient: "Legislature"; Due Date Description: "On or before January 1, 2027" (results table)

## Evidence and method

A search result from an official database. It shows the record as it stood on the read date. The table has eight columns: Authority, Agency, Description, Recipient, Due Date Description, Due Date, File Date and Report Title. The last three are empty for this record.

## Limits

The record shows that the database lists the report as due. It does not show who writes the report. An empty File Date means that the database had no filed report on the read date. The result can change after the read date. The statute text is in [SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md).
