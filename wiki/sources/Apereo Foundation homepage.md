# Apereo Foundation homepage

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "The Apereo Foundation." Website homepage, © 2026, publication date unknown. https://www.apereo.org/. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- This is a thin homepage. It is a pointer, not a source. Most of the page is navigation, news and logos.

## Background

The Apereo Foundation describes itself as "the source for open source in education". Its community "develops and delivers open source software solutions used in thousands of educational institutions worldwide." It hosts software projects such as Sakai, CAS, Opencast and uPortal.

## Key claims

- Membership classes in the navigation: Community Members, Foundation Members, Institutional Members, Sustaining Members, Commercial Members and Friends of Apereo. (homepage, "Join Us" menu)
- Sustaining Members are "organizations providing dedicated financial support to Apereo projects or communities (i.e., Fiscal Sponsorees)". (homepage, link title)
- Institutional Members support "locally or collaboratively (e.g., system, consortia, etc.) maintained open source projects". (homepage, link title)
- The "About" menu lists Governance, Board of Directors, Board Alumni and Staff pages. (homepage)
- A Nov. 11, 2026 event abstract says: "Commercial platforms promise convenience, but they often come with escalating license fees, vendor-driven roadmaps, and data locked inside someone else's system." (homepage, event promotion)
- Site content is under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. The footer says: "Some of the content on this site has been generated with the help of ChatGPT." (homepage, footer)

## Evidence and method

None. The page is navigation and promotion.

## Limits

- Thin homepage. The governance, board and membership pages are not in `raw/`.
