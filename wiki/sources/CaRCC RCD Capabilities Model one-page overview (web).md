# CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)

## Citation

Campus Research Computing Consortium (CaRCC), Internet2 and EDUCAUSE. "The Research Computing and Data Capabilities Model." One-page overview, version 2.5, PDF, February 2026. https://carcc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/RCDCM-1-pager-v2-5.pdf. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The Research Computing and Data (RCD) Capabilities Model is a self-assessment tool for how well an institution supports "computationally- and data-intensive research".
- An institution can choose an Essentials assessment, a Full assessment "with all ~150 capabilities", or a custom one. It can benchmark itself against peers.
- The tool has five areas: researcher-facing, research data management, research software, systems, and strategy and policy.

## Background

Internet2, CaRCC and EDUCAUSE developed the model. The overview says that an NSF Cyberinfrastructure Centers of Excellence (CI CoE) pilot award supports the work (OAC-2100003). The tool is a web application on the RCD-Nexus Portal. The [CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)](CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20%28web%29.md) page describes the working group that keeps the model.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "The Capabilities Model is designed for use as an input into strategic decision making and organizational planning, and is intended to be inclusive across types of institutions (large and small, public and private, etc.), as well as different organizational models (e.g., centralized or decentralized)." (paragraph 2)
- Definition: ""Research computing and data" (abbreviated as RCD) includes technology, services, and people supporting the needs of researchers and research, and is intended as a broad, inclusive term covering computing, data, networking, and software." (footnote 1)
- Benchmarking use: "To benchmark your institution’s support against peers, e.g., when making an argument for increased funding to remain competitive on faculty recruitment and retention." (common uses)
- Assessment types: "an Essentials version with a subset of capabilities for new and emerging RCD programs, a Full assessment with all ~150 capabilities covering a broad range of topics for established programs, or even a fully customized assessment using the Chart-Your-Own-Journey version." (How can my institution use the Capabilities Model?)
- Strategy area: "Issues related to Strategy and Policy roles, including institutional alignment and culture, funding, professional staff development, and diversity, equity, and inclusion." (five areas)
- Licence: "licensed for use under Creative Commons Attribution-NoDerivatives 4.0 International (CC BY-ND 4.0)" (last paragraph)

## Evidence and method

This is a one-page product overview. It describes the tool and its uses. It gives no assessment results and no count of institutions that use it.

## Limits

- The overview does not list the ~150 capabilities.
- It does not say how many institutions have done an assessment.
- It does not give benchmark data. It says that benchmarks grow "As the community contributes data".
