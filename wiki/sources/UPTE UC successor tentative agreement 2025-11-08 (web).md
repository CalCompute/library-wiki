# UPTE UC successor tentative agreement 2025-11-08 (web)

## Citation

University of California and UPTE-CWA 9119. "Memorandum of Understanding: Successor Agreement Between UPTE and the University of California, November 7, 2025 - September 30, 2028." Signed Nov. 8, 2025, with attached mediation tentative agreements. UPTE-CWA 9119. https://upte.org/s/UPTE-UC-Final-Full-CLEAN-TA-11-8-25_.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-22. Read 2026-11-22.

## Bottom line

- This is the signed memorandum of the successor agreement between the University of California (UC) and University Professional and Technical Employees (UPTE), for Nov. 7, 2025 to Sept. 30, 2028. It covers the HX, RX and TX units.
- It gives across-the-board (ATB) raises of 5 percent in July 2025 and July 2026, and 4 percent in July 2027 and July 2028. The minimum is $25 an hour.
- The attached layoff article keeps UC's "sole, non-grievable, non-arbitrable right" to decide when layoffs occur. The remote work text says that UC has the management right to decide where work is done.

## Background

The union signs as "UPTE-CWA 9119" and publishes the file on its website. The memorandum is four pages with 17 numbered items, from compensation to accreted titles. Many items refer to a "mediation TA", and the attached documents follow the signature page. They include health care, Article 16 on layoff for the units, reclassification, remote work and two side letters. UCnet's page of the TX contract is [UC UPTE TX contract page (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "5% in July 2025, 5% in July 2026, 4% in July 2027 and 4% in July 2028" (item 1.a.i, Across-The-Board (ATB) raises)
- "All workers on steps below $25/hr moved to at least $25/hr as of July 1, 2025" (item 1.c.i)
- "LBNL shall make all reasonable efforts to secure appropriate approval for the following" (item 1.e; LBNL is Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory)
- "The parties agree to meet within 60 days to finalize full language consistent with the memorandum." (memorandum, before the signatures)
- "Layoffs may be temporary or indefinite and may occur because of budgetary reasons, curtailment of operations, lack of work, reorganization, or redefinition of the University's or department's needs." (Article 16, A.1, RX/TX unit, mediation counter of Nov. 7, 2025)
- "The University shall have the sole, non-grievable, non-arbitrable right to determine:" (Article 16, A.2)
- "Prior to implementing any indefinite layoff, the University shall release all non-career employees covered by this agreement within the same layoff unit, except where there is a Bonafide business reason." (Article 16, A.7)
- "The parties acknowledge that the University has the management right to determine whether work is performed on-site, remotely, or in a hybrid format." (Remote Work, 2)

## Evidence and method

A signed memorandum between a public employer and a union, with the mediation texts that it adopts. It is a summary of agreed terms, not the full contract text.

## Limits

- The memorandum refers to some agreed texts that it does not attach, for example the vacation proposals of August and November 2024.
- The document does not name subcontracting, artificial intelligence or technology changes. The library holds the subcontracting and management rights articles of the earlier contracts only.
- It does not say that the members ratified it. The UCnet TX page says that a new contract was ratified.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this memorandum gives the term as "November 7, 2025 - September 30, 2028". The [UC UPTE TX contract page (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) gives "Effective November 1, 2025 – September 30, 2028".
