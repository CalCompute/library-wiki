# OpenSource at Stanford people (web)

## Citation

OpenSource@Stanford, Stanford University. "People." opensource.stanford.edu. Date not stated. https://opensource.stanford.edu/people. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The team page of OpenSource@Stanford. It lists four people: a faculty director, an executive director and two student interns.
- The program "is supported by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation and sits within the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)".

## Background

OpenSource@Stanford is a Stanford program on open source software. Its site has pages on a projects registry, a maintainers and contributors roundtable, pyOpenSci and rOpenSci, and an open source prize. See [Stanford University](../entities/Stanford%20University.md) and [Stanford HAI](../entities/Stanford%20HAI.md).

## Key claims

- "Russell Poldrack ... Principal Investigator, Faculty Director" (Team)
- "Dr. Russell Poldrack is the Albert Ray Lang Professor of Psychology, Associate Director of Stanford Data Science, and Director of the Stanford Center for Open and Reproducible Science (CORES)." (Team)
- "Zach Chandler ... Executive Director" (Team)
- "Zach Chandler is the Director of Open Scholarship Strategy within the Stanford Center for Open and Reproducible Science (CORES)." (Team)
- "OpenSource@Stanford is supported by the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation and sits within the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI (HAI)." (footer)

## Evidence and method

A team page of about 320 words, with a short biography for each person. It gives the program's funder and its home at Stanford. It gives no budget or staff numbers.

## Limits

The page has no date. [Chandler Stanford Profiles (web)](Chandler%20Stanford%20Profiles%20%28web%29.md) gives Zach Chandler a different title. It calls him "Director of Open Source Strategy, Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI)". It does not say what the Sloan Foundation gives or for how long.
