# MassTech AICR builder selection (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Technology Collaborative (MassTech), Massachusetts AI Hub. "Healey-Driscoll Administration Celebrates Selection of Cambridge Computer to Build Landmark Artificial Intelligence Resource." Press release, Boston, December 12, 2025. https://masstech.org/news/healey-driscoll-administration-celebrates-selection-cambridge-computer-build-landmark. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The release names Cambridge Computer, with Dell Technologies and VAST Data, to build the Artificial Intelligence Compute Resources (AICR) environment.
- AICR is at the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC) in Holyoke.
- A $31 million state grant and matching money from six universities fund AICR. The release expects total funding to "exceed $120 million" by 2030.

## Background

MassTech supports the Massachusetts AI Hub, which issued this release on December 12, 2025. In May 2025 the state announced the $31 million grant to MGHPCC to set up AICR. MGHPCC is a data center that six research universities, the Commonwealth and industry developed together. The release has statements from state officials, the six universities and the vendors.

## Key claims

- Selection: the administration announced "the selection of Cambridge Computer in partnership with Dell Technologies and VAST Data to create the Artificial Intelligence Compute Resources (AICR) environment at MGHPCC." (paragraph 1)
- Users: "This resource will serve as the backbone of the Massachusetts AI Hub by supporting startups, businesses, researchers and educators with access to sustainable, high-performance compute resources required for AI innovation." (paragraph 1)
- Hardware: "Located in Holyoke, AICR will be comprised of hundreds of NVIDIA B200 and RTX GPUs with hardware and software fine-tuned for AI workloads." (paragraph 2)
- Matching funds: "MGHPCC’s six university members – Boston University, Harvard University, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, University of Massachusetts and Yale University – will match that initial investment. By 2030, the total funding is expected to exceed $120 million." (paragraph 4)
- Roles: "MGHPCC will oversee the construction and installation of AICR. Cambridge Computer will provide integration services for the systems manufactured by Dell Technologies and VAST." (paragraph 6)
- Energy: "The AICR environment will be powered by 100 percent carbon-free energy from Holyoke Gas and Electric’s hydroelectric facility." (paragraph 6)
- Operations: "The member universities fund the ongoing operation of the data center." (About MGHPCC)

## Evidence and method

This is a press release from a state economic development agency. It names the builder, the partners, the hardware family and the funding sources. It gives no contract value, no GPU count and no access rules.

## Limits

- The release does not give the value of the Cambridge Computer contract.
- It gives "hundreds" of GPUs, not a number.
- It does not say how startups, businesses, researchers and educators will apply for time, or what they will pay.
- It does not give a date when AICR starts to operate.
- The May 2025 release gives joint investments that "will reach an estimated $120 million investment" over five years ([Massachusetts AI Hub release](Massachusetts%20AI%20Hub%20release.md)). This release says the total "is expected to exceed $120 million" by 2030.
