# CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)

## Citation

Campus Research Computing Consortium (CaRCC). "CaRCC Capabilities Model Working Group." Web page, date not stated. https://carcc.org/rcdcm/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- This is the page of the CaRCC working group that develops and supports the Research Computing and Data (RCD) Capabilities Model.
- The group is working on version 3. The group used an "alpha" version at a 2026 workshop. It hopes "to release the new version this Fall".
- National Science Foundation (NSF) awards OAC-1620695 and OAC-2100003 support the work.

## Background

CaRCC is the Campus Research Computing Consortium. Its working group keeps the Capabilities Model, a self-assessment tool for institutions. The page links to the [CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md). It names two co-chairs, from Semper Cogito and Internet2, and four more contributors.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "The Capabilities Model allows institutions to assess their support for computationally- and data-intensive research and education, to identify and prioritize potential areas for improvement, and to understand understand how the broader community views Research Computing and Data support." (introduction)
- Current work: "An "alpha" version of the new version 3 model was used in the 2026 RCD Nexus Day workshop co-located with ACM PEARC26, and the working group is now incorporating additional feedback we received during that workshop. We hope to release the new version this Fall." (Current work)
- Review of version 2: "The working group reviewed input from our summer 2025 workshop as well as in-depth review of the version 2 capabilities prose, identifying candidates for combination or removal, as well as suggested additions to address contemporary and emerging topics." (Current work)
- Funding: "This work has been supported in part by an RCN grant from the National Science Foundation (OAC-1620695 ...) and by an NSF Cyberinfrastructure Centers of Excellence (CI CoE) pilot award (OAC-2100003 ...)." (footer of the page; award titles cut here)

## Evidence and method

This is a working group page. It gives the purpose, the current work, the contributors and the funding. It has no assessment data.

## Limits

- The page does not give a date. "This Fall" refers to an unstated year; the page names PEARC26 and events in November and December 2026.
- It does not list the capabilities or the changes in version 3.
- It does not say how many institutions use the model.
