# UC HX contract page (web)

## Citation

University of California, UCnet. "HX — Health Care Professionals: Contract." Web page, no date. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/hx/contract.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-20 UC HX contract page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-20%20UC%20HX%20contract%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) lists the articles of its contract with University Professional and Technical Employees (UPTE) for the Health Care Professionals (HX) unit. The page gives a new contract for Nov. 1, 2025 to Sept. 30, 2028.
- The page says that the articles it posts "do not reflect the most recent negotiated language." The posted files carry 2019-2024 in their addresses.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the HX unit's pages. It links one file for each article, appendices and side letters. The library holds two of the articles: [UC UPTE HX contract Article 16 Management Rights (web)](UC%20UPTE%20HX%20contract%20Article%2016%20Management%20Rights%20%28web%29.md) and [UC UPTE HX contract Article 37 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20HX%20contract%20Article%2037%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Contract for the Health Care Professionals Unit (HX) between the University of California and the University Professional and Technical Employees Union"
- "November 1, 2025 – September 30, 2028"
- "New contract ratified for contract period 11/1/2025 to 9/30/2028."
- "The University and UPTE are currently reviewing and finalizing updates to the HX collective bargaining agreement articles. Until that process is complete, the articles currently posted on this website do not reflect the most recent negotiated language."
- "The draft collective bargaining agreement (CBA) is available to location labor managers."

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page gives no date of its last update.
- The notice is the same as on the [UC UPTE TX contract page (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md), which also names the HX articles.
- The library holds only Articles 16 and 37 of the posted files. It does not hold the draft agreement for 2025 to 2028.
