Oxford University Press. Published online May 29, 2024. https://doi.org/10.1093/polsoc/puae020. Article page: https://academic.oup.com/policyandsociety/article/44/1/85/7684910. Read 2026-11-06.

- A peer-reviewed journal article by three authors at the Center for Human-Compatible AI, UC Berkeley, with Mark Nitzberg among them.
