# Apache Software Foundation bylaws (web)

## Citation

The Apache Software Foundation (ASF). "Bylaws of The Apache Software Foundation." Web page, adopted June 1, 1999, with Amendment I (effective Nov. 15, 2002) and Amendment II (effective March 11, 2021). https://www.apache.org/foundation/bylaws.html. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The bylaws set up the ASF as a Delaware not-for-profit membership corporation. The members elect the Board of Directors each year.
- New members join only by a majority vote of the existing members, after a nomination by a current member.
- The Board can set up Project Management Committees (PMCs). Each PMC manages one or more open-source projects.

## Background

The ASF publishes its bylaws on apache.org, with a link to a plain-English version. The board adopted the bylaws on June 1, 1999. Two amendments follow the text. The first changes the admission rule in Section 4.1. The second changes gendered words to gender-neutral words. The page footer says that the ASF "is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization."

## Key claims

- Nonprofit status: "The corporation is organized and shall be operated as a not-for-profit membership corporation organized under Delaware law." (Article VIII)
- Admission: "members of the corporation shall be admitted as members of the corporation only by a majority vote of the existing members of the corporation" (Section 4.1)
- Nomination: "To be eligible for membership, a person or entity must be nominated by a current member of the corporation and must complete a written membership application" (Section 4.1)
- Board: "The business and affairs of the corporation shall be managed by or under the direction of the Board of Directors" (Section 5.1)
- Board size: "The corporation shall initially have nine (9) directors. Thereafter, the number of directors shall be fixed by the members at each annual meeting of members." (Section 5.4)
- Election: "the members shall elect directors to hold office until the next succeeding annual meeting." (Section 5.5)
- Projects: "Each Project Management Committee shall be responsible for the active management of one or more projects identified by resolution of the Board of Directors which may include, without limitation, the creation or maintenance of "open-source" software for distribution to the public at no charge." (Section 6.3)
- Project rules: the chair of each PMC "shall establish rules and procedures for the day to day management of project(s) for which the committee is responsible." (Section 6.3)
- Voting: "Each member (except emeritus members) shall be entitled to one vote on each matter submitted to a vote at a meeting of the members" (Section 3.10)

## Evidence and method

This is the legal governing document of the ASF, as the ASF publishes it. It states rules for members, directors, officers and PMCs. It gives no data on membership numbers, budgets or projects.

## Limits

- The bylaws do not name the current directors, officers or members.
- They give no membership fee. ASF membership is by nomination and vote, not by payment.
- They do not describe how a project enters the ASF, or the Apache Incubator.
- The trademark rules for ASF projects are in [Apache trademark policy (web)](Apache%20trademark%20policy%20%28web%29.md).
