## Bottom line

- On November 21, 2024, the CSU Board of Trustees approved a plan to integrate two campuses. California State University Maritime Academy (Cal Maritime) becomes part of California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo (Cal Poly).
- The Vallejo campus stays the home of Cal Maritime's license-track programs. Its students become Cal Poly students in fall 2026.

## Background

This is a news explainer from the CSU Office of the Chancellor. It was first published while the plan was a proposal, and the CSU updated it after the vote. It gives the reasons for the plan, which are Cal Maritime's financial problems, and what the plan means for students and faculty of both campuses.

## Key claims

The decision (opening note):

- "The CSU Board of Trustees voted to approve the integration of California State University Maritime Academy and California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo on November 21, 2024."

The reason (opening paragraphs):

- "The proposal seeks to address financial challenges at Cal Maritime, including declining enrollment and rising operations and compensation costs, that have made it unviable for the university to continue as a standalone institution."
- "As one of only six state maritime institutions in the U.S. and the only one on the West Coast and Pacific Rim, Cal Maritime is integral to preparing the next generation of U.S. Coast Guard-licensed Merchant Marine officers serving this region."

Facilities and funding ("New Opportunities for Cal Poly"):

- "In addition, the Vallejo campus would allow Cal Poly students and faculty to access new research facilities and lab space for instruction, experiential learning and research."
- "Lastly, the incorporation of Cal Maritime programs would allow Cal Poly to apply for greater funding related to national security and renewable energy programs, such as federal grants and contracts, that could bolster programs at both campuses."

The timeline ("What Integration Means for Current Cal Maritime Students"):

- "While integration efforts would begin in July 2025, maritime academy students would not be enrolled as Cal Poly students until fall 2026."
- "The Vallejo campus would remain the permanent location for Cal Maritime's license-track programs, research work and other degree programs"

## Evidence and method

This is a news explainer from the system office. It quotes CSU and campus leaders. Most of the text is in the conditional ("would"), from before the vote. It gives no budget figures.

## Limits

- The page says nothing about computing or research computing at either campus.
- It does not give the cost of the integration or the size of the one-time funds.
- Only the note at the top is from after the vote. The rest describes the proposal.
