# Jobs First 2025 results release

## Citation

Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development (GO-Biz). "Governor Newsom’s statewide Jobs First investments created more than 61,000 jobs, trained more than 142,000 workers in 2025." Press release, February 3, 2026. https://business.ca.gov/governor-newsoms-statewide-jobs-first-investments-created-more-than-61000-jobs-trained-more-than-142000-workers-in-2025/. ## Bottom line

- In 2025, California Jobs First invested nearly $1.6 billion across the 13 regions. The state says it trained more than 142,000 workers and helped create more than 61,000 jobs.
- The training money runs through existing state programs: the Employment Training Panel, apprenticeships and High Road Training Partnerships.

## Background

The program began in 2021 as a statewide planning process, and became California Jobs First in 2023 (para. "Bold vision, realized locally").

## Key claims

- "investing nearly $1.6 billion in 2025 across all 13 economic regions" (What you need to know)
- "the nearly $1.6 billion in investments from 2025 that went to train more than 142,000 workers and help create more than 61,000 new jobs across California’s 13 economic regions." (para. 1)
- "each region launching a planning body — or collaborative — with representation from a wide variety of community partners" (Bold vision, realized locally)
- Employment Training Panel: "In 2025, the panel invested over $82 million through 299 contracts with California businesses to support the training of 70,544 workers." (Employment Training Panel)
- Apprenticeships: DIR and DAS "awarded over $91 million in 2025 to expand apprenticeship opportunities, benefiting more than 69,000 apprentices and pre-apprentices." (Apprenticeships)
- High Road Training Partnerships: "$18.5 million" to train "over 3,100 workers", focused on "the strategic sectors in their region(s)". (HRTP)
- Quantum California, launched November 2025, is "a new statewide initiative uniting government, academia and industry to accelerate quantum innovation". (Bet sector investments)
- 2025 saw "the release of the Master Plan for Career Education". (Strengthening Workforce Alignment)
- Quotes from GO-Biz Director Dee Dee Myers and LWDA Secretary Stewart Knox. (para. 10, 11)

## Evidence and method

A press release. It reports totals and does not give the method behind the job and training counts.

## Limits

- A government press release: the totals are the state's claims, not an audit.
- The jobs and training counts mix several programs.
