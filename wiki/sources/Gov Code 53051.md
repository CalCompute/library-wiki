# Gov Code 53051

## Bottom line

- Each public agency must file a statement with the Secretary of State and the county clerk within 70 days after its legal existence starts.
- The statement gives the agency's legal name, mailing address, governing body members and officers. An amended statement is due within 10 days after a change.
- The Secretary of State and each county clerk keep the filings in a "Registry of Public Agencies", which is a public record.

## Background

The section is Article 3 (Roster of Public Agencies) of the Government Code chapter on powers and duties common to cities, counties and other agencies. The library's copy comes from the Legislature's code website through the Wayback Machine.

## Key claims

- "Within 70 days after the date of commencement of its legal existence, the governing body of each public agency shall file with the Secretary of State on a form prescribed by the Secretary of State and with the county clerk of each county in which the public agency maintains an office, a statement of all of the following facts" (subd. (a))
- "(1) The full, legal name of the public agency. (2) The official mailing address of the governing body of the public agency." (subd. (a)(1) and (2))
- "Within 10 days after any change in the facts required to be stated pursuant to subdivision (a), an amended statement containing the information required by subdivision (a) shall be filed as provided therein." (subd. (b))
- "It shall be the duty of the Secretary of State and of the county clerk of each county to establish and maintain an indexed “Registry of Public Agencies,” to be so designated, which shall contain all information filed as required in subdivisions (a) and (b), which roster is hereby declared to be a public record." (subd. (c))

## Limits

The section does not define "public agency"; section 53050 does, and this library does not hold it. The section does not say what follows when an agency does not file. The capture is from Feb. 21, 2025, and later amendments are not shown.
