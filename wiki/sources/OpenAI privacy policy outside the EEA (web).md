# OpenAI privacy policy outside the EEA (web)

## Citation

OpenAI. "Privacy policy." Version for individuals outside the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, Switzerland and the United States. Updated February 6, 2026. https://openai.com/policies/row-privacy-policy/. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20. Not rehosted: the host is not a government host and the page states no open licence.

## Bottom line

- The policy says what personal data OpenAI collects from users of ChatGPT, Sora and its other services. It says how OpenAI uses, shares and keeps the data.
- It does not apply to the content that OpenAI processes for customers of its business offerings, such as its API. Customer agreements govern that content.
- OpenAI can use the content that a user gives to train its models, and the user can opt out. Deleted data leaves OpenAI's systems in 30 days, with exceptions for safety, legal and financial reasons.

## Background

OpenAI has one privacy policy for the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and one for the United States. This is the version for the rest of the world. It has 13 sections, from the data that OpenAI collects to useful resources. It links a previous version of June 27, 2025. Compare [Anthropic Privacy Policy (web)](Anthropic%20Privacy%20Policy%20%28web%29.md). OpenAI's business terms are in [OpenAI Services Agreement (web)](OpenAI%20Services%20Agreement%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "This Privacy Policy does not apply to content that we process on behalf of customers of our business offerings, such as our API. Our use of that data is governed by our customer agreements covering access to and use of those offerings." (introduction)
- "We also collect information from other sources, like information that is publicly available on the internet, to develop the models that power our Services." (1. Personal Data we collect)
- "As noted above, we may use Content you provide us to improve our Services, for example to train the models that power ChatGPT." (2. How we use Personal Data)
- "When you join a ChatGPT Enterprise or business account, the administrators of that account may access and control your OpenAI account, including being able to access your Content." (3. Disclosure of Personal Data)
- "Once you choose to delete Personal Data, we will remove it from our systems within 30 days unless we need to retain it for longer as described below" (4. Retention)
- "If you enable Temporary Chat in ChatGPT, those conversations with ChatGPT will not appear in your history or be used to improve OpenAI’s models." (5. Data controls)
- "For this reason, you should not rely on the factual accuracy of output from our models." (6. Your rights)
- "Our Services are not directed to, or intended for, children under 13." and "Users under 18 must have permission from their parent or guardian to use our Services." (7. Children)
- "We don’t “sell” Personal Data or “share” Personal Data for cross-contextual behavioral advertising, and we do not process Personal Data for “targeted advertising” purposes" (9. Additional U.S. state disclosures)

## Evidence and method

A company privacy policy. It states the company's own practices. It lists the kinds of data, the purposes of use and the parties that get the data. It also gives the retention rules, the user controls and the rights of users. It gives no figures.

## Limits

The policy covers consumer services only; it does not give the terms for API, Enterprise or Edu customers. On the place of storage, it says only "in the United States, or in countries or territories where our affiliates and partners or our vendors and service providers are located". It does not give how long OpenAI keeps data that it retains for safety or legal reasons. The policy is the version for individuals outside the EEA, but its section 11 names the data controller for the EEA and Switzerland too. Its section 9 covers U.S. state laws, although U.S. individuals have a separate version.
