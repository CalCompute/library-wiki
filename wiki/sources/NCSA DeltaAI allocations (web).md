# NCSA DeltaAI allocations (web)

## Citation

National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. "DeltaAI Allocations." Web page, date not stated. https://delta.ncsa.illinois.edu/deltaai-allocations/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- DeltaAI is a graphics processing unit (GPU) system at NCSA that the National Science Foundation (NSF) funds. It allocates time in H100 GPU-hours and has no central processing unit (CPU) only allocation.
- Most DeltaAI time goes through ACCESS. A portion goes through the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot, and a portion goes to Illinois researchers.

## Background

This page on the Delta and DeltaAI website tells users how to get time on DeltaAI. It describes the one resource that users can request. It then gives four routes: ACCESS, the NAIRR Pilot, Illinois Computes and an Illinois discretionary route.

## Key claims

- "DeltaAI is a GPU-centric resource and does not have a CPU-only allocation due to the nature of the architecture (NVIDIA Grace Hopper)." (warning box)
- "DeltaAI compute nodes are quad (x4) Grace Hopper (GH) superchip nodes with each Grace Hopper superchip composed of a superchip with a 72 core ARM CPU and a H100 GPU. Allocations will be made in terms of H100 gpu-hours." (DeltaAI offers one allocatable resource)
- "The majority of DeltaAI is allocated through ACCESS and is the preferred path to getting compute time on DeltaAI." (ACCESS)
- "The lower three opportunities of Explore, Discover and Accelerate are open and processed continuously for fast turnaround. The Explore opportunity accepts requests submitted by Graduate Students with a faculty or staff co-PI. The Maximize opportunity is open twice a year." (ACCESS)
- "A portion of DeltaAI is allocated through the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot program." (NAIRR Pilot)
- "NAIRR Pilot requests for resources are reviewed on a continual basis." (NAIRR Pilot)
- "Researchers should apply to ACCESS first, as it makes small awards easily available and can scale out to much larger awards than the Illinois program can support." (Illinois)
- "In the DeltaAI Discretionary request, please include information about why your project is not suitable for an ACCESS request." (Illinois)

## Evidence and method

A user page with allocation rules. It names the routes, the unit of allocation and the review timing for each route. It also says that "Delta Storage is no longer an allocatable resource" and that storage requests go through the ACCESS or NCSA ticket system. An NCSA account is necessary for the discretionary request page, and account creation can take "up to 24 hours".

## Limits

The page does not give the share of time for each route. [NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)](NCSA%20DeltaAI%20award%20release%202023-07-05%20%28web%29.md) gives a 90 percent and 10 percent split between ACCESS and the university. The page does not give the number of nodes or a cost to users. It has no date.
