# NVIDIA NAIRR pilot contribution 2024-01-24 (web)

## Citation

Wells, Jack. "US National Science Foundation Launches National AI Research Resource Pilot." NVIDIA Blog, NVIDIA Corporation, Jan. 24, 2024. https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/2024/01/24/national-ai-research-resource-pilot/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- NVIDIA committed "$30 million in technology contributions over two years" to the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) pilot.
- The contribution includes DGX Cloud supercomputing and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software. The post quotes NSF officials on the pilot's goals and on reaching smaller and rural institutions.

## Background

NVIDIA published this post on the day that NSF launched the pilot ([NSF NAIRR pilot launch release 2024-01-24 (web)](NSF%20NAIRR%20pilot%20launch%20release%202024-01-24%20%28web%29.md)). It is a company statement of its own contribution. A FedTech Magazine article of April 2024 gives the parts of the $30 million. Its source is an NVIDIA spokesperson ([FedTech NAIRR pilot NVIDIA contribution breakdown 2024 (web)](FedTech%20NAIRR%20pilot%20NVIDIA%20contribution%20breakdown%202024%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "NVIDIA's commitment of $30 million in technology contributions over two years is a key factor in enlarging the scale of the pilot" (paragraph 4)
- "Accelerating both AI research and research done with AI, NVIDIA's contributions include NVIDIA DGX Cloud AI supercomputing resources and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software." (section "The Foundation for Modern AI")
- "Our primary goals for the NAIRR pilot are to support fundamental AI research and domain-specific research applying AI, reach broader communities, particularly those currently unable to participate in the AI innovation ecosystem, and refine the design for the future full NAIRR," (Katie Antypas, NSF, paragraph 6)
- "Smaller institutions, rural institutions, institutions serving underrepresented populations are key communities we're trying to reach with the NAIRR," (Katie Antypas, section "Leading in Advanced AI")
- "NVIDIA will harness insights from researchers using its platform, offering an opportunity to refine and enhance the effectiveness of its technology for science" (section "Accelerating Access to AI")
- "The NAIRR pilot welcomes additional private-sector and nonprofit partners." (section "Paving the Way for Future Investments")

## Evidence and method

A company blog post. It states the size and kind of NVIDIA's pledge and quotes NSF officials and NVIDIA's chief executive. It gives no breakdown of the $30 million and no data on use.

## Limits

- The post does not say how NVIDIA values its contribution.
- It does not report how much of the pledge researchers used.
- It is from the pilot's first day. [NAIRR Pilot resource catalog feed (web)](NAIRR%20Pilot%20resource%20catalog%20feed%20%28web%29.md) lists NVIDIA among the partners "currently unable to accept additional NAIRR Pilot awards".
