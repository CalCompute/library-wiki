# SDSC privacy policy (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "Privacy Policy." SDSC website, date not stated. https://www.sdsc.edu/privacy_policy.html. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The policy covers the information that the SDSC website collects: subscriptions to its publications and web log files. It does not cover the use of SDSC's computing systems.
- SDSC says it is "the sole owner of the information collected on this site". It does not sell, share or rent that information in other ways.

## Background

SDSC is a research computing center at UC San Diego. The page is the privacy policy of its website. The terms for its secure cloud are in [SDSC Sherlock legal (web)](SDSC%20Sherlock%20legal%20%28web%29.md). See [San Diego Supercomputer Center](../entities/San%20Diego%20Supercomputer%20Center.md).

## Key claims

- "The San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) is committed to your privacy within the applicable laws of the State of California and the Policies of the University of California." (introduction)
- "SDSC is the sole owner of the information collected on this site. We will not sell, share, or rent this information to others in ways different from what is disclosed in this statement." (Information Collection and Use)
- "We use Web log files to analyze trends, administer the site, track users' use of our site, and gather broad demographic information for usability studies. IP addresses are not linked to personally identifiable information." (Log Files)
- "This privacy statement applies solely to information collected by SDSC." (Links)
- "If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on our home page" (Notification of Changes)

## Evidence and method

A website privacy policy of about 600 words. It states practices. It cites no law by name and no audit.

## Limits

The policy does not cover the data of users on SDSC systems such as Expanse or Sherlock. It does not say how long SDSC keeps log files. It gives no date or version.
