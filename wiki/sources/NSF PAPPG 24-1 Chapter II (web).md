# NSF PAPPG 24-1 Chapter II (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF). "Chapter II: Proposal Preparation Instructions." *Proposal & Award Policies & Procedures Guide (PAPPG) (NSF 24-1)*. Web page, date not stated; the Suggestions page gives 2024-05-20. https://www.nsf.gov/policies/pappg/24-1/ch-2-proposal-preparation. ## Bottom line

- Chapter II tells proposers how to prepare an NSF proposal: its sections, its budget lines and its supplementary documents.
- Voluntary committed cost sharing "is prohibited". Mandatory cost sharing applies only when the NSF Director, the National Science Board (NSB) or a law authorizes it.
- An organization with no negotiated rate can apply "the *de minimis* rate of 10%" of modified total direct costs. Section II.E.7 lists the national computing resources that NSF supports, among them ACCESS.

## Background

The PAPPG is the NSF rule book for proposals and awards. Chapter II is the part on proposal preparation. A banner on the page says that two policy notices, NSF 26-200 and NSF 26-202, supplement PAPPG 24-1. The first is [NSF PAPPG 24-1 Supplement 1 (web)](NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Supplement%201%20%28web%29.md). The chapter is about 33,000 words. The library read the budget section (II.D.2.f), Facilities (II.D.2.g), the supplementary documents (II.D.2.i), collaborative proposals (II.E.3), advanced computing (II.E.7) and the planning proposal (II.F.1).

## Key claims

- Allowable costs: "The proposal may request funds under any of the categories listed so long as the item and amount are considered necessary, reasonable, allocable, and allowable under 2 CFR § 200, Subpart E, NSF policy, and/or the program solicitation." (II.D.2.f)
- Computer services: "The cost of computer services, including computer-based retrieval of scientific, technical, and educational information, may be requested only where it is institutional policy to charge such costs as direct charges." (II.D.2.f(vi)(d))
- Computing devices: "A computing device is considered a supply if the acquisition cost is less than the lesser of the capitalization level established by the proposer or $5,000, regardless of the length of its useful life." (II.D.2.f(vi)(a))
- Indirect costs: "Domestic proposing organizations that do not have a current negotiated rate agreement with a cognizant Federal agency may choose to apply the *de minimis* rate of 10% to a base of modified total direct costs (MTDC) as authorized by 2 CFR § 200.414(f)." (II.D.2.f(viii))
- Cost sharing: "Unless required by NSF (see the section on Mandatory Cost Sharing below), inclusion of voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited and Line M on the proposal budget will not be available for use by the proposer." (II.D.2.f(xii))
- Mandatory cost sharing: "Mandatory cost sharing will only be required for NSF programs when explicitly authorized by the NSF Director, the NSB, or legislation." (II.D.2.f(xii))
- Resources: "the Foundation **does expect** that the resources identified in the Facilities, Equipment and Other Resources section will be provided, or made available, should the proposal be funded." (II.D.2.g)
- Data: "Proposals must include a document of no more than two pages uploaded under “Data Management and Sharing Plan” in the supplementary documentation section of Research.gov." (II.D.2.i(ii))
- National compute: "Researchers and instructors in all disciplines can apply for allocations for computer or data resources from over two dozen high-performance computational systems via the Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services & Support (ACCESS) program." (II.E.7)
- Collaboration: "All collaborative proposals must clearly describe the roles to be played by the other organizations, specify the managerial arrangements, and explain the advantages of the multi-organizational effort within the Project Description." (II.E.3)
- Planning proposals: "Requests may be for up to $100,000 per year (including indirect costs) and for up to two years in duration." (II.F.1)

## Evidence and method

This is federal agency guidance. It states rules for proposers and gives no data. Section II.E.7 names four national resources. They are the Frontera system at the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) and ACCESS. The others are the computers of the National Center for Atmospheric Research (NCAR) and the Partnership to Advance Throughput Computing (PATh). It does not name the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot.

## Limits

- The library read only the sections named in Background. It did not read the cover sheet, biographical sketch or current and pending support rules in full.
- Supplement 1 changes parts of this chapter. It raises the planning proposal limit from $100,000 to $200,000 and adds a data sharing rule to II.D.2.i(ii) ([NSF PAPPG 24-1 Supplement 1 (web)](NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Supplement%201%20%28web%29.md)). The library has not read Supplement 2 (NSF 26-202).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this chapter gives a *de minimis* indirect cost rate of "10%" under 2 CFR § 200.414(f). The current text of § 200.414(f) gives "a de minimis rate of up to 15 percent of modified total direct costs (MTDC)" ([eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 Indirect costs (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20Indirect%20costs%20%28web%29.md)). The 10% figure matches the text of Sept. 30, 2024 ([eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 as of 2024-09-30 (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20as%20of%202024-09-30%20%28web%29.md)). The page banner says that 2 CFR applies where the two differ.
