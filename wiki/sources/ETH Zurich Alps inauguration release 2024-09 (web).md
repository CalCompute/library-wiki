# ETH Zurich Alps inauguration release 2024-09 (web)

## Citation

ETH Zurich, Corporate Communications. "New research infrastructure: 'Alps' supercomputer inaugurated." Press release, Zurich, 14 September 2024. PDF, 2 pages, hosted by the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS). https://www.cscs.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/contents_publications/news/news_2024/press-release-alps-supercomputer-inaugurated.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- ETH Zurich inaugurated the Alps supercomputer at CSCS in Lugano on 14 September 2024.
- Alps is an HPE Cray EX system with 10,752 NVIDIA Grace Hopper superchips. It was sixth on the June 2024 Top500 list.
- Alps is a central element of the Swiss AI initiative of ETH Zurich and EPFL.

## Background

ETH Zurich operates CSCS ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)). This release reports the inauguration event and quotes Federal Councillor Guy Parmelin, ETH officials and the CSCS director. It has two pages.

## Key claims

- Hardware: "The new research infrastructure is based on a Cray Supercomputer EX from Hewlett-Packard Enterprise (HPE) and has 10,752 of the world's coveted NVIDIA Grace Hopper superchips." (p. 1)
- Rank: "In the Top500 list of supercomputers from June 2024, it was ranked 6th in the first expansion stage." (p. 1)
- Public funding: the Federal Councillor "explained that the new research infrastructure is the result of the combined efforts of the Confederation, cantons and communes." (p. 1)
- Industry: "the latest supercomputer is part of a long tradition of collaboration between CSCS and industry." (p. 1)
- AI: "The new supercomputer is a central element of the Swiss AI initiative. It was launched by ETH Zurich and EPFL to position Switzerland as the world's leading hub for the development and implementation of transparent and trustworthy AI solutions." (p. 1)
- vClusters: "With its cloud-native architecture, we can create versatile software-defined clusters (vClusters) tailored to the specific needs of user communities, ensuring the maintenance of the necessary confidentiality". (p. 2)
- Users: "Several institutions are taking advantage of this new opportunity, most notably the Paul Scherrer Institute." (p. 2)
- Software staff: "the engineers from CSCS play an important role, working closely with the researchers to develop tools and software that translate scientific questions into the language of the computer." (p. 2)

## Evidence and method

This is a university press release about one event. It gives the hardware, the Top500 rank and quotations from officials. It gives no cost and no allocation figures.

## Limits

- The release gives no cost for Alps and no funding split between the Confederation, cantons and communes.
- It says that exact figures on the final expansion "are expected in November" 2024. This library does not hold them.
- It does not describe how researchers apply for time on Alps. The CSCS brochure describes the User Lab ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
