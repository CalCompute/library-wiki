## Bottom line

- The notice sets the fiscal year (FY) 2025 process to designate eligible institutions for grant programs under Titles III and V of the HEA. These include the Hispanic-Serving Institutions (HSI) programs.
- An institution must show an enrollment of needy students and Core Expenses per full-time equivalent (FTE) student below the average for its type. The notice gives the thresholds for base year 2022-2023.
- The Department "does not certify, nor designate, an institution as a Minority-Serving Institution or Hispanic-Serving Institution" through this process.

## Background

The Department of Education publishes this notice each year in the *Federal Register*. It covers the Strengthening Institutions Program and the HSI programs. It also covers programs for Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian, Predominantly Black, Native American and Asian American and Native American Pacific Islander institutions. The Department uses data from the Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS) to decide eligibility. An eligible institution can also get a one-year waiver of some non-federal cost-sharing requirements.

## Key claims

- Need: an institution "is considered to have an enrollment of needy students" if "at least 50 percent of its degree-seeking students received financial assistance under the Federal Pell Grant, FSEOG, or FWS programs", or if its Pell Grant share of half-time students exceeds the median of comparable institutions. (Enrollment of Needy Students)
- Spending: "An institution satisfies this program eligibility requirement if its Core Expenses for the 2022-2023 base year are less than the average Core Expenses of its comparable institutional category." (Core Expenses per FTE Student)
- Thresholds: the table gives a median Pell Grant percentage of "44" and average Core Expenses of "$19,020" for "2-year Public Institutions", and "37" and "39,396" for "4-year Public Institutions". (table)
- Data: "To make eligibility determinations for FY 2025, we use an institution's 2022-2023 enrollment and fiscal data." (Eligibility Matrix)
- Cost-share waiver: "Qualified (eligible) institutions may receive the FSEOG and FWS waivers for one year even if they do not receive a grant under a title III or V grant program." (Funding Opportunity Description)
- Designation: "the Department does not certify, nor designate, an institution as a Minority-Serving Institution or Hispanic-Serving Institution. This eligibility determination relates only to the institution's ability to apply for grants under certain titles III and V grant programs". (Eligibility Matrix)
- Dates: "Applications Available: March 6, 2025." and "Deadline for Transmittal of Applications: April 7, 2025." (DATES)

## Evidence and method

This is a federal notice. It gives eligibility rules, the waiver process and two data tables. One table gives Pell Grant medians and Core Expenses by institution type. The other gives 2023 low-income levels by family size. It gives no list of eligible institutions; the Department publishes that list on its eligibility website.

## Limits

- The notice does not list which California institutions are eligible.
- It does not give grant amounts or the number of awards.
- It covers FY 2025 only. The library has not read a later notice.
- One sentence in the copy still reads "before the application deadline of [30 days after the date of publication]". The DATES section gives April 7, 2025.
