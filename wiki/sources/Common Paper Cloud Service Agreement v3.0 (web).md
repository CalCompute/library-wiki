# Common Paper Cloud Service Agreement v3.0 (web)

## Citation

Common Paper. "Cloud Service Agreement." Standard Terms, Version 3.0, published 2026-09-30. Markdown file in the CommonPaper/CSA repository on GitHub, commit 84c7f60. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/CommonPaper/CSA/84c7f601504eaf33de29aa7adcb599073793b23c/CSA.md. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- This is a standard form of contract for a cloud service, with no named parties. It has 13 sections, from Service to Definitions.
- The business and legal details go on a separate Cover Page: an Order Form, Key Terms, or both. Examples are the fees, the Subscription Period, the liability cap and the Governing Law.
- It bars use of the product for "High Risk Activities". It bars "Prohibited Data", such as health records and government ID numbers, unless the Order Form or Key Terms allow it.

## Background

Common Paper publishes standard agreements as text files on GitHub. This file is Version 3.0 of its Cloud Service Agreement Standard Terms. It uses the roles "Provider" and "Customer". The parties fill in each "Variable" on a Cover Page, for example the Subscription Period or the Governing Law. The file does not state a licence.

## Key claims

- Access and use: Customer may "access and use the Cloud Service ... for its internal business purposes." (Section 1.1)
- User accounts: "Customer is responsible for all actions on Users’ accounts and for all Users’ compliance with this Agreement." (Section 1.3)
- Restrictions: Customer will not "use the Product with any High Risk Activities or with any activity prohibited by Applicable Laws" (Section 2.1(a)(viii))
- Suspension: Provider may suspend access if Customer "has an outstanding, undisputed balance on its account for more than 30 days" or breaches the restrictions. (Section 2.2)
- Prohibited data: "Customer will not (and will not allow anyone else to) submit Prohibited Data to the Product unless authorized by the Order Form or Key Terms." (Section 3.2)
- Data after termination: "Upon Customer’s request, Provider will delete Customer Content within 60 days." (Section 5.5(b))
- Provider warranty: Provider "will not materially reduce the general functionality of the Cloud Service during the Subscription Period." (Section 6.3)
- Liability cap: "each party’s total cumulative liability for all claims arising out of or relating to this Agreement will not be more than the General Cap Amount." (Section 8.1)
- High Risk Activity: "any situation where the use or failure of the Product could be reasonably expected to lead to death, bodily injury, or environmental damage." (Section 13.19)
- Prohibited Data includes "social security numbers, driver’s license numbers, or other unique and private government ID numbers" (Section 13.26)

## Evidence and method

This is a model contract. It is not a contract that is in force between named parties. It shows the clauses of a standard cloud service agreement and which terms it leaves to a Cover Page. The library read the full text.

## Limits

- The file gives no fees, cap amount, governing law or subscription term. These are Variables for the Cover Page.
- The file has no Cover Page form. Section 13.30 gives the address of the Standard Terms as blank ("posted at .").
- The file does not say who uses this form or for which services.
