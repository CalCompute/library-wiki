# PyTorch Foundation welcomes vLLM (web)

## Citation

PyTorch Foundation. "PyTorch Foundation Welcomes vLLM as a Hosted Project." Blog post, May 7, 2025. https://pytorch.org/blog/pytorch-foundation-welcomes-vllm/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- On May 7, 2025, the PyTorch Foundation announced vLLM as a PyTorch Foundation-hosted project. UC Berkeley contributed it.
- vLLM is an inference and serving engine for large language models (LLMs). It runs on GPUs (graphics processing units), CPUs and other accelerators from several vendors.
- Hosted projects are "officially governed and administered under the PyTorch Foundation’s neutral and transparent governance model."

## Background

The PyTorch Foundation published this post on pytorch.org. vLLM was one of the first new projects after the foundation became an umbrella foundation. The post describes vLLM, its features and its work with the PyTorch team. The site footer gives "Copyright © The Linux Foundation®".

## Key claims

- Contribution: "Contributed by the University of California – Berkeley, vLLM is a high-throughput, memory-efficient inference and serving engine designed for LLMs." (opening)
- Hardware: PyTorch supports "a wide array of hardware backends. These include NVIDIA GPUs, AMD GPUs, Google Cloud TPUs, Intel GPUs, Intel CPUs, Intel Gaudi HPUs, and AWS Neuron, among others." (opening)
- Governance: "Foundation-Hosted Projects are projects that fall under the umbrella, they are officially governed and administered under the PyTorch Foundation’s neutral and transparent governance model." (opening)
- Origin: "Originally built around the innovative PagedAttention algorithm, vLLM has grown into a comprehensive, state-of-the-art inference engine." (What is vLLM?)
- Community size: "vLLM has garnered significant attention, achieving over 46,500 GitHub stars and over 1000 contributors" (What is vLLM?)
- Steward: "vLLM gains a committed steward in the Foundation, ensuring long-term codebase maintenance, production stability, and transparent community governance." (Accelerating Open Source AI Together)
- Joint work: "vLLM will make sure the code runs on Torch nightly, and the PyTorch team will monitor to ensure all tests are passed." (Accelerating Open Source AI Together)

## Evidence and method

This is an announcement by the host foundation. It gives the project's features and its star and contributor counts at that date. It gives no measured performance data.

## Limits

- The post does not describe the PyTorch Foundation's governance model or any funding for vLLM.
- It does not say what UC Berkeley's role is after the contribution.
- The star count is from May 2025. On 2026-11-11 the repository had 96,755 stars ([vLLM GitHub repository record (web)](vLLM%20GitHub%20repository%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- The feature list is the project's own description. The method is in [Kwon et al 2023 PagedAttention arXiv record (web)](Kwon%20et%20al%202023%20PagedAttention%20arXiv%20record%20%28web%29.md).
