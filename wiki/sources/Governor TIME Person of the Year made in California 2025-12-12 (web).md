# Governor TIME Person of the Year made in California 2025-12-12 (web)

## Citation

Office of Governor Gavin Newsom. "TIME's Person of the Year was Made in California." Press release, Sacramento, December 12, 2025. https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/12/12/times-person-of-the-year-was-made-in-california/. Read 2026-10-20.

## Bottom line

- By December 2025, the Governor's office says California is home to "33 of the 50 top AI companies". Earlier releases said 32.
- It repeats the AI job-posting share: "15.7% of all U.S. AI job postings were in California", per the 2025 Stanford AI Index.
- It repeats the VC claim: "more than half" of 2024 global AI startup funding went to the Bay Area. The release does not mention CalCompute.

## Background

TIME named the "Architects of AI" its 2025 Person of the Year. The Office of Governor [Gavin Newsom](../entities/Gov.%20Gavin%20Newsom.md) used the cover to promote California's AI sector. The release lists eight people. One is Fei-Fei Li, who led the [California Report on Frontier AI Policy 2025-06-17](California%20Report%20on%20Frontier%20AI%20Policy%202025-06-17.md).

## Key claims

- "California is home to the world's leading AI companies and research institutions."
- "The Golden State leads and dominates in AI innovation, trust and safety standards, and workforce development".
- The release lists eight people. They are Jensen Huang (Nvidia), Lisa Su (AMD), Sundar Pichai (Google) and Sam Altman (OpenAI). They also include Mark Zuckerberg (Meta), Dario Amodei (Anthropic), Fei-Fei Li (Stanford) and Elon Musk (xAI).
- Fei-Fei Li "led California's Report on Frontier AI Policy to help guide Governor Newsom's development of responsible guardrails for the deployment of GenAI."
- "the state is home to 33 of the 50 top AI companies worldwide."
- "In 2024, 15.7% of all U.S. AI job postings were in California — #1 by state, well ahead of Texas (8.8%) and New York (5.8%), per the 2025 Stanford AI Index."
- "California is home to three of the four companies that have passed the $3 trillion valuation mark." The release names "Google, Apple, and Nvidia".
- "In 2024, more than half of global VC funding for AI and machine learning startups went to companies in the Bay Area."
- The state works with tech companies "to prepare the innovators of tomorrow." It names two research institutes: BAIR at Berkeley and SAIL at Stanford.
- Under "Don't just ask us, ask AI", the release prints answers from Grok, Gemini and ChatGPT. The Grok answer says "32 of the world's top 50 AI firms".

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It names the 2025 Stanford AI Index for the job-posting figures. Its other links go through a mailing-list tracker, so their targets do not show. The release lists forbes.com, cbinsights.com, techcrunch.com, gsv.ventures and news.stanford.edu as sources for the AI answers.

## Limits

- No CalCompute content.
- The source for "33 of the 50" does not show. It is probably the Forbes AI 50, as in the March 2025 release. The release does not say so.
- The release quotes chatbot answers as support. These are not evidence.
- Company values next to each name have no date or source.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This release says "33 of the 50 top AI companies". The Governor's releases of March 12, March 18 and September 29, 2025 say 32 ([Governor ICYMI 32 of top 50 AI companies 2025-03-12 (web)](Governor%20ICYMI%2032%20of%20top%2050%20AI%20companies%202025-03-12%20%28web%29.md), [Governor AI working group release 2025-03-18](Governor%20AI%20working%20group%20release%202025-03-18.md), [Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29](Newsom%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29.md)). The figure probably follows a newer list. Use 33 with the date and the list year.
