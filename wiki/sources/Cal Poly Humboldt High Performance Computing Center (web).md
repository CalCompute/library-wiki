# Cal Poly Humboldt High Performance Computing Center (web)

## Citation

Cal Poly Humboldt website, no date. https://www.humboldt.edu/its/research/high-performance-computing. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. ## Bottom line

- Cal Poly Humboldt's ITS gives faculty and students research computing help: pre-award consultations, hardware and code review, custom containers, parallel computing, software advice and storage.
- ITS says "many FREE resources" are available. Users quoted on the page ran their work on the National Research Platform (NRP) and TIDE.

## Background

This is the research computing page of ITS at Cal Poly Humboldt, a California State University (CSU) campus in Arcata. It lists what the research support team helps with and gives six quotes from users. The page links separate pages on free HPC resources and research questions, which this library does not hold.

## Key claims

Scope (opening paragraphs):

- "ITS is committed to supporting faculty and students as they explore grant opportunities to further research, creative activities, and program development. These projects may require high performance computing (HPC), storage, code review, or software purchases."
- "In addition to research resources, we offer limited support for instructional needs that require HPC."
- "If you are uncertain about the information technology needs of your project please contact us during the pre-award stage when you are applying for a grant. ITS offers early consultation and planning, with many FREE resources available."

Shared platforms ("Success Stories"):

- A postdoctoral research associate: "I doubled my planned study design factors because the NRP and TIDE made it possible to run my simulations in parallel in a shorter amount of time than I would otherwise be able to."
- An assistant professor: "NRP was a really nice resource for training my GPU hungry image segmentation models."

## Evidence and method

This is a campus service page. It gives a list of services and quotes from users. It gives no hardware figures, user counts or costs.

## Limits

- The page does not describe any campus cluster hardware.
- It does not say which resources are free or what the others cost.
- The quotes are testimonials that ITS chose. The page has no date.
