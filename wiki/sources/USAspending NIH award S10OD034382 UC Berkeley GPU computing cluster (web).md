## Bottom line

- NIH gives the University of California (UC), Berkeley $489,609.49 for a GPU computing cluster with petabyte-scale data arrays. The cluster is for the core computing facility of the College of Chemistry.
- The period of performance is May 15, 2023 to May 14, 2025. The record shows the full amount as spent.

## Background

USAspending.gov publishes federal spending records. This is its API record for one NIH grant. The NIH Office of the Director funds it, under Assistance Listing 93.351, "Research Infrastructure Programs", and funding opportunity PAR-22-080. The recipient is The Regents of the University of California, at an address in Berkeley, Alameda County. Its business categories are "Higher Education" and "Higher Education (Public)".

## Key claims

- "THE COLLEGE OF CHEMISTRY AT THE UNIVERSITY OF CALIFORNIA, BERKELEY SEEKS FUNDS TO PURCHASE A HIGH PERFORMANCE GPU COMPUTING CLUSTER WITH INTEGRATED PETABYTE SCALE DATA ARRAYS DEDICATED TO THE CURRENT AND FUTURE NEEDS OF A BROAD COMMUNITY OF NIH- SUPPORTED RESEARCH GROUPS" (description)
- "THIS PROPOSAL WILL ENABLE RESEARCH INNOVATION BY PUTTING STATE-OF-THE-ART COMPUTATIONAL RESOURCES INTO THE HANDS OF ALL NIH INVESTIGATORS, NOT JUST COMPUTATIONAL SPECIALISTS." (description)
- "UNFORTUNATELY, MOST SUPERCOMPUTING COMES WITH A BARE COMMAND LINE AND A VERY HIGH BARRIER TO ENTRY DUE TO THE LEARNING CURVE OF ARCANE QUEUING AND OPERATING SYSTEMS." (description)
- "OUR NIH FUNDED RESEARCHERS ARE CURRENTLY USING A >5 YEAR OLD CPU CLUSTER WHICH IS LACKING THE GPU RESOURCES NEEDED TO TACKLE NEXT GENERATION TRANSFORMATIVE PUBLIC HEALTH RESEARCH." (description)
- The cluster will use "AN EXISTING CPU CLUSTER AND A CORE FACILITY STAFFED BY PROFESSIONAL SCIENTISTS WITH MORE THAN 30 YEARS OF EXPERIENCE IN COMPUTATION." (description)
- "To be eligible for instrumentation and equipment programs, applications must be Private nonprofit institutions/organizations, Public nonprofit institutions/organizations." (Assistance Listing 93.351, applicant eligibility)
- `"total_obligation": 489609.49`, `"date_signed": "2023-05-05"`, `"total_outlay": 489609.49` (amounts and dates)

## Evidence and method

A federal award record. It gives the amount obligated and spent, the dates, the funding office, the Assistance Listing and the recipient. The description is the recipient's project summary at the time of the award, not a report on results. The record shows no subawards. The non-federal funding is $0.

## Limits

- The record gives no count or model of the GPUs and no storage capacity.
- It does not give the user rules or fees of the College of Chemistry facility.
- The place of performance gives the state only, with no city.
