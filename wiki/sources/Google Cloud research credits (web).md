# Google Cloud research credits (web)

## Citation

Google for Education. "Apply for Google Cloud research credits." Program page and application form, date not stated. https://edu.google.com/programs/credits/research/. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- Google gives Google Cloud Platform (GCP) credit to faculty, PhD students and postdoctoral researchers in listed countries. The application is one web form with a proposal of 250 words or fewer.
- The form asks for a cost estimate from the pricing calculator and a billing account. It also asks how the project will pay for itself "after your credit expires in 1 year".
- Credit lasts 365 days. It "may not be used for commercial purposes".

## Background

The page is both the program summary and the application form. The form has four parts: personal information, organization information, project information and additional information. Terms and conditions follow the form. The page links to program FAQs on a Google support site.

## Key claims

- Eligibility: "Eligibility for research grants is limited to faculty, PhD students and postdoctoral researchers from accredited higher education institutions and eligible nonprofit research institutions in approved countries." (Program overview)
- Countries: "The program is currently only available in the countries listed. If your country is not on this list, you can still express interest." The list includes the United States. (Personal information)
- Proposal: "Please provide a project proposal that includes: research problem to be solved, GCP tools to be used, project timeline, key milestones and outcomes, and how GCP can support your research in the future." The limit is "(250 words maximum)". (Project information)
- Use type: "How do you intend to apply awarded cloud credits?" The choices are "Proof of concept", "Evaluation/Comparison", "Workshop or Training" and "Developing Open Source Software". (Additional information)
- Funding and experience: the form asks "Does your research project have an external funding source?" and "Have you used cloud infrastructure for your research before?" (Additional information)
- Cost estimate: "Please calculate your expected costs using the pricing calculator. Submit the total amount and a link to your calculator results." (Additional information)
- Sustainability: "Please provide information on how you intend to fund your project after your credit expires in 1 year." (Additional information)
- Activation and expiry: "Credit awards must be activated within 60 days of the project start date indicated in the application form. Credit awards expire 365 days from the coupon redemption date, or when the credit amount has been fully used, whichever comes first." (Terms and conditions)
- "The credit may be issued in increments as You use the credit over the period of time during which the credit is valid." (Terms and conditions)
- Use rules: "The credit is non-transferable and may not be sold or bartered." "The credits do not have commercial value and may not be used for commercial purposes." (Terms and conditions)
- Faculty accept the credit "on behalf of Your educational institution and the credit can only be used on behalf of the educational entity in connection with the project described in this application form and not for Your personal use." (Terms and conditions)
- Ethics check: the applicant states that "the credit is consistent with all applicable laws and regulations, including relevant ethics rules and laws". (Terms and conditions)

## Evidence and method

The program's own application page. The form fields show what Google asks. They do not show how Google scores the answers.

## Limits

- The page does not state the award size.
- The page does not state who reviews, the criteria or the review time.
- The page does not state reporting or acknowledgement duties.
- The page does not state renewal.
- The page names no subsidized or paid tier.
