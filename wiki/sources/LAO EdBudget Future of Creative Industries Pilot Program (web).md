## Bottom line

- The LAO table gives the Proposition 98 spending of the California Community Colleges by program for 2024-25, 2025-26 and 2026-27.
- The 2026-27 enacted budget gives $15 million to the "Future of Creative Industries Pilot Project". The line has no money in the two earlier years.
- Total spending is $14,602 million in 2026-27, up $1,354 million (10.2 percent) from 2025-26.

## Background

The LAO is the nonpartisan fiscal and policy advisor of the California Legislature. Its EdBudget figures give education budget data. This figure is one table of the July 2026 set. AB 2504 (Chapter 305 of 2026) sets up the Future of Creative Industries Pilot Program ([AB 2504 chaptered text Chapter 305 of 2026](AB%202504%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20305%20of%202026.md)).

## Evidence and method

A budget table by the Legislature's fiscal office, in millions of dollars. It gives amounts by program, with notes on one-time funds and funds that other programs redirect.

## Limits

- The table does not say whether the $15 million is ongoing or one-time.
- It names the line "Pilot Project". AB 2504 names it the "Pilot Program".
- It gives no detail of how the money is spent, and no spending outside Proposition 98.
