## Bottom line

- AB 2392 adds Article 14, "Artificial Intelligence", to the Education Code, from section 66098.
- The California Community Colleges (CCC) Chancellor's office and the California State University (CSU) must convene a working group on generative artificial intelligence (GenAI). The University of California (UC) is requested to take part.
- The working group develops GenAI procurement standards and a training. It presents its recommendations to the system leaders on or before Jan. 1, 2028.
- The segments give the training to students, faculty or staff, review it each academic year and keep training records for four years.
- Until procurement standards are adopted, a segment reports to the Legislature within 60 days after it signs a systemwide GenAI contract.

## Key claims

- "The office of the Chancellor of the California Community Colleges and the California State University shall, and the University of California is requested to, do both of the following:" (Ed. Code § 66098.1)
- "The purpose of the working group is to develop responsible training protocols and procurement standards for the purchase, development, and use of GenAI systems for educational purposes that are consistent with current law regarding the regulation, procurement, development, and use of GenAI systems." (§ 66098.1(a)(1))
- "The system is configured to collect only data that is strictly necessary for the stated educational purpose and is subject to regular audits." (§ 66098.1(a)(3)(A)(vi), one of nine minimum protections)
- "The system’s vendor discloses all training data sources, including whether copyrighted content, personally identifiable information, or student-generated content was used." (§ 66098.1(a)(3)(A)(vii))
- "On or before January 1, 2028, present its recommendations for procurement standards and training developed pursuant to subparagraphs (A) and (B) to the respective system leaders." (§ 66098.1(a)(3)(C))
- "The process used in evaluating and selecting the system, including any risk assessments conducted, competing systems considered, and how the procurement decision was made." (§ 66098.3(b), one of four items in the contract report)
- "“Sycophancy” means the tendency of a GenAI system to overly agree with, validate, or flatter a user even when the user is communicating factually inaccurate or illegal ideas or actions for the primary purpose of optimizing engagement." (§ 66098(c))

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate. It requests UC to act and does not require it. The working group presents recommendations; the text does not say who adopts the procurement standards, or by when. The text sets no penalty.

The capture is of the Legislature's billNavClient page for the bill. The Wayback Machine has no billTextClient capture after the bill was chaptered. The committee analysis in the library describes the April 23, 2026 version, not this text.

The bill does not mention research compute or CalCompute.
