# CDT Anthropic partnership expansion 2026-07 (web)

## Citation

California Department of Technology. "California expands AI access across state government through Anthropic partnership." Newsroom release, July 8, 2026. https://www.cdt.ca.gov/newsroom/2026/07/california-expands-ai-access-across-state-government-through-anthropic-partnership/. ## Bottom line

- A statewide agreement lets state departments buy Anthropic's AI assistant Claude at a 50% discount through central procurement. Cities and counties get the same price, with free training and help from Anthropic.
- Claude is the first AI productivity platform on the California Department of Technology (CDT) Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal.

## Background

CDT published this release in its newsroom on July 8, 2026. It describes the agreement and the SITeS portal. It quotes the State Chief Information Officer and the CDT Deputy Director for Statewide Technology Procurement. It lists four state bodies that use Claude now.

## Key claims

- "The agreement allows state departments to purchase Claude at a 50% discount through a centralized procurement program. California cities and counties will also have access to the same pricing, along with free workforce training, technical assistance, and implementation support from Anthropic." (body)
- "Claude will be the first AI productivity platform offered statewide through the California Department of Technology’s new Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal, which is designed to simplify procurement of technology tools across government." (body)
- "The agreement is intended to help public employees improve efficiency, routine work, and services for Californians while ensuring AI will support and not replace the work of public employees." (body)
- "The technology is in use by the California Department of Technology and the California Governor’s Office of Emergency Services, to strengthen cybersecurity by helping scan, triage, and patch state software." (body)
- "Bringing purchasing together through one statewide agreement saves time, reduces costs, and helps departments focus on actually using the tools instead of navigating a lengthy procurement process." (Tiffany Angulo, CDT Deputy Director, Statewide Technology Procurement)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It gives the discount rate. It names four users: CDT, the Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES), the DMV and the Department of Health Care Services. It gives no list price, contract value, term or seat count.

## Limits

The release does not give the contract length or the number of state workers who use Claude. It does not mention research compute, the University of California or CalCompute.
