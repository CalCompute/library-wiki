# NERSC 2026 allocation year call for proposals (web)

## Citation

## Bottom line

- NERSC asked for proposals for allocation year (AY) 2026, which runs from January 21, 2026, to January 19, 2027. Proposals were due October 6, 2025.
- Awards give CPU and GPU node time on Perlmutter and storage. Proposals "must support the mission of the DOE Office of Science". Applicants use the ERCAP Request Form.

## Background

NERSC is a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Science user facility at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. This notice gives the dates, the resources, the review process and the submission route for AY 2026. NERSC also accepts proposals during the year.

## Key claims

- "The 2026 allocation year (AY 2026) runs from January 21, 2026, through January 19, 2027." (introduction)
- "Submissions open August 11, 2025, and are due October 6, 2025, 5 p.m. PDT." (introduction)
- Available resources: "CPU compute time on Perlmutter CPU-only nodes", "GPU compute time on Perlmutter's GPU-accelerated nodes", "Community File System (CFS) storage" and "HPSS Archival storage". (Available resources)
- "While NERSC accepts proposals year-round, the majority of its resources are distributed before the allocation year begins." (Why apply now?)
- "DOE Mission Science proposals are evaluated by the Office of Science program selected by the proposer. Each of these programs has a pool of time to distribute." (How awards are determined)
- "Exploratory, Education, and Director's Reserve projects are evaluated by NERSC staff, and allocations are drawn from NERSC allocation pools." (How awards are determined)
- "Requests to use NERSC resources are submitted via the ERCAP (Energy Research Computing Allocations Process) Request Form." (How to submit a proposal)
- "Requests submitted by the deadline will receive a determination, emailed to principal investigators and project managers, the week of December 15, 2025." (Award notifications)

## Evidence and method

A call for proposals on the NERSC website. It gives the dates, the resources, who reviews each project class and where to submit. It gives no count of awards and no total of node hours.

## Limits

The notice does not give the total time available or a success rate. It does not give the review criteria. ASCR Leadership Computing Challenge (ALCC) allocations "are managed separately". The notice has no posting date of its own.
