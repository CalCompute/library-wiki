## Bottom line

- AB 2504 adds Article 1.7, the Future of Creative Industries Pilot Program, to the Education Code, from section 78026.
- The Chancellor's Office of the California Community Colleges must set up the pilot with labor unions of creative workers and with industry. Up to 10 community colleges take part.
- The pilot gives reskilling and upskilling to creative and entertainment workers who have lost or can lose work to generative artificial intelligence (AI).
- Dates: eligibility criteria by June 30, 2027; start by July 1, 2028; a report to the Legislature by Jan. 1, 2032; repeal on Jan. 1, 2033. The article needs an appropriation.

## Background

Assembly Member Bauer-Kahan introduced AB 2504 on Feb. 20, 2026, with three coauthors. It passed the Senate on Aug. 20 and the Assembly on Aug. 24, 2026. The Governor approved it on Sept. 18, 2026. The 2026-27 budget gives $15 million to the program ([LAO EdBudget Future of Creative Industries Pilot Program (web)](LAO%20EdBudget%20Future%20of%20Creative%20Industries%20Pilot%20Program%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "The pilot program shall be developed in partnership with labor unions representing creative workers and with industry representatives." (§ 78026.2(a)(2))
- The first goal is "Supporting workers in California’s creative and entertainment industries, including film, television, music, touring, and live events, who have been or are at risk of being displaced by generative artificial intelligence, including sound engineers, voice actors, concept artists, and employees in entry-level positions, by providing structured reskilling and upskilling opportunities through the California Community Colleges." (§ 78026.2(a)(3)(A))
- A goal is "Establishing a coordinated, outcomes-driven model that California Community Colleges and workforce systems can replicate across sectors facing artificial intelligence-driven employment disruption." (§ 78026.2(a)(3)(C))
- "The pilot program shall be limited to up to 10 community colleges statewide, to be selected by the chancellor’s office." (§ 78026.2(c))
- "Implementation of the pilot program for the selected community colleges shall commence on or before July 1, 2028." (§ 78026.3)
- The report must include "the number of participants who completed the pilot program, a comprehensive list of the certificates or degrees awarded to participants who completed the pilot program, and outcomes and job placement rates of participants who completed the pilot program." (§ 78026.4(b)(1))
- "This article shall become operative only upon an appropriation by the Legislature in the annual Budget Act or another statute for its purposes." (§ 78026.5)

## Limits

- The bill sets no amount of money, no number of participants and no curriculum.
- It does not name the colleges, unions or industry partners.
- It does not define "creative and entertainment industries" beyond its list of examples.
