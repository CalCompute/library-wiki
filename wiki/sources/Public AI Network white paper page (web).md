# Public AI Network white paper page (web)

## Citation

Public AI Network. "Whitepaper" (web page). publicai.network, date not stated. https://publicai.network/whitepaper/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The page is the web summary of the Public AI Network's 2024 white paper. It calls for "building AI infrastructure for the common good".
- It gives the same three features as the paper: Public Access, Public Accountability and Permanent Public Goods. It names public investments, public utilities and public goods as the parts of a roadmap.

## Background

The [Public AI Network](../entities/Public%20AI%20Network.md) publishes this page. The full paper is in the library as [Public AI Network 2024 white paper](Public%20AI%20Network%202024%20white%20paper.md), from a PDF. This page is not that paper. It holds only the summary, about 300 words, and a link to a copy of the paper on Google Drive. The network's home page is [Public AI Network homepage (web)](Public%20AI%20Network%20homepage%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "This whitepaper sets out a vision for a different path for AI." "We call for a new collective enterprise: building AI infrastructure for the common good." (opening paragraph)
- "Yet the concentration of AI development within a handful of unaccountable firms threatens to write the public out of our story." (second paragraph)
- "**Public Access** – Certain capabilities are so important for participation in public life that access to them should be universal." (features list)
- "**Permanent Public Goods** – Public AI is funded and operated in a way to maintain the public goods it produces permanently, enabling innovators to safely build on a firm foundation." (features list)
- "We set out a roadmap for *public investments* into public goods at each layer of the AI stack to lower the costs of innovation, vertically-integrated *public utilities* to fund sustainable development, and innovative new *public goods* that invent and scale breakthroughs." (roadmap paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A summary page for an advocacy white paper. It states the paper's argument. It gives no data, no sources and no date.

## Limits

The page does not give the paper's version or date, or the examples in the paper, such as CalCompute and Empire AI. Read the paper for those. One sentence differs from the PDF. The page says "build on a firm foundation", and the PDF says "build atop its firm foundation" ([Public AI Network 2024 white paper](Public%20AI%20Network%202024%20white%20paper.md), Executive summary).
