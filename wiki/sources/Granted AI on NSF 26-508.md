# Granted AI on NSF 26-508

## Citation

Granted Research Team. "NSF Is Building a 56-State AI Coordination Layer. The July 16 Deadline for NSF 26-508 Decides Which Institution Owns Your State for the Next Three Years." Granted AI blog (vendor blog), May 25, 2026. https://grantedai.com/blog/nsf-26-508-techaccess-ai-ready-america-coordination-hubs-224-million-56-states-july-16-deadline-strategy-2026. ## Bottom line

- This is a vendor blog post that sells a grant-writing product.
- Its useful content is strategy advice: only one hub per state, and the lead must show statewide convening power. Round 1 closed on July 16, 2026. Round 2 letters of intent are due Dec. 15, 2026.
- The post is a reason to read the solicitation, not evidence.

## Background

NSF 26-508 funds "TechAccess: AI-Ready America" State/Territory Coordination Hubs.

## Key claims

The post says:

- NSF released NSF 26-508 "In April 2026". (section 1)
- (section 1)
- Round 1 letters of intent were due June 16, 2026 and full proposals July 16, 2026. Round 2 letters of intent are due Dec. 15, 2026. Round 3 letters of intent are due June 1, 2027. (section 1)
- Each institution may submit one proposal only. (section 1)
- Hubs do not fund "original research" or "direct service delivery to individual learners or workers". (section "What a Coordination Hub Actually Does")
- Required metrics include individuals trained by category, "K-12 students, post-secondary learners, incumbent workers, dislocated workers, small-business owners, government employees". (section "What the Solicitation Will Not Fund")
- Land-grant universities with Cooperative Extension, flagship universities and state nonprofit intermediaries are likely leads. (section "Who Should Lead the Proposal")
- Its last section mentions a companion program, NSF 26-503 CyberAICorps. (section "Partnerships the Solicitation Expects")

## Evidence and method

The post paraphrases the solicitation and adds opinion. It gives no method and no sources other than links to NSF and to its own blog.

## Limits

- Vendor marketing. The post ends with a sales pitch for the Granted product.
- It gets facts wrong against the solicitation:

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the post says NSF released NSF 26-508 "In April 2026". The solicitation page says "Posted: March 25, 2026". See [NSF 26-508 TechAccess solicitation (web)](NSF%2026-508%20TechAccess%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the post lists six trainee categories for the required metrics, including "government employees". The solicitation lists "(by category: educators, workforce, small business owners)" (Section V, Section 4).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the post says hubs do not do "direct service delivery". The solicitation's third responsibility is "AI Deployment Support", which is "hands-on assistance for AI adoption" (Section II). The post itself also describes "hands-on AI deployment assistance" in the same section.
