# FCC Consumer Advisory Committee twelfth term members (web)

## Citation

Federal Communications Commission (FCC). "FCC Consumer Advisory Committee Appointment of Membership and Announcement of First Meeting Date for the Twelfth Term." Public Notice DA 24-150, released February 20, 2024. https://docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/DA-24-150A1.pdf. Read 2026-11-06. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-06 FCC Consumer Advisory Committee twelfth term members (web).pdf`.

## Bottom line

- The FCC Chairwoman appointed 28 members to the twelfth term of the FCC Consumer Advisory Committee (CAC).
- Member 12 is The Kapor Center, with Lili Gangas, Chief Technology Community Officer, as its representative.

## Background

A public notice of the FCC. The CAC gives advice to the FCC on consumer protection. The notice names the members and the first meeting date, April 4, 2024. This is a profile page. Her entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "By this Public Notice, the Chairwoman of the Federal Communications Commission (FCC or Commission) announces the appointment of members for the twelfth term of the Commission’s Consumer Advisory Committee (CAC or Committee)" (page 1)
- The CAC "provides advice and recommendations to the Commission on a wide array of consumer protection related matters specified by the Commission." (page 1)
- "the Chairwoman has selected 28 members, as listed below." (page 1)
- The term "runs from October 16, 2022, through October 16, 2024." (footnote 1)
- "(12) The Kapor Center / Lili Gangas, Chief Technology Community Officer" (member list)

## Evidence and method

An official notice of a federal agency. It is a primary record of the appointment.

## Limits

- The notice covers the twelfth term only. It does not show later terms.
- It does not say what each member works on in the committee.
