# MIT AI Exchange homepage (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). "MIT AI Exchange." Homepage, date not stated; lists events to December 2, 2026. https://aiexchange.mit.edu. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The MIT AI Exchange is "a community-driven, Institute-wide effort" on AI in MIT's teaching and research. It has a Learning Exchange and a Research Exchange.
- Both exchanges share compute ("AI models and computing resources"), spaces, technical support and coordination.

## Background

The homepage names the leaders of the two exchanges. The Chancellor and the Provost lead them, with faculty directors. The page lists events for November and December 2026 and news from October and November 2026. It also announces a search for an Executive Director.

## Key claims

- "A community-driven, Institute-wide effort to adapt MIT's teaching and research to AI — with shared guidance, infrastructure, and resources." (heading)
- "Supporting researchers at MIT in benefitting from advances in AI — developing shared research tools, connecting expertise across research groups, and advancing responsible AI use to accelerate discovery and address pressing challenges." (Research Exchange)
- The page lists four things "Shared by both exchanges" (Structure):
  - "Compute": "AI models and computing resources"
  - "Spaces": "Collaboration and learning spaces"
  - "People": "Technical support, UROPs and a help desk"
  - "Coordination": "Communications and institutional support"
- "The MIT AI Exchange brings together two connected efforts: the Learning Exchange and the Research Exchange." (Structure)

## Evidence and method

A program homepage. It gives the structure, the leaders and the events. It gives no figures.

## Limits

The page does not say what compute the exchanges share, how much, or who pays for it. It gives no budget.
