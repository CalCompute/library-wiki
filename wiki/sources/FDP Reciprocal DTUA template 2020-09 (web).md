# FDP Reciprocal DTUA template 2020-09 (web)

## Citation

Federal Demonstration Partnership (FDP). "FDP Reciprocal Data Transfer and Use Agreement." Template, September 2020. https://thefdp.org/wp-content/uploads/Reciprocal_DTUA_September_2020.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20. Not rehosted: the host is not a government host and the file states no open licence.

## Bottom line

- The template is a standard contract for two institutions that give data sets to each other for one research project. Each party is a Providing Party for its own data and a Receiving Party for the other party's data.
- Only "Authorized Persons" can use the data, only for the project. The Receiving Party keeps control of the data and cannot give it to a third party without written consent.
- Each Receiving Party is liable for its own use of the data. Neither party indemnifies the other.

## Background

The Federal Demonstration Partnership is a group of federal agencies and research institutions that writes standard research agreements. This is its reciprocal data transfer and use agreement (DTUA) of September 2020. It has 13 terms, a signature page and three kinds of attachment. They give the project and the data of each party, data-specific terms, and permitted third parties. The one-way version of March 2024, for one Provider and one Recipient, is [FDP data use agreement template (web)](FDP%20data%20use%20agreement%20template%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Each Party is a Providing Party when providing Data and a Receiving Party when receiving Data." (Term 1)
- "Providing Party shall retain ownership of any rights it may have in the Data, and Receiving Party does not obtain any rights in the Data other than as set forth herein." (Term 1)
- "The Data will be used solely to conduct the Project and solely by Receiving Party’s Scientist and Receiving Party’s faculty, employees, fellows, students, and agents" (Term 2)
- "Receiving Party agrees to establish appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized use of or access to the Data" (Term 3)
- "the other Party will have thirty (30) days from receipt to review proposed manuscripts and ten (10) days from receipt to review proposed abstracts to ensure that the Data is appropriately protected." (Term 5)
- "Receiving Party may retain one (1) copy of the Data to the extent necessary to comply with the records retention requirements under any law, and for the purposes of research integrity and verification." (Term 7)
- "Each Receiving Party shall be liable for damages, losses, claims, and demands which may arise from its use, storage, disclosure, or disposal of the Data except to the extent (a) prohibited by law and/or (b) caused by the negligence, willful misconduct, or violation of applicable privacy or security laws and regulations by the Providing Party." (Term 9)
- "No indemnification for any damage, loss, claim, demand, or liability is intended or provided by either Party under this Agreement." (Term 9)
- "In the event of any conflict between the obligations set forth in the applicable Attachment 2 and this Agreement, the obligations set forth in Attachment 2 shall prevail." (Term 11)

## Evidence and method

A blank contract template of five pages, with no commentary. It gives the terms that the parties sign and the fields that they complete. Either party can end the agreement with 30 days of written notice (Term 7).

## Limits

The PDF does not have the text of Attachment 2, the data-specific terms; the parties write those terms. The template does not mention protected health information under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA). It has no cost terms. The one-way template of March 2024 says that the Recipient "assumes all liability". This reciprocal template makes an exception for harm that the Providing Party causes. This is a difference between two templates, not a contradiction.
