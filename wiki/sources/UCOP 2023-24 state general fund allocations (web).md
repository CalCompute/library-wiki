# UCOP 2023-24 state general fund allocations (web)

## Citation

University of California Office of the President, Budget Analysis and Planning. *University of California 2023-24 Summary of State General Fund Allocations.* Two-page PDF table, "Rev. 6/30/2026". https://www.ucop.edu/operating-budget/budgets-and-reports/other-resources/2023-24-summary-of-state-general-fund-allocations.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- This table shows how UC divided its 2023-24 State General Funds among the ten campuses, the Office of the President and systemwide items.
- The system total is $4,861,589,000: $4,719,044,000 permanent and $142,545,000 one-time.
- One research earmark is $2,550,000 for the "San Diego Supercomputer Ctr." in the San Diego column.

## Background

UCOP Budget Analysis and Planning prepares this table. Its rows are earmarks, systemwide commitments, campus-specific allocations, per-student funding and one-time allocations. Its columns are the ten campuses, a campus total, UCOP, the capital program, off-campus programs and a system total. The Markdown copy loses the table layout. The figures below come from the PDF.

## Key claims

- SDSC earmark: the row "San Diego Supercomputer Ctr." under "Earmarks not subject to cost adjustments", Research, shows "$ 2,550,000" for San Diego and the same amount as the system total. (p. 1)
- Other research earmarks: "CITRIS" shows "$ 1,200,000" for Berkeley; "Cal IT2" shows "$ 360,000" for Irvine and "$ 840,000" for San Diego. (p. 1)
- Per student: "$ per Weighted Budgeted Enrollment, Inc. Lottery Funds" is "$ 8,851" for most campuses. (p. 2)
- Permanent total: "Total Permanent State General Funds" has a system total of "$ 4,719,044,000". (p. 2)
- One-time total: "Total One-Time Allocations" has a system total of "$ 142,545,000". (p. 2)
- All support: "2023-24 State General Fund Support (Permanent + One-Time)" has a system total of "$ 4,861,589,000". (p. 2)
- Capital note: "The University's capital program includes $124.9 million dedicated for the AB 94 program". (p. 2, note 1)

## Evidence and method

This is an official budget table. It gives allocated amounts by line and by campus. It has no text that explains the lines.

## Limits

- The table does not explain what each earmark pays for, for example what the San Diego Supercomputer Center earmark buys.
- It covers 2023-24 only.
- The table gives no source for each earmark, such as a Budget Act item.
- The page footer says "Rev. 6/30/2026". The table does not say what changed in that revision.
