# CDT privacy policy (web)

## Citation

California Department of Technology (CDT). "California Department of Technology Privacy Policy." Effective May 23, 2017; last reviewed and revised June 25, 2025. cdt.ca.gov. https://www.cdt.ca.gov/privacy-policy/. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CDT privacy policy.html`.

## Bottom line

- CDT limits the personal information that its websites collect to what a lawful purpose needs. It states that purpose in a notice on the form that collects the information.
- CDT does not sell or distribute electronically collected personal information without the user's written permission, except as Government Code section 11015.5(b) allows.
- Users can ask to see, correct or delete their personal information.

## Background

CDT is the State's technology department under Government Code sections 11545 to 11548. The policy applies to CDT.ca.gov and to the CA.gov website that CDT runs. The CA.gov site also has its own policy, the [CA.gov privacy policy (web)](CA.gov%20privacy%20policy%20%28web%29.md). See [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md) and [CDT About the Department](CDT%20About%20the%20Department.md).

## Key claims

- "This Privacy Policy only applies to the CDT.ca.gov and CA.gov website run by the CDT." (Introduction)
- "CDT limits the collection of Personal Information to what is relevant and necessary to accomplish its lawful purpose, which is enumerated at or prior to the time of collection, in a privacy notice included on or with the form used to collect Personal Information." (Item 1)
- "CDT does not collect home, business or e-mail addresses, or account information from persons who simply browse the CA.gov website." (Item 2)
- "CDT will not distribute or sell any “Electronically Collected Personal Information” about users to any third party without prior written permission from the user except as set forth in Government Code section 11015.5(b)." (Item 9)
- "CDT’s website logs do not identify a visitor by Personal Information, and CDT makes no attempt to link other websites with the individuals that browse CDT’s website." (Item 11)

## Evidence and method

A state department privacy policy of about 1,900 words with the site's navigation text. It gives definitions, 12 practices and contact details.

## Limits

The policy gives no retention period. It covers websites only, not the data that CDT holds in the state data centers or cloud services that it runs.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this policy says it applies to "the CDT.ca.gov and CA.gov website run by the CDT", but the [CA.gov privacy policy (web)](CA.gov%20privacy%20policy%20%28web%29.md) says "This Privacy Policy only applies to CA.gov website." The two lists of the data collected on each visit are not the same: the CA.gov list adds cookies, form and survey use, external links clicked and analytics tools.
