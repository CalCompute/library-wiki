# UCR HPCC access and rates (web)

## Citation

UC Riverside High-Performance Computing Center (HPCC). "Access." HPCC website, last modified Oct. 27, 2026. https://hpcc.ucr.edu/about/overview/access/. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 UCR HPCC access and rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- An annual registration fee of $1,000 gives all members of a UC Riverside (UCR) lab access to the HPCC cluster. CPU use has no extra charge, within quotas of 384 cores for each user and 768 for each lab.
- Storage rental is $1,000 a year for each 10 TB. Owned storage has a support fee of $260 a year for each 10 TB.
- Labs can buy compute nodes. HPCC hosts and maintains them with no extra fee, gives the owner lab priority, and lets other users run short jobs on idle capacity.

## Background

The HPCC runs the shared research cluster of UC Riverside. This page states how to get an account and gives the recharge rates. It also gives the storage and node ownership options, and the rules for classes, external users and trial accounts. It points to a recharge rate PDF and a facility description for grant applications, which this library does not hold.

## Key claims

- "An annual registration fee of $1,000 gives all members of a UCR lab access to our high-performance computing infrastructure." (PI-based Registration Fee)
- The registration gives access to "Over 16,000 CPU cores (60% AMD and 40% Intel), ~230,000 cuda cores (Nvidia A100, H100, A6000 Ada, RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell, P100 and K80 GPUs), ~6PB parallel GPFS-based disk space". (PI-based Registration Fee)
- "Note: there is no extra charge for CPU usage but each user and lab have CPU quotas of 384 and 768 CPU cores, respectively." (PI-based Registration Fee)
- "$1,000 per year for each 10 TB of usable, backed-up storage." (Big data storage)
- "The annual support fee for owned disk storage is $260 per 10TB of usable and backed up storage space." (Ownership models)
- "HPCC provides rack space, power, cooling, standard system administration, and operational maintenance for integrated compute nodes without an additional hosting or compute-node maintenance fee." (Ownership models, Computer nodes)
- "To maximize the utilization of computing hardware and server-room infrastructure, idle capacity may be used for short jobs submitted by other registered users while preserving scheduling priority for the owner laboratory." (Ownership models, Computer nodes)
- "Accounts for external customers can only be granted if a lab has a strong affiliation with UC Riverside, such as a research collaboration with UCR researchers." (External user accounts)

## Evidence and method

A facility web page of about 1,700 words, with site navigation. It states the price list and access rules of one campus cluster. A footer dates its last change to Oct. 27, 2026, with the note "storage rate clarification". It gives no count of labs or users and no cost of operation.

## Limits

The page does not give a GPU price or a GPU quota. It does not say who funds the cluster beyond the fees, or what the fees recover. It does not give prices for owned nodes. A department can also buy nodes for its classes, under the same ownership model.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page lists "K80" GPUs. The hardware overview, last modified Nov. 13, 2026, lists P100, A100, H100, A6000 Ada and RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell GPUs and no K80 ([UCR HPCC hardware overview (web)](UCR%20HPCC%20hardware%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
