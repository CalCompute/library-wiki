# CPPA announcement California finalizes privacy regulations 2025-09-23 (web)

## Citation

California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA). "California Finalizes Regulations to Strengthen Consumers' Privacy." Announcement, Sept. 23, 2025 (date from the page address). cppa.ca.gov. https://cppa.ca.gov/announcements/2025/20250923.html. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CPPA announcement California finalizes privacy regulations 2025-09-23.html`.

## Bottom line

- The California Office of Administrative Law approved new CPPA regulations. They cover cybersecurity audits, risk assessments, automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT), insurance companies and updates to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regulations.
- The regulations take effect Jan. 1, 2026. Cybersecurity audit certifications are due from April 1, 2028, to April 1, 2030, by business revenue. ADMT duties for significant decisions start Jan. 1, 2027.

## Background

The CPPA enforces the CCPA. ## Key claims

- "the California Office of Administrative Law has approved regulations covering cybersecurity audits, risk assessments, automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT), insurance companies, and updates to existing CCPA regulations." (Paragraph 1)
- "The regulations go into effect January 1, 2026. However, there is additional time for businesses to comply with some of the new requirements, namely cybersecurity audits, risk assessments, and requirements for automated decisionmaking technologies." (Body)
- "April 1, 2028, if the business makes over $100 million; April 1, 2029, if the business makes between $50 million and $100 million; or April 1, 2030, if the business makes less than $50 million." (Cybersecurity audit certifications)
- "Businesses subject to risk assessment requirements must begin compliance by January 1, 2026. By April 1, 2028, they must submit to the CPPA" (Risk assessments)
- "Businesses that use ADMT to make significant decisions must comply with the ADMT requirements beginning January 1, 2027." (Automated Decisionmaking Technology)
