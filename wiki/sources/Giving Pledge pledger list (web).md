# Giving Pledge pledger list (web)

## Citation

The Giving Pledge. "Pledger List." The Giving Pledge, page date not stated. https://www.givingpledge.org/pledger-list/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The full list of the people who signed the Giving Pledge, by name only. It gives about 260 entries, in alphabetical order.
- The list shows "(d. YYYY)" after the name of a pledger who has died.

## Background

The Giving Pledge describes itself as "a promise by the world's wealthiest philanthropists to give the majority of their wealth to charitable causes in their lifetime or wills." The list page links to a separate page for each pledger.

## Key claims

- "All Pledger signatories are listed below." (Pledger List)
- The list includes "Lynne and Marc Benioff" and "Henry and Susan Samueli". (Pledger List)
- The list includes "John A. and Susan Sobrato" and "John and Timi Sobrato". (Pledger List)
- The list includes "Mark and Mary Stevens" and "Reid Hoffman and Michelle Yee". (Pledger List)
- The list includes "Sam Altman and Oliver Mulherin" and "Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan". (Pledger List)

## Evidence and method

A list of names. It gives no pledge year, location, amount or focus area. The pledger pages give those details, for example [Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)](Giving%20Pledge%20Henry%20and%20Susan%20Samueli%20%28web%29.md). Other sources in this library describe gifts by some pledgers:

- [Santa Clara University Sobrato 100 million gift 2017 (web)](Santa%20Clara%20University%20Sobrato%20100%20million%20gift%202017%20%28web%29.md)
- [UCSF Childrens Hospital Benioff 100 million gift 2010 (web)](UCSF%20Childrens%20Hospital%20Benioff%20100%20million%20gift%202010%20%28web%29.md)
- [USC Today Marc and Lynne Benioff (web)](USC%20Today%20Marc%20and%20Lynne%20Benioff%20%28web%29.md)
- [USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)

## Limits

The list does not say where each pledger lives, so it does not identify the California pledgers. It does not give amounts that pledgers have given. The page has no date.
