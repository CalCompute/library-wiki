# Bobra BIDS profile (web)

## Citation

Berkeley Institute for Data Science (BIDS). "An open approach to data science at the State of California with Monica Bobra." Event report, after Nov. 20, 2025. https://bids.berkeley.edu/node/390. Read 2026-10-29.

## Bottom line

- Monica Bobra is Principal Data Scientist at the California Office of Data and Innovation (ODI). She runs its Data Science Accelerator with state departments.
- She works in the open: ODI "provides standards for agencies to open their data and software".

## Key claims

- ODI "was created in 2022 under the State, providing data science services to other departments".
- Bobra was a Research Scientist at Stanford University, on the science team of NASA's Solar Dynamics Observatory.
- She said: "there is a driver morally to be transparent with data."
- State staff who want data science methods "are often disconnected from one another".

## Evidence and method

A report of a fireside chat with Jarrod Millman of the Berkeley Open Source Program Office.
