# SDSC TSCC condo details (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "TSCC Condo." SDSC website, no date; the page carries an update notice of September 2026. https://www.sdsc.edu/systems/tscc/condo_details.html. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 SDSC TSCC condo details.md`.

## Bottom line

- In the Triton Shared Computing Cluster (TSCC) condo program, researchers buy CPU or GPU nodes with grant or other funds. SDSC runs the nodes as part of the cluster for about five years.
- The page gives estimated node prices for 2026-2027: $27,000 and $44,000 for CPU nodes, and $34,900 to $96,000 for GPU nodes.
- At the UC rate, owners pay a setup fee of $2,519 for each node. The yearly operations fee is $1,417 for a CPU node and $4,668 for a GPU node.

## Background

SDSC at UC San Diego runs TSCC, a shared campus cluster. This page states the condo program: what researchers can buy, the service fees, the warranty, the rules for shared time and how to join. The same fees are on the SDSC service rates page ([SDSC service rates (web)](SDSC%20service%20rates%20%28web%29.md)). Each fee has a UC rate and a higher rate for users outside UC.

## Key claims

- "Condo computing is a shared ownership model in which researchers use equipment purchase funds from grants or other sources to purchase and contribute compute servers (i.e., nodes) to the system." (TSCC Condo Computing Program)
- "Participation in the program runs nominally for five years (the duration of equipment warranty)." (TSCC Condo Computing Program)
- "UPDATE: Sept 2026 - Currently due to vendor supply issues, the configuration/prices listed below are subject to change" (Compute Resources Available for Purchase)
- "NVIDIA RTX Pro 6000 Blackwell | 96GB | 4 | Intel Xeon 6960P 72-core 2.7 GHz | 2 | 1TB | $96,000" and "NVIDIA L40S | 48GB | 8 | ... | $74,000" (GPU Nodes table)
- "**System Setup Fee** one-time per node | Installation | $2,519 | $4,030" (Service Fees table, UC and non-UC rates)
- "**Condo Operations GPU** | Node/year | $4,668 | $7,468" (Service Fees table, UC and non-UC rates)
- "Each year, condo cluster participants will receive an allocation of cluster computing time proportional to the capacity of their purchased nodes." (Time-sharing on Condo Nodes)
- "Jobs that extend to other participants’ condo nodes have an eight–hour time limit." (Time-sharing on Condo Nodes)
- Costs of TSCC and other SDSC cloud services "are **exempt from the application of IDC** when acquired to support extramurally funded research." (Indirect Costs (IDC) for UC San Diego Users)

## Evidence and method

A program web page with tables of node configurations, estimated prices and fees. The node prices are estimates: "actual prices will vary with market conditions". The approved vendors are Dell and Supermicro. The page gives the join route: an email to TSCC User Support with the research, the nodes and the number of users. SDSC then sends a quote.

## Limits

- The page offers no H100, H200 or other data center GPU node.
- It gives no count of condo owners, nodes or users.
- At the end of the warranty, owners take back or surplus their nodes. The page does not say whether SDSC replaces them.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the text says that researchers "pay a monthly operations fee for each of their purchased nodes". The fee table on the same page gives the operations fee in "Node/year" ([SDSC TSCC condo details (web)](SDSC%20TSCC%20condo%20details%20%28web%29.md); [SDSC service rates (web)](SDSC%20service%20rates%20%28web%29.md) also gives "Node/year").
