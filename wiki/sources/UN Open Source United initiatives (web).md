# UN Open Source United initiatives (web)

## Citation

United Nations, Open Source United. "Initiatives." Open Source United Portal. No date on the page. https://opensource.un.org/en/initiatives. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- This page of the United Nations (UN) Open Source United Portal lists seven UN open source initiatives.
- They include the Open Source Enablement Compass (OSEC) and the Open Source Ecosystem Enabler (OSEE).
- They also include a GenAI for Good Challenge and an Open Source Programme Office (OSPO) for Trinidad and Tobago.
- OSEE runs from September 2023 to February 2027 with EUR 3 million from the European Commission.

## Background

The portal is a UN website about open source work in the UN system. Each initiative on this page has a short description and a link to its own page. The other initiatives are a staff course (Mind the Open Source Gap), the OSS4SDG hackathons and the Reboot the Earth hackathon.

## Key claims

- OSEC: "OSEC aims to provide a yearly, evidence-based assessment that helps countries benchmark progress, identify good practices, and strengthen their capacity to use and contribute to OSS as a strategic asset—not to rank them, but to enable self-improvement and shared learning". (Open Source Enablement Compass)
- OSEE lead: OSEE "is a flagship initiative led by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU) Bureau for Development in partnership with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), funded by the European Commission under the Global Gateway programme." (Open Source Ecosystem Enabler)
- OSEE term and budget: "Running from September 2023 to February 2027 with EUR 3 million in funding, OSEE supports countries in the Global South to establish Open Source Program Offices (OSPOs) and strengthen national digital capacity." (Open Source Ecosystem Enabler)
- OSEE countries: "Pilot countries: Kenya and Trinidad and Tobago." (Open Source Ecosystem Enabler)
- GenAI for Good: "Since its launch, 318 teams from 79 countries have worked on real-world prototypes in health, agriculture, and climate resilience". "All code is released under open source licenses". (GenAI for Good Challenge)
- Trinidad and Tobago OSPO: "The initiative promotes digital sovereignty, reduces dependency on proprietary vendors, improves interoperability across government systems, and builds local technical capacity." (Trinidad and Tobago Open Source Programme Office)
- OSPO tasks: "Establish a functioning OSPO with clear governance and mandate" and "Build technical capacity across Ministries, Departments and Agencies". (Trinidad and Tobago Open Source Programme Office, Objectives)

## Evidence and method

This is a UN portal page that lists initiatives. It gives aims, partners, some dates and counts. It gives no results or evaluations.

## Limits

- The page does not discuss compute infrastructure.
- It gives a budget only for OSEE.
- The OSEC section shows the logos of RISE (Research Institutes of Sweden) and ODET as the initiators, without text.
