# QuantZero homepage (web)

## Citation

QuantZero. Homepage, "AI-Driven Zero Trust Security for Enterprise & Federal Teams." Vendor web page, © 2026, date not stated. https://quantzero.io. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- QuantZero sells an AI-driven zero trust security platform for enterprise and federal teams. It says it deploys "in under 90 days" with no hardware.
- The page lists the frameworks it supports, among them NIST SP 800-207, CMMC, FedRAMP and CCPA. It is a marketing page.

## Background

QuantZero is a small company. The page says it is a veteran-owned small business, an AWS and Okta technology partner, and active in SAM, the federal System for Award Management. The page asks visitors to book a discovery call.

## Key claims

- "Continuous identity verification. Automated compliance enforcement. Deployed in under 90 days — no hardware required." (heading)
- "Every trust decision is powered by machine learning — not static rule sets." (Al-Native Risk Scoring)
- "AI-driven workflow automation reduces mean time to detect (MTTD) and respond (MTTR) across your environment. Human-in-the-loop controls for high-stakes decisions." (Incident Response Automation)
- "Every metric is drawn from a live QuantZero deployment — not projected, not estimated." (Core capabilities)

## Evidence and method

A vendor homepage. It makes product claims and shows no metrics, customers or tests, though it says its metrics come from a live deployment.

## Limits

The page gives no prices, no customers and no independent test of its claims. It does not say what the company's legal name or location is. Not for a public report.
