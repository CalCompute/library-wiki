# Microsoft Product Terms For Online Services (web)

## Citation

Microsoft. "For Online Services." Product Terms, Microsoft Licensing, date not stated. https://www.microsoft.com/licensing/terms/product/ForOnlineServices/all. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- This section of the Microsoft Product Terms applies to all Microsoft Online Services that a customer gets under a licensing agreement. It includes an Acceptable Use Policy of 12 items.
- Microsoft AI Services must follow the Acceptable Use Policy and the Microsoft Enterprise AI Services Code of Conduct. Microsoft can run automated classifiers to detect violations.
- Microsoft Generative AI Services do not use Customer Data to train a foundation model, except on the customer's documented instructions. Output Content is Customer Data.

## Background

Microsoft publishes the Product Terms on its licensing website. This page is the "For Online Services" section. It works with the customer's licensing agreement, the Glossary and the Data Protection Addendum (DPA). Terms do not change during a subscription. The Code of Conduct is in the library as [Microsoft Enterprise AI Services Code of Conduct 2026-05-01](Microsoft%20Enterprise%20AI%20Services%20Code%20of%20Conduct%202026-05-01.md).

## Key claims

- Stable terms: "When Customer renews or purchases a new subscription to an Online Service, the then-current terms will apply and will not change during Customer’s subscription for that Online Service." (Applicable Product Terms and Updates)
- Licences: "Customer must acquire and assign the appropriate subscription licenses required for its use of each Online Service." (Licensing the Online Services)
- Acceptable use: Customer may not use an Online Service "to mine cryptocurrency", "to reverse engineer any Online Service or exfiltrate the weights of any AI models" or "to attempt to circumvent the limitations Microsoft sets on the use of any APIs related to the Online Service". (Acceptable Use Policy)
- Suspension: "If Microsoft suspends the Online Service, Microsoft will suspend only to the extent reasonably necessary." (Acceptable Use Policy)
- AI services: "Customer must use Microsoft AI Services in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy and the Microsoft Enterprise AI Services Code of Conduct" (Responsible Use of Microsoft AI Services)
- Detection: "Microsoft may run automated classifiers and other automated evaluation systems to detect violations of the Acceptable Use Policy and Code of Conduct." (Responsible Use of Microsoft AI Services)
- High-Risk Use: "Customer’s High-Risk Use of the Online Services is at its own risk." (High-Risk Use)
- Training by Microsoft: "Microsoft Generative AI Services will not use Customer Data to train any generative AI foundation model, except pursuant to Customer’s documented instructions." (Use of Content for Training)
- Training by Customer: Customer will not use the services "to generate Output Content for the express purpose of creating synthetic training data to develop or train AI models or systems that have substantially similar functionality to a Microsoft AI service" (Use of Content for Training)
- Output: "Output Content is Customer Data. Microsoft does not own Customer's Output Content." (Output Content)
- Regulatory exit: "If Microsoft terminates an Online Service for regulatory reasons, Customers will receive a credit for any amount paid in advance for the period after termination." (Changes to and Availability of the Online Services)
