# USC Viterbi leadership (web)

## Citation

University of Southern California (USC) Viterbi School of Engineering. "Leadership." Web page, published December 16, 2016, last updated November 16, 2026. https://viterbischool.usc.edu/leadership/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The page lists the leaders of the USC Viterbi School of Engineering: the dean, vice deans, senior staff and department chairs. It also lists the members of five boards.
- Gaurav Sukhatme is Dean (Interim) of USC Viterbi and Director of the USC Stevens School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence.

## Background

USC Viterbi is the engineering school of USC, in Los Angeles. This page names its leadership team with a title and a link to a biography for each person. Three boards are the Board of Councilors, the Corporate Advisory Board and the Emerging Leaders Board. Two regional boards advise on East Asia & China and on India. The Corporate Advisory Board lists each member with a company.

## Key claims

- "Gaurav Sukhatme Dean (Interim), USC Viterbi School of Engineering Director, USC Stevens School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence" (Leadership)
- "Mahta Moghaddam Vice Dean for Research and Ming Hsieh Chair in Electrical and Computer Engineering-Electrophysics" (Leadership)
- "Steve Crago Vice Dean of Engineering and Michael Keston Executive Director (Interim), USC Information Sciences Institute" (Leadership)
- "Antonio Ortega Vice Dean for Faculty Affairs and Dean's Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering" (Leadership)
- "Rob Groome Chief Technology Officer" (Leadership)
- "The mission of the board is threefold: advise the dean on matters related to the school in the region; serve as advocate and ambassador for the school in the region; and support USC Viterbi in its development efforts among its constituencies." (The East Asia & China Advisory Board)

## Evidence and method

A dated institutional directory page. It gives names and titles, not biographies. The Corporate Advisory Board has 18 members from companies such as Boeing, Cisco, Google, Microsoft, Northrop Grumman and Qualcomm. The library read the whole page.

## Limits

The page gives no budgets, no computing resources and no reporting lines. It does not describe the work of the Board of Councilors or the Corporate Advisory Board. The library has read only two of its linked biographies: [Groome USC Viterbi CTO profile (web)](Groome%20USC%20Viterbi%20CTO%20profile%20%28web%29.md) and [Moghaddam USC Viterbi faculty profile (web)](Moghaddam%20USC%20Viterbi%20faculty%20profile%20%28web%29.md).
