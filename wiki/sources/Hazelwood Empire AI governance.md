# Hazelwood Empire AI governance

## Citation

Empire AI. "Our Governance." Website page, publication date unknown (copyright 2026). https://www.empireai.edu/governance/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Empire AI's board lists 17 names. Kim Hazelwood is on it as "Tech Executive". Tom Secunda, a Bloomberg co-founder, is Chair.
- Two seats are observers: the Simons Foundation and NYSTAR, the New York state science and technology office.
- The board mixes member universities, a funder, the state and independent experts.

## Background

Empire AI is New York's state-backed AI computing consortium. This page lists its board and staff.

## Key claims

- The board is "composed of nationally recognized leaders in academic research, technology and public policy". (Our Governance)
- The board and team are "expanding access to computing power typically available only to large corporations". (Our Governance)
- Most seats are member universities: CUNY, Rochester, Buffalo, NYU, RPI, Cornell, Icahn, SUNY and Columbia. (Board of Directors)
- The other seats are NY Creates, Liz Fine Associates, B Capital, Kim Hazelwood ("Tech Executive") and the Chair. Two are observers. (Board of Directors)
- Staff: 13 people, including a Director of Research Computing, six system administrators and a hardware technician. (Empire AI Team)

## Evidence and method

The organization's own page.

## Limits

- The page gives no terms, no voting rules and no selection process. The 2025 board release gives the seat totals.
