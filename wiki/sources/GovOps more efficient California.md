## Bottom line

- State agencies can buy Anthropic's Claude "at a 50% discounted price per seat", with free workforce training. It is "the first AI productivity tool" on CDT's new Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal.
- The state's new hiring guide "is projected to save over 500,000 hours of staff time". CalHR's Pooled Hiring Pilot lets one application reach many departments.
- The Office of Data and Innovation (ODI) is "California's in-house problem-solving team" of designers, data scientists and developers.

## Background

See [Government Operations Agency](../entities/Government%20Operations%20Agency.md) and [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md).

## Key claims

- Hiring: "The Department of Human Resources' (CalHR) Pooled Hiring Pilot lets job seekers send one application to multiple departments." It "helped departments hire people faster than the statewide average."
- ODI staff "have worked shoulder-to-shoulder with over 30 state agencies to date".
- Anthropic: "state agencies may access Anthropic's AI productivity assistant, Claude, at a 50% discounted price per seat, coupled with free workforce training as well as expert GenAI technical assistance and workflow input from Anthropic developers."
- SITeS "centralizes AI tools in one place with transparent pricing around key business use cases".
- CalKIDS: over 40,000 community college students had not claimed scholarships worth "$20 million" in total.

## Evidence and method

The agency's own page.

## Limits

- Claims of savings are projections from the agency.
- No seat count, price or term for the Anthropic agreement.
