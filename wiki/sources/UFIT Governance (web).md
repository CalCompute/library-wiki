# UFIT Governance (web)

## Citation

University of Florida (UF), UF Information Technology (UFIT). "Governance." Web page, date not stated. https://it.ufl.edu/about-ufit/governance/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- UF's IT governance has four parts. They are the President's Cabinet, the Office of the Chief Information Officer (CIO), the OneIT Leadership Group and four topical IT advisory workgroups.
- The President's Cabinet approves UF-wide IT policies, standards, strategic projects and their budgets. The Research Computing Advisory Workgroup is one of the four workgroups.

## Background

This short page in the About UFIT section of the UFIT website describes the governance structure. It says the structure follows "the One-UF IT Governance Model", which has its own page in the site menu. [UF Research Computing Advisory Workgroup (web)](UF%20Research%20Computing%20Advisory%20Workgroup%20%28web%29.md) gives the mandate and members of the research computing workgroup.

## Key claims

- "UF’s Information Technology governance is a well-defined, transparent process. The governance assists by advising on policies, standards, and priorities in support of the university’s mission and business goals, as defined by the One-UF IT Governance Model." (UFIT Governance Structure)
- "The President’s cabinet approves UF-wide policies and standards, strategic UF-wide projects and associated budget, which requires a UF-wide financial commitment." (The President’s Cabinet)
- "The CIO guides the decision-making processes through the IT Governance." (Office of the CIO)
- "The OneIT Leadership Group consists of IT leaders from each academic college, vice presidential areas, and select administrative and academic units as determined by the CIO." (OneIT Leadership Group)
- The four workgroups are "University Information Technology Committee", "Information Security Advisory Workgroup", "Research Computing Advisory Workgroup" and "Shared Infrastructure Advisory Workgroup". (Four topical IT Advisory Workgroups)

## Evidence and method

An institutional description of a governance structure, in four short parts. It gives no names, budgets, dates or decisions.

## Limits

- The page does not say which group reports to which. It does not say how the advice of a workgroup gets to the President's Cabinet.
- It lists the "University Information Technology Committee" as one of the four "topical IT Advisory Workgroups", but gives it the name of a committee.
- It does not say who sits on the President's Cabinet or the OneIT Leadership Group. The library has not read the One-UF IT Governance Model page.
