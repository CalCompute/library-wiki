# OSC and OARnet Advisory Board (web)

## Citation

Ohio Technology Consortium (OH-TECH). "OSC & OARnet Advisory Board." Web page, date not stated. https://www.oh-tech.org/about/board-members. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The page lists the 12 members of a joint advisory board, with a short biography for each. The board serves the Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC) and the Ohio Academic Resources Network (OARnet).
- The members include the Chancellor of the Ohio Department of Higher Education (ODHE) and the executive directors of OSC and OARnet. Five are university or college IT leaders.
- The other members are a chemistry professor, a managing director of technology, and staff from Honda R&D Americas and Procter & Gamble.

## Background

OH-TECH calls itself "A Division of the Ohio Department of Higher Education". Its members are OARnet, OSC and OhioLINK. This page is in the About section of the OH-TECH website. [OSC governance (web)](OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md) says that this joint board "recommends broad policy guidelines for OSC governance" and links to this list.

## Key claims

- The page heading is "OSC & OARnet Advisory Board". (heading)

## Limits

- The page does not give the board's charter, its duties, who appoints the members, their terms or how often the board meets.
- It does not give the employer of David Brewster or the university of John Herbert. The biographies are cut short on this page.
- It does not say which members can vote, or whether the two executive directors and the Chancellor are ex officio members.
- [Ohio Revised Code 3333.04 (web)](Ohio%20Revised%20Code%203333.04%20%28web%29.md), division (T), lets the Chancellor appoint "consortia of college and university personnel" to advise OSC and OARnet. This page does not say whether the board is one of these consortia.
