## Bottom line

- NSF award 2320345 funds DeltaAI at the National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA), University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign. It is a cooperative agreement from Sept. 1, 2023 to Sept. 30, 2029.
- The record gives an estimated total of $10,000,000 and obligations of $31,399,996 in fiscal years (FY) 2023 to 2025. DeltaAI has "more than 300 next-generation NVIDIA graphics processors".

## Background

This is the NSF awards API record for the DeltaAI award, in JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) format. The Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (OAC) manages it. The PI is William D Gropp. The title starts "Category I", the capacity category of the Advanced Computing Systems and Services program ([NSF 23-518 Advanced Computing Systems and Services solicitation (web)](NSF%2023-518%20Advanced%20Computing%20Systems%20and%20Services%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md)). The NCSA release on the award is [NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)](NCSA%20DeltaAI%20award%20release%202023-07-05%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Much of today's AI research relies on access to large volumes of data and advanced computational power, which are often unavailable to researchers not located at well-resourced technology companies and universities." (abstract)
- "The National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) at the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign will deploy and operate DeltaAI, an advanced computing and data resource that will be a companion system to NCSA’s Delta." (abstract)
- "The compute elements of DeltaAI will provide more than 300 next-generation NVIDIA graphics processors delivering over 600 petaflops of half-precision floating point computing" (abstract)
- Instrument: "Cooperative Agreement". Program: "EQUIPMENT ACQUISITIONS". Fund programs: "CISE Research Resources, CYBERINFRASTRUCTURE, NAIRR-Nat AI Research Resource, Innovative HPC". (record)
- Estimated total: "10000000". Obligated: "FY 2023 = $24,499,998.00", "FY 2024 = $4,899,998.00", "FY 2025 = $1,999,999.00"; total "31399996". (record)
- Start "09/01/2023", end "09/30/2029". (record)

## Evidence and method

A federal award record from the NSF awards API. It gives the recipient, the principal investigator (PI) and co-PIs, the program officer, the dates, the amounts and an abstract. It is a data record, not a report on results.

## Limits

- The record does not explain why the obligated total, $31,399,996, is larger than the estimated total, $10,000,000. It does not split the obligations between hardware and operations.
- It gives no allocation rules. In the NCSA release, NSF manages 90 percent of the computing through the national ACCESS program (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services & Support) ([NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)](NCSA%20DeltaAI%20award%20release%202023-07-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives no usage data.
