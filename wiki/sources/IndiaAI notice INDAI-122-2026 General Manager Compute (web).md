# IndiaAI notice INDAI-122-2026 General Manager Compute (web)

## Citation

India AI Division, Digital India Corporation (DIC), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. "Web Advertisement," Advt. No. INDAI/122/2026-INDAI: General Manager, Compute. September 8, 2026 (08.09.2026). PDF. https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/09/3a23d212be26782820a4903063b885d2.pdf. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The India AI Division of DIC advertises one contract post: General Manager, Compute. Applications closed on September 23, 2026.
- The post runs the IndiaAI Compute Capacity pillar. It finalises the public-private partnership (PPP) model for AI compute and drafts the request for proposals (RFP).
- It also manages the bid process, the contracts and the service level agreements (SLAs) with providers.

## Background

MeitY set up IndiaAI as an Independent Business Division under DIC to carry out the IndiaAI Mission ([PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)](PIB%20Cabinet%20approves%20IndiaAI%20Mission%202024-03-07%20%28web%29.md)). This notice is a recruitment advertisement on the MeitY website. It describes the Mission, DIC and the duties of the compute lead. DIC also advertises other posts in the same format ([Digital India Corporation notice DIC-10-2026-22 (web)](Digital%20India%20Corporation%20notice%20DIC-10-2026-22%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- Setup: "Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) has established India AI Independent Business Division (India AI) under Digital India Corporation for implementing the India AI mission." (page 1)
- Pillar: the post will "Drive the implementation of IndiaAI Compute Capacity pillar of the Mission." (Roles and Responsibilities)
- Sovereign cloud: the post will lead the design of an AI ecosystem "based on GPU computing, aiming to build a highly scalable AI compute cloud infrastructure on the Indian Sovereign Cloud." (Roles and Responsibilities)
- PPP model: "Finalize the PPP model for provisioning of the AI Compute Infrastructure." (Roles and Responsibilities)
- RFP: "Drafting the RFP for implementation of the project by defining the Scope of the Private Partner for building the AI Compute ecosystem as also provisioning of AI Compute to stakeholders from existing service providers." (Roles and Responsibilities)
- Contracts: "Bid process management, Contract Management and enforcement of SLAs." (Roles and Responsibilities)
- Experience: "Total of 15+ years of experience of which at least 10 years should be working with AI technologies, GPU computing, and large-scale parallel computing systems". (Desirable Skills & Experience)
- Procurement skill: "Knowledge of Government procurement systems and competency in RFP drafting and Bid process management." (Desirable Skills & Experience)
- Other DIC divisions: "Digital India Corporation has several Independent Business Divisions under it, and these include NeGD, MyGov, Bhashini, ISM, IndiaAI, and MSH." (About DIC)

## Evidence and method

This is a government recruitment notice. Its job description shows the work of the compute lead: project plans, PPP design, RFP drafting, bids, contracts and risk management. The lead also works with industry and research partners on GPU benchmarks. It gives the education and experience that the post needs. The notice gives no pay.

## Limits

- The notice describes the post, not the results of the compute pillar. For GPU counts and prices, see [PIB common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)](PIB%20common%20compute%20facility%20release%202025-01-30%20%28web%29.md) and [IndiaAI compute capacity (web)](IndiaAI%20compute%20capacity%20%28web%29.md).
- Its duties read like the start of the pillar ("Finalize the PPP model"). A 2025 PIB release reports a Request for Empanelment of cloud providers on August 16, 2024 ([PIB common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)](PIB%20common%20compute%20facility%20release%202025-01-30%20%28web%29.md)). The notice does not explain this.
- IndiaAI can end the appointment with one month's notice, or at once with one month's pay. The notice gives no term for the contract.
