# Governor appointments 2025-02-13 (web)

## Citation

Office of the Governor of California. "Governor Newsom announces appointments 2.13.25." Press release, Sacramento, February 13, 2025. https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/02/13/governor-newsom-announces-appointments-2-13-25/. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Governor appointments 2025-02-13.html`.

## Bottom line

- The Governor appoints Vera Zakem as State Chief Technology Innovation Officer at the California Department of Technology (CDT). The post does not need Senate confirmation and pays $192,000.
- The release also lists seven appointments to state boards and commissions that are not about technology.

## Background

The Office of the Governor publishes a release for each group of appointments. Each entry gives the person's city, the post, a career history, the education, whether the Senate must confirm and the pay.

## Key claims

- "Vera Zakem, of Walnut Creek, has been appointed State Chief Technology Innovation Officer at the California Department of Technology." (Zakem entry)
- "Zakem was Chief Digital Democracy and Rights Officer at the Agency for International Development from 2022 to 2025." (Zakem entry)
- "Zakem earned a Master of Arts degree in Government from Johns Hopkins University and a Bachelor of Arts degree in Politics from the University of San Francisco." (Zakem entry)
- "This position does not require Senate confirmation, and the compensation is $192,000." (Zakem entry)

## Evidence and method

A press release with eight appointments. The other seven appointments are to these bodies:

- California Building Standards Commission (two).
- Board of Pilot Commissioners for the Bays of San Francisco, San Pablo, and Suisun (three).
- 40th District Agricultural Association Yolo County Fair Board (one).
- California Arts Council (one).

## Limits

The release does not describe the duties of the Chief Technology Innovation Officer. The entry for William Benedict ends "Tynan is a Democrat.", which names the next appointee, so the party of Benedict is not stated correctly.
