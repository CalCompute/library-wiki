# SEIU Local 1000 statement on new AI executive order (web)

## Citation

SEIU Local 1000. "Statement on New AI Executive Order." Statement, May 22, 2026. https://www.seiu1000.org/statement-on-new-ai-executive-order/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The state workers' union SEIU Local 1000 answers Executive Order N-6-26 of May 21, 2026. The union says the order requires agencies to prepare support, such as better severance, for workers laid off due to AI.
- Its President, Anica Walls, says "state workers need a real seat at the table" as the State brings in AI.

## Background

SEIU Local 1000 is a union of California state employees. This short statement gives the union's response to the Governor's executive order on AI and the workforce. The order itself is [Executive Order N-6-26](Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md).

## Key claims

- "On May 21, 2026, Governor Gavin Newsom signed an Executive Order aimed at protecting workers from being displaced by AI in the workplace." (opening)
- "This Executive Order requires agencies to prepare support like improved severance packages for workers in the event that they are laid off due to AI implementation." (opening)
- "State workers are already raising concerns about AI tools that create more work, produce inaccuracies and still require significant human oversight." (Anica Walls, SEIU Local 1000 President)
- "If the State is serious about protecting workers, state workers need a real seat at the table as these technologies are introduced into our workplaces and public services." (Anica Walls, SEIU Local 1000 President)

## Evidence and method

A union statement with a quotation from its President. It summarises the order in one sentence and gives no other evidence.

## Limits

The statement is the union's view. Its summary of the order is short; read the order for its directives. It does not name the AI tools it refers to.
