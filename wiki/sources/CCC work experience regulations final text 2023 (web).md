# CCC work experience regulations final text 2023 (web)

## Citation

Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges. "Final Revisions to Title 5, California Code of Regulations, Related to Work Experience Education." PDF, July 26, 2023 (accessible version July 28, 2023), published by the California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office, Office of General Counsel. https://www.cccco.edu/-/media/CCCCO-Website/Office-of-General-Counsel/bgcccfinalworkexperiencetext20230726ffa11y.pdf. Read 2026-11-09. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-09 CCC work experience regulations final text 2023.pdf`.

## Bottom line

- The document is the final text of the 2023 revisions to the Title 5 rules on work experience education in the California Community Colleges.
- It replaces the old "Cooperative Work Experience Education" rules. Districts must adopt policies, find public or private employers, and sign an agreement with each employer. Districts may subsidize work experience with public employers.

## Background

Title 5 of the California Code of Regulations holds the rules for the community colleges. The Board of Governors adopts them. The document marks each section as amended, repealed or adopted. It covers sections 55002.5, 55040, 55250 to 55257 and 58003.1.

## Key claims

- "The purpose of work experience education is to provide students with an integrated instructional program that provides opportunities to connect academic curricula to applied experiential learning in the workplace." (§ 55250(a), new)
- "Work experience education may include paid or unpaid employment, full or part-time employment, and may be structured as separate credit or noncredit classes, or integrated as a component of a course." (§ 55250(b), new)
- "Work experience education should provide economically disadvantaged students with opportunities to earn a wage while completing program requirements and earning academic credit." (§ 55250(b), new)
- "Districts shall identify appropriate public or private employers to partner with in providing work experience education opportunities to students." (§ 55251(b), new)
- "Districts may subsidize student work experience education provided by public employers, or by private employers for the employment of students with disabilities." (§ 55251(c), new)
- "work experience education offered as a credit course: one semester unit of credit will be awarded for every 54 hours of work experience, or one quarter unit for every 33 hours of work experience" (§ 55253(a)(1))

## Evidence and method

A regulation text. It shows the old and new text of each section. It gives no data on students or employers.

## Limits

The original PDF marks deleted text with strikethrough and new text with underline. The Markdown copy loses those marks, so old and new text run together; read the original before a quote from §§ 55252 to 55257. The rules do not name AI, computing or state agencies as employers.
