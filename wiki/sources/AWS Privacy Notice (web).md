# AWS Privacy Notice (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS Privacy Notice." Last updated Nov. 17, 2026. aws.amazon.com. https://aws.amazon.com/privacy/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The notice covers the personal information that AWS collects about its customers and visitors. It does not cover the "content" that customers process, store or host on AWS. The customer agreement governs that content.
- AWS says it does not sell customers' personal information. It shares it with Amazon affiliates, service providers, sellers in third-party transactions, and when the law calls for it.

## Background

AWS is the cloud service of Amazon.com, Inc. This notice sits beside the [AWS Customer Agreement (web)](AWS%20Customer%20Agreement%20%28web%29.md) and the [AWS Acceptable Use Policy 2021-07-01](AWS%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%202021-07-01.md). The page links a prior version dated May 18, 2026. See [Amazon Web Services](../entities/Amazon%20Web%20Services.md).

## Key claims

- "This Privacy Notice does not apply to the “content” processed, stored, or hosted by our customers using AWS Offerings in connection with an AWS account." (Introduction)
- "Information about our customers is an important part of our business and we are not in the business of selling our customers’ personal information to others." (How We Share Personal Information)
- "Depending on the scope of your interactions with AWS Offerings, your personal information may be stored in or accessed from multiple countries, including the United States." (Location of Personal Information)
- "We release account and other personal information when we believe release is appropriate to comply with the law, enforce or apply our terms and other agreements, or protect the rights, property, or security of AWS, our customers, or others." (How We Share Personal Information)
- "To help you receive more useful and relevant ads on other sites and services and to measure their effectiveness, AWS shares limited personal information with our advertising partners." (Advertising)
- "We don’t provide AWS Offerings for purchase by children. If you’re under 18, you may use AWS Offerings only with the involvement of a parent or guardian." (Children’s Personal Information)

## Evidence and method

A company privacy notice of about 9,000 words with the site's navigation text. It states the company's practices, legal bases for some countries, and the rights of users. It cites the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the California Privacy Rights Act by name.

## Limits

The notice gives no fixed retention periods. It refers questions about customer content to the customer agreement and the AWS Data Privacy FAQ, which the library has not read. This page read the general sections and the start of the sections for certain jurisdictions.
