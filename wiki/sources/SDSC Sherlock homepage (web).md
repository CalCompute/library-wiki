# SDSC Sherlock homepage (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), Sherlock Cloud Services. "Take your research farther, faster." Homepage, date not stated. https://sherlock.sdsc.edu/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- Sherlock calls itself "a specialized IT services provider born out of academia" for regulated data. It is part of SDSC at UC San Diego.
- The page claims "$1.5M saved on building similar solutions in house" and 24 weeks saved to start projects. It offers "non-profit pricing" and names customers that include the UC Office of the President and City of Hope.

## Background

The homepage gives the mission, two headline figures, five customer quotes and the three services: Cloud Services, Government Services and Data Management. [SDSC Sherlock compliance (web)](SDSC%20Sherlock%20compliance%20%28web%29.md) compares the three services. [SDSC Sherlock Cloud services (web)](SDSC%20Sherlock%20Cloud%20services%20%28web%29.md) describes the first one.

## Key claims

- "Sherlock is a specialized IT services provider born out of academia. We empower you to respond rapidly to your regulated-data management efforts, and free you to pursue breakthrough discoveries and impact society." (Cloud Solutions & Services)
- "$1.5M saved on building similar solutions in house" and "24wks time saved to start and operationalize projects". (Our Mission)
- "Easily scale and augment your existing research-enabling technology services portfolio without giving up control." (Unburden Your IT Team)
- A Director of Risk IT Services at the UC Office of the President: "The Sherlock team was able to seamlessly take our data and application infrastructure from an open environment to their secure environment in six months." (Our Impact)
- A UC San Diego professor: "The project collected data from roughly 50,000 people and generated personalized and population-based models for continuous risk assessment" with data kept "to both HIPAA and CUI requirements". (Our Impact)
- "Get up and running quickly with Sherlock non-profit pricing." (Value)
- "Flexible agreements to meet your organizations needs, including grant proposal support" (Value)

## Evidence and method

A marketing homepage of about 860 words. The two figures have no method or base. The customer quotes are the provider's selection.

## Limits

The page does not say how it computed the $1.5 million or the 24 weeks. It gives no prices. The "non-profit pricing" page was not downloaded.
