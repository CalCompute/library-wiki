# CCST Science Fellows program (web)

## Citation

California Council on Science and Technology (CCST). "CCST Science & Technology Policy Fellowship." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://ccst.us/ccst-science-fellows-program/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Each year CCST places a cohort of PhD scientists and engineers in offices of the Legislature and the executive branch in Sacramento.
- Applicants need a PhD or equivalent and the right to work in the United States. Fellows get a salary and benefits.

## Background

CCST is a nonpartisan nonprofit that advises California policymakers on science and technology. This page gives the eligibility rules, the timeline and the purpose of the fellowship. It also lists other state and national science policy fellowships.

## Key claims

- "the CCST Science & Technology Policy Fellows program annually recruits, trains, and places a cohort of PhD scientists and engineers directly into policymaker offices in the California State Legislature and Executive Branch in Sacramento." (Sending Scientists to Sacramento)
- "PhD or equivalent in science, social science, or engineering field needed by Sept. 15 of application year" (Eligibility)
- "Applicants must be a U.S. citizen or permanent resident eligible to work in the United States, or have an applicable US work visa." (Eligibility)
- "The application period is closed and opens December 1st." (Timeline)
- "Much of this work, along with the State Fellowship Planning Grants, was funded by the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation as well as the Simons Foundation." (Science & Policy Beyond California)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It lists the policy areas where fellows worked on bills, for example energy, human services, education and health care. It names "California’s role in AI governance" as one of the issues that policymakers face.
