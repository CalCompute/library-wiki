# SEIU Local 1000 Master Table Update 2026-06-11 (web)

## Citation

SEIU Local 1000. "Master Table Update: June 11, 2026." Bargaining update, posted June 18, 2026. https://www.seiu1000.org/master-table-update-june-11-2026/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- SEIU Local 1000 represents "nearly 100,000 state workers". In its 2026 contract bargaining with the State, it proposed protections for workers "from the misuse of artificial intelligence (AI) and new technology".
- The union asks for training, transparency, privacy protections and a voice in how the State brings in new technology. The State gave a counter proposal.

## Background

SEIU Local 1000 is a union of California state employees. This is one of its updates on Master Table bargaining for its next contract with the State. The update covers pay, telework, AI, wellness and mandatory overtime. It is the union's account of the talks.

## Key claims

- "Since our last update, our bargaining team continued advancing proposals focused on improving working conditions and building a stronger contract for the nearly 100,000 state workers represented by SEIU Local 1000." (June 11, 2026)
- "One proposal focuses on protecting workers from the misuse of artificial intelligence (AI) and new technology." (Protecting Workers)
- "As the State increasingly uses automation and AI, our union is fighting to ensure new technology is used to support workers, not replace them. Workers deserve training, transparency, privacy protections and a real voice in how new technology is introduced in the workplace." (Protecting Workers)
- "The State provided a counter proposal on Tuesday and our bargaining team is preparing a response." (Protecting Workers)
- "That's why we are continuing to fight for fair pay, including our proposal for a 20% General Salary Increase (GSI) over the next three years: 7% in 2026, 7% in 2027 and 6% in 2028." (June 11, 2026)

## Evidence and method

A union bargaining update. It reports the union's proposals and says the State answered. It gives no text of either proposal.

## Limits

The update gives only the union's side. It does not give the text of the AI proposal or of the State's counter proposal, or the outcome of the talks.
