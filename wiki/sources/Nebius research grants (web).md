# Nebius research grants (web)

## Citation

Nebius. "Nebius research grants." Program web page, date not stated. https://nebius.com/nebius-research-grants. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Nebius gives researchers free GPU cloud credits, AI model tokens and Tavily search credits.
- Students, postdoctoral researchers and faculty at accredited academic institutions, and researchers at nonprofit research organizations, can apply.
- Applications are open all year. Nebius reviews each month's proposals in the next month. It sets the amount for each proposal.

## Background

Nebius publishes this page for its research grants program. It gives what an applicant gets, who can apply, the criteria and the review cycle. The application form needs JavaScript.

## Key claims

- Offer: "Nebius research grants program provides researchers with free access to GPU cloud, AI models, and Tavily’s agentic search engine, along with our expertise to accelerate discovery and deployment." (introduction)
- Amount: "The exact amount is determined individually based on each proposal." (What you can get)
- Applicants: "Faculty members from accredited academic institutions leading computational or data-intensive projects" and "Researchers from accredited academic institutions or non-profit research organizations" (Who can apply)
- Criteria: "Innovative use of GPUs for AI, machine learning, data analytics or HPC" and "Co-marketing: being open to publicly acknowledging Nebius support and joining communications" (Project qualification criteria)
- Cycle: "Applications are open year-round." and "If you submit your proposal in a given month, we will review it during the next calendar month." (How to apply)
- No reply: "If we haven’t contacted you by the end of the next calendar month, your proposal was not selected." (Decision)
- One award: "No, you are only eligible to apply one time for the Research Credits Program." (FAQ)

## Evidence and method

The program's own web page. It states the offer and the rules. It lists five research papers that Nebius supported.

## Limits

- The page does not state a range for the amount of credits.
- The page does not state the countries where applicants can be.
- The page states: "The offer is valid for a limited time only." It gives no end date.
