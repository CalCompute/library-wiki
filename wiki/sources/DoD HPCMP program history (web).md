# DoD HPCMP program history (web)

## Citation

High Performance Computing Modernization Program (HPCMP), Department of War (formerly the Department of Defense). "History." Web page, date not stated. https://www.hpc.mil/who-we-are/program-history. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Congress told the Director, Defense Research and Engineering (DDR&E) in 1990 to modernize the Department of Defense's research computing. The HPCMP started in fiscal year (FY) 1992.
- The program moved in 2011 from the Office of the Secretary of Defense to the Assistant Secretary of the Army. It works in three areas: centers, software development and a network.

## Background

This page gives the history of the HPCMP. The HPCMP Office (HPCMPO) runs the program from the Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC) of the US Army Corps of Engineers in Vicksburg, Mississippi. The page notes that Executive Order 14347 restored the name "Department of War (DoW)".

## Key claims

- "In 1990, Congress mandated the Director, Defense Research and Engineering (DDR&E) to modernize the computational capabilities available to the Department of Defense (DoD) researchers in the Science and Technology (S&T) communities." (first paragraph)
- "The resulting High Performance Computing Modernization Plan was approved, and the High Performance Computing Modernization Program (HPCMP) was established in fiscal Year (FY) 1992." (first paragraph)
- In 1994 DDR&E "established the High Performance Computing Modernization Office (HPCMO), (now called the High Performance Computing Modernization Program Office (HPCMPO))." (first paragraph)
- The program started "under the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD), and transitioning in 2011 to the Assistant Secretary of the Army, based at the Engineer Research and Development Center (ERDC) of the US Army Corps of Engineers (USACE) in Vicksburg, Mississippi." (second paragraph)
- The program has "three primary areas of concentration: centers where equipment is operated and maintained, software development to take advantage of the computing systems, and a network to link the users with the resources." (third paragraph)
- "The HPCMPO oversees the Program, including procurement, management of the resources, and establishing Program-wide processes." (third paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A short history on a federal program website. It gives dates, the offices that ran the program and its three areas of work. It cites no documents other than Executive Order 14347.

## Limits

The page gives no budget, no system counts and no user counts. It does not name the 1990 law. It does not explain why the program moved to the Army in 2011.
