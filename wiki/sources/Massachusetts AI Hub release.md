# Massachusetts AI Hub release

## Citation

Office of Governor Maura Healey and Lt. Governor Kim Driscoll, Commonwealth of Massachusetts. "Governor Healey Advances State's AI Leadership with Major Investments in Massachusetts AI Hub." Press release, May 6, 2025. https://www.mass.gov/news/governor-healey-advances-states-ai-leadership-with-major-investments-in-massachusetts-ai-hub. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-28 Massachusetts AI Hub release.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-28%20Massachusetts%20AI%20Hub%20release.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-10-28.

## Bottom line

- Massachusetts gave a $31 million state grant to the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC). The grant creates the Artificial Intelligence Compute Resources (AICR) environment.
- The state and MGHPCC's six member universities expect joint investment of "an estimated $120 million" over five years.
- AICR is open to universities, startups, businesses and residents.

## Background

Governor Maura Healey launched the Massachusetts AI Hub in December 2024. It uses money authorized in the Mass Leads Act. The Massachusetts Technology Collaborative (MassTech) supports the Hub.

## Key claims

- The grant: "a $31 million state grant to expand access to sustainable high-performance computing that is necessary for AI innovation". (para. 1)
- AICR: "Through the $31 million grant, the Healey-Driscoll Administration is partnering with Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC) to create the Artificial Intelligence Compute Resources (AICR) environment at MGHPCC". (para. 5)
- Users: "AICR will allow public and private higher education institutions, startups and businesses in the innovation ecosystem, and the residents of Massachusetts to access vital AI infrastructure." (para. 5)
- Partners: "MGHPCC's six member universities, Boston University, Harvard University, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, University of Massachusetts, and Yale University". (para. 6)
- Joint money: "Over the next five years, the partnership is expected involve joint investments from the Healey-Driscoll Administration and the MGHPCC universities that will reach an estimated $120 million investment with the potential to grow." (para. 6)
- Leadership: MassTech hired Sabrina Mansur "to serve as the inaugural executive director of the Massachusetts AI Hub". She started on May 5, 2025. (para. 11)
- Hub role: "The Massachusetts AI Hub is Massachusetts' central nexus for AI innovation and governance across industry, academia and the private sector." (About the Massachusetts AI Hub)
- Industry: the Hub "is pursuing a public-private partnership with IBM and Red Hat to establish an incubator for Massachusetts-based AI startups". (para. 8)
- Other state money: the AI Models Innovation Challenge, "more than $3 million", and a Sector Spark grant of "more than $1.9 million" to LabCentral and C10 Labs. (para. 13)
- Public sector use: Jason Snyder says AI that state agencies adopt must "meet strong AI procurement standards for safe and responsible AI". (para. 10)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It states the state's own commitments and quotes officials. It reports announced amounts, not money spent.

## Limits

- The release does not say how much of the $120 million the state pays and how much the universities pay.
- It gives no hardware, no capacity and no allocation rule for AICR.
- It does not say who owns the AICR hardware or who decides access.
- The $120 million is "an estimated" figure over five years.
