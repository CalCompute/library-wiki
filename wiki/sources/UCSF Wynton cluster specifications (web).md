# UCSF Wynton cluster specifications (web)

## Citation

UCSF Wynton HPC. "Cluster Specifications." Wynton HPC website, date not stated. https://wynton.ucsf.edu/hpc/about/specs.html. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 UCSF Wynton cluster specifications.md`.

## Bottom line

- The Wynton HPC cluster of UC San Francisco (UCSF) has 444 compute nodes and 15,932 physical cores. It has 235 graphics processing units (GPUs) on 61 GPU nodes.
- Of the GPUs, 88 on 22 nodes are communal. The other 147 on 39 nodes give priority to the groups that contributed them.
- It has 861 accounts and 783 projects. Its storage is not backed up, and it connects to the Pacific Research Platform at 100 Gbps.

## Background

The Wynton HPC team publishes this page. It gives an overview of the cluster in figures, a table of login, transfer, development and compute nodes, the file systems and the network. UCSF retires Wynton at the end of February 2027 ([UCSF Wynton HPC home (web)](UCSF%20Wynton%20HPC%20home%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Compute nodes: 444" and "Physical cores: 15932". (Overview)
- "235 GPUs on 61 GPU nodes (88/22 GPUs/nodes are communal and 147/39 GPUs/nodes are prioritized for GPU contributors)". (Overview)
- "Number of accounts: 861, where 0 are Wynton Protected accounts" and "Number of projects: 783". (Overview)
- "User home storage: 500 GiB/user (770 TiB in total)" and "Group storage: 6.5 PB". (Overview)
- "Importantly, note that the Wynton HPC storage is not backed up. Users and labs are responsible for backing up their own data outside of Wynton HPC." (User and Lab Storage)
- "The cluster itself connects to NSF's Pacific Research Platform at a speed of 100 Gbps" (Network)
- "The job scheduler is SGE 8.1.9 (Son of Grid Engine) which provides queues for both communal and lab-priority tasks." (Summary of Compute Environment)

## Evidence and method

A specifications page of about 1,400 words, with site navigation. It gives counts with no date. The account count links to a usage report that this library does not hold. It gives no costs and no GPU models; it links to a GPU page that this library does not hold.

## Limits

The page does not list the GPU models. It does not say how many accounts are active or how much of the cluster is in use. It does not give the age or cost of the hardware.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page gives home storage of "500 GiB/user" and 88 communal GPUs. The pricing page, which calls itself a mockup, gives home storage of "200 GiB" and says GPUs "are currently only available to groups who have contributed with their own GPU-equipped compute nodes" ([UCSF Wynton pricing (web)](UCSF%20Wynton%20pricing%20%28web%29.md)).
