# NIH Data Use Certification agreement (web)

## Citation

National Institutes of Health (NIH). "Data Use Certification Agreement." Version of December 20, 2023. NIH Scientific Data Sharing site, sharing.nih.gov. https://sharing.nih.gov/sites/default/files/flmngr/Universal_DUC.pdf. Read 2026-11-19. Rehosted: `raw/web/2026-11-19 NIH Data Use Certification agreement.pdf`.

## Bottom line

- The agreement sets the terms for controlled-access human genomic data in NIH repositories, such as dbGaP, under the NIH Genomic Data Sharing (GDS) Policy.
- Access is for one approved project for one year. A user who wants to use a cloud provider must ask for it in the Data Access Request (DAR) and name the provider.
- The PI must be eligible for independent research, not a student or postdoctoral fellow. Users must not identify participants, must not sell or pass on the data, and must report a data breach within 24 hours.

## Background

NIH keeps controlled-access genomic and phenotypic data in its designated repositories. A principal investigator (PI) and the PI's institution sign this agreement through a DAR, and a Data Access Committee (DAC) approves it. Each data set also has an Addendum with its own data use limits. The agreement points to the NIH Security Best Practices for Controlled-Access Data for security rules. See also [NIH STRIDES benefits (web)](NIH%20STRIDES%20benefits%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Investigators interested in using Cloud Computing for data storage and analysis must request permission to use Cloud Computing in the DAR and identify the Cloud Service Provider (CSP) or providers and/or Private Cloud System (PCS) that they propose to use." (Term 1)
- "Access to the requested dataset(s) is granted for a period of one (1) year, with the option to renew access or close-out a project at the end of that year." (Term 1)
- "The Requester certifies that the PI is in good standing (i.e., no known sanctions) with the institution, relevant funding agencies, and regulatory agencies and is eligible to conduct independent research (i.e., is not a postdoctoral fellow, student, or trainee)." (Term 2)
- "The PI agrees that information about themselves and the approved research use will be posted publicly on the dbGaP website." (Term 3)
- "Approved Users agree not to use the requested datasets, either alone or in concert with any other information, to identify or contact individual participants from whom data and/or samples were collected." (Term 4)
- "Data Derivatives of controlled-access datasets, in whole or in part, may not be sold to any individual at any point in time for any purpose." (Term 6)
- "The Requester and PI agree to notify the appropriate DAC(s) of any unauthorized data sharing, breaches of data security, or inadvertent data releases that may compromise data confidentiality within 24 hours of when the incident is identified." (Term 7)

## Evidence and method

A federal agreement form of about 5,000 words. It states the terms of access and cites the GDS Policy, 45 CFR 46 and section 301(d) of the Public Health Service Act. It gives no data about use.

## Limits

The library read Terms 1 to 9 in full and the rest in part. The agreement covers only genomic data under the GDS Policy. The security rules are in a separate NIH document that the library has not read. It does not give the time a DAC takes to review a request.
