# East Bay Broadband Consortium funding (web)

## Citation

East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC). "Broadband Funding Opportunities." Web page, date not stated. https://www.eastbaybroadband.org/funding. Read 2026-11-13.

## Bottom line

- EBBC helps public agencies, community organizations, housing partners and broadband providers in Alameda, Contra Costa and Solano counties find broadband grants and prepare applications.
- The page lists five programs of the California Advanced Services Fund (CASF), with their deadlines and eligible entities. It links each one to the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC).

## Background

This page is in the Resources section of the EBBC website. It has two parts. The first part says how EBBC helps applicants. The second part lists the current CASF programs.

## Key claims

- "EBBC helps public agencies, community organizations, housing partners, and broadband providers understand opportunities, build partnerships, and use regional data to advance competitive grant projects." (How EBBC can help)
- "We can help connect applicants with local partners, shape project concepts, coordinate letters of support, and identify relevant resources or technical assistance." (Partnership Application Readiness)
- "California’s CASF program supports broadband infrastructure, digital adoption, public housing connectivity, line extensions, and Tribal technical assistance." (Current CASF Funding Programs)
- Broadband Infrastructure Grant Account, deadline "November 1, 2026": "Funds broadband infrastructure projects that expand high-speed service to eligible unserved areas, including last-mile and qualifying middle-mile deployment." (Current CASF Funding Programs)
- Broadband Adoption Account, next batch "January 1, 2027": eligible entities are "Local governments, senior centers, schools, public libraries, nonprofit organizations, and community-based organizations." (Current CASF Funding Programs)
- "Funding details change. Applicants should confirm current eligibility, deadlines, and requirements directly with the California Public Utilities Commission before applying." (page end)

## Evidence and method

A list of state grant programs, written by a regional broadband consortium for applicants. Each program has an overview, a deadline or batch date and the eligible entities. It gives no funding amounts.

## Limits

The page is a summary, not the CASF rules. It tells applicants to check the rules with the CPUC. This library has no CPUC source on CASF. The page gives no grant amounts and no past awards.
