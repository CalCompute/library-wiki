# OpenAI for Nonprofits announcement (web)

## Citation

OpenAI. "Introducing OpenAI for Nonprofits." OpenAI announcement, May 30, 2024, with an update dated Feb. 6, 2026. https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-for-nonprofits/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- OpenAI gives nonprofits discounts on ChatGPT Business and ChatGPT Enterprise.
- The 2024 announcement gave 20 percent off ChatGPT Business and 50 percent off Enterprise. A Feb. 6, 2026 update says that nonprofits can get up to 75 percent off.
- A nonprofit applies through a validation page or contacts OpenAI sales.

## Background

OpenAI is an AI company. This post announced its program for nonprofits in 2024. It gives four examples of nonprofits that use ChatGPT. An update at the top changes the discount.

## Key claims

- Update: "Update on Feb 6, 2026: Nonprofits can now access up to a 75% discount on ChatGPT Business or ChatGPT Enterprise." (update note)
- 2024 offer: "nonprofit organizations can now access ChatGPT Business (formerly ChatGPT Team) at a discounted rate of 20% off monthly or annual plans." (body)
- Enterprise: "Larger nonprofits ready for large-scale deployment can contact our sales team to access a 50% discount on ChatGPT Enterprise." (body)
- Community: "we invite you to apply to join the nonprofit community we are building on the OpenAI Forum." (How nonprofits can use ChatGPT)
- Route: "Explore more details about OpenAI for Nonprofits or apply here to get started." The apply link goes to validate.poweredbypercent.com/openai. (closing)

## Evidence and method

A company announcement. It gives the offer and four examples from nonprofits, without figures on use.

## Limits

- The post does not state the eligibility rules. It links to a help article.
- The post gives two discount levels: the 2024 figures and the 2026 update. The update is the later statement.
- The post does not state how many nonprofits use the offer.
