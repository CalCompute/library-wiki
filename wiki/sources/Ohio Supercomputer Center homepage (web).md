# Ohio Supercomputer Center homepage (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Homepage." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC) is "A Division of the Ohio Department of Higher Education". It offers computing, storage, software and staff expertise to researchers, students and businesses.
- The page names three active clusters: Pitzer, Ascend and Cardinal.

## Background

OSC is a state supercomputer center in Columbus, Ohio. The homepage links to its services, news and client resources. The page footer says OSC is "A member of the Ohio Technology Consortium". The page is short. Most of its text is navigation, news and event lists.

## Key claims

- "A Division of the Ohio Department of Higher Education" (page header)
- "Accelerate your research achievements with easy access to the powerful, high performance computing, storage and software resources and staff expertise provided through OSC." (Researchers)
- "Enhance your computational science expertise with academic programs and workforce training opportunities." (Students)
- "Provide your company with a competitive advantage through modeling, simulation and analysis, which can reduce the time, labor and cost to bring new and improved products to market." (Businesses)
- "OSC systems staff will execute a rolling reboot across all active compute clusters (Pitzer, Ascend, and Cardinal) beginning Thursday, November 05." (notice)

## Evidence and method

An institutional homepage. It gives no figures. It shows the three groups OSC serves: researchers, students and businesses. Campus stories describe users at Miami University, Youngstown State University, Wright State University and Ohio University.
