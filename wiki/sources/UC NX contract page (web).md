# UC NX contract page (web)

## Citation

University of California, UCnet. "NX — Registered Nurses: Contract." Web page, no date. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/nx/contract.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-20 UC NX contract page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-20%20UC%20NX%20contract%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) lists its contract with the California Nurses Association (CNA) for the Registered Nurses (NX) unit, effective Nov. 22, 2025 to Jan. 2, 2030.
- The page says that updated individual articles are forthcoming and that Appendix A, the wage tables, is being updated. It links the complete contract for 2025 to 2030.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the NX unit's pages. The library holds the complete contract as [UC CNA NX nurse contract 2025-2030 (web)](UC%20CNA%20NX%20nurse%20contract%202025-2030%20%28web%29.md). The list of articles includes "Article 42 – New Technology".

## Key claims

- "Effective November 22, 2025 – January 2, 2030"
- "New contract ratified for contract period 11/22/2025 to 1/2/2030. Updated individual articles are forthcoming."
- "Appendix A – Wage Tables is being updated and will be added to the Complete Contract upon finalization."
- "Complete Contract for 2025-2030" (link)
- "Article 42 – New Technology" (list of articles)

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page gives no date of its last update.
- The individual articles have no links on the page; it says to use the table of contents of the complete contract.
