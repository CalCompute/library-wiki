# Stanford Marlowe access and rates (web)

## Citation

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (Stanford HAI). "Apply for access to Marlowe." Marlowe, Stanford AI Compute, date not stated (footer © 2026). https://marlowe-research.stanford.edu/access/. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Stanford Marlowe access and rates.md`.

## Bottom line

- Marlowe is open to all Stanford faculty principal investigators (PIs). A new PI gets 5,000 free GPU-hours for one year. At that tier, higher-priority work can preempt jobs.
- Medium and Large Projects run for one cycle of about 12 weeks with jobs that are not preempted. VPDoR sets their recharge rate below market value.
- From Sept. 1, 2026, to Aug. 31, 2027, a GPU-hour costs $0.30 for projects and $0.25 at the Basic Access tier.

## Background

Stanford HAI publishes this page on the Marlowe site. It gives the three access tiers and the recharge rates for two periods. It also gives the four steps from application to first job, and a list of questions and answers. Marlowe has 248 NVIDIA H100 GPUs ([Stanford Marlowe about (web)](Stanford%20Marlowe%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Marlowe is available to all Stanford faculty for GPU-accelerated research. New PIs receive 5,000 free introductory GPU-hours." (Header)
- Basic Access: "Basic Access is valid for one year" and "Jobs may be preempted by higher-priority work". "Application must be completed by the faculty PI personally." (Access tiers)
- Medium Project: "Up to 10,000 GPU-hours per cycle" and "Project access is valid for one cycle (~12 weeks)". Large Project: "10,000+ GPU-hours per cycle". Both require Basic Access first. (Access tiers)
- "Marlowe runs on a subsidized recharge model that sustains and improves the system. Rates are set by VPDoR, held below market value, and are subject to change" (Recharge rates)
- Compute "effective Jan 1 – Aug 31, 2026": "$0.25 / GPU-hour" non-preemptible and "$0.20 / GPU-hour" preemptible. Compute "effective Sep 1, 2026 – Aug 31, 2027": "$0.30 / GPU-hour" and "$0.25 / GPU-hour". (Recharge rates)
- CPU usage is "$0.010 / CPU-hour" non-preemptible and "$0.005 / CPU-hour" preemptible in both periods. Project storage is "$20 / TB per month". (Recharge rates)
- "Graduate students and postdocs can use the system under a faculty PI's allocation." (FAQ, Who is eligible?)
- "Basic Access applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and are typically processed within a few business days." (FAQ, How long does approval take?)

## Evidence and method

A program web page of about 850 words. It states the access rules and the price list of one campus GPU system. It gives no count of users or projects, no cost of operation and no amount of the subsidy.

## Limits

The page does not say what market value it compares its rates to. It does not give the size of the subsidy or who pays it. It does not say if users outside Stanford can apply. It does not say who reviews Medium and Large Project applications, or how; it points to an application guide that this library does not hold.
