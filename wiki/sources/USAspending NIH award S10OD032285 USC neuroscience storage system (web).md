## Bottom line

- NIH gives the University of Southern California (USC) $1,673,615 for new storage. The storage is for the computing center of the USC Laboratory of Neuro Imaging (LONI).
- The period of performance is Sept. 23, 2022 to Sept. 22, 2024. The record shows the full amount as spent.

## Background

USAspending.gov publishes federal spending records. This is its API record for one NIH grant. The NIH Office of the Director funds it, under funding opportunity PAR-21-126. Two Assistance Listings pay for it: 93.351, "Research Infrastructure Programs", $1,589,615, and 93.310, "Trans-NIH Research Support", $84,000. The recipient is in Los Angeles. Its business categories are "Higher Education" and "Higher Education (Private)".

## Key claims

- "THE REQUESTED INSTRUMENTATION PACKAGE, WHICH WILL UPGRADE THE STORAGE INFRASTRUCTURE OF OUR HIGH PERFORMANCE COMPUTATIONAL CENTER (HPCC) RESOURCE, ALLEVIATING SIGNIFICANT SPACE CONSTRAINTS IN ALL ASPECTS OF OUR COMPUTATIONAL NEUROSCIENCE INVESTIGATIONS." (description)
- "THE REQUESTED ADDITIONAL STORAGE INFRASTRUCTURE WILL BENEFIT MANY OF LONI’S COLLABORATORS BOTH AT USC AND ACROSS THE NATION." (description)
- The benefits are an "INCREASE IN STORAGE CAPACITY; CO-LOCALIZATION OF COMPUTE AND STORAGE; CO-LOCALIZATION OF PRE-PROCESSED AND ANALYZED RESULTS AND RAW DATA; AND STAGING FOR THIRD PARTY AND CLOUD RESIDENCE." (description)
- "AN ADMINISTRATIVE PLAN IS ALREADY IN PLACE BY WHICH THE EQUIPMENT CAN BE MANAGED EQUITABLY." (description)
- "To be eligible for instrumentation and equipment programs, applications must be Private nonprofit institutions/organizations, Public nonprofit institutions/organizations." (Assistance Listing 93.351, applicant eligibility)
- `"total_obligation": 1673615.0`, `"date_signed": "2022-09-22"`, `"total_outlay": 1673615.0` (amounts and dates)

## Evidence and method

A federal award record. It gives the amount obligated and spent, the dates, the funding office, the Assistance Listings and the recipient. The description is the recipient's project summary at the time of the award, not a report on results. The record shows no subawards. The non-federal funding is $0.

## Limits

- The record gives no capacity for the storage system and no cost of operation after the award.
- It does not say how the administrative plan shares the equipment, or which outside collaborators can use it.
- The place of performance gives the state only, with no city.
