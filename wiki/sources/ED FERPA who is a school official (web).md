# ED FERPA who is a school official (web)

## Citation

## Bottom line

- The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) lets a school outsource a service that involves education records. The outside party can be a contractor or another third party.
- The outside party must meet four conditions. It must do work that employees would otherwise do, and it must be "under the direct control" of the school for the records.
- The outside party can use personally identifiable information (PII) only for the purpose of the disclosure.

## Background

The Student Privacy Policy Office of the U.S. Department of Education runs the Protecting Student Privacy website. This page is one answer in its list of frequently asked questions. It is for K-12 and postsecondary school officials. It cites the FERPA regulation at § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B).

## Key claims

- Who: "A “school official” includes a teacher, school principal, president, chancellor, board member, trustee, registrar, counselor, admissions officer, attorney, accountant, human resources professional, information systems specialist, and support or clerical personnel."
- Outsourcing: "FERPA (§ 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B)) permits schools to outsource institutional services or functions that involve the disclosure of education records to contractors, consultants, volunteers, or other third parties provided that the outside party:"
- Condition 1: "Performs an institutional service or function for which the agency or institution would otherwise use employees;"
- Condition 2: "Is under the direct control of the agency or institution with respect to the use and maintenance of education records;"
- Condition 3: "Is subject to the requirements in § 99.33(a) that the personally identifiable information (PII) from education records may be used only for the purposes for which the disclosure was made".
- Condition 4: "Meets the criteria specified in the school or local educational agency’s (LEA’s) annual notification of FERPA rights for being a school official with a legitimate educational interest in the education records."
