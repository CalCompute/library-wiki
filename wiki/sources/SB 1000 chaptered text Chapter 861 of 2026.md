## Bottom line

- SB 1000 amends the California AI Transparency Act (CATA), Business and Professions Code sections 22757.1 to 22757.5. It is an urgency statute that takes effect immediately.
- A "covered provider" is a person that produces a generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) system that is publicly accessible in California. The bill deletes the threshold of 1,000,000 monthly users.
- A covered provider must offer a disclosure verification tool at no cost. It must put a latent disclosure in the image, video and audio content that its GenAI system creates or alters.
- The civil penalty is $5,000 for each violation, and each day in violation is one violation. Until January 1, 2029, a false statement that a system is assistive technology has a penalty of $50,000 for each violation.

## Key claims

- "“Covered provider” means a person that creates, codes, or otherwise produces a generative artificial intelligence system that is publicly accessible within the geographic boundaries of the state." (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22757.1(e))
- "“GenAI hosting platform” means an internet website or application that makes available for download the source code or model weights a generative artificial intelligence system by a resident of the state, regardless of whether the terms of that use include compensation." (§ 22757.1(h))
- "A covered provider shall make available a disclosure verification tool at no cost to the user that meets all of the following criteria:" (§ 22757.2(a))
- "To the extent it is technically feasible, a covered provider shall include a latent disclosure that meets all of the following criteria in any image, video, or audio content, or content that is any combination thereof, created or altered, except by minor modification, by the covered provider’s GenAI system:" (§ 22757.3(a))
- "If a covered provider licenses its GenAI system to a third party, the covered provider shall, when it provides the license, notify the licensee of the licensee’s obligations under this chapter." (§ 22757.3(b)(1))
- "A violator of this chapter shall be liable for a civil penalty in the amount of five thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation to be collected in a civil action filed by the Attorney General, a city attorney, or a county counsel." (§ 22757.4(a)(1))
- "Before January 1, 2029, this chapter does not apply to a GenAI system that is designed to primarily function as assistive technology." (§ 22757.5(b))

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate. It prints only the sections that it amends or adds. It does not print section 22757.3.1 on large online platforms; [AB 2713 chaptered text Chapter 856 of 2026](AB%202713%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20856%20of%202026.md) has that section. The printed sections define "GenAI hosting platform" and "capture device manufacturer" but give them no duty. The bill does not define "person".

It does not mention research compute or CalCompute. The copy shows the bill as chaptered, so later amendments do not show.
