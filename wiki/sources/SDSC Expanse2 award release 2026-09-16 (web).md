# SDSC Expanse2 award release 2026-09-16 (web)

## Citation

Bruch, Kimberly Mann. "New $10M NSF Award to SDSC at UC San Diego to Deploy Expanse2." San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. News release, published September 15, 2026. https://www.sdsc.edu/news/2026/PR20260916-Expanse2.html. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The National Science Foundation (NSF) gave SDSC $10 million to deploy Expanse2, which replaces the Expanse supercomputer. It enters production in 2027, under NSF award 2614012.
- Expanse2 will contribute to the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR). Its education, outreach and training work is for domestic workforce development, and industry outreach goes through the director's discretionary allocations.

## Background

SDSC published this news release on the NSF award. It describes the planned hardware in general terms: AMD processors and accelerators, and an all-NVMe (non-volatile memory express) file system. It quotes the UC San Diego chancellor, an NSF senior science advisor, the principal investigator (PI) Michael Norman and SDSC Director Frank Würthwein. It names the co-PIs and senior personnel.

## Key claims

- "the U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) has awarded $10 million to the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at the University of California San Diego Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing to deploy NSF Expanse2" (paragraph 1)
- "NSF Expanse2 will replace the NSF-funded Expanse supercomputer, which has supported researchers and educators since late 2020." (paragraph 3)
- "It will also contribute to the NSF-led National AI Research Resource, helping expand access to advanced computing and AI capabilities, strengthen U.S. research competitiveness and support the development of the nation's future STEM workforce." (paragraph 6)
- "When it enters production in 2027, NSF Expanse2 will be one of the newest resources available to the national HPC community, including researchers participating in NAIRR" (paragraph 7)
- "Following the success of Expanse, NSF Expanse2 will continue to serve the "long tail of science" and provide access to advanced computing resources for a broad community of researchers, institutions and scientific disciplines." (paragraph 7)
- "Additionally, the HPC system will offer user-friendly allocation and scheduling policies, support rapid application development and data analysis via Jupyter notebooks, provide extensive AI/ML frameworks and libraries, and connect users with federated and extensible data ecosystems." (paragraph 9)
- "While Expanse2 education, outreach and training activities will be focused on domestic workforce development across universities, teaching colleges and high school students to strengthen the U.S. scientific and technical workforce, industry outreach via the SDSC director’s discretionary allocation efforts will foster public-private partnerships." (paragraph 14)
- "The NSF Expanse2 project is funded under NSF award number 2614012." (last paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A news release from the center that got the award. It gives the award amount, the award number, the production year and the user groups. It gives no counts of nodes, processors or graphics processing units (GPUs). It says that Expanse "has served tens of thousands of researchers", in a quotation from the PI.

## Limits

The release does not give the size or peak performance of Expanse2, its allocation routes or its share for NAIRR. It does not say when Expanse stops. The page prints "Published September 15, 2026", but its address and the library's title use September 16. [SDSC Expanse User Guide (web)](SDSC%20Expanse%20User%20Guide%20%28web%29.md) describes the current Expanse system.
