# Berkeley SkyDeck homepage (web)

## Citation

Berkeley SkyDeck, University of California, Berkeley. "Berkeley SkyDeck" homepage. Date not stated; the footer shows © 2026. https://skydeck.berkeley.edu/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Berkeley SkyDeck is a startup accelerator program of UC Berkeley. Its Accelerator Program gives about 20 startups every six months a $200,000 investment from the Berkeley SkyDeck Fund.
- The Fund "shares one-half of fund profits with UC Berkeley". The page calls this "a first-of-its-kind, public-private partnership".

## Background

SkyDeck runs three programs: the Accelerator Program, the Innovation Partners Program and the Pad-13 incubator. The homepage gives program facts, figures on its startups, and its investors and industry partners. The Berkeley SkyDeck Fund is a separate venture fund that invests in each Accelerator startup.

## Key claims

- "Berkeley SkyDeck is a program of the University of California, Berkeley" (footer)
- "Roughly 20 startups are selected every 6 months to receive a $200,000 investment from the Berkeley SkyDeck Fund." (SkyDeck Accelerator Program)
- "In a first-of-its-kind, public-private partnership, the Berkeley SkyDeck Fund shares one-half of fund profits with UC Berkeley." (Berkeley SkyDeck Fund)
- "It is funded by organizations such as Sequoia Capital, Sierra Ventures, and Canvas Ventures, as well as other VC firms, individuals, and large corporations." (Berkeley SkyDeck Fund)
- "Startups applying to Pad-13 should have at least one founder affiliated with University of California." (Pad-13)
- "Berkeley SkyDeck hosts over 250 startups per year across three programs." (Why SkyDeck is the best accelerator for startups)
- "We help connect the intellectual firepower of UC Berkeley to our startups, matching hundreds of students, MBAs, and postdocs to intern and work for our startups." (Access to Talent)

## Evidence and method

A program homepage. It gives figures such as "$2.7B" raised by its startups and "17" exits through acquisitions. It does not give the source or date of these figures. It does not give the Fund's size or its legal form.

## Limits

The page does not say how the Fund's profit share reaches UC Berkeley, or how much it has paid. It does not describe any compute resources for startups. It gives no date for its figures.
