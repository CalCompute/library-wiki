# Cabaldon Senate biography (web)

## Citation

Office of Senator Christopher Cabaldon. "Biography." Official website of Senate District 3, California State Senate, date not stated. https://sd03.senate.ca.gov/biography. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Cabaldon Senate biography.html`.

## Bottom line

- Christopher Cabaldon represents the Third Senate District in the California State Senate. Voters elected him in 2024.
- Before the Senate, he was Mayor of West Sacramento for two decades and vice chancellor of the California Community Colleges.

## Background

The office of the senator for District 3 publishes this page. It gives his work as mayor, his state and national roles in higher education and workforce development, and his education.

## Key claims

- "Christopher Cabaldon was elected to the California State Senate in 2024, representing the people of Wine Country, the Delta, the North Bay, and the Sacramento Valley who make their home in the 3,000 square miles of the Third Senate District." (Biography)
- "Before his election to the Senate, he served two decades as Mayor of West Sacramento in Yolo County." (Biography)
- "He served previously as vice chancellor of the California Community Colleges and director of the higher education committee for the State Assembly." (Biography)
- "He led the work of the nation's mayors on youth employment, college and career pathways, early learning, digital badging, and rebooting workforce development to meet the disruptive impact of trade and technology." (Biography)
- He "was appointed in 2019 to a tenured faculty post in the same department as the Hazel Cramer Endowed Chair and Professor of Public Policy and Administration" at Sacramento State. (Biography)
