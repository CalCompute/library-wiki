# CZI homepage (web)

## Citation

Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI). Homepage, "About CZI", date not stated (copyright 2026). https://chanzuckerberg.com/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg founded CZI in 2015. Its focus areas are science and education.
- Since 2015, CZI has awarded "approximately $7.22 billion in grants" and invested "nearly $300 million in ventures".

## Background

CZI is a philanthropy. Its homepage gives its focus areas, its grants and ventures, its local giving and the legal entities through which it works.

## Key claims

- "The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative was founded in 2015 by Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg to help solve some of society’s toughest challenges — from curing or preventing disease to improving education and addressing the needs of our local communities." (About CZI)
- "We’re leading the new era of AI-powered biology. At the Biohub, we bring together scientists, engineers, and AI experts with a shared mission to help scientists cure or prevent all disease." (Science)
- "Since 2015, CZI has awarded approximately $7.22 billion in grants and invested nearly $300 million in ventures whose work aligns with our values." (Grants & Ventures)
- "CZI carries out its work through the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, LLC; the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Foundation (a 501(c)(3) private foundation); the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Donor-Advised Fund (DAF) at the Silicon Valley Community Foundation; and Chan Zuckerberg Initiative Advocacy, (a 501(c)(4) organization)." (page footer)

## Evidence and method

An organization homepage. It gives totals but no list of grants.

## Limits

The page does not describe the Biohub's computing, a compute grant program or grants in California. It does not say how to apply for a grant.
