# UF HiPerGator upgrade 2024 (web)

## Citation

University of Florida (UF), UF News. "University of Florida to acquire one of the world’s most powerful supercomputers." December 13, 2024. https://news.ufl.edu/2024/12/hipergator-upgrade/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The UF Board of Trustees approves $24 million for a new HiPerGator: an NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with 63 DGX B200 systems.
- UF shares HiPerGator with schools in the State University System of Florida. It powers Navigator AI, UF's generative AI tool for all faculty, students and staff.

## Background

UF News publishes this story. The new machine replaces the part of HiPerGator that NVIDIA co-founder Chris Malachowsky donated: a DGX SuperPOD with DGX A100 systems. The UF and NVIDIA collaboration began in 2020.

## Key claims

- "The University of Florida Board of Trustees today approved investing $24 million to acquire a more advanced version of UF’s HiPerGator supercomputer." (lead)
- "Additionally, UF will bring value to the state and the larger region by sharing access to HiPerGator with schools across the State University System of Florida." (fourth paragraph)
- "The part of HiPerGator originally donated by Malachowsky — an NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with DGX A100 systems — is being replaced by the new NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with 63 NVIDIA DGX B200 systems." (body)
- "Since 2020, UF has added more than 100 professors who specialize in AI teaching and research to the hundreds that were already at the university." (body)
- "HiPerGator is now used every semester in courses taught by faculty throughout UF and the State University System, and in research projects led by more than 6,000 researchers. HiPerGator is the power behind Navigator AI" (body)

## Evidence and method

A university news story with the approved amount, the hardware, quotations from UF leaders and project examples. It gives no operating cost.

## Limits

The story gives no allocation rules or prices. It counts "more than 6,000 researchers". The [UF AI HiPerGator page (web)](UF%20AI%20HiPerGator%20page%20%28web%29.md) counts "3,700+" users, at a later date.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this story gives "$24 million" for the new HiPerGator. The [UF AI HiPerGator page (web)](UF%20AI%20HiPerGator%20page%20%28web%29.md) calls the fourth generation "The $33 million purchase".
