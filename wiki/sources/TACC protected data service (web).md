# TACC protected data service (web)

## Citation

Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC), The University of Texas at Austin. "Protected Data Service." TACC website, Security and Compliance, date not stated. https://tacc.utexas.edu/about/security-and-compliance/protected-data-service/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) Protected Data Service (PDS) lets researchers use sensitive data on TACC systems. TACC does not accept data classified secret or above.
- The page lists health data under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), student records under FERPA, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), export-controlled data (ITAR, EAR) and personal data.
- A researcher fills out a request form and has a consultation. The researcher gives the documents that TACC asks for, such as data use agreements, then requests an allocation with secure storage.
- TACC says its program aligns with National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) SP 800-53 Revision 5 and SP 800-171. A third-party auditor audits it on a fixed schedule.

## Background

TACC is a supercomputing center at The University of Texas at Austin. The page describes how a project gets protected-data storage and compute. Protected work runs in separate queues from open work. See [TACC user policies (web)](TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md) for TACC's general rules.

## Key claims

- "TACC's Protected Data Service (PDS) supports standard security control needs that require safeguarding sensitive data used by researchers reducing the workload for individuals and research teams to satisfy these security needs." (Introduction)
- "Based on your project and that data being used, we will request the necessary documentation (data use agreements, BAA, MOU, etc)." (Consultation)
- "When approved, you will receive an email directing you to request an allocation on TACC's systems. This will include both corral-secure for storage and a computational system for compute resources." (Request Allocation)
- "TACC does not support data classified at secret or above" (Supported Data)
- "The audit assesses TACC's compliance with NIST 800-53 Revision 5, NIST 800-171 Revision 2, and the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) Security Rule." (Audit)

## Evidence and method

A service page of about 450 words of content. It states the steps and the standards. It names no auditor and gives no audit report.

## Limits

The page gives no cost, no review time and no rule on who can use the service. It does not name the auditor or the audit schedule. It does not say how the protected queues differ from the open queues.
