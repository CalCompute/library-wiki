# Moore Foundation about (web)

## Citation

Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation. "Our Story." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://www.moore.org/about. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Gordon and Betty Moore set up the foundation. Gordon Moore was a founder of Intel and made the 1965 prediction known as Moore's Law.
- The foundation calls itself "society’s venture capital". Its program areas are Environmental Conservation, Science and the San Francisco Bay Area.

## Background

The foundation is in Palo Alto, California. Its About page gives its grantmaking approach, its founders, its culture and its program areas.

## Key claims

- "We appreciate our role as society’s venture capital, making bold investments and taking considered risks, to attain significant results that can improve the future." (Our Grantmaking)
- "We seek to achieve through philanthropy what Gordon Moore accomplished for the world of computing: to create an ever more powerful engine of progress in society." (Our Grantmaking)
- "In 1965, Gordon made a rule-of-thumb prediction that later became known as Moore’s Law." (Our Founders)
- "As a founder of Intel, he helped shape what we now recognize as the Silicon Valley and technology sector." (Our Founders)

## Evidence and method

An organization page. It gives no figures.

## Limits

The page gives no grant totals, no assets and no grant list. It does not describe an AI or compute program.
