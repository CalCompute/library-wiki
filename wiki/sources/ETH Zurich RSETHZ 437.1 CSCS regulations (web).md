# ETH Zurich RSETHZ 437.1 CSCS regulations (web)

## Citation

ETH Zurich. *Swiss National Supercomputing Center (CSCS): General Terms and Conditions for the Operation of HPC Systems, Provision of HPC Resources, and Services Provided to the Swiss Research Community ("GTC CSCS").* RSETHZ 437.1, ETH Zurich legal collection. Edition August 2023, updated May 2025 (editorial changes). PDF, 16 pages. https://ethz.ch/content/dam/ethz/main/eth-zurich/organisation/rechtssammlung/437.1.pdf. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- These general terms and conditions (GTC) govern how CSCS runs high-performance computing (HPC) systems and sells HPC resources to Swiss research partners. Each partner signs a collaboration agreement (CA).
- CSCS ranks the uses of its building in order: the User Lab first, national missions such as MeteoSwiss second, and strategic ETH Domain projects third.
- Partners pay all costs of their systems and resources, through tariffs set for cost recovery. ETH Zurich/CSCS owns all systems, whoever paid for them.

## Background

RSETHZ is the legal collection of ETH Zurich (the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich). CSCS is a unit of ETH Zurich ([CSCS organization (web)](CSCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md)). This document applies to ETH Domain institutions, cantonal universities, universities of applied sciences, other federal research institutions and federal units such as MeteoSwiss. It covers three activities: dedicated systems for one partner, shared resources on non-dedicated systems, and consultancy. Other terms govern foreign universities, private companies and the "cscs2go" service.

## Key claims

- Scope: the GTC apply "for the benefit of the following Swiss Partners: a. The ETH-Domain Institutions, cantonal universities and universities of applied sciences as well as other Swiss federal research institutions b. Federal units in the districts 1 to 3 (in particular MeteoSwiss)." (section 3)
- Others: "Services provided under “cscs2go” or to foreign universities and private companies are governed by different GTC provisions." (section 3)
- Priorities: "ETH Zurich/CSCS is solely responsible for the allocation of this infrastructure to the various types of use, with the following priorities (in descending order) being applicable". The list is User Lab, national missions, strategic large ETH Domain projects, scientific collaborations, and others. (section 4)
- Planning: "The allocation of the available building infrastructure is based on a multi-annual plan prepared by CSCS on the basis of ETH Zurich planning decisions that is in alignment with the planning of the ETH-domain and the federal government." (section 4)
- Funding first: "The HPC system shall not be procured until the financing has been secured by the partner and the appropriated funds have been transferred to ETH Zurich/CSCS." (section 7)
- Default lifetime: "If the maximum lifetime is not defined, its default is 3 years." (section 8)
- Cost recovery: "The partner shall bear all costs related to the provisioning of HPC resources. In order to ensure cost recovery, ETH Zurich/CSCS defines the tariffs for the HPC resources in the CA and communicates it to the partner." (section 11)
- Pay-per-use: "Pay-per-use HPC resources may be available at a higher tariff, since ETH Zurich/CSCS bears the cost of the HPC system availability and under-utilization." (section 11)
- Capacity exchange: "User lab capacity can be exchanged with partners as long as the exchange is cost-neutral in the long-term." (section 10)
- Ownership: "HPC resources and HPC systems are in the inventory of ETH Zurich, regardless of any investments made by the partner." (section 13)
- No resale: the partner is not allowed to "Sell, resell, lease, or the functional equivalent, the HPC resources to a third party". (section 6)

## Evidence and method

This is a binding regulation of a public university, in 24 sections. It lists what the tariff covers: the system purchase price, maintenance, building extensions, power and cooling, licences and staff.

It sets free basic services: machine room space, building connection and a share of the network. It sets support hours (weekdays 8:00 to 17:00 CET) and data transfer through the SWITCH network. It also covers IT security, data protection, liability, value added tax (VAT), confidentiality, termination and Swiss law. A footnote ties the User Lab priority to a decision of the ETH Board of July 2011. The decision is on the High Performance Computing and Networking (HPCN) Strategy 2013-2016.

## Limits

- The document does not give any tariff. Tariffs are in each CA.
- It does not cover User Lab access for researchers. The User Lab is free of charge to researchers, as the CSCS brochure reports ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)). These GTC cover paying partners.
- It does not give the terms for foreign universities, private companies or cscs2go.
- The row date was unknown. The dates of the August 2023 edition and the May 2025 update come from the document itself.
