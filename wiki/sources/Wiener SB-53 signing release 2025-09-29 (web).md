# Wiener SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29 (web)

## Citation

Office of Senator Scott Wiener (D-San Francisco). "Governor Newsom Signs Senator Wiener's Landmark AI Law To Set Commonsense Guardrails, Boost Innovation." Press release, Sacramento, September 29, 2025. <https://sd11.senate.ca.gov/news/governor-newsom-signs-senator-wieners-landmark-ai-law-set-commonsense-guardrails-boost>. Read 2026-10-20.

## Bottom line

- The author's office says SB 53 "establishes a public cloud compute cluster" and is "creating 'CalCompute'". The statute creates a consortium to develop a framework, not the cluster.
- It frames CalCompute as "industrial policy for AI" for "startups and researchers".
- The co-sponsor calls CalCompute "a public option for computing power".

## Background

Governor Newsom signed SB 53 on September 29, 2025. His own release and signing message are on [Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29](Newsom%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29.md) and [SB-53 signing message 2025-09-29](SB-53%20signing%20message%202025-09-29.md). This page is the author's release for the same day. The working group report behind the law is [California Report on Frontier AI Policy 2025-06-17](California%20Report%20on%20Frontier%20AI%20Policy%202025-06-17.md).

## Key claims

- **Subtitle.** SB 53 "establishes a public cloud compute cluster to foster democratized AI innovation".
- **Industrial policy.** "It also advances an industrial policy for AI by creating "CalCompute," a public cloud compute cluster that provides AI infrastructure for startups and researchers."
- **Manufacturing link.** "CalCompute builds on Senator Wiener's recent legislation to boost semiconductor and other advanced manufacturing in California by streamlining permit approvals for advanced manufacturing plants".
- **Working group leads.** The group "is led by experts including the "godmother of AI" Dr. Fei-Fei Li, Co-Director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence; Dr. Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar, President of the Carnegie Endowment for International Peace; and Dr. Jennifer Tour Chayes, Dean of the UC Berkeley College of Computing, Data Science, and Society."
- **Sponsors.** "SB 53 is sponsored by the Encode AI, Economic Security Action California, and the Secure AI Project."
- **Teri Olle, "Director of Economic Security California Action (ESCAA)".**
  > By signing SB 53 into law, Governor Newsom recognizes that the tremendous innovative power of AI should benefit all of us, not just a handful of tech corporations and their investors [...] the creation of a public option for computing power through CalCompute will democratize access to critical AI infrastructure. This will make it possible for California's brightest minds to build AI tools that solve our most pressing public problems for generations to come
- **Senator Wiener** thanks the Governor for "working with us to refine the legislation". His quote does not mention CalCompute.

## Evidence and method

An advocacy statement by the author and sponsors. Most of the release covers the transparency and incident rules. CalCompute has two sentences and one sponsor quote.

## Limits

- The release does not mention the consortium, the Report, the University of California or subd. (k).
- The Nathan Calvin quote has no opening quotation mark in the source. The wording is as published.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The release says SB 53 "establishes a public cloud compute cluster" and is "creating "CalCompute"". Enacted § 11546.8 (a) establishes "a consortium that shall develop [...] a framework for the creation of a public cloud computing cluster to be known as "CalCompute."" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)). The March 2025 committee analysis says: "This bill does not establish CalCompute" ([SB-53 Senate GO analysis 2025-03-25 (web)](SB-53%20Senate%20GO%20analysis%202025-03-25%20%28web%29.md)).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The release says CalCompute "provides AI infrastructure for startups and researchers". Subd. (f)(2)(D) leaves the users to "a process for determining which users and projects will be supported by CalCompute" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
