# EDD long-term occupational employment projections dataset (web)

## Citation

California Employment Development Department (EDD). "Long-Term Occupational Employment Projections." Dataset page on the California Open Data portal, updated Oct. 28, 2026. https://publish.data.ca.gov/dataset/long-term-occupational-employment-projections. Read 2026-11-09. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-09 EDD long-term occupational employment projections dataset.html`.

## Bottom line

- The page is the open data record for the EDD's 10-year occupational employment projections for the state and its labor market regions.
- It has one CSV file, for 2024 to 2034, under a Creative Commons Attribution license. The EDD revises the projections each year.

## Background

The California Open Data portal lists state datasets. This record gives the dataset description, its publisher, license, update frequency, tags and a download link. The file is "lt-occ-emp-2024-2034.csv", 3.5 MB. The library took the record page, not the CSV.

## Key claims

- "Long-term Occupational Projections for a 10-year time horizon are provided for the State and its labor market regions to provide individuals and organizations with an occupational outlook to make informed decisions on individual career and organizational program development." (description)
- "Long-term projections are revised annually. Data are not available for geographies below the labor market regions." (description)
- "License: Creative Commons Attribution" (Published by)
- "Rights No restrictions on public use" (About this dataset)
- "Update frequency R/P1Y" (About this dataset)

## Evidence and method

A dataset catalog record. It describes the data and gives its terms. It holds none of the projection figures.

## Limits

The record has no numbers. A reader needs the CSV for occupation counts, growth or openings. The description on the page is cut short ("Detail may not add to..."). It does not name AI or computing occupations.
