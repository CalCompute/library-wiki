# CalHR MOU Unit 12 IUOE 2023-2026 (web)

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the International Union of Operating Engineers (IUOE) for State Bargaining Unit 12, craft and maintenance. Its term is July 1, 2023 to June 30, 2026.
- For a change in an area that the MOU does not cover, a state agency gets delegation from CalHR and gives IUOE 60 days notice. On request, the parties negotiate the impact within those 60 days.
- Departments send IUOE the bids and contract forms for personal services contracts with Unit 12 work. The MOU supports joint apprenticeship programs. It does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. Unit 12 holds the State's craft and maintenance classes, with apprentice salaries at the Department of Water Resources (Article 2.3). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The State proposes the term of this agreement be from July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2026." (Article 23, Duration, paragraph 1)
- "Except for those rights which are abridged or limited by this Agreement, all rights are reserved to the State." (Article 6, paragraph 1)
- "When a State agency finds it necessary to make such changes, the State agency shall seek delegation from the CalHR and if granted shall notify IUOE of the proposed change 60 days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 22, 22.1)
- "The purpose of this section is to provide the Union with notice and an opportunity to present alternatives which mitigate or avoid the need for contracting out, while still satisfying the needs of the State to provide services." (Article 20, 20.9.3)
- "Upon request of the Director of IUOE BU 12, the State will meet with IUOE BU 12 to discuss the State’s efforts to reduce the use of Personal Services Contracts." (Article 20, 20.9.4)
- "The State and the Union support the establishment and operation of apprenticeship programs where deemed appropriate within Unit 12." (Article 16, 16.3.1)
- "The State agrees to reimburse Unit 12 employees for expenses incurred as a result of completing training or education courses required by the department to assure adequate performance or increase job proficiency." (Article 16, 16.1.1)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, apprenticeship terms, leaves, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The term ended on June 30, 2026. The library holds no successor agreement for Unit 12.
- Article 23, paragraph 1, states the term as what "The State proposes", not as an agreed term.
- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence. Technological change appears only in the Government Code section 19995.2 entry of its list of superseded and incorporated sections.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
