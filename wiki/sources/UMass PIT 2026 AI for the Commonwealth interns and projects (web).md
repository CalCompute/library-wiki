# UMass PIT 2026 AI for the Commonwealth interns and projects (web)

## Citation

Public Interest Technology Initiative, University of Massachusetts Amherst. "2026 AI4CW Interns and Projects." News post, July 27, 2026. https://groups.cs.umass.edu/pit/2026/07/27/2026-ai4cw-interns-and-projects/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- In spring and summer 2026, 14 UMass sophomores and juniors took part in the AI for the Commonwealth (AI4CW) Internship program. It is a joint program of UMass's Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences and the Commonwealth's Executive Office of Technology Services and Security.
- Students took a spring course, then held paid summer internships to deliver AI prototypes to state and regional clients. AWS mentored each team.

## Background

The post lists five projects. The clients are the Massachusetts Department of Public Health, the Division of Ecological Restoration, MassHealth, UMass Human Resources and the Berkshire Regional Planning Commission. Three students from the 2025 cohort came back as mentors. The post says the students will present to Governor Maura Healey in September.

## Key claims

- "During the spring and summer of 2026, 14 UMass sophomores and juniors participated in the AI for the Commonwealth Internship program, a collaborative initiative of the Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences and the Executive Office of Technology Services and Security (EOTSS) for the Commonwealth." (opening)
- "The program began in the spring semester with an experiential learning course where students gained first hand experience with a human centered design approach paired with modern software engineering practices." (opening)
- "During the summer, the students held paid internships where they continued to collaborate with the UMass team and their clients to deliver mature prototypes to clients by the end of the summer." (opening)
- "Additionally, AWS supported each team throughout the spring and summer through a robust mentoring programming, guiding students on best practices in leveraging AWS services for the projects." (opening)
- "This project supports MassHealth in processing non-English documents more efficiently by partially automating their translation workflow through Optical Character Recognition (OCR) text extraction and multilingual form-field mapping." (MassHealth Form Processing)

## Evidence and method

A university news post. It describes the program and the five projects, with student names and photos. It gives no costs or results.

## Limits

The post does not give the interns' pay, the program budget or who funds it. It does not say whether clients use the prototypes. The 2025 cohort is on a separate page that the library does not have.
