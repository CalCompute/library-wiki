# Fresno State F3i Supercomputing Center launch (web)

## Citation

BoNhia Lee. "Supercomputer Center launches at Fresno State." Fresno State News, California State University (CSU), Fresno, July 6, 2026. https://today.fresnostate.edu/supercomputer-center-launches-at-fresno-state/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- F3 Innovate (F3i), Fresno State and the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) announce the F3i Supercomputing Center for California's food and agriculture sector.
- Fresno State is the home of the center. The center gives shared high-performance computing, applied AI services, and workforce and talent development.

## Background

Fresno State publishes this story on its news site. It announces a launch event on Thursday, July 9, in the Fresno State Library. It quotes the F3i chief executive officer, the Fresno State chief information officer and the SDSC Director. SDSC published a similar release later in July ([SDSC F3i Supercomputing Center release 2026-07-16 (web)](SDSC%20F3i%20Supercomputing%20Center%20release%202026-07-16%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "F3 Innovate (F3i), Fresno State and the San Diego Supercomputer Center at UC San Diego today announced the launch of the F3i Supercomputing Center, a regional innovation platform providing the computing power, technical support and talent pipeline needed to accelerate innovation across California’s food and agriculture supply chain." (first paragraph)
- "The center represents a first-of-its-kind partnership between industry and higher education to expand access to the advanced computing infrastructure needed to compete in an AI-driven economy." (first paragraph)
- "While these industries generate significant economic value, most of the investment supporting AI development and other advanced technologies has historically been located elsewhere." (body)
- "Instead of requiring businesses, researchers and educators to build their own computing systems, the F3i Supercomputing Center provides shared access to powerful computing resources and technical support" (body)
- "Fresno State will serve as the home of the center, expanding opportunities for applied research, workforce development and industry collaboration while reinforcing the university’s role as California’s leading agricultural university." (body)
- "Through this partnership, we are extending advanced computing capabilities beyond traditional research environments and into one of the nation’s most important production economies." (Frank Wuerthwein, director of SDSC)
- "**Affordable High-Performance Computing** The center offers shared access to advanced computing resources that enable growers, startups and researchers to train AI models, process large datasets and test emerging technologies without making significant infrastructure investments." (three integrated services)
- "**Workforce and Talent Development** Through F3i’s Data Challenges and AI Launchpad program with Microsoft, the center prepares students, educators, researchers and working professionals to use advanced computing and artificial intelligence in careers advancing agriculture’s next era." (three integrated services)

## Evidence and method

A university news story with quotes from the three partners. It gives the three services, the users that the center serves and the regional economic context. It says that the Central Valley "supports more than 14,000 farming and food-processing operations". It links SDSC to the National Research Platform and the National Data Platform, with support from the National Science Foundation (NSF).

## Limits

- The story does not give the hardware, the number of graphics processing units (GPUs), the cost, the funding source or an allocation process.
- It does not say what "Affordable" means in prices.
- It names an AI Launchpad program with Microsoft but does not describe it.
- Its text is close to the SDSC release. The SDSC release adds "students" to the users of the computing service and says "talent pathways" for "talent pipeline".
- [F3i Supercomputing Center (web)](F3i%20Supercomputing%20Center%20%28web%29.md) is F3 Innovate's own page on the center. It gives the role of each partner and the priority user groups.
