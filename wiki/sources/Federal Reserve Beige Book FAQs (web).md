# Federal Reserve Beige Book FAQs (web)

## Citation

Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System. "Beige Book: Frequently Asked Questions." Web page, last update January 05, 2021. https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/beige-book-faqs.htm. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 Federal Reserve Beige Book FAQs.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20Federal%20Reserve%20Beige%20Book%20FAQs.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Beige Book is a Federal Reserve report on economic conditions in the 12 Federal Reserve Districts, published eight times a year.
- Each Federal Reserve Bank collects mostly qualitative information from its own contacts. The contacts "are not selected at random".

## Background

The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System publishes this page. It answers five questions about the Beige Book. They cover its purpose, how the Federal Reserve collects and uses its information, and other data. For the list of reports, see [Federal Reserve Beige Book main page (web)](Federal%20Reserve%20Beige%20Book%20main%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The *Beige Book* is a Federal Reserve System publication about current economic conditions across the 12 Federal Reserve Districts." (What is the Beige Book?)
- "It characterizes regional economic conditions and prospects based on a variety of mostly qualitative information, gathered directly from each District’s sources." (same answer)
- "Each Federal Reserve Bank gathers information on current economic conditions in its District through reports from Bank and Branch directors, plus interviews and online questionnaires completed by businesses, community organizations, economists, market experts, and other sources." (How is the information collected?)
- "Contacts are not selected at random; rather, Banks strive to curate a diverse set of sources that can provide accurate and objective information about a broad range of economic activities." (same answer)
- "The *Beige Book* is not a commentary on the views of Federal Reserve officials." (What is the purpose of the Beige Book?)

## Evidence and method

An official agency page that describes a method. It gives no economic data. It says how the Federal Reserve collects and uses qualitative information from contacts.

## Limits

The page does not list the contacts or the questions. It does not give the size of the contact base for a District. It does not name California or the Twelfth District. The page was last updated in January 2021.
