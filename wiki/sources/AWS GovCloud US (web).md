# AWS GovCloud US (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS GovCloud (US)." Product web page, date not stated. https://aws.amazon.com/govcloud-us/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- AWS GovCloud (US) is two isolated AWS regions, US-East and US-West, "operated by U.S. citizens on U.S. soil", for controlled unclassified information (CUI).
- It is "for verified U.S. government agencies and entities". Root account holders must pass a screening of U.S. persons status.

## Background

AWS is a commercial cloud provider. This is its marketing page for the GovCloud (US) regions. The page says the regions are 15 years old. It lists compliance programs, customer stories and white papers. Customer stories include the Department of Veterans Affairs, the Department of Justice and cloud.gov.

## Key claims

- "It is comprised of two physically and logically isolated U.S. sovereign regions, AWS GovCloud (US-East and US-West), operated by U.S. citizens on U.S. soil" (Compliant Innovation).
- "AWS GovCloud (US) is an innovative compliant cloud solution that technology leaders trust to host sensitive and controlled unclassified information (CUI) data" (Compliant Innovation).
- "AWS GovCloud (US) is for verified U.S. government agencies and entities. Root Account holders must pass a screening process validating U.S. persons status" (Verified U.S. access).
- "It has a separate Identity and Access Management (IAM) system with unique credentials for account access and user access." (Verified U.S. access)
- "AWS GovCloud (US) Regions offer three Availability Zones with multiple, geographically distributed data centers for high availability." (Resilient innovation)
- "Build powerful interactive applications to efficiently transform citizen experience and accelerate mission outcomes with Amazon Bedrock and Amazon SageMaker, using NVIDIA GPUs." (Accelerate Business Outcomes)
- "The VA issued a FISMA High ATO for AWS GovCloud (US), using the regions to store and protect patient data critical to America's veterans" (Customer innovation stories).

## Evidence and method

A vendor's product page. It states features and names customers. It gives no prices, no capacity figures and no audit reports.

## Limits

- The page does not give GovCloud prices or GPU capacity.
- It does not say if a state agency or a state university qualifies as a "verified" entity. The getting-started page has the screening rules; the library does not have it.
- The compliance claims are the vendor's. The authorizations themselves are in the FedRAMP marketplace, which the library could not read.
