# Berkeley Savio Faculty Computing Allowance (web)

## Citation

UC Berkeley Research IT. "Faculty Computing Allowance." Research IT documentation, Berkeley Research Computing (BRC), date not stated. https://docs-research-it.berkeley.edu/services/high-performance-computing/getting-account/faculty-computing-allowance. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The Faculty Computing Allowance (FCA) gives up to 300,000 free Service Units (SUs) a year on the Savio cluster. Each qualified UC Berkeley principal investigator (PI) can get one.
- The allowance year runs from June 1 to May 31. A PI renews each year through the MyBRC User Portal, and a late application gets a prorated allowance.
- Professors and the Professional Research Series can be PIs. Postdoctoral researchers, project scientists and specialists cannot.

## Background

Berkeley Research Computing runs the Savio high performance computing (HPC) cluster at UC Berkeley. This page is the FCA overview in the Research IT documentation. It covers setup, eligibility, users, renewal, usage, charges and acknowledgement. Other pages, which this library does not hold, give the Service Unit rates and the options when an allowance runs out.

## Key claims

- "The Faculty Computing Allowance (FCA) provides up to 300K Service Units (SUs) of free compute time per academic year to all qualified UC Berkeley faculty/PIs" (Summary)
- "Allowances are prorated based on month of application during the allowance year, which starts on June 1 and ends on May 31 the following year." (Summary)
- "PIs can also pool their allowances if their groups are working together" (Summary)
- "As a service provided at no cost to researchers, an FCA does not guarantee access to the full amount of SUs." (Setting Up an FCA)
- "Postdoctoral Researchers/Scholars, Project Scientsts, Assistant Project Scientists, and Specialists are not considered to be part of the Professional Research Series, and so are not eligible to be the PIs of FCA projects on Savio." (Eligibility)
- "Once you've gone over your Service Unit limit for the year, users of the FCA will no longer be able to run new jobs." (Viewing the Usage of Your Allowance)
- The page asks users to acknowledge Savio. It gives this reason: "acknowledgements are an important factor in helping to justify ongoing funding for, and expansion of, the cluster." (Acknowledgements)

## Evidence and method

A program documentation page of about 1,800 words, most of it site navigation. It states the rules of one campus allocation. It gives no usage figures, no budget and no count of users.

## Limits

The page does not say how many SUs an hour of GPU or CPU time costs. It does not give the price of extra compute time. It does not say who funds the free allowance. The sample acknowledgement names the UC Berkeley Chancellor, Vice Chancellor for Research and Chief Information Officer as supporters. It does not cover users from other UC campuses.
