# Energy Commission backup generating project Charcot Avenue (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Charcot Avenue Backup Generating Facility." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/charcot-avenue-backup-generating-facility. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Charcot Avenue.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Charcot%20Avenue.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Charcot Avenue.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 99 megawatts (MW) for a proposed data center in San Jose. Its status is "Under Review".
- The facility has 44 diesel generators. Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E) serves the data center from its Trimble Substation, which PG&E will expand.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. Prologis, L.P. is the project owner. The docket number is 26-SPPE-03, the capacity is 99 MW and the location is San Jose, California. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The Charcot Avenue Backup Generating Facility (CABGF) would consist of forty-four (44) diesel-fired generators to be used to provide up to 99 megawatts (MW) of backup electricity generation to support the proposed Charcot Avenue Data Center (CADC)." (Project Description)
- "Of the 44 generators, 42 generators would each have a generating capacity of 3 MW and would be dedicated to replacing the electricity needs of the CADC in case of a loss of power from Pacific Gas and Electric Company (PG&E)." (Project Description)
- "The proposed CADC, CABGF, and substation would be located on a 12.88-acre site, at 326 Charcot Avenue in San Jose, California." (Project Description)
- "The CADC would be served electricity from the existing PG&E Trimble Substation located at 44467 Component Drive in the City of San Jose which will be expanded, owned, and operated by PG&E." (Project Description)
- "The Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants with facilities between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)
- "In granting an SPPE, the CEC is not the final approval necessary for construction and operation of a project." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Limits

- The description puts the new substation "adjacent to the SCDC". SCDC is the name that the [Silver Creek project page](Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Silver%20Creek%20%28web%29.md) uses for the Silver Creek Data Center. The page does not correct it.
- The page gives no key dates and no size of the data center building.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
