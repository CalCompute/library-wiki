# Berkeley AI Sandbox models list (web)

## Citation

UC Berkeley Campus AI Platform Team. "UC Berkeley AI Sandbox BETA: Available AI Models." Web page, no date. https://static.ai-sandbox.berkeley.edu/html/models.html. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 Berkeley AI Sandbox models list.md`.

## Bottom line

- The page lists the AI models in the UC Berkeley AI Sandbox BETA. They come from Amazon, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google, Meta, Microsoft, Mistral, OpenAI, Qwen and xAI.
- It gives a per-token price only for the recommended model, Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite. It limits Qwen models to about 2,000 words for each conversation.

## Background

The Campus AI Platform Team at UC Berkeley publishes this page. The AI Sandbox is a web chatbot for the campus. The page links each model to a chat in the Sandbox. The buttons work only for a user who is logged in.

## Key claims

Purpose (top of page):

- "This page lists AI models available through the UC Berkeley AI Sandbox BETA."
- "You must be logged in to the UC Berkeley AI Sandbox BETA for the direct "Start Chat" and "Copy Link" buttons to work properly."

Recommended model (section "Recommended Model"):

- "This recommended model choice offers a good balance between features, performance and cost."
- Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite: "Good choice for general use. • Web search (uncited); Vision; 1M; Jan 2025; $0.10/M in, $0.40/M out"

Open-weight models (section "OpenAI"):

- gpt-oss-120b: "an OpenAI open-weight model available under the Apache 2.0 license."

Limits in this deployment (section "Qwen"):

- "Note: in this deployment Qwen is currently limited to about 2,000 words (~3,500 tokens) per conversation."

Teaching model (section "Eliza"):

- "Eliza is a re-creation of the classic 1966 ELIZA chatbot that uses pattern-matching and scripted transformations to reflect user input."

## Evidence and method

This is a catalog page. It names each model and gives a short description, mostly in the words of each model's vendor. It gives no usage data.

## Limits

- The page does not say who pays for the model use or what the campus spends.
- It does not say where the models run. The [Berkeley Campus AI Sandbox page (web)](Berkeley%20Campus%20AI%20Sandbox%20page%20%28web%29.md) says the Sandbox runs on the UC contract with Amazon Web Services (AWS).
- The page has no date. The list can change without notice.
