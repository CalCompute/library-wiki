# SDSU AI-Ready California launch 2026-07 (web)

## Citation

SDSU News Team. "California launches first-in-the-nation AI literacy initiative with SDSU leadership." San Diego State University (SDSU) News, July 14, 2026. https://www.sdsu.edu/news/2026/07/california-launches-first-in-the-nation-ai-literacy-initiative-with-sdsu-leadership. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- AI-Ready California is a statewide pilot micro-credential in AI literacy. SDSU co-leads it, and students complete it through SDSU Global Campus.
- The pilot starts with 500 participants from the San Diego Community College District and a state reemployment program in the San Diego region.

## Background

SDSU published this news story on July 14, 2026. It describes the launch of AI-Ready California. The California State University (CSU) Industry AI Workforce Acceleration Board and SDSU developed the program. Their partners are the California Labor & Workforce Development Agency, the California Community Colleges, Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Instructure. It quotes the Labor Secretary, the SDSU President, the SDSU chief information officer and the San Diego Community College District chancellor. It ends with SDSU's earlier AI work from 2023 to 2025.

## Key claims

- "San Diego State University is co-leading AI-Ready California, a first-in-the-nation statewide pilot micro-credential program, offered to Californians interested in expanding their AI literacy and preparing to join the workforce of the future." (body)
- "The initiative was developed through the CSU-Industry AI Workforce Acceleration Board and SDSU in partnership with the California Labor & Workforce Development Agency, California Community Colleges, Amazon Web Services (AWS) and Instructure." (body)
- "The initial pilot is launching with 500 participants drawn from both the San Diego Community College District and individuals participating in the Reemployment Services and Eligibility Assessment (RESEA) program in the San Diego region." (body)
- "AI-Ready California, which is launching with support from the James Irvine Foundation, reflects a growing recognition that AI literacy is becoming an essential workforce competency." (Initiative addresses urgent training, educational demands)
- "Upon successful completion, participants will earn an AI literacy micro-credential that can serve as a pathway to additional educational opportunities, workforce development resources and career advancement while demonstrating foundational AI competency to employers." (body)
- "That same year, SDSU took an additional and significant step toward improving access to AI tools with a partnership with the CSU and OpenAI to provide the campus community with no-cost access to ChatGPT Edu" (California initiative builds on years of AI research and training)

## Evidence and method

A university news story. It names the partners, the funder, the delivery unit and the size of the first pilot. It gives no cost, no grant amount, no course length and no outcome data. The program is online and covers "responsible AI use and practical workforce applications".

## Limits

The story does not give the amount of support from the James Irvine Foundation. It does not give a start date for enrollment, a fee or a timeline after the pilot. It does not mention research compute or CalCompute. Enrollment details are on an SDSU Global Campus page that the library has not read.
