# Stanford NSSA SB 129 Budget Act of 2021 (web)

## Citation

National Student Support Accelerator (NSSA), Stanford University. "SB 129 Budget Act of 2021." Entry in the State Tutoring Efforts and Legislation Database, date not stated. https://nssa.stanford.edu/legislation/sb-129-budget-act-2021. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- A database entry on California's SB 129, the Budget Act of 2021. SB 129 funds the California for All College Corps.
- The College Corps gives undergraduates at 46 California colleges and universities $10,000 for one year of service. The Governor signed SB 129 on July 12, 2021.

## Background

The NSSA is a Stanford University program on high-impact tutoring. Its State Tutoring Efforts and Legislation Database lists state laws and programs about tutoring. This entry is about the College Corps because about half of its students tutor or mentor.

## Key claims

- "Includes funding for the California for All College Corps which provides undergraduate students at 46 colleges and universities across California the opportunity to earn $10,000 for committing to one year of service focused on three key issue areas for the state: K-12 education (tutoring and mentoring), climate action and food insecurity." (entry text)
- "Plans are for 3,250 students to be deployed in the 2022-23 school year with approximately 50% being tutors or mentors." (entry text)
- "Status: Signed by Governor" and "Date of Status: 07/12/2021" (entry fields)

## Evidence and method

A secondary database entry of about 100 words. It summarises the program and links the bill text and the College Corps page. It does not quote the bill.

## Limits

The entry gives plans for 2022-23, not results. It does not state the amount SB 129 gives the College Corps. The library does not have the bill text. The row on the Suggestions page names the publisher "National Service and Social Action"; the page itself names the National Student Support Accelerator.
