# CalHR 2026 employer health contributions by bargaining unit (web)

## Citation

California Department of Human Resources (CalHR). "Health Benefits." CalHR Benefits, date not stated. https://benefits.calhr.ca.gov/state-employees/general-benefits/health. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CalHR 2026 employer health contributions by bargaining unit.html`.

## Bottom line

- The page gives the monthly amount that the state pays toward a state employee's health premium, by bargaining unit. It gives 2027, and 2026 in its archive.
- In 2026 the employer health contribution is $867 to $1,032 for single cover and $2,249 to $2,414 for family cover. In 2027 it is $902 to $1,067 and $2,335 to $2,500.

## Background

CalHR administers dental, vision and voluntary plan benefits for state employees. The California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS) administers their health cover. Seven bargaining units and excluded employees get a Consolidated Benefits (CoBen) allowance in place of the employer health contribution.

## Key claims

- "The California Public Employees’ Retirement System (CalPERS) administers health insurance coverage for state employees and retirees." (Health)
- "Employees are eligible for health benefits if they have a permanent or limited term appointment of more than six months (at least six months plus one day) and a time base of half-time or more." (Eligibility and Enrollment)
- 2026 Employer Health Contributions by Bargaining Unit: "1 | $1,032 | $1,897 | $2,414" and "6 | $867 | $1,732 | $2,249" (Bargaining Unit, Single, 2-Party, Family; Archive)
- 2027 Employer Health Contributions by Bargaining Unit: "1 | $1,067 | $1,965 | $2,500" and "6 | $902 | $1,800 | $2,335" (Health Premiums and Employer Contributions by Bargaining Unit)
- "**Note**: The health contributions for Bargaining Units 1, 3, 4, 11, 14, 15, 20 and 21 (SEIU units) include an additional $165 supplemental health benefits contribution." (Health Premiums and Employer Contributions by Bargaining Unit)
- "Rank-and-file employees in Bargaining Units 2, 7, 8, 16, 17, 18, 19 and excluded employees who are eligible for health benefits are eligible for the CoBen allowance." (Health Premiums and Employer Contributions by Bargaining Unit)
- 2026 CoBen Allowances by Bargaining Unit: "Excluded | $968 | $1,870 | $2,417" (Archive)
- "These contribution amounts are subject to collective bargaining." (Health Premiums and Employer Contributions by Bargaining Unit)

## Evidence and method

An official state benefits page. It gives four tables of monthly amounts: employer health contributions and CoBen allowances, for 2027 and for 2026. It links the premiums to the CalPERS plans and rates page.

## Limits

The page does not give the health premiums, the share of the premium that the state pays, or the bargaining unit of each IT class. For Bargaining Unit 3, the 2027 row shows the 2026 rates; a footnote says this is "due to collective bargaining". The page has no date. Its 2027 tables are on the page with the 2026 tables, which the page calls its archive. It does not cover dental, vision or pension costs. The state pension rates are in [CalPERS circular letter 200-022-26 state contribution rates 2026-27 (web)](CalPERS%20circular%20letter%20200-022-26%20state%20contribution%20rates%202026-27%20%28web%29.md).
