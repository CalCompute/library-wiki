# World Bank Morocco digital transformation release 2026-06-12 (web)

## Citation

World Bank. "Two New Programs to Advance Morocco's Digital Transformation and Climate Resilience." Press release, Washington, June 12, 2026. https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/06/12/two-new-programs-to-advance-morocco-s-digital-transformation-and-climate-resilience. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The World Bank's Board approved two programs for Morocco with a total of $650 million.
- The $250 million Morocco Digital Transformation Acceleration Program supports the Digital Morocco 2030 strategy. It covers public digital services, cloud systems for government, AI centres of excellence, startup finance and digital skills.
- The $400 million Morocco Climate & Risk Finance Program covers climate, disaster and cyber risk finance.

## Background

The World Bank is an international lender to governments. This release announces two programs, with project numbers P513936 (digital) and P511813 (climate and risk). It quotes the World Bank Division Director for the Maghreb and Malta.

## Key claims

- Total: "The World Bank's Board of Directors has approved two new programs totaling $650 million". (paragraph 1)
- Digital program: "The $250 million Morocco Digital Transformation Acceleration Program will provide catalytic financing supporting the national Digital Morocco 2030 strategy." (paragraph 2)
- Scope: it will "support the government's transition to cloud systems, strengthen financing and capacity-building for the startup ecosystem, advance AI innovation". (paragraph 2)
- Cloud and AI: "The program will support public administrations in adopting cloud-based solutions for new IT investments, advance AI-based digital innovation capacity through centers of excellence". (paragraph 3)
- Results date: the program targets "measurable results by 2031". (paragraph 3)
- Private capital: "Through government-supported risk-sharing mechanisms, it is expected to mobilize close to $200 million in private capital for startup financing and MSMEs digitization." (paragraph 3)
- Risk program: "The $400 million Morocco Climate & Risk Finance Program will strengthen Morocco's financial resilience against climate, disaster, and cyber risks". (paragraph 4)

## Evidence and method

This is a press release from an international lender. It gives the amounts, the aims and the expected private capital. It does not give the loan terms.

## Limits

- The release does not say how much of the $250 million goes to cloud or AI.
- It does not describe AI compute infrastructure.
- It does not give the terms of the financing.
