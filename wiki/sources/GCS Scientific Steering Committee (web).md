# GCS Scientific Steering Committee (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing e.V. (GCS). "Scientific Steering Committee." Web page, date not stated. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us/organization/scientific-steering-committee. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The GCS Scientific Steering Committee awards computing time on the GCS high-performance computing (HPC) systems to large-scale projects. A peer review on scientific and technical excellence decides.
- It has three members for each of the three national centres, and six permanent guests.

## Background

GCS joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres: HLRS in Stuttgart, JSC in Jülich and LRZ in Garching ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)). This page states what the Scientific Steering Committee does and lists its current members. [GCS organization (web)](GCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md) says the committee also advises the GCS Council.

## Key claims

- "The scientific steering committee of GCS awards computer time grants on the GCS HPC systems to scientifically excellent GCS large-scale projects." (Scientific Steering Committee)
- "The projects are evaluated by a peer-review process which is strictly based on their scientific and technical excellence." (Scientific Steering Committee)
- "Chairman: Prof. Dr. Marcus Müller, University of Göttingen" and "Vice Chairman: Prof. Dr. Peter Bastian, Heidelberg University" (member list)
- "Prof. Dr.-Ing. Michael M. Resch, Director of HLRS, Chairman of the Board of GCS" (Permanent guests)
- "Dr. Claus Axel Müller, Managing Director of GCS" (Permanent guests)

## Evidence and method

A membership list on the publisher's own website. It groups the members by centre:

- HLRS: Thomas Ludwig (German Climate Computing Centre), Roland Potthast (German Weather Service) and Thomas Bönisch (HLRS);
- JSC: Marcus Müller (University of Göttingen), Dietrich Wolf (University of Duisburg) and Wolfgang Frings (JSC);
- LRZ: Peter Bastian (Heidelberg University), Gerhard Wellein (University of Erlangen) and Gerald Mathias (LRZ).

In each group, two members come from outside the centre and one from the centre itself. The permanent guests are the three centre directors, the GCS Managing Director and two staff of the GCS Coordination Office.

## Limits

- The page has no date.
- It does not say how large a "large-scale project" must be. It does not say how often calls open or how many hours the committee awards.
- It does not say how GCS chooses the members or how long they serve.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This page names Michael M. Resch "Chairman of the Board of GCS". [GCS organization (web)](GCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md) says "The current Chairman of the Board is Dieter Kranzlmüller (LRZ)", with Resch as a vice-chair. One of the two GCS pages is out of date.
