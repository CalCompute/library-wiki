# CSU statement on student assistants vote to join union (web)

## Citation

California State University. "CSU Statement on Student Assistants Vote to Join Employees Union." News statement, no date. https://calstate.edu/csu-system/news/Pages/CSU-Statement-on-Student-Assistants-Vote-to-Join-Employees-Union.aspx. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The California State University (CSU) says that its student assistants voted to join the California State University Employees Union (CSUEU). CSU employs nearly 20,000 student assistants.
- CSU will begin bargaining with the new unit. Until an agreement, the student assistant classification keeps its current standards and requirements.

## Background

The statement is on CSU's news pages and gives a quotation from the Vice Chancellor for Human Resources. CSUEU also represents CSU's Units 2, 5, 7 and 9 ([CSU CSUEU contract Units 2 5 7 9 2023-2026 (web)](CSU%20CSUEU%20contract%20Units%202%205%207%209%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "California State University (CSU) students who work on-campus jobs under the student assistant classification have voted to join the California State University Employees Union (CSUEU)."
- "With nearly 20,000 student assistants employed by the CSU, the new unit will be one of the nation’s largest represented groups of predominantly undergraduate student workers."
- "The CSU will begin bargaining with CSUEU’s student assistant unit in the near future. Until a final agreement is reached, the student assistant classification will maintain current standards and requirements."
- "The CSU respects the decision of student assistants to form a union and looks forward to bargaining in good faith with the newly formed CSUEU student assistant unit." (statement of the Vice Chancellor for Human Resources)

## Evidence and method

A short news statement of the employer. It gives one count, the number of student assistants, and no source for it.

## Limits

- The page gives no date for the statement or the vote.
- It gives no unit number, no vote count and no term of any agreement. The library holds no agreement for the student assistant unit.
