# Santa Clara University Sobrato 100 million gift 2017 (web)

## Citation

Santa Clara University. "$100 Million Gift from John A. and Susan Sobrato Launches Sobrato Campus for Discovery and Innovation at Santa Clara University." Press release, Santa Clara, Calif., January 21, 2017. https://www.scu.edu/news-and-events/press-releases/2017/january-2017/100-million-gift-from-john-a-and-susan-sobrato-launches-sobrato-campus-for-discovery-and-innovation-at-santa-clara-university-.html. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- John A. and Susan Sobrato gave $100 million to Santa Clara University for the Sobrato Campus for Discovery and Innovation. It is a building for undergraduate STEM education.
- It is the largest gift in the university's history. The release also lists earlier Sobrato gifts in Silicon Valley.

## Background

Santa Clara University is a Jesuit, Catholic university in Santa Clara, California. John A. Sobrato is a graduate of the university and a member of its Board of Trustees. He founded The Sobrato Organization, a commercial real estate developer. The couple and their three children started the Sobrato Family Foundation in 1996.

## Key claims

- "The University’s largest-ever gift will fund a 300,000-square-foot, state-of-the-art facility for transformational STEM education." (subheading)
- "The gift is the largest in Santa Clara’s 166-year history and ranks as the second-largest gift ever to a Catholic university." (paragraph 2)
- "Sue and I and our son John Michael have agreed to leave 100 percent of our wealth to the Sobrato Family Foundation to invest in organizations that improve the lives of the less fortunate in the greater Silicon Valley community and to institutions like Santa Clara University" (John A. Sobrato)
- The facility "will allow for the integration of the various engineering departments with the natural science disciplines of the College of Arts and Sciences" (John A. Sobrato)
- "Since 1996, the Sobrato Family has donated nearly $375 million in cash and real estate to educational, health, human services, and other charities." (biography)
- The foundation "runs three Sobrato Centers for Nonprofits, which provide 333,000 square feet of rent-free office space to 72 organizations" (biography)

## Evidence and method

A university press release with quotations from the donors and from the university president, Michael Engh, S.J. It gives the purpose and size of the building. Earlier gifts that it lists:

- $20 million to the university for the Harrington Learning Commons, Sobrato Technology Center and Orradre Library, completed in 2008.
- $20 million to Lucile Packard Children's Hospital.
- $10 million to Bellarmine College Preparatory.
- $5 million each to Valley Medical Center and National Hispanic University.

## Limits

The release does not give a schedule for the gift or a completion date for the building. It does not mention computing infrastructure or AI. It is from 2017, so its figures for the family's giving and the firm's buildings are of that date.
