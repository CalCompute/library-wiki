# UC AI Council roster (web)

## Citation

University of California Office of the President (UCOP). "AI Council Roster." Web page in the AI Communities section of the University of California Artificial Intelligence website, date not stated. https://ai.universityofcalifornia.edu/ai-communities/roster.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-09 UC AI Council roster.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-09%20UC%20AI%20Council%20roster.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The roster lists 35 people on the University of California (UC) AI Council, each with a short biography.
- The council has two co-chairs. Alex Bui is Director of the Medical & Imaging Informatics group at UCLA. Alexander Bustamante is UC's Senior Vice President and Chief Compliance and Audit Officer.
- Members' titles include chief information officers, privacy officers, counsel, procurement directors, compliance staff and professors.

## Background

The page is in the AI Communities section of the UC AI website. It has two parts. The first part, "Council Co-Chairs, Subcommittee Co-Chairs, Senior Advisors", has nine entries. The second part, "Members", has 28 entries. Two people, Shanda Hunt and Jennifer Lofthus, appear in both parts, so the page names 35 people.

The first part holds these roles, as the headings and biographies give them:

- two council co-chairs, Alex Bui and Alexander Bustamante;
- three subcommittee co-chairs: Joe Sabado (UC Santa Barbara) for Innovation and Impact, and Rolin Moe (UCOP) and Zainab Shakoor (UC Davis) for Risk and Transparency;
- Van Williams, UC Vice President of Information Technology Services and Chief Information Officer, as senior advisor to the Innovation and Impact subcommittee;
- three staff of the UCOP Office of Ethics, Compliance and Audit Services as advisors and special assistant.

The "Members" part marks two people as ex officio. They are Cora Han, Chief Health Data Officer for UC Health, and Hillary Noll Kalay of the UC Office of General Counsel.

## Key claims

- "Mr. Bustamante co-chaired the UC Presidential Working Group on Artificial Intelligence, which established guidelines for the responsible and ethical use of AI applications within the UC system." (Alexander Bustamante, Council Co-Chair)
- "He continues to serve as the co-chair of UC’s AI Council, tasked with integrating these guidelines, developing AI risk and impact assessment strategies, and documenting the University's use of AI-enabled technologies." (Alexander Bustamante, Council Co-Chair)
- "Rolin serves as Co-Chair of the Risk and Transparency subcommittee." (Rolin Moe, Associate Vice Provost for UC Online, Subcommittee Co-Chair)
- "Joe serves as the Co-Chair of the Subcommittee on Innovation and Impact." (Joe Sabado, Deputy CIO, Subcommittee Co-Chair)
- "VP Williams serves as senior advisor to the Council Subcommittee on Innovation and Impact bringing expertise from his systemwide leadership in AI enabled technology adoption." (Van Williams, UC VP IT and CIO, Senior Advisor)
- "Shanda was a founding member of the UC Presidential Working Group on Artificial Intelligence and has worked in the AI ethics and compliance space at UC since 2019." (Shanda Hunt, Director of Compliance, Senior Strategic Advisor)
- "Gigi Capuyan serves as UC’s systemwide procurement counsel." (Gigi Capuyan)

## Evidence and method

An institutional roster with biographies. Each entry gives a name and one or more paragraphs of career history. The page gives no terms of office, appointment process, meeting schedule or charter. This library page does not copy the full list of members.

## Limits

The page does not say who chooses the members or how long they serve. It does not say which subcommittee most members sit on. It names the Risk and Transparency subcommittee, but the [UC AI Council subcommittees (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20subcommittees%20%28web%29.md) page uses other names for the subcommittees. The page does not mention compute, funding or CalCompute.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This roster says two people co-chair the "Risk and Transparency subcommittee". The [UC AI Council subcommittees (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20subcommittees%20%28web%29.md) page names "Risk and Transparency; and Awareness and Education" as two subcommittees in its first paragraph, then describes one "Subcommittee on Risk, Transparency, and Awareness". The [UC AI governance and transparency (web)](UC%20AI%20governance%20and%20transparency%20%28web%29.md) page names an "AI Council’s Subcommittee on Transparency".
