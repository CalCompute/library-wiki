# Hochul Empire AI expansion 2025-05-09 (web)

## Citation

Office of the Governor of New York. "Governor Hochul Signs Legislation Expanding Historic Empire AI Consortium and Establishing Nation-Leading AI Protections for Responsible Growth as Part of the FY 2026 Budget." Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Hochul Empire AI expansion 2025-05-09.html`.

## Bottom line

- The release says that the FY 2026 New York budget gives "$90 million in capital funding" to Empire AI.
- The money is to increase computing power, expand access for SUNY researchers and add three new members.
- The release also announces AI workforce, small business and state workforce programs, and rules for AI companions.

## Background

The Office of the Governor of New York published this release on May 9, 2025. It reports laws that Governor Kathy Hochul signed as part of the FY 2026 Enacted Budget. Empire AI started in the FY 2025 budget. The release names Empire State Development Corporation (ESD) and the Department of Labor (DOL) as the agencies for some programs.

## Key claims

- Funding: "The Governor today secured $90 million in capital funding to substantially increase the computing power of Empire AI, expand access for SUNY researchers, and support the addition of new members including the University of Rochester, the Rochester Institute of Technology, and the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai." (Expand Historic Empire AI Consortium)
- Origin: "Last year, Governor Hochul reached historic agreement with the state legislature as part of the FY25 Budget to establish Empire AI, a first-of-its-kind independent consortium to secure New York’s place at the forefront of artificial intelligence research." (Expand Historic Empire AI Consortium)
- Next system: "Now, Governor Hochul’s secured expansion will allow Empire AI to secure a future full-scale computing system that supports expanded capacity for SUNY and the addition of new members." (Expand Historic Empire AI Consortium)
- Workforce: "ESD will launch AI Prep: a workforce development initiative focused on the training and placement of disadvantaged college students into AI jobs." (Diversify the Artificial Intelligence Pipeline With AI Prep)
- Startups: the Governor will launch "a public-private partnership with IBM and Armory Square Ventures to invest $20 million into New York AI start-ups." (Increase Capital Access for Underrepresented Startups)
- State workforce: "Training will provide basic education about how AI can be safely used in the workplace, and will be complemented with a secure generative AI toolset to help State employees with hands-on application of the training." (Provide Artificial Intelligence Upskilling for State Workforce)
- Layoff notices: "DOL has required businesses submitting notices of worker layoffs to convey whether a layoff is related to a businesses’ use of AI or other technological change." (Support Workers Displaced by AI)

## Evidence and method

This is a government press release. It states budget amounts and program names. It lists three current Empire AI research projects. It gives no budget table, no bill numbers and no data on use.

## Limits

- The release does not give the bill or the section of law for the Empire AI funding.
- It does not say how the $90 million divides between computing power, SUNY access and new members.
- It does not describe the "future full-scale computing system" in technical terms.
- It does not give the cost or the schedule of AI Prep or of the state workforce training.
