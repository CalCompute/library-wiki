# NSF 26-512 AI Datasets solicitation (web)

## Citation

U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF). "NSF 26-512: Unlocking Dataset Value for AI-Enabled Scientific Discovery (AI Datasets)." Program solicitation, posted July 21, 2026. https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/ai-datasets-unlocking-dataset-value-ai-enabled-scientific-discovery/nsf26-512/solicitation. Read 2026-11-11. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 NSF 26-512 AI Datasets solicitation.html`.

## Bottom line

- NSF 26-512 funds work that makes existing scientific datasets more usable by artificial intelligence (AI). It does not fund new data collection.
- NSF expects 25 to 50 awards and $60,000,000 to $100,000,000 in total. There are three award types: Planning, Impact and Flagship.
- Proposals must address dataset governance, security and integrity. NSF encourages them to use existing infrastructure, such as the NSF-led National AI Research Resource (NAIRR).

## Background

Six NSF directorates and the Office of Research Infrastructure run this program together. NSF posted it on July 21, 2026, with a full proposal deadline of Nov. 4, 2026. The NSF update on the NAIRR Operations Center names this program as a partner of the Operations Center ([NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)](NSF%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20update%20%28web%29.md)). A University of Rhode Island summary covers it with NSF 26-513 ([URI IACR NSF 26-512 and 26-513 summary (web)](URI%20IACR%20NSF%2026-512%20and%2026-513%20summary%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "The program anticipates 10 to 20 Impact awards of up to $2,000,000. In addition, the program also anticipates 5 to 10 Flagship awards of up $5M and 10 to 20 Planning grants up to $200,000" (Award Information)
- "Anticipated Funding Amount: $60,000,000 to $100,000,000" (Award Information)
- "Proposals are encouraged to leverage existing resources. These may include NSF data platforms, the NSF Integrated Data Systems and Services program, the NSF-led National AI Research Resource, the Genesis Mission platform, or other national infrastructure." (Synopsis of Program)
- "NSF is open to exploring partnerships with philanthropy, private industry, or the non-profit sector to support additional proposals or collaborative opportunities" (Synopsis of Program)
- "All proposals should also address dataset governance. Governance includes dataset access, retirement policies and the community contribution process." (II. Program Description)
- "Research to collect de novo datasets is out of scope and remains in the domain of individual scientific directorates." (I. Introduction)
- "Two- and four-year IHEs (including community colleges) accredited in, and having a campus located in the US, acting on behalf of their faculty members." (IV. Eligibility Information)
- "Inclusion of voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited." (Budgetary Information)

## Evidence and method

A federal funding solicitation. It states the program's aims, award sizes, eligibility, proposal sections, review criteria and deadline. It links the program to America's AI Action Plan and the Genesis Mission. Sections I to V were read in full for this page.

## Limits

- The award amounts are "subject to the availability of funds".
- The solicitation lists no awards. The first deadline was Nov. 4, 2026.
- It does not fund compute hardware. It expects proposals to use existing platforms.
