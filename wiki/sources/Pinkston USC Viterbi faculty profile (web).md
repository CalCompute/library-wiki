# Pinkston USC Viterbi faculty profile (web)

## Citation

University of Southern California (USC) Viterbi School of Engineering. "Timothy M. Pinkston." Faculty directory profile, date not stated. https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Pinkston/Timothy. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- Timothy M. Pinkston holds the George Pfleger Chair in Electrical and Computer Engineering and is a Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at USC.
- He heads the SMART Interconnects Group, which does research on interconnection networks for parallel computer systems. He served as a National Science Foundation (NSF) Program Director from 2005 to 2008.

## Background

This is his profile in the USC Viterbi faculty directory. It gives his degrees, a biography, a research summary and a list of awards. He joined USC in 1993, after work at Bell Laboratories, Hughes Research Laboratory and IBM. The biography states that he is the Vice Dean for Faculty Affairs at USC Viterbi.

## Key claims

- "He also is the Vice Dean for Faculty Affairs in the USC Viterbi School of Engineering." (Biography)
- "With over a hundred peer-reviewed technical publications, he has made key research contributions to deadlock-free adaptive routing, router microarchitecture and interconnection networks (both distributed and on-chip networks) that achieve high-performance and energy-efficient data movement in multicore and multiprocessor computer systems--from embedded processors to compute servers to large-scale datacenters." (Biography)
- "He served three years (2005-2008) as an NSF Program Director in the CISE Directorate, serving the last year of his stint as the inaugural Lead Program Director for the newly established Expeditions in Computing Program." (Biography)
- "a Board Member of the Computing Research Association (CRA), a member of the NSF Computer and Information Science and Engineering (CISE) Advisory Committee (from May 2021)" (Biography)
- "The SMART Interconnects Group, headed by Timothy Pinkston, is engaged in research on technologies and techniques for achieving high-performance communication in parallel computer systems" (Research Summary)
- "Dr. Pinkston is a AAAS Fellow, ACM Fellow, and IEEE Fellow." (Biography)

## Evidence and method

A standard faculty directory profile, in the third person. It gives his degrees with years. He has a B.S.E.E. from The Ohio State University (1985), and an M.S., 1986, and a Ph.D., 1993, from Stanford University. Its awards list runs from 1984 to 2024. It links a curriculum vitae, a research website and a publications list. The library has read none of them.

## Limits

The page has no date, so some posts can be out of date. It does not say whether his NSF CISE Advisory Committee term continues. It does not describe computing resources at USC.
