# NERSC Code of Conduct (web)

## Citation

National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC), Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. "NERSC Code of Conduct." Web page, date not stated. https://www.nersc.gov/users/become-a-nersc-user/user-agreements/nersc-code-of-conduct. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-12 NERSC Code of Conduct.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-12%20NERSC%20Code%20of%20Conduct.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The NERSC Code of Conduct sets the behavior rules for the NERSC community. It uses five Berkeley Lab Stewardship Values: Team Science, Service, Trust, Innovation and Respect.
- It lists inappropriate behavior, such as harassment, stalking and recording a person without consent. Actions "may include formal warnings and even dismissal from the activity or the community".
- Reports can be anonymous or personal, through University of California (UC) and Berkeley Lab channels.

## Background

NERSC publishes this code on its "User Agreements" pages. The [NERSC user account policies (web)](NERSC%20user%20account%20policies%20%28web%29.md) say that a user must agree to this code to keep an account. The code links to the UC Standards of Ethical Conduct and to a UC anti-discrimination policy. It gives no date.

## Key claims

- Values: "As community members of NERSC, we agree to incorporate the following Berkeley Lab Stewardship Values into our decisions and behaviors to continue fostering a culture where everyone feels safe and welcome to participate." (Code of Conduct)
- Ethics: "We behave ethically, in alignment with the UC Standards of Ethical Conduct." (Trust)
- Inappropriate behavior: "Inappropriate behavior includes but is not limited to, inappropriate physical contact, unwanted sexual misconduct, intimidation, stalking, harassment, photography or recording of an individual without consent, sustained disruption of talks or other events, or other problematic conduct." (Code of Conduct)
- Enforcement: "NERSC retains the right to take any reasonable action to maintain a welcoming environment for all. Actions for noncompliance or continued misbehavior may include formal warnings and even dismissal from the activity or the community." (Code of Conduct)
- Reports: "You can make a report either anonymously or by identifying the reporter." (Reporting misconduct)
- Confidentiality: "Confidentiality will be maintained to the extent permissible, but cannot be guaranteed." (Personal report)

## Evidence and method

This is a code of conduct. It gives values, examples of inappropriate behavior and reporting routes. It has no data on reports or actions.

## Limits

- The code does not say who decides on an action, or how.
- It does not give steps between a warning and dismissal.
- It does not separate rules for staff from rules for users.
- It does not give a date of publication or revision.
