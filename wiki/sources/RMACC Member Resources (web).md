# RMACC Member Resources (web)

## Citation

Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium (RMACC). "Member Resources." Web page, University of Colorado Boulder, date not stated. https://www.colorado.edu/partnerships/rmacc/sharedresources. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The RMACC member resources page lists one system: Alpine, a cluster at the University of Colorado (CU) Boulder.
- CU Boulder, CU Anschutz, Colorado State University and the National Science Foundation (NSF) fund Alpine together, under NSF award 2201538.

## Background

This is a short page on the RMACC website, at the address "sharedresources". It gives the hardware of Alpine in one sentence and its funders in a second. It links the Alpine user documentation.

## Key claims

- "Alpine is a heterogeneous supercomputing cluster based primarily on the AMD EPYC “Milan” CPUs with Nvidia A100 GPUs; AMD MI100 GPUs; HDR InfiniBand; and 25 Gb Ethernet." (Alpine- CU Boulder)
- "Alpine is jointly funded by the University of Colorado Boulder, the University of Colorado Anschutz, Colorado State University, and the National Science Foundation (award 2201538)." (Alpine- CU Boulder)
- "To learn more about how to use Alpine, including information on logging in, modules, running jobs, and file systems, visit our Alpine documentation for more details." (Alpine- CU Boulder)

## Evidence and method

A short resource page with two facts: hardware types and funders. It gives no node counts, no capacity and no access rules.

## Limits

The page does not say which RMACC members can use Alpine, on what terms or at what cost. It does not give the size of the system or the amount from each funder. The library has not read the linked Alpine documentation. [RMACC About us (web)](RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md) lists the member institutions.
