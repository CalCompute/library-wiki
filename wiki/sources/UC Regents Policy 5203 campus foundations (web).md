# UC Regents Policy 5203 campus foundations (web)

## Citation

Regents of the University of California. "Regents Policy 5203: Policy on Support Groups, Campus Foundations, and Alumni Associations." Approved September 15, 1995; amended September 22, 2005. https://regents.universityofcalifornia.edu/governance/policies/5203.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-05 UC Regents Policy 5203 campus foundations.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-05%20UC%20Regents%20Policy%205203%20campus%20foundations.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- A nonprofit that raises funds or gives support for UC must have "official and continued recognition from a Chancellor". Without it, it may not use UC's name, facilities or personnel.
- Each campus may have one Campus Foundation, "the only entity permitted by The Regents of the University of California to hold funds for investment purposes for the benefit of the campus it supports". No such entity may conduct research or employ or pay a UC employee outside UC policy.

## Background

The policy defines four kinds of outside entity: Support Groups, Campus Foundations, Campus Alumni Associations and Affiliated Organizations. It sets the rules for recognition, asset dedication, research and personnel, and annual reporting to the Senior Vice President, Business and Finance. The President may grant exceptions and must report them to the Regents.

## Key claims

- "Accordingly, each Support Group, Campus Foundation, or Alumni Association must receive official and continued recognition from a Chancellor. Official recognition establishes the privileges and responsibilities of the relationship between these entities and the University, including use of the University's name, facilities, and resources."
- "Without such recognition, no group may (a) represent itself as raising funds or otherwise providing support on behalf of or for the benefit of the University, a campus, or any institutional unit thereof; (b) use the name of the University or any of its campuses, facilities, or programs either expressly or by implication in connection with such activities; or (c) use University facilities, equipment, or personnel in connection with such activities."
- "The assets of each such entity must be irrevocably dedicated for the benefit of the University of California or one of its campuses."
- "No such entity may conduct research or clinical trials or accept funds for contract research by a named researcher on behalf of the University. Such agreements must be entered into directly by the University and the research sponsor. Additionally, no such entity may (a) directly or indirectly employ, or (b) supplement the salary of, or (c) provide any consulting fees, loans, or perquisites to any University employee outside of established University of California personnel policies and practices."
- "Campus Foundations: Each campus may have a single Campus Foundation, bearing the name of the campus, that is organized and operated for the purpose of fostering private giving, managing gift and endowment funds, and providing other support for the benefit of the campus. The Campus Foundation is the only entity permitted by The Regents of the University of California to hold funds for investment purposes for the benefit of the campus it supports." (Definitions, 2)
- "Affiliated Organizations: Under special circumstances, when an entity does not fit the above definitions, a campus may enter into an affiliation, for a defined time period, by written agreement. Such an agreement may permit the use of the University's name, facilities, personnel, and other resources in return for specified benefits received by the University." (Definitions, 4)

## Evidence and method

A governing policy of the Regents. It is the rule, not a description of practice.

## Limits

The policy covers entities that support UC. It does not cover a consortium or state program of which UC is a member. The Administrative Guidelines it refers to are separate documents the library has not read. The 2005 amendment is the latest shown; the page does not show later changes.
