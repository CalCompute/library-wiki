# Hochul Empire AI Beta fully online (web)

## Citation

Office of the Governor of New York. "Governor Hochul Announces Empire AI Beta Fully Online as Federal Government Takes Inspiration From New York to Launch State and Regional AI Infrastructure Hubs." Press release, August 5, 2026. https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-empire-ai-beta-fully-online-federal-government-takes-inspiration-new. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Hochul Empire AI Beta fully online.html`.

## Bottom line

- The release says that Empire AI Beta, a "$40 million NVIDIA-powered supercomputer" at the University at Buffalo, is online.
- It says that "over 300 research projects" wait to use Beta. The full-scale Gamma facility is to be complete "by the end of 2027".
- The release says that Empire AI is a model for the NSF State and Regional AI Infrastructure Hubs.

## Background

The Office of the Governor of New York published this release on August 5, 2026. It reports the start of the second Empire AI system, Beta, after Alpha in 2024. It quotes the Governor, the Empire AI Board Chairman and the Empire AI Research Computing Director. It also quotes the State University of New York (SUNY) and City University of New York (CUNY) chancellors and three legislators. It lists the ten member institutions.

## Key claims

- System: "Housed at the State University of New York at Buffalo, Empire AI Beta is a $40 million NVIDIA-powered supercomputer that dramatically expands the computing power available to academic researchers across New York State." (paragraph after the quotations)
- Capacity: "The system delivers an 11-fold increase in AI training capacity, a 40-fold boost in AI inference and an 8-fold expansion in data storage compared to Empire AI Alpha, the consortium’s initial system launched in 2024." (same paragraph)
- Demand: "With over 300 research projects already queued up to use the system, Beta will accelerate work across fields including health care, climate science, advanced manufacturing, education, cybersecurity, public safety and other areas that directly benefit New Yorkers." (same paragraph)
- Phases: "Alpha, the consortium’s initial system made possible by philanthropic support from the Simons Foundation, has already supported more than 130 research projects and hundreds of researchers across New York." (phased buildout paragraph)
- Gamma: construction continues "on Empire AI’s permanent, full-scale Gamma facility at the University at Buffalo, which is expected to be completed by the end of 2027." (phased buildout paragraph)
- Heat reuse: "process heat from Empire AI will be used to heat buildings on campus, dramatically improving the school’s ability to meet net zero goals." (Gamma facility paragraph)
- Funding: "Empire AI is backed by more than $500 million in public and private funding, and is made up of 10 member universities and research institutions." (funding paragraph)
- Federal model: "With Empire AI Beta fully online, New York is already delivering on the computing power, institutional partnership and research capacity that NSF is looking to replicate." (State of the State paragraph)
- Board chair: "New York is setting the standard for how the United States can build and maintain AI infrastructure by researchers and for researchers." (Tom Secunda quotation)

## Evidence and method

This is a government press release. It gives the system cost, capacity ratios against Alpha, project counts, the member list and the funding total. It gives no GPU count, no allocation rules and no operating budget. The claim that Empire AI is a model for NSF is the release's own claim. The release links the NSF hubs announcement but does not quote NSF.

## Limits

- The release does not give the number or type of GPUs in Beta. For the hardware, see [Hochul Empire AI Beta launch 2025-06-26 (web)](Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20Beta%20launch%202025-06-26%20%28web%29.md).
- It does not say how member institutions share Beta time or how projects enter the queue.
- It does not divide the "more than $500 million" between public and private sources.
- It does not give the cost of the Gamma facility.
