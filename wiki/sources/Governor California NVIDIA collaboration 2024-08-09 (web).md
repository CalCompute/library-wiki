# Governor California NVIDIA collaboration 2024-08-09 (web)

Office of the Governor of California. "California, NVIDIA launch first-of-its-kind AI collaboration." ## Bottom line

- Governor Newsom and NVIDIA's chief executive signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) on AI training, job creation and AI uses for Californians.
- Its main focus is the California Community Colleges. NVIDIA "aims to" give technical guidance and access to AI hardware, software and cloud resources for education and research.

## Background

The Office of the Governor issued this release in Santa Clara. It lists the three aims of the initiative and the MOU's objectives under four headings. It quotes the Governor, NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang and the Chancellor of the California Community Colleges. It links the MOU and the Governor's earlier GenAI actions.

## Key claims

- "NVIDIA aims to provide technical guidance, mentorship, and access to advanced AI hardware and software resources to support cutting-edge research initiatives." (Harness AI to grow the economy & create jobs)
- "California and NVIDIA plan to collaborate on creating AI laboratories in higher education facilities that can be equipped to meet the evolving needs of AI education and research." (Training for students, educators, and workers)
- "California will fund AI worker training initiatives across educational institutions and industries and work with NVIDIA to develop faculty programs to improve AI literacy and curriculum." (Training for students, educators, and workers)
- "California will adopt skills and training for state careers, including new roles for AI specialists in government." (Training for students, educators, and workers)
- "NVIDIA to help enable access to cutting-edge AI hardware, software, and cloud computing resources for educational and research initiatives." (Promote statewide innovation, real-world applications)
- "California’s world-class University of California and California State University systems will also partner with the state on these efforts in the future." (body)

## Evidence and method

A government press release that summarizes an MOU. Its objectives use the words "aims to", "plan to", "strives to" and "will explore". It gives no dollar amount, hardware quantity, term or schedule. The library has not read the MOU itself. The release links it through a mailing-list tracking address.

## Limits

The release does not say how much compute NVIDIA gives, or to whom. It does not say what the state's funding for worker training is. The release names the University of California and the California State University only as future partners. The release does not mention CalCompute. The Community Colleges figure of "more than 2 million students" is a quotation from the Chancellor, with no source.
