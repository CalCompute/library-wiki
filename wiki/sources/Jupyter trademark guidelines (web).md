# Jupyter trademark guidelines (web)

## Citation

Project Jupyter. "Trademark Usage Policy." Web page in the Jupyter Governance site, date not stated. https://jupyter.org/governance/trademarks/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- "Jupyter" and the Jupyter logos are trademarks of LF Charities, of which Project Jupyter is a part.
- The policy is "very permissive": anyone can use the name to refer to Jupyter, without approval, for commercial or non-commercial use.
- Any commercial use of the marks in a product or company name needs approval first. Names such as "Jupyter Cloud" will be refused.

## Background

Project Jupyter publishes this policy on its governance site. It is based on the trademark policy of the Python Software Foundation. A Trademark Subcommittee of four people answers questions. The policy covers derivative marks such as "JupyterLab" and "JupyterHub".

## Key claims

- Goal: "We wish to implement a very permissive policy that legally protects the trademarks while encouraging the community to liberally use the trademarks." (opening)
- Owner: "“Jupyter” is a trademark of LF Charities, of which Project Jupyter is a part. The Jupyter logos (in several variants) are trademarks of LF Charities as well." (Introduction)
- Registration: "The Jupyter Trademark is registered with the U.S. Patent & Trademark Office." (Introduction)
- No approval needed: "stating accurately that software, a service, or an activity uses Jupyter software, integrates with Jupyter software, that it is compatible with Jupyter software, or that it contains Jupyter software, is always allowed." (Uses that Never Require Approval)
- Approval needed: "Any commercial use of the Jupyter trademarks in product or company names must be approved first by Project Jupyter." (Uses that Always Require Approval)
- Refused names: "Some uses, like calling a company “The Jupyter Company,” or a product “Jupyter Hosting” or “Jupyter Cloud” will be refused." (Uses that Always Require Approval)
- Merchandise: "Offering Jupyter branded merchandise for sale without explicit approval is not permitted." (Use of the Jupyter trademark on merchandise)

## Evidence and method

This is a policy document of Project Jupyter. It states rules and examples. It gives no data on approvals or enforcement.

## Limits

- The policy does not say how long an approval takes or who in LF Charities signs it.
- The subcommittee table gives a term only for one member.
