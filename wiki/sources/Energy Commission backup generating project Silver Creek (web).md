# Energy Commission backup generating project Silver Creek (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Silver Creek Backup Generating Facility (SCBGF)." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/silver-creek-backup-generating-facility-scbgf. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Silver Creek.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Silver%20Creek.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Silver Creek.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 99 megawatts (MW) for a proposed data center in San Jose. Its status is "Under Review".
- The facility has 44 diesel generators. PG&E is to build a new Hellyer Switching Station about 1,400 feet from the site to serve the data center.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. Prologis, L.P. is the project owner. The docket number is 26-SPPE-02 and the location is San Jose, California. The page has no capacity field; its project description gives the capacity. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The Silver Creek Backup Generating Facility (SCBGF) would consist of forty-four (44) diesel-fired generators to be used to provide up to 99 megawatts (MW) of backup electricity generation to support the proposed Silver Creek Data Center (SCDC)." (Project Description)
- "The proposed SCDC, SCBGF, and project substation would be located on a 15.13-acre site, at 5977 Silver Creek Valley Road, in San Jose, California." (Project Description)
- "The SCBGF would only be connected to the SCDC and would not be interconnected to the electrical transmission or distribution grid." (Project Description)
- "The switching station would be constructed, owned, and operated by PG&E." (Project Description)
- "The Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants with facilities between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)
