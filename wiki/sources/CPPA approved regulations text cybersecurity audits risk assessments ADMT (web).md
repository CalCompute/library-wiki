# CPPA approved regulations text cybersecurity audits risk assessments ADMT (web)

## Citation

Approved text, 127 pages, date not stated. cppa.ca.gov. https://cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_updates_cyber_risk_admt_appr_text.pdf. Read 2026-11-19. ## Bottom line

- The regulations define automated decisionmaking technology (ADMT) as technology that processes personal information and replaces, or substantially replaces, human decisionmaking. A business that uses ADMT for a significant decision about a consumer must comply by Jan. 1, 2027.
- A significant decision is one about financial or lending services, housing, education, employment or healthcare. ADMT users must give a Pre-use Notice and honor requests to opt out and to access.
- Businesses whose processing has significant risk must do a risk assessment before they start, and some must complete yearly cybersecurity audits from 2028.

## Background

The CPPA enforces the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). This is the full approved text of its 2025 rule package, from section 7001 to section 7302. The [CPPA announcement California finalizes privacy regulations 2025-09-23 (web)](CPPA%20announcement%20California%20finalizes%20privacy%20regulations%202025-09-23%20%28web%29.md) reports its approval. ## Key claims

- "“Automated decisionmaking technology” or “ADMT” means any technology that processes personal information and uses computation to replace human decisionmaking or substantially replace human decisionmaking." (§ 7001(e), p. 1)
- "For purposes of this definition, to “substantially replace human decisionmaking” means a business uses the technology’s output to make a decision without human involvement." (§ 7001(e)(1), p. 1)
- "“Significant decision” means a decision that results in the provision or denial of financial or lending services, housing, education enrollment or opportunities, employment or independent contracting opportunities or compensation, or healthcare services." (§ 7001(ddd), p. 10)
- "Every business whose processing of consumers’ personal information presents significant risk to consumers’ privacy as set forth in subsection (b) must conduct a risk assessment before initiating that processing." (§ 7150(a), p. 100)
- "Using ADMT for a significant decision concerning a consumer." (§ 7150(b)(3), one of the processing activities that "presents significant risk", p. 100)
- "A business that uses ADMT for a significant decision prior to January 1, 2027, must be in compliance with the requirements of this Article no later than January 1, 2027." (§ 7200(b), p. 112)
- "A business that uses ADMT as set forth in section 7200, subsection (a), must provide consumers with a Pre-use Notice." (§ 7220(a), p. 112)
- "Processed the personal information of 250,000 or more consumers or households in the preceding calendar year; or" (§ 7120(b)(2)(A), one test for a cybersecurity audit, p. 88)
