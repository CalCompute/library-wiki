## Bottom line

- This is the text of § 200.414 on Sept. 30, 2024, before the 2024 revision of the Uniform Guidance took effect.
- It set the de minimis indirect cost rate at "10% of modified total direct costs (MTDC)". It required each federally negotiated rate to be "available publicly on an OMB-designated Federal website".

## Background

The eCFR keeps past versions of each section. This copy is the point-in-time text for Sept. 30, 2024. Its last amendment in the source note is 85 FR 49563 of Aug. 13, 2020. The current text is [eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 Indirect costs (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20Indirect%20costs%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Major nonprofit organizations are those which receive more than $10 million dollars in direct Federal funding." (§ 200.414(a))
- "The negotiated rates must be accepted by all Federal awarding agencies." (§ 200.414(c)(1))
- Any non-Federal entity without a current negotiated rate, with stated exceptions, "may elect to charge a de minimis rate of 10% of modified total direct costs (MTDC) which may be used indefinitely." (§ 200.414(f))
- "No documentation is required to justify the 10% de minimis indirect cost rate." (§ 200.414(f))
- "The federally negotiated indirect rate, distribution base, and rate type for a non-Federal entity (except for the Indian tribes or tribal organizations, as defined in the Indian Self Determination, Education and Assistance Act, 25 U.S.C. 450b(1)) must be available publicly on an OMB-designated Federal website." (§ 200.414(h))
