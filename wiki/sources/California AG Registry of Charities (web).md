# California AG Registry of Charities (web)

## Citation

California Department of Justice, Office of the Attorney General. "Charities." Program page, date not stated; it names an Online Filing Service that started Aug. 25, 2026. https://oag.ca.gov/charities. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-09 California AG Registry of Charities.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-09%20California%20AG%20Registry%20of%20Charities.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The California Attorney General regulates charities and the fundraisers that solicit for them.
- All charities and fundraisers must register with the Registry of Charities and Fundraisers and file annual financial reports, with limited exceptions. The public can search the filings.

## Background

The page is the entry page of the Attorney General's charities program. It names two parts: the Charitable Trusts Section, which investigates and sues, and the Registry of Charities and Fundraisers, which runs registration. It links the Online Filing Service, the Registry Search Tool and the Attorney General's Guide for Charities.

## Key claims

- "The Attorney General regulates charities and fundraisers who solicit on their behalf, such as charitable organizations, trusts, trustees, commercial fundraisers, fundraising counsel, charitable fundraising platforms, and other persons or entities that solicit or hold charitable assets." (Charities)
- "The Charitable Trusts Section investigates and brings legal actions against charities and fundraisers that misuse charitable assets or engage in fraudulent fundraising practices and other wrongdoings." (Charities)
- "All charities and fundraisers are required to register and file annual financial disclosure reports with the Registry, subject to limited exceptions." (Charities)
- "For charitable organizations, these public filings include a copy of federal annual informational return (IRS Forms 990, 990-PF, and 990-EZ) initial and renewal registration forms and data (e.g., Forms CT-1, RRF-1, CT-TR-1), other documents that organizations are required to file with the Registry, and incoming and outgoing Registry correspondence." (Charity Research Resources)
- "The Registry of Charities and Fundraisers has released its new Online Filing Service effective August 25, 2026." (Charities)

## Evidence and method

A state agency's program page. It states the legal duties of charities in general terms and links the forms and tools. It gives no counts of registrants and no fees.

## Limits

The page does not give the registration thresholds, the fees or the text of the statutes. It does not discuss any one charity. The details are in the linked Guide for Charities and program pages, which the library does not hold.
