# CROSS homepage

## Citation

Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS), UC Santa Cruz. "Center for Research in Open Source Software." Web page, cross.ucsc.edu, last modified Aug. 4, 2026. https://cross.ucsc.edu/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- A thin homepage (356 words). CROSS "provides funding to work on innovative projects after graduation". It works with the UC Open Source Program Office (OSPO) Network of "six UC campuses".
- It has no facts on the membership model.

## Background

See [CROSS](../entities/CROSS.md).

## Key claims

- "UCSC created CROSS as a center that assists students with their research prototypes to collaborate with open source communities and companies."
- "The UC OSPO Network is a groundbreaking initiative that harnesses the collective power of six UC campuses to revolutionize open-source practices in academia."
- News: CruzCon on Oct. 22, 2026; the 2026 Open Source Research Experience (OSRE) cohort; a PyTorch meetup "Sponsored by Red Hat and University of California, Santa Cruz".

## Evidence and method

The center's own page. The site map lists only four pages: home, News, About and Events.

Pages taken, and why:
- [CROSS About](CROSS%20About.md): the founding gift, the founding members, the leadership and the stakeholder model. This is the only page on the membership model.
- [CROSS Events](CROSS%20Events.md): the move from the CROSS symposium to the UC Open Source Summit.

## Limits

- Mostly menus and news. No figures on funding, members or staff.
