## Bottom line

- The fact sheet reports "over $6 billion in investments across the Federal Government, industry, academia, and philanthropy". It includes "$2.4B in SI for science tools and compute credits for the Genesis Mission Consortium from eleven industry partners".
- It also reports state and regional computing hubs in the Southeast, Georgia and the Carolinas. It reports new fellowships, among them a $100 million Genesis Mission Fellowship.

## Background

The White House issued the fact sheet at the *Science: A New Golden Age* Summit. It groups the initiatives under four headings: super intelligence (SI) for science, students and early-career scientists, national missions, and new institutions. It names agencies, companies and amounts.

## Key claims

- "representing over $6 billion in investments across the Federal Government, industry, academia, and philanthropy". (first paragraph)
- "The Trump Administration announced $2.4B in SI for science tools and compute credits for the Genesis Mission Consortium from eleven industry partners, which will support over 15 Federal agencies working towards National Science & Technology Challenges." (Equipping American Scientists)
- "Industry partner commitments include NVIDIA ($1B), AMD ($500M), OpenAI ($200M), $150M each from Anthropic and Google, $100M each from AMP and Emerald AI, and $50M each from AWS, Armada, Crusoe, and Micron." (Equipping American Scientists)
- "The Trump Administration announced historic investments in scientific computing through state and regional SI infrastructure hubs, making advanced SI resources more widely available to researchers across the country." (Equipping American Scientists)
- "More than 14 universities across 10 states launched the Southeast Regional SI Computing Consortium to build and share leadership-class compute infrastructure for scientific research." (Equipping American Scientists)
- "Universities, industry, and the State of Georgia announced a $1 billion investment in scientific computing and related workforce training efforts for students and faculty." (Equipping American Scientists)
- "The Trump Administration launched the Genesis Mission Fellowship, a $100 million initiative for students to pursue accelerated four-year PhDs with dual competency in super intelligence and another science or engineering discipline". (Refocusing on the Best and Brightest)

## Evidence and method

A White House fact sheet. It lists initiatives with amounts and partners. It gives no sources for the amounts and no time periods for most of them.

## Limits

The fact sheet does not say how the "over $6 billion" adds up. It does not say over how many years the industry commitments run, or what share is credits and what share is tools. It names no California hub. [DOE Genesis Mission 800 million partner commitments (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20800%20million%20partner%20commitments%20%28web%29.md) reported "more than $800 million" in partner support in July 2026. The fact sheet does not say how its $2.4 billion relates to that figure.
