# CSU APC Unit 4 tentative agreement 2026-10-07 (web)

## Citation

The California State University and Academic Professionals of California. "Tentative Agreement (APC & CSU)." Dated 10/07/2026; file name "executed". California State University. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Documents/unit4-apc/APC-CSU-TA-10-07-2026-executed.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-22. Read 2026-11-22.

## Bottom line

- This is the tentative agreement between the California State University (CSU) and Academic Professionals of California (APC) for Unit 4, Academic Support. It extends the contract term to June 30, 2028.
- Salaries go up 4 percent from July 1, 2026, and 4 percent from July 1, 2027. For 2025/2026, each eligible employee gets a one-time payment of $3,325.
- CSU sets up an advisory Health Benefits Review Work Group.

## Background

The agreement changes four articles of [CSU APC Unit 4 contract 2024-2027 (web)](CSU%20APC%20Unit%204%20contract%202024-2027%20%28web%29.md). They are Article 23 (Salary), Article 24 (Benefits), Article 32 (Work Environment) and Article 34 (Duration and Implementation). It has 18 pages. Each page carries the date 10/07/2026 and a signature for each party. New text is in bold and underlined, and removed text is struck out.

## Key claims

- "This Agreement shall become effective upon ratification by both parties and shall remain in full force and effect up to and including June 30, 2028" (Article 34, 34.1; "2027" struck out)
- "For fiscal year 2026/2027, effective July 1, 2026, all bargaining unit employees who are in active status (or on leave) at time of processing shall receive a GSI of four percent (4%)." (Article 23, 23.3)
- "For fiscal year 2027/2028, effective July 1, 2027, all bargaining unit employees in active pay status (or on leave) as of that date shall receive a GSI of four percent (4%)." (Article 23, 23.3, page 1 of the original)
- "For fiscal year 2025/2026, all eligible APC employees shall receive a single one-time bonus in the gross amount of $3325, prorated based upon time base." (Article 23, One-time Payment)
- "This amount represents a one-time compensation pool of approximately 5%." (Article 23, One-time Payment)
- "The parties acknowledge that the Work Group is advisory in nature and shall not have the authority to modify any collective bargaining agreement" (Article 24)
- "Effective January 1, 2029, the Employer contribution toward the cost of health benefits shall be determined in the successor contract negotiations." (Article 24)
- "Any term(s) of this Agreement which is deemed to carry an economic cost shall not be implemented until the amount required therefore is appropriated and made available to the CSU for expenditure for such purpose(s)." (Article 34, 34.4)

## Evidence and method

A tentative agreement between a public employer and a union, signed by one person for each party. It gives the changed contract text, not data.

## Limits

- The document does not say whether the members or the CSU Board of Trustees ratified it.
- The Markdown copy keeps the text that the agreement strikes out. Examples are "June 30, 2028 2027." in 34.1 and the old reopener clauses in 23.3. It also loses the rest of the 2027/2028 increase in 23.3. Read the original for the marked changes.
- The text does not name artificial intelligence or technology.
- The agreement does not change Article 7 (contracting out) or Article 16 (professional development).
