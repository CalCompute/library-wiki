# eCFR 2 CFR 200.1 Definitions (web)

## Bottom line

- Section 200.1 defines the terms of 2 CFR Part 200, the federal rules for grants and cooperative agreements. It defines indirect cost, Modified Total Direct Cost (MTDC), equipment, nonprofit organization, pass-through entity and subaward.
- MTDC counts "up to the first $50,000 of each subaward". Equipment is property with a per-unit cost at or above the lesser of the recipient's capitalization level "or $10,000".

## Background

2 CFR Part 200 is the federal Uniform Guidance for federal financial assistance. Section 200.1 is its list of definitions. The Office of the Federal Register publishes the eCFR. The text in this copy dates from the 2024 revision. The library read the definitions named in this page, not all of them.

## Key claims

- "*Indirect cost* means those costs incurred for a common or joint purpose benefitting more than one cost objective and not readily assignable to the cost objectives specifically benefitted, without effort disproportionate to the results achieved." (Indirect cost)
- "For Institutions of Higher Education (IHE), the term facilities and administrative (F&A) cost is often used to refer to indirect costs." (Indirect cost)
- "*Modified Total Direct Cost (MTDC)* means all direct salaries and wages, applicable fringe benefits, materials and supplies, services, travel, and up to the first $50,000 of each subaward (regardless of the period of performance of the subawards under the award)." (MTDC)
- "*Equipment* means tangible personal property (including information technology systems) having a useful life of more than one year and a per-unit acquisition cost that equals or exceeds the lesser of the capitalization level established by the recipient or subrecipient for financial statement purposes, or $10,000." (Equipment)
- A nonprofit organization "Is operated primarily for scientific, educational, service, charitable, or similar purposes in the public interest", "Is not organized primarily for profit", "Uses net proceeds to maintain, improve, or expand the organization's operations; and" "Is not an IHE." (Nonprofit organization)
- A cooperative agreement "Is distinguished from a grant in that it provides for substantial involvement of the Federal agency or pass-through entity in carrying out the activity contemplated by the Federal award." (Cooperative agreement)
- "*Pass-through entity* means a recipient or subrecipient that provides a subaward to a subrecipient (including lower tier subrecipients) to carry out part of a Federal program." (Pass-through entity)

## Evidence and method

Regulation text. It defines terms and gives no evidence. Program statutes and regulations take precedence over these definitions, as the section's opening states.

## Limits

The section defines terms only. The rate rules are in other sections, such as [eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 Indirect costs (web)](eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20Indirect%20costs%20%28web%29.md). This page applies to federal awards, not to state grants.
