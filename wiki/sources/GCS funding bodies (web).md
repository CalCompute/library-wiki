# GCS funding bodies (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing e.V. (GCS). "Funding Bodies." Web page, date not stated. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us/funding-bodies. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- GCS calls itself a national research institution. One federal ministry and three state ministries fund it jointly.
- The page names the four ministries. It gives no amounts.

## Background

GCS joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres in Stuttgart, Jülich and Garching ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)). This page lists its funders with their logos and links. The same four ministries sit on the [GCS Steering Committee (web)](GCS%20Steering%20Committee%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "As a national research institution, GCS is jointly funded by federal and state ministries." (Funding Bodies)
- "The Gauss Centre for Supercomputing is a national research institution. GCS is jointly funded by the following ministries:" (Funding Bodies)
- "Federal Ministry of Research, Technology, and Space--BMFTR" (list)
- "Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts of the German State of Baden-Württemberg" (list)
- "Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts" (list)
- "Ministry of Culture and Science of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia" (list)

## Evidence and method

A list page with four links. It gives no figures, no shares and no years.

## Limits

- The page gives no budget, no split between the federal and state shares, and no funding period.
- It does not say what the funds pay for.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This page names the "Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts" and the "Ministry of Culture and Science of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia". [Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md) names the "Bavarian State Ministry of Sciences, Research and the Arts" and the "Ministry of Culture and Research of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia". This page agrees with [EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md) on the North Rhine-Westphalia name.
