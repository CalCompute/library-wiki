# Energy Commission backup generating project Bowers (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Bowers Backup Generating Facility." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/bowers-backup-generating-facility. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Bowers.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Bowers.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Bowers.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 72 megawatts (MW) for the Bowers Data Center in Santa Clara. Its status is "Exempted".
- The facility has 32 Tier 4 diesel generators, for the uninterruptible power supply of the tenant's servers.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. GI Partners is the project owner and the docket number is 22-SPPE-01. The page has no capacity or location field; its project description gives both. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "GI Partners proposes to develop the Bowers Backup Generating Facility (BBGF or project) at 2805 Bowers Avenue, Santa Clara, California." (Project Description)
- "The BBGF would be an emergency backup generating facility with a generation capacity of up to 72 megawatts (MW) to support the need for the Bowers Data Center (BDC) to provide uninterruptible power supply for its tenant’s servers." (Project Description)
- "The BBGF would consist of 32 Tier 4 compliant diesel-fired backup generators arranged in a generation yard located on the north side of the BDC." (Project Description)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Evidence and method

## Limits

- The page has Events and Key Dates headings, but the copy shows no text under them. It gives no date for the exemption.
- It gives no size of the data center building and no utility for its normal power.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
