# Tech Talent Project state transitions (web)

## Citation

Tech Talent Project. "State transitions" (program page). techtalentproject.org, date not stated. https://techtalentproject.org/ova_dep/state-transitions/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Tech Talent Project helps new state administrations with technology leadership in their first 100 days of 2027. It gives playbooks, rapid response, recruiting and matching, and coaching.
- Its nine 2026 Transition Memos cover topics that include "AI readiness and delivery", talent, data and procurement. It recruits state chief technology, information, data, AI and privacy officers.

## Background

State transitions is one of the programs of Tech Talent Project; see [Tech Talent Project homepage (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20homepage%20%28web%29.md). The page describes the 2026 memos and the earlier memo series of 2020 and 2022. The release of the memos' Interactive Transition Guide is [Tech Talent Project incoming governors guide 2026-11-16 (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20incoming%20governors%20guide%202026-11-16%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "In 2026, 36 states held gubernatorial elections." "That window is the first 100 days of 2027." (The moment)
- "The 2026 memos cover AI readiness and delivery, benefits delivery, emergency response, permitting, talent, data, procurement, child welfare, and customer experience." (Implementation Playbooks)
- "Tech Talent activates an 11,000-plus technologist network and a decade of government experience to match senior technology leaders with in-house state roles. This includes chief technology officers, chief information officers, chief data officers, chief AI officers, and chief privacy officers, as well as senior engineers, designers, and product managers." (Recruiting and Matching)
- "Alumni in those placements delivered directly for residents: 220,000 or more benefits applications completed through one streamlined process in Maryland, and 63 agency websites transformed into accessible, resident-centered experiences in Pennsylvania." (What you achieve)
- "To date, Tech Talent Project has supported 30 states via one of our programs." (Track record)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It describes the program's four parts and reports outcomes of earlier placements. It gives no method for the outcome figures.

## Limits

The page does not say whether a state pays for the service. The release of Nov. 16, 2026, says the support is "at no cost to the state". The page does not name California among the states it supported.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page gives "an 11,000-plus technologist network". [Tech Talent Project about (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20about%20%28web%29.md) gives "10,000+ Technology Professionals".
