## Bottom line

- Gavin Newsom is the 40th Governor of California. Voters elected him on Nov. 6, 2018.
- The page gives his birth year and place, his schooling, his business and his offices in San Francisco.

## Background

The official biography of the Governor, on the Governor's website. It gives his early life, his business career, his offices in San Francisco and his family. This is a profile page. His entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "Gavin Newsom was elected as California's 40th Governor on November 6, 2018." (header)
- "Born in 1967 in San Francisco, Governor Newsom attended Redwood High School and later Santa Clara University." (paragraph 1)
- "After college, Newsom started a retail wine shop that grew into the PlumpJack Group, which managed restaurants, hotels, and wineries across California." (paragraph 1)
- "Newsom's first job in politics was on the San Francisco Parking and Traffic Commission, and later on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors." (paragraph 2)
- "In 2003, Newsom became the youngest elected Mayor of San Francisco in a century." (paragraph 2)
- "Governor Newsom is married to filmmaker Jennifer Siebel Newsom, the First Partner of California" (paragraph 5)
