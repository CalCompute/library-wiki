# Digital India Corporation notice DIC-10-2026-22 (web)

## Citation

Digital India Corporation (DIC), Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. "Web Advertisement," Advt. No. DIC/10/2026/22: Senior Manager, Government Partnerships and Business Development. October 22, 2026 (22.10.2026). PDF, 6 pages. https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2026/10/8d5f651287ec0e68e8fdded8d8ccc18e.pdf. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- DIC advertises one contract post: Senior Manager, Government Partnerships and Business Development. Applications closed on October 29, 2026.
- The post wins work for DIC from central and state government bodies, through outreach, partnerships, proposals and bids.
- DIC describes itself as a not-for-profit company under Section 8 of the Companies Act 2013, set up by MeitY.

## Background

DIC is the company that MeitY set up to carry out the Digital India programme. The IndiaAI Mission runs as a division of DIC ([PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)](PIB%20Cabinet%20approves%20IndiaAI%20Mission%202024-03-07%20%28web%29.md)). This notice is a recruitment advertisement on the MeitY website. It gives DIC's description of itself, the job description, the qualifications and the general conditions of hire.

## Key claims

- Legal form: "It is a ‘not for profit’ Company under Section 8 of the Companies Act 2013." (page 1)
- Origin: "Digital India Corporation has been set up by the ‘Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology, Government of India’, to innovate, develop and deploy ICT and other emerging technologies for the benefit of the common man." (page 1)
- Terms of hire: DIC invites applications "purely on Contract/ Consolidated basis". (page 1)
- Role: "The Senior Manager will be responsible for leading engagement with Central and State Government departments, public sector institutions and strategic partners to identify and develop business opportunities for the organisation." (Role Overview, page 2)
- Partnerships: the post will "Explore opportunities for joint initiatives, consortium arrangements, Memoranda of Understanding and other institutional collaborations." (Key Responsibilities, section 2, page 3)
- Experience: "Minimum 12–16 years of relevant professional experience, including substantial experience in government relations, public-sector business development, institutional partnerships, government consulting or client acquisition." (Essential Qualifications and Experience, page 4)
- No permanent right: "The positions are purely temporary in nature for the project of Digital India Corporation and the appointees shall not derive any right or claim for permanent appointment at Digital India Corporation". (General Conditions, item 6, pages 5 and 6)
- Conflicts: "Candidates shall disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest with Digital India Corporation or its projects." (General Conditions, item 3, page 5)

## Evidence and method

This is a government recruitment notice. It sets out five groups of duties: government engagement, strategic partnerships, business development, proposals and bids, and client relations. It gives a selection by an "objective evaluation matrix" and interview. Pay is "commensurate with qualifications and experience within the approved project norms" (page 5). The notice gives no salary figure.

## Limits

- The notice does not name IndiaAI or compute. It shows how DIC staffs a business-development role, not how it runs the IndiaAI Mission.
- It gives no pay, no headcount for DIC and no budget.
- The row date was unknown; the date of 22.10.2026 comes from the notice itself.
