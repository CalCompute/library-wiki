# CSUSB high performance computing article (web)

## Citation

California State University, San Bernardino (CSUSB), Office of Strategic Communication. "CSUSB accelerates research through connections to high performance computing." CSUSB Inside, October 7, 2022. https://www.csusb.edu/inside/article/561747/csusb-accelerates-research-through-connections-high-performance-computing. Read 2026-11-11. ## Bottom line

- CSUSB is a sub-awardee, with San Diego State University (SDSU), of a five-year, $6.7 million National Science Foundation (NSF) grant for cyberinfrastructure staff. The CSUSB share is $1.2 million.
- CSUSB's High Performance Computing Initiative (HPCI) gives faculty, students and staff access to computing at supercomputing centers. CSUSB has its own GPU node and joined the Pacific Research Platform (PRP) in 2017.

## Background

This is a campus news article. It describes how CSUSB moved from faculty finding computing on their own to a campus role. It names the HPCI members and affiliated faculty.

## Key claims

The grant (opening paragraphs):

- "The five-year, $6.7 million grant will help train and create cyber infrastructure professional positions to support faculty research. CSUSB and SDSU will lead the way to serve Hispanic-Serving Institutions and Minority-Serving Institutions. CSUSB's portion of the sub-award is $1.2 million over five years."

GPU node and HPCI (middle of article):

- "Last year, due to the volume of research computing by CSUSB faculty, Cal State San Bernardino was selected to have its own graphics processing unit (GPU) node, providing access to computing resources normally available only to Research 1-classified universities."
- "The new GPU node provides a boost to CSUSB's High Performance Computing Initiative (HPCI), which provides the university's faculty, students and staff with access to high-performance computing resources at various supercomputing centers. All the university's faculty and student projects that can use these resources are welcome and can begin immediately."
- "Originally, faculty worked on their own to garner resources from colleagues and partner institutions. However, in order to support its faculty and grow CSUSB's research infrastructure, the university took over the role of identifying high performance computing resources."

Network partners (later paragraphs):

- "CSUSB was the second CSU to join the Pacific Research Platform, or PRP, in 2017."
- Sam Sudhakar, Chief Information Officer: "CSUSB was one of eight CSU campuses in 2018 upgraded to a 100 Gig link to California Research and Education Network (CalREN), operated by CENIC."
