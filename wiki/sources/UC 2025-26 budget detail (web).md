# UC 2025-26 budget detail (web)

## Citation

University of California Office of the President. *2025-26 Budget for Current Operations: Context for the Budget Request.* PDF, date not stated. https://www.ucop.edu/operating-budget/_files/rbudget/2025-26-budget-detail.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- This is the University of California's (UC's) detailed budget volume for 2025-26. It explains each fund source and each program area of the operating budget.
- It says that State General Funds are 9.8% of UC's total operating budget and about 44% of its core funds.
- Its Research chapter says that each State research dollar brings about nine dollars from other sources.

## Background

The UC Office of the President (UCOP) publishes this volume with a companion, the 2025-26 Summary of the Budget Request. It has 21 chapters and appendices of budget, enrollment and tuition tables. The chapters cover the fund sources, instruction, research, public service, support activities, tuition, financial aid and the Department of Energy laboratories. The copy read is a two-column PDF; the Markdown copy mixes the two columns, so the quotes below follow the PDF.

## Key claims

- Total budget: "UC’s operating budget, totaling $53.6 billion in 2024-25, consists of funds from a variety of sources." (Sources of University Funds, p. 17)
- State support: "State General Fund support for UC totals $4.91 billion in 2024-25 and provides critical resources for the University." (Sources of University Funds, p. 17)
- Share: "Although State General Funds represent 9.8% of the University’s total operating budget, they represent approximately 44% of the University’s current core funds." (UC’s Role in the State of California, p. 12)
- Designation: "The majority of State General Funds, however, are undesignated, allowing them to be used where they are most needed to support the University’s core mission activities." (Sources of University Funds, p. 17)
- Research leverage: "For every State dollar invested in research, UC expends approximately nine dollars from federal, private, and other non-State sources". (Research, p. 71)
- Research awards: "In 2023-24, UC received about $7.5 billion in research awards." (Research, p. 72)
- State share of research: "In 2023-24, 10.6% of direct research expenditures came from State Funds (includes State General Funds and Special State Funds) and UC General Funds". (Research, p. 72)
- Challenges: the Research chapter names "the lack of investment in critical infrastructure to support fundamental academic research in emerging technological areas". (Research, p. 71)
- Coalition model: "UC is working on a coalition-based model for academic research across California that responds to emerging state and local priorities by attracting a combination of federal, state, and private funding sources." (Research, p. 71)

## Evidence and method

This is an official budget document of a public university system. It gives fund totals, shares and trend tables from UC's own accounts. It explains the budget request. It is not an audit.

## Limits

- The volume does not name a budget line for AI computing or for shared research computing.
- The agent read the fund sources, the opening of the UC's role chapter and the opening of the Research chapter. The agent did not read the other chapters or the appendix tables in full.
- The copy gives no date of publication.
