# SLAC Genesis Mission phase II projects (web)

## Citation

SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, Integrated Scientific and Data-intensive Computing (ISDCI). "Phase II Projects." Web page, date not stated. https://isdci.slac.stanford.edu/genesis-mission/phase-ii-projects. Read 2026-11-10.

## Bottom line

- SLAC says it is "partnering in seven Genesis Mission Phase II projects".
- Other institutions lead all seven: four national laboratories, the University of Washington, UC San Diego and one company, Commonwealth Fusion Systems LLC.

## Background

SLAC is a national laboratory in Menlo Park, California. Stanford University operates it for the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Science. The ISDCI site lists the laboratory's computing initiative areas, the Shared Science Data Facility among them. This page lists the Genesis Mission projects in which SLAC is a partner.

## Key claims

- "SLAC is partnering in seven Genesis Mission Phase II projects, listed below." (Phase II Projects)
- "AI4HPC: An Iterative Framework for AI-Assisted Scientific Software Development and Optimization", "Lead institution: Argonne National Laboratory" (SLAC collaboration projects)
- "The Multi-Office Accelerator Team Core (MOAT-Core) Project", "Lead institution: Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory" (SLAC collaboration projects)
- "Decoding the RNA Structurome to Secure AI Advantage for the Bioeconomy", "Lead institution: The Regents of the University of California - U.C. San Diego" (SLAC collaboration projects)
- "SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory is operated by Stanford University for the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science." (footer)

## Evidence and method

A laboratory web page with a list of project titles and lead institutions. The other leads are Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Fermi National Accelerator Laboratory.

## Limits

The page gives no amounts, no dates, no project descriptions and no SLAC role in each project. It does not say how Phase II differs from Phase I. The DOE list of the first Genesis Mission selections is a separate document: [DOE Genesis Mission awards list 2026 (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20awards%20list%202026%20%28web%29.md).
