# Irvine Foundation about us (web)

## Citation

The James Irvine Foundation. "About Us." Web page, date not stated. https://www.irvine.org/about-us/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The James Irvine Foundation is a private, independent foundation that gives grants only in California. Its goal is a California where all low-income workers can advance economically.
- It gave $160.2 million in grants in 2025. Its endowment was $3.6 billion at the end of 2025.

## Background

The foundation's About page gives its mission, its focus, its history, its values and its commitments on racial equity in economic opportunity.

## Key claims

- "Our singular goal is a California where all low-income workers have the power to advance economically. We focus specifically on California." (Our Focus)
- "The James Irvine Foundation is a private, independent foundation that provided $160.2 million in grants in 2025 to organizations in California." (Our History)
- "Since our founding in 1937, Irvine has made more than $2.92 billion in grants throughout the state." (Our History)
- "Our endowment, from which we make grants, totaled $3.6 billion by the end of 2025." (Our History)
- "Asian American, Black, Indigenous, Latino/a, and Pacific Islander people make up 80% of California’s low-income worker population." (What we believe)

## Evidence and method

An organization page. It gives totals for grants and the endowment, and about 80 employees in San Francisco and Los Angeles. It gives no source for the 80% figure.

## Limits

The page does not describe an AI, technology or compute program. It gives no grant list.
