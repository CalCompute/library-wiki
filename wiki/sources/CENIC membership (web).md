# CENIC membership (web)

## Citation

Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California (CENIC). "CENIC Membership." Web page, date not stated. https://cenic.org/about/membership. Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- CENIC has five membership classes. Charter Associates "make up the Board of Directors to set policy and guide the organization" and get the full set of networks and services.
- The other classes (Associate, Sponsored Associate, Auxiliary Associate, Affiliate) get a direct or indirect connection, with a full or shorter portfolio of services.

## Background

The page states CENIC's founding in 1996 by UC, Stanford, CSU, the California Community Colleges, USC and Caltech. K-12, public libraries and the Naval Postgraduate School joined later as Charter Associates. It then defines the five membership categories.

## Key claims

- "In 1996, visionary leaders at the University of California, Stanford, California State University, California Community Colleges, USC, and Caltech came together to create CENIC. Over time, the state's K-12 education system, California Public Libraries, and the Naval Postgraduate School joined as Charter Associates." (top of page)
- "Charter Associate: Founding and governing research and education members of CENIC that make up the Board of Directors to set policy and guide the organization; Charter Associates have full access to CENIC's entire portfolio of networks and services." (CENIC Membership Categories)
- "Associate: Independent research, education, medical, or technology institutions and organizations that engage with and connect directly to the broader CENIC community; Associates have direct access to CENIC's portfolio of networks and services." (CENIC Membership Categories)
- "Sponsored Associate: Cultural, scientific, or artistic institutions or organizations with a close tie to a Charter Associate that serves as a sponsor. Sponsored Associates have direct access to an abbreviated portfolio of CENIC's networks and services." (CENIC Membership Categories)
- "Affiliate: Education or research institutions and organizations that connect to CalREN through a Charter Associate that serves as a network access point. Affiliates make use of the CalREN network indirectly." (CENIC Membership Categories)

## Evidence and method

The organisation's own page. It defines categories; it gives no fees and no member counts.

## Limits

The page does not say how a Charter Associate's board seats are allocated or how fees are set. The lists of members in each class are on other pages, which the library has not read.
