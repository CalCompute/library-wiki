# FDP data use agreement template (web)

## Citation

Federal Demonstration Partnership (FDP). "FDP One-Way Data Transfer and Use Agreement." Template, March 2024. Copy hosted by Auburn University, Office of the Vice President for Research. https://research.auburn.edu/_assets/fdp-data-use-agreement.pdf. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The template is a standard contract for one institution (the Provider) to give a data set to another (the Recipient) for one research project.
- The Provider keeps its rights in the data. Only named "Authorized Persons" can use the data, and only for the project. The Recipient must keep safeguards and cannot give the data to a third party without written consent.
- The Provider can review a manuscript for 30 days before publication. At the end, the Recipient follows the disposition instructions but can keep one archival copy for three stated reasons.

## Background

The Federal Demonstration Partnership is a group of federal agencies and research institutions that writes standard research agreements. This copy is the March 2024 version of its one-way data transfer and use agreement, posted by Auburn University. It has 14 terms and three attachments: project information, data-specific terms and permitted collaborators.

## Key claims

- "Provider shall retain ownership of any rights it may have in the Data, and Recipient does not obtain any rights in the Data other than as set forth herein." (Term 2)
- "The Data will be used solely to conduct the Project and solely by Recipient Scientist and Recipient's faculty, employees, fellows, students, and agents ("Recipient Personnel") and Collaborator Personnel" (Term 3)
- "Recipient agrees to establish appropriate administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to prevent unauthorized use of or access to the Data" (Term 4)
- "the Provider will have thirty (30) days from receipt to review proposed manuscripts and ten (10) days from receipt to review proposed abstracts to ensure that the Data is appropriately protected." (Term 6)
- "Recipient may retain one (1) copy of the Data to the extent necessary to comply with the records retention requirements" (Term 8)
- "Except to the extent prohibited by law, the Recipient assumes all liability for damages which may arise from its use, storage, disclosure, or disposal of the Data." (Term 10)
- "Costs shall not include payments for research effort by the Provider." (Term 1, Reimbursement of Costs)

## Evidence and method

A blank contract template of about 1,400 words. It states terms. It gives no evidence and no data about its use.

## Limits

The template has blank fields, so it names no data set, project or party. Attachment 2, for data-specific security terms, is empty in the template. It does not name a law, such as HIPAA or FERPA, and it sets no technical security standard. The copy is from Auburn University's site. The file at FDP's own address, https://thefdp.org/wp-content/uploads/dtua_March-2024-v05.03.2024-final.pdf, is the same file, byte for byte (fetched 2026-11-20). The reciprocal version, for two parties that give data to each other, is [FDP Reciprocal DTUA template 2020-09 (web)](FDP%20Reciprocal%20DTUA%20template%202020-09%20%28web%29.md).
