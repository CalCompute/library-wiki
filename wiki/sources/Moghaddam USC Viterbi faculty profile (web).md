# Moghaddam USC Viterbi faculty profile (web)

## Citation

University of Southern California (USC) Viterbi School of Engineering, Ming Hsieh Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. "Mahta Moghaddam." Faculty directory profile, date not stated. https://minghsiehece.usc.edu/directory/faculty/profile/?lname=Moghaddam&fname=Mahta. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- Mahta Moghaddam is the Ming Hsieh Chair in Electrical and Computer Engineering and a Distinguished Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering at USC.
- Her appointments include Vice Dean for Research and Co-Director of the Center for Sustainability Solutions. Her research is on radar remote sensing.

## Background

This is her profile in the faculty directory of the Ming Hsieh Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering. It gives her degrees, a short biography, a research summary and her appointments. Before USC, she worked at the Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) and at the University of Michigan.

## Key claims

- "Ming Hsieh Chair in Electrical and Computer Engineering and Distinguished Professor of Electrical and Computer Engineering" (title line)
- "From 1991 to 2003, she was with the Radar Science and Engineering Section, Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL), California Institute of Technology, Pasadena, before joining the Radiation Laboratory in the EECS department at Michigan." (Biography)
- "Dr. Moghaddam joined the USC Ming Hsieh Department of Electrical Engineering in January 2012." (Biography)
- "She has also developed quantitative approaches for multisensor data fusion by combining radar and optical remote sensing data for nonlinear estimation of vegetation and surface parameters." (Research Summary)
- "She has been a a Systems Engineer for the Cassini Radar and the Science Chair of the JPL Team X (Advanced Mission Studies Team)." (Research Summary)
- "Vice Dean for Research" and "Director of Program, Center or Institute, Co-Director of the Center for Sustainability Solutions" (Appointments)

## Evidence and method

A standard faculty directory profile. It gives her degrees with years. She has a B.S. from the University of Kansas (1986), and an M.S., 1989, and a Ph.D., 1991, from the University of Illinois. It links a research summary file and a research website. The library has read neither.

## Limits
