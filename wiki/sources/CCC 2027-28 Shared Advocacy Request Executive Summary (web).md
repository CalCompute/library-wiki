## Bottom line

- The California Community Colleges (CCC) ask for $351.5 million one-time and $298.2 million ongoing in Proposition 98 funds for 2027-28. They also ask for other General Fund and bond money.
- One of six request groups is "The Future of Learning and Generative Artificial Intelligence". It totals $150 million one-time and $45 million ongoing in Proposition 98 funds.

## Background

The Board of Governors sends this request to the state each year. It ties the proposals to Vision 2030, the system's plan. The summary lists each proposal and its amount. Appendix E gives the detail of the AI proposals ([CCC 2027-28 Shared Advocacy Request Appendix E (web)](CCC%202027-28%20Shared%20Advocacy%20Request%20Appendix%20E%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Serving more than 2.2 million students across 116 colleges, the nation's largest and most diverse higher education system is a critical driver of educational attainment, workforce development, and economic mobility throughout the state." (p. 2)
- The request "includes $351.5 million in one-time and $298.2 million in ongoing Proposition 98 resources, along with a few proposals for technical policy changes." (p. 2)
- "Without a deliberate strategy, colleges will adopt AI unevenly, creating disparities in access, capacity, cybersecurity, and student outcomes across regions and communities." (p. 4)
- The AI requests are in "three interconnected areas: workforce capacity and professional learning, shared technology and cybersecurity infrastructure, and local innovation that can be scaled statewide." (p. 4)
- "Artificial Intelligence Workflows for Operational Efficiency and Classroom Success: $9 million one-time Proposition 98; $12 million ongoing Proposition 98" (p. 5, table)
- "In total, the budget request reflects $351.5 million one-time Proposition 98 General Fund requests, $298.2 million in ongoing Proposition 98 General Fund requests, approximately $77.8 million in General Fund requests, and $45 million in one-time Proposition 4 bond fund requests." (p. 5)

## Evidence and method

A budget summary with tables of proposals and amounts. It gives the system's size and the amounts asked. It gives no cost analysis.

## Limits

A request, not an enacted budget. The AI total above is the sum of the five AI lines in the summary tables. The summary itself does not give it. The summary does not name CENIC AIR or the partners; Appendix E does.
