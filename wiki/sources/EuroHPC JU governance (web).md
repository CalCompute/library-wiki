# EuroHPC JU governance (web)

## Citation

European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU). "Governance." EuroHPC JU website. No date on the page. https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/about/governance_en. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Three bodies govern EuroHPC JU: a Governing Board, an Executive Director and an Industrial and Scientific Advisory Board.
- The Governing Board has one representative of the European Commission and one of each Participating State. It makes the strategy and funding decisions, procurement included.
- An Access Resource Committee of independent experts helps to allocate the Union's share of access time on EuroHPC systems.

## Background

EuroHPC JU is a joint undertaking of the European Union, European countries and private partners. This page lists the members of the Governing Board by country, from Albania to the United Kingdom, Canada included. It also lists the members of the User Forum Coordination Group and the bureau of the Access Resource Committee.

## Key claims

- Bodies: "The governance of the EuroHPC Joint Undertaking is assured by three bodies: a Governing Board, an Executive Director and an Industrial and Scientific Advisory Board." (Governance)
- Board members: "The Commission and each Participating State appoint one representative to the Governing Board. Each representative may be accompanied by one expert." (Governing Board)
- Board role: "The Governing Board is responsible for strategic policy making and funding decisions related to the activities of the Joint Undertaking, including all the public procurement activities." (Governing Board)
- Executive Director: "He is the chief executive responsible for day-to-day management of the Joint Undertaking in accordance with the decisions of the Governing Board." (Executive Director)
- Advisory board: it "includes representatives of academia and industry as users and technology suppliers." It "provides independent advice on the strategic research and innovation agenda and on the acquisition and operation of the supercomputers, quantum computers and AI factories owned by the Joint Undertaking." (Industrial and Scientific Advisory Board)
- Users: "The User Forum Coordination Group (UFCG) was established to strengthen communication between EuroHPC JU and its user communities." (User Forum Coordination Group)
- Allocation: the Access Resource Committee supports "the allocation of the Union’s share of access time to EuroHPC supercomputers, AI Factories and quantum computers." It "ensures representation from academia, industry and the public sector." (Access Resource Committee)

## Evidence and method

This is a governance page from an EU body. It names the bodies, their roles and their members. It does not give voting rules or the legal basis.

## Limits

- It does not name the legal act that sets up EuroHPC JU.
