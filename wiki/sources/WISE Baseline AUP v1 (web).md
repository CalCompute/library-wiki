# WISE Baseline AUP v1 (web)

## Citation

Members of the WISE Community SCI Working Group. "The WISE Baseline Acceptable Use Policy and Conditions of Use." Version 1, Feb. 25, 2019 (template v1.0.1). PDF, Internet Archive copy of https://wise-community.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/WISE-SCI-Baseline-AUP-V1.pdf, captured May 15, 2021. http://web.archive.org/web/20210515203836id_/https://wise-community.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/WISE-SCI-Baseline-AUP-V1.pdf. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The WISE Baseline Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is a two-page template that any research community, agency or infrastructure can present to its users.
- It has a preamble, where the provider names itself and the purpose of use, and ten fixed clauses. The wording and numbering of clauses 1 to 10 "must not be changed".
- The clauses cover lawful use, credentials, security reports, personal data, and suspension "without prior notice and without compensation".

## Background

Members of the WISE Community SCI Working Group wrote this template. The AARC guide calls the group the "WISE Information Security for e-Infrastructures SCI working Group". The PDF says that it draws on the "EGI Acceptable Use Policy and Conditions of Use". It gives funding from the European Community's Horizon2020 Programme under the AARC2 grant (Grant Agreement No. 730941). The [AARC-I044 Implementers Guide to the WISE Baseline AUP (web)](AARC-I044%20Implementers%20Guide%20to%20the%20WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20%28web%29.md) explains how to use it.

## Key claims

- Template rule: "Other text should not be changed." Additions "must not conflict with the clauses 1 to 10 below, whose wording and numbering must not be changed." (page 1)
- Clause 1: "You shall only use the Services in a manner consistent with the purposes and limitations described above; you shall show consideration towards other users including by not causing harm to the Services; you have an obligation to collaborate in the resolution of issues arising from your use of the Services." (page 1)
- Clause 2: "You shall only use the Services for lawful purposes and not breach, attempt to breach, nor circumvent administrative or security controls." (page 2)
- Clause 4: "You shall protect your access credentials (e.g. passwords, private keys or multi-factor tokens); no intentional sharing is permitted." (page 2)
- Clause 7: "Reliance on the Services shall only be to the extent specified by any applicable service level agreements listed below. Use without such agreements is at your own risk." (page 2)
- Clause 9: "Your use of the Services may be restricted or suspended, for administrative, operational, or security reasons, without prior notice and without compensation." (page 2)
- Clause 10: "If you violate these rules, you may be liable for the consequences, which may include your account being suspended and a report being made to your home organisation or to law enforcement." (page 2)

## Evidence and method

This is a policy template. It gives fixed text and places for a provider to fill in. It has no data on which providers use it.

## Limits

- The template does not name the providers that use it.
- It has no clauses on data types, such as health or export-controlled data. A provider adds such terms as extra clauses.
- The library read an Internet Archive copy from 2021. The page does not say if WISE has a later version.
