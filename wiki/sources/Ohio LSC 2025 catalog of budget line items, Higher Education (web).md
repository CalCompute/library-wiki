## Bottom line

- The catalog describes each budget line item of the Ohio Department of Higher Education (ODHE). It gives actual spending for fiscal years (FY) 2022 to 2025 and appropriations for FY 2026 and FY 2027.
- Each entry also gives the legal basis and the purpose of the line item.
- The General Revenue Fund (GRF) line item for the Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC) is $5,086,000 in each of FY 2025, FY 2026 and FY 2027. The line item for the Ohio Academic Resources Network (OARnet) is $3,568,000 in each of those years.
- Both line items cite Ohio Revised Code (R.C.) 3333.04(T) and House Bill (H.B.) 96 of the 136th General Assembly as their legal basis.

## Background

The footer of each page names the "Legislative Budget Office of the Legislative Service Commission". This file is the part of the 2025 catalog that covers ODHE. It has 82 line items in the GRF and in other fund groups. Each entry has a six-year table of figures, a source of funds, a legal basis and a purpose.

## Key claims

- OSC: "This line item supports the operations of the Ohio Supercomputer Center, located at the Ohio State University. The center is a statewide high-performance computing resource available to faculty, students, and researchers at Ohio's public and private colleges and universities." (GRF 235510, p. 355)
- OSC and industry: "The resource is also made available to private industry on a cost-recovery basis." (GRF 235510, p. 355)
- OSC legal basis: "R.C. 3333.04(T); Sections 381.10 and 381.320 of H.B. 96 of the 136th G.A. (originally established by H.B. 171 of the 117th G.A.)" (GRF 235510, p. 355)
- OARnet: "This line item supports the operations of the Ohio Academic Resources Network (OARnet), a networking division of the Ohio Supercomputer Center." (GRF 235556, p. 364)
- OARnet users: "In addition to connecting institutions of higher education, OARnet also connects K-12 schools, local and state government, certain healthcare facilities, and public broadcasting stations." (GRF 235556, p. 364)
- Network security earmark: "In FY 2026 and FY 2027, H.B. 96 earmarks up to $1.2 million in each fiscal year for the same purpose. A portion of these earmarked funds may also be used by the Ohio Academic Resource Network (OARnet) and the Ohio Supercomputer Center to enhance their respective network security operations" (GRF 235321, Operating Expenses, p. 343)
- Shared IT for ODHE: the line item "supports the development and implementation of information technology solutions designed to improve ODHE's performance and capacity, which is provided by OH-TECH." (GRF 235417, Technology Maintenance and Operations, p. 346)

Other entries that touch on technology:

- GRF 235321, Operating Expenses (p. 343), carried an earmark of "$1.5 million in each fiscal year" in FY 2024 and FY 2025. The earmark paid for network security for all members of OH-TECH, "Ohio's statewide higher education technology consortium".
- GRF 235425, Ohio Work Ready Grant (p. 347), earmarks $500,000 in FY 2026 and FY 2027 for "five competitive grants of $100,000 each to community colleges to implement artificial intelligence (AI) initiatives".
- Fund 7014 line item 235639, Research Incentive Third Frontier - Tax (p. 381), is $8,000,000 in FY 2026 and FY 2027. It earmarks "up to $750,000 in each fiscal year for research regarding cyber security initiatives".

## Limits

- The catalog gives state GRF money only for OSC and OARnet. It does not give their total budgets, fee income, federal grants or the Ohio State University's share.
- It does not give OSC hardware, user numbers or the cost-recovery rates for industry.
- The OH-TECH earmark is an amount "up to" $1.2 million. The catalog does not say how much of it goes to OSC or OARnet.
- The catalog spells the network two ways: "Ohio Academic Resource Network" (p. 343) and "Ohio Academic Resources Network" (p. 364).
- The appropriation act, H.B. 96 of the 136th General Assembly, is the authority for the amounts. The library has not read it.
- [OSC governance (web)](OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md) says that the Ohio General Assembly funds OSC through ODHE. This catalog gives the amounts of that funding.
