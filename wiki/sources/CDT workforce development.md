# CDT workforce development

## Citation

California Department of Technology. "Training." Web page, cdt.ca.gov, date not stated. https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-26 CDT workforce development.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-26%20CDT%20workforce%20development.md) (original: `.html` beside it), fetched 2026-10-26. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- CDT runs a Training & Education Center for "public sector IT professionals": three leadership academies, three boot camps, a mainframe academy and online courses (PACe).
- It has a "GenAI Boot Camp" and a "Generative AI Technical Training" page. The state already trains its IT staff in AI.
- It does not list training in high-performance computing or in running AI infrastructure.

## Background

See [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md).

## Key claims

- Academies: Information Technology Leadership Academy; Project Management Leadership Academy; Information Security Leadership Academy, which "prepares candidates for the critical roles of Information Security Officer (ISO)".
- "GenAI Boot Camp": "designed for public sector professionals interested in building practical GenAI skills. Participants will learn responsible use practices, hands-on applications for government work".
- Emerging IT Leaders and Cybersecurity boot camps.
- "zSystems Academy": "A six-week training program designed for public-sector IT professionals who are new to mainframe technology".
- "PACe offers low cost yet robust training for IT and business professionals."
