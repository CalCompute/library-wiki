# ESP launches Economic Security California 2024-01-30 (web)

## Citation

Economic Security Project. "Economic Security Project launches state affiliate: Economic Security California." Press release, Sacramento, January 30, 2024. https://economicsecurityproject.org/news/economic-security-project-launches-state-affiliate-economic-security-california/. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- Economic Security California (ESCA) is the California state affiliate of the Economic Security Project (ESP). They are one organization family, not two separate groups.
- Teri Olle is the Director of ESCA. The SB-53 sponsor named "Economic Security California Action" in the Wiener release is the ESCA side of ESP.
- ESCA's 2024 agenda includes "devising new policies and guardrails around emerging AI".

## Background

ESP is a national organization. It is known for the Stockton guaranteed income pilot and for antimonopoly work. The release launches its California affiliate.

## Key claims

- ESP "proudly unveils its California state affiliate, Economic Security California (ESCA)".
- "The introduction of ESCA formalizes over seven years of work Economic Security Project has led in California."
- ESCA builds economic power by "challenging corporate consolidation, which includes devising new policies and guardrails around emerging AI."
- Teri Olle is "Director of Economic Security California". She names "AI, guaranteed income and tax policy" as ESCA's areas.
- The 2024 agenda includes "working with policymakers to harness the power of AI for the public interest".

## Evidence and method

An organization press release. It states the organization's own structure, so it is a primary source for that fact.

## Limits

- The release does not mention an "Action" arm. The name "Economic Security California Action" comes from the [Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md). It is probably ESCA's advocacy arm. This release does not confirm that.
- The release is from January 2024 and does not mention CalCompute.
