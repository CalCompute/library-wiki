# Morgan Stanley AI Market Trends 2026 (web)

## Citation

Morgan Stanley. "AI Is Now a Macro Variable. Are You Positioned?" Morgan Stanley Institute article, Morgan Stanley, March 9, 2026. https://www.morganstanley.com/insights/articles/ai-market-trends-institute-2026. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Morgan Stanley Research estimates about $2.9 trillion of global data center construction cost through 2028. It says that more than 80 percent of AI infrastructure spending is still ahead.
- It splits that figure by source of capital. Hyperscaler cash flows cover $1.4 trillion. Private credit has an $800 billion opportunity. Other capital gives $350 billion, corporate debt $200 billion and securitized credit $150 billion.

## Background

Morgan Stanley is an investment bank and wealth manager. The Morgan Stanley Institute publishes articles for investors and companies. This article collects views of the firm's research, investment banking and wealth management staff on AI. It says that it is "not a Research Report as defined under FINRA regulations".

## Key claims

- "With ~$2.9 trillion in global data center construction projected through 2028, AI has become a structural force in economic expansion" (Why It Matters)
- "Morgan Stanley Research estimates that nearly $3 trillion of AI-related infrastructure investment will flow through the global economy by 2028, with more than 80% of that spending still ahead." (The Big Picture)
- The chart "Est. $2.9tr Global Capex on Data Centers (2025-2028)" lists "$1.4tr Covered by Hyperscaler Cash Flows", "$200bn Corporate Debt Issuance", "$150bn Securitized Credit Inssuance (ABS, CMBS)", "$800bn Opportunity for Private Credit via Asset-based Finance & Debt Funding of JVs" and "$350bn Other Capital (Primarily PE, VC, Sovereign)". (Section 1)
- "This dynamic was on display in 2025, when Morgan Stanley advised Meta on the $27 billion structured JV for the U.S. AI data-center campus." (Section 3)
- "As the U.S. and China compete for AI leadership—across chips, compute, energy and data—tighter export controls, higher tariffs and localization pressures could fragment supply chains and raise costs." (Section 5)
- "The impact of geopolitics increases the value of **secure, domestic infrastructure**." (Section 5)

## Evidence and method

Market commentary from a financial firm. It quotes estimates from Morgan Stanley Research reports and links to them, but does not show their methods. It ends with quotes from named staff for investors and for companies.

## Limits

The article is investment commentary with a disclaimer; Morgan Stanley "makes no express or implied representations or warranties as to its accuracy or completeness". The figures are forecasts. The article gives no figures for California or for public compute. It does not show the method for the $2.9 trillion.
