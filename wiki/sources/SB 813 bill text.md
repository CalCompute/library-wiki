## Bottom line

- SB 813 adds Government Code sections 8898 to 8898.4, "California Artificial Intelligence Safety Independent Verification Organizations".
- Independent verification organizations (IVOs) assess the risks of AI systems and models. By Jan. 1, 2028, the Government Operations Agency (GovOps) must set their application requirements, criteria and suspension procedures.
- The agency must convene working groups and report their findings to the Legislature. An IVO reports each year. The chapter does not require any developer to use an IVO.

## Key claims

- "“AI auditor” means a person, partnership, academic institution, nonprofit, or corporation that conducts a covered audit on behalf of a third party." (Gov. Code § 8898(c))
- "“Independent verification organization” or “IVO” means an AI auditor that is designated by the agency as having demonstrated expertise in assessing the risks posed by an AI system or model and identifying the metrics and methodologies that form the basis for that assessment." (§ 8898(e))
- "An IVO may accept payment from a party being assessed at reasonable market rates but shall not accept terms which condition any payment or the amount of any payment on the results of their assessment." (§ 8898.1(c)(2)(C))
- "The agency shall convene working groups to solicit stakeholder input in the identification of standards and the development and revision of procedures, requirements, and criteria pursuant to this chapter. Working groups shall include, but not be limited to, engineers from AI companies that are competitors and AI safety experts." (§ 8898.2(b)(1))
- The agency "shall consult as appropriate with" a list that includes "(3) Academic institutions." (§ 8898.2(a))
- "Require any person, partnership, or corporation that develops, deploys, or operates an AI system or model to engage an IVO or to undergo a covered AI audit as a condition of developing, deploying, or operating an AI system or model in this state." (§ 8898.4(a)(3), one of the things the chapter does not do)

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate and no fee. It does not set the IVO criteria itself; GovOps sets them. It does not mention research compute or CalCompute. An audit under its standards is "relevant to, but not conclusive of" a harm action (§ 8898.4(b)).
