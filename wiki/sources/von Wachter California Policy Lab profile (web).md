# von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)

## Citation

California Policy Lab (CPL). "Till von Wachter." Staff profile, date not stated. https://capolicylab.org/staff/till-von-wachter/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Till von Wachter is a Professor of Economics at the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA). He is also Faculty Director of the California Policy Lab's UCLA site.
- His current research includes the effects of AI on the labor market, for example the California AI-Unemployment Tracker.

## Background

The California Policy Lab is a research institute at the University of California. This is its staff page for Till von Wachter. It gives his research topics, his funders and his advisory work.

## Key claims

- "Till von Wachter is a Professor of Economics at the University of California Los Angeles and Faculty Director of the California Policy Lab’s UCLA site." (profile)
- "His current research projects focus on the effects of AI on the labor market (such as the recently released California AI Unemployment Tracker), how workforce services help affected workers, and how AI can improve service outcomes." (profile)
- "Professor von Wachter’s research has been supported by the National Science Foundation, the National Institute on Aging, the Social Security Administration, the Smith Richardson Foundation, the Sloan Foundation, the Russell Sage Foundation, the Hilton Foundation, and Arnold Ventures." (profile)
- He "has provided expert assistance to the City and County of Los Angeles, the State of California, the U.S. Department of Labor, the Canadian Labor Ministry, the OECD, the United Nations, and the IMF." (profile)
