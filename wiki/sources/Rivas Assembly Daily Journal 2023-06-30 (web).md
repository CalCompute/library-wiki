## Bottom line

- The Journal records the oath of Robert Rivas as the 71st Speaker of the California State Assembly on June 30, 2023.
- The Assembly elected him Speaker by House Resolution No. 1 on Dec. 5, 2022.

## Background

The official daily record of the Assembly for June 30, 2023. It records the special order of business for the swearing-in of the Speaker and the text of his address. This is a profile page. His entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "Pursuant to the election of Robert Rivas to the Office of Speaker to the Assembly by the adoption of House Resolution No. 1 on December 5, 2022" (page 2515)
- "At 10:35 a.m., Speaker-designate Robert Rivas appeared at the bar of the Assembly and took and subscribed to the following oath of office administered by the Honorable Patricia Guerrero, Chief Justice of California." (page 2515)
- "Speaker pro Tempore Ward then presented the 71st Speaker of the California State Assembly, the Honorable Robert Rivas" (page 2516)
- Address by Speaker Rivas: "my grandfather, he was a farmworker his entire life and I grew up in farmworker housing. And today, I stand before you as Speaker of the California State Assembly." (address)
