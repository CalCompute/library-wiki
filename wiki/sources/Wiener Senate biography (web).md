## Bottom line

- Scott Wiener represents District 11 in the California State Senate. Voters first elected him in 2016.
- The page lists his committee roles, his work before the Senate, his education and a list of laws that he authored.

## Background

The full biography on the Senator's official office website. It lists his committee posts, his career and his major laws by topic. This is a profile page. His entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "First elected in 2016, Senator Scott Wiener represents District 11 in the California State Senate. District 11 includes San Francisco, Broadmoor, Colma, and Daly City, as well as portions of South San Francisco and San Bruno." (paragraph 1)
- "Senator Wiener has authored over 100 bills that have been signed into law" (paragraph 3)
- "Senator Wiener serves as Chair of the Senate Elections Committee. He previously served as Chair of the Senate Budget Committee, the Senate Housing Committee, and the Senate Human Services Committee." (paragraph 4)
- "Senator Wiener is a member of the Public Safety Committee, Judiciary Committee, Transportation Committee, and Privacy Committee." (paragraph 4)
- "Before his election to the Senate, Senator Wiener served on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors, representing the district previously represented by Supervisor Harvey Milk." (paragraph 5)
- "Senator Wiener spent fifteen years practicing law: as a Deputy City Attorney in the San Francisco City Attorney's Office, in private practice at Heller Ehrman White & McAuliffe, and as a law clerk for Justice Alan Handler on the New Jersey Supreme Court." (paragraph 6)
- "He received a bachelor's degree from Duke University and a law degree from Harvard Law School. He spent a year in Chile on a Fulbright Scholarship doing historical research." (paragraph 7)
- "**SB 822**, which enacted the strongest net neutrality protections in the nation" (Technology)

## Evidence and method

The Senator's office writes this text about the Senator. The list of laws is his office's own selection.

## Limits

It has no dates for his time on the Board of Supervisors or in the City Attorney's Office. Its list of laws does not name SB 1047 or SB 53.
