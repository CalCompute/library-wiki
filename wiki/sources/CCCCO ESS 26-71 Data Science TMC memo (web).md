## Bottom line

- The Chancellor's Office releases a new Data Science Transfer Model Curriculum (TMC). The Intersegmental Curriculum Council approved it in May 2026.
- The memo gives the codes for the degree, TOP Code 1799.00 and CIP 30.7001. It says that the TMC falls under Education Code section 66749.81, which allows up to 66 units for high-unit STEM pathways.

## Background

A Transfer Model Curriculum is the statewide template for an Associate Degree for Transfer (ADT) to the California State University. Colleges build their degree from it and submit it with the ADT form ([CCCCO Data Science ADT form (web)](CCCCO%20Data%20Science%20ADT%20form%20%28web%29.md)). The memo goes to college chief executive and instructional officers, the Academic Senate and curriculum specialists.

## Key claims

- "The purpose of this memo is to detail the release of a new Data Science Transfer Model Curriculum (TMC)." (p. 1)
- "From guidance of the Faculty Discipline Review Groups (FDRGs) April 2026, the Intersegmental Curriculum Council (ICC) voted May 2026 to approve the new TMC." (p. 1)
- TOP Code "1799.00"; Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) "30.7001" (p. 1, table)
- "In addition, the new TMC does fall under California Education Code 66749.81." (p. 1)
- "ADT pathways may be established that contain up to, but no more than, 66 units of lower division coursework" (p. 1, quoting Education Code section 66749.81(b)(2))

## Evidence and method

An administrative memo. It announces a decision, gives codes for the curriculum inventory and quotes the Education Code. It gives no data on students or colleges.

## Limits

The memo does not list the courses; the ADT form does. The title cell of the code table reads only "Science" in the PDF text. It does not say how many colleges will offer the degree.
