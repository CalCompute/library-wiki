# Apereo institutional members (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Institutional Members: OSPO as a Service." Membership web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/join-us/institutional-members. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Apereo Institutional membership gives an institution the services of an Open Source Program Office (OSPO), as "OSPO as a Service".
- The annual contribution goes from $1,080 for a member budget up to $25 million, to $8,100 for a budget of more than $4 billion.
- Each Institutional Member gets "One organizational board seat vote in foundation elections".

## Background

This page on apereo.org describes the Institutional membership. It lists the consulting services in eleven areas, from legal to communications. It gives a fee table by the member's annual budget and two example member profiles. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Service: "Apereo Foundation provides a “center of excellence” for the institution’s internal open source operations and structure, acting as an Open Source Program Office for the Institutional Member" (OSPO as a Service)
- Who it is for: "Institutional memberships are designed to support a range of organizations, from those actively involved in open source software development to others who simply rely on open source technologies as end-users." (OSPO as a Service)
- Services: the list includes "**Infrastructure:** Cloud Hosting, Enterprise Applications, Web & Network Services, Code Repositories, Bug & Issue Tracking, Email, Groups & Lists" and "**Fundraising:** Grants & Gifts, Partnerships, Sponsorships" (Membership Benefits)
- Vote: "**One organizational board seat vote in foundation elections**" (Membership Benefits)
- Extra service: "Should organizations require dedicated or increased service levels, each of the above can be offered through extended contracting." (Membership Benefits)
- Fee table (Institutional Membership Contribution, by member's annual budget):
  - "$0 to $25 million USD": "$1,080.00"
  - "$25 million to $100 million USD": "$2,340.00"
  - "$100 million to $750 million USD": "$4,860.00"
  - "$750 million to $4 billion USD": "$6,300.00"
  - "More than $4 billion USD": "$8,100.00"

## Evidence and method

This is a membership and price page. It gives a fee table and a list of services. The page calls its two member profiles, "Leslie State" and "Technische Forschungsanstalt Berlin", "Example Institutional Member Profiles". The page does not say that they are real institutions.

## Limits

- The page gives no number of Institutional Members and no list of them.
- It does not say what "50% of the cost for core foundation membership and Apereo services are available on a contract basis" means in dollars.
- It gives no service levels or response times.
