# OSC at a glance (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "OSC at a Glance." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/about. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- OSC serves "a diverse state and national community": students, faculty, scientists, engineers and clinicians.
- Its mission is to serve Ohio higher education institutions, public and private, and private industry. It offers computing, data storage, software and technical education. Its history goes back to 1987.

## Background

This is the "At A Glance" page in the About section of the OSC website. It has four short parts: Who We Are, What We Do, Our Affiliations and Our History. Each part links to a longer page.

## Key claims

- "The Ohio Supercomputer Center provides computing, storage, software, and educational resources and expertise to a diverse state and national community, including students, faculty, scientists, engineers and clinicians." (Who We Are)
- "At the Ohio Supercomputer Center, our mission is to empower Ohio higher educational institutions, both public and private, as well as private industry, by providing capable, accessible, reliable and secure computational services enhanced by training, consulting and research partnership." (Who We Are)
- "High-performance research computing", "Large capacity data storage", "Scientific software packages", "Technical education and expertise" (What We Do)
- "OSC continues to build upon a rich history of innovation and service going back to 1987." (Our History)

## Evidence and method

An institutional summary page. It states the mission and the user groups. It gives no figures.

## Limits

The page is a summary of about 150 words of body text. It does not give the history, affiliations or service terms; it links to other pages for them. This library has not read those pages.
