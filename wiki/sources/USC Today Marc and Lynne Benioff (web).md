# USC Today Marc and Lynne Benioff (web)

## Citation

Lynn Lipinski. "Marc and Lynne Benioff’s $20 million gift to USC supports research and treatment innovations to fight cancer." USC Today, University of Southern California (USC), September 29, 2016. https://today.usc.edu/?p=39136. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Marc and Lynne Benioff gave $20 million to USC for the building of the Lawrence J. Ellison Institute for Transformative Medicine of USC, a cancer center in Los Angeles.
- The article gives short biographies of both donors. Marc Benioff is a USC trustee and a USC graduate.

## Background

USC Today is the news site of USC. The article says that Marc Benioff is "chairman and CEO of Salesforce" and has been a USC trustee "since 2010". It says that Lynne Benioff is "an independent marketing consultant and philanthropist".

## Key claims

- The Benioffs "have given $20 million toward construction of the Lawrence J. Ellison Institute for Transformative Medicine of USC, a new center in Los Angeles that will combine interdisciplinary research with the holistic prevention and treatment of cancer." (paragraph 1)
- Marc Benioff created "the 1-1-1 model of philanthropy, donating 1 percent of Salesforce’s equity, employee time and product to nonprofits and educational institutions" (paragraph 2)
- "Today, more than 1,000 companies around the world have adopted this model through Pledge 1%." (paragraph 2)
- "The Benioffs have focused their extensive personal philanthropy on children’s health, public education, homelessness and other social issues." (paragraph 3)
- Lynne Benioff "is a member of the board for several organizations, including the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) Foundation, UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospital Oakland and Common Sense Media." (paragraph 5)
- "We truly believe that by empowering and funding institutions doing important cancer research we will be able to change the course of this disease." (Marc Benioff)

## Evidence and method

A university news article about one gift, with biographies of the donors. It quotes USC President C. L. Max Nikias, Marc Benioff and David B. Agus, who leads the institute. It says that the institute "will house interdisciplinary cancer research laboratories that will harness proteomics, molecular biology, genetics and nanotechnology".

## Limits

The article is from 2016. It does not mention computing infrastructure or AI, apart from the phrase "A pioneer of cloud computing" about Marc Benioff. The library also has a UCSF article on an earlier Benioff gift: [UCSF Childrens Hospital Benioff 100 million gift 2010 (web)](UCSF%20Childrens%20Hospital%20Benioff%20100%20million%20gift%202010%20%28web%29.md).
