# CalCareers IT Specialist III exam bulletin (web)

## Citation

California Department of Human Resources (CalHR). "Exam Bulletin: Information Technology Specialist III." Exam code EX-1752, CalCareers, continuous filing, date not stated. https://calcareers.ca.gov/CalHrPublic/Exams/ExamBulletin.aspx?ExamControlId=1752. Read 2026-11-09. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-09 CalCareers IT Specialist III exam bulletin.html`.

## Bottom line

- The bulletin is the open, servicewide exam for the state class Information Technology Specialist III, the "expert advisor level" of state IT work.
- The pay range is $9,230 to $12,369 each month. Applicants need one year as an IT Specialist II, or six years of IT experience. A degree can take the place of part of that experience.

## Background

CalHR runs the state civil service exams on CalCareers. An applicant who passes with 70 percent goes on an eligible list that departments use to fill jobs in the whole state. The exam is an online training and experience questionnaire.

## Key claims

- "INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY SPECIALIST III: $9,230.00 - $12,369.00 per Month" (Classification Details)
- "One year of experience as an Information Technology Specialist II; **or** Six years of information technology experience, two years of which shall include performance of highly specialized work in a lead or expert capacity on highly complex or specialized information technology systems or services in one or more of the major six domains or a closely related or emerging information technology field." (Minimum Qualifications)
- "A master's or doctorate degree from an accredited college or university may substitute for four years of the required general information technology experience." (Minimum Qualifications)
- "This is the expert advisor level." and "Incumbents represent the highest level of expertise available in state service within the Software Engineering, Information Security Engineering, Information Technology Project Management, or System Engineering domains." (Position Description)
- "A final score of 70% must be attained to be placed on the eligible list." (Examination Information)
- "Resulting Eligibility List(s) will be used to fill vacancies throughout the State of California." (Eligible List Information)

## Evidence and method

An official exam bulletin. It gives the pay range, minimum qualifications, duties, the knowledge and abilities the exam tests, and the 17 task statements of the questionnaire.

## Limits

The bulletin does not name AI, machine learning or research computing among its domains or tasks. It does not give how many people hold the class; [CalHR 5102 Statewide Report 2026-06-30 (web)](CalHR%205102%20Statewide%20Report%202026-06-30%20%28web%29.md) does. The page also had notices that the IT Series exams were offline from Nov. 5 to Nov. 17, 2026.
