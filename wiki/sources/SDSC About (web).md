# SDSC About (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "About." Web page, date not stated. https://www.sdsc.edu/about/index.html. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- SDSC is at UC San Diego and is part of its Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing (HSDSC). It has five areas of expertise and works at three scopes: national, state and University of California (UC).
- At the state scope, it works on wildfires, drought, extreme weather and earthquakes. At the UC scope, it gives UC faculty and researchers computing and data resources.

## Background

This is the "About" page of the SDSC website. It gives the areas of expertise, the three scopes, the mission and the vision. It quotes SDSC Director Frank Würthwein. It describes the place of SDSC in HSDSC, which the UC Board of Regents established on July 18, 2024. It names a partnership with the Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California (CENIC) on the CENIC AIR project.

## Key claims

- "Located at UC San Diego, SDSC engages millions of researchers, educators, students and multi-sector partners every year through its five key areas of expertise" (SDSC is a leader)
- "State – protecting the California way of life with approaches to managing wildfires, drought, extreme weather and earthquake disasters" (three geographic scopes)
- "University of California (UC) – providing the UC system and UC San Diego faculty and researchers with computational and data resources to accelerate discovery." (three geographic scopes)
- "SDSC enables discovery by architecting and operating innovative supercomputers; delivering research computing services and support; and providing infrastructure services including cloud, network, storage and data center colocation facilities." (page header)
- "SDSC is a foundational pillar of the Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing (HSDSC) at UC San Diego. Established July 18, 2024, by a unanimous vote of the UC Board of Regents" (Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing)
- "As part of HSDSC, SDSC also translates data science from classroom to research and operational scale through its partnership with The Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California (CENIC) and the CENIC Artificial Intelligence Resource project" (Halıcıoğlu School of Data Science and Computing)
- "SDSC is a global leader in delivering integrated data and computing solutions that enable translational research, multi-sector partnerships and workforce development." (Vision)

## Evidence and method

An institutional web page. It gives the scopes, the expertise areas, the mission and the vision, and the founding date of HSDSC. It gives no budget, staff count, system list or user count, apart from "millions" of people it engages each year.

## Limits

The page does not name SDSC's computers or its access routes.
