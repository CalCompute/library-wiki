# CSU UAW Unit 11 tentative agreement 2026-10-16 (web)

## Citation

The California State University and United Auto Workers 4123. "CSU/UAW 4123 Tentative Agreement – Subject to ratification by both Parties." Dated 10/16/2026; file name "Signed TA". California State University. https://www.calstate.edu/csu-system/faculty-staff/labor-and-employee-relations/Documents/unit11-uaw/UAWCSU-10162026-Signed-TA.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-22. Read 2026-11-22.

## Bottom line

- This is the tentative agreement between the California State University (CSU) and United Auto Workers (UAW) 4123 on a successor contract for Unit 11. Article 27 sets a term to Oct. 31, 2028.
- For fiscal year 2025/2026, CSU puts $3.2 million into a pool for one-time payments. Salaries go up 4 percent from July 1, 2026, and 4 percent for 2027/2028.
- Instructional Student Assistants accrue paid sick leave from Aug. 1, 2027.

## Background

Unit 11 holds CSU's Teaching Associates, Graduate Assistants and Instructional Student Assistants ([CSU UAW Unit 11 contract extension 2026-03-02 (web)](CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%20extension%202026-03-02%20%28web%29.md)). That memorandum extends the prior contract, [CSU UAW Unit 11 contract 2023-2025 (web)](CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%202023-2025%20%28web%29.md), to Sept. 30, 2026. A table of the 27 articles and an "ISA Classification" item gives a position for each: "Status Quo", "TA" or a numbered CSU proposal. The texts of the changed articles follow.

## Key claims

- "Include all current Appendices, Side Letters and MOUs" (table of articles)
- "This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect up to and including October 31, 2028." (Article 27, 27.1)
- "For fiscal year 2025/2026, the CSU shall allocate a total pool of $3.2 million for one-time payments to eligible UAW-represented employees and any applicable associated employer payroll taxes." (Article 18, 18.2 a)
- "For fiscal year 2026/2027, the salary of all bargaining unit employees in active pay status (or on leave) shall be increased by four percent" (Article 18, 18.2 b)
- "Accrual of Sick Leave for Instructional Student Assistants (TO BE IMPLEMENTED EFFECTIVE 8/1/2027)" (Article 20)
- "employees shall accrue one (1) hour of paid sick leave for each 30 hours of work." (Article 20, X.1)
- "any term(s) of this Agreement that carries an economic cost shall not be implemented until the amount required therefore is appropriated and made available to the CSU for expenditure for such purposes." (Article 27, 27.5)

## Evidence and method

A tentative agreement between a public employer and a union. Its first page says it is "Subject to ratiﬁcation by both Parties". It gives the changed contract text, not data.

## Limits

- The document does not say whether the members or the CSU Board of Trustees ratified it.
- The Markdown copy keeps the text that the agreement strikes out. Article 27.1 reads "October 31, 2028.October 31, 2025.", and Article 18.2 keeps the old increases for 2023-24 and 2024-25. Read the original for the marked changes.
- In the copy, the table of articles and the column of positions are out of line, so the position of each article is not clear. Read the original.
- The text does not name artificial intelligence or technology.
- The extension ended on Sept. 30, 2026, and this agreement is dated Oct. 16, 2026. The document does not say what applied between those dates.
