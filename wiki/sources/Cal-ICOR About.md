# Cal-ICOR About

## Citation

Cal-ICOR, UC Berkeley. "About the Cal-ICOR Initiative." Web page, cal-icor.org, date not stated. https://www.cal-icor.org/about/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Partners:
  - UC Berkeley (lead) and the California Education Learning Lab
  - faculty across UC, CSU and community colleges
  - the California Alliance for Data Science Education and the California Engineering Liaison Council
  - 2i2c, "Providing guidance on JupyterHub architecture"
- The page lists five staff, all with Berkeley addresses: Edwin Vargas Navarro, Eric Van Dusen, John DeNero, Sean Morris and Shane Knapp.
- Cal-ICOR calls itself "statewide educational infrastructure", "not as a short-term project".

## Key claims

- "a statewide effort to expand high-quality, accessible data science education across the University of California, California State University, and California Community College systems."
- "Led by UC Berkeley and supported by the California Education Learning Lab".
- UC Berkeley is the "Lead institution providing curriculum development, infrastructure expertise, and statewide coordination."
- Learning Lab is "Supporting the initiative's development, research, and cross-segment collaboration."
- Goal: integrate data science "without requiring specialized technical staff or costly IT investments."
- Cal-ICOR "strengthens pathways into data science for tens of thousands of students".

## Evidence and method

The program's own page. It lists staff by name and email, with no titles.

## Limits

- No titles, full-time counts, budget or funding amount.
- "tens of thousands of students" is not a count. The homepage counts 4,369 students all time ([Cal-ICOR homepage](Cal-ICOR%20homepage.md)).
