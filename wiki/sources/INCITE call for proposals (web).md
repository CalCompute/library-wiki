# INCITE call for proposals (web)

## Citation

DOE Leadership Computing, Innovative and Novel Computational Impact on Theory and Experiment (INCITE) program. "INCITE Call for Proposals." Web page, date not stated (the menu calls it the "2027 Call for Proposals"). https://doeleadershipcomputing.org/call-for-proposals/. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20. Not rehosted: the host is not a government host and the page states "All Rights Reserved".

## Bottom line

- The page is the full INCITE call for awards of time on the Frontier and Aurora exascale systems for calendar year 2027. Proposals were due on June 15, 2026.
- Researchers in and outside the United States, from academia, national laboratories, industry and federal agencies, can apply. Awards are for one to three years. The call expects awards of 500,000 to 2 million node-hours.
- A peer review and a technical review of each facility decide the awards. The call allows proprietary use with full cost recovery. Each institution must sign the user agreement of the facility.

## Background

The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Science runs the Argonne and Oak Ridge Leadership Computing Facility (LCF) centers. INCITE allocates up to 60 percent of their time. This page is the call on the program's own website. The news article on the same call is [OLCF INCITE 2027 call for proposals (web)](OLCF%20INCITE%202027%20call%20for%20proposals%20%28web%29.md). The awards of the 2026 call are in [INCITE 2026 announcement (web)](INCITE%202026%20announcement%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Up to 60% of the allocatable time on the **Frontier** exascale system at the Oak Ridge Leadership Computing Facility (OLCF), and the exascale system **Aurora**, at the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF) will be allocated for calendar year (CY) 2027 through the INCITE program." (introduction)
- "For each resource, allocations are anticipated to be between 500K – 2M node-hours on Aurora and Frontier. Individual awards may be higher." (introduction)
- "US and non-US based Individuals and/or teams of researchers from academia, national laboratories, industry, and federal agencies are eligible to apply, and awards of one, two, or three years may be granted." (Overview)
- "INCITE considers requests regardless of funding source." (Eligibility)
- "Requests smaller than 20% of the average award on any particular resource, and that do not make a strong case for the need for leadership computing resources, may be more appropriate for another LCF User Program" (Eligibility)
- "once an award is granted, the project will be assigned individual scientific and technical support." (Resources)
- "Potential impact is the predominant determinant for awards." (Selection)
- "Upon receipt of an INCITE award, each participating institution on the awarded proposal must accept or already have signed the user agreement" (User agreements)
- "Proprietary use (e.g., using the resources to generate data or results that they wish to designate as proprietary) of the HPC resources is allowed and will include full cost recovery through a proprietary user agreement." (Proprietary use)
- "The LCF centers have implemented cybersecurity programs at a moderate baseline with compensatory controls in accordance with the National Institute of Standards and Technology Special Publication 800-53." (Security)
- "The LCF centers cannot guarantee zero risks to information stored on their systems. Requests for processing sensitive (e.g., proprietary, or export-controlled) data must therefore be clearly identified in the proposal." (Data sensitivity and protection)

## Evidence and method

A program web page of about 2,000 words. It states the rules of the call: eligibility, the Early Career Track, resources, selection, user agreements, proprietary use, reporting, security, data protection and deadlines. A table gives the review criteria for new proposals and renewals, and the members of the INCITE Awards Committee.

## Limits

The page gives no cost of the time for open research, no number of awards and no success rate. It does not give the user agreements.

The page names two years for the same call. It says "INCITE is currently soliciting proposals of research for awards on LCF HPC machines for calendar year (CY) 2026". The next sentence says "CY 2027". Its Deadlines section says that access is "granted over the 2026 calendar year". Its menu and its June 15, 2026 deadline match the call for 2027. The page does not mention CalCompute.
