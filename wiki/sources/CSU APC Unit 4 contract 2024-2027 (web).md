## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the California State University (CSU) and Academic Professionals of California (APC) for Unit 4, Academic Support. Its term ends on June 30, 2027.
- A campus President can contract out work when the President finds it necessary. For contracting out of six months or more, CSU must notify the union, and the union can ask to meet and confer on its impact.
- Professional development includes the fee waiver program and training of direct benefit to the campus.

## Background

APC represents CSU's Unit 4. Appendix A of the agreement lists the unit's classes, for example Credential Analyst, Evaluator, Student Services Professional, Lead Library Assistant and Extended Education Specialist. The Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations Act (HEERA) governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The Trustees of the California State University recognize the Academic Professionals of California, the Union, as the sole and exclusive bargaining representative for bargaining Unit 4, as defined by PERB." (Article 1, 1.1)
- "The CSU retains and reserves unto itself, without limitation, whether exercised or not, all powers, rights, authorities, duties and responsibilities which have not been specifically abridged, delegated or modified by this Agreement." (Article 6, 6.1)
- "When the President deems it necessary in order to carry out the mission and operations of the campus, the President may contract out work." (Article 7, 7.1)
- "When the contracting out is to be on a long-term basis, the CSU shall notify the Union. The Union may request to meet and confer on the impact of contracting out work when such contracting out is to be on a long-term basis. “Long-term basis” in this provision means six (6) months or more." (Article 7, 7.2)
- "training directly of benefit to the campus;" (Article 16, 16.1.b, in the list of professional development opportunities)
- "This Agreement shall become effective upon ratification by both parties and shall remain in full force and effect up to and including June 30, 2027." (Article 34, 34.1)
- "Any term(s) of this Agreement which is deemed to carry an economic cost shall not be implemented until the amount required therefore is appropriated and made available to the CSU for expenditure for such purpose(s)." (Article 34, 34.4)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university system and a union. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The agreement has no article on technological change or artificial intelligence. A search of its text finds no use of "technology" or "technological".
- Its contracting out article has no limit on displacement. The agreement for Units 2, 5, 7 and 9 has one ([CSU CSUEU contract Units 2 5 7 9 2023-2026 (web)](CSU%20CSUEU%20contract%20Units%202%205%207%209%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md)).
