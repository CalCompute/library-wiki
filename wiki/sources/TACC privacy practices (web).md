# TACC privacy practices (web)

## Citation

Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC), The University of Texas at Austin. "Notice of Privacy Practices." TACC website, Security and Compliance section, date not stated. https://tacc.utexas.edu/about/security-and-compliance/privacy-practices/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- TACC gives a Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) notice on protected health information (PHI). It can collect PHI for approved projects or host it for the project owners.
- The project owner, for example the principal investigator, gets consent and gives the privacy notices. TACC applies access controls, encryption and monitoring.

## Background

TACC is a research computing center at The University of Texas at Austin. The notice is one paragraph in the Security and Compliance section of its website. Its general user rules are in [TACC user policies (web)](TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md), and its GDPR statement is in [TACC EU GDPR page (web)](TACC%20EU%20GDPR%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "In accordance with HIPAA, this notice describes how protected health information (PHI) may be collected, used, and disclosed and how individuals can access their information." (Collection, Use, and Disclosure of Protected Health Information)
- "The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC), in support of authorized projects, may collect and process PHI for approved research or operational purposes as defined by applicable project requirements and governing agreements, or may host and process PHI on behalf of project owners (e.g., Principal Investigators)." (same section)
- "Project owners are responsible for obtaining consent, providing required privacy notices, and determining permissible uses and disclosures of PHI where applicable." (same section)
- "Individuals have rights regarding their information, including access, amendment, and accounting of disclosures." (same section)
- "TACC implements administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect PHI in accordance with applicable security standards, including access controls, encryption, and monitoring, and limits access to authorized personnel only." (same section)

## Evidence and method

A notice of about 160 words. It states roles and safeguards in general terms. It names no agreement, audit or certification.

## Limits

The notice does not say which TACC systems can hold PHI, which agreements govern a project, or how a person asks for access or amendment. It gives no date or version.
