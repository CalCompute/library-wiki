# BCP 2025-26 Public Health AI in health care services AB 3030 (web)

## Bottom line

- CDPH asks for 3.0 positions and $672,000 each year from 2025-26 to carry out AB 3030. The money comes from the State Department of Public Health Licensing and Certification Program Fund.
- AB 3030 applies when a health facility, clinic or physician's office uses generative AI (GenAI) for patient communications about clinical information. The communication must have a disclaimer and contact instructions.
- CDPH estimates that 40 percent of 15,000 facilities will use AI tools, and that their review adds 6,000 hours of work each year.

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. The CDPH Center for Health Care Quality (CHCQ) licenses and inspects health care facilities. Its nurses would check the disclaimers during relicensing surveys. The law's text is in [AB 3030 chaptered text Chapter 848 of 2024](AB%203030%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20848%20of%202024.md).

## Key claims

- "The California Department of Public Health (CDPH) requests 3.0 positions and expenditure authority of $672,000 in 2025-26 and ongoing from the State Department of Public Health Licensing and Certification Program Fund to provide resources to implement the provisions of AB 3030" (Budget Request Summary).
- "This bill clarifies that if a communication is generated by GenAI and read and reviewed by a human licensed or certified health care provider, then a disclaimer described above is not required." (B. Justification)
- "Based on the increasing accessibility of generative AI, CDPH estimates that 40 percent of facilities will use AI tools in patient communication." (B. Justification)
- "Since a HFEN can complete 1,800 hours of work each year, CDPH will require approximately 3.0 permanent FTE HFENs to complete the new workload." (B. Justification)
- "This would result in costs of $390,000 in the second half of 2024-25 that will be absorbed, and annual, ongoing costs of approximately $672,000 beginning in 2025-26." (B. Justification)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form, with a resource history, a workload estimate and a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. The estimate is two hours of review for each of 6,000 facilities, with a relicensing survey every two years on average. The positions are three Health Facilities Evaluator Nurses (HFEN), with salaries of $348,000 a year in total.

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- The 2025-26 floor report lists $672,000 and 3 positions for AB 3030 in the enacted 2025-26 budget ([Assembly Budget floor report 2025-26 budget 2025-10 (web)](Assembly%20Budget%20floor%20report%202025-26%20budget%202025-10%20%28web%29.md), p. 22).
- Section C speaks of "secondhand cannabis smoke" and a substance-related branch. That text has no link to AB 3030 or to AI. It appears to come from another proposal.
- The 40 percent rate is an estimate. The proposal gives no source for it.
- It says nothing about CalCompute.
