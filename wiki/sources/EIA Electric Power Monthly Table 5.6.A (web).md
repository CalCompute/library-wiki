# EIA Electric Power Monthly Table 5.6.A (web)

## Citation

## Bottom line

- In August 2026, the average price of electricity in California was 29.02 cents per kilowatthour for all sectors and 24.12 cents for the industrial sector.
- The U.S. averages were 14.87 cents for all sectors and 9.64 cents for the industrial sector. The 2026 values are preliminary.

## Background

EIA is a federal statistics agency. Its page header says "Independent Statistics and Analysis". Electric Power Monthly gives monthly electricity data by state. Table 5.6.A gives the average price by sector: residential, commercial, industrial, transportation and all sectors. The page shows the table for the release of Oct. 23, 2026. The next release is Nov. 24, 2026, so the page changes each month.

## Evidence and method

Federal statistics from the utility survey Form EIA-861M. The table gives average revenue per kilowatthour sold to end users. It is one month of data, with the same month one year before.

## Limits

- One month only. Prices change by season and by year.
- An average price is not the rate that a data center pays under a specific tariff or contract.
- The URL always shows the latest release. The copy in `raw/web/` is the August 2026 table.
