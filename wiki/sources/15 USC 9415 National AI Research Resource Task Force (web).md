## Bottom line

- The section creates a 12-member task force on a National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR). The Director of the National Science Foundation (NSF) sets it up, with the Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP).
- The task force must give Congress and the President a roadmap and plan. The plan covers ownership, governance, shared computing, data, security and privacy. It must also say how to sustain the Resource "through Federal funding and partnerships with the private sector".

## Background

Section 9415 is the federal law that created the NAIRR Task Force. It came from the National Artificial Intelligence Initiative Act of 2020, in Pub. L. 116–283, enacted Jan. 1, 2021. The U.S. Government Publishing Office publishes the United States Code on govinfo. This copy is the 2023 edition of the Code.

## Key claims

- The task force is "to investigate the feasibility and advisability of establishing and sustaining a National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource; and" "to propose a roadmap detailing how such resource should be established and sustained." (§9415(a)(1)(A)(i) and (ii))
- "The Task Force shall be composed of 12 members selected by the co-chairpersons of the Task Force from among technical experts in artificial intelligence or related subjects" (§9415(a)(2)(A)). Four come from the Interagency Committee, four from "institutions of higher education" and four from "private organizations" (same subparagraph).
- The plan must include "an appropriate agency or organization responsible for the implementation, deployment, and administration of the Resource; and" "a governance structure for the Resource, including oversight and decision-making authorities." (§9415(b)(2)(B)(i) and (ii))
- The plan must state "Capabilities required to create and maintain a shared computing infrastructure to facilitate access to computing resources for researchers across the country, including scalability, secured access control, resident data engineering and curation expertise, provision of curated data sets, compute resources, educational tools and services, and a user interface portal." (§9415(b)(2)(D))
- "A plan for sustaining the Resource, including through Federal funding and partnerships with the private sector." (§9415(b)(2)(H))
- "The terms "National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource" and "Resource" mean a system that provides researchers and students across scientific fields and disciplines with access to compute resources, co-located with publicly-available, artificial intelligence-ready government and non-government data sets and a research environment with appropriate educational tools and user support." (§9415(g)(1))
- "The term "ownership" means responsibility and accountability for the implementation, deployment, and ongoing development of the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource, and for providing staff support to that effort." (§9415(g)(2))

## Evidence and method

Statute text. It states duties, a membership rule, a list of plan contents, 15 bodies to consult, report deadlines and two definitions. It gives no evidence and no funding amount.

## Limits

The section does not report what the task force found or did. It sets an interim report within 12 months of the appointments and a final report six months later. The task force ends 90 days after the final report (§9415(e) and (f)). The library has no copy of those reports. [S.2714 CREATE AI Act](S.2714%20CREATE%20AI%20Act.md) says the task force report was "issued on January 24, 2023". The Code's editorial notes mark two drafting errors "So in original".
