# Nitzberg SCAI 2025 participant page (web)

## Citation

Singapore Conference on AI (SCAI), Government of Singapore. "Mark NITZBERG." Participants of SCAI 2025, April 26, 2025. https://www.scai.gov.sg/2025/participants-of-scai-2025/mark-nitzberg/. Read 2026-11-06.

## Bottom line

- The Singapore Conference on AI lists Mark Nitzberg as a "Contributor" to SCAI 2025.
- It says he has worked at Bell Laboratories, Microsoft and Amazon, and that he co-wrote "The AI Generation".

## Background

A participant page on a website of the Government of Singapore. It gives a title and a short biography for each participant. This is a profile page. Mark Nitzberg's entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "Contributor". (header)
- "Executive Director, Center for Human-Compatible AI, US". (header)
- "Dr. Mark Nitzberg is a computer scientist and advisor to government and industry on AI and its impacts and governance." (biography)
- "He serves as founding Executive Director of the Center for Human-Compatible AI at UC Berkeley." (biography)
- "He has worked at Bell Laboratories, Microsoft, and Amazon, and developed and run diverse programs in industry and academia." (biography)
- "Co-author of The AI Generation – an examination of AI and how it reshapes human values, trust, and power around the world". (biography)

A government conference page.
