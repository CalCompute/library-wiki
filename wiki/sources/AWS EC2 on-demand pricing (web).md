# AWS EC2 on-demand pricing (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "Amazon EC2 On-Demand Pricing." AWS website, no date. https://aws.amazon.com/ec2/pricing/on-demand/. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 AWS EC2 on-demand pricing.md`.

## Bottom line

- AWS states the rules of on-demand pricing for Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2). Users pay for each instance by the hour or the second, with a minimum of 60 seconds and no long-term commitment.
- AWS gives 100 GB of free data transfer out to the internet each month, across all services and Regions except China and GovCloud.
- The fetched page holds no price for an instance type. The page loads the prices from the AWS price files.

## Background

AWS sells cloud computing. This page states how AWS charges for on-demand EC2 instances, data transfer, capacity reservations and other items. The instance price tables of the live page come from separate price files. This library holds two of them, for the US West (N. California) and US West (Oregon) Regions ([AWS EC2 on-demand Linux prices US West N California (web)](AWS%20EC2%20on-demand%20Linux%20prices%20US%20West%20N%20California%20%28web%29.md); [AWS EC2 on-demand Linux prices US West Oregon (web)](AWS%20EC2%20on-demand%20Linux%20prices%20US%20West%20Oregon%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "On-Demand Instances let you pay for compute capacity by the hour or second (minimum of 60 seconds) with no long-term commitments." (On-Demand Pricing)
- "Pricing is per instance-hour consumed for each instance, from the time an instance is launched until it is terminated or stopped." (On-Demand Pricing)
- "AWS customers receive 100 GB of free data transfer out to the internet free each month, aggregated across all AWS Services and Regions (except China and GovCloud)." (Data Transfer)
- "AWS also offers eligible customers free data transfer out to the internet when they move all of their data off of AWS or all of their data off of a particular AWS service." (Data Transfer)
- "On-Demand Capacity Reservations are priced exactly the same as their equivalent (On-Demand) instance usage." (On-Demand Capacity Reservations)
- "Except as otherwise noted, our prices are exclusive of applicable taxes and duties, including VAT and applicable sales tax." (Data Transfer)

## Evidence and method

A vendor pricing page. It states billing rules and a few fixed rates, for example CPU credits of T-family instances in Unlimited mode. It gives no method for the rates.
