## Bottom line

- The Argonne Leadership Computing Facility gives computing time through "competitive, peer-reviewed allocation programs supported by the DOE and Argonne".
- It names four programs: INCITE, ALCC, Director's Discretionary and the Early Science Program. In 2025, INCITE gave 29 million node hours and ALCC gave 14 million.

## Background

ALCF is a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) user facility at Argonne National Laboratory. "Argonne National Laboratory is a U.S. Department of Energy laboratory managed by UChicago Argonne, LLC." This page is part of the ALCF 2025 annual report. It describes how researchers apply for time on ALCF systems.

## Key claims

- "As a national user facility dedicated to open science, any researcher in the world with a large-scale computing problem can apply for time on ALCF computing resources." (Accessing ALCF Resources for Science)
- "Researchers gain access to ALCF systems for computational science and engineering projects through competitive, peer-reviewed allocation programs supported by the DOE and Argonne." (Accessing ALCF Resources for Science)
- The Innovative Novel Computational Impact on Theory and Experiment (INCITE) program awards time "to large-scale, computationally intensive projects that address grand challenges in science and engineering." (Allocation Programs, INCITE)
- The ASCR Leadership Computing Challenge (ALCC) program "allocates ALCF computing resources to projects that advance the DOE mission; help to broaden the community of researchers capable of using leadership computing resources; and serve the national interests". (Allocation Programs, ALCC)
- "Director's Discretionary projects are dedicated to leadership computing preparation, INCITE and ALCC scaling, and efforts to maximize scientific application efficiency and productivity on leadership computing platforms." (Allocation Programs)
- "2025 INCITE Node Hours 29M" and "2025 ALCC Node Hours 14M". (INCITE/ALCC)
- Physics has the largest share of INCITE time, "40" percent. Chemistry has the largest share of ALCC time, "35" percent. (2025 by Domain table)

## Evidence and method

A page of an institutional annual report. It describes the allocation programs and gives the 2025 node hours and the share of time by science domain. "ALCC data are from calendar year 2025." It gives no count of projects and no acceptance rate.

## Limits

The page does not give deadlines, review criteria or the number of applications. It does not say how many node hours Director's Discretionary or the Early Science Program gave. ALCF is in Illinois. The page says nothing about California.
