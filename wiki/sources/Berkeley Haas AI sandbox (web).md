# Berkeley Haas AI sandbox (web)

## Citation

UC Berkeley Haas School of Business, Technology Solutions. "AI Sandbox: Welcome to the Haas Campus AI Sandbox (BETA)." Web page, no date. https://haas.berkeley.edu/technology-solutions/how-do-i/ai-sandbox/. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 Berkeley Haas AI sandbox.md`.

## Bottom line

- The Haas School of Business offers the Campus AI Sandbox to its community with the Campus AI Platform Team. It is a beta pilot.
- A user needs CalNet credentials and an active affiliation. The school can change or end the pilot "at any time without prior notice".

## Background

Technology Solutions is the information technology unit of the Haas School of Business at UC Berkeley. This page tells the Haas community how to use the Sandbox. It links to the campus page, [Berkeley Campus AI Sandbox page (web)](Berkeley%20Campus%20AI%20Sandbox%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

Partners and phase (section "Welcome to the Haas Campus AI Sandbox (BETA)"):

- "We are excited to partner with the Campus AI Platform Team to launch the Haas-Campus AI Sandbox"
- "the AI Sandbox is currently in its BETA or pilot phase within the Haas community."
- "The Haas Campus AI Sandbox is envisioned as an experimental hub where faculty, staff and some student-workers can explore, innovate, and discover new applications of AI relevant to the Haas ecosystem."

Access (section "Log Into the AI Sandbox"):

- "You will need your CalNet credentials and an active affiliation to be able to access the AI Sandbox."

Terms (section "Disclaimer"):

- "the Haas Campus AI Sandbox is a pilot program and is provided on an "as-is" basis."
- "Models may be updated, reduced in scope, or new models may be added as part of the ongoing development and testing of this pilot program."
- "the Haas Campus AI Sandbox may be modified, its features altered, or the program may be terminated at any time without prior notice."

## Evidence and method

This is a help page with advice for users and a disclaimer. It gives no data.

## Limits

- The page says "faculty, staff and some student-workers". The campus page says all Haas students have access. The two pages differ on student access.
- It does not give cost or usage figures.
