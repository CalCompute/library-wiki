# Given personal website (web)

## Citation

Chris Given. "by Chris Given." Personal website, homepage with recent posts, posts from 2025. https://chrisgiven.com/. Read 2026-11-06.

## Bottom line

- Chris Given writes here about IRS Direct File, which his team built. Direct File went live on Jan. 29, 2024, and went dark on Nov. 5, 2025.
- In October 2025 he took the post of Deputy Secretary for Technology and Innovation at the California Government Operations Agency.

## Background

The homepage of his personal blog. It shows his recent posts, most of them about IRS Direct File. This is a profile page. His entity page lists it; no reading list does.

## Key claims

- "Direct File went live on January 29, 2024. It went dark on November 5, 2025." (IRS Direct File, 2024-2025)
- The final Direct File homepage message: "Over 500,000 of you used Direct File." (IRS Direct File, 2024-2025)
- "I recently accepted the position of Deputy Secretary for Technology and Innovation of the California Government Operations Agency. I also left DC and moved to Sacramento" (California, Oct. 19, 2025)
- "Working at the Office of Early Childhood in Connecticut, I had a modestly sized spreadsheet of every single publicly subsidized child care provider in the state." (California, Oct. 19, 2025)
- "When I arrived at the agency in 2021, Free File was described to me as “the third rail of the IRS.”" (The Things That Cannot Be Changed)
- "So Merici Vinton, my U.S. Digital Service (USDS) project co-lead, and I hatched a plan." (Saying Goodbye)
- "And so a team of three designers and four engineers (if you count me despite splitting time between coding, memos, and meetings), built a proof of concept version of Direct File in eight weeks." (Saying Goodbye)
- "The IRS has now published the vast majority of Direct File’s code on GitHub as open-source software." (Direct File on GitHub, May 30)

## Evidence and method

A personal blog. The posts are his own account and his own views.

## Limits

Posts give his views as well as facts. It does not give the cost or the number of states of the 2024 pilot.
