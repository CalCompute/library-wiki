# NERSC ERCAP allocation form (web)

## Citation

National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC). "The NERSC Allocation Request System (ERCAP)." NERSC Documentation, date not stated. https://docs.nersc.gov/allocations/ercap_form/. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 NERSC ERCAP allocation form.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20NERSC%20ERCAP%20allocation%20form.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- NERSC takes allocation requests through the Energy Research Computing Allocations Process (ERCAP) Request System at https://ercap.nersc.gov. "Initial requests to use NERSC resources can be submitted at any time. Renewals for approved projects are required annually."
- There are four project classes. NERSC "supports only open research intended to be published in open scientific journals. Purely proprietary research is not allowed."

## Background

This page in the NERSC documentation tells principal investigators (PIs) how to fill in the ERCAP form, tab by tab. The form asks for the project class, the DOE program, funding, security, project details, resources, codes and a usage agreement. The appropriate DOE program manager evaluates each submitted request.

## Key claims

- "Allocation requests are made via a web app known as the ERCAP (Energy Research Computing Allocations Process) Request System. Initial requests to use NERSC resources can be submitted at any time. Renewals for approved projects are required annually." (introduction)
- "If you do not already have a NERSC account, you will need to request a new PI ERCAP access account before you begin." (Accessing ERCAP)
- "DOE Mission Science: for projects that support science of interest to one of the DOE Offices of Science programs, or the SBIR program. This is the project class for the majority of projects at NERSC." (Project Class)
- "The maximum Exploratory awards are 250 Node hours and 1 TB of archival storage space in HPSS." (Project Class)
- "Some (but not all) programs will provide allocations to requests for research not funded by DOE." (Program)
- "Your request will be evaluated partly on the basis of alignment with a DOE program mission." (Funding Tab)
- "NERSC supports only open research intended to be published in open scientific journals. Purely proprietary research is not allowed." (Security Tab)
- NERSC does not allow "Classified or controlled military or defense information", "Export controlled or ITAR (International Traffic in Arms Regulations) information", "Personally identifiable information" or "Protected health information". (Security Tab)
- "There are two separate compute allocation pools: one for all CPU-only nodes (Perlmutter CPU-only nodes) and another for the Perlmutter GPU-accelerated nodes. Allocations, charging, and usage tracking will be in units of Node Hours." (Computational Resources)
- "Each project receives 20 TB by default." (Shared Project Storage)

## Evidence and method

A user guide for an online form. It lists what the form asks and how NERSC uses some answers. It is the practical route to a NERSC allocation. It gives no award totals and no success rates.

## Limits

The page does not give review criteria beyond alignment with a DOE program mission. It does not say how many requests NERSC approves. A project can ask for an exception to the security rules, but the page does not say when NERSC grants one. The page has no date.
