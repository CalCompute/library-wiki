## Bottom line

- SB 503 adds Chapter 25.3, "Clinical Decision Support Systems", to Division 8 of the Business and Professions Code. The chapter is one section, 22758.
- Developers and deployers must make reasonable efforts to find the clinical decision support systems with a known or foreseeable risk of "biased impacts".
- A developer must give deployers a statement of intended uses and risks, and documentation on training data, evaluation and data governance. A deployer must monitor the system and take steps to reduce the risk.

## Key claims

- "Developers and deployers shall make reasonable efforts to identify clinical decision support systems developed for use by deployers that are known or have a reasonably foreseeable risk of biased impacts resulting from deployment of the system in health programs or activities." (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22758(a)(1))
- "High-level summaries of the type or types of data used to train the clinical decision support system, including a description of its demographic representativeness when demographic data is available." (§ 22758(a)(3)(B)(i), one item of the documentation that a developer gives)
- "Deployers shall regularly monitor the clinical decision support systems identified pursuant to paragraph (1) and take reasonable and proportionate steps to mitigate the known or reasonably foreseeable risk of biased impacts." (§ 22758(a)(8))
- "“Biased impact” means an adverse impact, including diminished access to health care, quality of care, or outcomes, on an individual based on their protected characteristics." (§ 22758(c)(2))
- "“Developer” means a person, partnership, state or local governmental agency, corporation, or deployer that designs, codes, substantially modifies, or otherwise produces a clinical decision support system for commercial or public use." (§ 22758(c)(6))
- "Compliance with this section shall not be used as a defense to a claim of unlawful discrimination." (§ 22758(d))

## Limits

The section gives no penalty, no enforcing body and no operative date. A deployer is a health facility, clinic, physician's office or office of a group practice (§ 22758(c)(5)). A developer can follow national or industry standards on bias testing, or give the results of algorithmic impact assessments (§ 22758(a)(4)). A deployer that develops a system for its own use does not have to write the documentation in some conditions (§ 22758(a)(7)). The definition of "artificial intelligence" points to Government Code section 11546.45.5 ([AB 2885 chaptered text Chapter 843 of 2024](AB%202885%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20843%20of%202024.md)).

AB 1979 adds Chapter 25.5, section 22758.5, to the same division. It uses the same definition of "clinical decision support system" ([AB 1979 chaptered text Chapter 854 of 2026](AB%201979%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20854%20of%202026.md)). The Governor's release lists the two bills with the same subject line ([Governor AI bill signings including AB 2392 2026-09-30 (web)](Governor%20AI%20bill%20signings%20including%20AB%202392%202026-09-30%20%28web%29.md)).
