# ED PTAC written agreement checklist PDF (web)

## Citation

U.S. Department of Education, Privacy Technical Assistance Center (PTAC). "Written Agreement Checklist." PTAC-CL-3, April 2012 (revised July 2015). https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/sites/default/files/resource_document/file/Written_Agreement_Checklist.pdf. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 ED PTAC written agreement checklist PDF.pdf`.

## Bottom line

- The checklist gives the minimum terms of a written agreement under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA). The agreement applies when a school or education authority shares personally identifiable information (PII) from education records without consent.
- It covers two exceptions: the studies exception (34 CFR § 99.31(a)(6)(iii)(C)) and the audit or evaluation exception (34 CFR § 99.35(a)(3)).
- It adds best practices, such as Institutional Review Board (IRB) review, penalties, a right to audit, breach plans and set data destruction terms.

## Background

The Department of Education set up PTAC as a resource on data privacy, confidentiality and security for student data systems. This four-page document is one of its checklists. The landing page is [ED PTAC written agreement checklist (web)](ED%20PTAC%20written%20agreement%20checklist%20%28web%29.md). ## Key claims

- The studies exception covers studies "only for the purpose of developing, validating, or administering predictive tests; administering student aid programs; or improving instruction." (p. 1, Mandatory Elements)
- A studies agreement must specify the "purpose of the study to be conducted", "scope of the proposed study", "duration of the study" and "information to be disclosed". (p. 1)
- The organisation must "destroy all PII when the information is no longer needed for the purposes for which the study was conducted and within a specified time period." (p. 2)
- "Under the audit or evaluation exception, the purpose of data sharing can only be to carry out an audit or evaluation of Federal- or State-supported education programs, or to enforce or to comply with Federal legal requirements that relate to those programs." (p. 2)
- Best practices include "Mention Institutional Review Board review and approval", "Identify penalties for inappropriate disclosure" and "Maintain the right to audit." (pp. 2-3)
- "individual state privacy or procurement laws may contain more stringent requirements for data sharing written agreements" (p. 1, Purpose)

## Evidence and method

Federal technical assistance guidance of about 1,500 words. It summarises FERPA regulations and cites them by section. It is not a regulation itself.

## Limits

The checklist covers only FERPA and only two exceptions. It names other laws, such as the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA) and HIPAA, but does not cover their terms. It dates from 2015 and does not cover later FERPA guidance.
