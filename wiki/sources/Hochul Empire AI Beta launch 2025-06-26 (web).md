# Hochul Empire AI Beta launch 2025-06-26 (web)

## Citation

Office of the Governor of New York. "Governor Hochul Announces $40 Million to Launch Empire AI Beta Supercomputer." Press release, June 26, 2025. https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-40-million-launch-empire-ai-beta-supercomputer. Read 2026-11-11. Rehosted copy: `raw/web/2026-11-11 Hochul Empire AI Beta launch 2025-06-26.html`.

## Bottom line

- The release says that the Empire State Development (ESD) Board approved $40 million to buy the equipment for Empire AI Beta.
- Beta is to use NVIDIA's Blackwell platform, as one of the first academic DGX SuperPOD deployments with DGX GB200 systems.
- The release gives the funding mix: "up to $340 million in State capital grant investment", "$25 million over ten years in SUNY operating funding" and "more than $200 million" from founding institutions and philanthropy.

## Background

The Office of the Governor of New York published this release on June 26, 2025. It reports the first tranche of state funding for Beta, the second phase of the Empire AI supercomputer. It builds on the $90 million in the FY 2026 budget. It quotes the Governor, the ESD President, the SUNY Chancellor, the Empire AI Interim Executive Director, an NVIDIA executive and an Assemblymember.

## Key claims

- Approval: "Governor Kathy Hochul today announced that the Empire State Development (ESD) Board approved $40 million to launch Empire AI Beta, the second phase of the supercomputer powering New York’s nation-leading Empire AI initiative." (paragraph 1)
- Purpose of the money: "The funding approved today by ESD will allow the Empire AI consortium to purchase the equipment needed to power the second-phase supercomputer, housed at the University of Buffalo." (paragraph 3)
- Hardware: "Empire AI Beta also is expected to be among the first academic deployments of NVIDIA DGX SuperPOD with DGX GB200 systems." (paragraph 3)
- Phases: "Empire AI Alpha and Empire AI Beta allow member institutions to conduct critical AI research as soon as possible until the full-scale system is complete." (paragraph 5)
- Access gap: "As a result, researchers, public interest organizations, and small companies are being left behind, which has enormous implications for AI safety and society at large." (paragraph after the quotations)
- Scale: "By increasing collaboration between New York State’s world-class research institutions, Empire AI is creating efficiencies of scale not achievable by any single university" (next paragraph)
- Funding mix: "The initiative is currently funded by over $500 million in public and private investment, including up to $340 million in State capital grant investment and $25 million over ten years in SUNY operating funding." (last paragraph)
- Other funders: "The project will also receive more than $200 million from the founding institutions as well as philanthropic backers such as Tom Secunda and the Simons Foundation." (last paragraph)

## Evidence and method

This is a government press release. It states the ESD Board approval, the hardware platform, the performance ratios against Alpha and the funding sources by type. It gives no budget table and no allocation rules.

## Limits

- The release does not give the number of GPUs or nodes in Beta.
- It does not say how the "up to $340 million" divides across years or systems.
- It does not say who pays to operate Beta, beyond the SUNY operating funding.
- It writes "University of Buffalo". The later release writes "State University of New York at Buffalo" ([Hochul Empire AI Beta fully online (web)](Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20Beta%20fully%20online%20%28web%29.md)).
