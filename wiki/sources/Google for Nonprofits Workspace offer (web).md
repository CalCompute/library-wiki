# Google for Nonprofits Workspace offer (web)

## Citation

Google. "Google Workspace for Nonprofits." Google for Nonprofits web page, date not stated. https://www.google.com/nonprofits/offerings/workspace/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Google gives eligible nonprofits Google Workspace for Nonprofits at $0 for each user, and discounts on paid Workspace plans.
- Business Standard costs $3.50 for each user each month, "75%+ off standard pricing". Enterprise plans are "70%+" off.
- A nonprofit first requests a Google for Nonprofits account, and Google verifies its eligibility.

## Background

Google publishes this page for its nonprofit program. It compares the Workspace editions that nonprofits can get and their prices.

## Key claims

- Discount: "Through Google for Nonprofits, eligible organizations get access to Google Workspace offers at discounted rates" (Compare nonprofit offers)
- Free edition: Google Workspace for Nonprofits is "$0 USD / user / month", with "100 TB shared across all users". (Compare nonprofit offers)
- Business Standard: "$3.50 USD per user / month, 1 year commitment", "75%+ off standard pricing". (Compare nonprofit offers)
- Business Plus: "$6.16 USD per user / month, 1 year commitment", "72%+ off standard pricing". (Compare nonprofit offers)
- User limit: "Google Workspace for Nonprofits and nonprofit discounts on Business Standard and Business Plus have a maximum limit of 2,000 users. There is no minimum or maximum user limit for discounted Enterprise plans." (Compare nonprofit offers)
- Route: "Once we’ve verified your organization’s eligibility, you’ll be able to activate the Google products that best meet your needs." (Request a Google for Nonprofits account)

## Evidence and method

The program's own web page. It states the offer and prices. It gives no figures on use.

## Limits

- The page does not state the eligibility rules. It links to a separate page.
- The page does not give a price for Enterprise plans, only the discount.
- The page gives no date. Prices can change.
