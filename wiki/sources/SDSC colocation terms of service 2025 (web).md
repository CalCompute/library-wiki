# SDSC colocation terms of service 2025 (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "Colocation and NAP Service Terms." PDF, last updated March 27, 2025. https://www.sdsc.edu/_files/docs/COLO_terms_of_service_2025.pdf. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 SDSC colocation terms of service 2025.md`. Read in full.

## Bottom line

- These terms apply to customers of SDSC colocation. They cover data, security, support, physical access, logs and deliveries.
- Customers must not send Personally Identifiable Information (PII) or Protected Health Information (PHI) under this agreement. SDSC makes no uptime guarantee.

## Background

The terms add to the UCSD-Customer Service Agreement. Where the two conflict, these terms apply. The service page is [SDSC colocation services (web)](SDSC%20colocation%20services%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

Data (section 1):

- 1.2: "Customer should be prepared to certify that all data stored in SDSC is directly related to the project authorized by Customer's Service Agreement."
- 1.5: "SDSC will use Customer/End User Data only for the purpose of fulfilling its duties under Customer's Service Agreement(s) and for Customer's sole benefit"
- 1.6: "Customer agrees that no Personally Identifiable Information ("PII) as defined by California privacy laws (including California Civil Code sections 56-56.37) or Protected Health Information ("PHI") as defined by the Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA", 45CFR Parts 160 and 164) shall be transmitted to SDSC under this agreement."

Support (section 2):

- 2.1: "SDSC will make reasonable efforts to notify clients two weeks prior to any planned system outage."
- 2.2: "SDSC's service response goal for notification of outages affecting the SDSC Data Center and Colocation facilities is 60 minutes."

Physical security (section 3):

- 3.1: "Only individuals sponsored by an SDSC Customer or SDSC Staff member are given access to the SDSC Data Center."
- 3.8: "All access logs to SDSC data center and customer racks will be retained for 180 days"

Availability (section 3.10):

- "SDSC strives to ensure optimal network performance and availability; however, no guarantees are made regarding uptime."
- "SDSC's upstream provider claims availability of 99.0%."

## Evidence and method

This is a service agreement. It states duties of SDSC and of the customer. It gives no data.

## Limits

- The terms do not give prices. The service page gives them.
- They refer to separate agreements for PHI or PII. This library has not read those.
- The terms say "NAP" in the title but do not define it.
