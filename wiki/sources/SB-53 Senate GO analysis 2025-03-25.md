## Background

The Senate Committee on Governmental Organization heard SB 53 on March 25, 2025. It was the bill's first hearing.

## Key claims

- **Appointments.**
  > Requires the Secretary of GovOps to select eight members of the consortium, and the President pro Tempore of the Senate and the Speaker of the Assembly to each select three members.
- **CalCompute not established.** "This bill does not establish CalCompute, however, it does specify that if CalCompute is eventually established within the UC, the UC may receive private donations for the purposes of implementing CalCompute."
- **Supporters.** CalCompute is "a step towards democratizing access to advanced computational resources currently out of reach for many academics and startups in California."
