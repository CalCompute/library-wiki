## Bottom line

- CDT's Office of Information Security (OIS) asks for $408,000 General Fund in 2026-27 and $286,000 in 2027-28 for temporary help. The work carries out AB 979 (Chapter 285, Statutes of 2025).
- The temporary help would write and update AI security standards and assess AI use cases at state agencies.
- It would also help the California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) write the AI playbook by January 2027.

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. OIS is an office in CDT under Government Code section 11549. The temporary help would sit in its Security Risk and Governance (SRG) unit. The bill text is in [AB 979 AI cybersecurity bill text](AB%20979%20AI%20cybersecurity%20bill%20text.md). For the same bill, see [BCP 2026-27 Cal OES Cybersecurity Integration Center AI AB 979 (web)](BCP%202026-27%20Cal%20OES%20Cybersecurity%20Integration%20Center%20AI%20AB%20979%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "To support the goals of Chapter 285, Statutes of 2025 (AB 979) and ensure effective statewide security oversight of Artificial Intelligence (AI) systems, the Office of Information Security (OIS) requires $408,000 General Fund (GF) in Fiscal Year (FY) 2026-27 and $286,000 in FY 2027-28." (Budget Request Summary)
- "State departments are adopting new AI components into their systems at a rapid rate, which continue to present and introduce new cybersecurity risks to the state." (A. Problem Statement)
- "The temporary help would develop, implement, and continuously update the AI security standards required by the bill." (B. Justification)
- "They would work directly with state agencies assessing AI use cases (i.e. innovative ideas and projects), identifying vulnerabilities, and conducting risk assessments/consultations." (B. Justification)
- "This will allow 4 months of dedicated resources to ensure that the statewide AI playbook is created by the January 2027 deadline that is mentioned in the bill." (E. Implementation Plan)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form, with a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. The resource history and workload tables say "N/A" in every cell. The proposal refers to an attachment for the workload. The copy does not include it.

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- The 2026-27 floor report lists $408,000 in 2026-27 and $286,000 in 2027-28 for CDT to support AB 979 ([Assembly Budget floor report 2026-27 budget 2026-06-27 (web)](Assembly%20Budget%20floor%20report%202026-27%20budget%202026-06-27%20%28web%29.md), p. 112).
- It does not say how many people the temporary help is. It calls it "temporary help" and also "the limited term position".
- It says nothing about CalCompute.
- The workload attachment is not in the copy.
