## Citation

## Bottom line

- The Cal Poly Pomona HPC Initiative runs two clusters for research and instruction: Polaris, the new one, and Legacy.
- Polaris has three compute nodes of 160 CPU cores and 1.5 TB of RAM each, and a GPU node with eight NVIDIA H200 GPUs.
- Legacy has 20 compute nodes and four GPU nodes with eight Tesla P100 GPUs.

## Background

This is the HPC page of the Information Technology division of Cal Poly Pomona, a California State University (CSU) campus. It describes the two clusters and their hardware and software.

## Key claims

Purpose (opening paragraph):

- "The Cal Poly Pomona HPC Initiative supports research and instruction across colleges by providing access to high-performance computing resources."

Polaris ("Polaris HPC Specifications"):

- "Polaris includes three compute nodes, each equipped with 160 CPU cores, 1.5 TB of RAM, and a GPU node featuring eight NVIDIA H200 GPUs with 3 TB of RAM."
- "Polaris provides increased computing resources within a smaller physical footprint than our Legacy HPC enabling researchers to tackle larger and more complex problems while using campus data center space and energy more efficiently."

Legacy ("Legacy HPC Specfications"):

- "The Legacy HPC cluster based on the HP's Proliant server platform includes two DL360 management nodes, 20 DL160 compute nodes, and four GPU nodes with eight Tesla P100 GPUs."
- "The overall system throughput is approximately 36.6 Tflp in double-precision mode or 149.6 Tflp in half-precision mode."
- "Its software management suite utilizes HP Enterprise's HPC Software Stack, including Slurm, an open-source job scheduler, Insight CMU for cluster management, and other HPE software for node deployment and configuration."

## Evidence and method

This is a campus service page. It gives hardware and software specifications. It gives no dates, user counts or costs.
