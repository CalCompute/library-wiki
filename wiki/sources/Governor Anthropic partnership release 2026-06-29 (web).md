## Bottom line

- The state's agreement with Anthropic gives state agencies, cities and counties its AI assistant Claude at a 50 percent discount per seat. It adds free workforce training and technical help from Anthropic developers.
- The California Department of Technology (CDT) offers Claude through its new Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal.

## Background

The release announces the agreement and lists state uses of Claude. They include Engaged California, the Poppy platform, cyber defense at CDT and Cal OES, the DMV and the Department of Health Care Services. It quotes the Governor, Anthropic, the Government Operations Agency Secretary and the State Chief Information Officer. It then lists the Governor's earlier AI actions.

## Key claims

- "Through the agreement, state agencies may access Anthropic’s AI productivity assistant, Claude, at a 50% discounted price per seat, coupled with free workforce training as well as expert GenAI technical assistance and workflow input from Anthropic developers. The agreement also provides the same discounted offer for California’s local governments, including cities and counties." (body)
- "Claude is the first AI productivity tool that will be available to all State agencies though the California Department of Technology’s new Statewide Information Technology Shared Services (SITeS) portal." (Making AI easily accessible for state employees)
- "Claude was also used in the state’s development of Poppy – a simple AI tool designed by state workers for state workers through pre-built, easy-to-use queries tailored to common state business needs, facilitating more reliable, trustworthy outcomes." (body)
- "CDT and CalOES are partnering to use Claude for cyber defense — Claude Security and Claude Code for scanning, triaging, and patching state code." (body)
- "AI should not replace the human work of government; it should help our workers move faster, solve problems more effectively, and deliver better results for Californians." (Governor Gavin Newsom)

## Evidence and method

A government press release. It gives the discount rate but no contract value, term or seat count. The library does not hold the agreement.

## Limits

The release does not give the list price, the contract length or how many state workers use Claude. It does not mention research compute, the University of California or CalCompute. [GovOps more efficient California](GovOps%20more%20efficient%20California.md) also names the SITeS offer.
