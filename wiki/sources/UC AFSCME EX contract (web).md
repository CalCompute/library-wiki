# UC AFSCME EX contract (web)

## Citation

University of California and American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees (AFSCME) Local 3299. Agreement for the Patient Care Technical (EX) unit, Feb. 7, 2020 to July 31, 2024. University of California, UCnet. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/ex/docs/ex_000_complete.pdf. ## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the University of California (UC) and AFSCME Local 3299 for the Patient Care Technical (EX) unit. Its term ran from Feb. 7, 2020 to July 31, 2024. Without timely notice, it stays in effect from year to year.
- It sets "a general prohibition on contracting for services and functions that can be performed by University staff". UC can contract out covered services only in six listed cases, and contracts must give equivalent wages and benefits.
- UC must give the union notice before a contract with covered services over $100,000. Management rights include the right to introduce new equipment and to subcontract.

## Background

AFSCME Local 3299 represents two UC units: EX and the Service (SX) unit ([UC AFSCME SX contract (web)](UC%20AFSCME%20SX%20contract%20%28web%29.md)). The covered services of the EX agreement include billing and coding, sterile processing and medical imaging.

## Key claims

- "There shall be a general prohibition on contracting for services and functions that can be performed by University staff." (Article 5, A.1)
- "The University will utilize its employees to perform Covered Services (as defined in subparagraph 6 below) to the greatest extent possible before resorting to the use of contractors to provide such services." (Article 5, A.2)
- "cleaning, custodial, janitorial, or housekeeping services; security services; billing and coding services, sterile processing, hospital and nursing assistant services and medical imaging services." (Article 5, A.6, Covered Services)
- "Contracting out should be used sparingly and treated as an option of last resort to address temporary needs, not as a means to replace employees with contractors." (Article 5, C)
- "the services are of a specialized or technical nature and the expertise, knowledge, ability and/or equipment required is not available internally. This provision shall be interpreted narrowly and shall not be relied upon to avoid reasonable efforts to hire and train sufficient numbers of University employees." (Article 5, C.2)
- "The University shall provide notice to AFSCME 3299 prior to entering into, extending or renewing a contract that includes Covered Services valued at over $100,000." (Article 5, D.1)
- "The terms and conditions of this Agreement shall be in full force effective one minute past midnight on February 7, 2020 and terminating at 11:59 p.m. on July 31, 2024" (Article 8, A)
- "To introduce new or improved methods, equipment or facilities, or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment or facilities;" (Article 18, B.5)
- "To discontinue, relocate or subcontract all or any portion of any operation;" (Article 18, B.7)

## Evidence and method

A collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The term ended on July 31, 2024. The library holds no successor agreement.
- The [UC SX contract page (web)](UC%20SX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) says that UC is implementing terms of its last, best and final offer for the SX and EX units. The library does not hold that offer.
- The agreement has no article on artificial intelligence.
