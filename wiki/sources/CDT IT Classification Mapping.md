## Bottom line

- The state maps its IT work to a few civil service classifications. They are Information Technology Technician, Associate, Specialist I to III, Supervisor I and II, and Manager I and II.
- Six domains: Business Technology Management, Client Services, Information Security Engineering, IT Project Management, Software Engineering and System Engineering.
- They map to Information Technology Specialist I/II/III, Supervisor and Manager.

## Background

The mapping helps applicants find which state classification fits a functional title. See [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md).

## Key claims

- Domain 6, System Engineering, "Data & Analytics Engineering": "Define and develop code and data infrastructure for the movement and processing of data". Titles include "Data Engineer" and "Machine Learning Engineer" (p. 15).
- Domain 6, "Enterprise Architecture": "Cloud Architect", "Enterprise Architect" (p. 15).
- Domain 6, "Virtualization": "Create virtual computer hardware platforms, operating systems, storage devices, and network resources"; titles "Server Administrator", "Server Infrastructure Administrator", "IT Infrastructure Manager" (p. 16).
- Domain 1, "IT Legislative Management": "Perform impact analysis on proposed legislation with an IT component" (p. 2).
- Domain 1, "Policy and Program Development": "Formulate policies/programs related to IT, including IT governance" (p. 2).
- Each subject area maps to the same small set of classifications, for example "Information Technology Specialist I/II/III; Information Technology Supervisor I/II; Information Technology Manager I/II".

## Limits

- Titles and classifications only. No pay, counts of staff or vacancies.
- "Machine Learning Engineer" is under data engineering.
