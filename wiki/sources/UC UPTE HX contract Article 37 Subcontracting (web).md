## Bottom line

- Article 37 of the agreement between the University of California (UC) and UPTE for the Health Care Professionals (HX) unit keeps UC's right to subcontract. UC will not contract out services that cause the layoff of non-probationary career unit employees, except in three listed cases.
- For financial necessity in work paid from state funds, UC must first seek funding from the Legislature. A career employee laid off because of a contract gets the offer of another unit position at the same base rate of pay.
- Nothing in the article prevents action needed for a federal program, contract or grant, the Department of Energy agreement included.

## Background

UPTE represents UC's HX unit. UC posts each article as a separate file on its [UC HX contract page (web)](UC%20HX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md). The library also holds two other UPTE subcontracting articles ([UC UPTE TX contract Article 40 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2040%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md), [UC UPTE RX contract Article 40 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20RX%20contract%20Article%2040%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "The University retains the right to subcontract all or any portion of operations." (Article 37, A.1)
- "When the University decides to subcontract, and such subcontract will result in the layoff of employees in the bargaining unit, the University will provide UPTE with a copy of the Request for Proposals (RFP) seven (7) calendar days after it is issued." (Article 37, A.2)
- "Except as provided below, the University of California will not contract out services that result in the layoff of non-probationary career bargaining unit employees." (Article 37, B.1)
- "The need to obtain special services and equipment that are not available internally;" (Article 37, B.2.a)
- "Where financial necessity is the reason for the exception, before contracting for work which is fully or partially supported from state funds, including those at the teaching hospitals, the University shall first seek funding from the Legislature to address the financial necessity." (Article 37, B.3)
- "The available position shall be offered at the same base rate of pay earned by the employee when displaced." (Article 37, C.2)
- "Nothing in this article shall be interpreted as prohibiting action, which must be taken to establish or maintain eligibility for any federal program, contract or grant – including the contract requirements contained in the agreement between the University and the Department of Energy – where ineligibility would result in a loss of federal funds to the University of California." (Article 37, D)

## Evidence and method

One article of a collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The article has no date or signature in its text. Its file address names 2019-2024.
- The [UC HX contract page (web)](UC%20HX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) gives a new contract for Nov. 1, 2025 to Sept. 30, 2028, and says that the posted articles "do not reflect the most recent negotiated language."
- Section D on federal programs is not in [UC UPTE TX contract Article 40 Subcontracting (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2040%20Subcontracting%20%28web%29.md).
