## Bottom line

- The chart shows the agencies, departments and their heads under the Governor, as of Nov. 4, 2025.

## Background

The chart is one page. The Markdown copy is a broken table, because the PDF is a diagram. Read the PDF for the boxes and lines.

## Key claims

- Boxes on the chart include the Office of Emergency Services, the State Library and GO-Biz, each with its head.
- Each agency box lists its secretary, and its departments are under it.

An official chart from the Governor's office.
