# Ohio Revised Code 3333.04 (web)

## Citation

Ohio Revised Code section 3333.04, "Chancellor - powers and duties." Title 33, Chapter 3333 (Department of Higher Education). Effective September 30, 2025; latest legislation House Bill 96 of the 136th General Assembly. Ohio Laws website (codes.ohio.gov), Ohio Legislative Service Commission. https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3333.04. ## Bottom line

- Section 3333.04 lists the duties of Ohio's chancellor of higher education in divisions (A) to (V).
- Division (T) tells the chancellor to appoint consortia of college and university staff for statewide collaborative efforts. It names the Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC), the Ohio Academic Resources Network (OARnet), OhioLink and the Ohio Learning Network.
- For each consortium, the chancellor designates a college or university as fiscal agent, financial officer and employer. Appropriated funds go to that fiscal agent.

## Background

Chapter 3333 of the Ohio Revised Code sets up the Department of Higher Education. Section 3333.04 gives the chancellor duties on planning, program approval, budgets, rules and advisory committees. The page lists five versions of the section, from September 10, 2012, to September 30, 2025. [Ohio LSC 2025 catalog of budget line items, Higher Education (web)](Ohio%20LSC%202025%20catalog%20of%20budget%20line%20items%2C%20Higher%20Education%20%28web%29.md) gives "R.C. 3333.04(T)" as the legal basis of the OSC and OARnet line items.

## Key claims

- Consortia: the chancellor shall "Appoint consortia of college and university personnel to advise or participate in the development and operation of statewide collaborative efforts, including the Ohio supercomputer center, the Ohio academic resources network, OhioLink, and the Ohio learning network." (division (T))
- Fiscal agent: "For each consortium, the chancellor shall designate a college or university to serve as that consortium's fiscal agent, financial officer, and employer. Any funds appropriated for the consortia shall be distributed to the fiscal agents for the operation of the consortia." (division (T))
- Restructuring: "The chancellor may restructure existing consortia, appointed under this division, in accordance with procedures adopted under divisions (O)(1) to (5) of this section." (division (T))
- Rules and purchases: "A consortium shall follow the rules of the college or university that serves as its fiscal agent, except that when making a purchase with appropriated funds of any product that includes semiconductors, a consortium shall conduct the purchase in accordance with rules adopted by the director of administrative services under division (B) of section 125.09 of the Revised Code for giving preference to Buy Ohio products." (division (T), second paragraph)
- Public process: the procedures adopted under division (O) "shall include at least" "Provision for public notice of the proposed action" and "An opportunity for public comment on the proposed action". (division (O)(1) and (2))
- Advisory committees: the chancellor shall "Appoint advisory committees consisting of college and university personnel, or other persons knowledgeable in the field of higher education, or both, in order to obtain their advice and assistance in defining and suggesting solutions for the problems and needs of higher education in this state" (division (M))
- Budget review: the chancellor shall "Review the appropriation requests of the public community colleges and the state colleges and universities" and send recommendations on "the biennial higher education appropriation for the state". (division (J))

## Limits

- The section does not name the fiscal agent for OSC or OARnet. [OSC governance (web)](OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md) says "Ohio State serves as the fiscal agent for the Center".
- It gives no amounts. The amounts are in the appropriation act and in the catalog of budget line items.
- It does not name the Ohio Technology Consortium (OH-TECH). It does not set the size, membership or terms of a consortium.
- The website says that the LSC staff "updates the Revised Code on an ongoing basis". The page links an "Authenticated PDF" of the section. The library has not read the PDF.
