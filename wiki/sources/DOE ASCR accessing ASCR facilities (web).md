# DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)

## Citation

U.S. Department of Energy (DOE), Office of Science, Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR). "Accessing ASCR Facilities." Web page, date not stated. https://science.osti.gov/ascr/Facilities/Accessing-ASCR-Facilities. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- ASCR facilities are three high performance computing centers (OLCF, ALCF and NERSC) and the Energy Sciences Network (ESnet). Access "is open to the scientific and engineering research community including universities, industry, and the national labs."
- Five mechanisms give access to the computers: INCITE, ALCC, ERCAP, SBIR/STTR and Center Reserves. A table gives the share of machine time for each.

## Background

ASCR is an office of the DOE Office of Science. This page tells researchers how to get time on ASCR computers and how to use ESnet tools. It links to the ASCR Computational Facilities Allocation Policy. The table of mechanisms gives figures for 2015.

## Key claims

- "Access to ASCR facilities is open to the scientific and engineering research community including universities, industry, and the national labs." (introduction)
- "The primary objectives of the ASCR Computational Facilities Allocation Policy are: (i) support DOE Office of Science mission critical projects, and (ii) provide substantial allocations to the open science community through a peer review process for a small number of high-impact scientific research projects." (Accessing ASCR High Performance Computers)
- "ASCR High Performance Computers are accessible through five complementary mechanisms summarized in the table below." (Accessing ASCR High Performance Computers)
- Share of time: INCITE "LCFs (60%)"; ALCC "LCFs (30%) and NERSC (10%)"; ERCAP "NERSC (80%)"; Center Reserves "LCFs and NERSC (10%)". (Table of Mechanisms)
- Number of awards in 2015: INCITE "56", ALCC "43 (2015-2016)", ERCAP "634". (Table of Mechanisms)
- "NERSC – National Energy Research Scientific Computation (Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Berkeley, CA)". (Acronyms)
- The ESnet 100G testbed is open "to the research community and industry through a peer review process managed by ESnet." (Accessing ASCR High Throughput Network)

## Evidence and method

A federal program page. It gives the allocation mechanisms, the share of machine time for each, the managing office, the award length and 2015 award counts. It gives no budget figures.

## Limits

The award counts and mean project sizes are for 2015, and the page gives no newer figures. The table has four columns, but the text names five mechanisms; SBIR/STTR has no column of its own. The page does not give deadlines. It does not say how the review panels work.
