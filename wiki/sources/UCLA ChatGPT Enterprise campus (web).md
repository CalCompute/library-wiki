# UCLA ChatGPT Enterprise campus (web)

## Citation

UCLA Administration. "UCLA introduces ChatGPT Enterprise on campus – a first for California higher ed." News story, October 29, 2024. https://adminvc.ucla.edu/news/ucla-introduces-chatgpt-enterprise-campus-first-california-higher-ed. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- UCLA makes an agreement with OpenAI for ChatGPT Enterprise, the first university in California to do so.
- The UC Office of the President supported the agreement, which "paves the way for other UC campuses" to use a UC-specific version.

## Background

The UCLA Administration publishes campus news on this site. The story repeats a UCLA Newsroom announcement. It quotes Lucy Avetisyan, UCLA's associate vice chancellor and chief information officer.

## Key claims

- "This agreement with OpenAI, which was recently announced in the UCLA Newsroom, makes UCLA the first university in California to incorporate this advanced technology into its operations." (second paragraph)
- "The agreement, negotiated with support from the UC Office of the President, also paves the way for other UC campuses to access and use a UC-specific version of OpenAI’s interactive and natural language–based tool." (second paragraph)
- "Access to ChatGPT Enterprise — a version of ChatGPT intended for large organizations and tailored for University of California use — will be granted to UCLA students, faculty and staff engaged in initiatives to support their creativity and innovation in teaching, learning and research." (body)
- "Later this year, the campus will issue an open call for project ideas and proposals from students, faculty and staff" (body)

## Evidence and method

A campus news story. It gives no price, license count or contract term.

## Limits

The story gives no cost or user numbers. Access goes to people "engaged in initiatives", not to every member of the campus. The Suggestions row dates it 2024-09; the page says October 29, 2024.
