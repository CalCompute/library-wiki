# Google Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy 2024-12-17

## Citation

Google. "Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy." Policy web page, last modified 2024-12-17. https://policies.google.com/terms/generative-ai/use-policy. Read 2026-10-15.

## Bottom line

- This is the AI-specific hard-limits document of Google. It applies to "the Google products and services that refer to this policy".
- It prohibits automated decisions with "a material detrimental impact on individual rights without human supervision in high-risk domains".
- It allows exceptions for "educational, documentary, scientific, or artistic considerations". This is the widest written exception among the provider policies.

## Background

This policy adds AI limits to the [Google Cloud Acceptable Use Policy 2026-10-12](Google%20Cloud%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%202026-10-12.md). It has four numbered rules, each with a list.

## Key claims

1, "Do not engage in dangerous or illegal activities, or otherwise violate applicable law or regulations":

- "Relates to child sexual abuse or exploitation."
- "Facilitates violent extremism or terrorism."
- "Violates the rights of others, including privacy and intellectual property rights -- for example, using personal data or biometrics without legally-required consent."
- "Tracks or monitors people without their consent."
- "Makes automated decisions that have a material detrimental impact on individual rights without human supervision in high-risk domains -- for example, in employment, healthcare, finance, legal, housing, insurance, or social welfare."

2, "Do not compromise the security of others’ or Google’s services":

- "Spam, phishing, or malware."
- "Abuse of, harm to, interference with, or disruption to Google’s or others’ infrastructure or services."
- "Circumvention of abuse protections or safety filters -- for example, manipulating the model to contravene our policies."

3, "Do not engage in sexually explicit, violent, hateful, or harmful activities":

- "Harassment, bullying, intimidation, abuse, or the insulting of others."
- "Violence or the incitement of violence."

4, "Do not engage in misinformation, misrepresentation, or misleading activities":

- "Frauds, scams, or other deceptive actions."
- "Impersonating an individual (living or dead) without explicit disclosure, in order to deceive."
- "Facilitating misleading claims related to governmental or democratic processes or harmful health practices, in order to deceive."

Exceptions:

- "We may make exceptions to these policies based on educational, documentary, scientific, or artistic considerations, or where harms are outweighed by substantial benefits to the public."

## Evidence and method

This is a policy document, not research. It states rules. It gives no data on how often Google enforces them.

## Limits

- The policy does not name weapons or CBRN (chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear) threats. It covers them only as "illegal activities".
- The policy does not say how Google monitors or what it does on a violation. Enforcement for Google Cloud is in the Google Cloud AUP.
- The policy does not say how to ask for an exception.
- It applies only to products that "refer to this policy".
