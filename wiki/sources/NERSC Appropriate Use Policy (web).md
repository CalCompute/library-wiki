# NERSC Appropriate Use Policy (web)

## Citation

National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC), Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. "Appropriate Use Policy." NERSC Appropriate Use Policy, revision 4.0, January 1, 2025. Web page. https://www.nersc.gov/users/become-a-nersc-user/user-agreements/nersc-appropriate-use-policy. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-12 NERSC Appropriate Use Policy.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-12%20NERSC%20Appropriate%20Use%20Policy.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The NERSC Appropriate Use Policy is the user agreement for the computers of NERSC, a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) center at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (Berkeley Lab).
- NERSC supports only open research. It bans proprietary results, classified and controlled data, and use "for personal or private benefit".
- Project principal investigators (PIs) carry "primary responsibility" for their project. A project that runs workflow services has extra security duties, such as audit logs kept for one year.

## Background

NERSC publishes this policy on its "User Agreements" pages. The page gives "revision 4.0, date: January 1, 2025". To keep an account, a user must agree to this policy and to the [NERSC Code of Conduct (web)](NERSC%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20%28web%29.md). The [NERSC user account policies (web)](NERSC%20user%20account%20policies%20%28web%29.md) give this rule. The policy has four parts: general conditions, user responsibilities, PI responsibilities and definitions.

## Key claims

- Open research: "NERSC supports open research intended to be published in open scientific journals. The production of proprietary results and/or data is not permitted." (NERSC: An open scientific research center)
- Banned data: "NERSC does not allow the use of the following: Classified Information; Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI); Export-controlled or ITAR codes or data; Personally identifiable or protected health information" (NERSC: An open scientific research center; list items joined here)
- Review: "Use of NERSC resources is subject to review and approval by" NERSC, project PIs, the University of California, Berkeley Lab, and "Funding agencies or other State and Federal Government agencies." (General conditions, Resource use)
- Rights to work: "Absent any applicable agreement with your institution, the Federal government or Berkeley Lab may have rights to your work product under the Bayh-Dole Act and other legislation and regulations." (Government rights)
- Private benefit: "The use of NERSC resources for personal or private benefit is prohibited." (Prohibited use)
- No warranty: "NERSC resources are provided to users without any warranty. NERSC will not be held liable in the event of any system failure or loss of data." (Warranty)
- Termination: "Access to NERSC resources may be withheld or terminated for any reason at the sole discretion of NERSC." (Termination)
- Foreign nationals: "Foreign nationals are generally permitted to access NERSC, provided they fully comply with applicable export control regulations, regardless of whether access occurs within the United States or abroad." Access "is strictly prohibited for individuals or entities in embargoed countries, listed on restricted party lists, or engaged in prohibited end-use activities." (Use by foreign nationals)
- Account sharing: "Users are not allowed to share their individual accounts with others." (Account sharing)
- Monitoring: "Users have no explicit or implicit expectation of privacy." (Monitoring and privacy)
- Institutional agreement: an Institutional User Agreement between Berkeley Lab and the PI's organization "is required if the project" is not federally funded, operates EODF workflows, or "Is involved in proprietary research (which is only permitted under exceptional circumstances and with written permission from the NERSC Director)." (Institutional user agreement)
- Audit logs: "Authentication logs and other relevant auditing data on usage must be retained for a period not less than one (1) year and must be provided to NERSC staff on request." (Audit logs)

## Evidence and method

This is a policy text. It gives rules and definitions. It has no data on users, violations or enforcement.

## Limits

- The policy does not say how many users or projects it covers.
- It does not say how NERSC decides that a use is "personal or private benefit".
- It names "Experimental and Observational Data Facilities (EODF)" workflows, but it does not list which facilities use them.
- It says that "patent and intellectual property waivers may be available", but it does not give the terms of a waiver.
- The [NERSC ERCAP allocation form (web)](NERSC%20ERCAP%20allocation%20form%20%28web%29.md) says "Purely proprietary research is not allowed". This policy permits proprietary research "only" in "exceptional circumstances" with the Director's written permission. The two texts are close but not the same in wording.
