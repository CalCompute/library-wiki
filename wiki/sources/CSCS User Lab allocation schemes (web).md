# CSCS User Lab allocation schemes (web)

## Citation

Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS). "Allocation Schemes." CSCS website, User Lab section. No date on the page ("© CSCS 2026"). https://www.cscs.ch/user-lab/allocation-schemes. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The CSCS User Lab gives compute time to academia through five types of project: production, development, CHRONOS, small and preparatory.
- Production and development projects have two allocation periods a year, from April 1 and October 1. Production projects run for one year. CSCS grants them on scientific merit and technical feasibility.
- Small projects ask for 8,000 node hours or fewer. Preparatory projects let new users port and test codes first.

## Background

CSCS is a unit of ETH Zurich (the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich) in Lugano ([CSCS organization (web)](CSCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md)). This page of its website lists the allocation schemes of the User Lab, with one paragraph for each and a link to a detail page. The detail page for production projects is [CSCS production projects (web)](CSCS%20production%20projects%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- Scope: "CSCS resources currently distribute compute time to academia through the following allocation schemes." (Allocation Schemes)
- Production: "Projects are granted access based on scientific merit and technical feasibility. Allocations are valid for one year." (Production Projects)
- Development: "Small development projects are granted based on technical and feasibility assessment. Whereas large development projects are granted based on scientific merit and technical feasibility therefore deadlines always apply." (Development Projects)
- CHRONOS: "CHRONOS is an annual call for proposals issued by CSCS for high-impact, challenging and innovative research opened to all fields of science." (CHRONOS Projects)
- Small: "Small projects are limited to resources requests no larger than 8'000 node hours" (Small Projects)
- Preparatory: "Preparatory projects are intended for **users who are new to CSCS** to allow them to port and test their codes before applying for a Production Project." (Preparatory Projects)
- Renewal: "Multi-year projects are expected to submit a renewal report to assure then continuation of the project." (Renewal Reports)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional index page. It names each scheme, its purpose, its review basis and its timing. It gives no allocation sizes apart from the 8,000 node hour limit for small projects.

## Limits

- The page does not give the total compute that each scheme awards, or success rates.
- It does not say whether use is free. The CSCS brochure says that User Lab resources are free of charge ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- It covers academia only. It does not describe access for industry, which other CSCS sources mention.
- The library has not read the detail pages for development, CHRONOS, small and preparatory projects.
