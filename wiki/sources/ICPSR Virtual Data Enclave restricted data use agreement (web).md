# ICPSR Virtual Data Enclave restricted data use agreement (web)

## Citation

Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR), University of Michigan. "Restricted Data Use Agreement for Restricted Data in the Virtual Data Enclave (VDE)." Agreement form for Millennium Challenge Corporation data, date not stated. https://mcc.icpsr.umich.edu/files/VDE_RDUA.pdf. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The agreement gives an investigator and an institution access to restricted data only inside ICPSR's Virtual Data Enclave (VDE). The VDE is a monitored remote desktop with no download.
- Users must work from a closed "Secure Project Office" and must not try to identify any person. They send all outputs to ICPSR for a disclosure review before they share them.
- Many kinds of organizations can sign, a for-profit organization and a foreign entity included. The agreement runs for 24 months or until the IRB approval expires.

## Background

ICPSR is a data archive at the University of Michigan. This form is for restricted data from the Millennium Challenge Corporation (MCC), a U.S. foreign aid agency, which ICPSR holds as data steward. Access needs an online application with a research proposal, Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval and the signed agreement. Attachment A is the Data Security Plan for VDE users.

## Key claims

- "The VDE is isolated from the user's physical desktop computer, restricting the user from downloading files or parts of files to their physical computer." (Section I, Definitions, "VDE")
- "It is an institution of higher education a research organization, a research arm of a government agency, a nongovernmental, not for profit, agency, a for-profit organization, ... or a non-U.S. or foreign entity." (Section IV.A)
- "the Restricted Data will be used solely for statistical analysis and reporting of aggregated information relative to the project as identified in research description section of the Online Application" (Section VI.A)
- "No release of a sample statistic for which only a small number of records (e.g., 3, 5, or 10 depending on sample characteristics) in the Restricted Data provide a certain combination of values from key variables." (Section VI.G.2)
- "Investigator's separation from Institution immediately terminates this Agreement and access to the Restricted Data." (Section VI.L)
- "This Agreement will be effective for 24 months from execution or until the IRB approval related to the research project identified in the research description section of the Online Application expires, whichever occurs first." (Section X.B)
- "You must submit all statistical outputs/results/notes from the Restricted Data to ICPSR for a disclosure review prior to sharing or giving such outputs to unauthorized persons." (Attachment A, item 15)

## Evidence and method

A legal agreement form of about 5,100 words, with definitions, duties of each party, disclosure rules, penalties and a data security plan. It cites the National Center for Health Statistics disclosure checklist and FCSM Statistical Policy Working Paper 22 for its disclosure rules. It gives no data about use.

## Limits

The form is for one data provider, MCC. Its OMB control number is a placeholder ("XXXXXXXXXXX"), and it prints no date or version. It does not give the VDE's technical design, its cost or how long ICPSR takes to approve an application. ICPSR's general agreement for restricted data that the investigator downloads is a separate form ([ICPSR Restricted Data Use Agreement 2018 (web)](ICPSR%20Restricted%20Data%20Use%20Agreement%202018%20%28web%29.md)).
