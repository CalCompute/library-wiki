# Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust (web)

## Citation

Anthropic. "The Long-Term Benefit Trust." Anthropic News, Announcements. Published Sept. 19, 2023, with a footnote on the Trustees as of October 2026. https://www.anthropic.com/news/the-long-term-benefit-trust. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Anthropic describes its Long-Term Benefit Trust (LTBT), an independent body that holds a special class of stock. That stock lets the Trust elect and remove a part of Anthropic's board.
- The Trust "will elect a majority of the board within 4 years". Anthropic calls the structure "an experiment".

## Background

Anthropic is an AI company and a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation (PBC). This announcement explains why the company added the Trust to its PBC status. It gives the Trust's powers, its legal form, its amendment rules and its first Trustees. A footnote added later gives the Trustees as of October 2026. See [Anthropic](../entities/Anthropic.md).

## Key claims

- "The Trust is an independent body of five financially disinterested members with an authority to select and remove a portion of our Board that will grow over time (ultimately, a majority of our Board)." (introduction)
- "The public benefit purpose stated in Anthropic’s certificate is the responsible development and maintenance of advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity." (Baseline: Public Benefit Corporation)
- "Although the PBC form makes it legally permissible for directors to balance public interests with the maximization of stockholder value, it does not make the directors of the corporation directly accountable to other stakeholders or align their incentives with the interests of the general public." (Baseline: Public Benefit Corporation)
- "The Class T stock grants the Trust the authority to elect and remove a number of Anthropic’s board members that will phase in according to time- and funding-based milestones; in any event, the Trust will elect a majority of the board within 4 years." (LTBT: Basic Structure and Features)
- "The Trust is organized as a “purpose trust” under the common law of Delaware, with a purpose that is the same as that of Anthropic." (LTBT: Basic Structure and Features)
- "We do not expect the LTBT to intervene in these day-to-day decisions or in our ordinary commercial strategy." (Fine-tuning Anthropic’s Corporate Governance)
- "Owing to the Trust’s experimental nature, however, we have also designed a series of “failsafe” provisions that allow changes to the Trust and its powers without the consent of the Trustees if sufficiently large supermajorities of the stockholders agree." (A Corporate Governance Experiment)
- "Trustees serve one-year terms and future Trustees will be elected by a vote of the Trustees." (Meet the Initial Trustees)

## Evidence and method

A company announcement about its own governance. It describes the structure in plain terms and names the law firms and scholars who helped to design it. It does not publish the trust agreement or the charter text, and it gives no figures on the phase-in dates.

## Limits

The page does not give the milestones that set when the Trust elects each board seat. It does not give the supermajority levels of the failsafe provisions. The 2023 text says the Trust has five members; the footnote lists four Trustees "As of October 2026". The page does not discuss public compute or CalCompute.
