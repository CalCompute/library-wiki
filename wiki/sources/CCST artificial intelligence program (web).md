# CCST artificial intelligence program (web)

## Citation

California Council on Science and Technology (CCST). "AI Policy Initiative." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://ccst.us/artificial-intelligence/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- CCST's AI Policy Initiative gives science advice on AI to California policymakers. Its two main programs are the Legislative Staff Academy on AI and the AI Science Residency Program.
- The Residency placed California's first AI Science Advisors at the Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) and the California Department of Technology (CDT). Philanthropy pays for both programs.

## Background

CCST is a nonpartisan 501(c)(3) nonprofit that advises California policymakers. This page describes its AI programs, their funders and its AI funding campaign.

## Key claims

- "CCST's AI Policy Initiative provides nonpartisan, science-informed insights to facilitate California's leadership on AI governance in a rapidly evolving landscape." (AI Policy Initiative)
- "As part of the CCST Science Residency Program, CCST placed California's first AI Science Advisors in the Governor’s Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) and the California Department of Technology (CDT) to support California’s executive branch in implementing recent legislation on frontier AI safety." (AI Science Residency Program)
- "CCST launched the CCST Legislative Academy on AI in October 2025." (Legislative Staff Academy on AI)
- "The initial program began in fall 2025, with a full academy launching in Fall 2026 and continuing through 2027." (Legislative Staff Academy on AI)
- "The CCST AI Science Residency Program is made possible by philanthropic contributions including donations from Omidyar Network and Ralston Family Fund, in direct collaboration with placement agencies." (Powered by Partnership)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It names the funders: Omidyar Network, with the Kapor Foundation, for the Academy, and Omidyar Network and the Ralston Family Fund for the Residency. It says CCST launched its AI Policy Funding Campaign in October 2024. It lists four aims, among them AI Science Advisors in the executive branch and fellows "specializing in AI and data science".

## Limits

The page gives no amounts. It does not name the "recent legislation on frontier AI safety", and it does not mention CalCompute. It calls the Academy both "Legislative Staff Academy on AI" and "Legislative Academy on AI".
