## Bottom line

- Justin Norman, PhD, is the AI Science Advisor to the California Department of Technology (CDT), placed by the CCST AI Science Residency Program.

## Key claims

- CCST launched its AI Science Residency Program with two advisors, at the Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) and at CDT.
- Norman "will advise senior leadership on how developments in frontier AI development and governance impact key definitions and thresholds in California frontier AI safety legislation."
- He was acting Portfolio Director and Technical Director for AI and machine learning at the Defense Innovation Unit.
- He earned his doctorate at the I School in 2026 and was Technical Director of its Public Interest Technology Clinic.
