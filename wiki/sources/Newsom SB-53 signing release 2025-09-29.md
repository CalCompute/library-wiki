# Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29

## Citation

Office of Governor Gavin Newsom, "Governor Newsom signs SB 53, advancing California's world-leading artificial intelligence industry", press release, Sacramento, September 29, 2025. <https://www.gov.ca.gov/2025/09/29/governor-newsom-signs-sb-53-advancing-californias-world-leading-artificial-intelligence-industry/>. ## Bottom line

- The release describes CalCompute in one line, under "Innovation". It calls the consortium "CalCompute", but the statute gives that name to the cluster.
- The Governor and the three working group co-leads say the law balances safety and innovation. The co-leads say it follows the "'trust but verify' policy principles" of their report.

## Background

The Governor's office published this release on the day SB 53, the Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (TFAIA), became law.

## Key claims

- **"What the Law Does", Innovation.**
  > ✅ **Innovation**: Establishes a new consortium within the Government Operations Agency to develop a framework for creating a public computing cluster. The consortium, called CalCompute, will advance the development and deployment of artificial intelligence that is safe, ethical, equitable, and sustainable by fostering research and innovation.
- **Economy.** The law helps advance California's position as "the world's fourth-largest economy, the birthplace of new technology, and the top pipeline for tech talent."
- **Newsom quote.**
  > **"California has proven that we can establish regulations to protect our communities while also ensuring that the growing AI industry continues to thrive. This legislation strikes that balance. AI is the new frontier in innovation, and California is not only here for it – but stands strong as a national leader by enacting the first-in-the-nation frontier AI safety legislation that builds public trust as this emerging technology rapidly evolves."**
- **The state's approach.** "California works closely to foster tech leadership and create an environment where industry and talent thrive." The state is "embracing the technology to make our lives easier and make government more efficient, effective, and transparent."
- **Quote from the working group co-leads (Cuéllar, Li and Chayes).**
  > **"Last year Governor Newsom called upon us to study how California should properly approach frontier artificial intelligence development. The Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act (TFAIA) moves us towards the transparency and 'trust but verify' policy principles outlined in our report. As artificial intelligence continues its long journey of development, more frontier breakthroughs will occur. AI policy should continue emphasizing thoughtful scientific review and keeping America at the forefront of technology."**
- **"California's AI Dominance."** California is "home to **32 of the 50 top AI companies** worldwide". In 2024, "15.7% of all U.S. AI job postings were in California", per the 2025 Stanford AI Index. In 2024, "**more than half** of global VC funding for AI and machine learning startups went to companies in the Bay Area." The release adds that California is "#1 by state, well ahead of Texas (8.8% and New York (5.8%)". California is "home to three of the four companies that have passed the $3 trillion valuation mark".
- The law "builds on recommendations from **California's first-in-the-nation report**".

## Evidence and method

A press release. It cites the 2025 Stanford AI Index for the job-posting figures. It gives no source for the "32 of the 50" figure or the venture capital (VC) figure.

## Limits

- The Innovation line leaves out "cloud" and leaves out (b)(2), "Enabling equitable innovation by expanding access to computational resources."
- It says "The consortium, called CalCompute". The statute names the cluster "CalCompute", not the consortium.
- The quotes are statements of policy. They give no evidence about CalCompute.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This release calls California "the world's fourth-largest economy". The March 18, 2025 release calls it "the fifth-largest economy in the world" ([Governor AI working group release 2025-03-18](Governor%20AI%20working%20group%20release%202025-03-18.md)).
