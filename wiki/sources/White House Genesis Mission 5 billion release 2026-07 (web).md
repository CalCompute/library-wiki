# White House Genesis Mission 5 billion release 2026-07 (web)

## Citation

The White House. "Trump Administration Announces More Than $5 Billion for the Genesis Mission, a National Mission on AI for Science." Release, July 22, 2026. https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/45502/. ## Bottom line

- The Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) announced "more than $5 billion in Federal commitments" to the Genesis Mission. More than 15 federal agencies will contribute.
- The release names National Science and Technology Challenges in health, infrastructure, energy, industry, discovery and security. Agencies "will leverage the DOE-built American Science and Security Platform".

## Background

The President launched the Genesis Mission by executive order in November 2025. This release came on the day of the Genesis Mission 2026 Summit, when the U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) also selected 278 projects. It quotes leaders of several federal agencies and lists the challenges.

## Key claims

- "Today, Assistant to the President and Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy Michael Kratsios unveiled more than $5 billion in Federal commitments expanding the Genesis Mission alongside new mission-driven National Science and Technology Challenges." (first paragraph)
- "What began at the Department of Energy (DOE) is now a whole-of-government initiative: More than 15 Federal agencies will contribute research awards, funding opportunities, specialized scientific datasets, and research facilities to the National Science and Technology Challenges." (second paragraph)
- "These agencies will leverage the DOE-built American Science and Security Platform, which provides shared infrastructure that connects researchers with data, compute, and AI tools to accelerate scientific discovery." (second paragraph)
- "The 278 projects selected today represent the very best of our nation's scientific enterprise." (quote of the Secretary of Energy)
- "Doing so requires large amounts of organized, high-quality data and significant computing power, resources that already exist within DOE's National Laboratories and across the Federal government." (closing section)
- "The Genesis Mission sets out a new operating model for American science, aiming to double America's R&D output and translate scientific breakthroughs into practical benefits for the American people." (closing section)

## Evidence and method

A White House release. It gives one total for federal commitments, the count of agencies and the list of challenges. It quotes agency leaders. It does not break the total down.

## Limits

The release does not say how much each agency commits or over how many years. It does not say whether the $5 billion is new funding or existing programs. It does not name the 278 projects; [DOE Genesis Mission awards list 2026 (web)](DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20awards%20list%202026%20%28web%29.md) lists them.
