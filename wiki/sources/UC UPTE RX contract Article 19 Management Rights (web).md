## Bottom line

- Article 19 of the agreement between the University of California (UC) and UPTE lists the management rights that UC keeps for itself.
- They include the right to introduce or eliminate methods, equipment and facilities. They also include the right to set the size of the work force and to "subcontract all or any portion of any operation".
- UC "is not obligated to bargain with UPTE" on these areas during the term of the agreement.

## Background

The agreement is between UC and UPTE for the RX unit. UC posts each article as a separate file on UCnet. The library does not hold UC's contract page for the RX unit.

## Key claims

- "Management of the University is vested exclusively in the University." (Article 19, A)
- "Although the University may upon request consult with UPTE concerning the following areas, the University is not obligated to bargain with UPTE as to such areas during the term of this Agreement." (Article 19, A)
- "to introduce new or improved methods, programs, equipment, or facilities or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment, or facilities;" (Article 19, B.5)
- "to determine the location or relocation, reorganization, or discontinuance of operations; to determine where employees shall work; or subcontract all or any portion of any operation;" (Article 19, B.6)
- "to establish the size, composition, and qualifications of the work force;" (Article 19, B.8)
- "No action taken by the University with respect to a management right shall be subject to the grievance or arbitration procedure or collateral suit, unless the exercise thereof violates an express written provision of this Agreement." (Article 19, D)

## Evidence and method

One article of a collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.
