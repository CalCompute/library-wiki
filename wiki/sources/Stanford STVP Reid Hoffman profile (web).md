# Stanford STVP Reid Hoffman profile (web)

## Citation

Stanford Technology Ventures Program (STVP), Stanford University. "Reid Hoffman." People page, page date not stated. https://stvp.stanford.edu/people/reid-hoffman. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The STVP biography of Reid Hoffman. It calls him "the Co-Founder and Executive Chairman at LinkedIn" and "a partner at Greylock Partners".
- The text is old. It gives LinkedIn "more than 60 million members", so it does not show his roles today.

## Background

STVP is a program of Stanford University. Its site gives "free access to Stanford Technology Ventures Program’s teaching and resources", for example the Entrepreneurial Thought Leaders series. This page links three talks by Hoffman: a lecture of January 29, 2025 and two items from February 22, 2012.

## Key claims

- "Hoffman is the Co-Founder and Executive Chairman at LinkedIn." (biography)
- "Founded in 2003, LinkedIn is the world’s largest professional networking service, with more than 60 million members in 200 countries." (biography)
- "Hoffman also serves as a partner at Greylock Partners." (biography)
- "Prior to LinkedIn, Reid was Executive Vice President of PayPal." (biography)
- "Currently, in addition to LinkedIn, Reid serves on the Board of Directors for SixApart and Mozilla Corporation." (biography)
- "Reid graduated with distinction from Stanford University with a B.S. in Symbolic Systems and from Oxford University with a Master’s in philosophy." (biography)

## Evidence and method

A short biography by a Stanford program. It gives his roles, his past management work at Fujitsu Software Corporation and Apple, and his degrees. It gives no sources.

## Limits

The page has no date, and its figures belong to an early period of LinkedIn. It does not give his present roles, his philanthropy or his work on AI. For this reason the page has `citable: no`.
