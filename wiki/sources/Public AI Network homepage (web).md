# Public AI Network homepage (web)

## Citation

Public AI Network. "Public AI Network" (homepage). publicai.network, date not stated; the latest past event it lists is of Feb. 27, 2026. https://publicai.network/. Read 2026-11-19. The page states: "Work in this repository is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license."

## Bottom line

- The Public AI Network defines public artificial intelligence (AI) as "AI as public infrastructure". It gives three features: public access, public accountability and permanent public goods.
- The network calls itself "a growing coalition working to bring about public AI". It lists its aims, its events since 2023 and the organizations that give it operational support.

## Background

The page is the home page of the [Public AI Network](../entities/Public%20AI%20Network.md). It links to the network's 2024 white paper. The library has the white paper as [Public AI Network 2024 white paper](Public%20AI%20Network%202024%20white%20paper.md) and its web summary as [Public AI Network white paper page (web)](Public%20AI%20Network%20white%20paper%20page%20%28web%29.md). The page also lists the network's code repositories on GitHub, among them a "Public AI Inference Utility".

## Key claims

- "Public AI is **AI as public infrastructure**: provisioned like electricity, water, roads, libraries, or the Internet itself." (What is public AI?)
- "Public AI is **publicly accessible**: ensuring AI delivers benefits to all." "Public AI is **publicly accountable**: ensuring AI reflects society’s values." "Public AI creates **permanent public goods**: ensuring AI is a reliable foundation." (What is public AI?)
- "Private companies have a head start. Without investment in public capacity now, the gap will grow." (Why do we need public AI?)
- "The **Public AI Network** (PAINT) is a growing coalition working to bring about public AI." (Who we are)
- Aims: "Ensure public capacity-building is part of the conversation about AI design, policy, and funding" and "Support policymakers and technical teams seeking to implement public AI". (Who we are)
- "We gratefully acknowledge operational support by Metagov, Aspen Digital, Open Future, Public Knowledge, Code for Science and Society, Mozilla, the Rockefeller Foundation, the Internet Archive, Chatham House, the Berkman Klein Center, the Bertelsmann Foundation, the Patrick J. McGovern Foundation, and many more organizations." (Acknowledgements)

## Evidence and method

An organization's home page. It states the network's definition, aims and events. It gives no data and cites no study.

## Limits

The page does not give the network's governance, funding or members. It names CalCompute nowhere. Its events table lists one California event: "Public AI: Policy, Community, & the Future of National Labs. TPC 2025", San Jose, July 31, 2025.
