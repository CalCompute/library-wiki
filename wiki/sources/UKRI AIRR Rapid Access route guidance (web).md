# UKRI AIRR Rapid Access route guidance (web)

## Citation

UK Research and Innovation (UKRI). "AIRR Rapid Access route (UKRI guidance)." GOV.UK, updated 28 September 2026. https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-research-resource/airr-rapid-access-route-ukri-guidance. Read 2026-11-19. The page is a GOV.UK publication under the Open Government Licence v3.0.

## Bottom line

- The Rapid Access route of the UK AI Research Resource (AIRR) gives up to 20,000 GPU hours, for use within 3 months. It is for UK-registered micro, small and medium businesses that develop an AI product before market launch.
- The applicant must have a Companies House registration number. The route gives compute only, and the business funds the rest of the project costs by set support ratios.
- A UKRI and BIST team does a light-touch review each month, with a maximum of 2 months to resource access.

## Background

AIRR is the UK's set of AI supercomputers, described in [UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md). This UKRI guidance gives the rules of the Rapid Access route. Its summary also describes the Gateway and Innovator routes. See [UKRI AIRR Gateway route guidance (web)](UKRI%20AIRR%20Gateway%20route%20guidance%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Open to UK-registered micro, small and medium organisations, this route supports early-stage development of artificial intelligence (AI) products prior to market launch." (Summary)
- "Up to 20,000 GPU hours on AIRR services, to be used within 3 months of project start." (Summary)
- Innovator route: "Between 50,000 and 150,000 GPU hours on AIRR services, to be used within six months of project start." Its closing date was "4pm on 16 January 2026". (Summary, Innovator)
- "Your organisation must be a UK registered business and have a Companies House registration number." (Who can apply)
- "Small amounts of compute can be secured quickly, with a minimum amount of friction, to allow businesses to conduct research and development activities at short notice and respond rapidly to market opportunities." (Scope)
- "The balance between your total eligible project costs and the amount of compute resources awarded must be funded by your organisation." (Support ratios)
- "The aim is that this will be done on a monthly cadence, with a maximum time-to-resource-access of 2 months after the date of submission." (How your project will be assessed)

## Evidence and method

Official application guidance of about 2,000 words from a public research funder. It states rules, support ratios and three assessment questions. It gives no counts of applications or awards.

## Limits

The guidance does not name the AIRR systems that an award uses. It does not give a success rate or the award terms. Academic and charity applicants are not eligible for this route; the Gateway route covers them.
