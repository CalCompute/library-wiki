# UC SX contract page (web)

## Citation

University of California, UCnet. "SX — Service: Contract." Web page, no date. https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/labor/bargaining-units/sx/contract.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-20 UC SX contract page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-20%20UC%20SX%20contract%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) lists its contract with the American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees (AFSCME) for the Service (SX) unit. The term is Jan. 31, 2020 through Oct. 31, 2024.
- After impasse, UC is implementing terms of its last, best and final offer (LBFO) for the SX and EX units. The terms include wage increases to $25 an hour or 5 percent.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the SX unit's pages. It links the complete contract. The library holds it as [UC AFSCME SX contract (web)](UC%20AFSCME%20SX%20contract%20%28web%29.md). The page also links an implementation package of 2025 and side letters, one of them on contracting out.

## Key claims

- "After 16 months of negotiating and bargaining in good faith with AFSCME, reaching impasse and exhausting the impasse procedures, UC is implementing certain terms of its last, best and final offer (LBFO) for AFSCME SX and EX bargaining units."
- "These increases will move these UC team members to $25 an hour by July 1 or a 5% increase, whichever provides a greater benefit to its employees, provide health care credits to employees in pay bands one and two and implements 2025 health care premium rates across the AFSCME workforce."
- "Effective January 31, 2020 through October 31, 2024"
- "Side Letter – Contracting Out" (list of side letters)

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page gives no date of its last update. The implementation package address names June 2025; the page does not give the year of "July 1".
- The library holds neither the implementation package nor the side letter on contracting out.
