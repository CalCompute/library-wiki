# PECG Unit 9 State MOU 2025-2028 (web)

## Citation

Professional Engineers in California Government and the State of California. "Unit 9 Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) 2025-2028 between the Professional Engineers in California Government (PECG) and the State of California." Booklet, Professional Engineers in California Government. https://pecg.org/wp-content/uploads/PECG-2025-2028-MOU-Booklet.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20.

## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and Professional Engineers in California Government (PECG) for State Bargaining Unit 9. Its term is July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2028.
- The State keeps all rights that the MOU does not abridge. The State gives PECG 30 days notice of a change in an area that the MOU does not cover. It negotiates the impact when three conditions exist.
- Side Letter #27 suspended the return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 for Unit 9 until July 1, 2026.

## Background

Unit 9 is the State's engineers and related classes. PECG publishes the MOU as a booklet with a letter to its members. The California Department of Human Resources (CalHR) negotiated for the State. The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement. Article 19.2 lists the Government Code sections that the MOU incorporates.

## Key claims

- "The terms of this contract shall go into effect on July 1, 2025 and shall remain in full force and effect through and including June 30, 2028." (Article 2, 2.1)
- "All the functions, rights, powers and authority not specifically abridged by this MOU are retained by the employer." (Article 17, 17.1)
- "The parties recognize that during the term of this MOU, it may be necessary for the State to make changes in areas within the scope of negotiations. Where the State finds it necessary to make such changes, the State shall notify PECG of the proposed change 30 days prior to its proposed implementation." (Article 19, 19.1.b)
- "Provides for counseling and training programs for employees whose positions are to be eliminated by automation, technological or management-initiated changes." (Article 19, 19.2, Government Code section 19995.2)
- "The State shall reimburse bargaining unit employees for departmentally-approved expenses incurred as a result of attending authorized job-related or career-related training or education in accordance with CalHR rules." (Article 22, 22.1)
- "the State and the Union agree that the State’s return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 will be suspended immediately and reinstituted on July 1, 2026." (Side Letter #27, reached June 19, 2025)

## Evidence and method

A memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, with the union's letter to its members. It gives rules, salary schedules and a class listing.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on contracting out and no article on artificial intelligence.
- The letter to members at the front is the union's text, not a term of the MOU.
- Side Letter #27 is operative through June 30, 2026. The library holds no later document on the return-to-office requirements for Unit 9.
