# EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)

## Citation

European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU). "JUPITER: Launching Europe's Exascale Era." Press release, 5 September 2025. https://eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/jupiter-launching-europes-exascale-era-2025-09-05_en. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- JUPITER, at Jülich in Germany, is Europe's first exascale supercomputer. The German Chancellor and a European Commissioner inaugurated it on 5 September 2025.
- EuroHPC JU owns JUPITER and pays half of its cost. The German federal research ministry and the North Rhine-Westphalia culture and science ministry pay the other half in equal parts.
- Access is open to all users through EuroHPC access calls. EuroHPC JU and the Gauss Centre for Supercomputing manage the allocation together.

## Background

The Jülich Supercomputing Centre (JSC) designed JUPITER with EuroHPC JU, and EuroHPC JU procured it. The release reports the inauguration by the German Chancellor and a European Commissioner. It also gives background on EuroHPC JU: 11 supercomputers procured, AI Factories and quantum computers.

## Key claims

- Capability: "JUPITER stands as the first European supercomputer capable of performing one exaflop". (paragraph 2)
- Access: "JUPITER will accelerate innovation and scientific discovery across Europe with access open to all users via the EuroHPC access calls. The allocation of the computing resources is jointly managed by the EuroHPC JU and the Gauss Centre for Supercomputing." (paragraph 4)
- Early access: the JUPITER Research and Early Access Program (JUREAP) "empowered more than 30 lighthouse projects, 15 of which were selected by EuroHPC JU". (paragraph 5)
- Hardware: "JUPITER Booster is equipped with approximately 24,000 NVIDIA GH200 Grace Hopper Superchips". (More details)
- Heat reuse: "the supercomputer is designed to reuse the heat it generates during operation to heat buildings." (More details)
- Ownership and funding: JUPITER "is fully owned and co-funded half by the European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU). The remaining half is funded equally by the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR, formerly BMBF) and the German Ministry of Culture and Science of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia (MKW NRW)." (More details)
- AI Factory: the JUPITER AI Factory (JAIF) will "support industry, particularly start-ups and small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), to harness its power". (Next steps)
- Role of EuroHPC JU: "The EuroHPC JU is a legal and funding entity that brings together the European Union and participating countries to coordinate efforts and pool resources". (Background)
- Users: "European scientists and users from the public sector and industry can benefit from EuroHPC supercomputers via the EuroHPC Access Calls no matter where in Europe they are located". (Background)

## Evidence and method

This is a press release from an EU body. It gives the ownership and funding shares, the hardware and the access route. It gives no euro amounts.

## Limits

- The release gives the funding shares but not the cost in euros.
- It does not give the share of JUPITER time for each funder.
- It says EuroHPC JU oversees "13 AI factories" (Background). The AI Factories page, read on 2026-11-11, gives 19 ([EuroHPC JU AI Factories (web)](EuroHPC%20JU%20AI%20Factories%20%28web%29.md)).
