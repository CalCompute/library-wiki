# CENIC AIR initiative (web)

## Citation

Corporation for Education Network Initiatives in California (CENIC). "You Already Have Everything You Need: The CENIC AI Resource." Initiative page, date not stated. https://cenic.org/initiatives/cenic-air. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The CENIC AI Resource (CENIC AIR) is the California part of the National Research Platform (NRP). It runs on the Nautilus Kubernetes cluster over CalREN, with compute and storage that CENIC members give voluntarily.
- "Any faculty member with a CILogin can get access". Campuses can add their own hardware through "bring your own resources" (BYOR). Five CSU campuses have researchers on it.

## Background

CENIC runs the California Research and Education Network (CalREN). The page describes CENIC AIR, its link to the NRP, its reach to less-resourced campuses and the CSU, and how a user or campus joins. See [CENIC About (web)](CENIC%20About%20%28web%29.md) and [NAIRR and the National Research Platform](../concepts/NAIRR%20and%20the%20National%20Research%20Platform.md).

## Key claims

- "To facilitate this transformation there is a need for a national-level "AI Resource" that is open-access, scalable, and grown through the contributions of its user community." (introduction)
- "The NRP is a member-owned and led national cyberinfrastructure created by a partnership of more than 50 institutions nationwide." (CENIC AIR: the National Research Platform in California)
- "The California portion, CENIC AIR, consists of high-performance compute and storage infrastructure voluntarily contributed to by members of the CENIC community" (CENIC AIR: the National Research Platform in California)
- "The NRP community has grown during the last 5 years from 10 to 135 campuses, including campuses in 21 of the 28 states and territories identified as part of the NSF's Established Program to Stimulate Competitive Research (EPSCoR) and with 24 of them being Minority Serving Institutions (MSIs)." (Providing AI Resources to a Broader Data Science Community)
- "Currently, CSU campuses with researchers using CENIC AIR include CSU Northridge, CSU San Bernardino, San Diego State University, San Jose State University, and Cal Poly Humboldt." (Broadening Inclusion in California)
- "Any faculty member with a CILogin can get access to CENIC AIR." (Broadening Inclusion in California)
- Users can "pursue research at a scale up to hundreds of GPU hours and thousands of CPU core hours operating on terabytes of data" (Broadening Inclusion in California)
- "Should a campus choose to add resources to CENIC AIR and the NRP, it does so via the "bring your own resources" (BYOR) program." (Can a Campus Connect Equipment to CENIC AIR?)

## Evidence and method

An initiative page of about 1,200 words of body text, from the operator. It gives campus counts and named campuses. It gives no budget, hardware totals or usage data.

## Limits

The page does not say how many GPUs CENIC AIR has, what it costs, or who funds it. It names no access limits beyond the CILogon login. The linked FAQ is a Google Doc and was not downloaded.
