# NJ PL 2025 c404 supplemental appropriation (web)

## Citation

New Jersey Legislature. P.L. 2025, c. 404, "A Supplement to the annual appropriations act for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2026, P.L.2025, c.74." Approved January 20, 2026. https://pub.njleg.gov/bills/2024/PL25/404_.HTM. ## Bottom line

- This New Jersey act adds money to the state budget for fiscal year 2026. One line gives $25,000,000 for "New Jersey’s AI Supercomputer Initiative – Support and Advancement".
- The New Jersey Economic Development Authority (NJEDA) uses the money for a grant to a higher education institution that hosts a new supercomputer.

## Background

The act supplements the annual appropriations act for the fiscal year that ends June 30, 2026 (P.L. 2025, c. 74). It appropriates General Fund money to several departments: Agriculture, Community Affairs, Education, Human Services, State and the Treasury. The supercomputer line is under the Department of the Treasury, Economic Planning and Development, Grants-in-Aid. The act takes effect immediately.

## Key claims

- The Grants-in-Aid list gives "($25,000,000)" to "New Jersey’s AI Supercomputer Initiative –". The next line reads "Support and Advancement". (sec. 1, Department of the Treasury, Grants-in-Aid)
- "The amount hereinabove appropriated for New Jersey’s AI Supercomputer Initiative – Support and Advancement shall be used by the New Jersey Economic Development Authority to provide a grant to an institution of higher education to fund the supplementary infrastructure costs of the institution of higher education hosting a new state-of-the-art supercomputer" (sec. 1, Department of the Treasury)
- The grant covers costs "including system management, rental payments, salary costs of engineers, researchers, and support staff, security, user access, and research support, subject to the approval of the Director of the Division of Budget and Accounting." (sec. 1, Department of the Treasury)
- "This act shall take effect immediately." (sec. 3)
- "Approved January 20, 2026." (end of act)

## Evidence and method

The enacted text of a state appropriations supplement. It gives the amount, the agency that grants the money and the costs that the grant can pay. The other lines of the act, for example food security research and county projects, are not about computing.

## Limits

- The act does not name the host institution, the hardware or the users.
- It does not say how the NJEDA selects the grant recipient.
- It covers one fiscal year and gives no money for later years.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The act gives the grant to "an institution of higher education" that hosts the supercomputer. [NJ Governor release 2026-10-20 (web)](NJ%20Governor%20release%202026-10-20%20%28web%29.md) and [NJSCI homepage (web)](NJSCI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md) say that SHI International hosts and operates the infrastructure in its own New Jersey facilities. The act is from January 2026; the release is from October 2026.

The release and the homepage say that the state's $25 million "was appropriated for the initiative in the Fiscal Year 2026 budget". [Choose NJ NVIDIA supercomputer agreement (web)](Choose%20NJ%20NVIDIA%20supercomputer%20agreement%20%28web%29.md) says that the NJEDA will "manage and deploy" about $25 million.
