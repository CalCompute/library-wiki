# INCITE 2026 fact sheets (web)

## Citation

DOE Leadership Computing, Innovative and Novel Computational Impact on Theory and Experiment (INCITE) program. "2026 INCITE Awards" (fact sheets). PDF, May 2026 (upload folder). https://doeleadershipcomputing.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/123/2026/05/2026INCITEFactSheets.pdf. Fetched 2026-11-20. Read 2026-11-20. Not rehosted: the host is not a government host and the file states no open licence.

## Bottom line

- The PDF gives one fact sheet for each of the 75 INCITE awards of 2026: 56 new projects and 19 renewals.
- Each sheet gives the title, the principal investigator (PI) and co-investigators, the discipline, the node-hours on each machine and a research summary.
- The machines are Frontier at Oak Ridge National Laboratory, and Aurora and Polaris at Argonne National Laboratory. Fifteen of the 75 PIs give a California institution.

## Background

INCITE is a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) program that allocates time on the leadership computers at Oak Ridge and Argonne. INCITE allocates "up to 60 percent of available node-hours" on Aurora and Frontier, by the call for 2027 ([OLCF INCITE 2027 call for proposals (web)](OLCF%20INCITE%202027%20call%20for%20proposals%20%28web%29.md)). This PDF has no introduction, method or totals. Every page has the header "2026 INCITE Awards".

## Key claims

- The first sheet gives "Argonne National Laboratory", "HPE Cray EX - Intel Exascale Compute Blade Nodes" and "(1,300,000 Aurora node-hours)" for a chemistry project of Sorbonne University and Qubit Pharmaceuticals Inc. (p. 1)
- A Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory project on clinical large language models (LLMs) says: "Using an INCITE allocation, we developed two VA-specific LLMs—a 1.62B-parameter and a 13.6B-parameter model—pre-trained entirely on VA data". It has "(100,000 Aurora node-hours)" and "(700,000 Frontier node-hours)". (Crivelli fact sheet)
- The same project will "build a 100-billion-parameter energy-efficient foundation model capable of handling long EHR contexts". EHR means electronic health record. (Crivelli fact sheet)
- A Stanford University project has "(600,000 Frontier node-hours)" to "identify molecular mechanisms by which different drugs that bind to the same target can stimulate different cellular signaling pathways". (Dror fact sheet)
- An Oak Ridge project on a turbulence foundation model reports "1.1 EFLOPS performance with 32,768 GPUs" on Frontier, and says "This INCITE allocation will enable full-scale training runs on larger datasets and model sizes." (Zhang fact sheet, last page)

## Evidence and method

A list of award fact sheets, with no analysis. The agent read the PI, discipline and allocation lines of all 75 sheets, and read the research summaries in part. The counts on this page are the agent's counts of those lines; the PDF prints no totals.

- Frontier: 51 awards, 37,599,205 node-hours in all.
- Aurora: 36 awards, 29,338,000 node-hours in all.
- Polaris: 15 awards, 2,449,000 node-hours in all.
- PIs at California institutions: Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (4), Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (2), Stanford University (2), University of Southern California (2), UC Los Angeles (2), UC Irvine, UC Santa Cruz and UC San Diego (1 each).

Companies appear on the sheets as PI or co-investigator: General Atomics, GE Aerospace Research, Boeing, NVIDIA, Type One Energy Group, Qubit Pharmaceuticals and Dridam.

## Limits

The sheets give no award dates, no proposal numbers, no review scores and no cost or dollar value of the node-hours. This page gives the node-hours of each machine apart, and does not add them across machines. The PDF does not say if the list is complete. The library cannot rehost the file, so this page links to the publisher's address.

The PDF does not mention CalCompute.
