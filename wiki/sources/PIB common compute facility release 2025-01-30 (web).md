# PIB common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)

## Citation

Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India, for the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). "With robust and high end Common computing facility in place, India all set to launch its own safe & secure indigenous AI model at affordable cost soon: Shri Ashwini Vaishnaw." Press release, Release ID 2097709, PIB Delhi, January 30, 2025. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2097709. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The IndiaAI Mission reports a common compute facility of 18,693 graphics processing units (GPUs), above its first target of 10,000. About 10,000 are available at the start.
- MeitY empanelled cloud providers through a Request for Empanelment (RFE) of August 16, 2024. Ten bidders passed the technical evaluation. Other bidders can join a category if they match the lowest (L1) price.
- The release gives about ₹115.85 per GPU hour, and less than ₹100 per hour after a 40 percent government subsidy.

## Background

PIB issued this release after a media event of Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw at Electronics Niketan, New Delhi. It covers the compute facility, the empanelment of cloud services, the IndiaAI Safety Institute, a call for proposals for foundational models and AI applications. The Cabinet approved the Mission in March 2024 ([PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)](PIB%20Cabinet%20approves%20IndiaAI%20Mission%202024-03-07%20%28web%29.md)). The release links to three annexures with the L1 prices and the selected safety projects; the library has not read them.

## Key claims

- Size: "The facility now houses 18,693 GPUs, including 12,896 H100, 1,480 H200, and 7,200 MI 200 300 units, significantly exceeding the initial target of 10,000 GPUs." (Common Compute Facility is the strong foundation for Democratic AI Development)
- Start: "The AI model is beginning with the computation facility of roughly 10000 GPUs. Soon the remaining 8693 GPUs will be added." (paragraph 2)
- Empanelment: MeitY "published a Request for Empanelment (RFE) on August 16, 2024, to empanel AI services on cloud and offer the services to academia, MSMEs, startups, research community, governments, public sector agencies and other entities approved by IndiaAI via the CPP portal." (Empanelment of AI services on cloud)
- Bids: "19 bidders that included Cloud Service Providers (CSPs), Managed Service Providers (MSPs), and Data Center Service Providers submitted proposals." Ten were found eligible for the financial bids. (Empanelment of AI services on cloud)
- Price matching: "the bidder quoted the L1 rates for a particular category of compute unit will be empaneled first and all other bidders in that category of the compute unit will be eligible for empanelment if they agree to match the L1 bid." (Empanelment of AI services on cloud)
- Price: "The cost per GPU hour is approximately ₹115.85, significantly lower than the global benchmark of $2.5–$3 per hour." (Affordable Compute Facility for AI Development)
- Subsidy: "High-end computing will be available at ₹150 per hour, with a 40% government subsidy reducing the cost to less than ₹100 per hour for common Compute access." (Affordable Compute Facility for AI Development)
- Model funding: "Funding and support mechanisms include direct funding, which offers grants and AI compute credits with milestone-based disbursements, and equity-based investments". (Call for Proposals for Building India's Foundational AI Models)
- Term: "The India AI Mission operates under a four-year sunset clause with long-term sustainability." (Future Roadmap and Sustainability)

## Evidence and method

This is a government press release about a ministerial media event. It names the ten empanelled bidders and the GPU types they quoted, from Intel Gaudi 2 to NVIDIA H200 and AWS Inferentia2. It describes the evaluation in steps: 19 proposals, 13 presentations, 10 financial bids opened on January 22, 2025. It gives the evaluation criteria for the foundational model call. It gives no source for the comparisons with DeepSeek and ChatGPT.

## Limits

- The GPU types in the release add to 21,576 (12,896 + 1,480 + 7,200), not 18,693. The release does not explain the difference.
- It gives three price figures: ₹115.85 per GPU hour, ₹150 per hour for high-end compute, and less than ₹100 after subsidy. It does not say which GPU each figure is for.
- Later PIB documents give ₹65 per hour for more than 38,000 GPUs ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)). The price and the count changed between the two dates.
- The release says the facility "will soon be operational for widespread use". It does not give a date.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this release says the Mission "operates under a four-year sunset clause" (Future Roadmap and Sustainability). The PIB backgrounder of February 10, 2026 gives the outlay as "₹10,371.92 crore over five years" ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
