## Citation

University of California Office of the President, Undergraduate Admissions. "Data Science Transfer Pathway." ## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) Data Science Transfer Pathway lists the courses for transfer admission to nine data science majors. The majors are on eight UC campuses.
- The courses are introductory data science or statistics, single variable and multivariable calculus, linear algebra and two programming courses.

## Background

UC Admissions publishes transfer pathways for students who move from a community college to UC. Each pathway lists the courses that prepare a student for a group of majors. Students use ASSIST and the Transfer Admission Planner to find and track the courses. The community college Data Science degree has a similar core ([CCCCO Data Science ADT form (web)](CCCCO%20Data%20Science%20ADT%20form%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "Taking the following courses will meet all of the transfer admission course requirements for the participating majors listed below." (page head)
- Course expectations: "Introductory data science/statistics"; "Single variable calculus (full sequence)"; "Multivariable calculus (one semester course)"; "Linear algebra (one course)"; "Computer programming (two courses)" (Course expectations)
- Applicable majors include "Data science, B.A. at Berkeley", "Data theory, B.S. at Los Angeles", "Data science and computing, B.S. at Merced" and "Statistics and data science, B.S. at Santa Barbara" (Applicable majors)
- "More degree programs may be added in the future so you should check back periodically to see if your major has joined this list." (Applicable majors)
- "the minimum GPA and grade requirements for particular courses may vary from campus to campus." (Campus GPA requirements)

## Evidence and method

An admissions guidance page. It lists courses and majors. It gives no admission numbers.

## Limits

The page does not list UC Santa Cruz among the majors. It gives no grade point average and no admission rate. It does not mention the community college Data Science Associate Degree for Transfer, which targets the California State University.
