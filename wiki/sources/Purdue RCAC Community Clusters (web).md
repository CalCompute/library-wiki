# Purdue RCAC Community Clusters (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). "Community Clusters." Service page, date not stated. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/services/communityclusters. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- RCAC operates shared "community clusters" that grants, faculty startup packages and institutional funds paid for. Purdue faculty and staff buy access to them.
- RCAC pools funds from different disciplines to get better prices from vendors. It gives the system administration and the data center space, network, cooling and power.

## Background

This is the service page for the Community Cluster Program on the RCAC website. It invites Purdue faculty and staff to join. It lists three benefits and links an access purchase page and a 2025-2026 brochure. It says the program has run since the fourth quarter of 2006.

## Key claims

- "RCAC operates a significant shared cluster computing infrastructure developed over several years through focused acquisitions using funds from grants, faculty startup packages, and institutional sources." (paragraph 1)
- "These "community clusters" are now at the foundation of Purdue's research cyberinfrastructure." (paragraph 1)
- "RCAC system administrators take care of security patches, attempted hacks, operating system upgrades, and hardware repair so faculty and graduate students can concentrate on research." (Peace of Mind)
- "RCAC data centers provide infrastructure such as networking, racks, floor space, cooling, and power." (Low Overhead)
- "RCAC works with vendors to obtain the best price for computing resources by pooling funds from different disciplines to leverage greater group purchasing power." (Cost Effective)
- "Through the Community Cluster Program, Purdue affiliates have invested several million dollars in computational and storage resources from Q4 2006 to the present with great success in both the research accomplished and the money saved on equipment purchases." (paragraph 3)

## Evidence and method

A service page that promotes the program to Purdue faculty and staff. It describes the funding sources and the buy-in model in general terms. It gives no prices, no cluster names and no exact investment total.

## Limits

The page does not give prices or the terms of a purchase. It does not give the sum invested beyond "several million dollars". The library has not read the linked Access Purchase page or the brochure.
