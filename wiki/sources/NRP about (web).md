# NRP about (web)

## Citation

National Research Platform (NRP). "About: A national platform, run by the people who use it." Web page, no date. https://nrp.ai/about/. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 NRP about.md`.

## Bottom line

- NRP pools GPUs, storage and hosted AI models across more than 70 institutions. It is free to U.S. nonprofit research and education and funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF).
- Institutions that contribute nodes keep priority on them. Other users get the idle capacity. Nautilus is the Kubernetes cluster that NRP runs on.

## Background

The page names the principal investigator, Frank Würthwein, Director of the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), and four co-principal investigators. It lists the engineering and support staff and seven NSF awards.

## Key claims

What NRP is (top of page):

- "The National Research Platform is shared research computing: GPUs, storage, and hosted AI models, pooled across more than 70 institutions and offered free to U.S. nonprofit research and education. It is funded by the National Science Foundation and governed by the community that contributes to it."
- The page gives "400+ Nodes", "70+ Locations", "3 Continents" and "5K+ Users".

Contribution model (section "NRP is the platform"):

- "Institutions contribute nodes and keep priority on their own hardware; when they are not using it, everyone else gets opportunistic access."

Nautilus (section "Nautilus is the cluster"):

- "The Kubernetes cluster the NRP runs on. It is the name in the technical documentation, in hostnames like nrp-nautilus.io, and in the cluster tooling itself."

Funding (section "Supported by the NSF"):

- "The National Research Platform is supported in part by the U.S. National Science Foundation under the following awards." The list is CNS-1730158, ACI-1540112, ACI-1541349, OAC-1826967, OAC-2112167, CNS-2100237 and CNS-2120019.

## Evidence and method

This is an about page. It gives headline counts, the people and the awards. It does not say how it counts nodes or users.

## Limits

- The page does not say how the community governs the platform.
- It does not give the NSF award amounts.
- The access route is in [NRP Getting started (web)](NRP%20Getting%20started%20%28web%29.md) and [NRP Policies (web)](NRP%20Policies%20%28web%29.md).
