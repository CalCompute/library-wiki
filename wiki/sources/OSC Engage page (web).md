# OSC Engage page (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Engage with OSC." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/engagement. Read 2026-11-13.

## Bottom line

- The page lists the ways in which users and partners can take part in the Ohio Supercomputer Center: services, impact reports, future plans and events.
- Under "Help shape FUTURE PLANS", it asks users to contact OSC leadership, attend the OSC Community Briefing and give feedback on the OSC Client Portal.

## Background

OSC is a supercomputer center in Ohio. The page footer calls it "A Division of the Ohio Department of Higher Education" and "A member of the Ohio Technology Consortium". This page is a list of links in four groups. It has no body text.

## Key claims

- "Make use of AVAILABLE SERVICES" (group heading)
- "Learn about OSC'S IMPACT" (group heading); its links include "Attend the OSC Research Symposium" and "Read Annual Research Reports".
- "Help shape FUTURE PLANS" (group heading); its links are "Read About the Ohio Supercomputer Center", "Reach out to OSC leadership", "Attend the OSC Community Briefing" and "Provide feedback on the OSC Client Portal".
- "Find ways to ENGAGE WITH OSC" (group heading); its links include "Get an OSC account" and "Propose a Research Proposal Partnership".

## Evidence and method

A navigation page on OSC's own website. It gives link titles only, with no description, dates or figures. The events list on the day it was read showed only Virtual Help Desk sessions.

## Limits

The page does not say what the Community Briefing is, how often it meets or how OSC uses the feedback it gets. The pages it links to are not in this library, except the governance page: [OSC governance (web)](OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md).
