# OpenAI Services Agreement (web)

## Citation

OpenAI. "OpenAI Services Agreement." Policy web page, effective January 1, 2026. https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The OpenAI Services Agreement covers business and developer use of the OpenAI APIs, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT Business and ChatGPT for Clinicians. It does not cover consumer use.
- The customer keeps its Input and owns its Output. OpenAI "will not use Customer Content to develop or improve the Services, unless Customer explicitly agrees to such use."
- The Order Form sets the term, renewal and fees. Either party can end the agreement for an uncured material breach after thirty days.

## Background

OpenAI posts this agreement on its website, with a PDF copy and a link to its earlier business terms. The contracting party is the "OpenAI Contracting Party" that the agreement defines. Notices go to OpenAI OpCo, LLC in San Francisco or OpenAI Ireland Ltd in Dublin. The agreement has 17 sections, from Services to Definitions. The OpenAI usage policies are in the library as [OpenAI Usage Policies 2025-10-29](OpenAI%20Usage%20Policies%202025-10-29.md).

## Key claims

- Scope: the agreement "only applies to use of OpenAI’s APIs, ChatGPT Enterprise, ChatGPT Business, ChatGPT for Clinicians, and other services for customers who are businesses and developers" (opening note)
- Affiliates: "Customer Affiliates may use the Services under Customer’s Account, which means Customer and its Affiliates usage will occur in the same workspace and under the same organizational ID." (Section 1.4(a))
- Restrictions: Customer will not "allow minors to use OpenAI Services without consent from their parent or guardian" or, "except for a Permitted Exception, use Output to develop artificial intelligence models that compete with OpenAI’s products and services" (Section 3.3)
- Resale: "Customer may not resell or lease access to its Account or any End User Account." (Section 3.1)
- Ownership: Customer "(a) retains all ownership rights in Input; and (b) owns all Output." (Section 4.1)
- Use of content: "OpenAI will not use Customer Content to develop or improve the Services, unless Customer explicitly agrees to such use." (Section 4.2)
- Audit reports: "Upon Customer’s written request, but no more than once per year, OpenAI will provide Customer a copy of the most recent Audit Reports" (Section 5.2)
- Health data: Customer will not process Protected Health Information "unless it has signed the Healthcare Addendum." (Section 5.4)
- Price changes: "Price changes on the Pricing Page will be effective fourteen days after they are posted." (Section 6.6)
- Deletion: on termination, "OpenAI will delete all Customer Content from its systems within thirty days", with stated exceptions. (Section 11.3)
- Liability cap: total liability "WILL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL AMOUNT CUSTOMER PAID TO OPENAI DURING THE TWELVE MONTHS IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO THE EVENT GIVING RISE TO LIABILITY." (Section 14.2)
- Government entities: for U.S. federal Government Entities, "the Services are “commercial computer software” as defined at 48 C.F.R. § 2.101" (Section 16.14)

## Evidence and method

This is the legal text of a commercial contract. The library read Sections 1 to 16. The library did not read Section 17 (Definitions) in full.

## Limits

- The agreement gives no prices. The Order Form and the Pricing Page give them.
- The agreement defines the Governing Laws and the Venue in Section 17. The library did not read those definitions.
- Section 15 requires "final and binding arbitration" of Disputes. Section 15.3 names NAM as the arbitration forum. Section 16.14 gives specific terms for U.S. federal Government Entities only.
- Customers and End Users "may not access or offer access to the Services outside of the Supported Countries and Territories." (Section 16.12)
