# ABCI Agreement of Use 2026 Japanese (web)

## Citation

AIST Solutions Co. (AISol). 共用高性能計算機ABCI利用約款 (Agreement of Use for the shared high-performance computer ABCI). Established April 1, 2024 (令和6年4月1日); last amended March 23, 2026 (令和8年3月23日). PDF, in Japanese. https://abci.ai/ja/how_to_use/data/yakkan.pdf. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- This is the contract that governs use of AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure (ABCI). The National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST) owns ABCI. AIST Solutions Co. (AISol) sells the service.
- Only companies, universities, public bodies and nonprofits located in Japan can sign. Each user must be a resident of Japan, unless AISol approves a non-resident under export control law.
- Users buy ABCI points in advance. Points expire at the end of the fiscal year. Intellectual property from use stays with the user, as a rule.

## Background

The agreement has 42 articles and supplementary provisions. It took effect on April 1, 2024, when AISol took over the service from AIST. It changed on November 29, 2024 and March 23, 2026. It rests on an AIST rule on the management and use of its shared high-performance computers.

The library reads the Japanese text. The English renderings below are the library's own, not an official translation. For what ABCI is and its hardware, see [ABCI about (web)](ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md) and [ABCI computing resource (web)](ABCI%20computing%20resource%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- Ownership: "国立研究開発法人産業技術総合研究所（以下「研究所」といいます。）が所有する共用高性能計算機であるAI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure（以下「ABCI」といいます。）" (Article 1). Library's rendering: "AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure ('ABCI'), the shared high-performance computer that the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology ('the Institute') owns."
- Purpose: AISol offers the service "我が国における人工知能研究を推進し、社会実装を加速することを目的として" (Article 3). Library's rendering: "for the purpose of advancing artificial intelligence research in our country and accelerating its implementation in society."
- Eligible companies: "日本国内に所在地を有し、かつ登記されている会社法（平成17年法律第86号）に規定される法人である「企業」" (Article 4, item 1). Library's rendering: "'companies' that have a location in Japan and are registered corporations under the Companies Act (Act No. 86 of 2005)."
- Eligible public bodies: "日本国内に所在地を有する「大学」、独立行政法人及び財団法人等、学術・研究機関を含む「公的機関」並びに「非営利団体」" (Article 4, item 2). Library's rendering: "'universities' located in Japan, 'public bodies' including incorporated administrative agencies, foundations and academic and research institutions, and 'nonprofit organizations'."
- Residence: users must not breach export control law "かつ、日本国内の居住者でなければなりません" (Article 5, paragraph 1). Library's rendering: "and must be residents of Japan." A non-resident can use ABCI when AISol approves it within the export control laws (same paragraph).
- Purpose test: "利用目的が研究、開発及び評価、並びにそれらに通じた人材の育成を目的とするものであること。" (Article 6, paragraph 3, item 2). Library's rendering: "The purpose of use is research, development and evaluation, and the development of human resources through them."
- Ability to pay: the applicant "第14条に定めるABCIポイントに相当する利用料金の納付を負担する能力を有していること。" (Article 6, paragraph 3, item 5). Library's rendering: "has the ability to pay the usage fee equal to the ABCI points set out in Article 14."
- Multi-year exclusive use: "「ABCI計算資源の占有利用」契約は、複数年度での契約が締結できるものとします。" (Article 6-2, paragraph 3). Library's rendering: "A contract for 'exclusive use of ABCI computing resources' can be concluded for several fiscal years."
- Point expiry: "利用法人が取得したABCIポイントは、申請した年度の3月30日17時（日本標準時）をもって失効し、翌年度に持ち越すことはできません。" (Article 14, paragraph 5). Library's rendering: "ABCI points that a user corporation obtains expire at 17:00 (Japan Standard Time) on March 30 of the fiscal year of application, and cannot be carried over to the next fiscal year."
- Intellectual property: "利用者等がABCI利用サービスの利用により得られた知的財産権は、原則として利用者等に帰属するものとします。" (Article 15). Library's rendering: "Intellectual property rights that users obtain through use of the ABCI service belong, in principle, to the users."
- Acknowledgement: papers that use the service "当該論文等に、ABCI利用サービスを利用した事実を明示しなければなりません。" (Article 21, paragraph 2). Library's rendering: "must state in the paper the fact that the ABCI service was used."
- Operations data: AISol or AIST "運用データから利用者等が特定される情報を除外したデータ及びその統計データを公開することがあります。" (Article 28, paragraph 3). Library's rendering: "may publish operations data with the information that identifies users removed, and statistics of that data."

## Evidence and method

This is a set of standard terms (約款) for the contract between AISol and each user corporation. It defines the roles in a user group: the user corporation, a responsible person, an optional user manager and the users. It sets the application test, with eleven conditions (Article 6, paragraph 3). It also sets annual renewal by March 30, the points system, data handling, accounts and liability limits.

Support covers weekdays from 9:00 to 17:00 and only questions on how to use the service (Article 13). A late payment carries interest of 14.6 percent a year (Article 14, paragraph 8). Japanese law governs, and the Tokyo District Court has exclusive first-instance jurisdiction (Articles 40 and 41).

## Limits

- The agreement does not give the price of a point. The fee page gives it: 220 yen for one point in fiscal year 2026 ([ABCI usage fees (web)](ABCI%20usage%20fees%20%28web%29.md)).
- It refers to user guides, an application screen and conditions for exclusive use on the AISol website. The library has not read those.
- It does not say how AIST and AISol share revenue or costs.
