# Cal-ICOR Partner With Us

## Citation

Cal-ICOR, UC Berkeley. "How Cal-ICOR Works With Your Campus." Web page, cal-icor.org, date not stated. https://www.cal-icor.org/partner/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Cal-ICOR lists 20 "Current Partner Institutions": 15 CSU campuses, 2 UC campuses and 3 community colleges.
- Onboarding has four steps: reach out, explore materials, "Choose Your Support Model", launch and iterate. The page states no application form or fee.

## Background

See [Cal-ICOR](../entities/Cal-ICOR.md).

## Key claims

- Cal-ICOR serves "any California public higher-education institution".
- Support: "Co-design with experts", "Dedicated onboarding", "Responsive, ongoing support" with "direct access to staff".
- "No Local Infrastructure Required": "Our team manages the platform so you don't have to."
- Support models: "full onboarding assistance, light-touch coordination, curriculum adaptation guidance, or technical setup support."
- Partners: Cal Poly Pomona; CSU Bakersfield, Chico State, Dominguez Hills, East Bay, Fresno, Fullerton, Long Beach, Monterey Bay, Northridge, Sacramento, San Bernardino, San Jose, San Marcos and Sonoma; Diablo Valley College, Folsom Lake College and Fresno City College; UC Berkeley and UC Santa Cruz.
- "You do not need a fully formed plan, just an interest in exploring data-enabled teaching."

## Evidence and method

The program's own page.

## Limits

- No terms, costs or eligibility rules. The page is an invitation, not an agreement.
