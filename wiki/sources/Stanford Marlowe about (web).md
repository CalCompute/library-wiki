# Stanford Marlowe about (web)

## Citation

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (Stanford HAI). "About Marlowe." Marlowe, Stanford AI Compute, date not stated (footer © 2026). https://marlowe-research.stanford.edu/about/. Read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 Stanford Marlowe about.md`.

## Bottom line

- Marlowe is Stanford's first GPU-based computational instrument: 248 NVIDIA H100 graphics processing units (GPUs) on 31 nodes, for research in all seven schools.
- Stanford HAI manages it with the Vice Provost and Dean of Research (VPDoR) and University IT (UIT). SLAC houses it.
- A team of research data scientists at Stanford HAI works with faculty on their code and training runs. Stanford Research Computing deploys and operates the machine.

## Background

Stanford HAI publishes this page on the Marlowe site. It gives the purpose of the instrument, its technical specifications and the two teams that run it, with a profile of each staff member. It links to a Stanford Report feature of December 2024, which this library does not hold.

## Key claims

- "Stanford's first GPU-based computational instrument: 248 NVIDIA H100 GPUs powering frontier AI research across all seven schools, managed by Stanford HAI in partnership with VPDoR and UIT." (Header)
- "Marlowe is people as much as hardware — a research data science team partners directly with faculty to turn that hardware into science." (Header)
- Marlowe is "designed to give faculty the infrastructure to train foundation models, run large-scale simulations, and pursue computational work at scales previously available only to industry." (Marlowe: From film noir detective to frontier AI infrastructure)
- Specifications: "248x NVIDIA H100 80GB SXM5", "31 (8 GPUs per node)", "2 TB RAM per node", "NVIDIA InfiniBand NDR (400 Gb/s)" and "5.5 PB total (2.5 PB parallel scratch + 3 PB project/home)". (Technical Specifications)
- Data classification: "Low & Moderate Risk". (Technical Specifications)
- "Marlowe is run by two teams working together: research data scientists at Stanford HAI who partner directly with faculty, and the Stanford Research Computing staff who deploy and operate the machine." (The Team)

## Evidence and method

A program web page of about 1,200 words. It states the hardware and the staffing of one campus GPU system. It names a faculty director, six HAI staff and four Stanford Research Computing staff. It gives no cost of the system, no funding source and no use figures.

## Limits

The page does not say what Marlowe cost or who paid for it. It does not give a date of first operation. It does not say how many PIs use it. For access tiers and recharge rates, see [Stanford Marlowe access and rates (web)](Stanford%20Marlowe%20access%20and%20rates%20%28web%29.md).
