# IRS TEOS bulk data downloads (web)

## Citation

Internal Revenue Service (IRS). "Tax Exempt Organization Search bulk data downloads." Web page, last reviewed or updated 16-Sep-2026. https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/tax-exempt-organization-search-bulk-data-downloads. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 IRS TEOS bulk data downloads.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20IRS%20TEOS%20bulk%20data%20downloads.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The IRS gives four bulk data sets from Tax Exempt Organization Search (TEOS). They are Publication 78 data, the automatic revocation list, Form 990-N (e-Postcard) filings and Form 990 series filings.
- The first three are pipe-delimited text in ZIP files. The Form 990 series filings are XML. "The data set files are updated monthly."

## Background

TEOS is the IRS search tool for tax-exempt organizations. This page lists the bulk downloads behind it, with a posting date for each. It links to data dictionaries, schemas and the [IRS EO BMF extract page (web)](IRS%20EO%20BMF%20extract%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "You can download data sets of information about organizations' tax-exempt status and filings." (introduction)
- Publication 78 data is the "List of organizations eligible to receive tax-deductible charitable contributions." Its format is "Pipe-delimited ASCII text". (Publication 78 data)
- "Organizations that do not file a required annual information return or notice for three consecutive years automatically lose their tax-exempt status by operation of law." (Revocation date of certain organizations)
- Form 990 series: "The most recent 990 series filings on record." "Format: XML". "Latest data posting XML: **August 20, 2026**" (Form 990 series)
- "The data set files are updated monthly." (Downloading instructions)

## Evidence and method

An official download page. It names the data sets, their formats and their last posting dates. The library did not download the data sets.

## Limits

The page gives no record counts. It does not say how many returns are in the XML set or which years it covers. A notice on the page says some revocation dates in the list are wrong. Dates between April 1 and July 14, 2020 "should have a revocation date of July 15, 2020".
