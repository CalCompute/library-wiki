## Bottom line

- CDPH asks for $8 million one time in 2025-26, from the Internal Departmental Quality Improvement Account, for a contract for a generative AI (GenAI) tool.
- The tool helps inspectors write the reports of deficiencies at skilled nursing facilities and general acute care hospitals.
- CDPH may spend the money only after the Department of Technology and the Department of Finance approve a Minimum Viable Product (MVP).

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. The CDPH Center for Health Care Quality (CHCQ) licenses and inspects more than 15,000 health care facilities. Its surveyors write each Statement of Deficiencies by hand. A proof of concept (POC) of the tool ran in a Department of Technology sandbox. The proposal says that it aligns with Executive Order N-12-23.

## Key claims

- "The California Department of Public Health (CDPH) requests $8 million in 2025-26 from the Internal Departmental Quality Improvement Account (Fund 0942-222) to support a Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) Contract, with provisional language authorizing expenditure of funds upon approval of a Minimum Viable Product by the Department of Technology and the Department of Finance." (Budget Request Summary)
- "For example, in 2024 CHCQ received nearly 52,000 complaints across all facility types." (A. Problem Statement)
- "Surveyors’ clinical experience and professional judgment are irreplaceable, and it is for that reason that the GenAI solution will be designed to reduce the administrative burden while maintaining a "human-in-the-loop" approach, ensuring that surveyors remain in control of the contents and outcome of their investigation." (B. Justification)
- "For example, the POC was limited to public-facing information that could be transferred to a California Department of Technology sandbox. In the next phase, the POC environment must be housed in CDPH’s environment so that the product can be tested using investigations that contain protected health information." (D. Outcomes and Accountability)
- "The MVP will involve onboarding the GenAI solution into the CDPH environment, migrating the pilot solution to a Power Platform, integrating multiple large language models and enabling different roles (such as the inspector and the supervisor)." (E. Implementation Plan)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form. It has a resource history of the account, projected outcomes and a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. All $8,000,000 is on line 5340, Consulting and Professional Services, External. The projected outcomes table gives a 15 percent gain in writing time and in supervisory review in 2026-27, and 20 percent after. The proposal calls these "conservative, broad estimates based on capabilities demonstrated in the POC phase".

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- It does not name the vendor or the large language models.
- It says that the vendor-led POC data "is not an apples-to-apples comparison" with a live investigation.
- It says nothing about CalCompute.
