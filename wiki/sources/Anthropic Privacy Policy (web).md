# Anthropic Privacy Policy (web)

## Citation

Anthropic. "Privacy Policy." Effective Sep. 10, 2026. anthropic.com. https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The policy covers personal data from Anthropic's website, Claude.ai and its other services. It does not cover the content that Anthropic processes for customers of its business offerings, such as Enterprise accounts. Customer agreements govern that content.
- Anthropic can use Inputs and Outputs to train its models unless the user opts out. It still uses them when a safety review flags them or the user reports them.
- Deleted conversations leave the back-end within 30 days. Anthropic does not knowingly collect data from children under 18.

## Background

Anthropic makes the Claude models. The policy has 11 sections and regional supplements. A separate Non-User Privacy Policy covers training data from third parties; the library has not read it. ## Key claims

- "This Privacy Policy does not apply to content that we process on behalf of customers of our business offerings, such as our Enterprise accounts." (Introduction)
- "We may use your Inputs and Outputs to train and improve Anthropic AI models, unless you opt out through your account settings." (Section 2)
- "Even if you opt-out, we will use Inputs and Outputs for model improvement when: (i) your conversations are flagged for safety review to improve our ability to detect harmful content, enforce our policies, or advance AI safety research, or (ii) you've explicitly reported the materials to us" (Section 2)
- "You also are able to delete individual conversations, which will be removed immediately from your conversation history and automatically deleted from our back-end within 30 days." (Section 4, Deletion)
- "Anthropic does not engage in decision making based solely on automated processing or profiling in a manner which produces a legal effect" (Section 4, Automated decision-making)
- "Our Services are not directed towards, and we do not knowingly collect, use, disclose, sell, or share any information from children under the age of 18." (Section 7)

## Evidence and method

A company privacy policy of about 6,500 words. It lists the kinds of data, uses, recipients, rights and legal bases. It states practices and cites no audit.

## Limits

The policy gives no fixed retention period in its main text; it points to a privacy center page that the library has not read. It does not cover Enterprise or API customer content. This page read sections 1 to 8 and the headings of sections 9 to 11.
