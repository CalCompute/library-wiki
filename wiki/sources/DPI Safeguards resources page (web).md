# DPI Safeguards resources page (web)

## Citation

United Nations Office for Digital and Emerging Technologies (ODET) and United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), DPI Safeguards Initiative. "Resources." DPI Safeguards website. No date on the page ("© 2026"). https://www.dpi-safeguards.org/resources. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- This page lists the resources of the DPI Safeguards Initiative for digital public infrastructure (DPI).
- They are a resource hub, an interactive knowledge library, the guide to the Framework, adoption pathways and an assessment resource.
- It also links to resources from other bodies: the Principles for Digital Development, GovStack, the 50-in-5 campaign and the DPI Map.
- The page says ODET and UNDP initiated DPI Safeguards.

## Background

The DPI Safeguards Initiative published the Universal DPI Safeguards Framework in September 2024 ([UN OSET and UNDP 2024 DPI Safeguards](UN%20OSET%20and%20UNDP%202024%20DPI%20Safeguards.md)). This page links to the guide at https://framework-dpi-safeguards.org/frameworkpdf, which is the same version 1.0 guide that the library holds. Each resource has one or two lines of description and a link.

## Key claims

- Purpose: "Recognizing that DPI are a dynamic and evolving topic, this section aims to provide relevant resources for all stakeholders in the ecosystem." (Resources)
- Resource hub: "A community hub that features resources from and for partners, including the latest iterations of the Framework, DPI research, and implementation guidance". (DPI Safeguards Resource Hub)
- Knowledge library: "The digital tool allows users to explore the Universal DPI Safeguards Framework, generate scenarios tailored to their context and download applicable recommendations canvas." (Framework Interactive Knowledge Library)
- Pathways: "These pathways outline initial steps that government actors should take to embed safeguards by design in their DPI roadmaps." (The Adoption Pathways)
- Assessment: the assessment resource supports "responsible authorities (RAs) in assessing both the technical adherence (supply) and real-world impact (demand) of DPI Safeguards." (DPI Safeguards Assessment)
- 50-in-5: "By 2028, the 50-in-5 campaign will have helped 50 countries design, launch, and scale components of their digital public infrastructure." (50-in-5)
- Initiators: "DPI Safeguards is initiated by the United Nations Office for Digital and Emerging Technologies (ODET) and the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP)." (page footer)

## Evidence and method

This is an index page of links with short descriptions. It gives no evidence of its own.

## Limits

- The page does not discuss compute infrastructure.
- This library does not hold the linked pathways, assessment or knowledge library.
- The page names ODET and UNDP as initiators. The 2024 guide names the Office of the UN Secretary-General's Envoy on Technology (OSET) and UNDP ([UN OSET and UNDP 2024 DPI Safeguards](UN%20OSET%20and%20UNDP%202024%20DPI%20Safeguards.md)). Neither document explains the change of name.
