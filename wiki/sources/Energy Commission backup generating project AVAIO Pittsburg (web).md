# Energy Commission backup generating project AVAIO Pittsburg (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "AVAIO Pittsburg Backup Generating Facility (PBGF)." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/avaio-pittsburg-backup-generating-facility-pbgf. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. ## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 92 megawatts (MW) at the AVAIO Pittsburg Data Hub, a data center. Its status is "Exempted".
- The facility has 37 diesel generators of 3 MW. It runs only when power from Pittsburg Power Company is unavailable, and it has no connection to the grid.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. AVAIO Digital Partners I, LLC (AVAIO) is the project owner. The docket number is 24-SPPE-01, the capacity is 92 MW and the location is Pittsburg, California. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The PBGF would consist of a total of thirty-seven (37) 3-megawatt (MW) diesel fired generators that would be used exclusively to provide up to 92 MW of backup emergency generation to support the AVAIO Pittsburg Data Hub (PDH)." (Project Description)
- "The components of the PDH would include a three-story approximately 347,740 square foot data center building" (Project Description)
- "The proposed PDH site encompasses approximately 22.31 acres (APNs 095-160-001, 095-160-002, and 095-150-032) and would be located at 2232 Golf Club Road, Pittsburg, California." (Project Description)
- "The PBGF is designed to operate only when electricity from Pittsburg Power Company is unavailable to the PDH." (Project Description)
- "11/21/2025 - Final Initial Study/Mitigated Negative Declaration" (Key Dates)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Limits

- The page gives no date for the exemption decision. Its last key date is the final Initial Study and Mitigated Negative Declaration of Nov. 21, 2025.
- It gives no construction schedule and no number of servers or computing capacity.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
