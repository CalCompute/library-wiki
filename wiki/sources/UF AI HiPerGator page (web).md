# UF AI HiPerGator page (web)

## Citation

University of Florida (UF), UFIT Research Computing. "HiPerGator." Web page, date not stated. https://www.ai.ufl.edu/research/hipergator (the page served is under it.ufl.edu/rc/hipergator). Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- HiPerGator is UF's supercomputer. Its AI SuperPod has 504 NVIDIA B200 GPUs in 63 DGX B200 nodes. UF unveiled the fourth generation, a $33 million purchase, in October 2025.
- Faculty and students at all Florida colleges and universities can use it. Class allocations are free. Research groups buy allocations, under an investor model in use since 2013.

## Background

UFIT Research Computing runs HiPerGator. The page gives its user and project counts, who can use it, its configuration and its rankings. It reports more than 3,700 users and more than 500 projects.

## Key claims

- "UF received the first delivery of the NVIDIA DGX B200 in the world. With support from Mark III and NVIDIA, the fourth generation of HiPerGator was unveiled in October 2025. The $33 million purchase was a highly anticipated step" (Summary)
- "HiPerGator can be used by faculty and their students and researchers and their collaborators at all Florida colleges and universities for teaching and research" (Who can use HiPerGator?)
- "For teaching a class, allocations are free and they last one semester." (Who can use HiPerGator?, item 1)
- "For research, allocations can be purchased for periods ranging from three months to several years." (Who can use HiPerGator?, item 2)
- "Faculty members must be HiPerGator investors, and all other users must be sponsored by faculty members with an active investment to receive access to UFIT’s high-performance computing resources." (Who can use HiPerGator?)
- "504 Blackwell B200 GPUs (180 GB VRAM) in 63 NVIDIA DGX B200 nodes, each with 8 GPUs" (HiPerGator Configuration)
- "$734 million Awards dollars UF external awards enabled by HiPerGator" (page header figures)

## Evidence and method

An institutional service page. It gives hardware counts, user counts, award totals and access rules. It links the price sheets but does not show the rates.

## Limits

The page does not give the research allocation prices or the operating budget.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page says "The $33 million purchase" for the fourth generation of HiPerGator. [UF HiPerGator upgrade 2024 (web)](UF%20HiPerGator%20upgrade%202024%20%28web%29.md) says the Board of Trustees approved "investing $24 million" for it. Neither page explains the difference.
