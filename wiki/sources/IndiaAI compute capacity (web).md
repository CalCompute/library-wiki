# IndiaAI compute capacity (web)

## Citation

IndiaAI, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India. "IndiaAI Compute Capacity: GPU Compute Allocation." IndiaAI portal. No date on the page; the newest allocation in the table has the date 30/05/2025. https://indiaai.gov.in/hub/indiaai-compute-capacity. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- IndiaAI offers GPU compute from empanelled cloud providers to academia, MSMEs, startups, researchers, governments and other approved users.
- The page counts 100 onboarded users in seven categories. Government entities (26) and researchers or academia (20) are the largest groups.
- A table of 21 allocations gives, for each, the GPUs, the provider, the user category, the subsidy and the bill of materials.

## Background

IndiaAI is the agency of MeitY that runs the IndiaAI Mission. This page of the IndiaAI portal reports the allocations of GPU compute under the Mission. The providers in the table are E2E Networks Limited, NxtGen Datacenter and Cloud Technologies Private Limited and Yotta Data Services Private Limited. The allocations in the table date from 23/04/2025 to 30/05/2025.

## Key claims

- Offer: "IndiaAI has empaneled AI services on cloud and intends to offer AI compute infrastructure to academia, MSMEs, startups, research community, governments, public sector agencies and other entities registered and approved by IndiaAI." (GPU Compute Allocation)
- User counts: "20 Researchers or Academia", "15 Startups & MSMEs", "02 IndiaAI Fellowship", "15 Students", "17 Early-stage Startup", "05 Early-stage Researchers", "26 Govt Entities". (GPU Compute Allocation)
- Largest row: Sarvam AI Axonwise Pvt Ltd, "4096" "H100 SXM" GPUs from Yotta Data Services, subsidy "₹98,68,62,256.12" on a bill of materials of "₹2,46,71,55,640.32", 2025/06/01 to 2025/12/01. (table, row 13)
- Government use: the Digital India BHASHINI Division has "200" "H100 SXM" GPUs, subsidy "₹9,14,30,762.49" on a bill of "₹22,85,76,906.23". (table, row 21)
- State use: the Karnataka AI Cell Center for eGovernance has five rows of H100 and H200 GPUs, from 8 to 32 GPUs each. (table, rows 9 to 11, 19 and 20)
- Zero subsidy: two rows show a subsidy of "₹0.00", for Ola cabs and the National Informatics Centre. (table, rows 4 and 7)

## Evidence and method

This is a government allocation register. It lists each award with its date, GPU type and count, provider, category, subsidy and cost. It does not state the subsidy rule. In several rows the subsidy is 40 percent of the bill of materials, for example "₹8651.52" of "₹21,628.80" (row 1).

## Limits

- The table has 21 rows. The user counts add to 100. The page does not explain the difference.
- The table ends in May 2025. It does not show allocations after that date.
- The page does not give the subsidy rule or the hourly price. A Press Information Bureau document gives "₹65 per hour" ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
- The table names individual applicants. This library page names only their organisations.
