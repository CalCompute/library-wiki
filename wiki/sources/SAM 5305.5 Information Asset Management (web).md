# SAM 5305.5 Information Asset Management (web)

## Citation

California Department of General Services (DGS). State Administrative Manual (SAM) section 5305.5, "Information Asset Management." Revised October 2023. https://www.dgs.ca.gov/Resources/SAM/TOC/5300/5305-5. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-12 SAM 5305.5 Information Asset Management.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-12%20SAM%205305.5%20Information%20Asset%20Management.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- SAM 5305.5 tells each state entity to keep an inventory of all its information assets, with the classification and the level of protection of each.
- An asset whose level is not clear gets the "Moderate" level of Federal Information Processing Standards (FIPS) Publication 199. Personal and medical information gets at least "Moderate".

## Background

The State Administrative Manual holds the state's administrative policy, and DGS publishes it. Section 5305.5 is in the information security chapter, 5300. The revision of October 2023 replaces a version of June 2014. The state's cloud policy, [SAM 4983.1 cloud computing (web)](SAM%204983.1%20cloud%20computing%20%28web%29.md), also sets rules for data classification.

## Key claims

- Inventory: "each Agency/state entity shall establish and maintain an inventory of all its information assets, including information systems, information system components, and information repositories (both electronic and paper)." (Policy)
- Basis of classification: the inventory "must include categorization and classification of the information assets by program management and based on the Information Security Program Management Standard (SIMM 5305-A), California Public Records Act (Government Code sections 7920.000-7931.000), Information Practices Act of 1977 (Civil Code Section 1798, et seq.), FIPS Publication 199, and laws governing administration of the Agency/state entity’s programs." (Policy)
- Default level: "If the information asset’s level of protection is not clear, the Agency/state entity is to protect the asset to the categorization level of “Moderate” as defined by FIPS Publication 199." (Policy)
- Custodian role: "Where the Agency/state entity is the custodian or user of the information asset and not the owner, as in the case of Federal Tax Information, Criminal Justice Information Services information, and so forth the Agency/state entity shall ensure the data owner specifies the level of protection." (Policy)
- Inventory items: the list of eight items includes "Owner of the information asset.", "Custodians of the information asset." and "FIPS Publication 199categorization and level of protection (Low, Moderate, or High)." (Policy, items 2, 3 and 6)
- Personal and medical information: the entity "must protect all personal information defined by the California Information Practices Act (Civil Code Sections 1798.3 and 1798.29), and medical information and individually identifiable information defined by the California Medical Information Act (Civil Code Section 56.05) to a minimum FIPS Publication 199 categorization level of “Moderate”." (Policy)

## Evidence and method

This is a primary state policy, about 400 words. It names its implementation controls: the Planning and Program Management families of NIST SP 800-53, SIMM 5305-A and FIPS Publication 199. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The section does not give the controls for each level. It refers to SIMM 5305-A and NIST SP 800-53, which the library has not read.
- It does not mention research data, AI models or shared compute.
- It applies to an "Agency/state entity". It does not say whether it applies to UC or CSU.
