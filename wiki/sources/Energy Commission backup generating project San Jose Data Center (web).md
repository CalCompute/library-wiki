# Energy Commission backup generating project San Jose Data Center (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "San Jose Data Center." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/san-jose-data-center. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project San Jose Data Center.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20San%20Jose%20Data%20Center.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project San Jose Data Center.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for the Microsoft San Jose Data Center at 1657 Alviso-Milpitas Road. Its maximum load is up to 99 megawatts (MW), with an estimated load of 77 MW. Its status is "Exempted".
- Its backup is 224 renewable natural gas generators of 0.45 MW. They also serve load shedding, demand response and resource adequacy services. The CEC adopted its decision on July 14, 2022.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. Microsoft Corporation is the project owner. The docket number is 19-SPPE-04, the capacity is 99 MW and the location is San Jose. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "Microsoft Corporation proposes to construct and operate the San Jose Data Center (project) located at 1657 Alviso-Milpitas Road in San Jose, California." (Project Description)
- "The maximum electrical load of the project would be up to 99 megawatts (MW), although the estimated load would be 77 MW inclusive of information technology (IT) equipment, ancillary electrical/telecommunications equipment, and other electrical loads (administrative, heat rejection, and safety/security)." (Project Description)
- "the project includes 224 0.45 MW renewable natural gas generators to provide electrical power to support the data center uses during utility outages, certain onsite electrical equipment interruptions or failure, and for load shedding, demand response and behind -the -meter resource adequacy ancillary services." (Project Description)
- "The project would also include an onsite 115-kilovolt (kV) substation with two 115-kV electrical supply lines that would connect to PG&E’s Los Esteros Substation, located adjacent to the site." (Project Description)
- "7/14/2022 - Adoption Order on Committee Proposed Decision filed." (Key Dates)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Limits

- Of the 12 project pages, only this one names gas generators. It is also the only one whose generators serve grid services. The [RB Inyokern page](Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20RB%20Inyokern%20Data%20Center%20%28web%29.md) says that its generators do not.
- The key dates end on July 18, 2022. The page says nothing about construction or operation.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
