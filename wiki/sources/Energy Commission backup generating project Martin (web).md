# Energy Commission backup generating project Martin (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Martin Backup Generating Facility (MBGF)." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/martin-backup-generating-facility-mbgf. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Martin.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Martin.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Martin.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 96 megawatts (MW) for the Martin Data Center in Santa Clara. Its status is "Exempted".
- The facility has 44 diesel generators of 2.75 MW. Forty serve the data center and four are house generators.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. Martin Avenue Properties, LLC is the project owner. The docket number is 22-SPPE-03, the capacity is 96 MW and the location is Santa Clara, California. The project status is "Exempted" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The Martin Backup Generating Facility will consist of a total of forty-four (44) 2.75-MW diesel fired generators that will be used exclusively to provide up to 96 megawatts of backup emergency generation to support the Martin Data Center (MDC), to be located at 651 Martin Avenue in Santa Clara, California." (Project Description)
- "Forty (40) of the generators would be dedicated to replace the electricity needs of the data center in case of a loss of utility power" (Project Description)
- "11/17/2022 - Application materials received to docket with payment." (Key Dates)
- "2/16/2023 - Notice of Preparation filed to California State Clearinghouse." (Key Dates)
- "The CEC Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants proposing thermal power plants between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from the CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Evidence and method

A project page from a state agency's website. It gives the docket's fields, a project description and, for some projects, key dates with links to docket documents. This library does not hold the docket documents.

## Limits

- The status is "Exempted", but the last key date is Feb. 16, 2023. The page gives no date for the exemption.
- It gives no size of the data center building and no utility for its normal power.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
