# Jupyter Software Steering Council (web)

## Citation

Project Jupyter. "Software Steering Council." Web page in the Jupyter Governance site, date not stated. https://jupyter.org/governance/software-steering-council. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page gives the purpose, duties and membership of the Software Steering Council (SSC) of Project Jupyter.
- The SSC decides software matters that affect many Jupyter Subprojects. Each Subproject sends one representative.
- Technical decisions where the SSC has no explicit role go to the individual projects.

## Background

Project Jupyter publishes this page in its governance site. The SSC is one of the three bodies that anchor Jupyter's governance. The page lists ten current members and the Subproject that each represents. The site states the content licence "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)".

## Key claims

- Delegation: "Technical decisions and processes where the SSC isn’t explicitly involved are automatically delegated to the individual projects to manage their day-to-day activities". (Purpose)
- Proposals: the SSC is responsible for "Making decisions on JEPs after receiving community feedback". (Sole responsibilities)
- Security: the SSC is responsible for "Owning and managing anything related to security vulnerabilities across the project". (Sole responsibilities)
- Composition: "The Software Steering Council is composed of one representative from each of the Jupyter Subprojects." (Member composition)
- Other members: "the SSC will contain members from certain Working Groups and Standing Committees whose scope has important impact on SSC activities, such as Diversity and Inclusion, and Internationalization." (Member composition)
- Terms: "There is no term length for SSC membership, as each Jupyter Subproject is in charge of selecting its own representative." (Term length)
- Separation: "A person may not serve simultaneously on the SSC and the EC." (Membership)

## Evidence and method

This is the project's own governance rule for one body. It states duties and how members are chosen. It gives no record of decisions.

## Limits

- The page does not define a Jupyter Enhancement Proposal (JEP) in full. It points to the JEP process.
- It gives no meeting record. Meeting times are on the community calendar.
