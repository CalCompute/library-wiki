# OSC User Code of Ethics (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "OSC User Code of Ethics." Web page, © 2018. https://www.osc.edu/supercomputing/support/codeofethics. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The OSC User Code of Ethics sets the conditions of use for the Ohio Supercomputer Center. It has four parts: authorized use, appropriate use, responsible use and OSC responsibilities.
- OSC can suspend an account while it investigates a possible violation. The Director can limit use after the principal investigator (PI) gets notice of inappropriate use.
- Users include "students, faculty, staff members, and commercial clients throughout the state of Ohio".

## Background

OSC is a state-funded supercomputer center in Ohio. This code is on its user support pages and carries the date © 2018. Other pages of the library describe OSC's mission, fees and governance, for example [OSC governance (web)](OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md) and [OSC getting started and fees (web)](OSC%20getting%20started%20and%20fees%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- Mission: OSC exists "to provide state-of-the-art computing services to universities and colleges; to provide supercomputer services to Ohio scientists and engineers; to stimulate unique uses of supercomputers in Ohio". (opening paragraph)
- Users: "OSC serves a large number and variety of users including students, faculty, staff members, and commercial clients throughout the state of Ohio." (opening paragraph)
- Public resource: ethical and legal standards "derive directly from standards of common sense and common decency that apply to the use of any public resource." (opening paragraph)
- Charges: users agree "To respect the financial structure of computing systems; for example, users shall not intentionally develop or use any unauthorized mechanisms to alter or avoid charges levied by OSC for computing services." (Authorized Use, item 5)
- Suspension: "when OSC becomes aware of possible violations of these conditions, it will initiate an investigation. At the same time, in order to prevent further possible unauthorized activity, OSC may suspend the authorization of computing services to the individual or account in question." (Authorized Use)
- No judgment of value: "Beyond the allocation of computing resources, OSC normally cannot and does not judge the value or appropriateness of any user's computing." (Appropriate Use)
- PI first: "When possible inappropriate use of computing resources is encountered, OSC shall notify the principal investigator responsible." (Appropriate Use)
- Director: "Upon the recommendation of OSC staff, the Director may impose limitations on continued use of computing resources." (Appropriate Use)
- File review: "Examination of users files must be authorized by the Director of OSC or his designee." (OSC Responsibilities)

## Evidence and method

This is a code of conduct. It has no data.

## Limits

- The code does not give a data policy, such as rules for protected or export-controlled data.
- It does not set out a written notice or an appeal before a sanction.
- It carries the date © 2018. The page does not say if it changed after 2018.
