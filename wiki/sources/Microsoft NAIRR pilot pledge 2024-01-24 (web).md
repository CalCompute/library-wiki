# Microsoft NAIRR pilot pledge 2024-01-24 (web)

## Citation

Horvitz, Eric (Chief Scientific Officer, Microsoft). "Broadening AI innovation: Microsoft’s pledge to the National AI Research Resource pilot." Microsoft On the Issues blog, Microsoft Corporation, Jan. 24, 2024. https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2024/01/24/national-ai-research-resource-nairr-artificial-intelligence/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Microsoft pledged $20 million in Azure compute credits to the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) pilot.
- The pledge also gives access to models through Azure OpenAI Service, tools for trustworthy AI, resources for HIPAA-compliant computing, and work with Microsoft scientists.
- The author worked on the National Security Commission on AI (NSCAI), which proposed an early NAIRR.

## Background

Microsoft's Chief Scientific Officer wrote this post on the day that the NAIRR pilot launched. The post states Microsoft's contribution to the pilot. It links the pledge to Microsoft's earlier research programs, the Turing Academic Program and Accelerate Foundation Models Research.

## Key claims

- Purpose: the pilot "aligns with our commitment to broaden AI research and spur innovation by providing greater computing resources to AI researchers and engineers in academia and non-profit sectors." (body)
- Credits: "As part of our commitment to the NAIRR pilot, Microsoft will contribute: $20 million in Microsoft Azure compute credits" (list)
- Models: "Access to leading-edge models, including those available via Azure OpenAI Service" (list)
- Health research: "Resources to enable HIPAA-compliant computing in support of health care research" (list)
- People: "Collaborative opportunities with Microsoft’s scientists and engineers" (list)
- NAIRR goal: "The NAIRR’s objective is to democratize access to the computational tools essential for advancing AI in critical areas such as safety, reliability, security, privacy, environmental challenges, infrastructure, health care, and education." (body)
- Origin: "As a commissioner on the National Security Commission on AI (NSCAI), I worked with colleagues on the committee to propose an early conception of the NAIRR" (body)

## Evidence and method

A company announcement of a pledge. It states what Microsoft will give. It does not report delivery or use.

## Limits

- The post does not state over what period Microsoft gives the credits.
- The post does not state how researchers request the resources.
- The post does not report how much of the pledge researchers used.
