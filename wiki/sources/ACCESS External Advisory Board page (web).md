# ACCESS External Advisory Board page (web)

## Citation

ACCESS Coordination Office (ACO). "ACCESS External Advisory Board (EAB)." ACCESS Collaboration Portal. Page updated "Jul 22", year not printed; page author Ron Payne. https://access-ci.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/ACP/pages/1409025/ACCESS+External+Advisory+Board+EAB. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The ACCESS External Advisory Board (EAB) gives expert advice to the ACCESS Executive Council (EC). It meets four times a year.
- The page lists nine current members from universities, a national laboratory, Internet2 and one company, most since January 2025. It lists past members and the expertise the EC looks for.

## Background

ACCESS is the National Science Foundation (NSF) program for national research computing allocations. Its EC is the governance body of the award PIs ([ACCESS Executive Council charter (web)](ACCESS%20Executive%20Council%20charter%20%28web%29.md)). This page on the program's collaboration portal states the EAB's objective, members and membership criteria. It links to the EAB charter, approved October 9, 2024, as a Google Drive file.

## Key claims

- "Meetings are held four times per year (three virtual; one in-person)" (Meeting Overview)
- "The role of the EAB is to provide expert advice and feedback to the EC on the range of topics relevant for the success of the ACCESS program." (Objective)
- "While the EAB will generally meet with the representatives of all of the awards and NSF as a group, the option exists for the EAB to meet with individual award teams as appropriate." (Objective)
- "Providing feedback and input to ACCESS annual program plans" and "Making recommendations to the EC to serve the evolving CI ecosystem over the life of the ACCESS program" (Objective, activities)
- "EAB members shall be chosen for their expertise and demonstrated commitment to the community served by the ACCESS program." (Membership)
- "The EC seeks an EAB membership that will provide the widest possible range of input and thus will encourage a balanced membership with diverse viewpoints, diverse demographic backgrounds, and those at various points in their careers." (Membership)

## Evidence and method

A program web page with a member table. The current members are:

- Al Anderson, Internet2/Minority Serving Cyberinfrastructure Consortium;
- Yanni Chen, University of Notre Dame;
- Kaihua Ding, AstraZeneca PLC;
- Richard Gerber, NERSC / Berkeley Lab;
- Gwen Jacobs, University of Hawai'i System;
- Gretta Kellogg, Penn State University;
- William Lai, Cornell University, the Researcher Advisory Committee representative (since 2023);
- Rebecca Lindsey, University of Michigan;
- Nitin Sukhija, Slippery Rock University of PA.

The table gives "Jan. 2025" as the start for all except William Lai. The page lists thirteen past members. Among them are Robert Sinkovits (San Diego Supercomputer Center, UC San Diego) and Vivian Huangfu (San Diego State University). Two NSF program officers are ex officio observers. The page lists eight areas of expertise, among them workforce development, project governance and "Large-scale CI programs, systems, and services".

## Limits

- The page prints "Updated Jul 22" with no year. It lists terms that end in December 2025, so the update is from 2026 or later.
- It does not give the EAB charter text, the term length or the nomination process.
- The page links to the EAB recommendations and the contact list as internal Google Sheets. The library has not read them.
- The table marks the Resource Provider Forum Chair with one asterisk, but no current member carries that mark.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: [ACCESS governance (web)](ACCESS%20governance%20%28web%29.md) says "The EAB comprises 11 members", nine of them elected, one Resource Provider Forum Chair and one Researcher Advisory Council member. This page lists nine current members and no Resource Provider Forum Chair.
