# Pathways to College in California

## Citation

California Cradle-to-Career Data System (C2C). "Pathways to College in California." Student Pathways Data Story, web page, no date. https://c2c.ca.gov/data-stories/pathways-to-college-in-california/. Copy: [`raw/shared/2026-10-29 Pathways to College in California.md`](../../raw/shared/2026-10-29%20Pathways%20to%20College%20in%20California.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-10-29.

## Bottom line

- C2C is the state's longitudinal data system. This data story follows students from California public high schools into public colleges and the workforce.
- It reports earnings after a degree, the degrees and awards earned, and how many students work during college. It filters by school district, legislative district and student group.

## Background

The data story follows the high school class of 2014-2015 over nine years.

## Key claims

- "The Student Pathways Data Story provides information on key milestones for students as they navigate from high school to college and into their careers over time." (page head)
- Users can analyze "How much students earn after earning a degree", "Which types of degrees or awards students earn" and "How many students work while attending college". (Find your story)
- "Filter by California school district or legislative district to learn about students in your community." (Find your story)
- Cohort: "Students who graduated from a California public high school in 2014-2015 with a standard diploma." (Who’s included in the diagram?)
- "“Never Enrolled in a California Public College” currently includes students who attended private colleges within California and out-of-state colleges, as well as those who choose not to go to college." (Who’s included in the diagram?)

## Evidence and method

State administrative records, linked across systems. The numbers are in interactive charts that did not render in the copy.

## Limits

- The copy has no figures: the charts need JavaScript. A person must read the figures in a browser, or a C2C download must be found.
- One cohort only; private and out-of-state colleges are not separated.
