# RMACC About us (web)

## Citation

Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium (RMACC). "About us." Web page, University of Colorado Boulder, date not stated. https://www.colorado.edu/partnerships/rmacc/about. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- RMACC is a collaboration of academic and research institutions in the intermountain states, housed at the University of Colorado Boulder. Its mission is the wide and effective use of high-performance computing.
- A board of directors leads it. The page lists 44 member institutions, among them universities, community colleges, Idaho National Lab, National Jewish Health and Denver Botanic Gardens.

## Background

This is the "About us" page of RMACC, on the University of Colorado Boulder website. It gives the mission in three points. It names the board of directors for 2025-2026 and for 2027-2028. It shows the member institutions in a table of logos.

## Key claims

- "The Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium is a collaboration among academic and research institutions located throughout the intermountain states." (About us)
- "Educating graduate and undergraduate students, faculty, researchers, and industry partners on the use of computational science and high-performance computing." (mission, point 1)
- "Coordinating multi-institutional efforts to advance research, practice, and education in computational science in order to address important regional problems." (mission, point 2)
- "Bringing together a broad range of researchers, faculty, and industry partners with a depth of experience and expertise not available at any single institution and facilitate their collaboration in multi-disciplinary and multi-institutional teams." (mission, point 3)
- "RMACC is a regional collaborative group housed at the University of Colorado Boulder." (About us)

## Evidence and method

An institutional web page. The board lists give a chair, a vice-chair, an executive director and board members, each with an institution. The 2027-2028 board adds an administrative officer and a student representative. The member table names, among others, Arizona State University, the University of Utah and several community colleges. It gives some members by short form only, such as NCAR, NOAA, USGS and NLR. The library counted 44 entries in the table.

## Limits

The page does not give a membership fee, a budget, bylaws or how RMACC chooses its board. It does not say whether RMACC owns or shares compute. [RMACC Member Resources (web)](RMACC%20Member%20Resources%20%28web%29.md) names one shared system, Alpine. The page has no date; the board lists give its period.
