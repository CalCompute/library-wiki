## Citation

Gavin Newsom, Governor of California, signing message for Senate Bill 53, to the Members of the California State Senate, Office of the Governor, September 29, 2025. <https://www.gov.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/SB-53-Signing-Message.pdf>. ## Bottom line

- The signing message does not mention CalCompute, the consortium or the Report.
- It frames SB 53 as frontier AI oversight that "builds on the work of" the Governor's working group report.
- It is useful only as context. It gives the Report nothing on CalCompute.

## Background

The Governor addressed this letter to the Members of the California State Senate on the day of signing.

## Key claims

- SB 53 "will establish state-level oversight of the use, assessment, and governance of advanced artificial intelligence (AI) systems." (paragraph 1)
- California is "home to many of the world's top AI researchers and developers". (paragraph 2)
- The working group report "provides us with a roadmap to regulate frontier AI prudently and proactively". (paragraph 3)
- If federal standards come, "subsequent action will be necessary to provide alignment between policy frameworks". (paragraph 4)
