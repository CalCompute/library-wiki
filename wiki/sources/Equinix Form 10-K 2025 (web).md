# Equinix Form 10-K 2025 (web)

## Citation

Equinix, Inc. Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025. Filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), EDGAR, Feb. 11, 2026. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1101239/000110123926000032/eqix-20251231.htm. ## Bottom line

- Equinix has 280 data centers in 77 markets. Its offices are in Redwood City, California, and it has data centers in the Los Angeles and Silicon Valley metros. It builds a new Silicon Valley data center, SV18, in two phases for $440 million.
- The filing says that power, not space, limits many of its data centers, and that AI workloads increase the demand for high-density power.

## Background

Equinix sells colocation space, interconnection and managed infrastructure in its International Business Exchange (IBX) data centers. Its xScale data centers, in joint ventures, serve hyperscale cloud companies. A Form 10-K is the annual report that a public company must file with the SEC. This filing covers the year to Dec. 31, 2025. It gives the data centers by region, the projects under construction, the risk factors and the audited financial statements.

## Key claims

- "Our extensive global footprint spans 280 data centers, in 77 markets in 36 countries." (Item 1, Overview)
- "AI adoption is increasing the need for distributed, interconnected digital infrastructure. Training, inference, and model coordination require dense data exchange across cloud and edge environments." (Item 1, Overview)
- Infrastructure offerings "are typically billed based on the space and power a customer consumes in our IBX data centers, are delivered under a fixed duration contract and generate monthly recurring revenue" (Item 1, products)
- "Our executive offices are located in Redwood City, California" (Item 2, Properties). The table of metros marks Los Angeles and Silicon Valley as locations with leased and owned data centers.
- Americas IBX data centers: 109, with a capacity of 157,400 cabinets, 123,700 cabinets billed, 79 percent utilization, and $2,694 monthly recurring revenue (MRR) per cabinet. (Item 2, IBX portfolio table)
- "SV18 phase 1", Silicon Valley, target open date "Q2 2026", 2,100 sellable cabinets, $260 million total capital expenditure (capex). "SV18 phase 2", "Q2 2027", 850 cabinets, $180 million. (Item 2, projects under construction table)
- "As power, not space, is a limiting factor in many of our IBX data centers, our ability to fully utilize the space in those IBX data centers may be impacted." (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- "the workloads related to new and evolving technologies such as AI are increasing the demand for high density computing power." (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- "Limitations on generation, transmission and distribution may limit our ability to obtain sufficient power capacity for potential expansion sites in new or existing markets." (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- "In certain cases, we must commit to power purchases before an IBX center is fully operational, increasing fixed costs and the risk that these costs cannot be passed on to customers." (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- "We expect that we will continue to experience limited availability of water and power and grid constraints in many markets as well as shortages of associated equipment because of the current high demands and finite nature of these resources." (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- "We attempt to limit our exposure to system downtime by using backup generators, which are in turn supported by onsite fuel storage and through contracts with fuel suppliers" (Item 1A, Risk Factors)
- Contracts are "generally one to five years in length, and thereafter automatically renews in one-year increments." (Item 7, Revenues)
- Americas cost of revenues has "$23 million of higher utilities costs, driven by both increases in power costs and higher utility usage." (Item 7, results of operations)
- Total revenues for 2025 are $9,217 million: $4,111 million in the Americas, of which colocation is $2,683 million. "Total revenues attributed to the U.S. were $3.6 billion." (Note 18, Segment Information)

## Evidence and method

A legal filing that the company makes under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. The portfolio tables give counts, cabinets, utilization and revenue per cabinet at Dec. 31, 2025. The financial statements have an audit. The risk factors are the company's own statement of risks, not measured data.

## Limits

- The filing gives no count of data centers, cabinets or megawatts for each California metro. The IBX table gives totals for the Americas only.
- It gives the capex and cabinets of SV18, but not its power capacity or address.
- It does not give GPU or computer counts. Customers own the equipment in the space.
- The MRR per cabinet is an average for the Americas, not a California price.
