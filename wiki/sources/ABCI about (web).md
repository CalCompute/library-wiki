# ABCI about (web)

## Citation

National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST). "About ABCI." AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure (ABCI) website. No date on the page. https://abci.ai/en/about_abci/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure (ABCI) is an open AI computing infrastructure in Japan. AIST built it, and AIST Solutions Co. (AISol) operates it.
- ABCI started in August 2018, and moved to ABCI 3.0 in January 2025.
- From fiscal year 2024, ABCI gives compute mainly to domestic industry, academia and government in Japan.

## Background

ABCI is at the AIST Kashiwa Center on the Kashiwa II Campus of the University of Tokyo. The Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) funded it through a series of budget amendments. This page is the "About ABCI" page of the ABCI website. The site footer gives the copyright of AIST and AISol.

## Key claims

- What it is: ABCI "is an open computing infrastructure for both developing AI technology and bridging AI technology into the industry and the real world, constructed by National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST), and operated by AIST Solutions Co. (AISol)." (About ABCI)
- History: "ABCI started full-scale operation in August 2018, was upgraded to ABCI 2.0 in May 2021, and was upgraded to ABCI 3.0 in January 2025." (About ABCI)
- Funding: the system came "As part of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry’s “Global Research Center Development Project on Artificial Intelligence” (H28 Secondary Amendment)". (Largest in Japan, Super Energy Saving, Open AI Infrastructure)
- Purpose: "As an open innovation platform to accelerate AI research and development by industry, academia, and government in Japan, ABCI aims to research, develop, and demonstrate AI technologies". (Largest in Japan, Super Energy Saving, Open AI Infrastructure)
- Users: "From fiscal year 2024 onwards, in order to strengthen research and development capabilities in cutting-edge AI technologies, including generative AI models, ABCI will provide computational resources primarily for public use by domestic industry, academia, and government, particularly national research institutes and universities, startups, etc." (Largest in Japan, Super Energy Saving, Open AI Infrastructure)
- Hardware choice: "We design our products to be easy for private cloud providers to emulate and cost-effective for users." (Adoption of de facto standard architecture)
- Cooling: "The combination of hot water cooling and air cooling enables 70 kW of cooling per rack while drastically reducing the power required for cooling." (Designs ultra-high-density, ultra-power-efficient data centers from scratch)
- Access: "The ABCI can be accessed through a web browser." Users can use Open OnDemand and Jupyter Lab. (Leveraging the Software Ecosystem)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional web page about one national system. It gives the history, the funding programs by name, the user groups and the design features. It gives no budget figures and no user counts.
