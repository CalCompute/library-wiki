## Background

EPA publishes facility records in the FRS. Its public web service returns one record in JSON for one registry ID. The record is a work of the U.S. Government, so the library rehosts it. The library uses this record for the place of SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory.

## Evidence and method

A federal registry record with 11 fields: registry ID, facility name, address, supplemental location, city, county, state, ZIP code, FIPS code, latitude and longitude. The latitude and longitude are decimal degrees. The field names end in "83".

## Limits

- The record gives one point for the facility. It does not give the edge of the site.
- The record does not say how EPA got the point, and it does not describe the work of the laboratory.
- The FRS has other records under the same laboratory name or address, with other points. Other records at the same address name a former substation, cleanup sites and wireless sites.
