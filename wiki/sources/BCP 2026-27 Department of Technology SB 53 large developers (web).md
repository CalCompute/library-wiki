# BCP 2026-27 Department of Technology SB 53 large developers (web)

## Bottom line

- CDT asks for one ongoing Information Technology Manager II and $360,000 one time for consultants in 2026-27, to carry out SB 53.
- The total is $604,000 in 2026-27 and $244,000 each year after.
- The duties are annual reviews of the "frontier model" definitions, risk assessments, stakeholder consultations and an annual report to the Legislature.
- The proposal also names a "supportive role in CalCompute", which it lists as a contingent deliverable.

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. CDT's Office of Digital Services (ODS) would hold the position, a vacant position that CDT will reclassify. The proposal names two attachments that the copy does not include: an organization chart and a workload justification. The California Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) has its own SB 53 proposal: [BCP 2026-27 Cal OES SB 53 large developers (web)](BCP%202026-27%20Cal%20OES%20SB%2053%20large%20developers%20%28web%29.md). The Department of Justice has one too: [BCP 2026-27 Department of Justice SB 53 frontier AI act (web)](BCP%202026-27%20Department%20of%20Justice%20SB%2053%20frontier%20AI%20act%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The California Department of Technology (CDT) requests General Fund (GF) budget authority (ongoing) for 1.0 Information Technology Manager II (ITM II) and $360,000 (one-time) for temp help/consultants in Fiscal Year (FY) 2026-27 to implement Chapter 138, Statutes of 2025 (SB 53)." (Budget Request Summary)
- "First, this bill requires CDT to annually review and, if necessary, recommend updates to statutory definitions for “frontier model,” “frontier developer,” and “large frontier developer.”" (A. Problem Statement)
- "Finally, SB 53 references CDT’s role in supporting the Office of Government Operations Agency (GovOps) in the development of CalCompute, a statewide public cloud computing cluster." (A. Problem Statement)
- "CDT will contribute governance and technical expertise in coordination with its existing cloud services team." (A. Problem Statement)
- "The mandates create two distinct cost drivers: 1. Statutory obligations that CDT must fulfill, including annual definition reviews, technical and risk assessments, stakeholder consultations, and legislative reporting. 2. CDT’s supportive role in CalCompute." (B. Justification)
- "These resources will provide expertise in frontier model auditing, adversarial testing, risk modeling, and international regulatory analysis." (B. Justification, Consulting Resources)
- "Additionally, CDT would not be prepared to provide technical or governance support for CalCompute, weakening statewide coordination on AI infrastructure." (B. Justification, Consequences of Not Funding)
- "Category B – Contingent Deliverables: • CDT technical and governance support to GovOps for CalCompute." (D. Outcomes and Accountability)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form, with a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. The fund summary gives $682,000 General Fund in 2026-27 and $322,000 each year after. A reduction of $78,000 each year to the Technology Services Revolving Fund brings the totals to $604,000 and $244,000. The projected outcomes table gives one definition review, one legislative report and four stakeholder forums each year from 2026-27. It gives "CalCompute deliverables supported" as one each year from 2027-28.

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- The 2026-27 floor report lists one ongoing Information Technology Manager II and $360,000 one time for SB 53 at CDT in the budget agreement. It does not name CalCompute ([Assembly Budget floor report 2026-27 budget 2026-06-27 (web)](Assembly%20Budget%20floor%20report%202026-27%20budget%202026-06-27%20%28web%29.md), p. 112).
- It does not say what the CalCompute support is, beyond governance and technical expertise.
- It cites SB 53 for CDT's duties. Government Code section 11546.8, the CalCompute section in this library, does not name CDT ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)). The library holds no copy of the other SB 53 sections.
- It gives the first legislative report "by June 2027" and also says the duties begin "January 1, 2027". It does not give the statutory due date of the report.
- The two attachments are not in the copy.
