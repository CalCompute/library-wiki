# SF State HPC overview (web)

## Citation

San Francisco State University (SF State), Academic Technology. "High-Performance Computing (HPC) Overview." Academic Technology Help Center, no date. https://athelp.sfsu.edu/hc/en-us/articles/31635404607763-High-Performance-Computing-HPC-Overview. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-20 SF State HPC overview.md`.

## Bottom line

- SF State's Academic Technology runs a central HPC cluster for faculty and students. It has 14 compute nodes on AMD EPYC processors, three of them GPU nodes with four NVIDIA A100 (80GB) GPUs each.
- Access is by a request through ServiceNow or email. Students need a faculty sponsor.
- The cluster does not take HIPAA, FERPA or other protected data, and it has no backups.

## Background

This is a help article of Academic Technology at SF State, a California State University (CSU) campus. It gives the cluster specifications by partition, the software, how to get access and the usage policies. The usage policies call the cluster "Polaris".

## Key claims

The cluster (opening paragraph and "Cluster Specifications"):

- "Academic Technology offers faculty and students access to a centrally managed High-Performance Computing (HPC) cluster designed to support large-scale workflows that benefit from enhanced computational power."
- The table gives the gpucluster partition as "2" nodes and the gpuquick partition as "1" node, each with "4× NVIDIA A100 (80GB)" per node.
- "Network: InfiniBand (HDR/Ethernet 200 Gb/s)."

Access ("How to Get Started"):

- "If you are interested in accessing HPC for your research, submit a request via ServiceNOW or email at@sfsu.edu. This will usually lead to a brief introductory meeting with our systems team to discuss your research needs."
- "Note: All students require faculty sponsorship. Faculty must submit a request on the student's behalf for access to be granted."

Usage policies ("Usage Policies"):

- "The Polaris cluster does not support the storage or processing of HIPAA, FERPA, or PII protected data."
- "If your project requires more than 1 TB of storage, please contact us to discuss your storage needs."
- "Data stored on the cluster is not backed up."
