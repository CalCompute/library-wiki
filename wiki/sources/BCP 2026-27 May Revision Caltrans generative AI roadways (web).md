# BCP 2026-27 May Revision Caltrans generative AI roadways (web)

## Bottom line

- Caltrans asks for $16,428,000 one time in 2026-27, from the State Highway Account, for two generative AI (GenAI) tools and a Data and AI Office.
- The tools are Vulnerable Road User (VRU) safety and Traffic Mobility Insights (TMI). They run on Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud environments that the California Department of Technology (CDT) maintains.
- The request funds seven positions, consultants, and CDT and information technology support.

## Background

A Budget Change Proposal (BCP) is a department's request for a change to its budget, on the Department of Finance form DF-46. Caltrans led two GenAI proofs of concept (POC) under Executive Order N-12-23. It awarded the POC contracts with CDT and the Department of General Services in May 2024, and two implementation contracts in April 2025. Caltrans first paid for the implementation from internal funds. Accenture LLP and Deloitte LLP give technical support. The proposal also names SB 896 and AB 2013 ([SB 896 Generative AI Accountability Act bill text](SB%20896%20Generative%20AI%20Accountability%20Act%20bill%20text.md); [AB 2013 chaptered text Chapter 817 of 2024](AB%202013%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20817%20of%202024.md)).

## Key claims

- "The California Department of Transportation requests a one-year increase of $16,428,000 in Fiscal Year 2026-27 to advance Generative Artificial Intelligence for safer and smarter roadways." (Budget Request Summary)
- "Caltrans also redirected funds to CDT for hosting and delivering advanced GenAI services on a cloud-based platform." (B. Justification)
- "This includes professional services from Accenture LLP and Deloitte LLP, who provide critical technical support and updates, and CDT for cloud infrastructure costs to maintain Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud (GCP) environments." (B. Justification)
- "The requested positions include one position as Senior Transportation Electrical Engineer in the Division of Traffic Operations" (B. Justification). Six more positions, "four IT Specialist IIs and two IT Specialist IIIs", are in Caltrans IT (B. Justification).
- "The Project Manager and Business Analyst will focus on monitoring that CDT’s Project Delivery Lifecycle (PDL) requirements are fully met for the VRU and TMI projects, which did not previously undergo the Project Approval Lifecycle (PAL) process." (B. Justification)
- "Workforce training will pledge that at least 100 planners and engineers across all 12 districts are upskilled using the GenAI solutions for safety and mobility planning." (D. Outcomes and Accountability)

## Evidence and method

A state budget request on the DF-46 form, with a funding table, a resource history and a fiscal detail sheet in thousands of dollars. The funding table gives:

- salaries and wages: $941,000;
- Data and AI Office consulting services: $3,381,696;
- VRU consulting services: $2,509,841;
- TMI consulting services: $4,446,691;
- CDT and IT program support: $4,540,772.

The cover sheet gives total project costs of $8,908,090 (VRU) and $8,332,589 (TMI), with project approval on January 28, 2026.

## Limits

- The proposal does not say whether the Legislature approved the request. It is not the enacted budget.
- The 2026-27 floor report lists "a one-time increase of $7.2 million State Highway Account" for the two tools, not $16,428,000. It does not say why the amounts differ ([Assembly Budget floor report 2026-27 budget 2026-06-27 (web)](Assembly%20Budget%20floor%20report%202026-27%20budget%202026-06-27%20%28web%29.md), p. 83).
- It does not split the $4,540,772 of CDT and IT program support between cloud hosting and other costs.
- It refers to a "VRU & TMI GenAI Solutions Funding table". The copy does not include that table.
- It asks for one year only and does not say how Caltrans pays for the tools after 2026-27.
- It says nothing about CalCompute.
