# Energy Commission backup generating project Vernon (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "Vernon Backup Generating Facility (VBGF)." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/vernon-backup-generating-facility-vbgf. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Vernon.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20Vernon.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project Vernon.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 99 megawatts (MW) for a data center of two buildings in Vernon. Its status is "Under Review".
- Vernon Public Utility (VPU) supplies the data center through a new switching station. The 40 diesel generators run when VPU power is unavailable.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. GIC Vernon LLC is the project owner. The docket number is 25-SPPE-01, the capacity is 99 MW and the location is Vernon, California. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The Vernon Backup Generating Facility (VBGF) would consist of a total of forty (40) diesel fired generators that would be used exclusively to provide up to 99 megawatts (MW) of backup emergency generation to support the Goodman Energy Park Data Center (GEP) when electricity from Vernon Public Utility (VPU) is unavailable to the GEP." (Project Description)
- "The Building 1 address would be 3163 East Vernon Avenue and the Building 2 address would be 3049 East Vernon Avenue." (Project Description)
- "The GEP would be supplied electricity by VPU through a new switching station on the GEP site that would be owned and operated by VPU (VPU Switching Station)." (Project Description)
- "Thirty-eight (38) of the generators would each have a capacity of 3 MW and would provide backup of the electricity needs of the GEP’s critical operations." (Project Description)
- "The CEC does not permit data centers but does have jurisdiction over certain energy infrastructure associated with them under its exclusive permitting authority to certify all thermal generating facilities 50 megawatts (MW) and larger in California." (Data Centers)
- "The Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants with facilities between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Limits

- The page gives no key dates and no size of the data center buildings.
- It gives no normal electrical demand for the data center.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
