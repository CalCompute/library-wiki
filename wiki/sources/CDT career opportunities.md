## Bottom line

- CDT is "the recognized central Information Technology (IT) organization for the State of California". It hires through the state process: an exam "to get a job in a specific classification", then a CalCareer application.
- It links the "IT Classification Mapping" document. See [CDT IT Classification Mapping](CDT%20IT%20Classification%20Mapping.md).

## Background

See [California Department of Technology](../entities/California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md).

## Key claims

- CDT seeks people "ready to build a career in supporting the operations of some of the largest and most sophisticated computer systems in the state."
- "Find available exams to pass in order to get a job in a specific classification."
- "Create your CalCareer account with California State Jobs, and find a job opening at CDT."
- Student assistant jobs give "hands-on experience in your field of study."
