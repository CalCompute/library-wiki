## Bottom line

- AB 489 adds Chapter 15.5, "Health Advice From Artificial Intelligence", to Division 2 of the Business and Professions Code, from section 4999.8.
- An artificial intelligence (AI) system must not use a term that implies that a licensed person gives its care or advice.
- The laws against a false claim of a health care license apply to a person or entity that develops or deploys such a system.
- The health care licensing boards enforce the chapter. Each use of a prohibited term is a separate violation.

## Background

The bill's author is Assembly Member Bonta, as the Digest prints the name. The Governor approved it on Oct. 11, 2025. The text holds the Legislative Counsel's Digest and the new chapter in two sections: definitions and enforcement. The library's copy comes from the Legislature's website through the Wayback Machine.

## Key claims

- "The use of a term, letter, or phrase in the advertising or functionality of an AI or GenAI system, program, device, or similar technology that indicates or implies that the care, advice, reports, or assessments being offered through the AI or GenAI technology is being provided by a natural person in possession of the appropriate license or certificate to practice as a health care professional, is prohibited." (Bus. & Prof. Code § 4999.9(c))
- "A violation of this chapter is subject to the jurisdiction of the appropriate health care professional licensing board or enforcement agency." (§ 4999.9(a)(1))
- "The appropriate health care professional licensing board may pursue an injunction or restraining order to enforce the provisions of this chapter, as authorized by Section 125.5." (§ 4999.9(a)(2))
- "Each use of a prohibited term, letter, or phrase shall constitute a separate violation of this chapter." (§ 4999.9(d))
- "“Health care profession” means any profession that is the subject of licensure or regulation under this division or under any initiative act referred to in this division." (§ 4999.8(c))

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate, no operative date and no penalty amount. It takes its definitions of AI and generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) from Government Code sections 11546.45.5 and 11549.64, and does not print them. The first definition is in [AB 2885 chaptered text Chapter 843 of 2024](AB%202885%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20843%20of%202024.md). The capture is of Aug. 15, 2026, so a later change does not show.

The bill does not mention research compute or CalCompute.
