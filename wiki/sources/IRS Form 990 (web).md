# IRS Form 990 (web)

## Citation

Department of the Treasury, Internal Revenue Service (IRS). Form 990, "Return of Organization Exempt From Income Tax," for the 2025 calendar year. OMB No. 1545-0047, Cat. No. 11282Y. Form created 4/30/25. https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/f990.pdf. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 IRS Form 990.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20IRS%20Form%20990.md) (original: `.pdf` beside it). Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Form 990 is the yearly return of an organization exempt from income tax under section 501(c), 527 or 4947(a)(1), except private foundations. It is "Open to Public Inspection".
- It asks for revenue, expenses, program results, governance, policies and pay of officers and key employees. Part VI asks about the governing body, conflict of interest, whistleblower and document retention policies, and public disclosure.

## Background

The IRS publishes Form 990 each year. This copy is the form for tax year 2025. It has 12 parts, from Part I Summary to Part XII Financial Statements and Reporting, and a checklist of schedules in Part IV. The IRS publishes filed returns as data; see [IRS TEOS bulk data downloads (web)](IRS%20TEOS%20bulk%20data%20downloads%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Under section 501(c), 527, or 4947(a)(1) of the Internal Revenue Code (except private foundations)" (header, p. 1)
- "Do not enter social security numbers on this form as it may be made public." (header, p. 1)
- "Enter the number of voting members of the governing body at the end of the tax year." and "Enter the number of voting members included on line 1a, above, who are independent" (Part VI, Section A, lines 1a and 1b, p. 6)
- "Did the organization have members, stockholders, or other persons who had the power to elect or appoint one or more members of the governing body?" (Part VI, line 7a, p. 6)
- "Section B. Policies (This Section B requests information about policies not required by the Internal Revenue Code.)" (Part VI, p. 6)
- "Section 6104 requires an organization to make its Forms 1023 (1024 or 1024-A, if applicable), 990, and 990-T (section 501(c) (3)s only) available for public inspection." (Part VI, line 18, p. 6)
- "Section 501(c)(3) and 501(c)(4) organizations must complete all columns." The columns are "Total expenses", "Program service expenses", "Management and general expenses" and "Fundraising expenses". (Part IX, Statement of Functional Expenses, p. 10)

## Evidence and method

A blank federal tax form. It shows what a tax-exempt organization must report each year. It holds no data on any organization.

## Limits

The library read the form, not its separate instructions. The form does not say which organizations must file Form 990 instead of Form 990-EZ or 990-N; the instructions do. The schedules are separate documents that the library has not read.
