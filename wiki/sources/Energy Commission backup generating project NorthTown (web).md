# Energy Commission backup generating project NorthTown (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "NorthTown Backup Generating Facility." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/northtown-backup-generating-facility. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project NorthTown.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20NorthTown.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project NorthTown.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a backup generating facility of up to 97.3 megawatts (MW) for the NorthTown Data Center in San José. Its status is "Under Review".
- The facility has 42 diesel generators on a site of about 28.5 acres, with a new 115-34.5 kilovolt (kV) project substation.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. GIC San Jose, LLC is the project owner. The docket number is 25-SPPE-02, the capacity is 97.3 MW and the location is San José, California. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The NorthTown Backup Generating Facility (NTBGF) would consist of a total of forty two (42) diesel- fired generators that would be used exclusively to provide up to 97.3 megawatts (MW) of backup emergency generation to support NorthTown Data Center (NTDC) to provide uninterrupted power for its tenant’s servers." (Project Description)
- "The proposed project site is at the southwest corner of the intersection of West Trimble Road and Orchard Parkway in San José, California." (Project Description)
- "The entire project site is approximately 28.5 acres." (Project Description)
- "Of those 40 generators rated 3 MW, eight would be redundant." (Project Description)
- "The CEC does not permit data centers but does have jurisdiction over certain energy infrastructure associated with them under its exclusive permitting authority to certify all thermal generating facilities 50 megawatts (MW) and larger in California." (Data Centers)
- "The Small Power Plant Exemption (SPPE) process allows applicants with facilities between 50 and 100 MW to obtain an exemption from CEC’s jurisdiction and proceed with local permitting rather than requiring CEC certification." (Small Power Plant Exemption)

## Evidence and method

A project page from a state agency's website. It gives the docket's fields, a project description and, for some projects, key dates with links to docket documents. This library does not hold the docket documents.

## Limits

- The page gives no key dates and no size of the data center buildings.
- It names PG&E for a switching station, but it does not give the data center's normal electrical demand.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
