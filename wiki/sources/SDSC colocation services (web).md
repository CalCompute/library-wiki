# SDSC colocation services (web)

## Citation

San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), UC San Diego. "Colocation." Services web page, no date. https://www.sdsc.edu/services/colocation.html. Read 2026-11-11. Cached copy: `.cache/web/2026-11-11 SDSC colocation services.md`.

## Bottom line

- SDSC offers rack space in its data center to researchers who buy their own equipment. The data center has 19,000 square feet and 13 megawatts of power.
- UC researchers get the service through their campus representatives. The page gives monthly recharge rates for UC San Diego and other UC users. Non-UC rates are "Available upon request."

## Background

SDSC is a center of UC San Diego. The page says that SDSC designed the service with several UC campuses and the UC Office of the President. Its terms are in [SDSC colocation terms of service 2025 (web)](SDSC%20colocation%20terms%20of%20service%202025%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

The facility (top of page):

- "For researchers who choose to purchase their own IT equipment, SDSC offers rack colocation services."
- "Our 19,000-square foot climate-controlled and secure datacenter is fully equipped with 13 Megawatts of power, multi- 10-gigabit network connectivity, and a 24/7 operations staff."
- "The service was designed in partnership with representatives from several UC campuses and the UC Office of the President, and is available to UC researchers through their campus representatives."

Standard services (section "Standard colocation includes the following services"):

- "10+ Gb/s networking fabric connectivity both throughout SDSC aggregation fabric and into CENIC."

Rates (section "Colocation Rates"):

- "Colocation services at SDSC are designed to be cost-effective, leveraging historical UC investments and economies-of-scale to provide services at significantly lower rates than building/retrofitting a campus datacenter facility."
- Rack space each month: "$44.76" at the UCSD rate and "$572.60" at the UC rate.
- "Utilities will be charged at the actual rate of consumption. Utilities charges for UC San Diego customers are paid for by campus."
- "Recharge rates are subject to change with approval from the UC San Diego Recharge Rate Committee. Normal IDC rules apply."

## Evidence and method

This is a service page with a rate table. The cost claim against a campus data center has no figures to support it on the page.

## Limits

- The page does not say how much rack space is free or in use.
- It does not give non-UC rates.
- It does not say how a researcher outside UC applies.
