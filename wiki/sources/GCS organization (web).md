# GCS organization (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing e.V. (GCS). "GCS Organization." Web page, date not stated. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us/organization. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- GCS is a non-profit registered association (e.V.). It has five bodies: a Steering Committee of the funders, a Scientific Steering Committee, a Council, a Board of Directors and a management.
- The funders set the rules for use and the allocation process. Scientists award computing time by peer review. The Council decides all matters of the association.

## Background

GCS joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)). This page describes each GCS body in one paragraph, with an organisation chart. Separate pages list the members of the [GCS Steering Committee (web)](GCS%20Steering%20Committee%20%28web%29.md), the [GCS Scientific Steering Committee (web)](GCS%20Scientific%20Steering%20Committee%20%28web%29.md) and the [GCS Council (web)](GCS%20Council%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "GCS operates as a registered association (e.V.). The non-profit organization is led by a board of directors, a steering committee (funding bodies) and a scientific steering committee." (GCS Organization)
- "The committee is responsible for forming and guiding the GCS strategy as well as setting the rules and conditions for using GCS HPC resources, setting the process for reviewing and awarding computing time, and reviewing and approving allocations recommended by the scientific steering committee." (Steering Committee)
- "The GCS Scientific Steering Committee advises the GCS Council instrategic and scientific decisions. It awards computing time grants on the GCS HPC systems to scientifically excellent research projects." (Scientific Steering Committee)
- "The Scientific Steering Committee consists of three representatives (chair, vice chair, head of operations) from the current institutions on the GCS Council." (Scientific Steering Committee)
- "The GCS Council is the deliberative body of GCS and decides on all matters of the association. It is composed of institutional and private members." (GCS Council)
- "The GCS Board of Directors is elected by the GCS Council for a period of two years. It consists of a Chair and two Vice-Chairs. The current Chairman of the Board is Dieter Kranzlmüller (LRZ), the vice-chairs are Michael Resch (HLRS) and Thomas Lippert (JSC)." (GCS Board of Directors)
- "GCS management is responsible for all operations of the association and running the organization’s project management office, which is responsible for all projects supported by GCS as well as inter-European HPC initiatives." (GCS Management)

## Evidence and method

An institutional page that describes the structure. It names the members of the Council by institution: the Bavarian Academy of Sciences, Forschungszentrum Jülich and the University of Stuttgart. It names the private members as "representatives of the three GCS HPC centres". It names the Managing Director, Claus Axel Müller. It gives no figures.

## Limits

- The page has no date.
- It gives no budget, no staff numbers and no voting rules.
- It does not say how the funders' Steering Committee and the Council share decisions.
- The organisation chart is an image. The library has not read it.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: This page names Dieter Kranzlmüller Chairman of the Board, with Michael Resch as a vice-chair. [GCS Scientific Steering Committee (web)](GCS%20Scientific%20Steering%20Committee%20%28web%29.md) lists Resch as "Chairman of the Board of GCS".
