# Tech Talent Project about (web)

## Citation

Tech Talent Project. "About Tech Talent Project." techtalentproject.org, date not stated. https://techtalentproject.org/about-us/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Tech Talent Project says that tech leaders from Republican and Democratic administrations founded it in 2017. Its focus is "making government services work better through modern tech leadership".
- It names a network of "10,000+ Technology Professionals" and three partners: the Beeck Center at Georgetown University, New America and the American Enterprise Institute.

## Background

The page is the "About" page of Tech Talent Project. It gives the mission, theory of change, approach and network. The home page is [Tech Talent Project homepage (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20homepage%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Founded in 2017 by tech leaders from both Republican and Democratic administrations, we focus on one thing: making government services work better through modern tech leadership." (Our mission)
- "Great tech leaders in government change what's possible." (Our theory of change)
- "We look for inflection points where technology leadership can have outsized impact, then move quickly to seize those opportunities." (Our theory of change)
- "10,000+ Technology Professionals": "Experienced leaders from both private sector and government, vetted for technical excellence and commitment to public service." (Our network)
- "We collaborate with leading organizations including Beeck Center for Social Impact + Innovation at Georgetown University, New America's Digital Impact and Governance Initiative, and American Enterprise Institute." (Strategic Partnerships)

## Evidence and method

An organization's self-description. It gives one total for its network, with no method.

## Limits

The page does not give the organization's funding, staff or governance. It does not say how it vets the professionals in its network.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this page gives a network of "10,000+ Technology Professionals". [Tech Talent Project state transitions (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20state%20transitions%20%28web%29.md) gives "an 11,000-plus technologist network", and [Tech Talent Project incoming governors guide 2026-11-16 (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20incoming%20governors%20guide%202026-11-16%20%28web%29.md) gives "a network of more than 11,000 technologists".
