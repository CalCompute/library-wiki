## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the State of California and the American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees (AFSCME) for State Bargaining Unit 19. Its term is July 1, 2025 to June 30, 2028.
- The State "must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain Bargaining Unit 19 employees before resorting to the use of private contractors". A joint committee recommends contracts to cancel or reduce "as a budget solution", and explores an "In-House" Registry of unit employees.
- Departments "shall establish a telework program". Side Letter 8 suspended the return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 for Unit 19 until July 1, 2026. The MOU does not name artificial intelligence.

## Background

CalHR publishes the official text of each state memorandum of understanding (MOU) on its eServices site. The State recognizes AFSCME as the exclusive representative of Unit 19 (Article 1.1). The Ralph C. Dills Act governs the agreement.

## Key claims

- "The term of the Agreement shall be July 1, 2025, and remain in full force through June 30, 2028." (Article 20, 20.1.1)
- The rights of the State include the right "to determine the methods, means and personnel by which State operations are to be conducted". (Article 3, 3.1.2)
- "Where the State finds it necessary to make changes, the State shall notify AFSCME of the proposed change 30 days prior to its proposed implementation" (Article 19, 19.1.2)
- "Except in extremely unusual or urgent, time-limited circumstances, or under other circumstances where contracting out is recognized or required by law, Federal mandate, or court decisions/orders, the State must make every effort to hire, utilize and retain Bargaining Unit 19 employees before resorting to the use of private contractors." (Article 18, 18.1.2)
- "The Committee will also make recommendations regarding their findings with respect to which contracts may be cancelled or reduced by the State as a budget solution." (Article 19, 19.7.3)
- "The Committee will also explore the creation of an “In-House” Registry of Bargaining Unit 19 employees who would enjoy priority over outside contractors in performing additional work opportunities offered by the State." (Article 19, 19.7.4)
- "Departments shall establish a telework program. If the telework arrangement conforms to telework criteria established in the telework policy and guidelines, no employee’s request for telework shall be unreasonably denied." (Article 17, 17.4.2)
- "The State shall encourage such professional education/training by authorizing up to five (5) days for a total of 40 hours per fiscal year without loss of compensation for professional education and training." (Article 14, 14.1.3)
- "Effective upon the parties' execution of this Side Letter, for employees in Bargaining Unit 19, the State and the Union agree that the State's return-to-office requirements of Executive Order N-22-25 will be suspended immediately and reinstituted on July 1, 2026." (Side Letter 8)

## Evidence and method

The official text of a memorandum of understanding between the State and a union, as CalHR serves it. It gives rules, salaries, leaves, a contracting out committee, side letters and a grievance procedure.

## Limits

- The MOU has no article on artificial intelligence. Technological change appears only in the Government Code section 19995.2 entry of its list of superseded and incorporated sections.
- Side Letter 8 "is operative through June 30, 2026". The library holds no later document on the return-to-office requirements for Unit 19.
- The CalHR page gives no date of publication or of the last change.
