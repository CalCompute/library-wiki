# CAIT Frequently Asked Questions (web)

## Citation

California Policy Lab (CPL). "Frequently Asked Questions about the California AI-Unemployment Tracker." PDF, 3 pages, June 2026 (by its upload path). https://capolicylab.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/CAIT-Frequently-Asked-Questions.pdf. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The California AI-Unemployment Tracker (CAIT) links state unemployment insurance (UI) claims to measures of AI exposure by occupation. It is "an early warning system".
- It does not capture all AI job loss, and it cannot say whether AI caused one layoff.

## Background

CPL and the Employment Development Department (EDD) made the tracker. This FAQ answers 11 questions on what the tracker measures, how it defines AI exposure and how to read it. It sends readers to the full report and technical appendix for results and method.

## Key claims

- "It is designed to serve as an early warning system for emerging labor market changes associated with AI." (Q1)
- "Many workers who lose jobs never file for UI benefits, so will not appear in UI data." (Q3)
- "More broadly, no dataset currently exists that can definitively identify whether a particular job loss was caused by AI." (Q4)
- "The observed use measure reflects how frequently AI is currently being used in practice by users of Anthropic’s Claude tool." (Q6)
- "Occupations in the top 25 percent of AI exposure scores are classified as high exposure, occupations in the middle 50 percent are classified as moderate-exposure, and occupations in the bottom 25 percent are classified as low exposure." (Q7)

## Evidence and method

An explanatory note with no data. It names two exposure measures (Q6). "A team of OpenAI and academic researchers" made the potential measure in 2024. The observed measure is from the 2025 Anthropic Economic Index. It says the authors checked the tracker against large layoffs that employers publicly put down to AI, with WARN notices and UI claims (Q10).

## Limits

The FAQ gives no results. It does not name the layoffs used to check the tracker. It does not mention compute or CalCompute.
