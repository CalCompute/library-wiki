# Internet Archive about (web)

## Citation

Internet Archive. "About." Internet Archive Blogs, blog.archive.org. Date not stated. https://blog.archive.org/about/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Internet Archive is a non-profit research library, founded in 1996. It keeps "more than 1 trillion web pages via the Wayback Machine". It also keeps millions of books, recordings, videos, software programs and images.
- It is a Federal Depository Library and gives free access to its collections.

## Background

The page is the "About" page of the Internet Archive blog. Its text is one paragraph. The rest of the page holds reader comments from 2007 onward, which are not part of the source.

## Key claims

- "Internet Archive is a non-profit research library founded in 1996 with a mission of providing “Universal Access to All Knowledge.”" (About)
- "Internet Archive works with libraries, publishers, and other partners to preserve and provide access to more than 1 trillion web pages via the Wayback Machine, plus millions of books, audio recordings, videos, software programs, and images." (About)
- "As a Federal Depository Library, Internet Archive provides free access to collections for researchers, historians, scholars, people with print disabilities, and the general public." (About)

## Evidence and method

A one-paragraph self-description of about 90 words. It gives the founding year, the mission and the size of the web collection. It gives no other evidence.

## Limits

The page does not give the Archive's budget, funders, staff or governance. It does not say how it pays for storage or computing. The reader comments below the text are not statements of the Archive.
