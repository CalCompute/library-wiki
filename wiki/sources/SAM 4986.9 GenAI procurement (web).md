# SAM 4986.9 GenAI procurement (web)

## Citation

California Department of General Services (DGS). State Administrative Manual (SAM) section 4986.9, "GenAI Procurement." Revised November 2025. https://www.dgs.ca.gov/Resources/SAM/TOC/4900/4986-9. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-19 SAM 4986.9 GenAI procurement.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-19%20SAM%204986.9%20GenAI%20procurement.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- SAM 4986.9 is the state's policy for how state entities buy generative artificial intelligence (GenAI). It applies to all state entities, unless a statute or policy exempts them.
- Before an award, an entity completes the SIMM 5305-F GenAI risk assessment and sends it to the California Department of Technology (CDT). A moderate or high risk needs a CDT consultation.
- Every IT and telecommunications solicitation and contract must carry a fixed GenAI disclosure clause. For moderate and high-risk GenAI, the entity checks vendors against SAM 5300, the NIST AI Risk Management Framework and NIST 800-53.

## Background

The State Administrative Manual holds the state's administrative policy, and DGS publishes it. Section 4986.9 is one chapter of the GenAI policy in SAM 4986.1 to 4986.13, which [CDT Technology Letter 25-01 (web)](CDT%20Technology%20Letter%2025-01%20%28web%29.md) announced for Feb. 20, 2025. The page shows the revision of November 2025. It has two parts: the procurement policy (A) and contract security provisions (B). It refers to the State Contracting Manual, volume 2, chapter 23, for the procedure.

Compare [California GenAI guidelines for public sector procurement uses and training 2024-03 (web)](California%20GenAI%20guidelines%20for%20public%20sector%20procurement%20uses%20and%20training%202024-03%20%28web%29.md), the interim guidance of 2024.

## Key claims

- "A State Entity shall consider procurement and enterprise use opportunities in which GenAI can improve efficiency, effectiveness, accessibility, and equity of government operations pursuant to CDT's procurement policy." (A.1)
- "Contractor must notify the State in writing if it: (1) intends to provide GenAI as a deliverable to the State; or (2), intends to utilize GenAI, including GenAI from third parties, to complete all or a portion of any deliverable that materially impacts: (i) functionality of a State system, (ii) risk to the State, or (iii) Contract performance." (A.3, required clause)
- "For all IT, non-IT, and Telecommunication procurements, a State Entity must, prior to award, complete a SIMM 5305-F Generative Artificial Intelligence Risk Assessment to determine if the GenAI risk is low, moderate, or high. The State Entity shall submit the completed 5305-F to CDT for all risk levels." (A.5)
- "If a State Entity's SIMM 5305-F assessment indicates a moderate or high-risk GenAI, the State Entity shall seek consultation with CDT prior to the award." (A.5.1)
- "A State Entity must have a multidisciplinary governance team to continuously oversee and monitor for new, ongoing, and changing security, privacy, and operational risks throughout the lifecycle of the GenAI use." (A.7)
- "Ensuring that vendors and suppliers provide documentation on the development, training, and operation of their GenAI systems, including details on the origin of datasets, algorithms, and any pre-trained models used." (B.4)

## Evidence and method

A state policy section of about 600 words. It states duties for state entities and lists the purchases that are exempt from the risk assessment. It gives no data.

## Limits

The section does not reproduce SIMM 5305-F, SIMM 5310-C or the State Contracting Manual chapter that it cites. It does not say how CDT scores a risk level. The copy does not define "State Entity". That term is in SAM 4986.2, which the library does not hold. Its exemption list includes "Cloud computing software licenses or subscriptions limited to access of online content", but not other cloud or compute purchases.
