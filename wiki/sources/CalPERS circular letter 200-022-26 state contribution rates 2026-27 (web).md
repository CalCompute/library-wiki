# CalPERS circular letter 200-022-26 state contribution rates 2026-27 (web)

## Citation

California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS), Actuarial Office. "2026-27 State Employer and Employee Contribution Rates." Circular Letter 200-022-26, May 22, 2026. Signed by Scott Terando, Chief Actuary. https://www.calpers.ca.gov/employers/policies-and-procedures/circular-letters/200-022-26. Read 2026-11-19. Copy: `raw/web/2026-11-19 CalPERS circular letter 200-022-26 state contribution rates 2026-27.html`.

## Bottom line

- The letter gives the state employer and employee pension rates for fiscal year (FY) 2026-27. They start with the first payroll period that ends in July 2026.
- The actuarially required employer rate for State Miscellaneous members is 31.60 percent of compensation, and 31.70 percent with the Budget Act addition. Employee rates for State Miscellaneous members are 8.00 to 10.00 percent.

## Background

CalPERS sends circular letters to the employers in its system. This letter goes to state employers, agricultural districts, and state colleges and universities. The CalPERS Board of Administration approved the rates on April 14, 2026.

## Key claims

- "The purpose of this Circular Letter is to inform you of the following employer and employee pension contribution rates for fiscal year (FY) 2026-27 approved by the California Public Employees' Retirement System (CalPERS) Board of Administration on April 14, 2026." (Purpose)
- "The employer contribution rates are effective with the first payroll period that ends in July 2026 and are in effect for FY 2026-27." (Employer Contributions)
- "State Miscellaneous | 31.60% | 0.10% | 31.70%" (Member Category, Actuarially Required Employer Contribution for FY 2026-27, Additional Employer Contribution Rate, Budget Act Total Contribution Rate for FY 2026-27; Employer Contributions)
- "Pursuant to Government (Gov.) Code section 20683.2(d) of the Public Employees’ Retirement Law, the California State Legislature may adopt higher contribution rates than shown in the table above to redirect savings resulting from increases in member contribution rates for various state members." (Employer Contributions)
- "PEPRA members are required to contribute at least 50% of the total annual normal cost of their pension benefit. As per Gov. Code section 7522.30, state employees are excluded from this requirement except for employees of the Legislature, California State University (CSU), and the judicial branch." (Public Employees’ Pension Reform Act (PEPRA) Employee (Member) Contributions)
- "State Miscellaneous – CSU, Legislature and Judicial | 16.79% | 0.58% | No | 8.00%" (Member Category, Total Normal Cost, Change, Change Needed, Member Rate; Rates Effective July 1, 2026)
- "Member contribution rates for Bargaining Unit 18 will remain unchanged because half of the normal cost is lower than the current member rates." (Notes)

## Evidence and method

An official notice from the CalPERS Actuarial Office. It gives the employer rates for five member categories. It gives the employee rates and total normal cost for PEPRA members and for Bargaining Units 2, 5, 12, 16 and 18. It points to agenda item 5d of the Finance & Administration Committee of April 13, 2026 for the method.

## Limits

The letter gives pension rates only. It does not give health or other benefit costs; [CalHR 2026 employer health contributions by bargaining unit (web)](CalHR%202026%20employer%20health%20contributions%20by%20bargaining%20unit%20%28web%29.md) gives health contributions. The tables of the five bargaining units have no labels in the Markdown copy. The Budget Act rates depend on the Legislature: the letter says "it is anticipated that the Legislature will also adopt the increased rates for FY 2026-27". The letter does not say which member category the state IT classes are in. The letter says that the complete actuarial valuation report comes "later this summer".
