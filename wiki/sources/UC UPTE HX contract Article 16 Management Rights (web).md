## Bottom line

- Article 16 of the University of California (UC) agreement with UPTE for the Health Care Professionals (HX) unit is on management rights. It reserves to UC all rights that the agreement does not grant.
- The listed rights include the right to introduce new methods and equipment and the right to subcontract. UC is not obligated to bargain on these areas during the agreement.

## Background

UPTE represents UC's HX unit. UC posts each article as a separate file on its [UC HX contract page (web)](UC%20HX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Management of the University is vested exclusively in the University. The parties agree that all rights not specifically granted in this Agreement are reserved solely to the University." (Article 16, A)
- "Although the University may upon request consult with UPTE concerning the following areas, the University is not obligated to bargain with UPTE as to such areas during the term of this Agreement." (Article 16, A)
- "To introduce new or improved methods, programs, equipment, or facilities or change or eliminate existing methods, equipment, or facilities;" (Article 16, B.5)
- "To determine the location or relocation, reorganization, or discontinuance of operations; to determine where employees shall work; or subcontract all or any portion of any operation;" (Article 16, B.6)
- "No action taken by the University with respect to a management right shall be subject to the grievance or arbitration procedure or collateral suit, unless the exercise thereof violates an express written provision of this Agreement." (Article 16, D)

## Evidence and method

One article of a collective bargaining agreement between a public university and a union, as UC posts it. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The article has no date or signature in its text. Its file address names 2019-2024.
- The [UC HX contract page (web)](UC%20HX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md) gives a new contract for Nov. 1, 2025 to Sept. 30, 2028, and says that the posted articles "do not reflect the most recent negotiated language."
- Its text is the same as [UC UPTE TX contract Article 19 Management Rights (web)](UC%20UPTE%20TX%20contract%20Article%2019%20Management%20Rights%20%28web%29.md), apart from two commas and the line breaks.
