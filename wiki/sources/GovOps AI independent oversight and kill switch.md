## Bottom line

- The page names the Governor's AI "kill switch" order. Its link is `FINAL-N-9-26-AI-EO-9.18.26-SIGNED.pdf`: Executive Order N-9-26, signed on Sept. 18, 2026, by the file name.
- Under it, GovOps leads "new third-party oversight of safety and security risks in AI systems, as well as independent audits". It also works on an emergency shutoff for frontier models.

## Background

See [Government Operations Agency](../entities/Government%20Operations%20Agency.md).

## Key claims

- "GovOps is being guided by an executive order to dramatically accelerate the implementation of new third-party oversight of safety and security risks in AI systems, as well as independent audits."
- "The work convenes a group of world-leading experts to provide a guide for California to reinforce and strengthen its AI safety and security laws."
- "Proposals under consideration include requiring independent third parties to write safety plans for frontier AI companies, as well as requiring companies to develop an emergency shutoff, or “kill switch,” for frontier models."
- GovOps asks for input at AIOversight@Govops.ca.gov.
