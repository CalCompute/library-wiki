# National Compute docs Credits (web)

## Citation

National Compute Labs PBC. "Credits." National Compute Docs, date not stated. https://docs.nationalcompute.com/credits/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page explains how prepaid credits pay for use of the National Compute Grid. There are three kinds of credit: Standard, Restricted and Promo.
- Grants, such as the sign-up credits of a public research organization, become Promo credits. Promo credits expire 30 days after the grant by default. A billing hold stops capacity that no credits can pay for.

## Background

National Compute Labs PBC publishes the documentation for its compute platform. This page is the user guide to credit balances, spend order, debt, advances, payments and billing holds. The platform's agent, Marshall, and the public research pool are among the services that credits pay for.

## Key claims

- "**Standard** credits pay for anything and never expire." (Credit balances)
- "**Restricted** credits pay only for the clusters, sites or services they name" (Credit balances)
- "**Promo** credits come from grants and always expire." (Credit balances)
- "Grants become Promo credits: the sign-up credits of a public research organization, feedback rewards, data-sharing credits and promotions." (Promo credits)
- "Promo credits expire, 30 days after the grant by default; a promotion can carry its own term." (Promo credits)
- "An **advance** is authorized debt up to a limit that National Compute opens for your organization" (Advances)
- "A billing hold stops capacity that nothing can pay for." (Billing holds)

## Evidence and method

Product documentation. It states the platform's billing rules. It gives no prices and no usage figures.

## Limits

The page describes rules, not use. It gives no credit amounts except by reference to other pages. The amount of the sign-up credit is on [National Compute docs Public Research (web)](National%20Compute%20docs%20Public%20Research%20%28web%29.md). The page has no date, so the rules can change.
