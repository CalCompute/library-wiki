# California Agency Reports filing reports page (web)

## Citation

California Legislature, Legislative Data Center. "Filing Reports." agencyreports.ca.gov. https://agencyreports.ca.gov/content/filing-reports. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 California Agency Reports filing reports page.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20California%20Agency%20Reports%20filing%20reports%20page.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11. No date on the page.

## Bottom line

- An agency that must report to the Legislature sends an electronic copy to three offices. They are the Secretary of the Senate, the Chief Clerk of the Assembly and the Office of Legislative Counsel.
- The page points to Government Code sections 9795 and 10242.5 for the procedures.

## Background

The page is part of agencyreports.ca.gov, the agency reports site that the Legislative Data Center keeps. It gives the filing route for every report that a statute requires an agency to send to the Legislature. The Chief Clerk then records each report received in the Assembly Journal ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- "State or local agencies required to file reports with the Legislature shall submit an electronic copy to the Secretary of the Senate, the Chief Clerk of the Assembly, and the Office of Legislative Counsel." (Filing Reports)
- "Reports submitted to the Secretary of the Senate should be emailed to:" (Filing Reports)
- "Agencies should refer to Sections 9795 and 10242.5 of the Government Code for additional guidance as to the proper procedures for filing reports required to be submitted to the Legislature and Governor." (Filing Reports)

## Evidence and method

A short procedural web page from the Legislature's data center. It states the rule and the email route. It gives no examples.

## Limits

The page does not quote sections 9795 or 10242.5. Its email addresses are hidden by an email protection script, so the copy does not show them. It does not mention CalCompute.
