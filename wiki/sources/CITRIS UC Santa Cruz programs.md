# CITRIS UC Santa Cruz programs

## Citation

CITRIS and the Banatao Institute, UC Santa Cruz. "Programs." Web page, citris.ucsc.edu, date not stated. https://citris.ucsc.edu/programs/. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- CITRIS UC Santa Cruz runs its programs "only for UCSC faculty and student participation".
- It has two grant programs:
  - the Interdisciplinary Innovation Program (I2P): seed funds for "at least two PIs from at least two different campus divisions";
  - Tech for Social Good: student funds for IT projects "that aim to solve one of society's big challenges".

## Background

See [CITRIS UC Santa Cruz homepage](CITRIS%20UC%20Santa%20Cruz%20homepage.md).

## Key claims

- I2P "supports early-stage research by at least two PIs from at least two different campus divisions" with "a focus on Information Technology".
- Tech for Social Good: "UC Santa Cruz students to apply for competitive funding for projects or events with a focus on Information Technology".
- The menu also lists CIDER (drone education), Core Seed Funding and CITRIS Foundry.

## Evidence and method

The institute's own page (301 words).

## Limits

- No grant sizes or counts. Campus-only programs.
