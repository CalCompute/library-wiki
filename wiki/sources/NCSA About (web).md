# NCSA About (web)

## Citation

National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA), University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. "About." Web page, date not stated. https://www.ncsa.illinois.edu/about/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- NCSA is a supercomputing center at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. It started in 1986 as one of five centers of the National Science Foundation (NSF) Supercomputer Centers Program.
- The state of Illinois, the University of Illinois, NSF and other federal agencies support it. Its mission is to "Bring people, computing and data together to benefit society."

## Background

This is the "About" page of the NCSA website. It gives the center's start date, its supporters, and its vision, mission and purpose statements. It also gives a statement on inclusion and the contact details of the help desk. The page sits under the Office of the Vice Chancellor of Research and Innovation of the university.

## Key claims

- "Since 1986, the National Center for Supercomputing Applications has been home to visionary science and pioneering partnerships." (About)
- "One of five facilities created by the National Science Foundation (NSF)’s Supercomputer Centers Program, NCSA is supported by the state of Illinois, the University of Illinois, the NSF and various federal agencies working together for the benefit of all those we serve." (About)
- "Bring people, computing and data together to benefit society." (Mission)
- "Our advanced cyberinfrastructure and expertise make us a hub for transdisciplinary research aimed at pushing the boundaries of human understanding and making the world a better place." (About)
- "Help Desk is available from 7 a.m. to 11:30 p.m., seven days a week, 365 days a year." (Questions?)

## Evidence and method

An institutional web page with statements of mission and purpose. It names the bodies that support the center. It gives no figures for budget, staff, users or systems.

## Limits

The page does not give the amount that each supporter pays. It does not name the center's computers or its allocation routes. [NCSA DeltaAI allocations (web)](NCSA%20DeltaAI%20allocations%20%28web%29.md) gives the allocation routes for one system, DeltaAI.
