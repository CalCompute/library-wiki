# Energy Commission backup generating project RB Inyokern Data Center (web)

## Citation

California Energy Commission (CEC). "RB Inyokern Data Center." Project page of the Power Plant Listing, technology "Backup Generating System". Web page, no date. https://www.energy.ca.gov/powerplant/backup-generating-system/rb-inyokern-data-center. Fetched 2026-11-22; read 2026-11-22. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project RB Inyokern Data Center.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-22%20Energy%20Commission%20backup%20generating%20project%20RB%20Inyokern%20Data%20Center.md) (original: `raw/web/2026-11-22 Energy Commission backup generating project RB Inyokern Data Center.html`).

## Bottom line

- The CEC project page for a data center in Inyokern, Kern County, with up to 99 megawatts (MW) of backup generation. Its status is "Under Review".
- The data center building is about 238,000 square feet. Southern California Edison (SCE) serves it, and its 40 diesel generators take no part in grid-support operations.

## Background

The CEC is a California state agency. This page is one of the 12 project pages that the [Energy Commission power plant listing backup generating systems (web)](../sources/Energy%20Commission%20power%20plant%20listing%20backup%20generating%20systems%20%28web%29.md) links. R&L Capital, Inc. is the project owner. The docket number is 26-SPPE-01, the capacity is 99 MW and the location is Inyokern, Kern County, California. The project status is "Under Review" and the project type is "Small Power Plant Exemption".

## Key claims

- "The RB Inyokern Data Center (RBIDC) would include a total of forty (40) diesel-fired generator sets that would be used exclusively to provide up to 99 megawatts (MW) of backup emergency generation to support on-site, data center operations during utility outages." (Project Description)
- "Of the 40 generators, approximately 70 percent would be dedicated to replacing the critical IT load of the data center, while the remaining capacity would be used to support general facility loads, building systems, and life-safety services during emergency outages." (Project Description)
- "The RBIDC emergency backup generating facility would not be electrically interconnected to the transmission or distribution grid, and would not participate in demand response, peak shaving, or any grid-support operations." (Project Description)
- "The single-story data center building will be approximately 238,000 square feet, with a maximum structural height of 30 feet." (Project Description)
- "On-site fuel storage would be sized to sustain 72 hours of continuous operation at full design load, consistent with Tier III data center reliability criteria." (Project Description)
- "The CEC does not permit data centers but does have jurisdiction over certain energy infrastructure associated with them under its exclusive permitting authority to certify all thermal generating facilities 50 megawatts (MW) and larger in California." (Data Centers)

## Limits

- The page gives no key dates.
- It gives the share of generators for the IT load, but not the data center's normal electrical demand.
- The project description is the applicant's proposal, in the conditional. The page can change after the fetch date.
