# WSCUC where to find data on institutions (web)

## Citation

WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC). "Where to Find Data on WSCUC Institutions." Web page, date not stated. https://www.wscuc.org/resources/where-to-find-data-on-wscuc-institutions/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The page lists six places to find data on WSCUC-accredited institutions: WSCUC's Key Indicators Dashboard (KID), and five federal tools.
- The federal tools are College Navigator, the College Scorecard, the Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS), and two Federal Student Aid (FSA) data sets.

## Background

WSCUC accredits senior colleges and universities. This resource page points readers to public data. It also says that WSCUC publishes peer review team reports and commission action letters in its [directory of institutions](WSCUC%20directory%20of%20institutions%20%28web%29.md). The library holds one IPEDS file: [IPEDS HD2025 Directory information (web)](IPEDS%20HD2025%20Directory%20information%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- Federal data: "The following federal websites provide user-friendly access to comprehensive national data on higher education institutions, from operations and finance to student success and post-graduation outcomes." (opening paragraph)
- Transparency: "WSCUC promotes transparency by being one of the few accreditors that publishes these important records of institutional review and actions." (opening paragraph)
- KID: "Interactive, easy to use data dashboard that includes information on all WSCUC institutions that report to IPEDS." (WSCUC's Key Indicators Dashboard)
- College Scorecard: "Each Scorecard includes five key pieces of data about a college: costs, graduation rate, loan default rate, average amount borrowed, and employment." (College Scorecard)
- IPEDS: "Allows access to standardized federally reported data through a variety of tools and download functions." (IPEDS)
- Heightened Cash Monitoring: "The U.S. Department of Education (ED) may place institutions on a Heightened Cash Monitoring (HCM) payment method to provide additional oversight of cash management." (FSA Heightened Cash Monitoring)

## Evidence and method

This is a short resource page of an accrediting body, with one paragraph and six linked entries. It describes data tools; it gives no data of its own.

## Limits

- The page does not say which data the KID dashboard holds beyond IPEDS.
- It does not cover research activity, computing or staff in information technology.
- It prints the IPEDS name as "Integrated Postsecondary Education System Data". The National Center for Education Statistics uses "Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System".
