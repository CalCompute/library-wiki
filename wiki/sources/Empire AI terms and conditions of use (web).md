# Empire AI terms and conditions of use (web)

## Citation

Empire AI. "Terms and conditions of use of Empire AI resources." Knowledge base article, version dated May 27, 2026. https://empireai.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/157000364334-terms-and-conditions-of-use-of-empire-ai-resources. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- These 14 terms apply to "all use of Empire AI resources". Empire AI is the New York consortium that manages the state AI computing facility ([NY Economic Development Law 361 Empire AI (web)](NY%20Economic%20Development%20Law%20361%20Empire%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).
- A principal investigator (PI) must be faculty or staff of a consortium member institution. All users must have a current tie to a member institution and live in the United States, unless the Executive Director approves an exception.
- The systems take only non-confidential data. They "do not meet NIST requirements for controlled unclassified data", and Empire AI "presently performs no back ups".

## Background

Empire AI publishes this article in its user knowledge base. The page says that Robert Harrison made it and that it changed on May 29. Its text says "Version dated 5/27/2026." For how users get time, see [Empire AI access (web)](Empire%20AI%20access%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- PI duty: "Project principal investigators (PIs) are responsible for ensuring that all users associated with their project comply with these terms and conditions." (term 1)
- Eligibility: "PIs must be faculty or staff currently employed and/or appointed by a consortium member institution and domiciled in the US, and all users associated with a project must be officially and currently associated with a consortium member institution and domiciled in the US, as certified by the project PI and demonstrated via an institutional email address for each user, unless an exception is approved by the Empire AI Executive Director." (term 3)
- Export control: "Remote access is prohibited from countries in the restricted country access list in order to comply with export control and other laws." (term 5)
- Reports: "A brief project report and 1-slide highlight are due one month after the end of an allocation period and are required for an additional allocation". (term 7)
- Public information: project details "will be made public and might be used for reporting to the state and to promote Empire AI and consortium member institutions." (term 8)
- Data ownership: "Data stored on Empire AI systems are the property of the PI and associated project, consistent with relevant policies of the member institution and associated funding agencies, and Empire AI will not claim any IP rights to this data." (term 9)
- Data security: "our systems presently can only be used for non-confidential data and do not meet NIST requirements for controlled unclassified data. Support for HIPAA is planned for the near future, but is not presently available." (term 10)
- Backups: "Empire AI presently performs no back ups and is not responsible for any data loss". (term 11)
- End of a project: "Users have 60 days after project completion or allocation period ending to transfer data before accounts and data are removed." (term 12)
- Changes: "These terms and conditions may be intermittently updated without notice." (term 13)

## Evidence and method

This is a terms-of-use text. It has no data.

## Limits

- The terms do not list the consortium member institutions.
- They do not say what an exception to the eligibility rule needs.
- They give no notice or appeal step before suspension. Term 14 says access can end "at any time without notice".
- The restricted country list is on a separate page that the library has not read.
