# CENVAL-ARC homepage

## Citation

UC Merced. "CENVAL-ARC." Program homepage, publication date unknown (page dated Oct. 28, 2026, copyright 2026). https://cenval-arc.ucmerced.edu/. Read 2026-10-28.

## Bottom line

- CENVAL-ARC holds its next symposium at UC Merced on March 4 and 5, 2027. Registration is open (You Are Invited!).
- The page gives the program's vision: lower the barriers to high-performance computing (HPC) for researchers and students in California's Central Valley (Our Vision Statement).
- NSF award 2346744 funds it. The page gives no hardware, cost or access rules.

## Background

CENVAL-ARC is the Central Valley Accessible Research and Computational Hub. UC Merced leads it with CSU Fresno, CSU Sacramento and CSU Stanislaus.

## Key claims

- Symposium: "CENVAL-ARC Symposium 2027 – March 4th & 5th". (page banner)
- Sponsor: the symposium is "proudly sponsored by the National Science Foundation's Campus Cyberinfrastructure (CC) program". (You Are Invited!)
- Audience: "faculty member, graduate student, or undergraduate researcher" who need "high-performance computing beyond personal laptops or local resources". (Why Attend?)
- Offer: "Access FREE Open Science Grid (OSG) resources—and keep using them at your home institution". (What You'll Gain)
- Offer: "Connect with NSF program officers" and "Network with researchers across all career stages in computation throughout the Central Valley". (What You'll Gain)
- Vision: CENVAL-ARC "aims to lower barriers to high-performance computing, drive innovation, and build an inclusive community for groundbreaking research". (Our Vision Statement)
- Funding: "CENVAL-ARC is supported by NSF under the Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure CC* Regional Computing award No. 2346744". (page footer)

## Evidence and method

The program's own homepage on a UC Merced site. It is promotional text for an event.

## Limits

- The page does not list member campuses, users, nodes or GPUs.
- It does not say how CSU users get accounts on the UC Merced cluster or what they pay.
- The 2027 agenda and speakers are on other pages.
