# ACCESS governance (web)

## Citation

ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services and Support), U.S. National Science Foundation program. "Governance." Web page, no date. https://access-ci.org/about/governance/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- ACCESS has two governance bodies. The Executive Council is made of the principal investigators (PIs) of the ACCESS awards. An External Advisory Board (EAB) advises the Council.
- The EAB has 11 members. Nine come from an open nomination process. The NSF program officer is an ex officio observer.

## Background

ACCESS is the National Science Foundation (NSF) program that gives researchers allocations on national research computing resources ([ACCESS homepage](ACCESS%20homepage.md)). NSF funds it as a set of separate awards. This page states how the awardees coordinate their work and who advises them. The ACCESS Coordination Office supports the Executive Council.

## Key claims

- "The ACCESS Executive Council comprises the principal investigators (PIs) of ACCESS awards as primary members, several other ACCESS award senior personnel as secondary members or observers and ex officio members including the NSF ACCESS Program Officer." (Executive Council)
- "Its purpose is to effect top-level coordination and governance among all ACCESS awardees to ensure high operational efficiency and user-centric performance of the collective ACCESS resources" (Executive Council)
- "The ACCESS Coordination Office (ACO) has facilitated the creation of an agreed-upon charter and meeting cadence, provides coordination services for in-person and online meetings, tracks the Executive Council membership and prepares and disseminates meeting minutes among the members." (Executive Council)
- "The EAB comprises 11 members, nine of whom are elected via an open nomination process, one who is the chair of the ACCESS Resource Provider Forum and one from the ACCESS Researcher Advisory Council (with a backup)." (External Advisory Board)
- "NSF-cognizant program officer for the ACCESS program is an ex-officio observer." (External Advisory Board)
- "The EAB meets four times per year, three virtual and one in person." (External Advisory Board)

## Evidence and method

A program web page that describes governance. It names the bodies, their members by role and how often the EAB meets. It links to the EAB member list but does not print the names. It does not give the charter text.

## Limits

- The page has no date.
- It does not say how the Executive Council makes decisions or how often it meets. It says only that the Council meets "on a regular basis as defined in the Charter".
- It does not give the budget of ACCESS or of each award.
