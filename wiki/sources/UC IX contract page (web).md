## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) lists its contract with the American Federation of Teachers (AFT) for the Non-Senate Instructional (IX) unit, the lecturers.
- The page says that the agreement expired on June 30, 2026. The parties agreed a one-year extension through June 30, 2027, and bargaining continues.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the IX unit's pages. It links the complete contract for 2021 to 2026. The library holds it as [UC UC-AFT IX lecturer contract 2021-2026 (web)](UC%20UC-AFT%20IX%20lecturer%20contract%202021-2026%20%28web%29.md).
