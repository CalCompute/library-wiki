# TOP500 El Capitan system (web)

## Citation

TOP500. "El Capitan - HPE Cray EX255a, AMD 4th Gen EPYC 24C 1.8GHz, AMD Instinct MI300A, Slingshot-11, TOSS." System record, date not stated (lists to June 2026). https://top500.org/system/180307/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- TOP500 lists El Capitan at "DOE/NNSA/LLNL" with a Linpack performance (Rmax) of 1,809.00 petaflops and a power use of 29,684.62 kilowatts.
- It was number 1 on the lists from November 2024 to November 2025 and number 2 on the June 2026 list.

## Background

TOP500 publishes a list of the most powerful supercomputers twice a year, ranked by the Linpack benchmark. This record gives the system's site, maker, cores, benchmark results, power and its rank on each list.

## Key claims

- "Site: DOE/NNSA/LLNL", "Manufacturer: HPE", "Cores: 11,340,000", "Installation Year: 2024". (system table)
- "Linpack Performance (Rmax) 1,809.00 PFlop/s" and "Theoretical Peak (Rpeak) 2,821.10 PFlop/s". (Performance)
- "HPCG 17,406.0 TFlop/s". (Performance)
- "Power: 29,684.62 kW". (Power Consumption)
- Rank by list: "06/2026" rank "2"; "11/2025" rank "1"; "06/2025" rank "1"; "11/2024" rank "1". (list history)

## Evidence and method

A benchmark record. TOP500 reports the results of the Linpack (HPL) and HPCG benchmarks and the measured power. The power figure has "Power Measurement Level: 2". The record gives no cost and no GPU count.

## Limits

The record does not give the number of GPUs or say who can use the system. It does not name the system that took rank 1 in June 2026.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: this record gives a "Theoretical Peak (Rpeak)" of "2,821.10 PFlop/s". [LLNL El Capitan (web)](LLNL%20El%20Capitan%20%28web%29.md) gives "Peak PFLOPS (CPUs+GPUs) 2889.200".
