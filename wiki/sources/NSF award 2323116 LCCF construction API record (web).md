## Bottom line

- NSF award 2323116 funds construction of the Leadership-Class Computing Facility (LCCF) at the University of Texas at Austin. The estimated total is $457,447,968. NSF has obligated $376,000,000 in fiscal years (FY) 2024 to 2026.
- The abstract says that the Horizon system "will be hosted at a commercial hyperscale datacenter". The award is a cooperative agreement from July 1, 2024 to April 30, 2028.

## Background

This is the NSF awards API record for the LCCF construction award, in JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) format. The Office of Advanced Cyberinfrastructure (OAC) manages it. The principal investigator (PI) is Daniel Stanzione. The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) leads the project. The NSF budget chapter on the same facility is [NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The LCCF project is part of NSF’s broad, holistic advanced computing strategy intended to provide unique resources and services to enable discoveries in the largest and most computationally intensive S&E frontiers that could not advance otherwise." (abstract)
- "The Horizon system will be hosted at a commercial hyperscale datacenter by establishing a partnership to enhance academic-industry exchange." (abstract)
- "The project will partner with four Distributed Science Centers (DSCs) to expand its geographical footprint and leverage the deep expertise in our nation’s CI ecosystem." (abstract)
- "The EPO plans include programs and exhibits based at a Visitor Center that will be constructed at TACC." (abstract)
- Estimated total: "457447968". Obligated: "FY 2024 = $51,000,000.00", "FY 2025 = $124,000,016.00", "FY 2026 = $201,000,000.00"; total "376000000". (record)
- Start "07/01/2024", end "04/30/2028". Instrument: "Cooperative Agreement". Fund program: "Leadership-Class Computing Fac". (record)

## Evidence and method

A federal award record from the NSF awards API. It gives the recipient, the PI and co-PIs, the program officer, the dates, the amounts and an abstract. The primary program codes are in the NSF Major Research Equipment account. The estimated total matches the Total Project Cost of $457.45 million in the budget chapter. The FY 2026 obligation matches the FY 2026 request of $201,000,000.

## Limits

- The record does not name the data center company. The budget chapter names Sabey Inc.
- It does not give the size of Horizon or the operations budget.
- It does not name the four Distributed Science Centers. The budget chapter names them.
