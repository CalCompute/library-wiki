# Cryst Stanford HAI profile

## Citation

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI). "Elena Cryst, Director of Policy and Society at Stanford HAI." People page, publication date unknown (works to July 2026). https://hai.stanford.edu/people/elena-cryst. Read 2026-10-26.

## Bottom line

- Stanford HAI confirms Elena Cryst's role: "Director of Policy and Society at Stanford HAI". The spelling "Cryst" is correct.
- She co-wrote "Expanding Academia's Role in Public Sector AI" (Dec. 4, 2024).
- Her other works are on AI sovereignty, world models and low-resource languages.

## Background

Her HAI people page. It lists her latest and related works.

## Key claims

- Role: "Director of Policy and Society at Stanford HAI". (header)
- "Expanding Academia's Role in Public Sector AI" is an issue brief of Dec. 04, 2024. Its authors are Kevin Klyman, Aaron Bao, Caroline Meinhardt, Daniel Zhang, Elena Cryst and Russell Wald. Summary: "This brief analyzes the disparity between academia and industry in frontier AI research and presents policy recommendations for ensuring a stronger role for academia in public sector AI." (All Related)
- "AI Sovereignty's Definitional Dilemma", news, Feb. 17: "Governments worldwide are racing to control their AI futures, but unclear definitions hinder real policy progress." (Latest Work)
- "The World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era: Governing AI Beyond Language", issue brief, July 27, with Fei-Fei Li among the authors. (Latest Work)
- "Mind the (Language) Gap", white paper, Apr. 22, 2025. (All Related)
