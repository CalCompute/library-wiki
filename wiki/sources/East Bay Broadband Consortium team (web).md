# East Bay Broadband Consortium team (web)

## Citation

East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC). "Our Staff." Web page, date not stated. https://www.eastbaybroadband.org/our-team. Read 2026-11-13.

## Bottom line

- EBBC has a Director, Patrick Messac, and a Deputy Director, Cinthia Diaz Medrano.
- The page also lists seven other people. They work for East Bay city and county governments, a county library, the Kapor Center and Tech Exchange.

## Background

This page is under "Our Team" on the EBBC website. Its heading is "MEET OUR TEAM". The site menu has two links to this page: "Staff" and "Board Members". The page text does not say which people are on the board.

## Key claims

- "Patrick Messac is the Director for the East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC)." (Patrick Messac)
- "Prior to this role, Patrick served as the Director for the #OaklandUndivided Coalition" (Patrick Messac)
- "Cinthia is the Deputy Director of the East Bay Broadband Consortium’s (EBBC)." (Cinthia Diaz Medrano)
- "Tony Batalla serves as Chief Information Officer and Director of Information Technology for the City of Oakland" (Tony Batalla)
- "Her leadership on major IT upgrades and the library system’s transition to a state-sponsored broadband network directly supports EBBC’s goals to strengthen public access points and expand high-speed internet access across Contra Costa County." (Alison McKee)
- "Lili Gangas is Chief Technology Community Officer at the Kapor Center" (Lili Gangas)

## Evidence and method

A staff and team page. Each person has a name, a post and a short biography. The seven people after the two staff are from:

- the Alameda County Office of Education;
- the City of Fairfield;
- the City of Oakland;
- the Contra Costa County Library;
- the Kapor Center;
- the Solano County Department of IT;
- Tech Exchange.

## Limits

The page does not say which people are board members, or how EBBC chooses them. It has no date. One person, Stewart Bruce, is the "Regional IT Infrastructure and Operations Manager in Solano County's Department of IT". The page names "Solano Connected" but does not say what it is.
