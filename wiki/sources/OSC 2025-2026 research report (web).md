# OSC 2025-2026 research report (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "2025-2026 Complete Research Report." Web page with a director's letter by David Hudak, Executive Director, 2026. https://www.osc.edu/research/research-reports/2026/2025_2026_complete_research_report. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- OSC has more than 7,715 clients in Ohio. Its director says it gives "an estimated $50 million in annual cost savings compared to alternative providers".
- Ohio State's Wexner Medical Center and College of Medicine moved new GPU resources to OSC's Ascend cluster. Ascend now has eight times the computing power of the original 2022 cluster, and it serves all OSC clients.

## Background

This is the web page of OSC's 2025-2026 research report. It has ten client research spotlights, a link to the full report as a PDF, and a letter from the executive director. The spotlights include Open OnDemand, OSC's open-source web portal, at other universities.

## Key claims

- "As a shared resource used by more than 7,715 clients in Ohio, OSC offers an estimated $50 million in annual cost savings compared to alternative providers." (Director's Letter)
- "Our centralized computing systems located at the State of Ohio Computer Center also offer greater benefits than smaller stand-alone systems distributed across campuses, including reliability, disaster resilience, and cybersecurity" (Director's Letter)
- "States around the country are investing in centralized supercomputing resources and public-private partnerships that can capitalize on the growing interest and promise of artificial intelligence (AI) to advance technology innovation. The State of Ohio is a national leader in this field, continuously supporting OSC operations for almost 40 years." (Director's Letter)
- "One key example is OSC’s collaboration with The Ohio State University Wexner Medical Center and College of Medicine to transfer significant new computing resources to OSC’s Ascend HPC cluster over the last year." (Director's Letter)
- "The revamped Ascend, now boasting eight times the computing power of the original cluster launched in 2022, not only supports medical research and education at Ohio State, but also serves OSC’s broader client needs." (Director's Letter)

## Evidence and method

An annual report page from the center itself. The cost savings figure is OSC's own estimate; the page does not give its method. The spotlights are case stories, not data.

## Limits

This page draws only on the web page. It does not draw on the PDF of the full report. The page does not say how OSC estimates the $50 million, or what the alternative providers are. It does not give the cost of the Ascend upgrade.
