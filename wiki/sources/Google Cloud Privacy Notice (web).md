# Google Cloud Privacy Notice (web)

## Citation

Google LLC. "Google Cloud Privacy Notice." Google Cloud website, Terms. Effective date October 15, 2026. https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The notice covers only "Service Data": the account, billing, settings and usage data that Google collects when it provides Google Cloud and Google Workspace. It does not cover the data that customers put into the services.
- Google keeps most types of Service Data for up to 180 days, and longer for security, legal and tax reasons. It says that it does not sell Service Data.

## Background

Google sells Google Cloud Platform and Google Workspace, including Google Workspace for Education. The notice is one of the Google Cloud terms. The data that customers process in the services ("Customer Data") falls under their agreements and the Cloud Data Processing Addendum. The service terms are in [Google Cloud Platform Terms of Service (web)](Google%20Cloud%20Platform%20Terms%20of%20Service%20%28web%29.md). See [Google](../entities/Google.md).

## Key claims

- "This Privacy Notice applies solely to Service Data and **does not** apply to Customer Data or Partner Data." (introduction)
- "Service Data is the personal information Google collects or generates during the provision and administration of the Cloud Services and related technical support, excluding any Customer Data and Partner Data." (notice text)
- "Service Data may be processed on servers located outside of the country where our users and customers are located because Service Data is typically processed by centralized or regionalized operations like billing, support, and security." (notice text)
- "We do not sell your Service Data to any third parties." (notice text)
- "We retain most types of Service Data for a set period of up to 180 days (the exact number depends on the specific type of data). However, some Service Data may be kept for longer periods where there is a business need." (Retention and Deletion of Service Data)
- "We generally have longer retention periods (which can be over a year) for Service Data that is kept for the following purposes" (Retention and Deletion of Service Data), then security, fraud and abuse prevention, legal requirements, and tax, accounting or financial requirements.

## Evidence and method

A vendor privacy notice of about 4,000 words of text, with an effective date. It states Google's practices for one class of data. It gives no audit evidence.

## Limits

The notice does not cover Customer Data, which is the research or student data in a Google Cloud project. For that, it refers to the Cloud Data Processing Addendum, which this library has not read.
