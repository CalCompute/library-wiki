# Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)

## Citation

Gauss Centre for Supercomputing (GCS). "About us." GCS website. No date on the page. https://www.gauss-centre.eu/about-us. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The Gauss Centre for Supercomputing (GCS) is a non-profit registered association. It joins Germany's three national supercomputing centres: HLRS in Stuttgart, JSC in Jülich and LRZ in Garching.
- The German federal research ministry and the ministries of three states fund it jointly.
- Its HPC resources are open to national and European researchers from academia and industry.

## Background

The federal and state governments founded GCS in 2007 as a joint initiative. A board of directors, a project steering committee of the funding bodies and a scientific steering committee lead it. This page describes its mission, centres, organisation, funding and alliances.

## Key claims

- What it is: "It is a non-profit organization, combining the three national supercomputing centres—HLRS, JSC, and LRZ into Germany’s most powerful supercomputing organization." (introduction)
- Access: "Its world-class HPC resources are openly available to national and European researchers from academia and industry." (introduction)
- Origin: "Founded in 2007 as a joint initiative between federal and state government". (GCS - Who Are We?)
- Legal form: "GCS operates as a registered association (eingetragener Verein, e.V.)." (Organization)
- Mandate: "As a non-profit organization, GCS is commissioned by the German Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR) and the respective ministries of the states of Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria, and North Rhine-Westphalia." (Organization)
- Leadership: "The organization is led by a board of directors, a project steering committee (funding bodies) and a scientific steering committee (acting as an advisory council), all of which are elected on a rotating basis." (Organization)
- Funding: "As a national research institution, GCS is jointly funded by the Federal Ministry of Research, Technology and Space (BMFTR), the Ministry of Science, Research and the Arts of the German State of Baden-Württemberg, the Bavarian State Ministry of Sciences, Research and the Arts, and the Ministry of Culture and Research of the State of North Rhine-Westphalia." (Funding Bodies)
- Alliances: "At the national level, GCS is a founding member of the Gauß Allianz, the German HPC partnership that coordinates HPC-related activities at the state level." (National and International Alliances)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional "about" page. It gives the legal form, the members, the funders and the leadership bodies. It gives no budget figures.

## Limits

- The page gives no budget and no funding split between the federal and state ministries.
- It does not describe how researchers apply for time.
- It names the North Rhine-Westphalia ministry as the "Ministry of Culture and Research". The JUPITER release names it the "Ministry of Culture and Science" ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)).
