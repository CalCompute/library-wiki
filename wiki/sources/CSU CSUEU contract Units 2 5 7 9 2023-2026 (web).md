## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the California State University (CSU) and a union of CSU staff. It covers health care, operations, clerical and technical support staff in Units 2, 5, 7 and 9. Its term ends on June 30, 2026.
- CSU can contract out work only when it does not displace unit employees. CSU must give the union notice of long-term contracting out and meet and confer on its impact.
- Employees whose work changes "due to technological changes" can get release time for retraining before any layoff, "When possible".

## Background

The California State University Employees Union (CSUEU) is Service Employees International Union (SEIU) Local 2579. It represents four CSU staff units. Unit 9 is Technical Support Services. The Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations Act (HEERA) governs the agreement. A note on each page says that Articles 21, 23 and 29 changed on Nov. 30, 2023.

## Key claims

- "The Trustees of The California State University (CSU) recognize the California State University Employees Union (CSUEU), Service Employees International Union (SEIU) Local 2579, the Union, as the sole and exclusive bargaining representative for Bargaining Units 2, 5, 7 and 9" (Article 1, 1.1)
- "When the CSU deems it necessary in order to carry out the mission and operations of the campus, the CSU may contract out work to external vendors and third-party providers of services provided that the contracting out does not displace bargaining unit employees." (Article 3, 3.2)
- "\"Displacement\" includes layoff, demotion, involuntary transfer to a new classification, involuntary transfer to a new satellite campus location or a location requiring a change of residence, and involuntary timebase reductions." (Article 3, 3.2)
- "Long-term contracting out shall mean contracting out work which is more than one hundred eighty (180) days." (Article 3, 3.3)
- "Notice to the Union shall be no later than one hundred twenty (120) days prior to the commencement of long-term contracting out." (Article 3, 3.4)
- "the University will provide to CSUEU all existing relevant written information, which may include, the Request for Proposal, copies of all bids received, any cost analysis used by the University to evaluate the need for contracting out, and copies of all consultants' reports" (Article 3, 3.8)
- "When possible, employees whose work is being changed due to technological changes may be provided with reasonable release time to participate in retraining opportunities for campus specific opportunities prior to the implementation of any layoffs." (Article 22, 22.9)
- "The order of layoff shall be: a. first, temporary, and probationary employees; and b. last, permanent employees." (Article 24, 24.5)
- "This Agreement shall become effective upon ratification by both parties and shall remain in full force and effect up to and including June 30, 2026." (Article 29, 29.1)

## Evidence and method

The full text of a collective bargaining agreement, with its salary schedules and classification lists in appendices. It is a legal agreement between a public employer and a union. It gives rules, not data on how often CSU contracts out.

## Limits

- The agreement's own term is July 31, 2022 to June 30, 2026. The file address names "2023-2026".
- The term ended on June 30, 2026. The library holds no successor agreement or extension for these units.
- The text does not name artificial intelligence. Its technology rule is Article 22.9 only.
- Article 29.3 says that a term with an economic cost waits until the money is appropriated, under Government Code 3572(b).
