# CSU Information Security Data Classification standard (CSUSB copy) (web)

## Citation

California State University (CSU). "Information Security Data Classification Standards, 8065.S02 Information Security Data Classification." Implements CSU Policy #8065. PDF, 3 pages, final Sept. 28, 2011. Copy hosted by California State University, San Bernardino (CSUSB). https://www.csusb.edu/sites/default/files/upload/file/Information%20Security%20Data%20Classification.pdf. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- The CSU standard puts university information in three levels: Level 1 Confidential, Level 2 Internal Use and Level 3 General.
- Level 1 covers information exempt from disclosure or with severe risk, for example Social Security numbers with names and medical records. Level 3 is information that a campus can make public.

## Background

The standard implements CSU Policy #8065 on information security. This copy is on the CSUSB website. Its revision table shows a draft in June 2011 and approval on Sept. 28, 2011. The University of California uses four protection levels instead; see [UC Classification of Information and IT Resources (web)](UC%20Classification%20of%20Information%20and%20IT%20Resources%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- Scope: "This document describes the three levels of data classification that the University has adopted regarding the level of security placed on the particular types of information assets." (1.0 Introduction, p. 1)
- Not for records requests: "this classification standard is not intended to be used to determine eligibility of requests for information under the California Public Records Act or HEERA." (1.0 Introduction, p. 1)
- Level 1 test: "Severe risk - Information whose unauthorized use, access, disclosure, acquisition, modification, loss, or deletion could result in severe damage to the CSU, its students, employees, or customers." (Level 1, p. 1)
- Level 2 test: "Sensitivity - Information which must be protected due to proprietary, ethical, contractual or privacy considerations." (Level 2, p. 2)
- Research in Level 2: the Level 2 examples include "Trade secrets or intellectual property such as research activities". (Level 2, p. 2)
- Level 3: "Information at this level requires no specific protective measures but may be subject to appropriate review or disclosure procedures at the discretion of the campus in order to mitigate potential risks." (Level 3, p. 2)

## Evidence and method

This is a university system standard, in three short sections with lists of examples. The Markdown copy runs the two-column example lists together, so check the PDF before you quote an example. It gives definitions and examples, not data.

## Limits

- The copy is the 2011 version. It does not say whether CSU has revised the standard since then.
- It refers to the "CSU Asset Management Standards" for the controls on each level. The library has not read them.
- It does not name research data types such as human subject data or export-controlled data.
- The policy link in the copy is an old calstate.edu address.
