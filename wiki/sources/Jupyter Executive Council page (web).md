# Jupyter Executive Council page (web)

## Citation

Project Jupyter. "Executive Council." Web page in the Jupyter Governance site, date not stated. https://jupyter.org/governance/executive-council/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page gives the purpose, duties, membership and elections of the Executive Council (EC) of Project Jupyter.
- The EC has six members on two-year terms. It raises and manages all project funding and manages the project's legal matters.
- The Union of Councils elects some seats and the EC fills the others.

## Background

Project Jupyter publishes this page in its governance site. The EC is one of the three bodies that anchor Jupyter's governance. The page lists six current members with their terms. It links to a separate "team compass" at ec.jupyter.org for operations and contacts. The site states the content licence "Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)".

## Key claims

- Funding: the EC has the responsibility to "Raise, allocate, and manage all Project funding and sponsorships, and set policies and procedures for third parties to request external funding with the official endorsement of Project Jupyter." (Sole responsibilities)
- Legal entity: the EC manages "the Project’s relationship with LF Charities, which is Project Jupyter’s parent nonprofit organization and legal entity." (Sole responsibilities)
- Assets: the EC manages "the Project’s relationship with the Linux Foundation, which holds assets in custody for Project Jupyter." (Sole responsibilities)
- Override: "The EC can override decisions of the SSC, Software Subprojects, Standing Committees, and Working Groups in exceptional situations as the Project’s final decision-maker." (Sole responsibilities)
- Shared decisions: the EC and the Software Steering Council (SSC) share approval of governance changes and of new and removed Subprojects; "the change will be approved only if both bodies approve." (Shared responsibilities with the Software Steering Council)
- Size and terms: "The EC consists of 6 Council members." "Each EC member is elected to a 2-year term." (Council membership and elections)
- Term limit: "EC members cannot begin a new term if they have served on the EC for 3 of the last 4 years." (How long are term lengths and limits?)
- Seat balance: "After each election, the cumulative total number of seats filled by the UoC must be equal to, or one more than, the number filled by the EC." (Who elects the Executive Council?)
- Separation: "A person may not serve simultaneously on the EC and SSC." (Who is elegible for serving on the Executive Council?)
- Removal: "A removal motion passes if two-thirds of the entire EC votes in favor of removal." (How can an EC member be removed?)

## Evidence and method

This is the project's own governance rule for one body. It states duties, election rules and current members. It gives no record of decisions.

## Limits

- The page does not give the project's budget or its funding sources.
- It does not describe the Community Advisory Panel.
- Operations, meetings and contacts are on a separate site that this library has not read.
