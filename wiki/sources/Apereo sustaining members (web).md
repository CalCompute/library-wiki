# Apereo sustaining members (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Sustaining Members: Direct Financial Support for Apereo Projects." Membership web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/join-us/sustaining-members. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Sustaining membership lets an organization that uses an Apereo project fund that project directly.
- Each project's management committee alone manages the funds that sustaining members give to it.
- Dues differ by project. For example, CAS dues go from $1,000 to $15,000 a year by the member's annual budget, and Bedework dues are $500 at every budget level.

## Background

This page on apereo.org describes the Sustaining membership. It lists the foundation's support services and gives dues tables for five projects: Bedework, CAS, Sakai, uPortal and Xerte. For Opencast, it points to that project's own sponsor page. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Purpose: "Sustaining memberships allow organizations that have implemented an Apereo project to directly fund the open source software they rely on." (Direct Financial Support for Apereo Projects)
- Use of funds: "Becoming a Sustaining Member ensures dollars are available for ongoing maintenance, building new features, securing the software, and extending availability."
- Control: "Funds contributed to an Apereo project by sustaining members are managed solely by that project’s management committee."
- Model: "Each project has its own contribution model." (Sustaining Membership Contribution)
- CAS dues, by member's annual budget: "$0-25 million" pays "$1,000.00"; "More than 4 billion" pays "$15,000.00". (CAS table)
- Sakai dues: "$0-25 million" pays "$900.00"; "More than 4 billion" pays "$6,750.00". (Sakai table)
- uPortal dues are by level, not budget: "Bronze - uPortal Friend" pays "$1,000.00"; "Platinum -uPortal Champion" pays "$20,000.00". (uPortal table)
- Vote: "**One organizational board seat vote in foundation elections**" (list of services)

## Evidence and method

This is a membership and price page. It gives dues tables for each project and an example member profile. The page calls its profile of "Tokugawa International University" an "Example Sustaining Member Profile". The page does not say that it is a real institution.

## Limits

- The page gives no number of sustaining members and no total funds for any project.
- It gives no Opencast dues.
- It does not say how a project management committee reports on the use of the funds.
