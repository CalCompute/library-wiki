# UCSF Childrens Hospital Benioff 100 million gift 2010 (web)

## Citation

Kate Vidinsky. "UCSF Children's Hospital receives $100 million gift to build new hospital." University of California, San Francisco (UCSF), News, June 22, 2010. Archived article. https://www.ucsf.edu/news/2010/06/96880/ucsf-childrens-hospital-receives-100-million-gift-build-new-hospital. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Lynne and Marc Benioff gave $100 million to UCSF Children's Hospital for its new hospital at the Mission Bay campus in San Francisco.
- The hospital took the name UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital. The gift completed a matching gift from The Atlantic Philanthropies.

## Background

UCSF describes itself as "a leading university dedicated to promoting health worldwide through advanced biomedical research, graduate-level education in the life sciences and health professions, and excellence in patient care." The article says that Marc Benioff is "founder, chairman and CEO of salesforce.com". UCSF marks the article as archived, "only made available for historical reference".

## Key claims

- The gift "is both the largest gift the donors have ever made and the largest gift ever granted specifically to the UCSF Children’s Hospital." (paragraph 1)
- "It is the fourth largest philanthropic gift in UCSF’s history." (paragraph 6)
- "We will give exclusively to UCSF Benioff Children’s Hospital, which has the research base for the next generation of discoveries" (Marc Benioff)
- "The current gift completes the $125 million matching gift to the Campaign for UCSF Medical Center made in March 2009 by The Atlantic Philanthropies and its founder Charles F. Feeney." (paragraph 8)
- The match "brings the total raised for the $600 million capital campaign to $320 million." (paragraph 8)
- "The Mission Bay project is now the only capital project at any University of California campus to receive two pledges of $100 million or more." (paragraph 8)
- "Previously, the donors have supported various research projects and have endowed four departmental chairs in gastroenterology, hospital medicine, maternal fetal medicine, and psychiatry." (last paragraph)

## Evidence and method

A university news article with quotations from the UCSF Chancellor, the chief executive officer (CEO) of UCSF Medical Center and Marc Benioff. It gives the gift amount, the campaign totals and the plans for the hospital. A website for ideas about the new hospital "is powered through salesforce.com’s technology."

## Limits

The article is from 2010. It gives plans, for example "Upon completion in 2014", not results. It does not mention computing infrastructure or AI.
