## Bottom line

- This is the contract between the California State University (CSU) and United Auto Workers (UAW) Local 4123 for Unit 11. The unit holds Teaching Associates, Graduate Assistants and Instructional Student Assistants. Its own term ends on Oct. 31, 2025.
- CSU keeps the right to "alter, extend or discontinue existing equipment, facilities, and location of operations" and to set the number and duties of employees.
- CSU gives unit employees free access to the support that the campus President or designee deems required. The list of examples includes a computer with internet access and instruction-related software.
- Required training counts as part of the workload.

## Background

Unit 11 holds the academic student employees of CSU. The Public Employment Relations Board (PERB) certified UAW for the unit. The agreement covers appointments, pay, workload, training and grievances. A later memorandum extends it: see [CSU UAW Unit 11 contract extension 2026-03-02 (web)](CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%20extension%202026-03-02%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "The classifications included in this unit are: Teaching Associates (2309, 2324, 2353, 2354, and 2453), Graduate Assistants (2355, 2325 and 2326) and Instructional Student Assistants (1150, 1151, 1152 and 1153)." (Article 1, 1.2)
- "The CSU shall notify the Union sixty (60) days prior to the effective date of: (a) new classifications related to bargaining unit classifications or (b) revised bargaining unit classifications." (Article 4, 4.1)
- "Computer with internet access, free printing, and instruction-related software" (Article 13, 13.1, item 4)
- "to alter, extend or discontinue existing equipment, facilities, and location of operations; to determine or modify the number, qualifications, scheduling, responsibilities and assignment of employees and all other personnel" (Article 15, 15.1)
- "All required training and orientation undertaken during the term of the appointment shall be considered part of the workload for the term." (Article 22, 22.1)
- "This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect up to and including October 31, 2025." (Article 27, 27.1)

## Evidence and method

The full text of a collective bargaining agreement, with appendices. It is a legal agreement between a public employer and a union. It gives rules, not data.

## Limits

- The text has no article on contracting out and does not name artificial intelligence.
- Its own term ends on Oct. 31, 2025. The extension of March 2026 says that the agreement "is set to expire on February 27, 2026".
