# UC AI Council subcommittees (web)

## Citation

University of California Office of the President (UCOP). "AI Council Subcommittees." Web page in the AI Communities section of the University of California Artificial Intelligence website, date not stated. https://ai.universityofcalifornia.edu/ai-communities/ai-council-subcommittees.html. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-09 UC AI Council subcommittees.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-09%20UC%20AI%20Council%20subcommittees.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) AI Council has subcommittees that put the UC Responsible AI Principles into practice.
- The Innovation and Impact subcommittee supports AI projects across campuses, builds an AI inventory and aligns UC work with state, federal and industry initiatives.
- A second subcommittee works on risk, transparency and awareness. The page names it in two different ways.

## Background

The page is in the AI Communities section of the UC AI website. It has one opening paragraph and two sections, one for each subcommittee it describes. It gives no names of members.

## Key claims

- "The UC AI Council's Subcommittees—Innovation and Impact; Risk and Transparency; and Awareness and Education—play a pivotal role in advancing the implementation of the UC Responsible AI Principles." (AI Council Subcommittees)
- "The Innovation and Impact Subcommittee works to advance high-impact AI initiatives across UC campuses, promote collaboration, sharing of best practices, development of an AI inventory, and to align UC efforts with state, federal, and industry initiatives for the responsible and transformative use of AI." (Innovation and Impact)
- "The AI Council Subcommittee on Risk, Transparency, and Awareness helps stakeholders across the University of California understand and assess the risk associated with the use of artificial intelligence, identify strategies to mitigate risk, and connect to the appropriate resources to help address risk." (Risk, Transparency, and Awareness)
- "The Subcommittee focuses on coordinating and promoting comprehensive engagement and awareness programs for the UC system by sharing up-to-date information, events, and best practices related to AI within the UC community." (Risk, Transparency, and Awareness)

## Evidence and method

An institutional description of the council's subcommittees. It states each group's purpose. It gives no members, dates, outputs or budget.

## Limits

The page is thin. It does not say when the subcommittees started, who sits on them or what they have produced. It does not mention compute, funding or CalCompute. The [UC AI Council roster (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20roster%20%28web%29.md) names the co-chairs.

> ⚠️ Contradiction: The first paragraph names three subcommittees: "Innovation and Impact; Risk and Transparency; and Awareness and Education". The page then describes two: "Innovation and Impact" and "Risk, Transparency, and Awareness". The [UC AI Council roster (web)](UC%20AI%20Council%20roster%20%28web%29.md) names a "Risk and Transparency subcommittee". The [UC AI governance and transparency (web)](UC%20AI%20governance%20and%20transparency%20%28web%29.md) page names a "Subcommittee on Transparency".
