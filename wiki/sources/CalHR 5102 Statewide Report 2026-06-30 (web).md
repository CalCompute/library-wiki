## Bottom line

- The workbook counts state employees by job classification, race and ethnicity, and gender, as of June 30, 2026.
- Sheet 1 has 234,477 employees. Sheet 1 counts 391 Information Technology (IT) Specialist III, 2,415 IT Specialist II and 6,289 IT Specialist I.

## Background

CalHR publishes the 5102 report on the state workforce. Each classification has a row with its class code and title. Rows for each race and ethnicity group follow. Each row counts women (F), men (M) and non-binary (NB) employees. Sheet 1 uses "Alone" categories. Sheet 2 uses "Alone or In Combination" categories.

## Limits

The two sheets give different totals: 234,477 on Sheet 1 and 211,945 on Sheet 2. Sheet 2 has 356 IT Specialist III. The workbook does not explain the difference. It gives no pay, vacancy or department figures, and no class for AI or research computing.
