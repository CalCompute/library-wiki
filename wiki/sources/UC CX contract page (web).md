# UC CX contract page (web)

## Citation

## Bottom line

- The University of California (UC) links its complete contract with Teamsters Local 2010 for the Clerical and Allied Services (CX) unit for 2022 to 2026.
- The page says that the agreement, first set to expire on March 31, 2026, was extended by mutual agreement through May 31, 2026.

## Background

UCnet is UC's site for its employees. This page is the contract tab of the CX unit's pages. It links one file, the complete contract, which the library holds as [UC Teamsters CX contract 2022-2026 (web)](UC%20Teamsters%20CX%20contract%202022-2026%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Contract between the University of California and Teamsters Local 2010"
- "The current Teamsters agreement, originally set to expire March 31, 2026, has been extended by mutual agreement through May 31, 2026."
- "New contract expiration date – May 31, 2026"
- "2022-2026 Complete Contract" (link)

## Evidence and method

An index page of an employer's website. It gives the contract term and links. It holds no contract text.

## Limits

- The page gives no date of its last update and says nothing about the agreement after May 31, 2026.
- The library holds no text of the extension.
