# USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)

## Citation

Chinyere Cindy Amobi. "Venture capitalist Mark Stevens returns to USC Board of Trustees." USC Today, University of Southern California (USC), June 5, 2024. https://today.usc.edu/venture-capitalist-mark-stevens-returns-to-usc-board-of-trustees/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- Mark Stevens, a venture capitalist and USC graduate, starts a second term on the USC Board of Trustees in fall 2024.
- With his wife, Mary Stevens, he has given "more than $100 million to USC programs and entities".

## Background

USC Today is the news site of USC. The article is a profile of Mark Stevens. It gives his education, his career at Sequoia Capital and S-Cubed Capital, his earlier service on USC boards and the gifts of the couple.

## Key claims

- "Since Stevens first became a board member more than two decades ago, he and his wife, Mary Stevens, have donated more than $100 million to USC programs and entities across the University Park and Health Sciences campuses." (paragraph 2)
- "Since 2012, Stevens has served as founder and managing partner of S-Cubed Capital, a private family office investment firm." (paragraph 6)
- Before that, "he was an associate and then managing partner at Sequoia Capital, a venture capital firm he worked at for 23 years in Menlo Park, Calif." (paragraph 6)
- "In 2004, a $22 million endowment by Mark and Mary Stevens established the USC Stevens Institute for Technology Commercialization to encourage entrepreneurship and support patent applications at USC Viterbi." (USC roots)
- "Three years later, the pair donated $50 million to name the USC Mark and Mary Stevens Neuroimaging and Informatics Institute" (Gifts, sporting and spiritual)
- At the time of his first term, "he served as a bridge for the university to tap into the burgeoning technological revolution in Silicon Valley." (paragraph 8)

## Evidence and method

A profile page in a university news article. It quotes Mark Stevens, USC President Carol Folt and the Board of Trustees Chair, Suzanne Nora Johnson. Facts about his career that it gives:

- Bachelor's degrees in electrical engineering and economics from USC in 1981.
- A master's degree in computer engineering from USC in 1984, while he worked for Intel Corp.
- An MBA from Harvard Business School in 1989.
- USC Board of Trustees from 2001 to 2021.

## Limits

The article does not give the amount of the gift to the USC Caruso Catholic Center. It does not mention AI or computing infrastructure. The article does not state the year of the $50 million gift. Its phrase "Three years later" follows a paragraph about 2012.
