# AWS Activate startup credits (web)

## Citation

Amazon Web Services (AWS). "AWS Activate Credits." Program page, date not stated. https://aws.amazon.com/startups/credits/. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- AWS Activate gives promotional cloud credits to startups. The amount depends on the startup's funding stage.
- A self-funded startup can get up to $5,000. A startup with an Activate Provider, such as an accelerator or a venture capital firm, can get up to $200,000. AI startups can get more, by invitation only.

## Background

Amazon Web Services publishes this page on its startups site. It describes three tiers of Activate Credits and the four steps to apply. It names some Activate Providers: Andreessen Horowitz, Brex, Carta, Greylock Partners, Sequoia and Y Combinator. It also promotes Amazon Bedrock, migration help and Kiro Startup Credits.

## Key claims

- "Apply for up to $200,000 in AWS Activate Credits, with additional credits available for AI startups ready to scale." (How today's startups build for tomorrow)
- "AWS offers promotional credits based on your startup's funding stage." (Find the right tier for you)
- "Eligibility for AWS Activate: Pre-series B, founded in the last 10 years, AWS Account on Paid Tier Plan, and either new to Activate Credits or requesting more credits than previously received" (Find the right tier for you)
- Activate Founders: "Up to $5,000 USD in Activate Credits \*Start with $1,000 USD in Activate Credits. Select participants may qualify for additional credits up to $5,000" (tier table)
- Activate Portfolio: "Up to $200,000 USD in Activate Credits \*You must have an Organization ID (Org ID) from your Activate Provider, such as an accelerator, angel investor, and venture capital firm" (tier table)
- AWS Credits for AI Startups: "Invite only for startups ready to scale post Activate Portfolio" and "$200,000+ in AWS Credits" (tier table)
- "You'll hear back within 5–10 business days." (How to apply, step 4)

## Evidence and method

A vendor program page. It gives the tiers, the credit limits, the eligibility rules and the application steps. It gives no count of startups that got credits, no total credit value and no expiry period. The credits are "subject to the AWS Promotional Credit Terms & Conditions", which this page links but does not quote.

## Limits

The page does not cover researchers, universities or public agencies. [AWS Cloud Credit for Research (web)](AWS%20Cloud%20Credit%20for%20Research%20%28web%29.md) covers credits for research. The page does not say how AWS chooses the "Select participants" who get more than $1,000 in the Founders tier. It does not give the size of the AI startups tier above $200,000. It does not mention California.
