# Purdue RCAC Anvil Advisory Board (web)

## Citation

Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC). "Anvil Advisory Board." Web page, date not stated. https://www.rcac.purdue.edu/anvil/advisoryboard. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The page lists six members of the advisory board for Anvil, a Purdue system that the National Science Foundation (NSF) supports under Grant No. 2005632.
- The members come from two national laboratories, NVIDIA, the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) and three universities.

## Background

This page is part of the Anvil section of the RCAC website. It gives each member's name, post and a short biography. It does not describe the board's charge or how often it meets. The members are:

- Ashley Barker, Group Leader for User Assistance and Outreach, Oak Ridge National Laboratory.
- Jeff Layton, Senior Technical Marketing Engineer, NVIDIA.
- Erika Parsons, Associate Professor, University of Washington, Bothell.
- Bronis R. de Supinski, Chief Technology Officer, Livermore Computing, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.
- Michela Taufer, Dongarra Professor, University of Tennessee, Knoxville.
- John West, Director of Strategic Initiatives, TACC.

## Key claims

- "Anvil is supported by the National Science Foundation under Grant No. 2005632." (footer)
- "UAO is responsible for facilitating access to OLCF resources, providing training, documentation, and technical support to users, collecting and reporting on user facility data, and acquainting the public with the work conducted at the OLCF through scientific highlights." (Ashley Barker)
- "In this role, he is responsible for formulating LLNL's large-scale computing strategy and overseeing its implementation." (Bronis R. de Supinski)
- "TACC provides advanced computational services and expertise to the open science community, and is the largest provider of supercomputing resources in the NSF cyberinfrastructure community." (John West)
- "He is currently a senior Technical Marketing Engineer at NVIDIA working primarily on AI/ML and HPC applications, specifically storage characterization." (Jeff Layton)

## Evidence and method

A list of board members with biographies that the operator publishes. The biographies give past posts, degrees and awards. The mix of members is the only evidence about the board. It has national laboratory computing staff, a vendor engineer, faculty and a director at a national computing center.

## Limits

The page does not give the board's terms of reference, its terms of office, its meetings or its advice. It has no date, so the list can be out of date. The library leaves out the email addresses that the page prints. [Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md) describes Anvil and its funding.
