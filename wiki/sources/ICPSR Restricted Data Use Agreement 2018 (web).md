# ICPSR Restricted Data Use Agreement 2018 (web)

## Citation

Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR), University of Michigan. "Restricted Data Use Agreement for Restricted Data from the Inter-university Consortium for Political and Social Research (ICPSR)." Agreement form, date not printed, in Deep Blue, the University of Michigan repository. https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstreams/aebe854c-6528-46c5-90ae-c1be0940a47d/download. Fetched 2026-11-20; read 2026-11-20. Not rehosted: the library reads it at the publisher's address.

## Bottom line

- The agreement is ICPSR's general form for restricted research data that the investigator downloads or receives on removable media. It is not the form for the Virtual Data Enclave.
- The investigator must have a PhD or other terminal degree and a faculty or principal investigator appointment. The institution must be a higher education, research, government research or nonprofit body.
- The institution follows a data security plan, keeps to disclosure rules, sends annual reports and destroys the data at the end. The agreement runs for 24 months or until the IRB approval expires.

## Background

ICPSR is a data archive at the University of Michigan. This form gives an investigator and an institution access to restricted data. Access needs an online application with a research description, a data security plan and Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval. The form has ten sections: definitions, disclosure risk, the duties of each party, violations, confidentiality, incorporation by reference and other terms.

## Key claims

- "Investigators must meet each of the following criteria: 1. Have a PhD or other research-appropriate terminal degree; and 2. Hold a faculty appointment or have an appointment that is eligible to be a principal investigator at Institution." (Section III.C)
- "An institution of higher education, a research organization, a research arm of a government agency, or a nongovernmental, not-for-profit, agency." (Section IV.A)
- "Provide the Restricted Data requested by the Investigator in the Restricted Data Order Summary within a reasonable time of execution of this Agreement by Institution and to make the Restricted Data available to Investigator via download or removable media." (Section V.A)
- "That the Restricted Data will be used solely for research or statistical purposes relative to the project as identified in the Research Description of the Online Application" (Section VI.A)
- "No release of the statistic if the contribution of a few observations dominates the estimate of a particular cell. For example, in no instance should the quantity figures be released if one case contributes more than 60 percent of the quantity amount." (Section VI.F.4)
- "Investigator will complete and notarize an Affidavit of Destruction, attesting to the destruction of the Restricted Data." (Section VI.H)
- "Investigator’s separation from Institution terminates this Agreement." (Section VI.K)
- "Report the inappropriate use or disclosure to the appropriate federal and private agencies or foundations that fund scientific and public policy research." (Section VII.B.3)
- "This agreement shall be effective for 24 months from execution or until the IRB expires." (Section X.B)

## Evidence and method

A legal agreement form of about 3,300 words, with definitions, duties of each party, eleven disclosure rules, penalties and signature blocks. Its disclosure rules cite the U.S. Census Bureau disclosure checklist, the National Center for Health Statistics (NCHS) disclosure checklist and FCSM Statistical Policy Working Paper 22. It gives no data about use.

## Limits

The form prints no date or version. The year 2018 is in the file name of its earlier Deep Blue address, `ICPSRRestrictedDataUseAgreement_2018.pdf`. It does not give the data security plan, ICPSR's fees or how long ICPSR takes to approve an application. It does not say which datasets it covers.

The agreement for the Virtual Data Enclave accepts more kinds of institution, for example "a for-profit organization". It keeps the data in a remote desktop with no download ([ICPSR Virtual Data Enclave restricted data use agreement (web)](ICPSR%20Virtual%20Data%20Enclave%20restricted%20data%20use%20agreement%20%28web%29.md)). This form lists four kinds of institution and sends the data to the investigator. The two forms are for different access routes; the library does not take the difference as a contradiction.
