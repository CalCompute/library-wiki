# MGHPCC governance (web)

## Citation

Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC). "Governance." Web page, date not stated. https://mghpcc.org/governance/. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The page says that a nonprofit corporation operates MGHPCC. Its members are Boston University, Harvard University, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Northeastern University, the University of Massachusetts and Yale University.
- The page lists a Board of Directors from the member universities, an Executive Committee, a Research, Education and Outreach Group and a Program Management Group.

## Background

MGHPCC is a computing facility at 100 Bigelow Street in Holyoke, Massachusetts. This page in the About section of its website lists who governs it. The board chair is Robert Brown of Boston University. The executive director is John Goodhue.

## Key claims

- Operator: "The MGHPCC is operated by the Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center Inc., a 501(3)c whose members are Boston University, Harvard University, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, the University of Massachusetts, and Yale University." (opening line)
- Research group: "The Research Education and Outreach Working Group is made up of representatives from each of the founding universities." (Research, Education, & Outreach Group)
- Research group purpose: "The working group was formed to maximize the impact of the facility on current research, support the new collaborations that the facility makes possible, and encourage the submission of collaborative proposals for computationally intensive research and infrastructure." (Research, Education, & Outreach Group)
- Operations group: "The Program Management group works with MGHPCC staff to ensure that business and technical operations for the facility work smoothly, and the facility is prepared to meet current and future business and infrastructure needs." (Program Management Group)
- Operations scope: "Considerations include security, shared network infrastructure, space and power utilization, energy efficiency, budgeting, and cost allocation." (Program Management Group)

## Evidence and method

This is an institutional web page. It names the corporation, its six members and the people in each governance body, with their university titles. It marks the Executive Committee members with an asterisk. It gives no bylaws, no budget and no rules for how members share the facility.

## Limits

- The page does not say how members vote, pay or share costs.
- It does not state the state's role in MGHPCC. For the state grant to the AI Compute Resources at MGHPCC, see [MassTech AICR builder selection (web)](MassTech%20AICR%20builder%20selection%20%28web%29.md).
- It has no date.
- The page writes "501(3)c". The usual form is 501(c)(3).
