# EuroHPC JU AI Factories (web)

## Citation

European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU). "AI Factories." EuroHPC JU website. No date on the page. https://www.eurohpc-ju.europa.eu/ai-factories_en. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The European Union has set up 19 AI Factories and 13 AI Factory Antennas. They give free, customised support to small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) and startups.
- An AI Factory is built around an AI-optimised supercomputer. It gives compute and support services to European industry and scientific users.
- An Antenna extends the reach and the services of an existing AI Factory.

## Background

EuroHPC JU is "a joint initiative between the EU, European countries and private partners to develop a World Class Supercomputing Ecosystem" (page footer). This page is the entry page for its AI Factories. It lists the AI Factories by host country, their Antennas, and six new AI Factories. It links to pages on access modes, support and a helpdesk, which this library does not hold.

## Key claims

- Headline: "AI Factories: computing power and customised support services for free!" (page heading)
- Count: "The European Union has established 19 AI Factories and 13 AI Factory Antennas that offer **free, customised support** to SMEs and Startups." (introduction)
- Definition: AI Factories "are dynamic ecosystems that will build around AI-optimised supercomputers, offering computing resources and support services to the European industry, as well as to the European scientific users for the development of large AI models". (What are EuroHPC AI Factories and Antennas?)
- Skills: the definition also names "the development of skills and knowledge in the domain of AI." (What are EuroHPC AI Factories and Antennas?)
- Antennas: "AI Factory Antennas complement existing AI Factories by expanding the reach and complementing their services." (What are EuroHPC AI Factories and Antennas?)
- Non-EU hosts: the list gives Antennas in Switzerland, Serbia, Iceland, North Macedonia and the United Kingdom ("HammerHAI Antenna: United Kingdom"). (AI Factories list)
- New AI Factory: "The RO AI Factory aims to transform SMEs from passive technology adopters into active AI innovators by prioritising services, training, and infrastructural access." (New AI Factories)

## Evidence and method

This is an entry page from an EU body. It gives counts, a definition and a list by country. It gives no budgets, no hardware figures and no usage figures.

## Limits

- The page gives no funding amounts for the AI Factories.
- It does not describe the access modes on this page. It links to a separate page.
- The JUPITER release of 5 September 2025 says that EuroHPC JU oversees "13 AI factories" ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)). This page, read on 2026-11-11, gives 19. The two documents have different dates.
