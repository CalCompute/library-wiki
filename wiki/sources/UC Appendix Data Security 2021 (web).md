# UC Appendix Data Security 2021 (web)

## Citation

University of California. "Appendix: Data Security." Standard contract appendix for suppliers, dated 8/20/2021, 12 pages. Copy hosted by UCLA Health. https://www.uclahealth.org/sites/default/files/documents/0c/appendix-data-security-082021-1.pdf. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- The appendix sets the cybersecurity duties of a supplier that gets access to University of California (UC) "Institutional Information" or "IT Resources".
- The supplier must keep an information security plan that follows a recognized framework and has a third-party review. It must use UC data only to provide the contracted goods or services.
- The supplier must report a suspected or confirmed breach or security incident within 72 hours. It must return and dispose of UC data within 30 days after the contract ends.

## Background

UC attaches this appendix, often called Appendix DS, to supplier contracts. The copy is the version of Aug. 20, 2021, posted by UCLA Health. Its articles cover access, the security plan, audits, notice of changes, return of data, breach response and background checks. Exhibits describe the data and the plan. [UC Procurement OpenAI UC-wide agreement (web)](UC%20Procurement%20OpenAI%20UC-wide%20agreement%20%28web%29.md) says the UC-wide OpenAI agreement includes the Appendix DS.

## Key claims

- "Supplier's information security plan must be supported by a third-party review or certification." (Article 1.C.1)
- "Examples include the latest versions of PCI DSS, NIST CSF, CIS Critical Security Controls, IS0 27000 series, NIST SP 800-53 and NIST SP 800-171." (footnote 1)
- "Supplier may not access, use, or disclose Institutional Information and IT Resources outside the scope of the Agreement for purposes of, including but not limited to: marketing, advertising, research, sale, or licensing unless expressly approved in writing by UC." (Article 3.A.2)
- "the most stringent requirement (including but not limited to: least risk to UC, shortest time, best practice, etc.) will apply." (Article 1.F)
- "Within thirty (30) calendar days of the termination, cancellation, expiration, or other conclusion of the Agreement, Supplier must return all Institutional Information to UC and then dispose of the Institutional Information in possession of Supplier" (Article 7.A)
- "Supplier must provide such notifications no later than (1) seventy-two (72) calendar hours after the initial suspicion of a Security Incident and/or Breach" (Article 9)
- "Before Supplier's employee, sub-supplier, or agent may access Institutional Information and/or IT Resources classified at Protection Level 3 or Protection Level 4, Supplier must conduct a thorough and pertinent background check." (Article 11.A)

## Evidence and method

A standard contract appendix of about 3,700 words. It states duties and definitions. It gives no data. The library read Articles 1 to 9, Article 11 and the start of Exhibit 1.

## Limits

The copy is from 2021 and from UCLA Health's site; the library has no source that says whether UC has a later version. The appendix refers to UC standards, such as the Institutional Information Disposal Standard and the protection level classification, that the library has not read. It covers suppliers only, not UC's own users or research data that UC shares with another institution.
