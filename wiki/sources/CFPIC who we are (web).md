# CFPIC who we are (web)

## Citation

Child and Family Policy Institute of California (CFPIC). "Who We Are." Web page with mission, board and staff, date not stated. https://cfpic.org/who-we-are/. Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- CFPIC is a nonprofit, founded in 2004, that "partner[s] with public Human Services agencies". Its board is "ten California Human Service Agency Directors and other professionals".
- It is an example of a nonprofit intermediary governed by the county agency heads it serves, with program directors drawn from county careers.

## Background

The page gives CFPIC's mission, vision and values, and a link to its 20th Anniversary Report and Strategic Plan. It lists the board and about 30 staff with biographies. The staff biographies describe careers in county human services departments and the California Department of Social Services. The page holds staff email addresses; this library does not record them.

## Key claims

- "CFPIC was established in 2004 to partner with public Human Services agencies to address emerging needs of vulnerable families and opportunities to improve their well-being." (Who We Are)
- "We partner with leaders in building capacity at every level to address emerging needs and opportunities in Human Services." (Our Mission)
- "CFPIC operates under the authority of the CFPIC Board of Directors, composed of ten California Human Service Agency Directors and other professionals with vast expertise and experience in Adult Services, Child Welfare and Self-Sufficiency programs. CFPIC's Board provides oversight and strategic direction for the organization." (Our Board)
- "Our Leadership Team has extensive experience directing public Adult Services, Child Welfare and Human Service organizations in California. Our program and administrative staff bring diverse experience across a wide variety of public and private Human Services work." (Our Team)
- The Director Emeritus "retired in 2004 to found CFPIC" after "32 years in public human services, including 15 years as the Child Welfare Director in San Mateo County". (Our Team, Stuart Oppenheim)

## Evidence and method

The organisation's own page. It gives names, titles and career histories. It gives no budget, no funding sources and no legal form beyond "nonprofit".

## Limits

The page is about human services, not computing. It does not say how CFPIC is funded or how board members are chosen. The markdown copy also holds a stray fragment of a prevention-services flow chart that is not part of the page text.
