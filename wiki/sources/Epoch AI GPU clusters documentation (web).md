# Epoch AI GPU clusters documentation (web)

## Citation

Epoch AI. "GPU clusters documentation: Overview." Web page, date not stated. https://epoch.ai/data/gpu-clusters-documentation. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- The overview page of the documentation for Epoch AI's GPU clusters dataset. The dataset tracks "over 500 GPU clusters and supercomputers".
- The data is free to use under the Creative Commons Attribution license. Epoch AI refreshes the CSV file daily.

## Background

Epoch AI publishes data and research on AI. This page is the first of seven documentation pages: Overview, Inclusion, Coverage, Records, Estimation, Downloads and Acknowledgements. The library has only the Overview. The dataset itself is [Epoch AI GPU clusters (web)](Epoch%20AI%20GPU%20clusters%20%28web%29.md), and the data explorer is [Epoch AI GPU clusters](Epoch%20AI%20GPU%20clusters.md).

## Key claims

- "Our GPU clusters dataset is a collection of compute clusters made with chips that may be used for AI training and inference in addition to other high performance computing tasks." (Overview)
- "The dataset tracks key details such as their performance, hardware type, location, and estimated cost and power draw." (Overview)
- "The dataset is accessible on our website as a visualization or table, and is available for download as a CSV file, refreshed daily." (Overview)
- "Epoch's data is free to use, distribute, and reproduce provided the source and authors are credited under the Creative Commons Attribution license." (Use This Work)
- "Our database of over 500 GPU clusters and supercomputers tracks large hardware facilities, including those used for AI training and inference." (page summary)

## Evidence and method

The dataset owner's documentation page. It describes the dataset and its license. It gives no data.

## Limits

- The copy has the Overview only. The inclusion rules, coverage, record fields and estimation method are on other pages that the library does not have.
