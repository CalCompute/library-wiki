# RAISE at UCR people (web)

## Citation

UC Riverside. "Research Areas." People and thrusts page of the UC Riverside Artificial Intelligence Research and Education Institute (RAISE@UCR), date not stated. https://raise.ucr.edu/people. Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- RAISE@UCR has three pillars and eleven thrusts, each with a faculty lead from one of UCR's colleges and schools.
- Kevin Esterling (Political Science) leads the Social Sciences thrust. Mariam Salloum (Computer Science and Engineering) leads Education and Outreach.

## Background

The page lists the institute's structure. The thrusts and leads are:

- AI Foundations: Machine Learning (Vagelis Papalexakis), Robotics and Physical AI (Konstantinos Karydis), Systems and Security (Srikanth Krishnamurthy), Scientific Computing (Mark Alber).
- AI Applications: Agriculture, Environmental, and Geospatial Sciences (Hoori Ajami), Physical Sciences (Chia-en Chang), Bioinformatics and Biomedical Sciences (Monica Carson), Public Health (Mario Sims), Social Sciences (Kevin Esterling), Economics and Business (Gloria Gonzalez-Rivera).
- AI Education: Education and Outreach (Mariam Salloum).

## Key claims

- "Research at RAISE@UCR is structured around three core pillars: AI Foundations, AI Applications, and AI Education. The institute brings together distinguished faculty from across all UCR colleges and schools, each contributing unique expertise to advance the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence." (top of page)
- "Social Sciences. Thrust Lead: Kevin Esterling (Political Science, CHASS)" (AI Applications)
- "Education and Outreach. Thrust Lead: Mariam Salloum (Computer Science and Engineering, BCOE)" (AI Education)
- "Scientific Computing. Thrust Lead: Mark Alber (Mathematics, CNAS)" (AI Foundations)

## Evidence and method

The institute's own page. Names and departments only.

## Limits

No dates, no description of each thrust's work and no staff beyond the leads. The page title is "Research Areas", not "People".
