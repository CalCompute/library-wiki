# Apereo vendor policies (web)

## Citation

Apereo Foundation. "Apereo Foundation: Vendor Policies." Web page, date not stated. https://www.apereo.org/about/governance/vendor-policies. Read 2026-11-11.

## Bottom line

- The policies tell how the Apereo Foundation decides if a worker is an employee or a contractor, and what a vendor invoice must contain.
- Unless a contract says otherwise, payment is due 7 days after the invoice date for grants and awards.
- Payment is due after 15 days for individuals and 30 days for organizations.
- A project manager approves invoices for software development; an Apereo Foundation employee approves all other invoices.

## Background

This page is in the governance section of apereo.org. It covers employee and contractor status, the vendor definition, invoices, payment terms, approvals, sanctioned countries and tax documents. The page says that it is not tax, legal or accounting advice. The site puts its content "under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 United States License."

## Key claims

- Purpose: "The Apereo Foundation vendor policies are intended to provide guidance on whether services fall into employment or vendor relationships, further define vendors and invoice requirements, and tax documentation requirements." (Introduction)
- Test: "In general, if control falls to the Apereo Foundation, an individual is an employee. If the individual is independent, they are an independent contractor." (Factors)
- Key work: "Work that is a key aspect of business should not be contracted out and should be performed by an employee." (Factors, item 4.2)
- California: "A law in California limits the work a contractor can do for a company. Work that is in a company’s normal course of business cannot be contracted out." (Factors)
- Vendors: "Vendors include legal entities that are paid for invoices or independent contractor agreements, as well as awards and honorariums." (Vendor Definition)
- Payment terms: "7 days for grants and awards", "15 days for individuals" and "30 days for organizations" (Payment Terms)
- Approvals: "Invoices related to software development or user experience must be approved by the appropriate project manager" (Approval Process)

## Evidence and method

This is an internal operations policy that the foundation publishes. It states rules and refers to Internal Revenue Service (IRS) forms. It gives no data on vendors or payments.

## Limits

- The page links to a news article for the California law and does not name the statute.
- It gives no total vendor spending and no list of contractors.
- It is not legal advice, as the page itself says.
