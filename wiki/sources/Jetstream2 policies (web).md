# Jetstream2 policies (web)

## Citation

Jetstream2, Indiana University. "Acceptable Use Policies and Research and Export Control Guidance." Jetstream2 Documentation, web page, last updated October 27, 2026 (first made August 18, 2021). https://docs.jetstream-cloud.org/general/policies/. Read 2026-11-12.

## Bottom line

- Jetstream2 is a research cloud at Indiana University. Its users must follow the ACCESS Acceptable Use Policy and Indiana University policies.
- Use requires "an active ACCESS allocation". Thirty days after an allocation expires or is overdrawn, Jetstream2 destroys its resources, and they "will not be recoverable".
- Protected data such as HIPAA, FERPA and export-controlled data cannot go on the system without specific approval. The page permits fundamental, publishable research.

## Background

Indiana University runs Jetstream2 and publishes its documentation. This page holds the usage policies of the system. It also covers the GPU and large-memory nodes, a monthly GPU maintenance window, IP addresses and router quotas.

## Key claims

- Base policies: "Jetstream2 requires compliance with all ACCESS and Indiana University policies". (Acceptable Use of Jetstream2)
- Regional clouds: "Regional clouds may have additional usage and security policies." (same section)
- Protected data: users agree that data protected by federal law "(e.g., HIPAA, FERPA, ITAR, classified information, export control, etc.) are **not** stored on this system unless such storage and usage is specifically authorized by the responsible University administrator". (Protected Data and Jetstream2)
- Default security: "The Jetstream2 system is not intended, by default, to meet the security requirements of these laws or regulations". (same section)
- Research: "In general, fundamental, publishable research is permitted on Jetstream2 from any non-EAR sanctioned countries." (Jetstream2 and General Research Policies)
- Allocation: "Jetstream2 use requires an active ACCESS allocation. If your allocation expires, you will not be able to access Jetstream2 or your resources." (Allocation Policies)
- Notices: "Allocation PIs receive expiration notifications from ACCESS at 90, 60, and 30 days prior to expiration." (same section)
- End of an allocation: "30 days from expiration: If the allocation has not been renewed (preferred) or extended, all resources (instances, volumes, shares, images) on the allocation will be destroyed and will not be recoverable." (same section)
- Scarce GPUs: "If GPU or large memory resources become scarce in the future, it is possible that a form of scheduling or runtime limitation may be implemented to ensure equitable access for all; however, this is not currently anticipated." (Specialty System Policies)

## Evidence and method

This is a policy page in the system documentation. It has no data. It gives dates for the GPU maintenance window and for the last update.

## Limits

- The page does not give the size of Jetstream2 or the number of users.
- It does not say who pays for Jetstream2 or what a user pays.
- It refers to Indiana University policies IT-02 and IT-12 and to a separate export control page, which the library has not read.
- It does not describe the regional clouds or their policies.
