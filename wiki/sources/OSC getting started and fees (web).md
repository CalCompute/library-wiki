# OSC getting started and fees (web)

## Citation

Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC). "Getting Started." Web page, date not stated. https://www.osc.edu/resources/getting_started. Read 2026-11-09.

## Bottom line

- OSC is free for Ohio classroom use. Academic researchers in Ohio get credits that "largely or completely offset fees". Commercial and nonprofit clients buy services at set rates.
- OSC offers three clusters with GPUs, data storage, software, research software engineering, data analytics and machine learning, and education.

## Background

This is the first page of the Getting Started section of the OSC website. It lists OSC's seven services and says who uses OSC, what it costs and what training OSC offers.

## Key claims

- "OSC offers three supercomputer clusters – Pitzer, Cardinal, and Ascend – that all support GPU computing." (item 1, Cluster computing)
- "Ohio researchers may access licenses for some software packages through our statewide software program." (item 3, Software)
- "Classroom accounts are available to instructors seeking to incorporate HPC work into courses." (item 7, Education)
- "Commercial and nonprofit clients across the United States use OSC for research, simulations, development and testing of products." (How does the commercial and nonprofit community use OSC?)
- "As an academic computing resource for the State of Ohio, OSC is always free for Ohio classroom usage, and academic researchers in Ohio qualify for credits that largely or completely offset fees. Commercial and nonprofit clients purchase services at set rates." (What does it cost to use OSC?)

## Evidence and method

An institutional guide page for new clients. It states the cost model in one paragraph. It lists training: a new user guide, self-paced courses through Ohio State continuing education, help sessions, workshops and webinars.

## Limits

The page does not give the rates or the size of the academic credits. It links to a fee model page ("Service Costs") and an "FY27 budgets" page that this library has not read.
