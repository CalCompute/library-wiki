## Bottom line

- SB 942 adds Chapter 25, the California AI Transparency Act, to Division 8 of the Business and Professions Code, from section 22757.
- A covered provider is a maker of a generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) system with over 1,000,000 monthly visitors or users.
- A covered provider must make a free AI detection tool available. It must offer a manifest disclosure and put a latent disclosure in the content that its system makes.
- The civil penalty is $5,000 for each violation. The Attorney General, a city attorney or a county counsel can collect it.
- The bill makes the chapter operative on Jan. 1, 2026.

## Background

The bill's author is Senator Becker, as the Digest prints the name. The Governor approved it on Sept. 19, 2024. The text holds the Legislative Counsel's Digest and the new chapter in seven sections. Two later bills amend the chapter. AB 853 moves the operative date to Aug. 2, 2026 and adds duties for platforms and capture devices ([AB 853 chaptered text Chapter 674 of 2025](AB%20853%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20674%20of%202025.md)). AB 2713 amends section 22757.3.1 ([AB 2713 chaptered text Chapter 856 of 2026](AB%202713%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20856%20of%202026.md)). ## Key claims

- "“Covered provider” means a person that creates, codes, or otherwise produces a generative artificial intelligence system that has over 1,000,000 monthly visitors or users and is publicly accessible within the geographic boundaries of the state." (Bus. & Prof. Code § 22757.1(b))
- "A covered provider shall make available an AI detection tool at no cost to the user that meets all of the following criteria:" (§ 22757.2(a))
- "The tool supports an application programming interface that allows a user to invoke the tool without visiting the covered provider’s internet website." (§ 22757.2(a)(6))
- "The disclosure is permanent or extraordinarily difficult to remove, to the extent it is technically feasible." (§ 22757.3(b)(4), on the latent disclosure)
- "If a covered provider knows that a third-party licensee modified a licensed GenAI system such that it is no longer capable of including a disclosure required by subdivision (b) in content the system creates or alters, the covered provider shall revoke the license within 96 hours of discovering the licensee’s action." (§ 22757.3(c)(2))
- "This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2026." (§ 22757.6)

## Limits

The bill gives no cost estimate. The disclosure duties cover image, video and audio content; they do not name text. The chapter does not apply to a product that provides "exclusively non-user-generated video game, television, streaming, movie, or interactive experiences" (§ 22757.5). The copy shows the 2024 text.

AB 853 later changed section 22757.6: the chapter is operative on Aug. 2, 2026, not on Jan. 1, 2026 ([AB 853 chaptered text Chapter 674 of 2025](AB%20853%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20674%20of%202025.md)).

The bill does not mention research compute or CalCompute.
