# PSC allocations (web)

## Citation

Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (PSC). "Allocation and Affiliation Information." Web page, date not stated. https://www.psc.edu/resources/allocations/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- PSC gives free supercomputing time to researchers and educators at United States (U.S.) academic and non-profit research institutions, for non-proprietary projects. National Science Foundation (NSF) and National Institutes of Health (NIH) funds pay for it.
- Users apply for Bridges-2 and Neocortex through ACCESS or the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) pilot. Proprietary research goes through the Corporate Affiliates Program.

## Background

This PSC page sets out who can get time on PSC systems and how. It covers non-proprietary research, coursework grants and proprietary research. It then describes three systems, Bridges-2, Anton and Neocortex, with the route to each. It ends with affiliated data resources.

## Key claims

- "Funding from the NSF and the NIH enables PSC to provide allocations of free supercomputing time to researchers and educators affiliated with academic and non-profit research institutions in the United States." (Non-proprietary research)
- "A principal investigator (PI) may not be a high school, undergraduate, or graduate student; a qualified advisor must serve in this capacity. Postdocs are eligible to serve as PI." (Non-proprietary research)
- "A primary mission of the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center is to train students, including undergraduates, in high performance computing." (Coursework grants)
- "Users conducting proprietary research who are interested in using PSC systems can do so by requesting affiliation through the center’s Corporate Affiliates Program." (Proprietary research)
- "Researchers or educators affiliated with academic or non-profit research institutions in the United States may apply to use Bridges-2 through the NSF’s ACCESS program or via the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) pilot program." (How can I use Bridges-2?)
- "Anton is allocated annually via a Request for Proposal with proposals reviewed by a committee convened by the National Research Council at the National Academies of Science, Engineering, and Medicine." (How can I use Anton ?)
- "The policies documented here are evaluated regularly to assure adequate and responsible administration of PSC systems for users. As such, they are subject to change at any time." (introduction)

## Evidence and method

A policy and user page. It states eligibility rules, the principal investigator rule and the application route for each system. It gives hardware facts: Bridges-2 has "3 tiers of per-node RAM: 256GB, 512GB, and 4TB", and Neocortex has "two Cerebras CS-2 systems".

D. E. Shaw Research makes Anton available "without cost" for non-commercial research. Anton 3 "became operational on April 1, 2025".

## Limits

The page does not give the fees or terms of the Corporate Affiliates Program. It does not give allocation sizes, review times or node counts. It has no date. [PSC About (web)](PSC%20About%20%28web%29.md) describes the center itself.
