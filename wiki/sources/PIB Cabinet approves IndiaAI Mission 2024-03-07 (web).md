# PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)

## Citation

Press Information Bureau (PIB), Government of India, for the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). "Cabinet Approves Ambitious IndiaAI Mission to Strengthen the AI Innovation Ecosystem." Press release, Release ID 2012357, PIB Delhi, March 7, 2024. https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2012357. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- On March 7, 2024, the Union Cabinet of India approved the IndiaAI Mission with a budget outlay of Rs. 10,371.92 crore.
- The Mission has seven components. The first is AI compute of 10,000 or more graphics processing units (GPUs), built through public-private partnership.
- The IndiaAI Independent Business Division (IBD) under Digital India Corporation (DIC) carries out the Mission.

## Background

PIB is the Government of India's agency for information to the media. This release reports the Cabinet decision and lists the seven components of the Mission. Later PIB documents report what the compute component delivered ([PIB common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)](PIB%20common%20compute%20facility%20release%202025-01-30%20%28web%29.md); [PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).

## Key claims

- Approval: "the Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi has approved the comprehensive national-level IndiaAI mission with a budget outlay of Rs.10,371.92 crore." (paragraph 1)
- Implementer: "The Mission will be implemented by ‘IndiaAI’ Independent Business Division (IBD) under Digital India Corporation (DIC)". (paragraph 3)
- Compute: "The ecosystem will comprise AI compute infrastructure of 10,000 or more Graphics Processing Units (GPUs), built through public-private partnership." (component 1, IndiaAI Compute Capacity)
- Marketplace: "Further, an AI marketplace will be designed to offer AI as a service and pre-trained models to AI innovators." (component 1)
- Models: the IndiaAI Innovation Centre "will undertake the development and deployment of indigenous Large Multimodal Models (LMMs) and domain-specific foundational models in critical sectors." (component 2)
- Skills: IndiaAI FutureSkills "will increase AI courses in undergraduate, masters-level, and Ph.D. programs. Further, Data and AI Labs will be set-up in Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities across India to impart foundational level courses." (component 5)
- Public problems: the Application Development Initiative works on "problem statements sourced from Central Ministries, State Departments, and other institutions." (component 4)

## Evidence and method

This is a government press release of a Cabinet decision. It gives the total outlay and a short description of each of the seven components. They are compute capacity, innovation centre, datasets platform, application development, FutureSkills, startup financing, and safe and trusted AI. It gives no split of the budget by component.

## Limits

- The release does not give the period of the outlay. A later PIB backgrounder gives "over five years" ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
- It does not say how the public-private partnership for compute works, who owns the GPUs or what users pay.
- It gives no milestones or measures of success.
