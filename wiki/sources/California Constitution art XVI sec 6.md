# California Constitution art XVI sec 6

## Bottom line

- Article XVI, section 6 forbids the Legislature to give or lend the credit of the State or a local government to any person or corporation.
- It forbids a gift of "any public money or thing of value" to any individual or corporation.
- It forbids the State or a political subdivision to buy stock in a corporation. It gives exceptions for irrigation districts, insurance pools, veterans, disaster debris and temporary transfers by county and city treasurers.

## Background

Section 6 is part of the public finance article of the California Constitution. Proposition 8 amended it on Nov. 2, 1982. The library's copy comes from the Legislature's code website through the Wayback Machine, because that website refuses scripts.

## Key claims

- "The Legislature shall have no power to give or to lend, or to authorize the giving or lending, of the credit of the State, or of any county, city and county, city, township or other political corporation or subdivision of the State now existing, or that may be hereafter established, in aid of or to any person, association, or corporation, whether municipal or otherwise" (sec. 6, first clause)
- "nor shall it have power to make any gift or authorize the making of any gift, of any public money or thing of value to any individual, municipal or other corporation whatever" (sec. 6)
- "it shall not have power to authorize the State, or any political subdivision thereof, to subscribe for stock, or to become a stockholder in any corporation whatever" (sec. 6)
- "this section shall not prohibit any county, city and county, city, township, or other political corporation or subdivision of the State from joining with other such agencies in providing for the payment of workers’ compensation, unemployment compensation, tort liability, or public liability losses incurred by such agencies, by entry into an insurance pooling arrangement under a joint exercise of powers agreement, or by membership in such publicly-owned nonprofit corporation or other public agency as may be authorized by the Legislature" (sec. 6, second proviso)
- "(Sec. 6 amended Nov. 2, 1982, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 60, 1982.)" (history note)
