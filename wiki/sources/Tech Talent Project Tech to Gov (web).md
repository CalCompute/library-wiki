# Tech Talent Project Tech to Gov (web)

## Citation

Tech Talent Project. "Tech to Gov" (program page). techtalentproject.org, date not stated; it lists an event of Oct. 29, 2026. https://techtalentproject.org/ova_dep/tech-to-gov/. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- Tech to Gov is a virtual hiring event. State and federal agencies meet pre-screened technologists in one-on-one conversations and get training on technical hiring.
- The page reports results of past events. After the October 2023 event, one agency got about 3,300 applications and made 62 selections.

## Background

Tech to Gov is one of the programs of Tech Talent Project; see [Tech Talent Project homepage (web)](Tech%20Talent%20Project%20homepage%20%28web%29.md). The page says that a coalition of public interest organizations founded it. It lists the next event as a "Tech to Gov Hiring Forum + Job Fair" on Oct. 29, 2026.

## Key claims

- "Tech to Gov is a virtual hiring event that brings government agencies into direct conversation with technologists from across the private and public sectors." (For Government Leaders)
- "After the October 2023 Tech to Gov event, one agency posted five announcements, received around 3,300 applications, scheduled 130+ candidate interviews, and made 62 selections across 22 teams." (For Government Leaders)
- "In past events, 65 percent of attendees said they had not considered government service before but would now." (For Technologists)
- "Eleven percent of the candidate pool at the October 2024 event were senior executives with 20 or more years of experience." (For Technologists)
- "Founding coalition partners include Tech Talent Project, the Volcker Alliance, United States Digital Response, Technologists for Public Good, the Beeck Center, New America, NobleReach Foundation, PopVox, the Federation of American Scientists, the Partnership for Public Service, AnitaB, Coding it Forward, and the Horizon Institute for Public Service." (closing section)

## Evidence and method

A program page. It reports figures from past events and three short quotations from participants. It does not give the survey method or the number of respondents.

## Limits

The page does not name the agency in the October 2023 result. It does not say how Tech Talent Project screens candidates, or what an event costs an agency. It names no California agency.
