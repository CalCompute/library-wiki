# Governor's Innovation Fellows program (web)

## Citation

California Office of Data and Innovation (ODI). "Governor's Innovation Fellows Program." Program page, date not stated; describes cohorts to June 2026. https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-05 Governors Innovation Fellows program.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-05%20Governors%20Innovation%20Fellows%20program.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- The Governor's Innovation Fellows are current state employees. For six months they spend 80 percent of their time on improvement projects, with training from ODI, CalAcademy and private and academic partners.
- Three cohorts so far: 21 Fellows from August 2025, a second cohort from January 2026, and 15 Fellows from June 2026. New Fellows join every four months.

## Background

ODI runs the program under an executive order of Governor Newsom. The page says the program is "a delivery engine" rather than a traditional leadership program. It lists what Fellows do, why the program exists and the three cohorts. The library's [ODI homepage (web)](ODI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md) and [Bobra BIDS profile (web)](Bobra%20BIDS%20profile%20%28web%29.md) also mention the program.

## Key claims

- "The Governor's Innovation Fellows are state employees solving real public sector challenges. The program brings them together to improve services for Californians." (top of page)
- "Spend 80% of their time on projects that improve efficiency, service delivery, and customer experience"; "Get training and hands-on help from the Office of Data and Innovation, CalAcademy, and partners in the private sector and academia". (What the Fellows do)
- "Governor Newsom launched this program as part of a broader executive order. Its goal is to make state government efficient, engaged, and effective." (Why it matters)
- "The Innovation Fellows Program isn't like a traditional leadership program. We designed it to be a delivery engine. It develops leaders while creating real improvements that help Californians." (Why it matters)
- "The program will grow over time. New Fellows will join every 4 months. They will overlap with the earlier cohort so they can share knowledge and work together." (Looking ahead)
- "The third cohort started in June 2026. The 15 Fellows come from 7 agencies and 11 departments." "The 21 inaugural Fellows served from August 2025 to February 2026. They came from 9 agencies and 15 departments." (Meet the Fellows)

## Evidence and method

A state program page. It gives cohort sizes and dates; it gives no cost, no evaluation results and no list of projects on this page.

## Limits

The page does not name the executive order. It does not say if the Fellows' projects involve AI or compute, or what the training curriculum is. The cohort pages it links to were not read.
