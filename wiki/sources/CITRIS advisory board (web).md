# CITRIS advisory board (web)

## Citation

CITRIS and the Banatao Institute. "Advisory Board." Web page, date not stated (copyright 2026). https://citris-uc.org/people/advisory-board/. Read 2026-11-18.

## Bottom line

- The page lists the 16 current members of the CITRIS Advisory Board, three advisors and fellows, and 17 past board members, each with one title.
- Most current members come from industry and investment firms. One is a former Governor of California and one is the State Chief Information Officer (CIO).

## Background

CITRIS is the Center for Information Technology Research in the Interest of Society, a four-campus UC institute ([CITRIS About (web)](CITRIS%20About%20%28web%29.md)). This page in its People section lists the members of its Advisory Board. Each name links to a person page. [CITRIS leadership (web)](CITRIS%20leadership%20%28web%29.md) lists the staff leadership.

## Limits

- The page does not say what the board does, how CITRIS chooses the members or how long they serve.
- It does not name a current chair.
- The page gives one title for each person and no dates, so a title can be out of date.
