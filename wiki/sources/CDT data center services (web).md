# CDT data center services (web)

## Citation

California Department of Technology. "Data Center Overview." Service web page. https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/data-center/. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-11 CDT data center services.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-11%20CDT%20data%20center%20services.md) (original: `.html` beside it). Read 2026-11-11. No date on the page.

## Bottom line

- The California Department of Technology (CDT) runs two data centers for state, county, federal and local government entities. One is state-owned, in Rancho Cordova, and one is leased, in Vacaville.
- Both are built to Tier III equivalent standards, and each is the disaster recovery site for the other.

## Background

These are the data centers behind the "State Data Center" that CDT names on its own pages ([CDT About the Department](CDT%20About%20the%20Department.md)). The page describes their size, design, power, cooling, fire and physical security. It also lists energy awards and the Vacaville site's green building rating.

## Key claims

- "The California Department of Technology (CDT) data centers serve as the Tier III equivalent data centers for State, county, federal, and local government entities throughout California." (About the Data Center)
- "The state-owned Rancho Cordova data center is approximately 42,000 square feet and serves as the State’s primary production data center and a disaster recovery site for the Vacaville data center." (About the Data Center)
- "The Vacaville data center provides approximately 7,000 square feet of leased space and serves as both a production data center and a disaster recovery site for the Rancho Cordova data center." (About the Data Center)
- The sites have "uninterruptible power supply (UPS) with redundant generators (at uptime of 99.982%, 1.6 hours downtime per year), in a fault-tolerance design at N+1 minimum up to 2N maximum, with 72-hour power outage protection" (Tier III Design)
- "The Vacaville facility is the recipient of LEED NC Gold (v2.2) certification." It uses "300kW renewable photovoltaic panels" (Vacaville)

## Evidence and method

A department service page. It states the design standards and features of each site. It gives floor areas and the uptime figure. It gives no power capacity in megawatts, no rack count and no cost.

## Limits

The page does not say how much of the space is in use or free. It does not mention GPUs, AI work or high-performance computing. The energy awards it names date from 2012.
