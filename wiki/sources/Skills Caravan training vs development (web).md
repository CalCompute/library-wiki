# Skills Caravan training vs development (web)

## Citation

Shreya Verma, Skills Caravan. "Training vs Development: 10 Key Differences Explained (2026)." Vendor blog post, updated September 26, 2026. https://www.skillscaravan.com/post/10-differences-between-training-and-development. Read 2026-11-05.

## Bottom line

- A learning-platform vendor defines training as "short-term and job-focused" for the current role, and development as "long-term and growth-focused" for future roles.
- It gives ten differences in a table: focus, timeframe, scope, goal, driver, orientation, methods, measurement, scope of change and outcome. It ends with a pitch for its product.

## Background

A blog post by a vendor of a corporate learning platform. It defines the two terms, compares them, gives examples and a checklist, and promotes the vendor's platform.

## Key claims

- "Training is short-term and job-focused — it builds the skills someone needs for their current role. Development is long-term and growth-focused — it grows a person's potential for future roles." (The short answer)
- "A structured, short-term process of teaching the specific skills or knowledge a person needs to perform their current job well. It is role-specific, has clear objectives, and shows results fairly quickly." (What each one means, Training)
- "A broader, long-term process of growing a person's knowledge, behaviour, and potential for future roles and career progression. It goes beyond the current job to build capability over time." (What each one means, Development)
- "Training is usually pushed by the business to close a gap. Development works best when the individual owns it, with the organisation enabling and guiding." (The differences that matter most, 3)
