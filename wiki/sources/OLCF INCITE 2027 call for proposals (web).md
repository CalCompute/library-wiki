# OLCF INCITE 2027 call for proposals (web)

## Citation

Bethea, Katie. "INCITE 2027 Call for Proposals." Oak Ridge Leadership Computing Facility (OLCF), Oak Ridge National Laboratory, April 21, 2026. https://www.olcf.ornl.gov/2026/04/21/incite-2027-call-for-proposals/. Read 2026-11-19. ## Bottom line

- The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Innovative and Novel Computational Impact on Theory and Experiment (INCITE) program took proposals for calendar year 2027 until June 15, 2026.
- INCITE allocates up to 60 percent of the node-hours on the Aurora and Frontier exascale systems. Researchers from academia, government laboratories and industry can apply.
- INCITE gives 10 percent of its time to an Early Career Track. Director's Discretionary requests for early access can be made at any time.

## Background

The OLCF at Oak Ridge National Laboratory and the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF) are DOE Office of Science user facilities. This news article announces the INCITE call for 2027. It quotes the directors of the two facilities and gives the dates of two proposal webinars. Compare the DOE laboratory system in [LLNL Tuolumne (web)](LLNL%20Tuolumne%20%28web%29.md).

## Key claims

- "Proposals must be submitted by June 15, 2026." (paragraph 1)
- "Open to researchers from academia, government labs, and industry, the INCITE program is the major means by which the scientific community gains access to the most capable, most productive, fastest open science supercomputers in the nation." (paragraph 2)
- "The program allocates up to 60 percent of available node-hours on the Aurora exascale system at Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF) and the Frontier exascale system at the Oak Ridge Leadership Computing Facility." (paragraph 3)
- "Successful proposals must demonstrate a clear need for and the ability to harness leadership-class computing resources" (paragraph 6)
- "As of 2022, INCITE is committing 10% of allocatable time to an Early Career Track." (paragraph 7)
- "Researchers within ten years from earning their PhD (PhD on or after December 31, 2016) and who have not been a previous INCITE PI may choose to apply." (paragraph 7)
- "Director’s Discretionary requests for access to Frontier and Aurora can be submitted any time." (Early Access)

## Evidence and method

A facility news article of about 1,400 words, with the related article list. It states the call terms and quotes two laboratory directors. The full call text is on doeleadershipcomputing.org, which this library does not hold.

## Limits

The article does not give the review process, the number of awards, the size of a typical award or the cost to users. It does not say what share of the 2027 time goes to AI projects. It links to "The 2026 INCITE Call for Proposals" for the full call, although the article is about the 2027 call.
