# GitHub General Privacy Statement (web)

## Citation

GitHub, Inc. "GitHub General Privacy Statement." GitHub Docs, Site policy. Effective date April 27, 2026. https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- A school or employer that supplies a GitHub account becomes the data controller for most personal data. GitHub then acts as a data processor under a Data Protection Agreement.
- GitHub uses personal data to develop its products, "including artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies", and it can share data with Microsoft for that purpose.

## Background

GitHub is a code hosting service owned by Microsoft. This statement covers the personal data that GitHub, Inc. or GitHub B.V. processes as the data controller. GitHub's service terms are in [GitHub Terms of Service (web)](GitHub%20Terms%20of%20Service%20%28web%29.md). See [GitHub](../entities/GitHub.md).

## Key claims

- "When a school or employer supplies your GitHub account, they assume the role of Data Controller for most Personal Data used in our Services." (End User Notice: Organization-Provided GitHub Accounts)
- "In such circumstances, GitHub functions as a Data Processor, adhering to the Data Controller's instructions regarding your Personal Data's processing. A Data Protection Agreement governs the relationship between GitHub and the Data Controller." (same section)
- "We use Personal Data to develop and improve our products, services, and technologies, including artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies. This includes improving features, developing new offerings, enhancing safety and security capabilities, and training models." (Processing Purposes)
- "Personal Data may be shared with GitHub affiliates, including Microsoft, to facilitate customer service, marketing and advertising, order fulfillment, billing, technical support, legal and compliance obligations, product development and improvement (including training and improving artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies)" (Sharing of Personal Data)
- "GitHub also complies with the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (EU-U.S. DPF), the UK Extension to the EU-U.S. DPF, and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (Swiss-U.S. DPF) as set forth by the U.S. Department of Commerce." (Data Privacy Framework)
- "We’ll retain your Personal Data as long as your account is active and as needed to fulfill contractual obligations, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements." (Security and Retention)
- "We do not “sell” or “share” the personal information of known minors under 16 years of age." (California, Mandatory Disclosures)

## Evidence and method

A vendor privacy statement of about 6,000 words, with an effective date. It states GitHub's practices and the rights of users under the laws of the European Union, the United Kingdom and several U.S. states. It gives no audit evidence.

## Limits

For access to private repositories, the statement refers to the GitHub Terms of Service. For organization accounts, it refers to the Data Protection Agreement. This page does not quote those documents. It does not say which data types go into model training or how a user opts out. The cookie section and the translations were not read in detail.
