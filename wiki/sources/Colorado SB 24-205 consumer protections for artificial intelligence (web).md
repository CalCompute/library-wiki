# Colorado SB 24-205 consumer protections for artificial intelligence (web)

## Citation

Colorado General Assembly. Senate Bill 24-205, "Concerning consumer protections in interactions with artificial intelligence systems." Signed act, 2024 regular session. 26 pages. https://leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/2024a_205_signed.pdf. Copy: [`raw/web/2026-11-19 Colorado SB 24-205 consumer protections for artificial intelligence.md`](../../raw/web/2026-11-19%20Colorado%20SB%2024-205%20consumer%20protections%20for%20artificial%20intelligence.md) (original: `.pdf` beside it). Read 2026-11-19.

## Bottom line

- SB 24-205 adds Part 17, "Artificial Intelligence", to the Colorado Consumer Protection Act (C.R.S. 6-1-1701 to 6-1-1707). Its duties start on Feb. 1, 2026.
- A developer and a deployer of a "high-risk artificial intelligence system" must use reasonable care to protect consumers from algorithmic discrimination. A deployer needs a risk management program and an impact assessment.
- The attorney general has exclusive authority to enforce Part 17. A violation is an unfair trade practice.

## Background

The act is Colorado's law on high-risk AI systems that make, or are a substantial factor in making, a "consequential decision". Such decisions cover education, employment, lending, essential government services, health care, housing, insurance and legal services. The act names ISO/IEC 42001 and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework as frameworks for a risk program. It gives an affirmative defense to a person who discovers and cures a violation. The signature page of the copy is a scan, and the approval date is not legible in the Markdown copy.

## Key claims

- "ON AND AFTER FEBRUARY 1, 2026, A DEVELOPER OF A HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM SHALL USE REASONABLE CARE TO PROTECT CONSUMERS FROM ANY KNOWN OR REASONABLY FORESEEABLE RISKS OF ALGORITHMIC DISCRIMINATION ARISING FROM THE INTENDED AND CONTRACTED USES OF THE HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM." (6-1-1702(1), p. 6)
- "'HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM' MEANS ANY ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM THAT, WHEN DEPLOYED, MAKES, OR IS A SUBSTANTIAL FACTOR IN MAKING, A CONSEQUENTIAL DECISION." (6-1-1701(9)(a), p. 3)
- "A DEPLOYER OF A HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM SHALL IMPLEMENT A RISK MANAGEMENT POLICY AND PROGRAM TO GOVERN THE DEPLOYER'S DEPLOYMENT OF THE HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM." (6-1-1703(2)(a), p. 10)
- A deployer is exempt from subsections (2), (3) and (5) if it "EMPLOYS FEWER THAN FIFTY FULL-TIME EQUIVALENT EMPLOYEES" and "DOES NOT USE THE DEPLOYER'S OWN DATA TO TRAIN THE HIGH-RISK ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM", among other conditions. (6-1-1703(6), p. 16)
- A deployer or developer of an AI system "INTENDED TO INTERACT WITH CONSUMERS SHALL ENSURE THE DISCLOSURE TO EACH CONSUMER WHO INTERACTS WITH THE ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM THAT THE CONSUMER IS INTERACTING WITH AN ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM." (6-1-1704(1))
- "NOTWITHSTANDING SECTION 6-1-103, THE ATTORNEY GENERAL HAS EXCLUSIVE AUTHORITY TO ENFORCE THIS PART 17." (6-1-1706(1))

## Evidence and method

A signed state act. It states legal duties and definitions. It gives no data or findings of fact, apart from a safety clause.

## Limits

The copy is the act as signed in 2024. It does not show later amendments, and the library has no source on whether the Feb. 1, 2026, date still holds. The act covers persons "doing business in this state", Colorado residents and consequential decisions.

It does not set rules for research computing. Its list of systems that are not high-risk includes "DATA STORAGE", "NETWORKING" and "WEB HOSTING". The exception does not apply when such a system makes, or is a substantial factor in, a consequential decision. The Markdown copy breaks some lines into tables; the quotes follow the text order.
