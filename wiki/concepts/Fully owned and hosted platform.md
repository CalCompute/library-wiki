# Fully owned and hosted platform

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- **Statute.** (d)(1): "A fully owned and hosted cloud platform" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- **State cloud with outside models.** Poppy runs on "CDT-managed cloud" but sends queries to commercial models by API ([Poppy service page](../sources/Poppy%20service%20page.md)).
- **UC-owned baseline.** UC plans "UC-owned open source and open-weight infrastructure" ([UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16, p. 7](../sources/UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md)).
- **State cloud policy.** State entities use cloud services through CDT or buy them through DGS, keep data in "the Continental United States or Hawaii", and must have an exit strategy with a backup in a data center "unrelated to the cloud provider" ([SAM 4983.1 cloud computing (web)](../sources/SAM%204983.1%20cloud%20computing%20%28web%29.md)).
- **State-managed public cloud.** California Managed Cloud (CAMC) delivers "Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) through trusted providers like Azure and AWS". CDT secures the environment, and its users skip the state's cloud assessment ([CDT California Managed Cloud CAMC (web)](../sources/CDT%20California%20Managed%20Cloud%20CAMC%20%28web%29.md)). CDT's cloud services use "VMWare Virtual Cloud Foundation (VCF) Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)" ([CDT cloud services (web)](../sources/CDT%20cloud%20services%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Federal platform on a commercial cloud.** cloud.gov is a federal Platform as a Service that "runs on top of industry-provided infrastructure". In the capture of Aug. 14, 2025, Amazon Web Services (AWS) is the provider, and the platform is open source ([cloud.gov what is cloud.gov (web)](../sources/cloud.gov%20what%20is%20cloud.gov%20%28web%29.md)).
- **State-owned data center.** "The state-owned Rancho Cordova data center is approximately 42,000 square feet and serves as the State’s primary production data center". The Vacaville site is "leased space" ([CDT data center services (web)](../sources/CDT%20data%20center%20services%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Host owns partner-funded systems (Switzerland).** Under ETH Zurich's terms for CSCS, "HPC resources and HPC systems are in the inventory of ETH Zurich, regardless of any investments made by the partner" ([ETH Zurich RSETHZ 437.1 CSCS regulations (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20RSETHZ%20437.1%20CSCS%20regulations%20%28web%29.md), section 13).
- **Make or buy.** A 2021 Stanford HAI white paper says that "based solely on hardware costs, it is more cost-effective to own infrastructure when computing demand is close to continuous". It recommends negotiated commercial cloud first and "a pilot for public infrastructure" for the long run ([Stanford HAI National Research Cloud blueprint 2021 (web)](../sources/Stanford%20HAI%20National%20Research%20Cloud%20blueprint%202021%20%28web%29.md), p. 11).
- **Federal system in leased commercial space.** The LCCF puts its Horizon system in "32,000 square feet of customized datacenter space" under a colocation agreement with Sabey Inc. When NSF support ends, "the main data center will be returned to the colocation provider" ([NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](../sources/NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md), pp. MREFC-15 and MREFC-18). Under NSF cooperative agreements, title to equipment "will conditionally vest upon acquisition in the recipient" ([NSF cooperative agreement terms CA-FATC 2024-10 (web)](../sources/NSF%20cooperative%20agreement%20terms%20CA-FATC%202024-10%20%28web%29.md), Term 8.a).
- **Federated, not owned by the operator.** The federal NAIRR Task Force says its Operating Entity "should not itself operate the totality of the computer hardware". Partner resource providers deliver computing "as services" ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md), p. v).
- **National ownership.** "Only a small number of economies – primarily China and the US – can come close to achieving extensive domestic ownership" ([WEF AI Infrastructure in the Age of Sovereignty 2026 (web), Section 2.1, p. 10](../sources/WEF%20AI%20Infrastructure%20in%20the%20Age%20of%20Sovereignty%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Campus-owned GPU system.** Cal Poly says its AI Factory gives "full ownership of the data, models and applications developed on it" ([Cal Poly AI Factory release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20release%20%28web%29.md)). A professor there says it lets researchers train models locally on protected patient data ([Cal Poly AI Factory unveiling release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20unveiling%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Campus chatbot on a UC cloud contract.** UC Berkeley's Campus AI Sandbox runs "in the cloud using our UC Contract with AWS". Its models come under UC contracts ([Berkeley Campus AI Sandbox page (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20Campus%20AI%20Sandbox%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
