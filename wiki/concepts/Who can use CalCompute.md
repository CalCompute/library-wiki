# Who can use CalCompute

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- **Statute.** (b)(1) "Fostering research and innovation that benefits the public" and (b)(2) "Enabling equitable innovation by expanding access to computational resources" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- **Sponsors and author.** "academics and startups" ([SB-53 Assembly Floor analysis 2025-09-08](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Floor%20analysis%202025-09-08.md)). "free and low-cost access to compute for startups and academic researchers" ([Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md)). "AI researchers and smaller developers", with the community college system ([Wiener SB 294 announcement 2023-09-13](../sources/Wiener%20SB%20294%20announcement%202023-09-13.md)).
- **Ability to pay.** UC plans access that "does not depend on a campus's or laboratory's ability to pay" ([UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16, p. 7](../sources/UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md)).
