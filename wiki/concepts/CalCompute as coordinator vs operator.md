# CalCompute as coordinator vs operator

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- **Integrate, not build.** SDSC says the national plan is to "integrate leading existing capabilities rather than build isolated infrastructure" ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).
- **Operating Entity over federated providers.** The NAIRR Task Force plan gives daily operations to "an independent Operating Entity". That entity "should not itself operate the totality of the computer hardware"; partner resource providers deliver computing, data, testing and training "as services" ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md), p. v).
- **Empanelled cloud providers (India).** MeitY published a Request for Empanelment on August 16, 2024, "to empanel AI services on cloud". Other bidders join a GPU category "if they agree to match the L1 bid" ([PIB common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)](../sources/PIB%20common%20compute%20facility%20release%202025-01-30%20%28web%29.md)).
- **National coordinator.** NSF says the NAIRR Operations Center "will serve as the operational backbone of NAIRR" and "coordinate participating resource providers" ([NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)](../sources/NSF%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20update%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Council of separate awards (ACCESS).** The ACCESS Executive Council is "the primary governance body providing the necessary coordination and integration of the day-to-day execution of all ACCESS awards". The award principal investigators vote, and the NSF program officer arbitrates when the Council cannot agree ([ACCESS Executive Council charter (web)](../sources/ACCESS%20Executive%20Council%20charter%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Intermediary administrator for a federal consortium.** TechWerx, "a DOE partnership intermediary operated by RTI International", administers the Genesis Mission Consortium. DOE says it "will serve as a single, coordinated access point for members and their resources" ([DOE Genesis Mission Consortium launch release 2026-02-09 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20launch%20release%202026-02-09%20%28web%29.md)). A university member's connected resource "must be discoverable and accessible to Consortium members" ([Genesis Mission Consortium University Membership (web)](../sources/Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20University%20Membership%20%28web%29.md), p. 3).
- **Operator.** The statute requires "A fully owned and hosted cloud platform" and staff "to operate and maintain" it ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)). The 2025 Floor analysis expected UC "to operate CalCompute" if placed there ([2026-10-14 SB-53 Assembly Floor analysis, p. 7](../sources/2026-10-14%20SB-53%20Assembly%20Floor%20analysis.md)).
- **Publicly owned and operated (GovOps report).** The Government Operations Agency report of Nov. 16, 2026 says that "the need for publicly owned and operated compute has become more pressing as the demand for chips has risen" ([GovOps AI Safety in 2026 and Beyond (web), p. 7](../sources/GovOps%20AI%20Safety%20in%202026%20and%20Beyond%20%28web%29.md)). It recommends "standing up this compute cluster" ([GovOps AI Safety in 2026 and Beyond (web), p. 50](../sources/GovOps%20AI%20Safety%20in%202026%20and%20Beyond%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Lead body and host operator.** At the F3i Supercomputing Center, F3 Innovate leads, SDSC gives cyberinfrastructure, and "California State University, Fresno hosts and operates the physical compute environment" ([F3i Supercomputing Center (web)](../sources/F3i%20Supercomputing%20Center%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Shared center instead of own systems.** The F3i Supercomputing Center "provides shared access to powerful computing resources and technical support" instead of requiring users "to build their own computing systems"; Fresno State is its home ([SDSC F3i Supercomputing Center release 2026-07-16 (web)](../sources/SDSC%20F3i%20Supercomputing%20Center%20release%202026-07-16%20%28web%29.md)).
- **State owner, nonprofit operator.** New York law sets up "a state-owned research and computing facility" at the University at Buffalo. A not-for-profit consortium operates and manages it ([NY Economic Development Law 361 Empire AI (web)](../sources/NY%20Economic%20Development%20Law%20361%20Empire%20AI%20%28web%29.md), subds. 1(b) and 2).
- **Public owner, owned company as operator (Japan).** AIST owns ABCI, and AIST Solutions Co. (AISol) sells the service under its terms of use ([ABCI Agreement of Use 2026 Japanese (web)](../sources/ABCI%20Agreement%20of%20Use%202026%20Japanese%20%28web%29.md), Article 1). AIST set up AISol on April 1, 2023, as a company that it owns 100 percent ([AIST release AIST Solutions established 2023-04-01 (web)](../sources/AIST%20release%20AIST%20Solutions%20established%202023-04-01%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Regional hubs or a national hub.** A 2018 paper on JupyterHub says that a single national hub "is difficult to realize due to high coordination costs with thousands of universities". It says that "several regional hubs can reduce the burden of deployment and maintenance costs" ([SciPy Majora-4af1f417-002 JupyterHub paper (web)](../sources/SciPy%20Majora-4af1f417-002%20JupyterHub%20paper%20%28web%29.md), p. 17).
- **Central service on request.** In Canada's Syzygy model, "Any Canadian University can simply ask Syzygy for a JupyterHub and a new cluster will be set up" ([SciPy Majora-4af1f417-002 JupyterHub paper (web)](../sources/SciPy%20Majora-4af1f417-002%20JupyterHub%20paper%20%28web%29.md), p. 16).
- **State chancellor appoints, a university operates (Ohio).** Ohio's chancellor of higher education appoints consortia "to advise or participate in the development and operation of statewide collaborative efforts, including the Ohio supercomputer center". A designated college or university is each consortium's "fiscal agent, financial officer, and employer" ([Ohio Revised Code 3333.04 (web)](../sources/Ohio%20Revised%20Code%203333.04%20%28web%29.md), division (T)).
