# NAIRR and the National Research Platform

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- **Origin.** SB 294 built CalCompute on Stanford's National Research Cloud proposal, which led to NAIRR ([Wiener SB 294 announcement 2023-09-13](../sources/Wiener%20SB%20294%20announcement%202023-09-13.md)).
- **Free allocations.** ACCESS, another NSF program, gives compute "at no cost" ([ACCESS homepage](../sources/ACCESS%20homepage.md)).
