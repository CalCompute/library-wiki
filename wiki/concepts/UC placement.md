# UC placement

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- **Author's intent.** Scott Wiener's release says "housed at the University of California" ([Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md)). SB 294 said "housed in California's world-class public university system" ([Wiener SB 294 announcement 2023-09-13](../sources/Wiener%20SB%20294%20announcement%202023-09-13.md)).
- **Committee.** The July 2025 analysis says the consortium "must prioritize locating CalCompute within the University of California" ([SB-53 Assembly Privacy analysis 2025-07-08](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Privacy%20analysis%202025-07-08.md)).
- **Cost.** UC faces cost pressures "should it be established within the UC", offset "to some extent by private donations" ([2026-10-14 SB-53 Assembly Floor analysis, pp. 7-8](../sources/2026-10-14%20SB-53%20Assembly%20Floor%20analysis.md)).
