# Funding models

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- Under (j), UC may receive private donations only when CalCompute sits within UC ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- Executive Order N-6-26 lists programs that direct "a portion of revenue generated by AI companies" to public good ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
- ACCESS gives compute "at no cost" ([ACCESS homepage](../sources/ACCESS%20homepage.md)).
- **Consortium cost.** The Government Operations Agency estimated "$2.5 million" for the consortium's work ([SB-53 Assembly Floor analysis 2025-09-08](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Floor%20analysis%202025-09-08.md)).
