# Funding models

_This page reports what the sources say. It is not a position of the consortium._

## Positions

- Under (j), UC may receive private donations only when CalCompute sits within UC ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- Executive Order N-6-26 lists programs that direct "a portion of revenue generated by AI companies" to public good ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
- ACCESS gives compute "at no cost" ([ACCESS homepage](../sources/ACCESS%20homepage.md)).
- **Consortium cost.** The Government Operations Agency estimated "$2.5 million" for the consortium's work ([SB-53 Assembly Floor analysis 2025-09-08](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Floor%20analysis%202025-09-08.md)).
- **University central funding.** Arizona State University's Enterprise Technology funds standard use of its CreateAI platform within monthly project limits, and "ASU may evaluate additional funding, allocation, top-up, or departmental chargeback approaches" ([ASU CreateAI usage costs (web)](../sources/ASU%20CreateAI%20usage%20costs%20%28web%29.md)).
- **State-funded center with credits.** OSC "is always free for Ohio classroom usage, and academic researchers in Ohio qualify for credits that largely or completely offset fees" ([OSC getting started and fees (web)](../sources/OSC%20getting%20started%20and%20fees%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Public-private partnerships.** "Governments are increasingly co-investing in large-scale data centre and compute projects through PPPs" ([WEF Rethinking AI Sovereignty 2026 (web), Section 2.1, p. 11](../sources/WEF%20Rethinking%20AI%20Sovereignty%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Philanthropic gift.** "In 2020, The Robert N. Noyce Trust made a gift to the University of California system to launch the UC Noyce Initiative"; the page gives no amount ([UC Noyce Initiative homepage (web)](../sources/UC%20Noyce%20Initiative%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Partner network support.** CCST "operates in partnership with, as well as receives financial and mission support from, a network of public and private higher-education institutions and Federally funded research institutions" ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Nonprofit compute buyer.** Chips for Humanity says: "We are an independent nonprofit, so funders can give specifically for compute." ([Chips for Humanity homepage (web)](../sources/Chips%20for%20Humanity%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Pooled regional contributions with a federal share.** NSF's AI Infrastructure Hubs "leverage contributions from research institutions, philanthropic organizations, state and local governments, and the private sector". NSF invests in "each hub's consortium coordination, AI infrastructure workforce development and AI for science faculty training" ([NSF news AI Infrastructure Hubs (web)](../sources/NSF%20news%20AI%20Infrastructure%20Hubs%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Federal cooperative agreement.** The National Data Platform has a five-year, $24.5 million NSF Cooperative Agreement, one of two Category I awards in an "$83 million, agency-wide investment" ([SDSC National Data Platform release 2026-08-10 (web)](../sources/SDSC%20National%20Data%20Platform%20release%202026-08-10%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Shared-services fee.** For Sherlock, "the annual cost of participating in Sherlock’s shared-services model may be comparable to the cost of adding only a limited number of staff". The release gives no figure ([SDSC Sherlock FedRAMP release 2026-09-21 (web)](../sources/SDSC%20Sherlock%20FedRAMP%20release%202026-09-21%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Vendor in-kind support.** Under a 2024 MOU, "NVIDIA to help enable access to cutting-edge AI hardware, software, and cloud computing resources for educational and research initiatives". The release gives no amount ([Governor California NVIDIA collaboration 2024-08-09 (web)](../sources/Governor%20California%20NVIDIA%20collaboration%202024-08-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Discounted statewide licence.** State departments "purchase Claude at a 50% discount through a centralized procurement program", and cities and counties get "the same pricing" ([CDT Anthropic partnership expansion 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CDT%20Anthropic%20partnership%20expansion%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Startup cloud credits.** AWS Activate gives "promotional credits based on your startup's funding stage": up to $5,000 for a self-funded startup and up to $200,000 through an Activate Provider ([AWS Activate startup credits (web)](../sources/AWS%20Activate%20startup%20credits%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Cost-recovery fees.** The California Policy Lab is "funded mainly by foundation grants" and charges hosted projects fees that "merely recoup our costs"; UC approves the rates each year ([California Policy Lab hosted projects (web)](../sources/California%20Policy%20Lab%20hosted%20projects%20%28web%29.md)).
