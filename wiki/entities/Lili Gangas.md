# Lili Gangas

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: Kapor Center
- Title: Chief Technology Community Officer, Kapor Center
- Role: Co-leads the Kapor Center's Equitable Tech Policy Initiative on closing the digital divide, responsible AI, and new tech workforce models

## Background

- At the Kapor Center she co-leads the Equitable Tech Policy Initiative. Its interests include the digital divide and "scaling new tech workforce models" ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- Before Kapor she worked at Accenture Technology Lab, on the Booz Allen Innovation Services team, and as a software engineer at Raytheon ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She holds an MBA from New York University Stern and a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Southern California ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
