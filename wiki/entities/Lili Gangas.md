# Lili Gangas

## Bottom line

- Lili Gangas is Chief Technology Community Officer at the Kapor Center ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She co-leads the Kapor Center's Equitable Tech Policy Initiative ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Kapor Center](../entities/Kapor%20Center.md): Chief Technology Community Officer ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- Kapor Center, Equitable Tech Policy Initiative: co-lead ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).

## Background

- The interests of the Equitable Tech Policy Initiative include the digital divide and "scaling new tech workforce models" ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- In 2024 the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) named her to its Consumer Advisory Committee ([FCC Consumer Advisory Committee twelfth term members (web)](../sources/FCC%20Consumer%20Advisory%20Committee%20twelfth%20term%20members%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Kapor Center profile lists her in the FCC Diversity & Inclusion working group ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- The profile lists her in the Aspen Latinos & Society Digital Equity working group ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- The profile lists her in the Aspen Fair Data Future working group ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- The profile lists her in the Rock Health Latine Digital Health Innovators working group ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- The Unity Council lists her on its Board of Directors ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was an associate principal on the Open Innovation team of Accenture Technology Lab, in Silicon Valley ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was a founding member of the Innovation Services team at Booz Allen ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She was a Senior Multi-Disciplined Software Engineer at Raytheon Space and Airborne Systems ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She holds an MBA from New York University Stern School of Business ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She holds a B.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Southern California ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- The Kapor Center profile lists a fellowship in Omidyar Network's Digital Luminaries ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md)).
- She was a New America California fellow, with a focus on Tech for Good ([Gangas Kapor Center profile](../sources/Gangas%20Kapor%20Center%20profile.md); [Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The SF Business Times named her to its Most Influential Women in Business and 40 Under 40 lists ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is an MBA lecturer at Mills College ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She helped organize the first TEDxOakland ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She helped launch the Women's, Impact and Latinx in Tech editions of Startup Weekend ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is an advisor to the nonprofits AI-4-All.org and 1Degree.org ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is an immigrant from Bolivia ([Gangas Unity Council board page (web)](../sources/Gangas%20Unity%20Council%20board%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
