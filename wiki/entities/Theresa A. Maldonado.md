# Theresa A. Maldonado

## Bottom line

- Theresa A. Maldonado is the systemwide Vice President for Research & Innovation at the University of California Office of the President (UCOP). She is the UC system's chief research officer ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The page says she leads initiatives across the 10 UC campuses, the three UC-managed national laboratories of the U.S. Department of Energy, UC Agriculture & Natural Resources and UC Health ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- University of California Office of the President, Research & Innovation ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).
- American Association for the Advancement of Science (AAAS): current President ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- She served two terms at the National Science Foundation, including as Division Director in the Engineering Directorate ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is a registered Professional Engineer in Texas ([UCOP Research and Innovation VP (web)](../sources/UCOP%20Research%20and%20Innovation%20VP%20%28web%29.md)).
