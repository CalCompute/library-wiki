# CloudBank

## Bottom line

- CloudBank is a collaboration of UC San Diego, UC Berkeley and the University of Washington. It gives access to public cloud resources, "funded via the National Science Foundation’s (NSF) Cloud Access program" ([CloudBank Acceptable Use Policy (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- CloudBank "is available to any U.S. researcher or educator through the ACCESS and NAIRR Pilot programs". It gives allocations on Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud, IBM Cloud and Microsoft Azure ([CloudBank FAQ (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
- UC San Diego manages CloudBank and "does not charge indirect costs (IDC) on cloud resources" ([CloudBank FAQ (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its Acceptable Use Policy bans use "for commercial purposes, financial gain, personal gain, or any unlawful purpose". Users get notices at 20%, 10% and 5% of their allocation ([CloudBank Acceptable Use Policy (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20Acceptable%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [San Diego Supercomputer Center](San%20Diego%20Supercomputer%20Center.md): one of the four partners in the NSF Cooperative Agreement ([CloudBank FAQ (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
- [U.S. National Science Foundation](U.S.%20National%20Science%20Foundation.md): NSF supports CloudBank under awards 1925001 and 2505560 ([CloudBank FAQ (web)](../sources/CloudBank%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
