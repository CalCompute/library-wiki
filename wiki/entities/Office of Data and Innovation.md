# Office of Data and Innovation

## Bottom line

- The California Office of Data and Innovation (ODI) gives state departments data science, design and technology help. It trains state employees as Governor's Innovation Fellows ([ODI homepage (web)](../sources/ODI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md); [Bobra BIDS profile (web)](../sources/Bobra%20BIDS%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- ODI trains current state staff through its fellows program ([ODI homepage (web)](../sources/ODI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- ODI "provides standards for agencies to open their data and software" ([Bobra BIDS profile (web)](../sources/Bobra%20BIDS%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
