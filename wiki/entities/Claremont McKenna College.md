# Claremont McKenna College

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as Claremont McKenna College in Claremont, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Special Focus: Arts and Sciences" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Private not-for-profit" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- NSF award 2408259 (2024), $918,485, lets Claremont McKenna College and Harvey Mudd College "acquire a high-performance computing (HPC) cluster" ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
