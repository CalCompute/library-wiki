# SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory

## Bottom line

- SLAC is a national laboratory in Menlo Park, California, that "is operated by Stanford University for the U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science" ([SLAC Genesis Mission phase II projects (web)](../sources/SLAC%20Genesis%20Mission%20phase%20II%20projects%20%28web%29.md)).
- SLAC is a partner in seven Genesis Mission Phase II projects ([SLAC Genesis Mission phase II projects (web)](../sources/SLAC%20Genesis%20Mission%20phase%20II%20projects%20%28web%29.md)). It connects to CalREN as a research organization ([CENIC research education and cultural community (web)](../sources/CENIC%20research%20education%20and%20cultural%20community%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: Stanford's SRCF-II data center on the SLAC grounds "will double the current data center capabilities, for a total of 6 megawatts of power capacity" ([SLAC computing infrastructure expansion 2022-07-27 (web)](../sources/SLAC%20computing%20infrastructure%20expansion%202022-07-27%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element A: The SLAC Shared Science Data Facility (S3DF) "will be able to support 80% of SLAC's computing needs" ([SLAC computing infrastructure expansion 2022-07-27 (web)](../sources/SLAC%20computing%20infrastructure%20expansion%202022-07-27%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element B: "Large parts of the S3DF infrastructure are funded by the Department of Energy's Office of Science." ([SLAC computing infrastructure expansion 2022-07-27 (web)](../sources/SLAC%20computing%20infrastructure%20expansion%202022-07-27%20%28web%29.md))
