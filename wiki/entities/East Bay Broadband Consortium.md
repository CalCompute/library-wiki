# East Bay Broadband Consortium

![East Bay Broadband Consortium](../../raw/assets/logos/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium.png)

_Logo: East Bay Broadband Consortium, [homepage](https://www.eastbaybroadband.org/). Fetched 2026-11-13._

## Bottom line

- The East Bay Broadband Consortium (EBBC) works with community organizations, local governments and other partners to close the digital divide. It works in Alameda, Contra Costa and Solano counties ([East Bay Broadband Consortium homepage (web)](../sources/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- Patrick Messac is its Director, and Cinthia Diaz Medrano is its Deputy Director ([East Bay Broadband Consortium team (web)](../sources/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium%20team%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It works to improve broadband affordability and availability, to expand device access and to "Facilitate Regional Collaboration" (A, F) ([East Bay Broadband Consortium homepage (web)](../sources/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- It helps public agencies, community organizations, housing partners and broadband providers find grants and prepare applications. It lists five California Advanced Services Fund (CASF) programs (B, F) ([East Bay Broadband Consortium funding (web)](../sources/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium%20funding%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its team page lists seven people besides its two staff. They come from East Bay city and county governments, a county library, the Kapor Center and Tech Exchange (F) ([East Bay Broadband Consortium team (web)](../sources/East%20Bay%20Broadband%20Consortium%20team%20%28web%29.md)).
