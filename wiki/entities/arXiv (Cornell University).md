# arXiv (Cornell University)

## Bottom line

- arXiv is an open preprint server for research papers. Each record gives an arXiv identifier, subject classes and an arXiv-issued DOI (Digital Object Identifier) "via DataCite" ([Kwon et al 2023 PagedAttention arXiv record (web)](../sources/Kwon%20et%20al%202023%20PagedAttention%20arXiv%20record%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- arXiv holds the preprint of the PagedAttention paper, which presents the vLLM serving system ([Kwon et al 2023 PagedAttention arXiv record (web)](../sources/Kwon%20et%20al%202023%20PagedAttention%20arXiv%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- arXiv holds the GovAI whitepaper on the governance roles of compute providers: "securers, record keepers, verifiers, and, in some cases, even enforcers" ([Heim et al 2024 Governing Through the Cloud (web)](../sources/Heim%20et%20al%202024%20Governing%20Through%20the%20Cloud%20%28web%29.md)).
- arXiv holds the OpenRouter and a16z study of one commercial inference platform: "over 100 trillion tokens of real-world LLM interactions" ([Aubakirova et al 2025 OpenRouter State of AI](../sources/OpenRouter%20State%20of%20AI%20100%20Trillion%20Token%20Study%20%28web%29.md)).
