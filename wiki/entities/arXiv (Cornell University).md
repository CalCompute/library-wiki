# arXiv (Cornell University)

## Bottom line

- arXiv is an open preprint server for research papers. Each record gives an arXiv identifier, subject classes and an arXiv-issued DOI (Digital Object Identifier) "via DataCite" ([Kwon et al 2023 PagedAttention arXiv record (web)](../sources/Kwon%20et%20al%202023%20PagedAttention%20arXiv%20record%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- arXiv holds the preprint of the PagedAttention paper, which presents the vLLM serving system ([Kwon et al 2023 PagedAttention arXiv record (web)](../sources/Kwon%20et%20al%202023%20PagedAttention%20arXiv%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
