# California Department of Technology

## Bottom line

- The California Department of Technology (CDT) runs Poppy, the state's own generative AI platform for state staff.
- Chris Given moved from GovOps to CDT as Director on April 29, 2026 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Poppy is a public sector platform "built by state staff for state staff". Its pilot started in Sept. 2025 with more than 2,800 employees in 67 departments ([Poppy overview](../sources/Poppy%20overview.md); [Poppy service page](../sources/Poppy%20service%20page.md)).
- Its request process needs approval by the agency's information officer and a user list ([Poppy service page](../sources/Poppy%20service%20page.md)).
- Poppy runs on "CDT-managed cloud" but sends queries to commercial models. This tests what "A fully owned and hosted cloud platform" means ([Poppy service page](../sources/Poppy%20service%20page.md)).
- The Governor appointed Ayushi Roy State Chief Technology Officer on Aug. 19, 2026 ([Governor appointments 2026-08-19](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-08-19.md)). She designed IT systems for public benefits at New America. Chris Given names "legacy-system modernization, digital identity and artificial intelligence" as early priorities ([StateScoop California CTO Ayushi Roy](../sources/StateScoop%20California%20CTO%20Ayushi%20Roy.md)).

- CDT oversees "statewide IT strategic planning, project delivery, procurement, policy and standards, and enterprise architecture". It is "Home to the State Data Center", and its Director is the State CIO ([CDT About the Department](../sources/CDT%20About%20the%20Department.md)).
- Its Training & Education Center runs IT leadership academies and a "GenAI Boot Camp" for public sector staff ([CDT workforce development](../sources/CDT%20workforce%20development.md)). Its AI community of practice meets about every two months, with UC speakers ([CDT AI community of practice](../sources/CDT%20AI%20community%20of%20practice.md)).
- State IT jobs map to a few classifications, Information Technology Technician to Manager II. "Machine Learning Engineer" and "Cloud Architect" are state titles ([CDT IT Classification Mapping](../sources/CDT%20IT%20Classification%20Mapping.md); [CDT career opportunities](../sources/CDT%20career%20opportunities.md)).
- CDT's new SITeS portal offers Anthropic's Claude to state agencies ([GovOps more efficient California](../sources/GovOps%20more%20efficient%20California.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Government Operations Agency](Government%20Operations%20Agency.md): CDT is one of the 16 state departments and offices under its "direct responsibility" ([GovOps homepage](../sources/GovOps%20homepage.md)).
