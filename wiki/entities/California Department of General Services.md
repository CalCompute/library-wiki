# California Department of General Services

## Bottom line

- The California Department of General Services (DGS) publishes the State Administrative Manual, which holds the state's Cloud Computing Policy, SAM 4983.1. State entities buy cloud services that the California Department of Technology does not offer through DGS ([SAM 4983.1 cloud computing (web)](../sources/SAM%204983.1%20cloud%20computing%20%28web%29.md)).
