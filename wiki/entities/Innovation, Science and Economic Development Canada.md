# Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada

## Bottom line

- Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED) is a department of the Government of Canada. It publishes the Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy ([Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (web)](../sources/Canadian%20Sovereign%20AI%20Compute%20Strategy%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The strategy rests on "$2 billion over five years, starting in 2024–25" from Budget 2024 ([Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (web)](../sources/Canadian%20Sovereign%20AI%20Compute%20Strategy%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives up to $700 million to commercial AI data centres and up to $1 billion to public supercomputing ([Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (web)](../sources/Canadian%20Sovereign%20AI%20Compute%20Strategy%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives up to $300 million to an AI Compute Access Fund ([Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (web)](../sources/Canadian%20Sovereign%20AI%20Compute%20Strategy%20%28web%29.md)).
- The public element includes a "Canadian-owned and located supercomputing system" ([Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy (web)](../sources/Canadian%20Sovereign%20AI%20Compute%20Strategy%20%28web%29.md)).
