# Kim Hazelwood

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: Empire AI
- Title: At-Large Board Member, Empire AI
- Role: Sits on the board of a public AI computing consortium already running in another state, the governance and cost questions CalCompute faces in practice

## Background

- Empire AI named her an At-Large Board Member on Oct. 8, 2025. Its board then had 16 members, a size and mix set by New York law ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md)).
- Empire AI's governance page lists her as "Tech Executive" ([Hazelwood Empire AI governance](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20governance.md)).
- Her career includes a tenured post at the University of Virginia and leadership roles at Google, Yahoo and Meta. She contributed to the Google TPU and the Facebook AI Research Supercluster ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She holds a PhD in Computer Science from Harvard. She served on the Computing Research Association board from 2017 to 2025 ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).
- Her papers include "Sustainable AI" (2022) and "Applied machine learning at Facebook: A datacenter infrastructure perspective" (2018) ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the Empire AI release lists past roles at "Meta, Intel, and Yahoo Labs". The NC State page lists "Google, Yahoo, and Meta" ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md); [Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
