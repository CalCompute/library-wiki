# Kim Hazelwood

## Bottom line

- Kim Hazelwood is an At-Large Board Member of Empire AI, a New York consortium for AI computing ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md)).
- NC State honored her in 2025 as "A globally recognized leader in AI infrastructure and AI systems research" ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Empire AI](../entities/Empire%20AI.md) named her an At-Large Board Member on Oct. 8, 2025 ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md)).
- The Empire AI board then had 16 members, a size and mix set by New York law ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md)).
- Empire AI's governance page lists her as "Tech Executive" ([Hazelwood Empire AI governance](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20governance.md)).
- She served on the Computing Research Association board from 2017 to 2025 ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).

## Background

- She was a tenured associate professor of computer science at the University of Virginia ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She held technical and executive leadership roles at [Google](../entities/Google.md), Yahoo and Meta ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She contributed to the Pin Dynamic Binary Instrumentation tool and the Google Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She also contributed to the Facebook AI Research Supercluster ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She holds a PhD in Computer Science from Harvard University ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).
- Her site says she has "authored over 70 peer-reviewed publications and a book" ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).
- Her papers include "Sustainable AI" (2022) and "Applied machine learning at Facebook: A datacenter infrastructure perspective" (2018) ([Hazelwood personal site](../sources/Hazelwood%20personal%20site.md)).
- She was the principal investigator of a CAREER award at the University of Virginia, from 2008 to 2012 ([Hazelwood NSF CAREER award 0747273 (web)](../sources/Hazelwood%20NSF%20CAREER%20award%200747273%20%28web%29.md)).
- The National Science Foundation (NSF) CAREER award, 0747273, was on hardware and software collaborative design ([Hazelwood NSF CAREER award 0747273 (web)](../sources/Hazelwood%20NSF%20CAREER%20award%200747273%20%28web%29.md)).
- Her awards include the 2025 Top 100 Women in AI Award ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She received the MIT Technology Review Top 35 Innovators Under 35 Award ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- Her awards also include the ACM SIGPLAN 10-Year Test of Time Award and the Anita Borg Early Career Award ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- She served on advisory boards for MIT SystemsThatLearn, EPFL EcoCloud and Bruin AI ([Hazelwood NC State ECE honor](../sources/Hazelwood%20NC%20State%20ECE%20honor.md)).
- The Empire AI release says she served on advisory councils for the NSF and the National Academy of Sciences ([Hazelwood Empire AI board appointment 2025-10-08](../sources/Hazelwood%20Empire%20AI%20board%20appointment%202025-10-08.md)).
