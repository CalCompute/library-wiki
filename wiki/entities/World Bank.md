# World Bank

## Bottom line

- The World Bank Group "provides financing, advice, and innovative solutions" in "more than 100 countries" ([World Bank Morocco digital transformation release 2026-06-12 (web)](../sources/World%20Bank%20Morocco%20digital%20transformation%20release%202026-06-12%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- On June 12, 2026, its Board approved two programs for Morocco with a total of $650 million ([World Bank Morocco digital transformation release 2026-06-12 (web)](../sources/World%20Bank%20Morocco%20digital%20transformation%20release%202026-06-12%20%28web%29.md)).
- The $250 million digital program supports "AI-based digital innovation capacity through centers of excellence" ([World Bank Morocco digital transformation release 2026-06-12 (web)](../sources/World%20Bank%20Morocco%20digital%20transformation%20release%202026-06-12%20%28web%29.md)).
