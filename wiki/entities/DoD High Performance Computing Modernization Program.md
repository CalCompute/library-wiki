# DoD High Performance Computing Modernization Program

## Bottom line

- The High Performance Computing Modernization Program (HPCMP) is a program of the Department of War, formerly the Department of Defense (DoD). It started in fiscal year 1992. Its office is at the Engineer Research and Development Center in Vicksburg, Mississippi ([DoD HPCMP program history (web)](../sources/DoD%20HPCMP%20program%20history%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element C: Congress "mandated" the modernization in 1990. The program moved from the Office of the Secretary of Defense to the Assistant Secretary of the Army in 2011 ([DoD HPCMP program history (web)](../sources/DoD%20HPCMP%20program%20history%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element C: The program works through "five DoW Supercomputing Resource Centers (DSRCs)" and "four Affiliated Resource Centers (ARCs)" ([DoD HPCMP computation centers (web)](../sources/DoD%20HPCMP%20computation%20centers%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element F: The latest supercomputers in all five centers came "from Liqid and Penguin Computing" ([DoD HPCMP computation centers (web)](../sources/DoD%20HPCMP%20computation%20centers%20%28web%29.md)).
