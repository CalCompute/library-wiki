# UAW Local 4811

## Bottom line

- UAW Local 4811 represents UC's academic student employees (BX), graduate student researchers (BR) and postdoctoral scholars (PX).
- The BX and BR contract runs from March 20, 2026 to Dec. 31, 2029 ([UC UAW BX BR contract 2026-2029 (web)](../sources/UC%20UAW%20BX%20BR%20contract%202026-2029%20%28web%29.md)). The PX contract runs from Dec. 9, 2022 to Sept. 30, 2027 ([UC UAW PX postdoc contract 2022-2027 (web)](../sources/UC%20UAW%20PX%20postdoc%20contract%202022-2027%20%28web%29.md)).
