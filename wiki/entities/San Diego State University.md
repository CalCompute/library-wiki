# San Diego State University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as San Diego State University in San Diego, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- SDSU hosts TIDE (Technology Infrastructure for Data Exploration), National Science Foundation (NSF) award 2346701. TIDE adds 72 GPUs to the Nautilus cluster, with preferential scheduling for California State University (CSU) users ([SDSU TIDE technology (web)](../sources/SDSU%20TIDE%20technology%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2022, SDSU and CSU San Bernardino received a five-year, $6.7 million NSF grant for cyberinfrastructure staff ([CSUSB high performance computing article (web)](../sources/CSUSB%20high%20performance%20computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
