# Campus Research Computing Consortium

## Bottom line

The Campus Research Computing Consortium (CaRCC) has a working group that "provides ongoing development and community support for the Capabilities Model" ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20%28web%29.md)). It developed the Research Computing and Data (RCD) Capabilities Model with Internet2 and EDUCAUSE ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Elements A and C: an institution uses the Capabilities Model "as an input into strategic decision making and organizational planning" ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element E: one of the five areas of the model covers "professional staff development" ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- National Science Foundation (NSF) awards OAC-1620695 and OAC-2100003 support the work ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20%28web%29.md)).
- The working group plans version 3 of the model ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20%28web%29.md)).
