# U.S. Environmental Protection Agency

## Bottom line

- The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) publishes facility records in its Facility Registry Service (FRS). Each record gives a facility's address, county, latitude and longitude ([EPA FRS facility record LLNL (web)](../sources/EPA%20FRS%20facility%20record%20LLNL%20%28web%29.md)).
