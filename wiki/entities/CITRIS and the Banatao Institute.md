# CITRIS and the Banatao Institute

## Bottom line

- CITRIS (the Center for Information Technology Research in the Interest of Society) is a four-campus UC institute at Berkeley, Davis, Merced and Santa Cruz. The Legislature created it in 2001 as one of four Institutes for Science and Innovation ([CITRIS About (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20About%20%28web%29.md); [CITRIS mission and history (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20mission%20and%20history%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It is a state-created, multi-campus research institute inside UC. The state, UC and businesses funded it at the start ([CITRIS About (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20About%20%28web%29.md); [CITRIS mission and history (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20mission%20and%20history%20%28web%29.md)). The UC Santa Cruz unit has its own page: [CITRIS and the Banatao Institute, UC Santa Cruz](CITRIS%20and%20the%20Banatao%20Institute%2C%20UC%20Santa%20Cruz.md).
- Education Code § 92900 lets the Regents establish four California Institutes for Science and Innovation. A panel that the Governor selected chose them. Section 92901(e) requires two nonstate dollars for each state dollar ([Ed Code 92900-92901 Cal ISI (web)](../sources/Ed%20Code%2092900-92901%20Cal%20ISI.md)).
- The LAO says the state "eliminated this earmark and folded the associated funds into UC's main appropriation". UC "continues to allocate this amount of General Fund to the institutes" ([LAO 2021-22 Cal Institutes for Science and Innovation (web)](../sources/LAO%202021-22%20Cal%20Institutes%20for%20Science%20and%20Innovation%20%28web%29.md)).
- Director Alexandre Bayen; Executive Director Camille Crittenden; Campus Directors at UC Davis, UC Merced and UC Santa Cruz ([CITRIS leadership (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20leadership%20%28web%29.md)).
- A 16-member advisory board, mostly from companies and investment firms, with the State CIO and former Governor Gray Davis ([CITRIS advisory board (web)](../sources/CITRIS%20advisory%20board%20%28web%29.md)).
