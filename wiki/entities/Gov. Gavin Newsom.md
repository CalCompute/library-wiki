# Gov. Gavin Newsom

## Bottom line

- Gavin Newsom is the 40th Governor of California. Voters elected him on Nov. 6, 2018 ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Office of the Governor](Office%20of%20the%20Governor.md): Governor ([Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29](../sources/Newsom%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29.md)).

## Background

- He was born in San Francisco in 1967 ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He attended Redwood High School and Santa Clara University ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- After college, he started a retail wine shop that grew into the PlumpJack Group ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- His first job in politics was on the San Francisco Parking and Traffic Commission. He then served on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2003, he "became the youngest elected Mayor of San Francisco in a century" ([Newsom Governor about page (web)](../sources/Newsom%20Governor%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2023, he signed an executive order on state generative AI (GenAI) procurement ([Governor ICYMI 32 of top 50 AI companies 2025-03-12 (web)](../sources/Governor%20ICYMI%2032%20of%20top%2050%20AI%20companies%202025-03-12%20%28web%29.md)).
- In August 2024, the state started an AI collaboration with NVIDIA. The Governor and NVIDIA's founder, Jensen Huang, signed it ([Governor AI working group release 2025-03-18](../sources/Governor%20AI%20working%20group%20release%202025-03-18.md)).
- On Sept. 29, 2024, he returned SB 1047 to the Senate without his signature ([Newsom SB 1047 veto message 2024-09-29 (web)](../sources/Newsom%20SB%201047%20veto%20message%202024-09-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- His veto message says that SB 1047 "could give the public a false sense of security about controlling this fast-moving technology" ([Newsom SB 1047 veto message 2024-09-29 (web)](../sources/Newsom%20SB%201047%20veto%20message%202024-09-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- He asked a group of AI experts to study frontier models. Its draft report came out on March 18, 2025 ([Governor AI working group release 2025-03-18](../sources/Governor%20AI%20working%20group%20release%202025-03-18.md)).
- The lead authors of that report are Fei-Fei Li, Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar and Jennifer Tour Chayes ([Governor AI working group release 2025-03-18](../sources/Governor%20AI%20working%20group%20release%202025-03-18.md)).
- He signed SB 53 on Sept. 29, 2025 ([Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29](../sources/Newsom%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29.md)).
- On signing, he said: "California has proven that we can establish regulations to protect our communities while also ensuring that the growing AI industry continues to thrive. This legislation strikes that balance." ([Newsom SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29](../sources/Newsom%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29.md)).
- His signing message says that SB 53 "will establish state-level oversight" of advanced AI systems ([SB-53 signing message 2025-09-29](../sources/SB-53%20signing%20message%202025-09-29.md)).
- On May 21, 2026, he signed Executive Order N-6-26, on AI and the workforce ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
- His office says that California "is home to 32 of 50 top AI companies worldwide" ([Governor ICYMI 32 of top 50 AI companies 2025-03-12 (web)](../sources/Governor%20ICYMI%2032%20of%20top%2050%20AI%20companies%202025-03-12%20%28web%29.md)).
- His office says: "In 2024, 15.7% of all U.S. AI job postings were in California" ([Governor TIME Person of the Year made in California 2025-12-12 (web)](../sources/Governor%20TIME%20Person%20of%20the%20Year%20made%20in%20California%202025-12-12%20%28web%29.md)).
