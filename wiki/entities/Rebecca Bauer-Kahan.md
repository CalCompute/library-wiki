# Rebecca Bauer-Kahan

## Bottom line

Rebecca Bauer-Kahan represents Assembly District 16 in the California State Assembly. Voters first elected her in 2018 ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- As Chair of the Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection, she "is helping California lead the way on regulating artificial intelligence tools" (C) ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

- She wrote AB 1405, Chapter 178 of 2026, which creates an AI Auditor Registry at the Government Operations Agency (C) ([AB 1405 bill text](../sources/AB%201405%20bill%20text.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Assembly](California%20State%20Assembly.md): Assemblymember, District 16 ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- [Assembly Committee on Privacy and Consumer Protection](Assembly%20Committee%20on%20Privacy%20and%20Consumer%20Protection.md): Chair ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- Founder and Chair of the Select Committee on Reproductive Health; former Chair of the Committee on Water, Parks, and Wildlife ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- She worked as an environmental attorney and taught law at Santa Clara University and Golden Gate University ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is a graduate of the University of Pennsylvania and Georgetown University Law Center ([Bauer-Kahan Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Bauer-Kahan%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
