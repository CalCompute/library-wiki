# Texas Advanced Computing Center

## Bottom line

- The Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) is a research computing center at The University of Texas at Austin (UT Austin) ([TACC user policies (web)](../sources/TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its Acceptable Use Policy lets a principal investigator (PI) from "a US domiciled institution" lead a project, with "non-profit or for-profit entities" included. Users outside the United States can join "if affiliated with a US PI" ([TACC user policies (web)](../sources/TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).
- The policy says that data made on TACC systems is "the property of the PI and Project" ([TACC user policies (web)](../sources/TACC%20user%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).
- TACC is the partner of the San Diego Supercomputer Center in the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Operations Center ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).
- TACC leads the NSF Leadership-Class Computing Facility (LCCF), whose core system is Horizon. NSF's cooperative agreement with UT Austin has an estimated total of $457,447,968 ([NSF award 2323116 LCCF construction API record (web)](../sources/NSF%20award%202323116%20LCCF%20construction%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- A panel of independent experts reviews Horizon allocation requests, and NSF gives "final approval" ([TACC LCCF Horizon allocations (web)](../sources/TACC%20LCCF%20Horizon%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)).
- NSF oversees the LCCF through a Program Officer and an Integrated Project Team. A Technology Advisory Board and a Science Advisory Board advise the Project Director ([NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](../sources/NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md), p. MREFC-17).

## Affiliations

- [San Diego Supercomputer Center](San%20Diego%20Supercomputer%20Center.md): its partner in the NAIRR Operations Center ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).

## Background

- Sabey Inc. gives the data center space for Horizon. The colocation agreement dates from August 2024 ([NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](../sources/NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md), p. MREFC-15).
