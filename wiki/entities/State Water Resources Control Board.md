# State Water Resources Control Board

## Bottom line

- The State Water Resources Control Board and nine Regional Water Quality Control Boards are the California Water Boards. They sit under the California Environmental Protection Agency ([State Water Resources Control Board About (web)](../sources/State%20Water%20Resources%20Control%20Board%20About%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It is an example of a state board with regional boards under a cabinet agency. It acts under a federal and a state statute ([State Water Resources Control Board About (web)](../sources/State%20Water%20Resources%20Control%20Board%20About%20%28web%29.md)).
