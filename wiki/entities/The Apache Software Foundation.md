# The Apache Software Foundation

## Bottom line

- The Apache Software Foundation (ASF) is "a not-for-profit membership corporation organized under Delaware law" ([Apache Software Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Apache%20Software%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article VIII). Its site says that it "is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization."

## Why they matter

- Members join only "by a majority vote of the existing members" ([Apache Software Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Apache%20Software%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Section 4.1).
- The members elect the Board of Directors each year ([Apache Software Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Apache%20Software%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Section 5.5).
- The Board sets up Project Management Committees, each responsible for "the active management of one or more projects" ([Apache Software Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Apache%20Software%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Section 6.3).
- The ASF owns the trademarks of all its projects "on behalf of our project communities" and calls itself "a vendor-neutral organization" ([Apache trademark policy (web)](../sources/Apache%20trademark%20policy%20%28web%29.md)).
