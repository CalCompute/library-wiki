# Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System

## Bottom line

The Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System publishes the Beige Book eight times a year. The report covers economic conditions in the 12 Federal Reserve Districts ([Federal Reserve Beige Book FAQs (web)](../sources/Federal%20Reserve%20Beige%20Book%20FAQs%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The Beige Book uses "mostly qualitative information, gathered directly from each District’s sources" ([Federal Reserve Beige Book FAQs (web)](../sources/Federal%20Reserve%20Beige%20Book%20FAQs%20%28web%29.md)). (A)
- The Board lists seven Beige Book reports for 2026, from January 14 to October 14 ([Federal Reserve Beige Book main page (web)](../sources/Federal%20Reserve%20Beige%20Book%20main%20page%20%28web%29.md)). (A)
