# Government Operations Agency

## Bottom line

- The Government Operations Agency (GovOps) is the state agency that § 11546.8 names for the consortium's Report. It also owes the Governor options on public-good AI under Executive Order N-6-26.
- Sarah Soto-Taylor became Undersecretary in October 2024 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)). Chris Given left GovOps to lead CDT on April 29, 2026 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- AB 1405 makes the agency set up an AI Auditor Registry on its website by Jan. 1, 2029, and fix registration fees ([AB 1405 bill text](../sources/AB%201405%20bill%20text.md)).
- SB 813 makes the agency set application requirements and criteria for independent verification organizations by Jan. 1, 2028, and convene working groups ([SB 813 bill text](../sources/SB%20813%20bill%20text.md)).
- The Governor's release of Aug. 10, 2026 on the AI Cyber Defense Program quotes Secretary Nick Maduros ([Governor AI Cyber Defense Program release (web)](../sources/Governor%20AI%20Cyber%20Defense%20Program%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
