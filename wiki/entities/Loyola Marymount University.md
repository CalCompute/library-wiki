# Loyola Marymount University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as Loyola Marymount University in Los Angeles, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Private not-for-profit" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It is one of the campuses on the SoCal Science DMZ network of the Los Nettos Regional Network, NSF award 2126319 (2021) ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
