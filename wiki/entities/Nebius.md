# Nebius

## Bottom line

Nebius is a company. Its research grants program gives researchers GPU cloud credits, Token Factory tokens and Tavily credits ([Nebius research grants (web)](../sources/Nebius%20research%20grants%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its research grants give "free access to GPU cloud, AI models, and Tavily’s agentic search engine". Academic and nonprofit researchers can apply ([Nebius research grants (web)](../sources/Nebius%20research%20grants%20%28web%29.md)).
