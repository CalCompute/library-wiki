# Stephanie Lieggi

## Bottom line

- Stephanie Lieggi is Executive Director of the Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) at UC Santa Cruz ([CROSS About](../sources/CROSS%20About.md)).
- She is also Executive Director of the UC Santa Cruz Open Source Program Office (OSPO) ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).

## Affiliations

- UC Santa Cruz, [CROSS](../entities/CROSS.md): Executive Director ([CROSS About](../sources/CROSS%20About.md)).
- [UC Santa Cruz Open Source Program Office](../entities/UC%20Santa%20Cruz%20Open%20Source%20Program%20Office.md): Executive Director ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- CHAOSS University Working Group, on metrics: co-chair ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).

## Background

- She joined CROSS at UC Santa Cruz in 2016 ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- She "led the effort to create an OSPO Network in the University of California system" ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)). See [UC OSPO Network](../entities/UC%20OSPO%20Network.md).
- She was a co-principal investigator (co-PI) on UC Santa Cruz's first POSE grant. POSE is the National Science Foundation (NSF) Pathways to Enable Open Source Ecosystems program ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- Before CROSS, she was a senior researcher and adjunct professor at the Center for Nonproliferation Studies ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- The Center for Nonproliferation Studies is part of the Middlebury Institute of International Studies at Monterey ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- Her research there was on national security and global trade ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- She is the principal investigator of NSF award 2226407, Repeto, on reproducibility in experimental computer science ([Lieggi NSF award 2226407 (web)](../sources/Lieggi%20NSF%20award%202226407%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Repeto award to UC Santa Cruz is $929,913, from Oct. 1, 2022 to Sept. 30, 2026 ([Lieggi NSF award 2226407 (web)](../sources/Lieggi%20NSF%20award%202226407%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is a co-PI of NSF award 2347360, Beginnings ([Lieggi NSF award 2347360 (web)](../sources/Lieggi%20NSF%20award%202347360%20%28web%29.md)).
- Beginnings is an open source learning program for undergraduates from historically Black colleges and universities ([Lieggi NSF award 2347360 (web)](../sources/Lieggi%20NSF%20award%202347360%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Beginnings award is $999,924, from July 15, 2024 to June 30, 2028 ([Lieggi NSF award 2347360 (web)](../sources/Lieggi%20NSF%20award%202347360%20%28web%29.md)).
- She co-wrote a November 2023 response to the Office of the National Cyber Director's request for information on open-source software security ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- Her profile lists a forthcoming report, "Securing Open Source: Applying the Logic of Post-9/11 Trade Security to the Software Supply Chain" ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- She co-wrote "Policy recommendations to ensure that research software is openly accessible and reusable", in *PLoS Biology* in 2023 ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
