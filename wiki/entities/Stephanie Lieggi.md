# Stephanie Lieggi

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: UC Santa Cruz
- Title: Executive Director, CROSS & OSPO
- Role: Open source infrastructure leader; technology governance and reproducibility UC

## Background

- She joined CROSS at UC Santa Cruz in 2016, and "led the effort to create an OSPO Network in the University of California system" ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- She was a co-PI on UC Santa Cruz's first NSF POSE grant, and co-chairs the CHAOSS University Working Group ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- Before CROSS she researched national security and global trade at the Middlebury Institute's Center for Nonproliferation Studies ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
- Her work includes a forthcoming report on software supply chain security, and a 2023 response to the National Cyber Director on open-source security ([Lieggi UCSC OSPO profile](../sources/Lieggi%20UCSC%20OSPO%20profile.md)).
