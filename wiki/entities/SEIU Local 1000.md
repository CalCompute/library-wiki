# SEIU Local 1000

## Bottom line

- SEIU Local 1000 is the union of "nearly 100,000 state workers" ([SEIU Local 1000 Master Table Update 2026-06-11](../sources/SEIU%20Local%201000%20Master%20Table%20Update%202026-06-11%20%28web%29.md)).
- In its 2026 bargaining with the state, it proposed protections from "the misuse of artificial intelligence (AI) and new technology" ([SEIU Local 1000 Master Table Update 2026-06-11](../sources/SEIU%20Local%201000%20Master%20Table%20Update%202026-06-11%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element G, key finding 3: its president said state workers "are already raising concerns about AI tools that create more work, produce inaccuracies and still require significant human oversight" ([SEIU Local 1000 statement on new AI executive order](../sources/SEIU%20Local%201000%20statement%20on%20new%20AI%20executive%20order%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element G, recommendation 3: it supports the state's Apprenticeship Pilot Programs, with the Governor's Office, GovOps, the Labor and Workforce Development Agency, CDT and CalHR ([GovOps apprenticeships](../sources/GovOps%20apprenticeships%20%28web%29.md)).
- Elements E and G, subdivision (e) labor standards: Anthony Suen names it for the union review of the workforce part. It does "some fellowships or workforce training with the state uh to my knowledge" ([2026-11-18 Call E & G, 57:54, 58:40](../sources/Public%20AI%20Network%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element C: Anthony Suen: one union's support "will be a huge political thing for us" ([2026-11-18 Call E & G, 1:00:25](../sources/Public%20AI%20Network%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element G, recommendation 2: the state gives it 60 to 180 days' notice of automation with a significant impact. It may then bargain the impact ([SEIU Local 1000 State MOU 2023-2026 (web)](../sources/SEIU%20Local%201000%20State%20MOU%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md), § 14.5).
