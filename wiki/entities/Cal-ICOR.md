# Cal-ICOR

## Bottom line

- Cal-ICOR is a cloud JupyterHub teaching service led by UC Berkeley.
- It is an existing California platform that expands access to compute, and a working model of staff who operate, support and train.

## Affiliations

- UC Berkeley: "Led by UC Berkeley and supported by the California Education Learning Lab" ([Cal-ICOR About](../sources/Cal-ICOR%20About.md)).
