# New Jersey Supercomputer Initiative

## Bottom line

- The New Jersey Supercomputer Initiative (NJSCI) is a public-private partnership for a shared state supercomputer, with users from early 2027 ([NJSCI homepage (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- An independent nonprofit, also named NJSCI, is to administer the supercomputer from 2027. SHI International hosts and operates it ([NJ Supercomputer Initiative launch release](../sources/NJ%20Governor%20release%202026-10-20%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Until NJSCI is an entity, "a partner agreement with the New Jersey Innovation Authority (NJIA)" governs it. Then its Executive Director reports to "the Initiative's governing board" ([NJSCI Executive Director posting (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20Executive%20Director%20posting%20%28web%29.md)).
- The board is to approve the governance, allocation and cost recovery policies ([NJSCI Executive Director posting (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20Executive%20Director%20posting%20%28web%29.md)).
- Money: a $75 million gift from Chris and Melody Malachowsky, $25 million from SHI International, and $25 million from the state ([NJSCI homepage (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)). The state's $25 million goes through the New Jersey Economic Development Authority, as a grant to the host, "an institution of higher education" ([NJ PL 2025 c404 supplemental appropriation (web)](../sources/NJ%20PL%202025%20c404%20supplemental%20appropriation%20%28web%29.md)).
- Founding partners: eight universities and the New Jersey Council of County Colleges. "Each institution plans to provide annual contributions to the initiative's budget" ([NJSCI homepage (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [State of New Jersey, Office of the Governor](State%20of%20New%20Jersey%2C%20Office%20of%20the%20Governor.md): launched the initiative on Oct. 20, 2026 ([NJ Supercomputer Initiative launch release](../sources/NJ%20Governor%20release%202026-10-20%20%28web%29.md)).
- [New Jersey Institute of Technology](New%20Jersey%20Institute%20of%20Technology.md): a founding partner ([NJSCI homepage (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- A "fiscally-sponsored initiative of the National Center for Civic Innovation" hires the Executive Director ([NJSCI Executive Director posting (web)](../sources/NJSCI%20Executive%20Director%20posting%20%28web%29.md)).
