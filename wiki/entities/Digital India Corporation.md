# Digital India Corporation

## Bottom line

- India's Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) set up Digital India Corporation (DIC). It is "a 'not for profit' Company under Section 8 of the Companies Act 2013" ([Digital India Corporation notice DIC-10-2026-22 (web)](../sources/Digital%20India%20Corporation%20notice%20DIC-10-2026-22%20%28web%29.md)).
- The IndiaAI Mission "will be implemented by 'IndiaAI' Independent Business Division (IBD) under Digital India Corporation (DIC)" ([PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)](../sources/PIB%20Cabinet%20approves%20IndiaAI%20Mission%202024-03-07%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It is the legal home of the IndiaAI division, which runs the national AI compute pillar ([IndiaAI notice INDAI-122-2026 General Manager Compute (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20notice%20INDAI-122-2026%20General%20Manager%20Compute%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [IndiaAI](IndiaAI.md): an independent business division of DIC ([IndiaAI notice INDAI-122-2026 General Manager Compute (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20notice%20INDAI-122-2026%20General%20Manager%20Compute%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- It was formerly Media Lab Asia ([IndiaAI notice INDAI-122-2026 General Manager Compute (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20notice%20INDAI-122-2026%20General%20Manager%20Compute%20%28web%29.md)).
