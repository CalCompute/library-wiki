# ETH Zurich

## Bottom line

- ETH Zurich operates the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) in Lugano ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- ETH Zurich inaugurated the Alps supercomputer at CSCS on 14 September 2024 ([ETH Zurich Alps inauguration release 2024-09 (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20Alps%20inauguration%20release%202024-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- Alps is "a central element of the Swiss AI initiative", which ETH Zurich and EPFL launched ([ETH Zurich Alps inauguration release 2024-09 (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20Alps%20inauguration%20release%202024-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Swiss Confederation funds CSCS through the Board of the Federal Institutes of Technology and ETH Zurich ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
