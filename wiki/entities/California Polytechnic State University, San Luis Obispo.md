# California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California Polytechnic State University-San Luis Obispo in San Luis Obispo, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Cal Poly runs an AI Factory with four NVIDIA DGX B200 systems. The Noyce School of Applied Computing paid $3 million for it ([Cal Poly AI Factory release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20release%20%28web%29.md); [Cal Poly AI Factory unveiling release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20unveiling%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
- Cal Poly formally unveiled the AI Factory on Oct. 22, 2026 ([Cal Poly AI Factory unveiling release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20unveiling%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
- "Regional businesses and organizations may be able to leverage the system's computing power by working with Cal Poly faculty and students" ([Cal Poly AI Factory release (web)](../sources/Cal%20Poly%20AI%20Factory%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
