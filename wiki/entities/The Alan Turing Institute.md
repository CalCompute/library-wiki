# The Alan Turing Institute

## Bottom line

The Alan Turing Institute is a research institute in the United Kingdom. Its Public Policy Programme mapped generative AI against public sector work in Great Britain ([Turing 2025 Mapping the Potential GenAI public sector work (web)](../sources/Turing%202025%20Mapping%20the%20Potential%20GenAI%20public%20sector%20work%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Element G.** The report estimates that generative AI could support about 41 percent of public sector work time in Great Britain ([Turing 2025 Mapping the Potential GenAI public sector work (web)](../sources/Turing%202025%20Mapping%20the%20Potential%20GenAI%20public%20sector%20work%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- The report comes from a collaboration with the Office for National Statistics and the Department for Transport ([Turing 2025 Mapping the Potential GenAI public sector work (web)](../sources/Turing%202025%20Mapping%20the%20Potential%20GenAI%20public%20sector%20work%20%28web%29.md)).
