# Gauss Centre for Supercomputing

## Bottom line

- The Gauss Centre for Supercomputing (GCS) is a German non-profit registered association. It joins the three national supercomputing centres HLRS, JSC and LRZ ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](../sources/Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The federal research ministry and the ministries of Baden-Württemberg, Bavaria and North Rhine-Westphalia fund GCS jointly ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](../sources/Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its resources are "openly available to national and European researchers from academia and industry" ([Gauss Centre for Supercomputing about (web)](../sources/Gauss%20Centre%20for%20Supercomputing%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- EuroHPC JU and GCS manage the allocation of JUPITER together ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- The funding for the Hunter supercomputer at HLRS goes "through the Gauss Centre for Supercomputing" ([HLRS Hunter system (web)](../sources/HLRS%20Hunter%20system%20%28web%29.md)).
