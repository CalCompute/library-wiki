# Meta Engineering

## Bottom line

Engineering at Meta is the engineering blog of the company Meta. A 2024 post describes two of Meta's 24,576-GPU clusters for generative AI ([Meta Building GenAI infrastructure 2024-03-12 (web)](../sources/Meta%20Building%20GenAI%20infrastructure%202024-03-12%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Element A.** Meta plans "350,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs" by the end of 2024, in a portfolio equal to "nearly 600,000 H100s" ([Meta Building GenAI infrastructure 2024-03-12 (web)](../sources/Meta%20Building%20GenAI%20infrastructure%202024-03-12%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Meta gave its Grand Teton GPU platform to the Open Compute Project. Meta says it is "the largest and primary contributor to PyTorch" ([Meta Building GenAI infrastructure 2024-03-12 (web)](../sources/Meta%20Building%20GenAI%20infrastructure%202024-03-12%20%28web%29.md)).
