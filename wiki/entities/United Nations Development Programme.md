# United Nations Development Programme

## Bottom line

- The United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) initiated the DPI Safeguards Initiative for digital public infrastructure (DPI) ([DPI Safeguards resources page (web)](../sources/DPI%20Safeguards%20resources%20page%20%28web%29.md)). It is a partner in the Open Source Ecosystem Enabler (OSEE) ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- UNDP published the DPI Safeguards Framework with the office of the UN Envoy on Technology in September 2024 ([UN OSET and UNDP 2024 DPI Safeguards](../sources/UN%20OSET%20and%20UNDP%202024%20DPI%20Safeguards.md)).
- The DPI Safeguards resources include adoption pathways and an assessment resource for "responsible authorities" ([DPI Safeguards resources page (web)](../sources/DPI%20Safeguards%20resources%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- UNDP implements an Open Source Programme Office for Trinidad and Tobago ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
