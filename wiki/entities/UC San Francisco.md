# UC San Francisco

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of California-San Francisco in San Francisco, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Special Focus: Medical Schools and Centers" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In 2010, Lynne and Marc Benioff gave $100 million to UCSF Children's Hospital for a new hospital at Mission Bay. The hospital took the name UCSF Benioff Children's Hospital (B, F) ([UCSF Childrens Hospital Benioff 100 million gift 2010 (web)](../sources/UCSF%20Childrens%20Hospital%20Benioff%20100%20million%20gift%202010%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Patrick J. McGovern Foundation lists grants for AI and data projects in health at UCSF. One is "to support deep learning models that improve prenatal diagnosis of congenital heart defects" (B, F) ([McGovern Foundation Our Grants (web)](../sources/McGovern%20Foundation%20Our%20Grants%20%28web%29.md)).
