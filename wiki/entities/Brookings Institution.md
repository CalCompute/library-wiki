# Brookings Institution

## Bottom line

The Brookings Institution is a research institution. Its Economic Studies program published an article on the influence of industry in AI research ([Ahmed Thompson Brookings industry influence in AI research 2023-12-05 (web)](../sources/Ahmed%20Thompson%20Brookings%20industry%20influence%20in%20AI%20research%202023-12-05%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Element A.** The article measures the gap between industry and academia in data, talent and compute ([Ahmed Thompson Brookings industry influence in AI research 2023-12-05 (web)](../sources/Ahmed%20Thompson%20Brookings%20industry%20influence%20in%20AI%20research%202023-12-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Element E.** It reports that industry hired "nearly 70% of AI Ph.D. holders" in 2020 ([Ahmed Thompson Brookings industry influence in AI research 2023-12-05 (web)](../sources/Ahmed%20Thompson%20Brookings%20industry%20influence%20in%20AI%20research%202023-12-05%20%28web%29.md)).
