# Kevin Michael Esterling

## Bottom line

- Kevin Michael Esterling is a professor of public policy and political science at UC Riverside ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- He has been Chair of the Department of Political Science since July 1, 2024 ([Esterling department chair appointment (web)](../sources/Esterling%20department%20chair%20appointment%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [UC Riverside](../entities/UC%20Riverside.md): professor of public policy and political science since 2003 ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- UC Riverside, Department of Political Science: Chair since July 1, 2024 ([Esterling department chair appointment (web)](../sources/Esterling%20department%20chair%20appointment%20%28web%29.md)).
- UC Riverside, Laboratory for Technology, Communication and Democracy (TeCD-Lab): his lab ([Esterling TECD lab homepage](../sources/Esterling%20TECD%20lab%20homepage.md)).
- UC Riverside AI institute, RAISE@UCR: lead of the Social Sciences thrust ([RAISE at UCR people (web)](../sources/RAISE%20at%20UCR%20people%20%28web%29.md)).
- Berkeley Initiative on Transparency in the Social Sciences: member of the Executive Committee since 2012 ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- *Research & Politics*: editor since 2018 ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).

## Background

- He was Associate Dean of the Graduate Division at UC Riverside from 2012 to 2016 ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- He was Interim Dean of the Graduate Division from 2016 to 2017 ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- He holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from the University of Chicago (1999) ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- He holds a BA in Government and Foreign Affairs from the University of Virginia (1989) ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- The TeCD-Lab started in 2019 ([Esterling TECD lab homepage](../sources/Esterling%20TECD%20lab%20homepage.md)).
- The TeCD-Lab builds Prytaneum, an open source webinar platform for large public meetings ([Esterling TECD lab homepage](../sources/Esterling%20TECD%20lab%20homepage.md)).
- The Democracy Fund gave him a grant for "The Prytanea Project: A Web-Based Town Hall Platform" (2020 to 2021) ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- The Institute of Education Sciences gave him a grant for "Connecting Classrooms to Congress" (2021 to 2025) ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- The California Air Resources Board gave him a grant on community engagement through deliberative online town halls ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- He is a co-author of *Politics with the People: Building a Directly Representative Democracy* (Cambridge University Press, 2018), with Michael A. Neblo and David M. J. Lazer ([Esterling Politics with the People Cambridge record (web)](../sources/Esterling%20Politics%20with%20the%20People%20Cambridge%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- In the book's experiments, members of Congress met their constituents in online town hall meetings ([Esterling Politics with the People Cambridge record (web)](../sources/Esterling%20Politics%20with%20the%20People%20Cambridge%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a co-author of "Post-January 6th deplatforming reduced the reach of misinformation on Twitter" (*Nature*, 2024) ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- A 2026 work compares the opinions and justifications of large language models and web search engines ([Esterling ORCID record](../sources/Esterling%20ORCID%20record.md)).
