# Morgan Stanley

## Bottom line

Morgan Stanley is an investment bank and wealth manager. The Morgan Stanley Institute publishes articles on markets, among them one on AI infrastructure spending ([Morgan Stanley AI Market Trends 2026 (web)](../sources/Morgan%20Stanley%20AI%20Market%20Trends%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Elements A and B.** Morgan Stanley Research estimates "~$2.9 trillion in global data center construction cost alone through 2028". It splits the figure by source of capital ([Morgan Stanley AI Market Trends 2026 (web)](../sources/Morgan%20Stanley%20AI%20Market%20Trends%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Morgan Stanley "advised Meta on the $27 billion structured JV for the U.S. AI data-center campus" in 2025 ([Morgan Stanley AI Market Trends 2026 (web)](../sources/Morgan%20Stanley%20AI%20Market%20Trends%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
