# California Legislature

## Bottom line

- The California Legislature makes the state's statutes. Its website, California Legislative Information, publishes bill texts and code sections ([AB 979 AI cybersecurity bill text](../sources/AB%20979%20AI%20cybersecurity%20bill%20text.md); [Gov Code 53051](../sources/Gov%20Code%2053051.md)).

## Why they matter

- AB 1405 (2026) makes the Government Operations Agency set up an AI Auditor Registry by Jan. 1, 2029 (C) ([AB 1405 bill text](../sources/AB%201405%20bill%20text.md)).
- SB 813 (2026) makes the same agency set criteria for independent verification organizations that assess AI risks by Jan. 1, 2028 (C) ([SB 813 bill text](../sources/SB%20813%20bill%20text.md)).
- SB 1119 (2026) requires risk assessments and independent child safety audits for companion chatbots (C, D) ([SB 1119 companion chatbot child safety bill text](../sources/SB%201119%20companion%20chatbot%20child%20safety%20bill%20text.md)).
- AB 979, enrolled in 2025, requires a California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook by Jan. 1, 2027 (C, D) ([AB 979 AI cybersecurity bill text](../sources/AB%20979%20AI%20cybersecurity%20bill%20text.md)).
- SB 53 (2025) makes developers of frontier AI models publish safety frameworks and report critical safety incidents to the Office of Emergency Services. It also sets up the CalCompute consortium (C, D) ([SB 53 bill text (web)](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- SB 1000 and AB 2713 (2026) amend the California AI Transparency Act. Each producer of a generative AI system that the public can reach in California is a covered provider (D) ([SB 1000 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%201000%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20861%20of%202026.md); [AB 2713 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%202713%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20856%20of%202026.md)).
- SB 947 (2026) limits an employer's use of automated decision systems in discipline and termination. Public employers are in its scope (G) ([SB 947 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20947%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20859%20of%202026.md)).
- SB 1159 (2026) says that an AI system is not a person under the state's public records and open meeting laws (C) ([SB 1159 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%201159%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20863%20of%202026.md)).
- SB 951, AB 1331 and AB 1883 (2026) set rules for AI at work: layoff notices that name AI as the cause, and limits on workplace surveillance tools (E, G) ([SB 951 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20951%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20860%20of%202026.md); [AB 1331 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%201331%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20851%20of%202026.md); [AB 1883 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%201883%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20853%20of%202026.md)).
- SB 503 and AB 1979 (2026) set rules for AI clinical decision support systems and for health care chatbots (D) ([SB 503 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20503%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20857%20of%202026.md); [AB 1979 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%201979%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20854%20of%202026.md)).
- AB 2504 (2026) has the community colleges run a pilot that retrains creative workers whom generative AI displaces (E) ([AB 2504 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%202504%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20305%20of%202026.md)).
- AB 2392 (2026) makes the community colleges and the California State University set standards for GenAI procurement and training (D, E, F) ([AB 2392 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%202392%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20855%20of%202026.md)).
- SB 1111 (2026) adds digital replicas to the law on the use of a person's voice or likeness, and SB 574 (2026) sets rules for generative AI in legal work (D, G) ([SB 1111 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%201111%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20862%20of%202026.md); [SB 574 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20574%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20858%20of%202026.md)).
- Five 2025 laws set rules for companion chatbots (SB 243), content provenance (AB 853), AI as a defense in civil actions (AB 316), health advice from AI (AB 489) and police reports written with AI (SB 524) (C, D, G) ([SB 243 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20243%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20677%20of%202025.md); [AB 853 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%20853%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20674%20of%202025.md); [AB 316 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%20316%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20672%20of%202025.md); [AB 489 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%20489%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20615%20of%202025.md); [SB 524 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20524%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20587%20of%202025.md)).
- Five 2024 laws: the first California AI Transparency Act (SB 942), training data documentation for generative AI (AB 2013), one definition of artificial intelligence (AB 2885), AI disclaimers in patient communications (AB 3030) and AI in health plan reviews (SB 1120) (C, D) ([SB 942 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%20942%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20291%20of%202024.md); [AB 2013 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%202013%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20817%20of%202024.md); [AB 2885 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%202885%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20843%20of%202024.md); [AB 3030 bill text (web)](../sources/AB%203030%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20848%20of%202024.md); [SB 1120 bill text (web)](../sources/SB%201120%20chaptered%20text%20Chapter%20879%20of%202024.md)).
- SB 896 (2024) sets rules for state use of generative AI. State GenAI messages to the public must say so (C, D, G) ([SB 896 Generative AI Accountability Act bill text](../sources/SB%20896%20Generative%20AI%20Accountability%20Act%20bill%20text.md)).
- The Constitution forbids gifts of public money and state stock ownership (B, C) ([California Constitution art XVI sec 6](../sources/California%20Constitution%20art%20XVI%20sec%206.md)). One statute defines small businesses for state contracts (B, F) ([Gov Code 14837](../sources/Gov%20Code%2014837.md)). Another makes public agencies file with the Registry of Public Agencies (C) ([Gov Code 53051](../sources/Gov%20Code%2053051.md)).
