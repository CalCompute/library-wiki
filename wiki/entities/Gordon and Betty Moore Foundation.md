# Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation

![Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/Gordon%20and%20Betty%20Moore%20Foundation.png)

_Logo: Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation, [Our Story page](https://www.moore.org/about). Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

Gordon and Betty Moore set up the foundation in Palo Alto. Its program areas are Environmental Conservation, Science and the San Francisco Bay Area ([Moore Foundation about (web)](../sources/Moore%20Foundation%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It calls its role "society’s venture capital" (B) ([Moore Foundation about (web)](../sources/Moore%20Foundation%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- It funded, with the Simons Foundation, much of CCST's work to help other states start science policy fellowships (F) ([CCST Science Fellows program (web)](../sources/CCST%20Science%20Fellows%20program%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Gordon Moore was a founder of Intel. In 1965 he made the prediction "that later became known as Moore’s Law" ([Moore Foundation about (web)](../sources/Moore%20Foundation%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
