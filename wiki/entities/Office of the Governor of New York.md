# Office of the Governor of New York

## Bottom line

- The Office of the Governor of New York publishes the Governor's press releases. Its release of May 9, 2025 reports "$90 million in capital funding" for Empire AI in the FY 2026 budget ([Hochul Empire AI expansion 2025-05-09 (web)](../sources/Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20expansion%202025-05-09%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The release says that the FY 2025 budget set up Empire AI as "a first-of-its-kind independent consortium" ([Hochul Empire AI expansion 2025-05-09 (web)](../sources/Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20expansion%202025-05-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- The same release announces AI Prep, AI training for small businesses and AI training for the state workforce ([Hochul Empire AI expansion 2025-05-09 (web)](../sources/Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20expansion%202025-05-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its release of June 26, 2025 reports that the Empire State Development (ESD) Board approved $40 million for Empire AI Beta. It gives "up to $340 million in State capital grant investment" ([Hochul Empire AI Beta launch 2025-06-26 (web)](../sources/Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20Beta%20launch%202025-06-26%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its release of August 5, 2026 says Beta is online, with "over 300 research projects already queued up" ([Hochul Empire AI Beta fully online (web)](../sources/Hochul%20Empire%20AI%20Beta%20fully%20online%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its release of November 10, 2026 launches the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research at Binghamton University. Empire AI gives the Center its computing power ([Hochul NY AI science and safety research launch (web)](../sources/Hochul%20NY%20AI%20science%20and%20safety%20research%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
