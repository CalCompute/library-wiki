# Patrick J. McGovern Foundation

## Bottom line

The Patrick J. McGovern Foundation is a grantmaker. It says that it "has awarded nearly $500 million in charitable contributions" since its inception ([McGovern Foundation Our Grants (web)](../sources/McGovern%20Foundation%20Our%20Grants%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its grants database lists awards from July 2017 to 2026, many of them for AI and data projects. Many grantees are in California (B, F) ([McGovern Foundation Our Grants (web)](../sources/McGovern%20Foundation%20Our%20Grants%20%28web%29.md)).
- A 2026 grant of $400,000 to Skillup Coalition, San Francisco, is "to build shared AI infrastructure for low-income household stability" (B) ([McGovern Foundation Our Grants (web)](../sources/McGovern%20Foundation%20Our%20Grants%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report puts the name of Nick Cain, "VP, Strategy and Innovation, Patrick J. McGovern Foundation", beside a quotation in its section for funders ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).
