# CROSS

## Bottom line

- The Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) is a UC Santa Cruz center. It started in 2015 with a $2 million gift and three founding industry members ([CROSS About](../sources/CROSS%20About.md)).

## Affiliations

- [UC Santa Cruz](UC%20Santa%20Cruz.md): a center of the campus ([CROSS About](../sources/CROSS%20About.md)).
- [UC OSPO Network](UC%20OSPO%20Network.md): works with the network of "six UC campuses" ([CROSS homepage](../sources/CROSS%20homepage.md)).
