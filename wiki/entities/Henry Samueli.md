# Henry Samueli

## Bottom line

Henry Samueli co-founded Broadcom Corporation in 1991. The UCLA Samueli School of Engineering carries his name ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)). He and his wife, Susan Samueli, pledged in 2012 to the Giving Pledge ([Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20Henry%20and%20Susan%20Samueli%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Their foundation "has granted over $500 million in gifts in the areas of education, health, social services, and the arts" (B) ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- One of its largest early gifts endowed the UCLA school of engineering (B, F) ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Their pledge letter names "enhancing engineering education at the university level" as one aim (B) ([Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20Henry%20and%20Susan%20Samueli%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- UCLA faculty member, on leave ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Member of the UC President's Board on Science and Innovation ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Chair of the UCLA Chancellor's Competitiveness Council, and member of the Dean's Executive Board at UCLA Samueli ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He earned bachelor's, master's and Ph.D. degrees at UCLA's School of Engineering ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a member of the National Academy of Engineering (2003) ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the two sources give different years for the Broadcom public offering, 1993 ([Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20Henry%20and%20Susan%20Samueli%20%28web%29.md)) and 1998 ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
