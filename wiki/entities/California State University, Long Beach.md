# California State University, Long Beach

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California State University-Long Beach in Long Beach, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its Division of Information Technology runs a Central High Performance Computing (HPC) environment for faculty and principal investigators. The HPC2 cluster has 1,664 cores and 20 GPUs ([CSULB High Performance Computing article (web)](../sources/CSULB%20High%20Performance%20Computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
- The cluster uses a condo model. Faculty who invest "will receive priority to resources they purchased in addition to idle resources" ([CSULB High Performance Computing article (web)](../sources/CSULB%20High%20Performance%20Computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2024, it was one of four campuses that "may be able to use" Cal-ICOR early ([Berkeley VCR Cal-ICOR compute hub launch (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20VCR%20Cal-ICOR%20compute%20hub%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
