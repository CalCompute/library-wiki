# EDUCAUSE

## Bottom line

EDUCAUSE developed the Research Computing and Data (RCD) Capabilities Model with Internet2 and CaRCC ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Elements A and C: the Capabilities Model assesses support for "computationally- and data-intensive research" ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
