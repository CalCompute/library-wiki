# California Department of Justice

## Bottom line

- The California Department of Justice is the office of the Attorney General. Its Registry of Charities and Fundraisers registers charities and fundraisers. Its Charitable Trusts Section takes legal action against the misuse of charitable assets ([California AG Registry of Charities (web)](../sources/California%20AG%20Registry%20of%20Charities%20%28web%29.md)).
