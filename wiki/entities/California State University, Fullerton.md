# California State University, Fullerton

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California State University-Fullerton in Fullerton, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: its Center for Computational and Applied Mathematics opened its second HPC cluster, Turing, in 2022 ([CSUF second supercomputer Turing 2022 (web)](../sources/CSUF%20second%20supercomputer%20Turing%202022%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element A: its Titan Supercomputing Center serves students and faculty ([CSUF Titan Supercomputing Center (web)](../sources/CSUF%20Titan%20Supercomputing%20Center%20%28web%29.md)). Its request form offers Nautilus and the campus clusters Turing and Kepler ([CSUF request for Titan Supercomputing resources (web)](../sources/CSUF%20request%20for%20Titan%20Supercomputing%20resources%20%28web%29.md)).
