# Internet2

## Bottom line

Internet2 developed the Research Computing and Data (RCD) Capabilities Model with CaRCC and EDUCAUSE. It shares the copyright of the model's overview with CaRCC ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Elements A and C: the Capabilities Model assesses support for "computationally- and data-intensive research" ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model one-page overview (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20one-page%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- A co-chair of the CaRCC Capabilities Model Working Group is from Internet2 ([CaRCC RCD Capabilities Model (web)](../sources/CaRCC%20RCD%20Capabilities%20Model%20%28web%29.md)).
