# National Science Foundation

## Bottom line

- The U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) funds national compute access and new state AI hubs: ACCESS, the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR), NSF 26-508 and NSF 26-513.

## Why they matter

- A 26-508 hub must keep a public inventory of AI resources, which could share data with the landscape ([NSF 26-508 TechAccess solicitation (web)](../sources/NSF%2026-508%20TechAccess%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md)).
- NSF funds the NAIRR Operations Center at SDSC ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)) and ACCESS ([ACCESS homepage](../sources/ACCESS%20homepage.md)).
