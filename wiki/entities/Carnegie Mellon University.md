# Carnegie Mellon University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie Mellon University (CMU) is a university in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. With the University of Pittsburgh, it runs the Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (PSC) as "a joint research center" ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its news story of July 6, 2026 reports a "$10 million NSF award" for Bridges-3 at PSC ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- The story names ACCESS and the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot as the main allocation routes for Bridges-3 ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- Joint operator of the [Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center](Pittsburgh%20Supercomputing%20Center.md), with the University of Pittsburgh ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
