# Till von Wachter

## Bottom line

Till von Wachter is a Professor of Economics at UCLA and Faculty Director of the California Policy Lab's UCLA site ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- His current research includes "the effects of AI on the labor market" and "how workforce services help affected workers" (E) ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a co-author of the report behind the California AI-Unemployment Tracker (E) ([CPL Tracking AI-Related Job Loss Using UI Claims Data (web)](../sources/CPL%20Tracking%20AI-Related%20Job%20Loss%20Using%20UI%20Claims%20Data%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Governor's release quotes him: "This new tracker helps replace speculation with evidence" (E) ([Governor AI-Unemployment Tracker launch release 2026-06-25 (web)](../sources/Governor%20AI-Unemployment%20Tracker%20launch%20release%202026-06-25%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California Policy Lab](California%20Policy%20Lab.md): Faculty Director, UCLA site ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- UCLA: Professor of Economics ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Federal Statistical Research Data Center: Director, as the page heading gives it ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He has given expert assistance to the State of California, the U.S. Department of Labor, the OECD, the United Nations and the IMF, among others ([von Wachter California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/von%20Wachter%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
