# Oregon State University

## Bottom line

- Oregon State University (OSU) is a public university in Corvallis, Oregon. It is building the $213 million Jen-Hsun and Lori Huang Collaborative Innovation Complex, with a supercomputer. The state gives $72 million in bonds ([Oregon State CIC 72 million legislature release (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20CIC%2072%20million%20legislature%20release%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- A $50 million gift from Jen-Hsun and Lori Huang to the OSU Foundation started the complex ([Oregon State CIC 72 million legislature release (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20CIC%2072%20million%20legislature%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
- OSU says the complex "is different from a commercial data center". Students at other Oregon universities can take part through partnerships ([Oregon State Huang Complex FAQ (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20Huang%20Complex%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
- For its supercomputing, "No prior computational expertise is required" ([Oregon State Huang Complex inside (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20Huang%20Complex%20inside%20%28web%29.md)).
- Construction is to finish at the end of 2026, with a grand opening in 2027 ([Oregon State Huang Complex FAQ (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20Huang%20Complex%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The 2023 news release comes from the OSU News and Research Communications office ([Oregon State CIC 72 million legislature release (web)](../sources/Oregon%20State%20CIC%2072%20million%20legislature%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
- The bond amount is in House Bill 5005 of 2023 ([Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed (web)](../sources/Oregon%20HB%205005%202023%20A-Engrossed%20%28web%29.md)).
