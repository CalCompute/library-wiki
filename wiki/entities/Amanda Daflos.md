# Amanda Daflos

## Bottom line

Amanda Daflos is a Deputy Mayor of the City of Los Angeles, for the Office of Strategic Partnerships (OSP) ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Her office helps the city "cultivate, coordinate, and strengthen cross-sector partnerships" (F) ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- At Deloitte Consulting, she worked on "government transformation, strategy, and technology". Her clients were in federal, state and local government (G) ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- City of Los Angeles: Deputy Mayor, Office of Strategic Partnerships ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- [Centre for Public Impact](Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact.md): CPI Global Board ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: Deloitte Consulting, for more than ten years ([Amanda Daflos Centre for Public Impact profile (web)](../sources/Amanda%20Daflos%20Centre%20for%20Public%20Impact%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
