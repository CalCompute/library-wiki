# U.S. Department of Education, Office of Postsecondary Education

## Bottom line

- The Office of Postsecondary Education of the U.S. Department of Education issues the *Federal Register* notice on eligibility for Title III and V grant programs of the Higher Education Act of 1965 (HEA) ([ED Title III and V eligibility notice FY2025 (web)](../sources/ED%20Title%20III%20and%20V%20eligibility%20notice%20FY2025%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- For fiscal year 2025, it uses each institution's "2022-2023 enrollment and fiscal data" to decide eligibility. The programs include those for Hispanic-Serving Institutions ([ED Title III and V eligibility notice FY2025 (web)](../sources/ED%20Title%20III%20and%20V%20eligibility%20notice%20FY2025%20%28web%29.md)).
