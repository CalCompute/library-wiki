# Equinix

![Equinix](../../raw/assets/logos/Equinix.svg)

## Bottom line

- Equinix is a data center and colocation company with its executive offices in Redwood City, California. It has leased and owned data centers in the Los Angeles and Silicon Valley metro areas ([Equinix Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Equinix%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).
- It is building SV18 in Silicon Valley. The first phase has 2,100 cabinets for $260 million ([Equinix Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Equinix%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A, commercial infrastructure: its annual report gives no count of data centers, floor area or power for a California metro. Schedule III gives the cost of its buildings and improvements. It is $1,422 million in Silicon Valley and $461 million in Los Angeles ([Equinix Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Equinix%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).
