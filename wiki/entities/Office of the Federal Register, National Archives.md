# Office of the Federal Register, National Archives

## Bottom line

The Office of the Federal Register, National Archives, publishes the Electronic Code of Federal Regulations (eCFR) and the text of executive orders ([eCFR 2 CFR 200.1 Definitions (web)](../sources/eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.1%20Definitions%20%28web%29.md); [Executive Order 14365 national AI policy framework (web)](../sources/Executive%20Order%2014365%20national%20AI%20policy%20framework%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The eCFR gives the federal Uniform Guidance, 2 CFR Part 200: its definitions and its indirect cost rules, current and as of Sept. 30, 2024 ([eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 Indirect costs (web)](../sources/eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20Indirect%20costs%20%28web%29.md); [eCFR 2 CFR 200.414 as of 2024-09-30 (web)](../sources/eCFR%202%20CFR%20200.414%20as%20of%202024-09-30%20%28web%29.md)). (B, C)
