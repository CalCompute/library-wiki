# National Research Platform

## Bottom line

- NRP: "The National Research Platform is shared research computing: GPUs, storage, and hosted AI models, pooled across more than 70 institutions and offered free to U.S. nonprofit research and education" ([NRP about (web)](../sources/NRP%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- The National Science Foundation (NSF) funds it. Its principal investigator is Frank Würthwein, Director of the San Diego Supercomputer Center ([NRP about (web)](../sources/NRP%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- "Institutions contribute nodes and keep priority on their own hardware; when they are not using it, everyone else gets opportunistic access" ([NRP about (web)](../sources/NRP%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- The California State University TIDE project adds 72 GPUs to Nautilus, the NRP cluster. CSU users get preferential scheduling on those nodes ([SDSU TIDE technology (web)](../sources/SDSU%20TIDE%20technology%20%28web%29.md)).
