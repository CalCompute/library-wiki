# Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

## Bottom line

- Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) is a national laboratory in Livermore, California. Lawrence Livermore National Security, LLC operates it for the Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration ([LLNL Tuolumne (web)](../sources/LLNL%20Tuolumne%20%28web%29.md)).
