# Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

## Bottom line

- Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) is a national laboratory in Livermore, California. Lawrence Livermore National Security, LLC operates it for the Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration ([LLNL Tuolumne (web)](../sources/LLNL%20Tuolumne%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: LLNL operates El Capitan, "the CORAL2 flagship system", with 11,520 total nodes and 46,080 graphics processing units (GPUs). LLNL commissioned it in 2024 ([LLNL El Capitan (web)](../sources/LLNL%20El%20Capitan%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element A: TOP500 ranks El Capitan "2" on the June 2026 list and "1" on the three lists before it ([TOP500 El Capitan system (web)](../sources/TOP500%20El%20Capitan%20system%20%28web%29.md)).
