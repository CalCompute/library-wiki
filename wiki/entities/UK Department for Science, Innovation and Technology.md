# UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology

## Bottom line

- The Department for Science, Innovation and Technology (DSIT) is a UK government department. It publishes the AI Research Resource (AIRR) page on GOV.UK, with two other bodies ([UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](../sources/UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- AIRR is "a suite of advanced supercomputers that provides AI-specialised compute capacity to researchers, academia, and industry" ([UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](../sources/UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- AIRR gives access through Isambard-AI at the University of Bristol and Zenith at the University of Cambridge ([UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](../sources/UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- An update of 28 September 2026 says "References to DSIT amended to BIST" ([UK AI Research Resource publication page (web)](../sources/UK%20AI%20Research%20Resource%20publication%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
