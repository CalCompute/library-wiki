# University of Colorado Boulder

## Bottom line

- The University of Colorado Boulder (CU Boulder) runs the Alpine cluster through its Research Computing unit (CURC) ([CU Research Computing Alpine overview (web)](../sources/CU%20Research%20Computing%20Alpine%20overview%20%28web%29.md)). It houses the Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- "Alpine is the University of Colorado Boulder Research Computing’s third-generation High-Performance Computing (HPC) cluster." Its hardware comes from CU Boulder, Colorado State University and the Anschutz Medical Campus ([CU Research Computing Alpine overview (web)](../sources/CU%20Research%20Computing%20Alpine%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
- CURC allocates CU Boulder's and RMACC's time on Alpine. "Allocations for CSU and AMC are managed by those institutions." ([CU Research Computing Alpine allocations (web)](../sources/CU%20Research%20Computing%20Alpine%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- "RMACC is a regional collaborative group housed at the University of Colorado Boulder." ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
