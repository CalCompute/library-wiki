# National Center for Supercomputing Applications

## Bottom line

- The National Center for Supercomputing Applications (NCSA) is a computing center at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. It deploys and operates DeltaAI, a system funded by the National Science Foundation (NSF) ([NSF award 2320345 DeltaAI API record (web)](../sources/NSF%20award%202320345%20DeltaAI%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- NCSA is "One of five facilities created by the National Science Foundation (NSF)'s Supercomputer Centers Program". The state of Illinois, the University of Illinois, NSF and other federal agencies support it ([NCSA About (web)](../sources/NCSA%20About%20%28web%29.md)).
- For DeltaAI, "NSF will manage 90 percent of the available computing resources via ACCESS". The university allocates the other 10 percent ([NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)](../sources/NCSA%20DeltaAI%20award%20release%202023-07-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- A portion of DeltaAI goes through the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot ([NCSA DeltaAI allocations (web)](../sources/NCSA%20DeltaAI%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)).
- The campus program Illinois Computes "supplied 20 supplemental nodes integrated into the system" ([NCSA DeltaAI launch release 2024-11-18 (web)](../sources/NCSA%20DeltaAI%20launch%20release%202024-11-18%20%28web%29.md)).
- NCSA is one of the four Distributed Science Centers of the NSF Leadership-Class Computing Facility ([NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](../sources/NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md), p. MREFC-14).

## Affiliations

- University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign: the NSF awardee for DeltaAI ([NSF award 2320345 DeltaAI API record (web)](../sources/NSF%20award%202320345%20DeltaAI%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)). The NCSA page header names the university's "Office of the Vice Chancellor of Research and Innovation" ([NCSA About (web)](../sources/NCSA%20About%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Bill Gropp was "NCSA Director and Principal Investigator" for DeltaAI in 2023 ([NCSA DeltaAI award release 2023-07-05 (web)](../sources/NCSA%20DeltaAI%20award%20release%202023-07-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- DeltaAI is "Built and operated at the world-class National Petascale Computing Facility", on the Urbana-Champaign campus ([NCSA DeltaAI launch release 2024-11-18 (web)](../sources/NCSA%20DeltaAI%20launch%20release%202024-11-18%20%28web%29.md)).
