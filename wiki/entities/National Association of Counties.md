# National Association of Counties

## Bottom line

- The National Association of Counties (NACo) is the national association of U.S. county governments. It published 22 county AI use cases in June 2025, five of them from California counties ([NACo county AI use cases (web)](../sources/NACo%20county%20AI%20use%20cases%20%28web%29.md)).
