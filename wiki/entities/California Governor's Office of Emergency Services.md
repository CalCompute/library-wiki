# California Governor's Office of Emergency Services

![California Governor's Office of Emergency Services](../../raw/assets/logos/California%20Governor%27s%20Office%20of%20Emergency%20Services.png)

_Logo: California Governor's Office of Emergency Services, [home page](https://www.caloes.ca.gov/). Fetched 2026-10-29._

## Bottom line

- The California Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) leads the state's emergency response.

## Why they matter

- The org chart confirms Nancy Ward as head ([California Executive Branch org chart 2025-11-04](../sources/California%20Executive%20Branch%20org%20chart%202025-11-04.md)).

- Cal OES leads the California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC). AB 979 makes the center develop a California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook by Jan. 1, 2027 ([AB 979 AI cybersecurity bill text](../sources/AB%20979%20AI%20cybersecurity%20bill%20text.md)).
- SB 896 makes Cal OES analyse GenAI threats to critical infrastructure. It sends the Legislature a summary each year ([SB 896 Generative AI Accountability Act bill text](../sources/SB%20896%20Generative%20AI%20Accountability%20Act%20bill%20text.md)).
- On Aug. 10, 2026, the Governor directed an AI Cyber Defense Program within Cal-CSIC. The release quotes Director Caroline Thomas Jacobs ([Governor AI Cyber Defense Program release (web)](../sources/Governor%20AI%20Cyber%20Defense%20Program%20release%20%28web%29.md)).
