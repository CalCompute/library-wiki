# The White House

## Bottom line

- The White House publishes the President's executive orders, releases and fact sheets at whitehouse.gov. It published Executive Order 14363, which established the Genesis Mission on November 24, 2025 ([Executive Order 14363 Launching the Genesis Mission (web)](../sources/Executive%20Order%2014363%20Launching%20the%20Genesis%20Mission%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element C: Under Executive Order 14363, the Assistant to the President for Science and Technology "shall provide general leadership of the Mission" ([Executive Order 14363 Launching the Genesis Mission (web)](../sources/Executive%20Order%2014363%20Launching%20the%20Genesis%20Mission%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element B: A July 2026 release reports "more than $5 billion in Federal commitments" to the Genesis Mission ([White House Genesis Mission 5 billion release 2026-07 (web)](../sources/White%20House%20Genesis%20Mission%205%20billion%20release%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element F: An October 2026 fact sheet reports "$2.4B in SI for science tools and compute credits for the Genesis Mission Consortium from eleven industry partners" ([White House science initiatives fact sheet 2026-10 (web)](../sources/White%20House%20science%20initiatives%20fact%20sheet%202026-10%20%28web%29.md)).
