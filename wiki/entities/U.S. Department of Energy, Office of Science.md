# U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science

## Bottom line

- The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Science funds its user facilities "through congressional appropriations" ([DOE Office of Science user facilities at a glance (web)](../sources/DOE%20Office%20of%20Science%20user%20facilities%20at%20a%20glance%20%28web%29.md)).
- It posted the list of the first Genesis Mission selections on July 22, 2026 ([DOE Genesis Mission awards list 2026 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20awards%20list%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element D: Its Advanced Scientific Computing Research (ASCR) office gives access to OLCF, ALCF and NERSC through "five complementary mechanisms": INCITE, ALCC, ERCAP, SBIR/STTR and Center Reserves ([DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)](../sources/DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element D: ASCR access "is open to the scientific and engineering research community including universities, industry, and the national labs" ([DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)](../sources/DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
