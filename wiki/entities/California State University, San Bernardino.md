# California State University, San Bernardino

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California State University-San Bernardino in San Bernardino, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In 2022, CSUSB was a sub-awardee, with San Diego State University, of a five-year, $6.7 million National Science Foundation (NSF) grant for cyberinfrastructure staff. Its share is $1.2 million ([CSUSB high performance computing article (web)](../sources/CSUSB%20high%20performance%20computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its High Performance Computing Initiative (HPCI) gives users access to "high-performance computing resources at various supercomputing centers" ([CSUSB high performance computing article (web)](../sources/CSUSB%20high%20performance%20computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
- It joined the Pacific Research Platform in 2017. In 2018, it got a 100 Gig link to CENIC's California Research and Education Network (CalREN) ([CSUSB high performance computing article (web)](../sources/CSUSB%20high%20performance%20computing%20article%20%28web%29.md)).
