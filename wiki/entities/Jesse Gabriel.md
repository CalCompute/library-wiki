# Jesse Gabriel

![Jesse Gabriel](../../raw/assets/people/Jesse%20Gabriel.jpg)

_Photo: Office of Assemblymember Jesse Gabriel, [biography page](https://gabriel.asmdc.org/biography). Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

Jesse Gabriel represents the San Fernando Valley in the California State Assembly. He is Chair of the Assembly Budget Committee ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- As Budget Chair, he works "with legislative leadership and the Governor to craft California’s state budget" (B) ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Assembly](California%20State%20Assembly.md): Assemblymember; Chair of the Assembly Budget Committee ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- California Legislative Jewish Caucus: Co-Chair ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He worked as a constitutional rights attorney before he entered public service ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a graduate of UC Berkeley and Harvard Law School ([Gabriel Assembly biography (web)](../sources/Gabriel%20Assembly%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
