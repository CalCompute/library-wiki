# Encode AI

## Bottom line

- Encode AI co-sponsored SB-53, the bill that created CalCompute.

## Why they matter

- The author's release names it as a sponsor. It promises "free and low-cost access to compute for startups and academic researchers" ([Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md)).
- Sunny Gandhi, Vice President of Political Affairs at Encode: "With CalCompute, we're democratizing access to the computational resources that power AI innovation" ([Wiener SB-53 introduction release 2025-02-28 (web)](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20introduction%20release%202025-02-28%20%28web%29.md)).
