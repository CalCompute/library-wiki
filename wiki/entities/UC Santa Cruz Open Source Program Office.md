# UC Santa Cruz Open Source Program Office

## Why they matter

- The UC Santa Cruz Open Source Program Office (OSPO) hosted CruzCon on Oct. 22, 2026, with the Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) and CITRIS and the Banatao Institute. The day was about "open source in research and innovation" ([UCSC CruzCon 2026 event page (web)](../sources/UCSC%20CruzCon%202026%20event%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The program had a panel, "Campus Infrastructure Supporting Open Source and Research Impact". It also had a discussion, "Institutional Infrastructure, Research & OSPOs" ([UCSC CruzCon 2026 event page (web)](../sources/UCSC%20CruzCon%202026%20event%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
