# UC Irvine

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of California-Irvine in Irvine, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element B: UC Irvine leads MAESTRO, one of the twelve Genesis Mission Phase II projects that DOE announced in October 2026. MAESTRO "will establish an SI platform to accurately map, stimulate, and manage deep underground reservoirs" ([DOE Genesis Mission Phase II awards release 2026-10 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Phase%20II%20awards%20release%202026-10%20%28web%29.md)).
