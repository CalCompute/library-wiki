# Elena Cryst

## Bottom line

- Elena Cryst is the Director of Policy and Society at the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) ([Cryst Stanford HAI profile](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20HAI%20profile.md); [Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- She was in the writing and editing group of the California Report on Frontier AI Policy (2025) ([California Report on Frontier AI Policy 2025-06-17](../sources/California%20Report%20on%20Frontier%20AI%20Policy%202025-06-17.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Stanford HAI](Stanford%20HAI.md): Director of Policy and Society ([Cryst Stanford HAI profile](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20HAI%20profile.md)).
- Earlier: director roles at the Stanford Institute for Economic Policy Research ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- Earlier: director roles at the Freeman Spogli Institute for International Studies and three of its centers, the Cyber Policy Center among them ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).

## Background

- At HAI, she leads the work to bring Stanford's AI research to policymakers worldwide ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- She builds collaborations with civil society, philanthropy and social impact leaders ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- The report lists her in its writing and editing group, at Stanford University, on page 2 ([California Report on Frontier AI Policy 2025-06-17](../sources/California%20Report%20on%20Frontier%20AI%20Policy%202025-06-17.md)).
- She co-wrote the HAI issue brief "Expanding Academia's Role in Public Sector AI" (2024) ([Cryst Expanding Academia's Role in Public Sector AI (web)](../sources/Cryst%20Expanding%20Academia%27s%20Role%20in%20Public%20Sector%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).
- That brief says that companies have more than 1,000 times the compute of universities ([Cryst Expanding Academia's Role in Public Sector AI (web)](../sources/Cryst%20Expanding%20Academia%27s%20Role%20in%20Public%20Sector%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).
- She co-wrote the HAI white paper "Mind the (Language) Gap" (2025), on language models for low-resource languages ([Cryst Mind the Language Gap (web)](../sources/Cryst%20Mind%20the%20Language%20Gap%20%28web%29.md)).
- She co-wrote the HAI article "AI Sovereignty's Definitional Dilemma" (2026) ([Cryst AI Sovereignty's Definitional Dilemma (web)](../sources/Cryst%20AI%20Sovereignty%27s%20Definitional%20Dilemma%20%28web%29.md)).
- She co-wrote the HAI issue brief "The World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era" (2026) ([Cryst World Model and Spatial Intelligence Era (web)](../sources/Cryst%20World%20Model%20and%20Spatial%20Intelligence%20Era%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is the first author of the "Letter from the Editor" in the first issue of the Journal of Online Trust and Safety, 2021 ([Cryst Introducing the Journal of Online Trust and Safety (web)](../sources/Cryst%20Introducing%20the%20Journal%20of%20Online%20Trust%20and%20Safety%20%28web%29.md)).
- B.A., International Relations, Stanford, 2010 ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- M.A., Latin American Studies, Stanford, 2011 ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
- M.B.A., UC Berkeley Haas, 2020 ([Cryst Stanford Profiles](../sources/Cryst%20Stanford%20Profiles.md)).
