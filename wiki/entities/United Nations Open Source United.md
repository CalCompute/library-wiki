# United Nations Open Source United

## Bottom line

- Open Source United runs the Open Source United Portal of the United Nations, at opensource.un.org. The portal lists UN open source initiatives ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The portal lists seven initiatives. They include the Open Source Ecosystem Enabler (OSEE) and an Open Source Programme Office for Trinidad and Tobago ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
- It gives the term and budget of OSEE: "September 2023 to February 2027 with EUR 3 million in funding" ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
