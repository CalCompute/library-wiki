# Los Nettos Regional Network

## Bottom line

- "The Los Nettos Regional Network is a long-standing regional research and education (R&E) network with a history of supporting science and engineering research for its more than 30 members and associates in the greater Los Angeles area" ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- NSF award 2126319 (2021), $999,777 to the University of Southern California, builds "a friction-free regional Science DMZ network across multiple Southern California college campuses": Loyola Marymount University, Occidental College and the Claremont Colleges ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- NSF award 2232917 (2023) to USC's Center for Advanced Research Computing builds on "the existing collaboration between USC and the Los Nettos consortium, comprised of 6 member universities and 30 associate networks" ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
