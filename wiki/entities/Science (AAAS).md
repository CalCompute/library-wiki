# Science (AAAS)

## Bottom line

*Science* is a peer-reviewed journal. It published "The growing influence of industry in AI research" by Nur Ahmed, Muntasir Wahed and Neil C. Thompson in 2023 ([Ahmed Wahed Thompson 2023 Science record (web)](../sources/Ahmed%20Wahed%20Thompson%20industry%20influence%20in%20AI%20research%20Science%202023%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Elements A and E.** The article finds that industry "increasingly dominates the three key ingredients of modern AI research: computing power, large datasets, and highly skilled researchers" ([Ahmed Wahed Thompson 2023 Science record (web)](../sources/Ahmed%20Wahed%20Thompson%20industry%20influence%20in%20AI%20research%20Science%202023%20%28web%29.md)).
