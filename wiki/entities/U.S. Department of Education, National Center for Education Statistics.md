# U.S. Department of Education, National Center for Education Statistics

## Bottom line

- The National Center for Education Statistics (NCES), in the U.S. Department of Education, publishes the Integrated Postsecondary Education Data System (IPEDS). Its HD2025 file gives the county, the latitude and the longitude of each institution by IPEDS unit ID ([IPEDS HD2025 Directory information (web)](../sources/IPEDS%20HD2025%20Directory%20information%20%28web%29.md)).
