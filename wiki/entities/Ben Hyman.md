# Ben Hyman

## Bottom line

Ben Hyman is an economist and senior researcher at the California Policy Lab, on its labor team at UCLA ([Hyman California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/Hyman%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- He helps build the California Policy Lab's "research agenda on AI and the Future of Work" (E) ([Hyman California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/Hyman%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is the first author of the report behind the California AI-Unemployment Tracker (E) ([CPL Tracking AI-Related Job Loss Using UI Claims Data (web)](../sources/CPL%20Tracking%20AI-Related%20Job%20Loss%20Using%20UI%20Claims%20Data%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Governor's release quotes him: "Right now, we are not seeing evidence of large-scale AI-related layoffs in California’s labor market" (E) ([Governor AI-Unemployment Tracker launch release 2026-06-25 (web)](../sources/Governor%20AI-Unemployment%20Tracker%20launch%20release%202026-06-25%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California Policy Lab](California%20Policy%20Lab.md): Senior Researcher, Los Angeles ([Hyman California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/Hyman%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: economist, Research and Statistics Group, Federal Reserve Bank of New York ([Hyman California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/Hyman%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He holds a Ph.D. in Applied Economics from the Wharton School, University of Pennsylvania. He also has a master's degree in urban and regional planning from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology ([Hyman California Policy Lab profile (web)](../sources/Hyman%20California%20Policy%20Lab%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
