# ACCESS, U.S. National Science Foundation

![ACCESS, U.S. National Science Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/ACCESS%2C%20U.S.%20National%20Science%20Foundation.svg)

_Logo: ACCESS, [home page](https://access-ci.org/). Fetched 2026-10-29._

## Why they matter

- Its Acceptable Use Policy (version 1.4, Dec. 9, 2025) says that allocations are "awarded for open research intended for publication". It bans use "for financial gain nor any unlawful purpose" ([ACCESS acceptable use policy (web)](../sources/ACCESS%20acceptable%20use%20policy%20%28web%29.md)).
- ACCESS MATCH Services gives free help from an expert, "typically 1-5 sessions with a mentor" ([ACCESS MATCH services (web)](../sources/ACCESS%20MATCH%20services%20%28web%29.md)).
- The ACCESS Resource Advisor is "a guided questionnaire" that helps new users choose a resource ([ACCESS Resource Advisor announcement (web)](../sources/ACCESS%20Resource%20Advisor%20announcement%20%28web%29.md)).
- ACCESS has an Executive Council of the principal investigators of its awards and an External Advisory Board of 11 members. The Board "meets four times per year, three virtual and one in person" ([ACCESS governance (web)](../sources/ACCESS%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
