# Oregon Legislative Assembly

## Bottom line

- The Oregon Legislative Assembly is the legislature of the State of Oregon. Its 2023 bond bill, House Bill (HB) 5005, authorizes $72,680,000 in bonds for the Oregon State University Collaborative Innovation Complex ([Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed (web)](../sources/Oregon%20HB%205005%202023%20A-Engrossed%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Before the state issues the bonds, HB 5005 requires matching funds "at least equal to the amount of indebtedness incurred by the state" ([Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed (web)](../sources/Oregon%20HB%205005%202023%20A-Engrossed%20%28web%29.md), Section 9).
- Contractors on the complex must use apprentices for 15 percent of work hours. They must give health and retirement benefits ([Oregon HB 5005 2023 A-Engrossed (web)](../sources/Oregon%20HB%205005%202023%20A-Engrossed%20%28web%29.md), Section 11).
