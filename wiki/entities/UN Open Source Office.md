# UN Open Source Office

## Why they matter

- UN Open Source Week 2026 was June 22 to 26 in New York. ODET and OICT co-organize it. June 23 was "Open Source for AI and Emerging Technologies (Open Source x AI)" ([UN Open Source Week homepage](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20Week%20homepage.md)). UC Santa Cruz's Emily Lovell spoke on a university OSPO panel ([UN Open Source Week 2026 agenda](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20Week%202026%20agenda.md)).
- The head of ODET says the Global Digital Compact recognizes "open AI models, as well as open access to training data, and compute resources" ([UN Open Source Week 2025 Conference Report, p. 16](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20Week%202025%20Conference%20Report.md)).
