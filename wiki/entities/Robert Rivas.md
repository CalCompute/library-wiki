# Robert Rivas

![Robert Rivas](../../raw/assets/people/Robert%20Rivas.jpg)

_Photo: California State Assembly, [member page](https://www.assembly.ca.gov/assemblymembers/29). Fetched 2026-11-14._

## Bottom line

- Robert Rivas is the 71st Speaker of the California State Assembly ([Rivas Assembly Daily Journal 2023-06-30 (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20Daily%20Journal%202023-06-30%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is the Assembly Member for District 29 ([Rivas Assembly member profile (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20member%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Assembly](California%20State%20Assembly.md): Assembly Member, District 29 ([Rivas Assembly member profile (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20member%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- [California State Assembly](California%20State%20Assembly.md): Speaker, from June 30, 2023 ([Rivas Assembly Daily Journal 2023-06-30 (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20Daily%20Journal%202023-06-30%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- District 29 includes parts of Monterey, San Benito, Santa Clara and Santa Cruz counties ([Rivas Assembly member profile (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20member%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- His party is Democrat ([Rivas Assembly member profile (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20member%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a member of the Joint Committee on Rules ([Rivas Assembly member profile (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20member%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Assembly elected him Speaker by House Resolution No. 1 on Dec. 5, 2022 ([Rivas Assembly Daily Journal 2023-06-30 (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20Daily%20Journal%202023-06-30%20%28web%29.md)).
- He took the oath of office as Speaker on June 30, 2023. Chief Justice Patricia Guerrero gave the oath ([Rivas Assembly Daily Journal 2023-06-30 (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20Daily%20Journal%202023-06-30%20%28web%29.md)).
- In his address as Speaker, he said: "my grandfather, he was a farmworker his entire life and I grew up in farmworker housing" ([Rivas Assembly Daily Journal 2023-06-30 (web)](../sources/Rivas%20Assembly%20Daily%20Journal%202023-06-30%20%28web%29.md)).
