# Cielo V. Castro

## Bottom line

Cielo V. Castro is the Chief Impact Officer at the California Community Foundation (CCF), in Los Angeles County. The CSU Foundation lists her on its Meet the Board and Staff pages ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- At CCF, she leads four teams: "Community Investment & Engagement, Policy & Advocacy, Impact & Outcomes, and Grantmaking" (B) ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was "the principal architect" of the CCF 2025–2035 Strategic Framework ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- California Community Foundation: Chief Impact Officer ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- [California State University](California%20State%20University.md): listed on the CSU Foundation's Meet the Board and Staff pages ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Housing Authority of the City of Los Angeles: Board Chair ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- Hispanics in Philanthropy: board member ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: Fairplex, where she led its change into "a county-designated community benefit organization" ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- She holds a B.S. from Boston University and an M.P.A. from the Harvard Kennedy School. She is a 2024 Eisenhower USA Fellow ([Cielo V. Castro CSU Foundation profile (web)](../sources/Cielo%20V.%20Castro%20CSU%20Foundation%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
