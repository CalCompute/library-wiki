# University of Florida

## Why they matter

- UF has an agreement with the Board of Governors of the State University System of Florida. Under it, "all faculty at universities in the State of Florida can use HiPerGator" ([UF Research Computing Other Universities policy (web)](../sources/UF%20Research%20Computing%20Other%20Universities%20policy%20%28web%29.md)).
- HiPerGator's advisory body is the Research Computing Advisory Workgroup, "a topical workgroup in the IT Governance structure" ([UF Research Computing Advisory Workgroup (web)](../sources/UF%20Research%20Computing%20Advisory%20Workgroup%20%28web%29.md)).
