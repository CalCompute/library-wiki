# Project Jupyter

![Project Jupyter](../../raw/assets/logos/Project%20Jupyter.svg)

_Logo: Project Jupyter, [home page](https://jupyter.org/). Fetched 2026-10-29._

## Bottom line

- Project Jupyter is an open-source software project. Three bodies govern it. One of them, the Jupyter Foundation, is "a directed fund of the Linux Foundation 501(c)(6)" ([Jupyter governance overview page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20governance%20overview%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Jupyter changed its governance model in December 2022. It replaced a "Benevolent Dictator For Life (BDFL) + Steering Council governance model" ([Jupyter governance overview page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20governance%20overview%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Executive Council raises and manages "all Project funding and sponsorships" ([Jupyter Executive Council page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20Executive%20Council%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- LF Charities is "Project Jupyter’s parent nonprofit organization and legal entity" ([Jupyter Executive Council page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20Executive%20Council%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- All Jupyter councils decide by consensus seeking, with a vote by simple majority and a 50% quorum when needed ([Jupyter decision-making guidelines (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20decision-making%20guidelines%20%28web%29.md)).

- "“Jupyter” is a trademark of LF Charities, of which Project Jupyter is a part." Any commercial use of the marks in product or company names "must be approved first by Project Jupyter" ([Jupyter trademark guidelines (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20trademark%20guidelines%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- The Linux Foundation "holds assets in custody for Project Jupyter" ([Jupyter Executive Council page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20Executive%20Council%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
