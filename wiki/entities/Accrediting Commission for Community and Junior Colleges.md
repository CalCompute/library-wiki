# Accrediting Commission for Community and Junior Colleges

## Bottom line

- The Accrediting Commission for Community and Junior Colleges (ACCJC) accredits community and junior colleges. Its office is in Sacramento ([ACCJC directory of institutions (web)](../sources/ACCJC%20directory%20of%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its directory lists 139 institutions; it gives "California" as the state of 124 of them ([ACCJC directory of institutions (web)](../sources/ACCJC%20directory%20of%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).
