# San José State University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as San Jose State University in San Jose, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- SJSU IT offers a private cloud and enterprise accounts on Google Cloud Platform and Microsoft Azure for research. It also hosts High Performance Computing (HPC) systems that colleges buy with grants ([SJSU IT computational resources (web)](../sources/SJSU%20IT%20computational%20resources%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2024, SJSU was one of four campuses that "may be able to use" Cal-ICOR early ([Berkeley VCR Cal-ICOR compute hub launch (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20VCR%20Cal-ICOR%20compute%20hub%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
