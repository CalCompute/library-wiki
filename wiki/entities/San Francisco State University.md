# San Francisco State University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as San Francisco State University in San Francisco, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 2: High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: its central cluster, POLARIS, has 12 NVIDIA A100 80GB on three nodes. Students get access through a faculty request ([SF State HPC overview (web)](../sources/SF%20State%20HPC%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
