# Vera Zakem

## Bottom line

- Vera Zakem is State Chief Technology Innovation Officer at the California Department of Technology (CDT), appointed by Governor Newsom ([Zakem Berkeley I School profile (web)](../sources/Zakem%20Berkeley%20I%20School%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California Department of Technology](California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md): Chief Technology Innovation Officer ([Zakem Berkeley I School profile (web)](../sources/Zakem%20Berkeley%20I%20School%20profile%20%28web%29.md); [CDT executive team](../sources/CDT%20executive%20team.md)).
- past: Chief Digital Democracy and Rights Officer, USAID ([Zakem Berkeley I School profile (web)](../sources/Zakem%20Berkeley%20I%20School%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The Governor announced her appointment on February 13, 2025. The post "does not require Senate confirmation, and the compensation is $192,000" ([Governor appointments 2025-02-13 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202025-02-13%20%28web%29.md)).
- She earned a Master of Arts in Government at Johns Hopkins University and a Bachelor of Arts in Politics at the University of San Francisco ([Governor appointments 2025-02-13 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202025-02-13%20%28web%29.md)).
