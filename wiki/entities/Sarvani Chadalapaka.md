# Sarvani Chadalapaka

## Bottom line

- Sarvani Chadalapaka is director of Cyberinfrastructure and Research Technologies at UC Merced ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She is the principal investigator (PI) of CENVAL-ARC, a regional computing hub for the Central Valley ([Chadalapaka NSF award 2346744](../sources/Chadalapaka%20NSF%20award%202346744.md)).

## Affiliations

- [UC Merced](../entities/UC%20Merced.md): director of Cyberinfrastructure and Research Technologies ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).

## Background

- CENVAL-ARC is National Science Foundation (NSF) award 2346744: $1,000,000 from June 1, 2024 to May 31, 2027 ([Chadalapaka NSF award 2346744](../sources/Chadalapaka%20NSF%20award%202346744.md)).
- UC Merced leads CENVAL-ARC with CSU Sacramento, CSU Stanislaus and CSU Fresno ([Chadalapaka NSF award 2346744](../sources/Chadalapaka%20NSF%20award%202346744.md)).
- The co-PIs are Anna Klimaszewski-Patterson (CSU Sacramento), Suzanne S Sindi and Emily Jane McTavish (UC Merced) ([Chadalapaka NSF award 2346744](../sources/Chadalapaka%20NSF%20award%202346744.md)).
- CENVAL-ARC adds GPU and CPU nodes to UC Merced's Pinnacles cluster, for Hispanic-Serving institutions in the Central Valley ([Chadalapaka NSF award 2346744](../sources/Chadalapaka%20NSF%20award%202346744.md)).
- Her award nomination says she grew the researchers on core high-performance computing (HPC) resources "from 40 to 600" ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She won the 2023 UC Tech Awards silver award for diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) leadership ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She created a worldwide HPC mentorship program with Women in HPC ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- The mentorship program "operates across five continents, over 25 countries, and with over 60 volunteers" ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She was DEI Co-Chair of the PEARC (Practice and Experience in Advanced Research Computing) conference ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- As DEI Co-Chair, she worked for "on-site childcare and dependent care grants" ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She received the Internet2 Inclusivity Initiative Scholarship Award in 2017 ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She later co-chaired the steering committee of the Internet2 Inclusivity Initiative ([Chadalapaka UC Tech award 2023](../sources/Chadalapaka%20UC%20Tech%20award%202023.md)).
- She is first author of "Cultivating Computational Equity: Building a Collaborative Research Computing Community in California's Central Valley", at PEARC '25 ([PEARC 2025 Cultivating Computational Equity Central Valley](../sources/PEARC%202025%20Cultivating%20Computational%20Equity%20Central%20Valley.md)).
- She co-wrote "Kickstarting Research Support at Under-Resourced Institutions", a 2024 ACM paper ([Chadalapaka PEARC 2024 starter guide NSF PAR record (web)](../sources/Chadalapaka%20PEARC%202024%20starter%20guide%20NSF%20PAR%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
