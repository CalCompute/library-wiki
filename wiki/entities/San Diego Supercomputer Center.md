# San Diego Supercomputer Center

## Bottom line

- The San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at UC San Diego runs national compute services. They include the NSF NAIRR Operations Center, with the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC).

## Why they matter

- The National Science Foundation (NSF) gave SDSC $35 million for five years. The money runs the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Operations Center ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)). SDSC says the national plan is to "integrate leading existing capabilities rather than build isolated infrastructure" (same source).

## Affiliations

- UC San Diego: a center of UC San Diego ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).
