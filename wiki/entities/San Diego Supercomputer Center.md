# San Diego Supercomputer Center

## Bottom line

- The San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at UC San Diego runs national compute services. They include the NSF NAIRR Operations Center, with the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC).

## Why they matter

- The National Science Foundation (NSF) gave SDSC $35 million for five years. The money runs the National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) Operations Center ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)). SDSC says the national plan is to "integrate leading existing capabilities rather than build isolated infrastructure" (same source).

- SDSC rents rack space to researchers who buy their own equipment, in a data center with "13 Megawatts of power". UC researchers get it "through their campus representatives" at monthly recharge rates ([SDSC colocation services (web)](../sources/SDSC%20colocation%20services%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its colocation terms bar Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and Protected Health Information (PHI) under the standard agreement ([SDSC colocation terms of service 2025 (web)](../sources/SDSC%20colocation%20terms%20of%20service%202025%20%28web%29.md)).
- SDSC operates Expanse, an NSF-funded ACCESS cluster "delivering 5.16 peak petaflops". Time on its Expanse AI nodes, with NVIDIA H100 GPUs, "is allocated via the NAIRR Pilot Program" ([SDSC Expanse User Guide (web)](../sources/SDSC%20Expanse%20User%20Guide%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- UC San Diego: a center of UC San Diego ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).
