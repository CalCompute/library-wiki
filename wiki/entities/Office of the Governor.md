# Office of the Governor

## Why they matter

- Directive 9 asked the Government Operations Agency for options by Oct. 15, 2026. They include "securing dedicated access to computing power for research and development of AI that meets specified criteria for advancing the public good" ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
- The same directive names programs that direct "a portion of revenue generated by AI companies" to public-good uses ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
