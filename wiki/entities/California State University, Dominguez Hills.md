# California State University, Dominguez Hills

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California State University-Dominguez Hills in Carson, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- NIH grant S10GM164901 (2026), $224,992, pays for "A POWERWULF™ ZXR1 HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTING (HPC) ENGINE", a shared system for "NINE MAJOR USERS ACROSS FIVE DEPARTMENTS" and five courses ([USAspending NIH award S10GM164901 CSU Dominguez Hills HPC engine (web)](../sources/USAspending%20NIH%20award%20S10GM164901%20CSU%20Dominguez%20Hills%20HPC%20engine%20%28web%29.md)).
