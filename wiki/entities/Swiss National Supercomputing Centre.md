# Swiss National Supercomputing Centre

## Bottom line

- The Swiss National Supercomputing Centre (CSCS) is the national high-performance computing centre of Switzerland, in Lugano. ETH Zurich operates it ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The Swiss Confederation funds CSCS "through the Board of the Federal Institutes of Technology and ETH Zurich" ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its User Lab gives compute "free of charge" to Swiss and international researchers, through peer review ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its main system, Alps, has 10,752 NVIDIA Grace Hopper superchips ([ETH Zurich Alps inauguration release 2024-09 (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20Alps%20inauguration%20release%202024-09%20%28web%29.md)). Alps started production in January 2025 ([CSCS brochure 2026 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20brochure%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Alps can make software-defined clusters (vClusters) for each user community ([ETH Zurich Alps inauguration release 2024-09 (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20Alps%20inauguration%20release%202024-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- CSCS is "a service and research unit of ETH Zurich" ([ETH Zurich RSETHZ 437.1 CSCS terms (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20RSETHZ%20437.1%20CSCS%20regulations%20%28web%29.md)). It reports to ETH Zurich's Vice President for Research ([CSCS organization (web)](../sources/CSCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md)).
- "ETH Zurich/CSCS is the sole owner of the HPC resources and HPC systems", even when a partner pays ([ETH Zurich RSETHZ 437.1 CSCS terms (web)](../sources/ETH%20Zurich%20RSETHZ%20437.1%20CSCS%20regulations%20%28web%29.md)).
- A Scientific Advisory Committee of five permanent members "gives recommendations on the project allocations" ([CSCS organization (web)](../sources/CSCS%20organization%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2022 its income was CHF 57.9 million. Of this, CHF 21.8 million came from ETH Zurich and CHF 23.4 million from the ETH Board ([CSCS annual report 2022 (web)](../sources/CSCS%20annual%20report%202022%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations
