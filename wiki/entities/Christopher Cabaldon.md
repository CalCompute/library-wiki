# Christopher Cabaldon

![Christopher Cabaldon](../../raw/assets/people/Christopher%20Cabaldon.jpg)

_Photo: California State Senate, [biography page](https://sd03.senate.ca.gov/biography). Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

Christopher Cabaldon represents the Third Senate District in the California State Senate. Voters elected him in 2024 ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- He led the work of the nation's mayors on "college and career pathways" and on "rebooting workforce development" (E) ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was vice chancellor of the California Community Colleges (E) ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Senate](California%20State%20Senate.md): Senator, District 3 ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- Sacramento State: Hazel Cramer Endowed Chair and Professor of Public Policy and Administration, appointed in 2019 ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: Mayor of West Sacramento for two decades; California's member of the Western Interstate Commission on Higher Education ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He earned a bachelor's degree in environmental economics at UC Berkeley and a master's degree at Sacramento State ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- The page says he is "the first Filipino American ever to serve in the California Senate" ([Cabaldon Senate biography (web)](../sources/Cabaldon%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
