# UC Santa Barbara

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of California-Santa Barbara in Santa Barbara, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Three NSF awards since 2019 pay for campus computing. Award 1925717 (2019) is a GPU cluster with "24 state-of-the-art NVIDIA Tesla V100 32 GB GPUs". Award 2503029 (2025) is the DataSHORE storage system, and award 2509729 (2026) a central computing cluster ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- DataSHORE gives "25% of its capacity" to the Open Science Data Federation ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
