# Academic Professionals of California

## Bottom line

- Academic Professionals of California (APC) represents CSU's Unit 4. Its agreement runs from Nov. 20, 2024 to June 30, 2027 ([CSU APC Unit 4 contract 2024-2027 (web)](../sources/CSU%20APC%20Unit%204%20contract%202024-2027%20%28web%29.md)).
- A tentative agreement of Oct. 7, 2026 reopens salary, benefits and two other articles, and extends the term to June 30, 2028 ([CSU APC Unit 4 tentative agreement 2026-10-07 (web)](../sources/CSU%20APC%20Unit%204%20tentative%20agreement%202026-10-07%20%28web%29.md)).
