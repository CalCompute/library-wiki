# Leilani H. Gilpin

## Bottom line

- Leilani H. Gilpin is an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering at UC Santa Cruz ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She leads the AI Explainability and Accountability (AIEA) Lab ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).

## Affiliations

- [UC Santa Cruz](../entities/UC%20Santa%20Cruz.md), Jack Baskin School of Engineering: Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering since 2021 ([Gilpin ORCID record](../sources/Gilpin%20ORCID%20record.md)).
- [AI Explainability and Accountability Lab, UC Santa Cruz](../entities/AI%20Explainability%20and%20Accountability%20Lab%2C%20UC%20Santa%20Cruz.md): her lab ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md); [Gilpin AIEA Lab homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20AIEA%20Lab%20homepage.md)).
- UC Santa Cruz, Science & Justice Research Center: affiliate ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- UC Santa Cruz, Generative AI Center: affiliate ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).

## Background

- She was a research scientist at Sony AI, on explainability in AI agents ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from MIT (2020) ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She holds an M.S. in Computational and Mathematical Engineering from Stanford (2013) ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She holds a BSc in Computer Science and Mathematics from UC San Diego (2011) ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- Her research is on machines that explain themselves "for diagnosis, accountability, and liability" ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She won an Air Force Office of Scientific Research Young Investigator Program (AFOSR YIP) grant in December 2023 ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She was general chair of the 19th Conference on Neurosymbolic Learning and Reasoning (NeSy 2025) ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- Her lab works on faithful reasoning for "high-stakes use cases" ([Gilpin AIEA Lab homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20AIEA%20Lab%20homepage.md)).
- Her lab works on neuro-symbolic AI for legal use cases ([Gilpin AIEA Lab homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20AIEA%20Lab%20homepage.md)).
- She is a co-author of "Explaining explanations: An overview of interpretability of machine learning" (IEEE DSAA, 2018) ([Gilpin ORCID record](../sources/Gilpin%20ORCID%20record.md)).
