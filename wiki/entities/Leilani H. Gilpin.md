# Leilani H. Gilpin

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: UC Santa Cruz
- Title: Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Engineering
- Role: AI Safety Researcher & Developer

## Background

- She has been an Assistant Professor at UC Santa Cruz since 2021. She was a research scientist at Sony AI ([Gilpin ORCID record](../sources/Gilpin%20ORCID%20record.md); [Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She holds a PhD in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from MIT (2020), an M.S. from Stanford and a BSc from UC San Diego ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- Her research is on machines that explain themselves "for diagnosis, accountability, and liability" ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- She won an AFOSR Young Investigator Program grant in December 2023, and chaired the NeSy 2025 conference ([Gilpin UCSC homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20UCSC%20homepage.md)).
- Her lab works on faithful reasoning for "high-stakes use cases" and on AI for law ([Gilpin AIEA Lab homepage](../sources/Gilpin%20AIEA%20Lab%20homepage.md)).
