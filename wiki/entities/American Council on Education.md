# American Council on Education

## Bottom line

- The American Council on Education (ACE) publishes the Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education. Its 2025 Public Data File gives the 2025 Research Activity Designation of each institution ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
