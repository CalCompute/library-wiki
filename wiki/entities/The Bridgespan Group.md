# The Bridgespan Group

## Bottom line

The Bridgespan Group "is a global nonprofit that strives to make the world more equitable and just." With NTEN, it published the 2026 State of Nonprofit AI report ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In its 2026 report with NTEN, most nonprofit executives have no AI budget. Most also lack tools and infrastructure for AI (B, D) ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- It says that "Since 2000, we have had the privilege of advising and collaborating with many of the world’s most extraordinary social change leaders" ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Nate Wong signs the report's introduction for The Bridgespan Group ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
