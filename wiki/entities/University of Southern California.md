# University of Southern California

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of Southern California in Los Angeles, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Private not-for-profit" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In 2016, Marc and Lynne Benioff gave it $20 million toward the building of the Lawrence J. Ellison Institute for Transformative Medicine of USC (B, F) ([USC Today Marc and Lynne Benioff (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Marc%20and%20Lynne%20Benioff%20%28web%29.md)).
- Mark and Mary Stevens "have donated more than $100 million to USC programs and entities", among them a $22 million endowment in 2004 for technology commercialization (B, F) ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
