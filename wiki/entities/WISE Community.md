# WISE Community

## Bottom line

Members of the WISE Community SCI Working Group wrote the WISE Baseline Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), a template for research infrastructures ([WISE Baseline AUP v1 (web)](../sources/WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20v1%20%28web%29.md)). The AARC guide calls the group the "WISE Information Security for e-Infrastructures SCI working Group" ([AARC-I044 Implementers Guide to the WISE Baseline AUP (web)](../sources/AARC-I044%20Implementers%20Guide%20to%20the%20WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Elements D and F: the ten clauses of the baseline AUP are fixed. Their "wording and numbering must not be changed" ([WISE Baseline AUP v1 (web)](../sources/WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20v1%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The template draws on the "EGI Acceptable Use Policy and Conditions of Use" ([WISE Baseline AUP v1 (web)](../sources/WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20v1%20%28web%29.md)).
