# University Council-American Federation of Teachers

## Bottom line

- The University Council-American Federation of Teachers (UC-AFT) represents UC's non-Senate instructional faculty, the IX unit ([UC UC-AFT IX lecturer contract 2021-2026 (web)](../sources/UC%20UC-AFT%20IX%20lecturer%20contract%202021-2026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its agreement ran from Nov. 23, 2021 to June 30, 2026. An extension takes it to June 30, 2027 ([UC IX contract page (web)](../sources/UC%20IX%20contract%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
