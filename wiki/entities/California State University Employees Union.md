# California State University Employees Union

## Bottom line

- California State University Employees Union (CSUEU) represents CSU's Units 2, 5, 7 and 9. Unit 9 is technical support ([CSU CSUEU contract Units 2 5 7 9 2023-2026 (web)](../sources/CSU%20CSUEU%20contract%20Units%202%205%207%209%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its agreement with CSU ran from July 31, 2022 to June 30, 2026 ([CSU CSUEU contract Units 2 5 7 9 2023-2026 (web)](../sources/CSU%20CSUEU%20contract%20Units%202%205%207%209%202023-2026%20%28web%29.md)).
- A tentative agreement of Oct. 31, 2026 runs from ratification to June 30, 2029. It adds a memorandum on artificial intelligence systems ([CSU CSUEU tentative agreement 2026-10-31 (web)](../sources/CSU%20CSUEU%20tentative%20agreement%202026-10-31%20%28web%29.md)).
