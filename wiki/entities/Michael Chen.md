# Michael Chen

## Bottom line

- Michael Chen is the AI Science Advisor to the California Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES). The California Council on Science and Technology (CCST) placed him there in June 2026 ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California Governor's Office of Emergency Services](California%20Governor%27s%20Office%20of%20Emergency%20Services.md): AI Science Advisor, CCST AI Science Residency Program ([Chen CCST AI Science Advisor announcement (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Advisor%20announcement%20%28web%29.md)).
- [California Council on Science and Technology](California%20Council%20on%20Science%20and%20Technology.md): AI Science Advisor. CCST formally employs its AI Science Advisors ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He advises the senior leadership of Cal OES on frontier AI safety and risk assessment ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- His work has "a particular emphasis on critical safety incidents, AI and cyber defense, and risk from developers' internal deployment of AI" ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- He reports to Commander Matthew Sage of the California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- He worked on AI policy at METR, an AI evaluations nonprofit in Berkeley, California ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- At METR, he helped AI developers design frontier safety policies and gave input on public policy ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He helped with third-party evaluations, including a review of a developer's report on sabotage risk from AI agents ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- He worked with U.S. government bodies on frontier AI evaluation. One is the Center for AI Standards and Innovation at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- He did research at the UC Berkeley Center for Human-Compatible AI on learning human preferences for language models ([Chen CCST AI Science Residency launch (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Residency%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- Before METR, he was a software engineer at Stripe ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a part-time PhD student at the University of Oxford and an affiliate of the Oxford Martin AI Governance Initiative ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He coauthored "AI deception: A survey of examples, risks, and potential solutions" in *Patterns*, 2024 ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He says that *The Guardian* and *MIT Technology Review* covered that paper, and that *Time* interviewed him ([Chen personal website (web)](../sources/Chen%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
