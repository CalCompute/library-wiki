# PyTorch Foundation

## Bottom line

- The PyTorch Foundation is the host foundation of the PyTorch project. In 2025 it announced its "expansion to an umbrella foundation" for more projects ([PyTorch Foundation welcomes vLLM (web)](../sources/PyTorch%20Foundation%20welcomes%20vLLM%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- On May 7, 2025, it took in vLLM, "Contributed by the University of California – Berkeley", as a hosted project ([PyTorch Foundation welcomes vLLM (web)](../sources/PyTorch%20Foundation%20welcomes%20vLLM%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its hosted projects are "officially governed and administered under the PyTorch Foundation’s neutral and transparent governance model" ([PyTorch Foundation welcomes vLLM (web)](../sources/PyTorch%20Foundation%20welcomes%20vLLM%20%28web%29.md)).
