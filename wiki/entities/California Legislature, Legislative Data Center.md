# California Legislature, Legislative Data Center

## Bottom line

- The Legislative Data Center keeps agencyreports.ca.gov, a website on agency reports to the Legislature ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](../sources/Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its database has one record for "CalCompute": the report under "Section 11546.8 of the Government Code", due to the Legislature "On or before January 1, 2027". The File Date is empty on Nov. 11, 2026 ([California Agency Reports CalCompute report record (web)](../sources/California%20Agency%20Reports%20CalCompute%20report%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its Filing Reports page names the three offices that get each required report ([California Agency Reports filing reports page (web)](../sources/California%20Agency%20Reports%20filing%20reports%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Assembly](California%20State%20Assembly.md): its journal names the center as keeper of the site ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](../sources/Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
