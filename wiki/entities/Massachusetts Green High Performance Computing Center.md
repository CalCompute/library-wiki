# Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center

## Bottom line

- The Massachusetts Green High Performance Computing Center (MGHPCC) is a computing facility in Holyoke, Massachusetts. A nonprofit corporation whose members are six universities operates it ([MGHPCC governance (web)](../sources/MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its members are Boston University, Harvard University, the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, Northeastern University, the University of Massachusetts and Yale University ([MGHPCC governance (web)](../sources/MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
- A Program Management Group handles "security, shared network infrastructure, space and power utilization, energy efficiency, budgeting, and cost allocation" ([MGHPCC governance (web)](../sources/MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
- The state's AI Compute Resources (AICR) environment is to be built at MGHPCC ([MassTech AICR builder selection (web)](../sources/MassTech%20AICR%20builder%20selection%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Board chair: Robert Brown, Boston University. Executive director: John Goodhue ([MGHPCC governance (web)](../sources/MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
- Address: 100 Bigelow Street, Holyoke, MA 01040 ([MGHPCC governance (web)](../sources/MGHPCC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
