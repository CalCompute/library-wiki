# Ohio Supercomputer Center

## Bottom line

- The Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC) is a supercomputer center in Ohio. The Ohio General Assembly funds it "through the Ohio Department of Higher Education" ([OSC governance (web)](../sources/OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its User Code of Ethics says that OSC serves "students, faculty, staff members, and commercial clients throughout the state of Ohio". OSC can suspend an account while it investigates a possible violation ([OSC User Code of Ethics (web)](../sources/OSC%20User%20Code%20of%20Ethics%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its Engage page asks users to "Help shape FUTURE PLANS". The routes are OSC leadership, the OSC Community Briefing and feedback on the Client Portal (C) ([OSC Engage page (web)](../sources/OSC%20Engage%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
