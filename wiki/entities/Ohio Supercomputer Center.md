# Ohio Supercomputer Center

## Bottom line

- The Ohio Supercomputer Center (OSC) is a supercomputer center in Ohio. The Ohio General Assembly funds it "through the Ohio Department of Higher Education" ([OSC governance (web)](../sources/OSC%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its User Code of Ethics says that OSC serves "students, faculty, staff members, and commercial clients throughout the state of Ohio". OSC can suspend an account while it investigates a possible violation ([OSC User Code of Ethics (web)](../sources/OSC%20User%20Code%20of%20Ethics%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its Engage page asks users to "Help shape FUTURE PLANS". The routes are OSC leadership, the OSC Community Briefing and feedback on the Client Portal (C) ([OSC Engage page (web)](../sources/OSC%20Engage%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its state line is GRF 235510, $5,086,000 in each of FY 2026 and FY 2027, legal basis R.C. 3333.04(T) ([Ohio LSC 2025 catalog of budget line items, Higher Education (web)](../sources/Ohio%20LSC%202025%20catalog%20of%20budget%20line%20items%2C%20Higher%20Education%20%28web%29.md)).
- Under Ohio Revised Code § 3333.04(T), the chancellor appoints consortia of college and university personnel for OSC. The chancellor names a college or university as fiscal agent and employer ([Ohio Revised Code 3333.04 (web)](../sources/Ohio%20Revised%20Code%203333.04%20%28web%29.md)).
- The OSC-OARnet Advisory Board has 12 members. They are campus IT leaders, staff of Honda R&D Americas and Procter & Gamble, and the Chancellor of the Ohio Department of Higher Education. The OSC and OARnet executive directors also sit on it ([OSC and OARnet Advisory Board (web)](../sources/OSC%20and%20OARnet%20Advisory%20Board%20%28web%29.md)).
