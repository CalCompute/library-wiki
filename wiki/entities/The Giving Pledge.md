# The Giving Pledge

## Bottom line

The Giving Pledge "is a promise by the world's wealthiest philanthropists to give the majority of their wealth to charitable causes in their lifetime or wills." Its site lists the pledgers and gives a page with a letter for each ([Giving Pledge pledger list (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20pledger%20list%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its list includes "Lynne and Marc Benioff", "John A. and Susan Sobrato" and "Mark and Mary Stevens". Other sources here record their gifts to California universities (B, F) ([Giving Pledge pledger list (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20pledger%20list%20%28web%29.md)).
- The pledge letter of Henry and Susan Samueli names "enhancing engineering education at the university level" as one of their aims (B, F) ([Giving Pledge Henry and Susan Samueli (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20Henry%20and%20Susan%20Samueli%20%28web%29.md)).
