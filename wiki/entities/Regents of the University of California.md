# Regents of the University of California

## Bottom line

- The Regents of the University of California are the governing board of UC. Their policies bind the campuses ([UC Regents Policy 5203 campus foundations (web)](../sources/UC%20Regents%20Policy%205203%20campus%20foundations%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Regents Policy 5203 sets the rules for support groups, campus foundations and affiliated organizations. They need Chancellor recognition and dedicated assets, and may not conduct research or pay UC staff outside UC policy ([UC Regents Policy 5203 campus foundations (web)](../sources/UC%20Regents%20Policy%205203%20campus%20foundations%20%28web%29.md)).
