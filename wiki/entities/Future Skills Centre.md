# Future Skills Centre

## Bottom line

The Future Skills Centre (FSC) is a Canadian skills research centre in Toronto. The Government of Canada's Future Skills Program funds its reports ([Future Skills Centre Adoption Ready public sector AI exposure (web)](../sources/Future%20Skills%20Centre%20Adoption%20Ready%20public%20sector%20AI%20exposure%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Element G.** An FSC project with The Dais found that 74 percent of Canada's public sector workers are in AI-exposed occupations. The figure for the whole workforce is 56 percent ([Future Skills Centre Adoption Ready public sector AI exposure (web)](../sources/Future%20Skills%20Centre%20Adoption%20Ready%20public%20sector%20AI%20exposure%20%28web%29.md)).
