# UAW Local 4123

## Bottom line

- UAW Local 4123 represents CSU's Unit 11, the academic student employees ([CSU UAW Unit 11 contract 2023-2025 (web)](../sources/CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%202023-2025%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Unit 11 agreement ran from Nov. 7, 2023 to Oct. 31, 2025. An extension kept it in force to Sept. 30, 2026 ([CSU UAW Unit 11 contract extension 2026-03-02 (web)](../sources/CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20contract%20extension%202026-03-02%20%28web%29.md)).
- A tentative agreement of Oct. 16, 2026 runs from ratification to Oct. 31, 2028 ([CSU UAW Unit 11 tentative agreement 2026-10-16 (web)](../sources/CSU%20UAW%20Unit%2011%20tentative%20agreement%202026-10-16%20%28web%29.md)).
