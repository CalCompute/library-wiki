# UC Berkeley

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of California-Berkeley in Berkeley, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- UC Berkeley runs the Campus AI Sandbox, a web chatbot with models from several vendors. The pilot began on March 20, 2025, for faculty, staff and student workers ([Berkeley Campus AI Sandbox page (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20Campus%20AI%20Sandbox%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- One aim of the pilot is to understand costs "to enable a sustainable funding model for this" ([Berkeley Campus AI Sandbox page (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20Campus%20AI%20Sandbox%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The California Education Learning Lab gave UC Berkeley a $1.5 million grant for Cal-ICOR. Cal-ICOR is a JupyterHub course hub for California public colleges and universities ([Berkeley VCR Cal-ICOR compute hub launch (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20VCR%20Cal-ICOR%20compute%20hub%20launch%20%28web%29.md)).
- A 2018 paper says that UC Berkeley's JupyterHub "subsists on cloud credits" from cloud vendors. It then served "more than 3,000 students in Berkeley’s Data Science connectors, modules, and upper division courses" ([SciPy Majora-4af1f417-002 JupyterHub paper (web)](../sources/SciPy%20Majora-4af1f417-002%20JupyterHub%20paper%20%28web%29.md), p. 15).
- UC Berkeley contributed the vLLM inference engine to the PyTorch Foundation in May 2025 ([PyTorch Foundation welcomes vLLM (web)](../sources/PyTorch%20Foundation%20welcomes%20vLLM%20%28web%29.md)).
