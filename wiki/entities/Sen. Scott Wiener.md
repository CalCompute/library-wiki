# Sen. Scott Wiener

![Sen. Scott Wiener](../../raw/assets/people/Sen.%20Scott%20Wiener.jpg)

_Photo: California State Senate, Public domain, via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Scott_Wiener_Official_Biography_Portrait.jpg). Fetched 2026-10-26._

## Bottom line

- Scott Wiener represents District 11 in the California State Senate. Voters first elected him in 2016 ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Senate](California%20State%20Senate.md): State Senator, author of SB-53 ([Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md)).

## Background

- District 11 "includes San Francisco, Broadmoor, Colma, and Daly City, as well as portions of South San Francisco and San Bruno" ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He "has authored over 100 bills that have been signed into law" ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is Chair of the Senate Elections Committee ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was Chair of the Senate Budget Committee, the Senate Housing Committee and the Senate Human Services Committee ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is Co-Chair of the California Legislative Jewish Caucus and Chair of the Senate Mental Health Caucus ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was Chair of the California Legislative LGBTQ Caucus ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a member of the Public Safety, Judiciary, Transportation and Privacy Committees ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- Before the Senate, he served on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He chaired the San Francisco County Transportation Authority ([Wiener Senate District 11 homepage (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20District%2011%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- He practiced law for fifteen years. He was a Deputy City Attorney in San Francisco for nearly a decade ([Wiener Senate District 11 homepage (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20District%2011%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- He also practiced law at Heller Ehrman White & McAuliffe and was a law clerk on the New Jersey Supreme Court ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He has a bachelor's degree from Duke University and a law degree from Harvard Law School ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He spent a year in Chile on a Fulbright Scholarship ([Wiener Senate biography (web)](../sources/Wiener%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- On Sept. 13, 2023, his office announced SB 294, an intent bill ([Wiener SB 294 announcement 2023-09-13](../sources/Wiener%20SB%20294%20announcement%202023-09-13.md)).
- He introduced SB 1047 on Feb. 7, 2024 ([Wiener SB 1047 introduction release 2024-02-07 (web)](../sources/Wiener%20SB%201047%20introduction%20release%202024-02-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- On Feb. 28, 2025, his office announced that he introduced SB 53 ([Wiener SB-53 introduction release 2025-02-28 (web)](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20introduction%20release%202025-02-28%20%28web%29.md)).
- On July 9, 2025, he announced amendments that added frontier AI transparency rules to SB 53 ([Wiener SB-53 expansion release 2025-07-09](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20expansion%20release%202025-07-09.md)).
- The statute is "Senate Bill No. 53 (Wiener), Chapter 138, Statutes of 2025" ([SB-53 Gov Code 11546.8](../sources/SB-53%20Gov%20Code%2011546.8.md)).
- On Sept. 29, 2025, he said: "With this law, California is stepping up, once again, as a global leader on both technology innovation and safety" ([Wiener SB-53 signing release 2025-09-29 (web)](../sources/Wiener%20SB-53%20signing%20release%202025-09-29%20%28web%29.md)).
