# Genesis Mission Consortium

## Bottom line

- The Genesis Mission Consortium is the U.S. Department of Energy's (DOE) "public-private partnership" for the Genesis Mission, launched on Feb. 9, 2026. "The consortium will be administered by TechWerx, a DOE partnership intermediary operated by RTI International." ([DOE Genesis Mission Consortium launch release 2026-02-09 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20launch%20release%202026-02-09%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- "There are no annual membership dues at this time, though the Consortium's Executive Committee may establish dues in the future if needed." Members contribute "technical infrastructure, personnel time, data, or financial or in-kind support" ([Genesis Mission Consortium FAQ (web)](../sources/Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)).
- University members connect a "high-value resource" to the Genesis Mission platform or make a workforce commitment. Working groups are to define "what access controls apply, and how cost recovery might work" ([Genesis Mission Consortium University Membership (web)](../sources/Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20University%20Membership%20%28web%29.md)).
- Members get access "through Project Agreements shared across members and the National Laboratories" ([Genesis Mission Consortium homepage (web)](../sources/Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- It does "non-technical contract management and meeting planning" for the Mission's collaborators ([DOE Genesis Mission P3 team fact sheet (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20P3%20team%20fact%20sheet%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [U.S. Department of Energy](U.S.%20Department%20of%20Energy.md): launched it ([DOE Genesis Mission Consortium launch release 2026-02-09 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20launch%20release%202026-02-09%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- It has five working groups: model development and validation, data integration and standards, high-performance computing and cloud infrastructure, robotics and automation, and workforce development and training ([DOE Genesis Mission Collaboration page (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Collaboration%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- Contact: info@genesismissionconsortium.org ([Genesis Mission Consortium homepage (web)](../sources/Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
