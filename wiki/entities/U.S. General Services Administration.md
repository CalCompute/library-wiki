# U.S. General Services Administration

## Bottom line

The U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) is the federal agency that runs the Federal Risk and Authorization Management Program (FedRAMP). FedRAMP assesses and authorizes cloud services for federal agencies ([FedRAMP 20x (web)](../sources/FedRAMP%2020x%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- GSA announced FedRAMP 20x in March 2025, "a new path to be developed with industry and government, tested in public, and delivered incrementally" ([FedRAMP 20x (web)](../sources/FedRAMP%2020x%20%28web%29.md)). (A, C, D)
- GSA produces the FedRAMP Marketplace data file, with 533 authorized cloud services in its metrics block ([FedRAMP Marketplace data (web)](../sources/FedRAMP%20Marketplace%20data%20%28web%29.md)). (A, D)
