# California State Assembly

## Bottom line

- The California State Assembly is one house of the California Legislature. Its Chief Clerk records each agency report that the Assembly receives under Government Code section 9795 ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](../sources/Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- An agency sends each required report to three offices. The Chief Clerk of the Assembly is one of them ([California Agency Reports filing reports page (web)](../sources/California%20Agency%20Reports%20filing%20reports%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The March 2026 record lists two reports from the California Department of Technology (CDT). One is "Envision 2026: California’s Technology Future, Year 3 Roadmap" (p. 137). The other is the Statewide Information Technology Annual Report, 2024–25 ([Assembly Journal record of agency reports March 2026 (web)](../sources/Assembly%20Journal%20record%20of%20agency%20reports%20March%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
