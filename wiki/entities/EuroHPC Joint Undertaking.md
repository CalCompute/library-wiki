# EuroHPC Joint Undertaking

## Bottom line

- The European High Performance Computing Joint Undertaking (EuroHPC JU) is "a legal and funding entity that brings together the European Union and participating countries to coordinate efforts and pool resources" ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- EuroHPC JU has procured 11 supercomputers. It owns JUPITER in Germany and pays half of its cost ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- Access to its supercomputers is through EuroHPC access calls, for users in science, the public sector and industry ([EuroHPC JUPITER launch release 2025-09-05 (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JUPITER%20launch%20release%202025-09-05%20%28web%29.md)).
- The European Union has set up 19 AI Factories and 13 AI Factory Antennas. They give "free, customised support" to SMEs and startups ([EuroHPC JU AI Factories (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JU%20AI%20Factories%20%28web%29.md)).
- A Governing Board of the European Commission and each Participating State makes the strategy and funding decisions ([EuroHPC JU governance (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JU%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
- An Access Resource Committee of independent experts helps to allocate "the Union’s share of access time" ([EuroHPC JU governance (web)](../sources/EuroHPC%20JU%20governance%20%28web%29.md)).
