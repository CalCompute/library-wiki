# Tech Talent Project

![Tech Talent Project](../../raw/assets/logos/Tech%20Talent%20Project.png)

_Logo: Tech Talent Project, from its own website ([Tech Talent Project homepage](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)). Fetched 2026-11-19._

## Bottom line

- Tech Talent Project places technology leaders in government roles. It was "Founded in 2017 by tech leaders from both Republican and Democratic administrations" ([Tech Talent Project about](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- It "has matched more than 200 tech and data leaders with government roles" and has worked in "30 states" ([Tech Talent Project incoming governors guide 2026-11-16](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20incoming%20governors%20guide%202026-11-16%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It places "chief technology officers, chief information officers, chief data officers, chief AI officers" and senior engineers "with in-house state roles" ([Tech Talent Project state transitions](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20state%20transitions%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its Tech Leaders in Residence put a senior leader in a new governor's transition team "for 45 to 60 days", "at no cost to the state" ([Tech Talent Project incoming governors guide 2026-11-16](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20incoming%20governors%20guide%202026-11-16%20%28web%29.md)).
- Tech to Gov is "a virtual hiring event that brings government agencies into direct conversation with technologists" ([Tech Talent Project Tech to Gov](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20Tech%20to%20Gov%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- Partners: the Beeck Center for Social Impact + Innovation at Georgetown University, New America and the American Enterprise Institute ([Tech Talent Project about](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Its 2026 State Memos for a Tech Transition cover nine topics, among them "AI readiness and delivery" and "talent" ([Tech Talent Project state transitions](../sources/Tech%20Talent%20Project%20state%20transitions%20%28web%29.md)).
