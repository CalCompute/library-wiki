# AIST Solutions

## Bottom line

- AIST Solutions Co. (AISol) is a Japanese company. The National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST) set it up on April 1, 2023, with 100 percent of its capital ([AIST release AIST Solutions established 2023-04-01 (web)](../sources/AIST%20release%20AIST%20Solutions%20established%202023-04-01%20%28web%29.md)).
- It provides the ABCI service on the AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure, which AIST owns ([ABCI Agreement of Use 2026 (web)](../sources/ABCI%20Agreement%20of%20Use%202026%20Japanese%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Every ABCI user contract is between AISol and the user, and AISol sets the fees ([ABCI Agreement of Use 2026 (web)](../sources/ABCI%20Agreement%20of%20Use%202026%20Japanese%20%28web%29.md)).
- In fiscal year 2026, "1 ABCI Point costs 220 yen (tax included)". Use is free only when "the Institute and AIST Solutions" approve it ([ABCI usage fees (web)](../sources/ABCI%20usage%20fees%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology](National%20Institute%20of%20Advanced%20Industrial%20Science%20and%20Technology.md): parent, sole shareholder ([AIST release AIST Solutions established 2023-04-01 (web)](../sources/AIST%20release%20AIST%20Solutions%20established%202023-04-01%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- AISol is a corporation under the Act on the Activation of Science, Technology and Innovation Creation. Its capital is 100 million yen ([AIST release AIST Solutions established 2023-04-01 (web)](../sources/AIST%20release%20AIST%20Solutions%20established%202023-04-01%20%28web%29.md)).
