# WASC Senior College and University Commission

## Bottom line

- The WASC Senior College and University Commission (WSCUC) accredits senior colleges and universities. Its directory lists accredited and candidate institutions in the United States and other countries ([WSCUC directory of institutions (web)](../sources/WSCUC%20directory%20of%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- WSCUC says that it is "one of the few accreditors that publishes these important records of institutional review and actions" ([WSCUC where to find data on institutions (web)](../sources/WSCUC%20where%20to%20find%20data%20on%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).
- It runs a Key Indicators Dashboard (KID) with data on "all WSCUC institutions that report to IPEDS" ([WSCUC where to find data on institutions (web)](../sources/WSCUC%20where%20to%20find%20data%20on%20institutions%20%28web%29.md)).
