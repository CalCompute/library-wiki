# Proceedings of Machine Learning and Systems (MLSys)

## Bottom line

- The proceedings publish the papers of the MLSys Conference on machine learning and systems. Volume 4 holds the papers of the fifth conference, in Santa Clara, California, in 2022 ([Wu et al Sustainable AI MLSys 2022 (web)](../sources/Wu%20et%20al%20Sustainable%20AI%20MLSys%202022%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The 2022 proceedings hold a paper on the carbon footprint of AI computing at Meta ([Wu et al Sustainable AI MLSys 2022 (web)](../sources/Wu%20et%20al%20Sustainable%20AI%20MLSys%202022%20%28web%29.md)).
