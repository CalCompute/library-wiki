# NTEN

## Bottom line

NTEN "is a community of nonprofit professionals who want to make the world a better place through the skillful and equitable use of technology." With The Bridgespan Group, it published the 2026 State of Nonprofit AI report ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its 2026 survey of 917 nonprofit professionals found that 57% of executives have no AI budget (B) ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- Only 24% of executives in that survey report "Sufficient tools or infrastructure to use and deploy AI" in place now (D) ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Amy Sample Ward signs the report's introduction for NTEN ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
- The two authors of the report are alumni of NTEN's Open Source Fellowship ([NTEN Bridgespan State of Nonprofit AI 2026 (web)](../sources/NTEN%20Bridgespan%20State%20of%20Nonprofit%20AI%202026%20%28web%29.md)).
