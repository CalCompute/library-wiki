# Sarah Soto-Taylor

## Bottom line

- Sarah Soto-Taylor is Deputy Secretary of Strategic Development at the California Government Operations Agency (GovOps) ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She has "nearly three decades in public service" ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Government Operations Agency](Government%20Operations%20Agency.md): Deputy Secretary of Strategic Development ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- [Government Operations Agency](Government%20Operations%20Agency.md): Undersecretary, from October 2024; Deputy Secretary for Business Transformation and Strategic Development, 2019 to 2024 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- GovOps says her focus is "modernizing how the state does business—from bringing Generative AI into government operations to rethinking the delivery of services for the future" ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She helped lead the Census 2020 outreach effort of California ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was Senior Consultant at Covered California from 2014 to 2019 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was Deputy Director of Community Relations at Covered California from 2012 to 2014 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)).
- She held several positions at the Managed Risk Medical Insurance Board from 2001 to 2012 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)).
- She was a Contract and Outreach Manager at the California Department of Health Care Services from 2000 to 2001 ([Governor appointments 2024-10-24 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202024-10-24%20%28web%29.md)).
- Her portfolio in these roles "included health policy, systems integration, and community engagement" ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She has a Bachelor of Science degree in Community Health Education from San Diego State University ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She has a Master of Public Health from San Diego State University ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- She is an alum of the HOPE Leadership Institute ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- GovOps names Russell Fong as Undersecretary, appointed in April 2026 ([Soto-Taylor GovOps leadership page (web)](../sources/Soto-Taylor%20GovOps%20leadership%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
