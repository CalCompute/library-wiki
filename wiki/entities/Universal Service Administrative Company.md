# Universal Service Administrative Company

## Bottom line

- The Universal Service Administrative Company (USAC) publishes the E-Rate discount matrix for school districts and library systems ([USAC E-Rate Discount Matrix (web)](../sources/USAC%20E-Rate%20Discount%20Matrix%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The matrix sets E-Rate discounts from "20%" to "90%". The discount depends on National School Lunch Program (NSLP) eligibility and on urban or rural location ([USAC E-Rate Discount Matrix (web)](../sources/USAC%20E-Rate%20Discount%20Matrix%20%28web%29.md)). (B)
