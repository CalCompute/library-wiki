# National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology

## Bottom line

- The National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST) is a Japanese research institute. It built the AI Bridging Cloud Infrastructure (ABCI), which AIST Solutions Co. (AISol) operates ([ABCI about (web)](../sources/ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- ABCI is "an open computing infrastructure" for AI in Japan. It started in August 2018 and moved to ABCI 3.0 in January 2025 ([ABCI about (web)](../sources/ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry funded ABCI through a series of budget amendments ([ABCI about (web)](../sources/ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- From fiscal year 2024, ABCI gives compute "primarily for public use by domestic industry, academia, and government" ([ABCI about (web)](../sources/ABCI%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- ABCI 3.0 has 6,128 NVIDIA H200 GPUs in 766 nodes and 75 PB of storage ([ABCI computing resource (web)](../sources/ABCI%20computing%20resource%20%28web%29.md)).
