# Frank Würthwein

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: UC San Diego
- Title: Director, San Diego Supercomputer Center
- Role: Runs the San Diego Supercomputer Center and the two shared computing systems that come closest to showing what public AI infrastructure looks like in practice

## Background

- SDSC's page names him "Director of the San Diego Supercomputer Center", with faculty posts in UC San Diego Physics and HDSI ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- His research is on "globally distributed compute and data systems": OSG, NRP and OSDF ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- HDSI also calls him "executive director of the Open Science Grid (OSG)" ([Würthwein HDSI profile](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20HDSI%20profile.md)).
- CENIC's board page lists him as "Science Advisor" ([Würthwein CENIC board of directors](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20CENIC%20board%20of%20directors.md)).
- NRP is one of his research platforms. It pools GPUs across "more than 70 institutions", free to U.S. nonprofit research and education ([Würthwein National Research Platform homepage](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20National%20Research%20Platform%20homepage.md)).

> SDSC's page says "Director", and CENIC's page already lists him as Science Advisor ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md); [Würthwein CENIC board of directors](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20CENIC%20board%20of%20directors.md)).
