# Frank Würthwein

## Bottom line

- Frank Würthwein is Director of the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) at UC San Diego ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- The National Science Foundation (NSF) funds the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Operations Center (NAIRR-OC). He is the principal investigator (PI) of its award ([Würthwein NSF award 2616251 (web)](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202616251%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [San Diego Supercomputer Center](../entities/San%20Diego%20Supercomputer%20Center.md): Director ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- UC San Diego, Physics Department and [Halıcıoğlu Data Science Institute](../entities/Hal%C4%B1c%C4%B1o%C4%9Flu%20Data%20Science%20Institute%2C%20UC%20San%20Diego.md) (HDSI): faculty appointments ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- Open Science Grid (OSG): HDSI calls him its "executive director" ([Würthwein HDSI profile](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20HDSI%20profile.md)).
- [CENIC](../entities/CENIC.md): its board page lists him as "Science Advisor" ([Würthwein CENIC board of directors](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20CENIC%20board%20of%20directors.md)).

## Background

- His research is on "globally distributed compute and data systems". His examples are OSG, the National Research Platform (NRP) and the Open Science Data Federation (OSDF) ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- His research also covers experimental particle physics and distributed high-throughput computing ([Würthwein SDSC leadership](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20SDSC%20leadership.md)).
- He is the PI of NSF award 2112167, which deploys the "Prototype National Research Platform" ([Würthwein NSF award 2112167 (web)](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202112167%20%28web%29.md)).
- The NRP award runs from July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2028 ([Würthwein NSF award 2112167 (web)](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202112167%20%28web%29.md)).
- [NRP](../entities/National%20Research%20Platform.md) pools GPUs across "more than 70 institutions", free to U.S. nonprofit research and education ([Würthwein National Research Platform homepage](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20National%20Research%20Platform%20homepage.md)).
- The NRP homepage gives its scale as "400+" nodes, "70+" locations, "3" continents and "5K+" users ([Würthwein National Research Platform homepage](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20National%20Research%20Platform%20homepage.md)).
- His NAIRR-OC award, NSF award 2616251, is a cooperative agreement with an estimated total of $35,000,000 ([Würthwein NSF award 2616251 (web)](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202616251%20%28web%29.md)).
- The NAIRR-OC award runs from Oct. 1, 2026 to Sept. 30, 2031 ([Würthwein NSF award 2616251 (web)](../sources/W%C3%BCrthwein%20NSF%20award%202616251%20%28web%29.md)).
- SDSC runs the NAIRR-OC with the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC) ([SDSC NAIRR Operations Center launch](../sources/SDSC%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20launch.md)).
