# Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory

## Bottom line

- Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (LBNL) is a national laboratory in Berkeley, California. It "is supported by the Office of Science of the United States Department of Energy" ([LBNL 2024 US Data Center Energy Usage Report](../sources/LBNL%202024%20US%20Data%20Center%20Energy%20Usage%20Report.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: LBNL is the home of the [National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center](National%20Energy%20Research%20Scientific%20Computing%20Center.md) (NERSC). "Berkeley Lab is a multiprogram national laboratory managed by the University of California for the U.S. Department of Energy's Office of Science." ([NERSC Doudna announcement 2025-05-29 (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Doudna%20announcement%202025-05-29%20%28web%29.md))
- Element D: NERSC needs an Institutional User Agreement with Berkeley Lab for some projects. One case is a project that "Is not federally (U.S.) funded". "Absent any applicable agreement with your institution, the Federal government or Berkeley Lab may have rights to your work product under the Bayh-Dole Act and other legislation and regulations." ([NERSC Appropriate Use Policy (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Appropriate%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md))
- Element B: LBNL leads MOAT-Core, a Genesis Mission Phase II project. DOE announced it in October 2026 ([DOE Genesis Mission Phase II awards release 2026-10 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Phase%20II%20awards%20release%202026-10%20%28web%29.md)).
- Elements A, B and D: LBNL's Science IT group runs Lawrencium, a condo cluster for the laboratory's "midrange computing requirement". Condo users run free within their contributions, and other users pay "$0.01 per Service Unit" ([Berkeley Lab Lawrencium recharge rates (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20Lab%20Lawrencium%20recharge%20rates%20%28web%29.md)). Its Einsteinium GPU cluster has H100, H200 and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell nodes ([Berkeley Lab Einsteinium GPU cluster (web)](../sources/Berkeley%20Lab%20Einsteinium%20GPU%20cluster%20%28web%29.md)).
