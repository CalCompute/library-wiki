# Legislative Analyst's Office

## Bottom line

- The Legislative Analyst's Office (LAO) is "The California Legislature's Nonpartisan Fiscal and Policy Advisor" ([LAO California State Library spending by program 2026-09 (web)](../sources/LAO%20California%20State%20Library%20spending%20by%20program%202026-09%20%28web%29.md), page footer).

## Why they matter

- Its EdBudget figures give state budget tables by agency. Its September 2026 table gives the California State Library $100.5 million in the 2026-27 enacted budget ([LAO California State Library spending by program 2026-09 (web)](../sources/LAO%20California%20State%20Library%20spending%20by%20program%202026-09%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its July 2026 table of community college programs gives the Future of Creative Industries Pilot Project $15 million in 2026-27 (E) ([LAO EdBudget Future of Creative Industries Pilot Project](../sources/LAO%20EdBudget%20Future%20of%20Creative%20Industries%20Pilot%20Program%20%28web%29.md)).
