# International Telecommunication Union

## Bottom line

- The International Telecommunication Union (ITU) is a United Nations agency. Its Bureau for Development leads the Open Source Ecosystem Enabler (OSEE) with the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- OSEE helps "local and regional public and private actors to effectively adopt open source technologies for delivering digital government services" ([ITU OSEE project page (web)](../sources/ITU%20OSEE%20project%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- OSEE runs "from September 2023 to February 2027 with EUR 3 million in funding" from the European Commission ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
- ITU leads the GenAI for Good Challenge with IEEE Humanitarian Technologies ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
- The ITU Open Source Programme Office (OSPO) supports OSEE ([ITU OSEE project page (web)](../sources/ITU%20OSEE%20project%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
