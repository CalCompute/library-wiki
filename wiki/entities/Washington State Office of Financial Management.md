# Washington State Office of Financial Management

## Bottom line

The Washington Office of Financial Management (OFM) is a Washington state agency. Its State Human Resources Division wrote a report on generative AI and the state workforce ([Washington OFM GenAI impact on state workforce (web)](../sources/Washington%20OFM%20GenAI%20impact%20on%20state%20workforce%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Elements G and E.** Executive Order No. 24-01 section 9 directed OFM's State Human Resources Division to assess the impact of generative AI on the state workforce and to support skill programs. The report records two views on the role of labor in adoption decisions: that of labor representatives and that of OFM ([Washington OFM GenAI impact on state workforce (web)](../sources/Washington%20OFM%20GenAI%20impact%20on%20state%20workforce%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- OFM's partners on the report were Washington Technology Solutions, the Workforce Training and Education Coordinating Board and state employee unions ([Washington OFM GenAI impact on state workforce (web)](../sources/Washington%20OFM%20GenAI%20impact%20on%20state%20workforce%20%28web%29.md)).
