# U.S. National Science Foundation, NAIRR Pilot

## Why they matter

- Its Deep Partnerships track pairs a project with one industry partner for no more than 12 months. Results "must be open and publishable" ([NAIRR Pilot deep partnerships (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Pilot%20deep%20partnerships%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its About page expands NAIRR as "National Architecture for Innovation, Research, and Resource-sharing" and lists the pilot's operations teams ([NAIRR Pilot overview (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Pilot%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
