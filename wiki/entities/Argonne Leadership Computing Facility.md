# Argonne Leadership Computing Facility

## Bottom line

- The Argonne Leadership Computing Facility (ALCF) is "a national user facility dedicated to open science" at Argonne National Laboratory. "Argonne National Laboratory is a U.S. Department of Energy laboratory managed by UChicago Argonne, LLC." ([ALCF 2025 annual report allocation programs (web)](../sources/ALCF%202025%20annual%20report%20allocation%20programs%20%28web%29.md))

## Why they matter

- Element D: ALCF gives computing time through "competitive, peer-reviewed allocation programs supported by the DOE and Argonne": INCITE, ALCC, Director's Discretionary and the Early Science Program ([ALCF 2025 annual report allocation programs (web)](../sources/ALCF%202025%20annual%20report%20allocation%20programs%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element D: INCITE gives 60 percent of the time at the Leadership Computing Facilities, ALCF and OLCF ([DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)](../sources/DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
