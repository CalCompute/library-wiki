# U.S. National Science Foundation

## Why they matter

- It announced the AI Infrastructure Hubs program as "a $100 million initiative", with up to 10 hubs at first ([NSF news AI Infrastructure Hubs (web)](../sources/NSF%20news%20AI%20Infrastructure%20Hubs%20%28web%29.md)).
- It is setting up the NAIRR Operations Center. The San Diego Supercomputer Center at UC San Diego leads it ([NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)](../sources/NSF%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20update%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its AI Datasets program, NSF 26-512, anticipates "$60,000,000 to $100,000,000" for work on existing scientific datasets ([NSF 26-512 AI Datasets solicitation (web)](../sources/NSF%2026-512%20AI%20Datasets%20solicitation%20%28web%29.md)).

- Its Proposal & Award Policies & Procedures Guide (PAPPG 24-1) sets who may submit. Eligible proposers include accredited U.S. colleges "(including community colleges)" and U.S. nonprofit research organizations ([NSF PAPPG Chapter I eligibility (web)](../sources/NSF%20PAPPG%20Chapter%20I%20eligibility%20%28web%29.md)). (D, F)
- The PAPPG prohibits voluntary committed cost sharing and gives "the *de minimis* rate of 10%" for organizations with no negotiated rate. It names ACCESS among the national resources it supports ([NSF PAPPG 24-1 Chapter II (web)](../sources/NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Chapter%20II%20%28web%29.md)). (B, D)
- NSF reviews proposals against "two National Science Board approved merit review criteria" ([NSF PAPPG 24-1 Chapter III merit review (web)](../sources/NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Chapter%20III%20merit%20review%20%28web%29.md)). (C, D)
- Its policy notice NSF 26-200 changes the PAPPG for awards from Dec. 8, 2025, and defers PAPPG 26-1 ([NSF PAPPG 24-1 Supplement 1 (web)](../sources/NSF%20PAPPG%2024-1%20Supplement%201%20%28web%29.md)). (B, C, D)
