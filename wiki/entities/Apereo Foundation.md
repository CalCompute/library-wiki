# Apereo Foundation

![Apereo Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/Apereo%20Foundation.png)

_Logo: Apereo Foundation, [home page](https://www.apereo.org/). Fetched 2026-10-29._

## Bottom line

- The Apereo Foundation is a member-governed, open-source foundation in education. It offers membership classes and fiscal sponsorship.
- It is "a New Jersey non-stock, nonprofit corporation", incorporated on Dec. 28, 2012 ([Apereo bylaws (web)](../sources/Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article I).

## Why they matter

- Only Organizational Members vote on foundation matters, with "One (1) vote is allowed per member organization" ([Apereo bylaws (web)](../sources/Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article III, Section 3.3).
- Its software communities "are empowered to govern themselves within the policies and principles established by the Foundation", under a principle of subsidiarity ([Apereo bylaws (web)](../sources/Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article IX).
- It has six membership types. Community membership has no cost, and Institutional and Foundation membership use a sliding scale on the member's annual budget ([Apereo join us (web)](../sources/Apereo%20join%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Institutional membership is "OSPO as a Service" and costs from $1,080 to $8,100 a year ([Apereo institutional members (web)](../sources/Apereo%20institutional%20members%20%28web%29.md)).
- Sustaining members fund one project directly. That project's management committee alone manages the funds ([Apereo sustaining members (web)](../sources/Apereo%20sustaining%20members%20%28web%29.md)).
- It publishes rules for contractors, invoices and payment terms ([Apereo vendor policies (web)](../sources/Apereo%20vendor%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Its board has organizational, individual, appointed and ex-officio seats. The board page lists 16 people ([Apereo board of directors (web)](../sources/Apereo%20board%20of%20directors%20%28web%29.md)).
- The board includes people from UC Santa Cruz and California State University, Northridge ([Apereo board of directors (web)](../sources/Apereo%20board%20of%20directors%20%28web%29.md)).

> ⚠️ Contradiction: the governance page says that the board can appoint "up to four additional board members" ([Apereo governance (web)](../sources/Apereo%20governance%20%28web%29.md)). The bylaws say "up to three (3) additional Directors" ([Apereo bylaws (web)](../sources/Apereo%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Article V, Section 2.6).
