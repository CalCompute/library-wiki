# Chris Given

## Bottom line

- Chris Given is State Chief Information Officer and Director of the California Department of Technology (CDT) ([CDT executive team](../sources/CDT%20executive%20team.md)).
- Before that, he was Direct File Product Lead at the Internal Revenue Service (IRS), from 2024 to 2025 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California Department of Technology](California%20Department%20of%20Technology.md): Director and State Chief Information Officer ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md); [CDT executive team](../sources/CDT%20executive%20team.md)).
- past: [Government Operations Agency](Government%20Operations%20Agency.md): Deputy Secretary for Technology and Innovation, 2025 to 2026 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The Governor appointed him Director of CDT on April 29, 2026. The release says: "This position requires Senate confirmation" ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- He calls himself "a government technologist" ([Given personal website about page (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- In October 2025, he accepted a post at the Government Operations Agency. He moved from Washington, D.C., to Sacramento ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He says: "I led the team that designed and built IRS Direct File" ([Given personal website about page (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was Direct File Product Lead at the IRS from 2024 to 2025 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- Direct File went live on Jan. 29, 2024, and went dark on Nov. 5, 2025 ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- The final Direct File homepage told taxpayers: "Over 500,000 of you used Direct File" ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- Merici Vinton was his U.S. Digital Service (USDS) project co-lead on Direct File ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- A team of three designers and four engineers built a proof of concept of Direct File in eight weeks ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- The IRS published most of the Direct File code on GitHub as open-source software ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He arrived at the IRS in 2021 ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was a Digital Services Expert at USDS from 2016 to 2019 and from 2020 to 2024 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was Senior Data Engineer at Skylight Digital from 2019 to 2020 ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- He says that he has "worked for ten different federal agencies and four state governments" ([Given personal website about page (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20about%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He worked at the Office of Early Childhood in Connecticut ([Given personal website (web)](../sources/Given%20personal%20website%20%28web%29.md)).
- He has a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Studies from Bard College ([Governor appointments 2026-04-29 (web)](../sources/Governor%20appointments%202026-04-29%20%28web%29.md)).
