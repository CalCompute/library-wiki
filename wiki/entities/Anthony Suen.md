# Anthony Suen

## Bottom line

- Anthony Suen is on the staff of UC Berkeley's College of Computing, Data Science, and Society (CDSS). His D-Lab profile gives his title as "Director of Programs" ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He launched the Data Science Discovery Program at UC Berkeley ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).

## Affiliations

- [UC Berkeley College of Computing, Data Science, and Society](../entities/UC%20Berkeley%20College%20of%20Computing%2C%20Data%20Science%2C%20and%20Society.md): Director of Programs ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- [Berkeley Institute for Data Science](../entities/Berkeley%20Institute%20for%20Data%20Science.md): he was a Data Science Fellow ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).

## Background

- He joined UC Berkeley's data science division in 2015 "as its first staff member" ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).
- In 2019 he was Director of Programs for the Data Science Education Program ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).
- He received the 2019 Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).
- He founded several data science programs: Data Science Discovery, Data Science Modules, Data Scholars and Data Peer Consulting ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He also founded the National Workshop on Data Science Education and the UC Berkeley Cloud Meetup ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He holds a master's in International Policy from Stanford ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He holds a Master of Information Management and Systems from UC Berkeley ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He was a Schneider Fellow at the Beijing office of the Natural Resources Defense Council ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He co-founded the environmental nonprofit Energyfolks ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
