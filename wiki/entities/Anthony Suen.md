# Anthony Suen

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: UC Berkeley
- Title: Founder, Cal-ICOR
- Role: Built UC Berkeley's data science education and access programs, the kind of workforce pathway SB-53 asks the CalCompute consortium to analyze

## Background

- He joined UC Berkeley's data science division in 2015 "as its first staff member", and launched the Data Science Discovery Program ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).
- He received the 2019 Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award ([Suen Chancellor's Outstanding Staff Award](../sources/Suen%20Chancellor%27s%20Outstanding%20Staff%20Award.md)).
- He founded several data science programs, including a national workshop on data science education and the UC Berkeley Cloud Meetup ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
- He holds a master's in International Policy from Stanford and a Master of Information Management and Systems from UC Berkeley ([Suen D-Lab profile](../sources/Suen%20D-Lab%20profile.md)).
