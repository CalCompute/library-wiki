# The Chronicle of Philanthropy

## Bottom line

The Chronicle of Philanthropy publishes news on the nonprofit world ([Chronicle of Philanthropy nonprofit AI gap (web)](../sources/Chronicle%20of%20Philanthropy%20nonprofit%20AI%20gap%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It reported an NTEN and Bridgespan survey: only 12 percent of nonprofit executives had a grant for an AI project ([Chronicle of Philanthropy nonprofit AI gap (web)](../sources/Chronicle%20of%20Philanthropy%20nonprofit%20AI%20gap%20%28web%29.md)).
- It reported a Fast Forward survey: funding for systems, tools and talent is the top need of 84 percent of AI-powered nonprofits ([Chronicle of Philanthropy nonprofits embracing AI funding (web)](../sources/Chronicle%20of%20Philanthropy%20nonprofits%20embracing%20AI%20funding%20%28web%29.md)).
