# Jetstream2, Indiana University

## Bottom line

- Jetstream2 is a research cloud at Indiana University. Its use "requires an active ACCESS allocation" ([Jetstream2 policies (web)](../sources/Jetstream2%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Jetstream2 "requires compliance with all ACCESS and Indiana University policies", among them the ACCESS Acceptable Use Policy ([Jetstream2 policies (web)](../sources/Jetstream2%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).
- Thirty days after an allocation expires, Jetstream2 destroys its resources, which "will not be recoverable" ([Jetstream2 policies (web)](../sources/Jetstream2%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [ACCESS, U.S. National Science Foundation](ACCESS%2C%20U.S.%20National%20Science%20Foundation.md): Jetstream2 time comes through an ACCESS allocation ([Jetstream2 policies (web)](../sources/Jetstream2%20policies%20%28web%29.md)).
