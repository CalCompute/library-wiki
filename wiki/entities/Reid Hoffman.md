## Bottom line

Reid Hoffman is a co-founder of LinkedIn and a partner at Greylock Partners, in an undated Stanford biography ([Stanford STVP Reid Hoffman profile (web)](../sources/Stanford%20STVP%20Reid%20Hoffman%20profile%20%28web%29.md)). The Giving Pledge lists "Reid Hoffman and Michelle Yee" ([Giving Pledge pledger list (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20pledger%20list%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He was Executive Vice President of PayPal before LinkedIn ([Stanford STVP Reid Hoffman profile (web)](../sources/Stanford%20STVP%20Reid%20Hoffman%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He has a B.S. in Symbolic Systems from Stanford University and a master's in philosophy from Oxford University ([Stanford STVP Reid Hoffman profile (web)](../sources/Stanford%20STVP%20Reid%20Hoffman%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
