# IndiaAI

## Bottom line

- IndiaAI is part of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) of the Government of India. It runs the GPU compute allocations of the IndiaAI Mission ([IndiaAI compute capacity (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20compute%20capacity%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- IndiaAI offers compute from empanelled cloud providers to "academia, MSMEs, startups, research community, governments, public sector agencies" and other approved users ([IndiaAI compute capacity (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20compute%20capacity%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its allocation table shows a subsidy and a bill of materials for each award ([IndiaAI compute capacity (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20compute%20capacity%20%28web%29.md)).
- Under the Mission, more than 38,000 GPUs are available at ₹65 per hour ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](../sources/PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Mission has an outlay of ₹10,371.92 crore over five years, approved in March 2024 ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](../sources/PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
- "The Mission will be implemented by 'IndiaAI' Independent Business Division (IBD) under Digital India Corporation (DIC)" ([PIB Cabinet approves IndiaAI Mission 2024-03-07 (web)](../sources/PIB%20Cabinet%20approves%20IndiaAI%20Mission%202024-03-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- Users "approved by IndiaAI" get compute from empanelled cloud providers with a "40% government subsidy". The mission "operates under a four-year sunset clause" ([PIB IndiaAI common compute facility release 2025-01-30 (web)](../sources/PIB%20common%20compute%20facility%20release%202025-01-30%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [Digital India Corporation](Digital%20India%20Corporation.md): parent; IndiaAI is an independent business division under it ([IndiaAI notice INDAI-122-2026 General Manager Compute (web)](../sources/IndiaAI%20notice%20INDAI-122-2026%20General%20Manager%20Compute%20%28web%29.md)).
