# OSG Consortium

## Bottom line

- The OSG Consortium publishes the pages of the OSPool. The OSPool is computing capacity "accessible to any researcher affiliated with a US academic institution" ([OSPool service page (web)](../sources/OSPool%20service%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The OSPool capacity comes from "members of the OSG Compute Federation, typically campuses, government-supported supercomputing centers, and research collaborations". Each member sets its own policy for what it gives ([OSPool service page (web)](../sources/OSPool%20service%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The page says the OSPool is "Operated by PATh". The National Science Foundation (NSF) supports the work under grants 2030508, 1836650 and 2609485 ([OSPool service page (web)](../sources/OSPool%20service%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
