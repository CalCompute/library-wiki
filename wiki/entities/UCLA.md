# UCLA

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as University of California-Los Angeles in Los Angeles, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its engineering school carries the name of Henry Samueli. One of the largest early gifts of the Samueli foundation endowed the school (B, F) ([UCLA Samueli School Henry Samueli profile (web)](../sources/UCLA%20Samueli%20School%20Henry%20Samueli%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
