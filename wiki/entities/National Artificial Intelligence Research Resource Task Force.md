# National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource Task Force

## Bottom line

- The National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Task Force is a federal task force that the National AI Initiative Act of 2020 set up. It wrote the January 2023 plan for the NAIRR ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its plan gives a governance model of one administrative home agency, a Steering Committee of agencies and an independent Operating Entity. It estimates "$2.6 billion over an initial six-year period" ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md), pp. v to vii).

## Affiliations

- U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF) and the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy (OSTP) launched the task force in June 2021 ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md), p. iii).
