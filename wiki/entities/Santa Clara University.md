# Santa Clara University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as Santa Clara University in Santa Clara, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Private not-for-profit" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In 2017, John A. and Susan Sobrato gave it $100 million for the Sobrato Campus for Discovery and Innovation, a building for STEM education. The release calls it "the largest in Santa Clara’s 166-year history" (B, F) ([Santa Clara University Sobrato 100 million gift 2017 (web)](../sources/Santa%20Clara%20University%20Sobrato%20100%20million%20gift%202017%20%28web%29.md)).
