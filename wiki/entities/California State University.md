# California State University

## Bottom line

- The California State University (CSU) has three levels of data classification: Level 1 Confidential, Level 2 Internal Use and Level 3 General ([CSU Information Security Data Classification standard (CSUSB copy) (web)](../sources/CSU%20Information%20Security%20Data%20Classification%20standard%20%28CSUSB%20copy%29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element C: its APC agreement allows contracting out with notice of contracts of six months or more ([CSU APC Unit 4 contract 2024-2027 (web)](../sources/CSU%20APC%20Unit%204%20contract%202024-2027%20%28web%29.md)). Its student assistants have no agreement yet ([CSU statement on student assistants vote to join union (web)](../sources/CSU%20statement%20on%20student%20assistants%20vote%20to%20join%20union%20%28web%29.md)).
