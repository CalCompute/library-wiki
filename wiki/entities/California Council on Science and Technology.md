# California Council on Science and Technology

## Bottom line

The California Council on Science and Technology (CCST) is a nonpartisan nonprofit. The California Legislature chartered it in 1988 to give science and technology advice to the state ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It responds to the Governor, the Legislature and other state entities that request "independent assessment of public policy issues" ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- It places science and technology PhDs in the Legislature and executive branch offices for one year ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- It places AI Science Advisors at the Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) ([Chen CCST AI Science Advisor announcement (web)](../sources/Chen%20CCST%20AI%20Science%20Advisor%20announcement%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- Academic partners: University of California, California State University, California Community Colleges, Caltech, Stanford University and the University of Southern California ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
- Federal laboratory partners: NASA Ames Research Center, the Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Berkeley Lab, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory and SLAC ([CCST about (web)](../sources/CCST%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
