# Digital Realty

## Bottom line

- Digital Realty Trust is a data center real estate company. At the end of 2025, its consolidated portfolio had 18 data center buildings in California metro areas. They have 2,769 thousand net rentable square feet ([Digital Realty Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Digital%20Realty%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A, commercial infrastructure: its annual report gives buildings, floor area and occupancy for each California metro area ([Digital Realty Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Digital%20Realty%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element B: occupancy was 60.1 percent in San Francisco and 81.7 percent in Silicon Valley ([Digital Realty Form 10-K 2025 (web)](../sources/Digital%20Realty%20Form%2010-K%202025%20%28web%29.md)).
