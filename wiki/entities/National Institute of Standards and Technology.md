# National Institute of Standards and Technology

## Bottom line

- The National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) publishes the Special Publication (SP) 800 series on computer security ([NIST SP 800-171 Rev 3 landing page (web)](../sources/NIST%20SP%20800-171%20Rev%203%20landing%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- SP 800-171 Rev. 3 gives federal agencies "recommended security requirements for protecting the confidentiality of CUI when the information is resident in nonfederal systems and organizations" ([NIST SP 800-171 Rev 3 landing page (web)](../sources/NIST%20SP%20800-171%20Rev%203%20landing%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
