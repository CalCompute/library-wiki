# National Compute Labs PBC

![National Compute Labs PBC](../../raw/assets/logos/National%20Compute%20Labs%20PBC.svg)

_Logo: National Compute, [home page](https://nationalcompute.com/). Fetched 2026-10-29._

## Bottom line

National Compute Labs PBC runs the National Compute Grid. The Grid is a private platform that pools AI compute and allocates it by auction ([National Compute home page (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20home%20page%20%28web%29.md); [National Compute docs FAQ (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20docs%20FAQ%20%28web%29.md)). Users with a `.edu`, `.mil` or `.gov` address can sign in; other users join a waitlist ([National Compute home page (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20home%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- **Element A.** The Grid paper says that single-tenant clusters often have "average net FLOPs utilization below 15 percent". It describes a shared pool as the answer ([National Compute Grid paper (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20Grid%20paper%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Element B.** Members pay in Grid Credits for completed work. The paper calls the Grid "a private undertaking, financed and operated by its members, at no cost to the taxpayer" ([National Compute Grid paper (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20Grid%20paper%20%28web%29.md)). Grants become Promo credits that expire after 30 days by default ([National Compute docs Credits (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20docs%20Credits%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Element C.** The paper asks for comment on security tiers and governance rules before a "Grid Constitution" ([National Compute Grid paper (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20Grid%20paper%20%28web%29.md)).
- **Element D.** Public Research gives one 8-GPU node at a time to an individual researcher, in 4-hour leases, with $100 of Promo credits at sign-up. A `.edu` organization takes part in data sharing at tier 2 ([National Compute docs Public Research (web)](../sources/National%20Compute%20docs%20Public%20Research%20%28web%29.md)).
