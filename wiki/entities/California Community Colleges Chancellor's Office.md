# California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office

## Bottom line

- The California Community Colleges Chancellor's Office sets AI literacy, AI safety and digital identity policy for the community colleges. Craig Hayward is Vice Chancellor, AI Strategy and Digital Transformation.

## Why they matter

- Its PEOPLE framework sets AI literacy for the colleges ([CCC ISTI 25-300-01 AI Literacy](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%2025-300-01%20AI%20Literacy.md)).
- Colleges must build AI sandboxes. A shared sandbox is a possible CalCompute use case ([CCC ISTI 25-300-02 AI Safety and Security](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%2025-300-02%20AI%20Safety%20and%20Security.md)).
- Its "Open, Conditional, Restricted, or Closed" use tiers are a template for acceptable use ([CCC ISTI 25-300-02 AI Safety and Security](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%2025-300-02%20AI%20Safety%20and%20Security.md)).
- Memo ISTI 26-300-02 limits the CCCStart admissions form to what admission needs; other data waits for onboarding. New questions go through a central review ([CCC ISTI 26-300-02 CCCStart Supplemental Data Collection](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%2026-300-02%20CCCStart%20Supplemental%20Data%20Collection.md); [CCC ISTI memos index](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%20memos%20index.md)).
- Its Data Governance Advisory Workgroup has three chairs and 18 members from 13 statewide associations. CISOA and The RP Group co-lead it ([CCC ISTI Data Governance Advisory Workgroup](../sources/CCC%20ISTI%20Data%20Governance%20Advisory%20Workgroup.md)).
