# National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center

## Bottom line

- The National Energy Research Scientific Computing Center (NERSC) is "a U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) Office of Science (SC) user facility" ([NERSC 2026 allocation year call for proposals (web)](../sources/NERSC%202026%20allocation%20year%20call%20for%20proposals%20%28web%29.md)). It is at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory in Berkeley, California ([DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)](../sources/DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
- It supports "more than 11,000 scientists and 800 projects annually" ([NERSC Doudna announcement 2025-05-29 (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Doudna%20announcement%202025-05-29%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Element A: Its current flagship system, Perlmutter, has "3,072 CPU-only and 1,792 GPU-accelerated nodes" ([NERSC Perlmutter architecture (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Perlmutter%20architecture%20%28web%29.md)). Its next system, *Doudna*, is "due in 2026" ([NERSC Doudna announcement 2025-05-29 (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Doudna%20announcement%202025-05-29%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element D: Requests go through the ERCAP Request System. NERSC "supports only open research intended to be published in open scientific journals" ([NERSC ERCAP allocation form (web)](../sources/NERSC%20ERCAP%20allocation%20form%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element D: The NERSC Appropriate Use Policy bans "Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)", export-controlled data and "Personally identifiable or protected health information". It says that "The use of NERSC resources for personal or private benefit is prohibited" ([NERSC Appropriate Use Policy (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Appropriate%20Use%20Policy%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element D: An account "must be associated with a project". NERSC vets each new user. This "generally takes less than a week". The project principal investigator then approves the user ([NERSC user account policies (web)](../sources/NERSC%20user%20account%20policies%20%28web%29.md)). Users must also agree to the [NERSC Code of Conduct (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Code%20of%20Conduct%20%28web%29.md).
- Element D: ERCAP gives 80 percent of NERSC time ([DOE ASCR accessing ASCR facilities (web)](../sources/DOE%20ASCR%20accessing%20ASCR%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)). DOE Office of Science programs review most proposals ([NERSC 2026 allocation year call for proposals (web)](../sources/NERSC%202026%20allocation%20year%20call%20for%20proposals%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory: "Located at Berkeley Lab, NERSC is the mission high performance computing facility for the DOE Office of Science" ([NERSC Doudna announcement 2025-05-29 (web)](../sources/NERSC%20Doudna%20announcement%202025-05-29%20%28web%29.md)).
