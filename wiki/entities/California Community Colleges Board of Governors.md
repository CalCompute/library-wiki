# California Community Colleges Board of Governors

## Bottom line

- The Board of Governors of the California Community Colleges sends the system's yearly budget and legislative request to the state. The system has "more than 2.2 million students across 116 colleges" ([CCC 2027-28 Shared Advocacy Request Executive Summary (web)](../sources/CCC%202027-28%20Shared%20Advocacy%20Request%20Executive%20Summary%20%28web%29.md), p. 2).

## Why they matter

- Its 2027-28 request asks for $351.5 million one-time and $298.2 million ongoing in Proposition 98 funds ([CCC 2027-28 Shared Advocacy Request Executive Summary (web)](../sources/CCC%202027-28%20Shared%20Advocacy%20Request%20Executive%20Summary%20%28web%29.md), p. 2).
- It asks for $9 million one-time and $12 million ongoing for a partnership with UC San Diego, the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC) and CENIC. The partnership gives all community colleges access to the CENIC AI Resource ([CCC 2027-28 Shared Advocacy Request Appendix E (web)](../sources/CCC%202027-28%20Shared%20Advocacy%20Request%20Appendix%20E%20%28web%29.md), p. 4).
