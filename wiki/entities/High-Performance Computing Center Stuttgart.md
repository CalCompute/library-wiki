# High-Performance Computing Center Stuttgart

## Bottom line

- The High-Performance Computing Center Stuttgart (HLRS) is one of three German national HPC centres. It is a central unit of the University of Stuttgart and a member of the Gauss Centre for Supercomputing ([HLRS Hunter system (web)](../sources/HLRS%20Hunter%20system%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its flagship system, Hunter, started operation in February 2025, with 752 AMD MI300A APUs and a peak of 48.1 PFlop/s ([HLRS Hunter system (web)](../sources/HLRS%20Hunter%20system%20%28web%29.md)).
- The federal research ministry and the Baden-Württemberg science ministry fund Hunter through GCS ([HLRS Hunter system (web)](../sources/HLRS%20Hunter%20system%20%28web%29.md)).
- Installation of its next system, Herder, "is scheduled to begin in 2027" ([HLRS Hunter system (web)](../sources/HLRS%20Hunter%20system%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations
