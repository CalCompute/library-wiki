# UC Santa Cruz

## Bottom line

- The Center for Research in Open Source Software (CROSS) is one of its centers ([CROSS About](../sources/CROSS%20About.md)).
- Carnegie lists it as University of California-Santa Cruz in Santa Cruz, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
