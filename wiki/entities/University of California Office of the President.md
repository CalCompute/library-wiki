# University of California Office of the President

## Bottom line

- The UC Office of the President (UCOP) and the UC Regents lead UC's systemwide AI plan. It includes a "Sovereign AI Cloud", described as UC-owned and UC-governed compute.

## Why they matter

- UC is "inventorying existing compute across campuses and national laboratories" ([UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16, p. 7](../sources/UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md)).
- The sizing and funding proposal is due no later than Oct. 2027, after the Report ([UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16, p. 7](../sources/UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md)). UC has AWS credits of $14 million and a Google program of $32.7 million (p. 6).
- The UC AI Steering Committee has working groups on discovery, public good and AI literacy ([UC Regents B4 Update on AI 2026-09-16, pp. 2, 4-5](../sources/UC%20Regents%20B4%20Update%20on%20AI%202026-09-16.md)).
- The University of California is a registered supporter of SB 53 in the Assembly Privacy analyses of July and September 2025 ([SB-53 Assembly Privacy analysis 2025-07-08](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Privacy%20analysis%202025-07-08.md); [SB-53 Assembly Privacy analysis 2025-09-05](../sources/SB-53%20Assembly%20Privacy%20analysis%202025-09-05.md)).
