# David and Lucile Packard Foundation

![David and Lucile Packard Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/David%20and%20Lucile%20Packard%20Foundation.svg)

_Logo: The David and Lucile Packard Foundation, [history page](https://www.packard.org/about/our-history/). The site shows the words of the mark in white. This copy colours them dark grey for a light page. Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

David and Lucile Packard set up the foundation in 1964. David Packard was one of the founders of Hewlett-Packard ([Packard Foundation history (web)](../sources/Packard%20Foundation%20history%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It set up the Packard Fellowships for Science and Engineering in 1988, for early-career scientists and engineers (B, F) ([Packard Foundation history (web)](../sources/Packard%20Foundation%20history%20%28web%29.md)).
- In 2024 it set "a minimum indirect cost rate of 15-25% on all project grants" (B) ([Packard Foundation history (web)](../sources/Packard%20Foundation%20history%20%28web%29.md)).
