# Ruby Guillen

## Bottom line

- Ruby Guillen is a Governing Councilor of a section of the American Public Health Association (APHA), 2025 to 2027. The section is Health Informatics Information Technology (HIIT) ([Guillen APHA HIIT section leadership (web)](../sources/Guillen%20APHA%20HIIT%20section%20leadership%20%28web%29.md)).
- A 2015 news profile describes her as a Los Angeles County child welfare social worker ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).

## Background

- A 2015 news profile describes her as a Los Angeles County child welfare social worker since 1995 ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- In 2015 she estimated that she had assessed the safety of 6,000 children in their homes ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- She joined the U.S. Air Force in the 1980s ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- She got a degree in computer information systems in 2010 ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- In 2015 her team won two Hack for LA hackathons with child-welfare apps ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- She said: "I'm more for people-centered technology" ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
