# Ruby Guillen

Member of the CalCompute Consortium.

- Institution: UNA-USA
- Title: Co-Chair, UNA-USA Human Rights Affinity Group
- Role: Child welfare technologist; United Nations civil society leader; ethical AI and public-interest technology advocate

## Background

- A 2015 news profile describes her as a Los Angeles County child welfare social worker since 1995 ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- She served in the U.S. Air Force, and got a degree in computer information systems in 2010 ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
- In 2015 her team won two Hack for LA hackathons with child-welfare apps. She said: "I'm more for people-centered technology" ([Guillen Imprint News profile](../sources/Guillen%20Imprint%20News%20profile.md)).
