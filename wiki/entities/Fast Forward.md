# Fast Forward

## Bottom line

Fast Forward is a nonprofit accelerator ([Chronicle of Philanthropy nonprofits embracing AI funding (web)](../sources/Chronicle%20of%20Philanthropy%20nonprofits%20embracing%20AI%20funding%20%28web%29.md)). It says that for more than a decade it has "backed builders reimagining how technology can serve humanity". It published the 2025 AI for Humanity Report ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In its 2025 survey, 84% of AI-powered nonprofits say that they need more funding (B) ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).
- The same report says that 41% of AI-powered nonprofits cite a lack of in-house technical expertise (E) ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Kevin Barenblat and Shannon Farley are its co-founders ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).
- Google.org supported the 2025 report ([Fast Forward 2025 AI for Humanity Report (web)](../sources/Fast%20Forward%202025%20AI%20for%20Humanity%20Report%20%28web%29.md)).
