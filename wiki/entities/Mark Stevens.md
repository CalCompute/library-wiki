# Mark Stevens

## Bottom line

Mark Stevens is a venture capitalist and the founder and managing partner of S-Cubed Capital. He started a second term on the USC Board of Trustees in 2024 ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- He and his wife, Mary Stevens, "have donated more than $100 million to USC programs and entities" (B) ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
- Their $22 million endowment in 2004 established the USC Stevens Institute for Technology Commercialization. It took the name USC Stevens Center for Innovation in 2007 (B, F) ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Giving Pledge lists "Mark and Mary Stevens" ([Giving Pledge pledger list (web)](../sources/Giving%20Pledge%20pledger%20list%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- USC Board of Trustees, 2001 to 2021, and again from 2024 ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
- S-Cubed Capital, founder and managing partner since 2012 ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He worked at Sequoia Capital for 23 years, as an associate and then managing partner ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
- At USC, he earned bachelor's degrees in electrical engineering and economics (1981) and a master's in computer engineering (1984). He has an MBA from Harvard Business School (1989) ([USC Today Mark Stevens returns to Board of Trustees (web)](../sources/USC%20Today%20Mark%20Stevens%20returns%20to%20Board%20of%20Trustees%20%28web%29.md)).
