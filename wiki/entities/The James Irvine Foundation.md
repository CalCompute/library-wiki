# The James Irvine Foundation

![The James Irvine Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/The%20James%20Irvine%20Foundation.svg)

_Logo: The James Irvine Foundation, [About Us page](https://www.irvine.org/about-us/). The wordmark only, without the white flower mark that the site shows beside it. Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

The James Irvine Foundation is a private, independent foundation that gives grants in California. Its goal is "a California where all low-income workers have the power to advance economically" ([Irvine Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Irvine%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It gave "$160.2 million in grants in 2025 to organizations in California" (B) ([Irvine Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Irvine%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its endowment "totaled $3.6 billion by the end of 2025" (B) ([Irvine Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Irvine%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- The California Policy Lab names it among its supporters in the report behind the California AI-Unemployment Tracker (E) ([CPL Tracking AI-Related Job Loss Using UI Claims Data (web)](../sources/CPL%20Tracking%20AI-Related%20Job%20Loss%20Using%20UI%20Claims%20Data%20%28web%29.md)).
