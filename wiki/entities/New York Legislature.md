# New York Legislature

## Bottom line

- The New York Legislature makes New York's state laws. Economic Development Law § 361 sets up the Empire AI research institute at the University at Buffalo ([NY Economic Development Law 361 Empire AI (web)](../sources/NY%20Economic%20Development%20Law%20361%20Empire%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Section 361 says the institute "shall be operated and managed by the consortium", a not-for-profit corporation ([NY Economic Development Law 361 Empire AI (web)](../sources/NY%20Economic%20Development%20Law%20361%20Empire%20AI%20%28web%29.md)).
