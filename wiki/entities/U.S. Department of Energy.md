# U.S. Department of Energy

## Bottom line

- The U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) is the federal department that implements the Genesis Mission under Executive Order 14363 ([Executive Order 14363 Launching the Genesis Mission (web)](../sources/Executive%20Order%2014363%20Launching%20the%20Genesis%20Mission%20%28web%29.md)). Its Office of Science has its own entity page: [U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science](U.S.%20Department%20of%20Energy%2C%20Office%20of%20Science.md).

## Why they matter

- Element F: In 2020, DOE, IBM and the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy led the COVID-19 High Performance Computing Consortium. It gave supercomputer time "at no cost" ([DOE COVID-19 HPC Consortium Q&A 2020-03-30 (web)](../sources/DOE%20COVID-19%20HPC%20Consortium%20Q%26A%202020-03-30%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element F: DOE reported "more than $800 million in committed partner support" to the Genesis Mission in July 2026 ([DOE Genesis Mission 800 million partner commitments (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20800%20million%20partner%20commitments%20%28web%29.md)).
- Element B: DOE announced "12 Phase II Genesis Mission project awards totaling $159 million" on October 8, 2026 ([DOE Genesis Mission Phase II awards release 2026-10 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Phase%20II%20awards%20release%202026-10%20%28web%29.md)).
- "Under Secretary for Science Darío Gil serves as DOE’s director for the Genesis Mission." ([DOE Genesis Mission Collaboration page (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Collaboration%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- DOE launched the [Genesis Mission Consortium](Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium.md) on Feb. 9, 2026; TechWerx, operated by RTI International, administers it ([DOE Genesis Mission Consortium launch release 2026-02-09 (web)](../sources/DOE%20Genesis%20Mission%20Consortium%20launch%20release%202026-02-09%20%28web%29.md)).
