# William and Flora Hewlett Foundation

![William and Flora Hewlett Foundation](../../raw/assets/logos/William%20and%20Flora%20Hewlett%20Foundation.svg)

_Logo: The William and Flora Hewlett Foundation, [About Us page](https://hewlett.org/about-us/). Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

The Hewlett Foundation is a nonpartisan philanthropy in the Bay Area, set up in 1966 by Bill and Flora Hewlett ([Hewlett Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Hewlett%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- In 2024 it "awarded $631 million in grants, with nearly three-fourths of that total awarded as flexible funding" (B) ([Hewlett Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Hewlett%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its assets were "approximately $13.9 billion" on December 31, 2024 (B) ([Hewlett Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Hewlett%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- It is "wholly independent of HP, Hewlett Packard Enterprise (HPE), and the HPE Foundation" ([Hewlett Foundation about us (web)](../sources/Hewlett%20Foundation%20about%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
