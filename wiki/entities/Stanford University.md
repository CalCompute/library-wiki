# Stanford University

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as Stanford University in Stanford, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Doctorate Large" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research 1: Very High Spending and Doctorate Production". Its control is "Private not-for-profit" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Stanford Research Computing runs the Stanford Research Computing Facility (SRCF), a data center on SLAC land. It opened in 2013 and grew in 2023 ([Stanford Research Computing facilities (web)](../sources/Stanford%20Research%20Computing%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Provost funds the base of Sherlock, a shared cluster "available for the use of any Stanford faculty member, and associated research teams". Faculty can add nodes with grant funds ([Stanford Research Computing facilities (web)](../sources/Stanford%20Research%20Computing%20facilities%20%28web%29.md)).
