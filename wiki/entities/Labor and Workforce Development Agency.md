# Labor and Workforce Development Agency

## Bottom line

- The California Labor and Workforce Development Agency (LWDA) leads the workforce reviews in Executive Order N-6-26.

## Why they matter

- Directive 4 reviews collective bargaining and AI, including "worker voice". Directive 5 reviews workforce training. Both were due Oct. 15, 2026 ([Executive Order N-6-26](../sources/Executive%20Order%20N-6-26.md)).
