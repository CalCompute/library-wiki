# GÉANT

## Bottom line

GÉANT published document AARC-I044, a guide to the WISE Baseline Acceptable Use Policy (AUP), "on behalf of the AARC project" ([AARC-I044 Implementers Guide to the WISE Baseline AUP (web)](../sources/AARC-I044%20Implementers%20Guide%20to%20the%20WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Elements D and F: the guide says that a user sees an AUP "preferably just a single time" ([AARC-I044 Implementers Guide to the WISE Baseline AUP (web)](../sources/AARC-I044%20Implementers%20Guide%20to%20the%20WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- The AARC2 project had funding from the European Community's Horizon2020 Programme, Grant Agreement No. 730941 ([AARC-I044 Implementers Guide to the WISE Baseline AUP (web)](../sources/AARC-I044%20Implementers%20Guide%20to%20the%20WISE%20Baseline%20AUP%20%28web%29.md)).
