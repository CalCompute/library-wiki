# NAIRR

## Bottom line

- The National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) is the national program for shared AI compute. NSF funds its Operations Center at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), with the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC).

## Why they matter

- The federal task force plan of 2023 estimates "$2.6 billion over an initial six-year period" ([NAIRR Task Force final report 2023 (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Task%20Force%20final%20report%202023%20%28web%29.md)).
- NSF says the pilot "has supported over 800 research projects" since 2024. The Operations Center is to take its functions "into the long-term national infrastructure" ([NSF NAIRR Operations Center update (web)](../sources/NSF%20NAIRR%20Operations%20Center%20update%20%28web%29.md)).
- The pilot portal now expands NAIRR as "National Architecture for Innovation, Research, and Resource-sharing" ([NAIRR Pilot overview (web)](../sources/NAIRR%20Pilot%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [National Science Foundation](National%20Science%20Foundation.md): the NAIRR Pilot is a federal program that NSF leads ([NAIRR Pilot proposal instructions](../sources/NAIRR%20Pilot%20proposal%20instructions.md)).
