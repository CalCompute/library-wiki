# NAIRR

## Bottom line

- The National AI Research Resource (NAIRR) is the national program for shared AI compute. NSF funds its Operations Center at the San Diego Supercomputer Center (SDSC), with the Texas Advanced Computing Center (TACC).

## Affiliations

- [National Science Foundation](National%20Science%20Foundation.md): the NAIRR Pilot is a federal program that NSF leads ([NAIRR Pilot proposal instructions](../sources/NAIRR%20Pilot%20proposal%20instructions.md)).
