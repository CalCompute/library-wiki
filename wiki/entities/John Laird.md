# John Laird

## Bottom line

John Laird is a member of the California State Senate for a district of Santa Cruz, San Benito, Monterey and most of San Luis Obispo counties. Voters re-elected him on November 5, 2024 ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- He chairs or serves on the Budget and Fiscal Review Committee and the Joint Legislative Budget Committee (B) ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [California State Senate](California%20State%20Senate.md): Senator ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: Secretary of the California Natural Resources Agency, January 5, 2011, to January 7, 2019 ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- past: Assembly member for the 27th District, elected in 2002, 2004 and 2006 ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- He taught environmental policy at UC Santa Cruz ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
- He graduated in politics from UC Santa Cruz in 1972 ([Laird Senate biography (web)](../sources/Laird%20Senate%20biography%20%28web%29.md)).
