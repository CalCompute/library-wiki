# Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center

## Bottom line

- The Pittsburgh Supercomputing Center (PSC) is "a joint computational research center with Carnegie Mellon University and the University of Pittsburgh" ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md)). It runs the Bridges-2 supercomputer and is to build Bridges-3 ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania gave PSC $500,000 in 2020-21 and again in 2021-22. The money is for workforce development and outreach ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md); [PSC state funding release 2021-07-12 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-07-12%20%28web%29.md)).
- A $1 million state capital grant helped pay for power and cooling upgrades for Bridges-2 ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md)).
- PSC says its state funding over six years helped bring "over $200 million of Federal funding into the State" ([PSC state funding release 2021-07-12 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-07-12%20%28web%29.md)).
- A "$10 million NSF award" funds Bridges-3. ACCESS and the National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot are its main allocation routes ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
- NSF's $10,000,000 cooperative agreement for Bridges-3 is with Carnegie Mellon University, in the ACSS program ([NSF award 2614022 Bridges-3 API record (web)](../sources/NSF%20award%202614022%20Bridges-3%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
- "several federal agencies, the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, and private industry" support PSC ([PSC About (web)](../sources/PSC%20About%20%28web%29.md)). Proprietary users come in through the "Corporate Affiliates Program" ([PSC allocations (web)](../sources/PSC%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)).
- PSC is one of the four Distributed Science Centers of the LCCF ([NSF FY2026 budget request LCCF chapter (web)](../sources/NSF%20FY2026%20budget%20request%20LCCF%20chapter%20%28web%29.md), p. MREFC-14).

## Affiliations

- Joint center of [Carnegie Mellon University](Carnegie%20Mellon%20University.md) and the University of Pittsburgh ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Its machine room is in the Next Tier Connect @ Pittsburgh East building in Monroeville ([PSC state funding release 2021-01-21 (web)](../sources/PSC%20state%20funding%20release%202021-01-21%20%28web%29.md)).
- Construction of Bridges-3 is to start "at PSC’s new data center in early 2027" ([CMU NSF award for Bridges-3 2026-07 (web)](../sources/CMU%20NSF%20award%20for%20Bridges-3%202026-07%20%28web%29.md)).
