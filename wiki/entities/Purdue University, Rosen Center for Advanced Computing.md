# Purdue University, Rosen Center for Advanced Computing

## Bottom line

- The Rosen Center for Advanced Computing (RCAC) is the research computing center of Purdue University in Indiana. It runs the Gautschi community cluster and the National Science Foundation (NSF) Anvil supercomputer ([Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Its community cluster program is "20+ years" old ([Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md)). Researchers buy Gautschi access in units of CPU cores or "5 GPU-year" packages ([Purdue RCAC Gautschi overview (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%20Gautschi%20overview%20%28web%29.md)).
- Anvil became a National Artificial Intelligence Research Resource (NAIRR) Pilot resource provider in May 2024. It added 84 NVIDIA H100 GPUs ([Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md)).
- RCAC hosts AnvilGPT and Purdue GenAI Studio "entirely on-premises" ([Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md)).
- Purdue is a Lighthouse Institution of the Argonne Leadership Computing Facility ([Purdue RCAC 2025 annual report (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%202025%20annual%20report%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Anvil Advisory Board has six members from universities, TACC, two DOE laboratories and NVIDIA ([Purdue RCAC Anvil Advisory Board (web)](../sources/Purdue%20RCAC%20Anvil%20Advisory%20Board%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background
