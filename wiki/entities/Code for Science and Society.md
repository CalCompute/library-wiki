# Code for Science and Society

![Code for Science and Society](../../raw/assets/logos/Code%20for%20Science%20and%20Society.svg)

_Logo: Code for Science & Society, [codeforsociety.org](https://www.codeforsociety.org/about). Fetched 2026-11-19._

## Bottom line

- Code for Science & Society (CS&S) is "a registered US public charity" in Portland, Oregon, with "programs centered on social infrastructure in research and technology" ([Code for Science and Society about (web)](../sources/Code%20for%20Science%20and%20Society%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It promotes "technology initiatives that are equitable, open-source, and sustainable", and partners with "nonprofits, funders, universities, and other stakeholders to explore community governance, develop participatory funding programs" ([Code for Science and Society about (web)](../sources/Code%20for%20Science%20and%20Society%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
