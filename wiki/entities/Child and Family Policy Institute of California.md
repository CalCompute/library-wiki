# Child and Family Policy Institute of California

## Bottom line

- The Child and Family Policy Institute of California (CFPIC) is a nonprofit, founded in 2004, that partners with county human services agencies. Its board is ten county agency directors and other professionals ([CFPIC who we are (web)](../sources/CFPIC%20who%20we%20are%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It is a model of a nonprofit intermediary. The agency heads it serves sit on its board, and its program staff come from county careers ([CFPIC who we are (web)](../sources/CFPIC%20who%20we%20are%20%28web%29.md)).
