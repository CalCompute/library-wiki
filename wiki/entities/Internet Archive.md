## Bottom line

- The Internet Archive is "a non-profit research library founded in 1996", and "a Federal Depository Library" ([Internet Archive about (web)](../sources/Internet%20Archive%20about%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It works "with libraries, publishers, and other partners to preserve and provide access to more than 1 trillion web pages via the Wayback Machine" ([Internet Archive about (web)](../sources/Internet%20Archive%20about%20%28web%29.md)).
