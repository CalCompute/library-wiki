# Center for AI Safety

## Bottom line

- The Center for AI Safety (CAIS) is an AI safety nonprofit. It runs a free cluster of 80 A100 GPUs for ML safety research ([Center for AI Safety About (web)](../sources/Center%20for%20AI%20Safety%20About%20%28web%29.md); [Center for AI Safety Compute Cluster (web)](../sources/Center%20for%20AI%20Safety%20Compute%20Cluster%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It gives compute to university researchers under a use rule (ML safety), with a support team. It is not taking new external applications ([Center for AI Safety About (web)](../sources/Center%20for%20AI%20Safety%20About%20%28web%29.md); [Center for AI Safety Compute Cluster (web)](../sources/Center%20for%20AI%20Safety%20Compute%20Cluster%20%28web%29.md)).
