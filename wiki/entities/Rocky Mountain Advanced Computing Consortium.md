# Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium

## Bottom line

- The Rocky Mountain Advanced Computing Consortium (RMACC) is "a collaboration among academic and research institutions located throughout the intermountain states". It is "housed at the University of Colorado Boulder" ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- It lists 44 member institutions, among them universities, colleges, Idaho National Lab, NCAR, NOAA, NLR and USGS ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its board of directors has a chair, a vice-chair, an executive director and board members from member institutions. The 2027-2028 list adds a student representative ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its member resource is the Alpine cluster at CU Boulder ([RMACC Member Resources (web)](../sources/RMACC%20Member%20Resources%20%28web%29.md)). RMACC users get a Trailhead share and an "RMACC Ascent" tier of 250,000 SUs on Alpine ([CU Research Computing Alpine allocations (web)](../sources/CU%20Research%20Computing%20Alpine%20allocations%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- [University of Colorado Boulder](University%20of%20Colorado%20Boulder.md): host ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Chair: Shelley Knuth, University of Colorado Boulder. Executive Director: Becky Yeager, University of Colorado Boulder ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
- Contact: rmacc@colorado.edu ([RMACC About us (web)](../sources/RMACC%20About%20us%20%28web%29.md)).
