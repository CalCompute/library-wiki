# Chan Zuckerberg Initiative

![Chan Zuckerberg Initiative](../../raw/assets/logos/Chan%20Zuckerberg%20Initiative.svg)

_Logo: Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, [home page](https://chanzuckerberg.com/). Fetched 2026-11-11._

## Bottom line

Priscilla Chan and Mark Zuckerberg founded the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative (CZI) in 2015. Its focus areas are science and education ([CZI homepage (web)](../sources/CZI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Since 2015, CZI "has awarded approximately $7.22 billion in grants and invested nearly $300 million in ventures" (B) ([CZI homepage (web)](../sources/CZI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its science work is "the new era of AI-powered biology", at the Biohub (F) ([CZI homepage (web)](../sources/CZI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- It works through an LLC, a 501(c)(3) private foundation, a donor-advised fund at the Silicon Valley Community Foundation and a 501(c)(4) organization ([CZI homepage (web)](../sources/CZI%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
