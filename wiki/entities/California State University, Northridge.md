# California State University, Northridge

## Bottom line

- Carnegie lists it as California State University-Northridge in Northridge, California. Its 2025 Institutional Classification is "Mixed Undergraduate/Graduate-Master's Large/Medium" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).
- Its 2025 Research Activity Designation is "Research Colleges and Universities". Its control is "Public" ([Carnegie 2025 Public Data File (web)](../sources/Carnegie%202025%20Public%20Data%20File%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- Three NSF awards since 2021 pay for CSU Northridge clusters: computational chemistry and materials (2117956), computational biology (2320846) and quantum information and materials (2406524). The first is its "first mixed GPU/CPU high performance computing (HPC) cluster" ([NSF campus computing awards California 2019-2026 (web)](../sources/NSF%20computing%20awards%20to%20California%20campuses%202019-2026%20API%20record%20%28web%29.md)).
