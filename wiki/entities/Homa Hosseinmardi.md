# Homa Hosseinmardi

## Bottom line

- Homa Hosseinmardi is Assistant Professor of Data Science (DataX) and Computational Communication at UCLA ([Hosseinmardi UCLA DataX profile (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20UCLA%20DataX%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- She directs the OASIS Lab at UCLA, which she co-founded with Amir Ghasemian ([Hosseinmardi UCLA DataX profile (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20UCLA%20DataX%20profile%20%28web%29.md); [Hosseinmardi OASIS Lab homepage (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20OASIS%20Lab%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- UCLA: Assistant Professor of Data Science (DataX) and Computational Communication ([Hosseinmardi UCLA DataX profile (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20UCLA%20DataX%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- UCLA DataX: affiliated faculty ([Hosseinmardi UCLA DataX profile (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20UCLA%20DataX%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- OASIS Lab, UCLA: co-founder and Principal Investigator ([Hosseinmardi OASIS Lab homepage (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20OASIS%20Lab%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Her research studies sociotechnical systems and information ecosystems at large scale, with a focus on safety and trustworthiness ([Hosseinmardi UCLA DataX profile (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20UCLA%20DataX%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- The OASIS Lab (Online and AI Systems' Integrity & Safety) studies the safety and integrity of online ecosystems ([Hosseinmardi OASIS Lab homepage (web)](../sources/Hosseinmardi%20OASIS%20Lab%20homepage%20%28web%29.md)).
