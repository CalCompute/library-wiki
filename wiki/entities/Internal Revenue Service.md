# Internal Revenue Service

## Bottom line

The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) is the federal tax agency of the Department of the Treasury. It publishes Form 990 and public data on tax-exempt organizations ([IRS Form 990 (web)](../sources/IRS%20Form%20990%20%28web%29.md); [IRS EO BMF extract page (web)](../sources/IRS%20EO%20BMF%20extract%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The IRS posts the Exempt Organizations Business Master File Extract (EO BMF) by state. The posting of 10/13/2026 has 1,975,975 records ([IRS EO BMF extract page (web)](../sources/IRS%20EO%20BMF%20extract%20page%20%28web%29.md)). (A)
- Its field layout gives each organization a National Taxonomy of Exempt Entities (NTEE) code. One code is U41 "Computer Science" ([IRS EO BMF field layout (web)](../sources/IRS%20EO%20BMF%20field%20layout%20%28web%29.md)). (A)
- Form 990 asks an exempt organization about its governing body, its policies and its public disclosure ([IRS Form 990 (web)](../sources/IRS%20Form%20990%20%28web%29.md)). (B, C)
- The IRS gives Form 990 series filings as XML and other data sets in bulk, "updated monthly" ([IRS TEOS bulk data downloads (web)](../sources/IRS%20TEOS%20bulk%20data%20downloads%20%28web%29.md)). (A)
