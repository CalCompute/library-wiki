# Mark Nitzberg

## Bottom line

- Mark Nitzberg is Executive Director of the Center for Human-Compatible AI (CHAI) at UC Berkeley ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He leads strategic outreach for Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) and directs technology research at the Berkeley Roundtable on the International Economy (BRIE) ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- CHAI, UC Berkeley: Executive Director ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md); [Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- BAIR Lab: Head of Strategic Outreach ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md); [Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- BRIE: Director of Technology Research ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- BRIE calls him a computer scientist and entrepreneur who studies how AI affects society, the economy and democracy ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He began to study AI at MIT in the early 1980s ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He wrote his Ph.D. in Computer Vision and Human Perception at Harvard University in 1991, under David Mumford ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- B.A., Mathematics and Music Composition, University of Oregon ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was founding CEO of The Blindsight Corporation, a maker of assistive technologies for low vision and active aging ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- After A9/Amazon acquired Blindsight, he was Director of Computer Vision Products at A9/Amazon ([Nitzberg CHAI people page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20CHAI%20people%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He was founding chief technology officer (CTO) of Smartleaf, which automates the management of investment portfolios ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He built technology ventures that apply AI in health care, finance, education and data analytics for developing regions ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He has worked at Bell Laboratories, Microsoft and Amazon ([Nitzberg SCAI 2025 participant page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20SCAI%202025%20participant%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a co-author of "Solomon's Code", a book on how AI changes human values, trust and power ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- He is a co-author of "The AI Generation" ([Nitzberg SCAI 2025 participant page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20SCAI%202025%20participant%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- He co-wrote "When code isn't law" with Brian Judge and Stuart Russell, in Policy and Society, 2025 ([Nitzberg When code isn't law (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20When%20code%20isn%27t%20law%20%28web%29.md)).
- He co-wrote the journal article "Governing AI" with John Zysman, in 2020 ([Nitzberg BRIE profile (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20BRIE%20profile%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Singapore Conference on AI (SCAI) lists him as a contributor to SCAI 2025 ([Nitzberg SCAI 2025 participant page (web)](../sources/Nitzberg%20SCAI%202025%20participant%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
