# Press Information Bureau, Government of India

## Bottom line

- The Press Information Bureau (PIB) publishes information from the Government of India. Its research unit, PIB Research, wrote the two IndiaAI documents in this library ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026217795001 (web)](../sources/PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026217795001%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- PIB reports that more than 38,000 GPUs are available at ₹65 per hour under the IndiaAI Mission ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026210784401 (web)](../sources/PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026210784401%20%28web%29.md)).
- PIB reports the announcement of 20,000 more GPUs on 17 February 2026 ([PIB IndiaAI document doc2026217795001 (web)](../sources/PIB%20IndiaAI%20document%20doc2026217795001%20%28web%29.md)).
