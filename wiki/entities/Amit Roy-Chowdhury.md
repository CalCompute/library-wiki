# Amit Roy-Chowdhury

## Bottom line

- Amit K. Roy-Chowdhury is Professor and UC Presidential Chair at UC Riverside. He co-directs the UC Riverside AI Research and Education (RAISE) Institute ([Roy-Chowdhury UCR personal page (web)](../sources/Roy-Chowdhury%20UCR%20personal%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- He co-directs RAISE@UCR, the campus AI institute with three pillars and eleven thrusts ([RAISE at UCR homepage (web)](../sources/RAISE%20at%20UCR%20homepage%20%28web%29.md); [RAISE at UCR people (web)](../sources/RAISE%20at%20UCR%20people%20%28web%29.md)).
- His group works on computer vision, image processing and machine learning, and lists "AI for Science" as an interest ([Roy-Chowdhury UCR personal page (web)](../sources/Roy-Chowdhury%20UCR%20personal%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Affiliations

- UC Riverside, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering; Co-Director of a DoD Center of Excellence; Chair of the Robotics program ([Roy-Chowdhury UCR personal page (web)](../sources/Roy-Chowdhury%20UCR%20personal%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Background

- Chair of the ECE Department at UCR from 2018 to 2021; Fellow of the IEEE and IAPR ([Roy-Chowdhury UCR personal page (web)](../sources/Roy-Chowdhury%20UCR%20personal%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
