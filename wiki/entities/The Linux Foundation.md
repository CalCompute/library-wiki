# The Linux Foundation

## Bottom line

- The Linux Foundation is "a nonprofit mutual benefit corporation" under Section 501(c)(6), organised in Oregon. Its purposes are "to support, promote, protect and standardize Linux and other open source software and technologies" ([Linux Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Linux%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Section 2.1).

## Why they matter

- Its bylaws have three voting member classes, Platinum, Gold and Silver, and non-voting Associates. Nonprofit and government entities can be Associates only ([Linux Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Linux%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Sections 3.1 and 3.2(d)).
- Each Platinum Member appoints one director, up to twenty ([Linux Foundation bylaws (web)](../sources/Linux%20Foundation%20bylaws%20%28web%29.md), Section 5.3(a)).

## Affiliations

- The Jupyter Foundation is "a directed fund of the Linux Foundation 501(c)(6)" ([Jupyter governance overview page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20governance%20overview%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The Linux Foundation "holds assets in custody for Project Jupyter" ([Jupyter Executive Council page (web)](../sources/Jupyter%20Executive%20Council%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
