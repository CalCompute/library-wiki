# United Nations Office for Digital and Emerging Technologies

## Bottom line

- The United Nations Office for Digital and Emerging Technologies (ODET) is a UN office. With the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP), it initiated the DPI Safeguards Initiative ([DPI Safeguards resources page (web)](../sources/DPI%20Safeguards%20resources%20page%20%28web%29.md)).

## Why they matter

- The DPI Safeguards resources include the guide to the Universal DPI Safeguards Framework, adoption pathways and an assessment resource ([DPI Safeguards resources page (web)](../sources/DPI%20Safeguards%20resources%20page%20%28web%29.md)).
- The UN Open Source United portal shows the ODET logo under the Open Source Enablement Compass (OSEC) ([UN Open Source United initiatives (web)](../sources/UN%20Open%20Source%20United%20initiatives%20%28web%29.md)).
