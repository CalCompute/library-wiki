## Leadership Academies

### [Information Technology Leadership Academy](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/information-technology-leadership-academy/)

This program is for public sector IT professionals who wish to expand their leadership skills and business acumen.

### [Project Management Leadership Academy](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/project-management-leadership-academy/)

This academy is a course of study for public sector IT professionals seeking to expand their project management and leadership skills.

### [Information Security Leadership Academy](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/isla-academy/)

This program prepares candidates for the critical roles of Information Security Officer (ISO), Agency Information Security Officer (AIO), or an expanded role within their department’s security office.

## Boot camps

### [GenAI Boot Camp](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/gen-ai-bootcamp/)

This boot camp is designed for public sector professionals interested in building practical GenAI skills. Participants will learn responsible use practices, hands-on applications for government work, and innovative approaches to solving real public sector challenges.

### [Emerging IT Leaders Boot Camp](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/emerging-it-leaders-boot-camp/)

This boot camp is geared for entry-level IT supervisors, new IT managers, or those functioning in a demonstrated lead capacity who wish to expand or refresh their foundational leadership skills. This boot camp is well suited for those that cannot commit to the longer academies or need a more economical alternative for training.

### [Cybersecurity Boot Camp](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/workforce-development/cybersecurity-boot-camp/)

This boot camp is geared for senior or managerial Information Security professionals who wish to expand or refresh their foundational skills in cybersecurity methodology and best practices. This boot camp is well suited for those that cannot commit to the longer academies or need a more economical alternative for training.
