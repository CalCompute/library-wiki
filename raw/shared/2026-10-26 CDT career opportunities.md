## Build a career at California Department of Technology

As the recognized central Information Technology (IT) organization for the State of California, the California Department of Technology (CDT) is searching for talented, highly qualified individuals who are ready to build a career in supporting the operations of some of the largest and most sophisticated computer systems in the state.

Whether you’re interested in the field of IT or administrative support services, we offer excellent opportunities and a comfortable work environment. So, come build a career at CDT! Be a part of advancing California’s IT infrastructure and making California State government more effective, mobile, and accessible.

[See Our Excellent Benefits](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/career-opportunities/our-benefits/)

See the list of benefits offered by CDT.

[What is the State Hiring Process?](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/career-opportunities/state-hiring-process/)

Become a member of the CDT team or any State department or agency.

[Find examinations](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/career-opportunities/state-hiring-process/examinations/)

Find available exams to pass in order to get a job in a specific classification.

[Find CDT job openings](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/career-opportunities/job-openings/)

Create your CalCareer account with [California State Jobs](https://calcareers.ca.gov/CalHRPublic/CreateNewAccount.aspx), and find a job opening at CDT.

[Student assistant jobs](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/career-opportunities/students/)

Begin your career with a competitive edge by gaining hands-on experience in your field of study.

For more information visit our [IT Classification Mapping](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ADA_IT-Classification-Mapping-rev5-15-25.pdf) document.
