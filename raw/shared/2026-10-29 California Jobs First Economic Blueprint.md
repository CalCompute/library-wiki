## California Jobs First State Economic Blueprint

##### Built on the foundation of the thirteen regional plans, the Blueprint is California’s economic roadmap to create, attract, and ensure access to good-paying jobs for all Californians.

[Check out the Blueprint](https://jobsfirst.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/Economic-Blueprint.pdf)

![Blueprint booklet](//test.jobsfirst.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/Blueprint-booklet.png)

## California Strategic Sectors

##### The Blueprint identifies the strategic sectors vital for driving California’s future growth and innovation, representing the diverse strengths of each region and organized into four distinct categories.

### Strengthen

Sectors where California has an established competitive position and/or significant employment, but where there is leveling growth or wages.

##### Creative Economy

The creative economy has deep roots in California’s history and continues to be an engine for cultural expression and economic growth.

[Learn More](https://business.ca.gov/industries/creative-economy/)

This sector includes:

![film icon](//test.jobsfirst.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/CAJ1_SubSector_Icons_Film_FullColor.png)
[Film, TV, & the Arts](https://film.ca.gov/)

##### Manufacturing

California is home to a large, highly skilled manufacturing workforce, producing goods on a large scale, typically for mass-market products and helping to support the state’s other strategic sectors.

[Learn More](https://business.ca.gov/industries/manufacturing/)

##### Financial & Professional Services

Encompassing a range of industries from banking to insurance and accounting, California’s financial & professional services sector provides thousands of high-paying jobs and supports the state’s economic activities.

[Learn More](https://business.ca.gov/industries/financial-professional-services/)

##### Tourism & Outdoor Recreation

Home to exceptional natural beauty and tourist attractions, California leads the nation in total spending on tourism.

[Learn More](https://industry.visitcalifornia.com/)

##### Working Lands & Water

California’s working lands, led by top-tier farming and food production, are boosted by strong agtech investment, while growing bio- and blue economies benefit from natural resources and coastal access.

[Learn More](https://business.ca.gov/industries/working-lands-water/)

This sector includes:

![agricultural icon](//test.jobsfirst.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/CAJ1_SubSector_Icons_AgProduction_FullColor.png)
[Agricultural Production](https://www.cdfa.ca.gov/)

![food processing icon](//test.jobsfirst.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/CAJ1_SubSector_Icons_FoodProcessing_FullColor.png)Food Processing

##### Transport & Logistics

California’s transport & logistics sector is diverse, with investment driving greener, more equitable initiatives to continue setting the standard for sustainable and inclusive development.

[Learn More](https://business.ca.gov/resources/supply-chain/)

### Accelerate

Sectors with moderate to high projected growth that are ready for expansion, where additional investments (e.g., capital, infrastructure) could “bend the curve” to generate growth.

Backed by an experienced workforce, existing infrastructure and public-private funding, California is well-positioned to drive the future of aerospace & defense innovation.

### Anchor

Regional anchors that are critical for attracting and supporting industry activities while often providing quality, good-paying jobs within local communities.

##### Childcare

Childcare is essential for many Californians, helping with the balance between work and family responsibilities and contributing to higher workforce participation among parents, particularly women.

##### Education & Skilled Workforce

Education plays a crucial role in developing a skilled workforce that meets industry needs. As the economy evolves, the state’s education and workforce systems must adapt to meet the demands of the future of work.

##### Healthcare

California’s healthcare sector employs around 2 million people, providing essential care across the state while offering pathways to accessible, good-paying job opportunities.

##### Housing

Affordable, quality housing provides workforce stability and allows residents to live in their preferred locations and near their workplaces.

##### Infrastructure

Infrastructure plays a critical role in supporting industry and community activities, providing housing and consequently offering pathways to good-paying jobs.

This sector includes:

## Pilot Sectors

##### Four pilot sectors have been identified to model strategies that accelerate industry attraction and expand access to good-paying jobs.​

##### Building on strengths in research & development and a strong manufacturing workforce, California is  well-positioned for the precision and advanced manufacturing activities required for each pilot sector. Over the next two years, the state will focus on these four to drive meaningful impact across regions.
