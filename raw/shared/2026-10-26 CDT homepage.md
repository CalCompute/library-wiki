## [California is the home of GenAI](https://genai.ca.gov/)

California is the birthplace of innovations that have changed the world. Generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) is no exception. As the home of Silicon Valley, it’s no surprise we’re home to some of the top artificial intelligence companies in the world.

California state government is building a model for how to responsibly implement GenAI in the public sector.
