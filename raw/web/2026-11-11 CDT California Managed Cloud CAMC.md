## Why CAMC?

California Managed Cloud (CAMC) offers a public cloud experience for California government organizations – delivering Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) through trusted providers like Azure and AWS. CAMC gives you a cost-effective, scalable alternative to traditional on-premises IT, with seamless access to modern cloud capabilities in a secure, state-compliant environment.

With CAMC you get:

![No CCSA icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/No-CCSA-2.png)

No CCSA

Because CAMC environments (CDT managed or Self-managed) are already secured and fully compliant with State IT security requirements, you can bypass the California Cloud Services Assessment (CCSA) process.

![State secured and compliant icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/CCS-Compliance.png)

State secured, compliant

Modern cloud features wrapped in state security and compliance controls.

![Private marketplace icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/CCS-marketplace.png)

Private Marketplace

Access a curated catalog of secure, compliant cloud services vetted and pre-approved by CDT, ensuring streamlined procurement and simplified service selection.

![Flexible operating models icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/02/CAMC-op-models-150x150.png)

Flexible operating models

Same secure environment, but you choose whether CDT manages it for you, you manage it yourself, or you consume PaaS on top of it.

![Integrated services icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/CCS-Security.png)

Integrated services

SOCaaS, Cloud Provider Interconnect (CPI), 24x7x365 monitoring and help desk, OS patching, backups, etc., as part of the CAMC ecosystem where applicable.

![Data residency and privacy icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/CCS-data-residency.png)

Meets Data Residency & Privacy Requirements

Hosting, storage, and backup solutions through state-approved providers, always supporting California’s regulatory mandates.

![Predictable costs and expert support icon](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/01/CCS-support.png)

Predictable Costs & Expert Support

Stable pricing, trusted expert support from CDT for all of your cloud needs.

## Benefits at a glance

Even with CAMC, whether you choose a managed or self-managed approach, CDT’s secure cloud environment is built to give you the flexibility, protection, and support you need to streamline operations and achieve your organization’s goals with confidence.

### Cost-efficient scaling

Adjust resources up or down as needs change, without large capital expenses.

### Faster delivery

Quickly provision cloud resources so projects move from design to production sooner.

### Right sized environments

Choose instance sizes and platforms that align to your application and performance needs.

### Higher availability

Use native cloud resiliency features to minimize downtime and recover more quickly from issues.

## What are the CAMC service offerings?

California government organizations can choose between two secure, state-compliant cloud service models designed to fit your unique business and technical needs, while allowing you to skip the CCSA process for faster, easier adoption.

## Which CAMC Model Should I Choose?

Choosing between CAMC’s managed or self-managed service comes down to how much day-to-day control and operational responsibility your team wants. Use the matrix below to quickly compare each option and identify who manages what – helping you focus on the right fit for your needs.

| IF THIS SOUNDS LIKE YOU… | CHOOSE | WHY CHOOSE THIS MODEL? | YOU CONTROL | CDT PROVIDES |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| *“We want CDT to handle infrastructure, patching, and monitoring so we can focus on our mission.”* | **CDT Managed** | **Minimal operational burden** - CDT handles the technical heavy lifting for a secure, reliable environment. | Your applications and settings | * servers * OS * security * compliance |
| *“We want full control and will manage servers, OS, and applications ourselves.”* | **Self Managed** | **Maximum flexibility** - Your team configures and manages everything inside your secure virtual environment. | * servers * OS * applications* some networking | * data center * core infrastructure |

## Request service

We’ll help you find the best fit – together. Review the [California Cloud Services Comparison Matrix](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/services/california-cloud-services/#whattoselect) to get an overview, then connect with your [CDT Account Lead](https://www.cdt.ca.gov/account-lead-lookup/) to set up a ***collaborative conversation with a CDT expert.*** Together, we’ll explore your goals and priorities to identify your ideal path forward.

## FAQs

### 1. How long will it take to build my services in the cloud?

Generally, it takes a couple of days to have a scoping meeting to determine your needs and then build to those specifications.

### 2. How do I get billed for Cloud Services?

All Cloud Provider charges incurred are pass-through charges on your bill. Support charges will be added according to the services chosen.

### 3. What group inside CDT will support my services?

A cross-functional team will assist with designing, scoping, building, and supporting your system.

### 4. Do I need to go through the Enterprise Service Delivery (ESD) process for these builds?

Yes, our newly improved ESD process focuses on modernizing how services are delivered to better align with today’s cloud technologies and evolving requirements. The objective is to provide you a more efficient, higher-quality experience.

### 5. What is comprised of the 17% Fee?

A 17% Administrative Support Fee pays for shared services that keep your platforms secure and compliant:

* 4.58% – VHSS: Shared hosting and platform operations, billing, coordination, and compliance support
* 12.19% – CAMC Cloud Services team + CAMC infrastructure/software, that includes:
  + Standardized cloud provisioning & account vending
  + Network & connectivity
  + Private marketplace management
  + Enterprise-grade security logging, monitoring & threat detection
  + Identify & access management
  + Policy & governance enforcement
  + Cost management, spend tracking & enterprise support
  + Control tower & landing zones

This single, bundled fee reduces your team’s overhead, maintains consistent security and compliance, and simplifies vendor and license management so you can focus on mission delivery.
