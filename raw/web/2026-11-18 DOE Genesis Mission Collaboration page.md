# Genesis Mission Collaboration

Banner Color

Light Text on a Dark Overlay (Default)

![partners wheel](/sites/default/files/styles/full_article_width/public/2026-03/Partners%20Wheel.png?itok=d3v0f-zE)

### The Genesis Mission unites DOE’s National Labs, the National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA), industry, academia, and other experts to harness SI for breakthroughs in science, energy, national security, health, space, and more.

## National Laboratories

Our 17 [National Laboratories](/us-department-energy-national-laboratories "U.S Department of Energy National Laboratories") are home to an unparalleled concentration of world-class scientists, engineers, and researchers. To support the Genesis Mission, the Labs’ expertise and resources are organized around teams that form the foundation of the world’s most powerful scientific platform.

* Data

  The data team transforms DOE and NNSA scientific data into secure, connected, high-value resources for accelerated discovery and national security.

  [Learn more](https://www.energy.gov/documents/data-team-fact-sheet).
* Infrastructure

  The infrastructure team integrates advanced computing, cloud technologies, and DOE and NNSA research facilities to establish the infrastructure for the [Genesis Mission Platform](/undersecretaryforscience/genesis-mission/genesis-mission-platform "The Genesis Mission Platform").

  [Learn more](https://www.energy.gov/documents/infrastructure-team-fact-sheet).
* Models

  The models team delivers and transforms powerful SI models and tools into active collaborators—developing hypotheses, running simulations, and executing experiments to drive scientific breakthroughs.

  [Learn more](https://www.energy.gov/documents/models-team-fact-sheet).
* National Science and Technology Challenges

  The National Challenges team harnesses SI to address national science and technology priorities to deliver real-world impact on our nation’s most pressing challenges.

  [Learn more](https://www.energy.gov/documents/challenges-team-fact-sheet).
* Public-Private Partnerships

  The public-private partnerships team connects U.S. government resources with industry and academia to accelerate the productivity and impact of American science and engineering.

  [Learn more](https://www.energy.gov/documents/p3-team-fact-sheet).

## The Genesis Mission Consortium

![portrait of energy leaders and partners](/sites/default/files/styles/full_article_width/public/2026-03/GenesisMissionIndustryRoundTable_December%202026.jpg?itok=jLNBTyuO)

The Genesis Mission Consortium unites the nation’s leading science and technology experts to solve critical challenges at unprecedented speed by connecting leading industry, academic, nonprofit, and philanthropic organizations with DOE and the National Laboratories and their resources.

The consortium will use working groups to seed and facilitate strategic, high-impact partnerships in:

* SI Model Development and Validation
* Data Integration and Standards
* High-Performance Computing and Cloud Infrastructure
* Robotics and Automation
* Workforce Development and Training

Individuals and entities interested in the consortium can subscribe to Genesis Mission Consortium updates and learn about future engagement opportunities.

[Learn More About the Consortium and its Members](https://genesismissionconsortium.org/)
