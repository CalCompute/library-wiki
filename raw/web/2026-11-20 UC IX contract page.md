# IX — Non-Senate Instructional (Lecturers)

American Federation of Teachers (AFT)

[Home](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/employment-policies-contracts/bargaining-units/non-senate-instructional/)
[About](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/employment-policies-contracts/bargaining-units/non-senate-instructional/about/)
[Contract](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/employment-policies-contracts/bargaining-units/non-senate-instructional/contract/)
[News](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/employment-policies-contracts/bargaining-units/non-senate-instructional/news/)

The previous collective bargaining agreement between the University of California and the American Federation of Teachers (AFT) Non-Senate Instructional (IX) unit expired on June 30, 2026. The parties reached agreement on a one-year contract extension through June 30, 2027, and collective bargaining is continuing.

Use the links below to browse the agreement by article, appendix or side letter. If you have a question about a specific topic, start with the relevant section. For help understanding how a provision applies to your situation, contact your location’s labor relations office or union representative.

Updates on bargaining negotiations can be found [**here.**](https://ucnet.universityofcalifornia.edu/resources/employment-policies-contracts/bargaining-units/non-senate-instructional/news/)

***New contract expiration date* – June 30, 2027**
