Association of Independent California Colleges and Universities

Panela Ong (she/her), Director of Data and Research

Pamela Ong is the Director of Data and Research at the

Association of Independent California Colleges and

Universities (AICCU). In this role, she leads data efforts

that support the organization’s policy and advocacy

work on behalf of California’s nonprofit independent

colleges and universities.

She has extensive experience in postsecondary

education, with a background in institutional research,

financial aid, and strategic planning. Prior to joining

AICCU, she held various operational roles across the

independent sector and has a strong understanding of how state and federal policies

impact institutions and students. Pam holds a B.S. in Psychology from the University of

California, Davis, and an M.A. in Experimental Psychology from San Jose State University.

She currently serves on the Board of Directors for the California Association for

Institutional Research (CAIR).

Jessica Moldoff, PhD (she/her), Deputy Director of Data, Analysis and Research

Jessica Moldoff, Ph.D., is the Deputy Director of Data,

Analysis and Research at the California Student Aid

Commission (CSAC). As Deputy Director, Jessica is

responsible for facilitating research on program

effectiveness and financial aid administration, as well as

overseeing reports, program projections, and ad hoc

data requests. After graduating from undergrad, Jessica

her dissertation on the idea and implementation of competency-based education. She

then moved to California and has worked at CSAC since 2017.

California State University

Edward Sullivan, PhD (he/him), Assistant Vice Chancellor for Institutional

Research and Analyses

Ed Sullivan is the Assistant Vice Chancellor for

Institutional Research and Analysis at the California

Department of Developmental Services

Maricris Acon, Deputy Director for Children, Adolescents and Young Adult

Services Division

Maricris Acon is the Deputy Director for Children,

Adolescents and Young Adult Services Division at

the California Department of Developmental

Services (DDS). The Division provides leadership and

support to California’s early intervention community,

regional center staff, providers, and contractors

serving children and youth with

intellectual/developmental delays and disabilities

ages birth through 22.  As the Deputy Director,

Maricris oversees initiatives and investments for

improving access and delivery of services in the

Early Start program, and for helping children and youth plan a life consisting of a strong

sense of belonging in the community, independence and self-direction, and

employment.  Before leading CAYAS, Maricris was the Deputy Director for Federal

Programs Division in DDS and was responsible for overseeing compliance with a wide

range of federal requirements.

Prior to coming to DDS, Maricris held a variety of leadership positions for 12 years at

California Department of Finance, overseeing the state budgets for Medi-Cal and

various health programs.  She holds a Master in Public Administration degree from

California State University, East Bay.

Department of Developmental Services

Van Vu, Chief Data Officer

Van Vu is the Chief Data Officer at the Department

of Developmental Services and oversees the

Enterprise Data Operations Office. Van and her

technical team work closely with our dedicated

Program staff, the Information Security Office and

Office of Legal Affairs to facilitate data sharing

within the department and with state agencies and

external organizations. Her office is responsible for

data management and ensuring that data is

ingested into our data warehouse which is made

available to our decision makers for effective

analysis and reporting that will lead them to inform and improve the services provided

to individuals in California with developmental disabilities.

Employment Development Department

Juan Barrios, Chief, Labor Market Information Division

Juan is Chief of the Labor Market Information Division

at the California Employment Development

Department. The division collects, researches,

analyzes, and publishes economic and

demographic information to support labor market

research, program planning, economic

development, and career exploration, as well as

overseeing applied research and collaborative

projects with public and private research

organizations on issues of California’s labor force and economic conditions. The division

also includes out stationed staff that provide local expertise throughout California,

offering tailored research, analysis, and training on labor market information access, as

well as providing local context and responding to inquiries from television, radio, and

print media.

ScholarShare Investment Board

Noah Lightman (he/him), Children’s Savings Account Initiatives Manager

Noah Lightman is the Manager of Children’s Savings
Account (CSA) Initiatives with the ScholarShare
Investment Board (SIB), the state agency that
administers California’s ScholarShare 529 college
savings plan. In this role, he oversees the
development and implementation of California’s
statewide CSA program, the California Kids
Investment and Development Savings Program
(CalKIDS). The CalKIDS Program is the nation’s largest
CSA program, serving over 5 million enrolled
participants. He also manages ScholarShare 529’s
partnerships with CSA programs across the country.

Prior to joining SIB, Noah served as a Legislative Aide
in the California State Assembly, where he
negotiated legislation and provided policy
recommendations on numerous statewide issues.
During this time, he successfully secured the original funding for CalKIDS through the
state budget. He began his career working in different campaign and policy capacities
for political candidates and a labor union in California.

Noah earned his Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science from the University of
California, San Diego.
