A.  Problem Statement

The California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) requests a one-time increase of
$16,428,000 in 2026-27 to advance generative artificial Intelligence (GenAI) for safer and smarter
roadways. This will allow functionality of the generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) solutions
developed to enhance vulnerable roadway user safety and gain traffic mobility insights on the
state highway system. This funding will allow Caltrans to implement the GenAI solutions and
establish ongoing support to seamlessly integrate these solutions into daily operational practices
at Caltrans, advancing both California State Transportation Agency’s (CalSTA’s) and the
Department’s shared vision for a safer, more efficient, and climate-resilient transportation system
across California.
As the pace of technological advancement accelerates, Artificial Intelligence (AI) and data tools
are transforming how public agencies deliver services. Caltrans is operating at a pivotal moment,
where the widespread availability of GenAI tools presents both a unique opportunity and new
challenges. Without proper governance, these tools (many of which are low-cost and easily
accessible) can unintentionally expose sensitive information or lead to flawed decision-making if
used without the appropriate context. The growing complexity of transportation data, combined
with the urgency of safety and mobility challenges, demands a structured, enterprise-wide
approach to AI integration and data management.
California Governor’s Executive Order N-12-23 on Generative AI, along with the Government
Operations (GovOps) “Benefits and Risks of Generative Artificial Intelligence” report, underscores
the importance of robust data stewardship and responsible AI adoption. Caltrans aims to make
any GenAI solutions in transportation reflect broader policy objectives and adhere to ethical and
security standards from the start.
Data and AI are transforming the transportation sector, enabling predictive maintenance, real-
time traffic analytics, and AI-enhanced infrastructure planning. The integration of GenAI into
Caltrans’ operations requires robust governance, ethical oversight, strong data stewardship, and
strategic leadership. Beyond the Generative AI proof of concepts, Caltrans plans to address the
following department-wide challenges:
1. Data Governance
a. Identify disjointed and siloed data throughout the department which includes
multiple petabytes of structured and unstructured data
b. Determine the data lineage to verify focus is on the correct data
c. Define and prioritize the data for quality management required to help meet the
Generative AI data appetite
d. Safeguard data labeling so that only appropriate data is provided to the
Generative AI models
e. Establish data governance to improve data quality to support decision making
2. AI Governance
a. Identify, prioritize and decide which AI projects will provide the highest return on
investment
b. Develop a roadmap for maturing policies, standards, processes and procedures for
implementing, maintaining and supporting AI solutions to advance Caltrans mission
c. Avoid technological sprawl associated with many nascent technologies
d. Align the AI vision, strategies and road map with the California Governor’s Executive
Order N-12-23 and the Caltrans’ 2024-2028 Strategic Plan
e. Address both democratized Generative AI (such as Copilot) as well as future
applications and enhancements including, but not limited to, TMI and VRU
f. Prioritize AI IT Projects to safeguard alignment with the approved AI Vision and Road
Map
g. Lead the Generative AI Working Committee as part of the existing IT Governance
Council to provide recommendations at the Deputy Director level

h. Facilitate alignment of the department policies and directives with those at the
state and federal levels
Caltrans will secure a cohesive strategy and approach for data governance and AI deployment
with support from IT and across the enterprise. Perhaps no other single technology has the
potential for a more significant impact on effective decision making, and the funding will enable
the responsible development, adoption, and implementation of this technology. As Caltrans
navigates the evolving landscape of data-driven decision-making and AI, continued investment
becomes imperative.
Complete Development and Deployment of the Generative AI Solutions for Enhancing
Vulnerable Road User (VRU) Safety and Gaining Traffic Mobility Insights (TMI):
In alignment with the Governor’s Executive Order N-12-23, Caltrans was selected to lead two
proof-of-concept initiatives: enhancing vulnerable roadway user (VRU) safety and gaining traffic
mobility insights (TMI). In May 2024, Caltrans partnered with the California Department of
Technology (CDT) and the Department of General Services (DGS) to award two proof-of-concept
(POC) contracts. The POC phase was completed in January 2025, validating the potential of
GenAI to deliver real-time safety and mobility insights for the State Highway System and a safe
system approach. The POC was extensively evaluated by users, and feedback was used to guide
enhancements in the final tool. Building on this success, Caltrans awarded two implementation
contracts in April 2025. Caltrans redirected existing internal resources to demonstrate Caltrans’
commitment to efficiency and effectiveness efforts. In his remarks at the launch of these
contracts, Governor Newsom emphasized that “California is a global leader in GenAI innovation,
and signing these contracts provides the state an essential tool to help solve some of our most
pressing transportation challenges. California is demonstrating that GenAI can help us improve
the way we do business for Californians,” and added, “this is not a pilot for pilot’s sake—this is
about real outcomes and delivering real change.” However, without formal support and
sustainable funding, Caltrans risks losing momentum at a time when strategic implementation is
critical to realizing long-term benefits for public safety, congestion reduction, and infrastructure
investment.
Caltrans’ core mission to provide a safe, sustainable, and efficient transportation system is
increasingly challenged by rising fatalities among vulnerable road users and growing congestion.
Traditional manual data analysis methods are not sufficient to process the massive and varied
data streams Caltrans must evaluate. GenAI offers the ability to automate and synthesize data
from sensors, cameras, and external sources to drive real-time, evidence-based decisions.
Summary
Funding GenAI in transportation, along with ongoing investment in VRU and TMI initiatives, is
crucial for advancing California's roadway safety and mobility. These efforts aim to support public
interests through proper oversight, accountability, and innovation.
The FY 2026-27 funding request for institutionalizing the Data and AI Office and implementing the
GenAI solutions for VRU and TMI for $16,428,000 is shown below. The requested resources will fund
seven (7) positions and operating costs as the Department builds internal infrastructure and
capabilities to sustain GenAI solutions and processes.

B.  Justification
To align with federal and industry best practices and to uphold responsible, strategic management of
data and AI at Caltrans, funding is critical in building and maintaining a comprehensive framework
for data stewardship, AI governance, and operational effectiveness. It will fund necessary oversight
to support the safe, secure, and successful implementation of high-impact initiatives such as the
Vulnerable Roadway User safety (VRU) and Traffic Mobility Insights (TMI) GenAI projects.

The consulting services are requested to support the institutionalization of the California Governor’s
Executive Orders N-12-23 and N-5-26 on Generative AI, including:

• Organizational Design: Define structure, roles, and assist with onboarding.
• Strategic Planning: Develop a roadmap aligned with Caltrans’ mission and statewide
policies.
• Data Governance: Support the development of policies, standards, and workflows for
data quality, security, and privacy.
• AI Governance & Ethics: Create frameworks for ethical AI use, risk management, and
compliance.
• Technology and Use Case Assessment: Recommend and support the integration of data
management and AI tools.
• Change Management & Training: Support adoption through organizational change,
management, training, and culture-building initiatives.
• Collaboration & Stakeholder Engagement: Facilitate coordination with agencies and
partners, including Caltrans leadership, CalSTA, CDT, GovOps, California Department of
Human Resources, Office of Data and Innovation, DGS, and others to coordinate aligned
AI implementation and verify compliance for tool/system/solution selection, deployment,
security, training, etc.
• Project Management: Oversee the implementation and monitor the progress of AI
initiatives.
Complete Development and Deployment of the Generative AI Solutions for Enhancing Vulnerable
Road User (VRU) Safety and Gaining Traffic Mobility Insights (TMI):
To support the implementation contracts, Caltrans strategically redirected internal resources in
2024-25 to cover the initial contracts with the primary vendor for a six month period ending on
October 10, 2025. Caltrans also redirected funds to CDT for hosting and delivering advanced
GenAI services on a cloud-based platform. The redirected funds covered the ongoing
maintenance and operations costs required to operate these tools throughout 2025-26, before
Caltrans could secure additional external funding through a Budget Change Proposal. This
temporary measure demonstrated Caltrans’ dedication to delivering tangible outcomes and
keeping California at the forefront of GenAI adoption in the public sector.
Caltrans expects to continue to operate and integrate these two GenAI solutions into daily
operations. To maintain ongoing functionality and support for staff, Caltrans is requesting funding
to work on additional use cases and sustain the annual maintenance and operation of both
solutions. This includes professional services from Accenture LLP and Deloitte LLP, who provide
critical technical support and updates, and CDT for cloud infrastructure costs to maintain
Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud (GCP) environments. These platforms are essential for staff to
access and use the solutions as part of their daily workflow, and the exact funding amounts are
elaborated in the VRU & TMI GenAI Solutions Funding table.
Ongoing Maintenance and Operations support for the VRU and TMI GenAI technology solutions,
and their associated complex technical components, will be required from both the Program and
IT teams. This includes support for interactive Geographic Information System (GIS) mapping,
GenAI-driven analytics, data visualizations, and conversational chat modules. A dedicated team
of state staff will be needed to support the development, deployment, and sustained operations
of these innovative, large-scale GenAI solutions, which integrate advanced technologies such as
GenAI models, GIS systems, cloud-based platforms, and sophisticated data integration pipelines.
This funding request is essential to fund the maintenance, operation, and delivery of the
transformative promise of these GenAI solutions. It will unlock the operational potential of the VRU
and TMI modules, moving beyond a limited pilot phase to a comprehensive, data-driven platform
that addresses the evolving needs of California’s transportation system. By investing in the

implementation and sustained operations of these solutions, Caltrans will transform innovative
pilots into powerful drivers of long-term safety, mobility, and climate resilience outcomes-affirming
the Administration’s and CalSTA’s strategic priorities of efficiency, effectiveness, safety,
sustainability, and social equity through bold and innovative investments, and demonstrating a
proactive approach to integrating cutting-edge digital solutions that drive measurable, long-
lasting improvements for all who rely on California’s transportation system.
The GenAI solutions for VRU and TMI have already demonstrated transformative potential during
the Proof-of-Concept (POC) phase and throughout the current execution of the implementation
contracts. Leveraging the advanced capabilities of Azure and GCP, these solutions have shown
that GenAI can convert vast data into actionable insights, enhancing staff’s ability to analyze
complex data through a user-friendly interface with simple natural language prompts. By
integrating historical and real-time data, GenAI quickly identifies high-collision concentration
locations (HCCLs) and generates advanced traffic mobility insights, aiding decision-making for
road safety and traffic congestion management. Its visualization capabilities, such as GIS layers
and interactive maps, enable staff to detect HCCLs, traffic incidents, and bottlenecks while
providing data-driven safety recommendations. After data analysis, GenAI produces insights into
conceptual reports and safety improvement planning. It promotes equity in project delivery by
incorporating demographic and safety data, verifying enhancements prioritize underserved
communities. Moreover, GenAI automates repetitive tasks, allowing staff to focus on higher
priorities like conducting traffic investigations, improving incident detection and response times,
and engaging effectively with communities. By providing sophisticated analytical and decision-
support capabilities, these solutions will empower staff to work smarter, proactively, and more
effectively, supporting their professional development and reinforcing their role as critical
stewards of California’s transportation system.
VRU and TMI’s natural language interfaces democratize access to complex analytics,
empowering planners and engineers to identify bottlenecks, prioritize projects for underserved
communities, and develop data-informed safety and mobility strategies. They also automate
repetitive data tasks, freeing staff capacity to focus on incident investigations, public
engagement, and climate-resilient transportation improvements that support a broad range of
mobility options and an overarching safe system approach. The GenAI solutions to enhance VRU
safety and gain TMI represent a crucial first step in realizing the full potential of GenAI. These early
deployments have demonstrated how GenAI can empower staff with actionable insights and
data-driven decision support.
Executive Order N-12-23 (GenAI EO) guides the state to prepare for the advancements and
potential risks associated with GenAI, and the seven (7) positions being requested are intended to
align Caltrans operational capabilities with this Executive Order, as well as maintain Caltrans
compliance with new and evolving State laws, regulations, and policy, including:
• Senate Bill (SB) 896, the GenAI Accountability Act, aims to regulate and mitigate risks
associated with GenAI technologies in California by requiring transparency, risk
assessments, and clear communication when Artificial Intelligence (AI) is used in state
government services.
• Assembly Bill (AB) 2013, Generative artificial intelligence: training data transparency,
mandates the public disclosure of the data used to train GenAI models, including
summaries of the data sets and their sources.
The requested positions include one position as Senior Transportation Electrical Engineer in the
Division of Traffic Operations to manage day-to-day work associated with delivering all the use
cases of these projects with the statewide team, developing the solutions with vendors to verify
that the solution meets the project objectives, and working with Caltrans IT and CDT to resolve
any development issues. In addition, six of the requested positions – four IT Specialist IIs and two IT

Specialist IIIs – will be housed in Caltrans IT. These positions include the functions of DevOps Cloud
Engineer, Data Pipeline Architect, Full Stack Developer, Cloud Architect, Project Manager, and
Business Analyst. The technical roles will support cloud environments, data management, and
security requirements, and will be involved in GenAI cloud and data infrastructure as well as
preparing for future use cases. The Project Manager and Business Analyst will focus on monitoring
that CDT’s Project Delivery Lifecycle (PDL) requirements are fully met for the VRU and TMI projects,
which did not previously undergo the Project Approval Lifecycle (PAL) process. In addition to
coordinating the retrospective documentation effort, these roles will also establish repeatable
practices and provide sustained capacity to meet future PDL requirements for anticipated
additional AI use cases. Together, these resources will provide the necessary technical and
project governance capacity to meet statewide PDL requirements while sustaining existing GenAI
use cases. IT will also be securing professional services for setting up cloud connectivity
infrastructure.
The current internal resource redirection was a strategic measure that showcased Caltrans’
commitment to advancing the Administration’s and CalSTA’s priorities of efficiency, engagement,
and effectiveness. While this temporary funding shift demonstrated the Department’s dedication
to innovation, it postponed other important safety, research, and operational initiatives and
cannot be repeated without long-term consequences. This initial investment was not intended to
be a permanent funding solution—it was a catalyst to unlock the transformative potential of VRU
and TMI and demonstrate their value in real-world applications.
To fully realize this potential, new funding is essential to close the structural funding shortfall and
deploy the modules for VRU and TMI. With the deployment, these solutions will become fully
integrated, scalable components of daily workflows, delivering robust, real-time safety and
mobility insights that support staff and address California’s evolving transportation challenges.
Investing in these GenAI solutions' development, ongoing maintenance, and operations will
enable Caltrans to meet its safety and mobility goals and set a national standard for how data-
driven, collaborative innovation can reshape public transportation management.
C. Departmentwide and Statewide Considerations
Caltrans has over 22,000 employees statewide and numerous service programs, including
engineering, project management, environmental planning, public outreach, maintenance, and
operations of the State’s highway system. Caltrans operates 12 districts that interact with all 58
counties and local transportation planning agencies. California’s transportation system includes
over 52,000 lane miles of state highways and 162,000 lane miles of local roads, supporting the
state’s economy and communities.
The development, streamlining, and seamless integration of these two efforts directly advance
the Administration’s and CalSTA’s strategic goals while aligning with the Caltrans 2024–2028
Strategic Plan’s pillars of safety, climate action, stewardship, collaboration, and operational
excellence. This initiative is central to Caltrans’ mission to lead a safety culture change, maximize
the use of advanced safety technologies, and advance equitable, multimodal transportation
solutions that foster healthy and livable communities—objectives that are further reinforced by
the CalSTA 2024–2026 Strategic Plan, the Climate Action Plan for Transportation Infrastructure
(CAPTI), and the California Transportation Plan (CTP) 2050, which together call for climate-smart,
equity-focused investments and innovative, data-driven solutions for a sustainable, resilient, and
inclusive transportation future:
• Leading Safety Culture Change: By equipping staff with advanced data tools that identify
HCCLs and vulnerable road user risks, this proposal reinforces Caltrans’ commitment to

fostering a proactive, safety-first culture responsive to emerging trends and local needs.
This aligns with the Strategic Plan’s goal to “improve safety for all users,” working toward
zero fatalities and serious injuries.”
• Maximizing the use of Advanced Safety Technologies: The deployment and the usage of
the VRU and TMI solutions leverage GenAI’s predictive analytics, proactive strategies, and
real-time operational data to make smarter, faster, more efficient, and effective decisions.
This supports the Caltrans Strategic Plan’s focus on enhancing operational excellence and
leveraging emerging technologies, the CAPTI’s call to integrate climate-smart, equity-
focused strategies, and the Strategic Plan’s pillar of Employee Excellence, which
underscores the importance of providing staff with cutting-edge tools and resources to
thrive.
• Advancing Equitable and Reliable Multimodal Transportation Solutions: These GenAI
solutions integrate climate, equity, and resilience considerations directly into decision-
making, verifying that transportation investments and interventions address the needs of
historically underserved communities while enhancing system-wide performance. This
aligns with the CalSTA Strategic Plan’s pillars of “equity and climate stewardship,” the
Caltrans Strategic Plan’s pillars of Equity and Climate Action, the CAPTI, and the Active
Transportation Program (ATP), which promotes safe and reliable mobility options for all
users, including non-motorized travelers.
• Promoting Prosperity and Employee Excellence: Beyond operational impacts, this proposal
also supports the Strategic Plan’s pillars of Prosperity and Employee Excellence by
empowering staff with new skills, fostering a culture of innovation, and creating a
foundation for future workforce growth. By making data-driven, equity-centered decisions,
Caltrans can deliver more inclusive, community-focused improvements that boost local
economies and improve transportation access for all Californians.
Additionally, this proposal supports the CTP 2050, which envisions a sustainable, resilient, and
equitable transportation future for all Californians. By leveraging GenAI to reduce congestion,
improve safety, proactively integrate climate considerations, and build the capacity for
additional GenAI solutions, this initiative fully aligns with the CTP 2050’s focus on “data-driven,
equitable investments that achieve zero traffic fatalities and reduced greenhouse gas emissions.”
Operationally, the requested funding will:
• Provide staff with secure, real-time access to robust data streams for more informed and
data-driven decision-making that directly addresses critical safety and mobility challenges.
• Develop a roadmap that defines how GenAI can be implemented statewide and across
the business in alignment with strategic goals.
• Automating data processing will reduce manual work, freeing up staff capacity to focus
on proactive safety interventions, meaningful community engagement, and the
development of climate-resilient, long-term transportation plans.
• Promote a culture of innovation and implement GenAI responsibly, consistent with state
policies on transparency, accountability, and ethical AI governance.
• Identify and support staff training and skill development needs, fostering professional
growth and enhancing workforce capabilities in GenAI and data-driven decision-making.
• Improve visual communication and public engagement through intuitive dashboards and
visualizations, fostering more inclusive and informed discussions with communities across
California.
Beyond Caltrans, GenAI can generate broad statewide benefits. These solutions will strengthen
California’s ability to meet climate goals by reducing greenhouse gas emissions from congestion
and vehicle idling, enhancing operational resilience, and aligning transportation investments with

community needs and public mandate. They will also support key statewide initiatives, including
the Zero Traffic Fatalities Task Force and the Safe System Approach.
D. Outcomes and Accountability
Complete Development and Deployment of the Generative AI Solutions for Enhancing
Vulnerable Road User (VRU) Safety and Gaining Traffic Mobility Insights (TMI):
The Maintenance and Operations of the four modules for VRU and TMI outlined in the executed
contracts will deliver clear, measurable outcomes aligned with Caltrans’ “A thriving and
connected California” vision and “Improving lives and communities through transportation”
mission, as well as the department’s safety, equity, and climate stewardship goals.
Specifically, this proposal will:
• Improve analysis of data and development of safety and mobility solutions: The VRU
modules will enable staff to analyze high-collision concentration locations (HCCLs) annually
per district, prioritizing systemic safety enhancements using data such as lane
configurations, equity overlays, lighting, and weather conditions. The TMI modules will
analyze recurring bottlenecks and traffic disruptions across all 12 Caltrans districts, informing
data-driven operational strategies to reduce delays and congestion, and supporting
statewide performance goals.
• Foster innovation and intelligent risk-taking: The GenAI solutions incorporate interactive
natural language chat modules and advanced analytics capabilities, empowering staff to
iteratively refine insights and explore innovative safety and mobility strategies in a data-
supported environment. Staff will receive targeted training on new data modules and
participate in 2–3 scenario-based GenAI pilots, encouraging a culture of innovation while
effectively managing risk.
• Enhance transportation network safety: Real-time, GIS-based visualization dashboards will
map targeted safety interventions, including proven countermeasures and adaptive
congestion relief strategies. For example, the VRU’s visualization module will include
clickable prototype development for mapping pedestrian and cyclist safety improvements
at over 100 targeted locations statewide, allowing for precise, actionable data to guide
decision-making.
• Reduce delays and improve response: TMI’s incident detection and disruption forecasting
modules will enable rerouting and bottleneck detection in all 12 Caltrans districts. These
capabilities are expected to reduce response times by 10–15% compared to manual
processes, aligning with Caltrans’ goal of improving operational readiness and minimizing
delays during incidents.
• Optimize equitable infrastructure investments: both contracts prioritize underserved
communities through explicit scoring frameworks and equity overlays, confirming that
identified safety improvement locations directly address their needs. This approach
reinforces Caltrans’ commitment to equitable transportation investments and safety
interventions that close historic gaps.
• Augment workforce and improve staff skill set: These solutions provide intuitive, map-based
dashboards and automated analytical outputs, reducing manual data-processing tasks.
This will free staff to focus on proactive safety planning, community engagement, and
innovative operational strategies. Workforce training will pledge that at least 100 planners
and engineers across all 12 districts are upskilled using the GenAI solutions for safety and
mobility planning.

E. Implementation Plan
Caltrans has developed an implementation plan to facilitate a coordinated transition from current
policies, strategies, and operations to a revised framework for managing data, analytics, and
artificial intelligence. The planned activities from July 2026 – July 2027 is the full integration of data
and AI functions, alignment with Caltrans’ long-term digital strategy, and post-implementation
evaluation.
