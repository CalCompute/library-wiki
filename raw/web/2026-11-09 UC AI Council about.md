# Artificial Intelligence at UC

Artificial Intelligence is not just a technological advancement – it promises to be a transformative force that will shape the future of our world. At the University of California, we are at the forefront of this revolutionary technology, respecting both its potential and challenges.

### Here, you will find resources, insights, and community engagement opportunities centered around the ethical and impactful use of AI at the UC.

* **AI Communities:** Learn about the various AI communities within the UC system, including the UC AI Council. Discover how these groups are driving AI initiatives and contributing to the development of responsible AI practices.
* **Governance and Transparency:** Access crucial information on AI governance, including links to relevant laws and policies. Review the UC AI Transparency Report to see how AI is currently being used across the system, ensuring accountability and ethical standards.
* **Tools and Resources:** Find valuable tools and resources to support your AI endeavors. This includes an introductory AI 101 training, a glossary of common AI terms, and a model risk assessment framework. These resources are designed to equip you with the knowledge and tools necessary for responsible AI implementation.
* **News and Events:** Stay informed about the latest AI-related news and events within the UC system. Learn about upcoming conferences, workshops, and other opportunities to engage with AI experts and practitioners.

Together, we will illuminate AI’s frontiers to enhance learning, empower innovation, and ensure a brighter future for all through the responsible use of AI. Fiat Lux.

#### Join us as we explore the frontiers of AI at the UC

From incorporation of AI into our pedagogy, to the use of AI to optimize operational efficiencies and our cutting-edge research – the UC leads while embracing and realizing the [UC Responsible AI Principles](https://www.ucop.edu/ethics-compliance-audit-services/compliance/presidential-working-group-on-artificial-intelligence.html) to guide our use of this powerful tool. UC was the first university system to publish a set of guiding principles for the use of AI within its system, and we continue to lead by example by tackling the hard issues in using AI responsibly.
