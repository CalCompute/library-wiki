# Work with us

ODI’s services help state departments solve business problems and spend more time serving Californians.

![](/img/wordpress/2026/05/workwithus-presentation-chart.png)

## Analytics Accelerator

The Analytics Accelerator helps teams:

* Save analyst time by streamlining mandatory reporting via adoption of PowerBI
* Optimize that liberated analyst time via training in modern analytics approaches

You’ll learn automation and data modeling concepts, data visualization techniques, and user centered data practices.

[Learn more about the Analytics Accelerator](/work-with-us/analytics-accelerator/)

---

![](/img/wordpress/2026/05/workwithus-notepad.png)

## Data Advisory Service

You’ll talk with a data expert about how to define, scope, and build a data solution to meet your need. You’ll get advice, not a deliverable, to help you solve complex problems about data engineering and data science.

[Learn more about the Data Advisory Service](/work-with-us/data-advisory-service/)

---

![](/img/wordpress/2026/05/workwithus-flowchart.png)

## Data Science Accelerator

The Data Science Accelerator helps teams harness the power of advanced analytics and statistics to improve their services. Teams work with ODI to refine a problem, identify the right statistical method to address it, and implement a service change to improve based on what we learn.

[Learn more about the Data Science Accelerator](/work-with-us/data-science-accelerator/)

---

![](/img/wordpress/2026/10/work-with-us-flask.png)

## Innovation Lab

No matter where you are in the product lifecycle, the Lab can help your team de-risk decision making. Our time-boxed, structured engagement helps you make data-informed decisions with confidence.

[Learn more about the Innovation Lab](/work-with-us/innovation-lab/)

---

![](/img/wordpress/2026/05/workwithus-data-stack.png)

## Modern Data Stack Accelerator

The Modern Data Stack Accelerator (MDSA) will help teams rapidly learn and adopt modern cloud-based data tools to solve problems like combining data across systems or program areas. Departments often rely on costly and time consuming RFPs or contractors to do this work. This experimental service will test if we can meet this need in house in a way that is faster and cheaper than traditional methods.

[Learn more about the Modern Data Stack Accelerator](/work-with-us/modern-data-stack-accelerator/)

---

![](/img/wordpress/2026/05/workwithus-certificate.png)

## Training

We offer self-paced and real-time training that help you improve your innovation skills. Our courses cover:

* Human-centered design
* Plain language
* Product approach
* Working with data

Most are free and have individual and group options.

[Learn more about our training](/work-with-us/training/)
