better serve clients who store sensitive data that is subject to the highest data privacy
standards imposed by federal regulations and national research organizations.

(3) up to $2.0 million each fiscal year for OTCs that provide business consultation with
matching local dollars, with preference given to industries with in-demand jobs or
regionally emerging fields.
