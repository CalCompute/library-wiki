Office of Data and Innovation

We’re improving services for all Californians

The Office of Data and Innovation (ODI) helps departments meet Californians where they are. We use data, human-centered design and technology to create services that are easier for everyone to use.

Shaping California, one conversation at a time

ODI is proud to introduce Engaged California, an innovative new program designed to foster meaningful dialogue and strengthen trust between Californians and their government. The first use case of the program is focused on survivors of the LA firestorm and those who have been impacted by the recovery.

The Governor’s Innovation Fellows are state employees making our government better. ODI gives them training and help to improve services for Californians.
