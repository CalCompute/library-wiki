A. Problem Statement
The Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) requests two positions and $717,000 General Fund in
FY 2026-27 and $463,000 in FY 2027-28 to create the California Artificial Intelligence Cybersecurity
Collaboration Playbook (the playbook) pursuant to Government Code Section 8586.5.
The California Cybersecurity Integration Center (Cal-CSIC) was created in 2015 and codified in
2018 by Gov. Code §8586.5, within Cal OES. It is a joint effort between four California organizations
who provide the personnel that make up the Cal-CSIC – Cal OES, California Department of
Technology, California Military Department and California Highway Patrol. The Cal-CSIC’s mission
is to reduce the likelihood and severity of cyber incidents that could damage California’s
economy, its critical infrastructure, or public and private sector computer networks. As the central
organizing hub of California’s cybersecurity activities, the Cal-CSIC coordinates information
sharing with local, state and federal agencies, tribal governments, utilities and other service
providers, as well as with academic institutions, and non-governmental organizations.
Government Code Section 8586.5(g)(1) requires Cal OES, namely Cal-CSIC, to develop prior to
January 1, 2027, in consultation with the Office of Information Security and the Government
Operations Agency, a California Artificial Intelligence (AI) Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook
to facilitate information sharing across cyber and AI communities, strengthening cyber defenses
against emerging threats. To create the playbook, Cal OES must review federal requirements,
standards, industry best practices, and use those resources to inform the development of the
playbook. The playbook must include mandatory mechanisms for information sharing of potential
threats and vulnerability known to state contractors and vendors providing AI services regarding
those contracted or purchased services, to a state entity identified in the playbook.
B. Justification
Currently, Cal CSIC coordinates cybersecurity throughout the state to reduce the likelihood and
severity of cyber incidents that could damage California’s economy, critical infrastructure, or
private sector computer networks. Assembly Bill (AB) 979 will expand the responsibilities to include
the identification, assessment and the mitigation of AI related threats.
At its current funding level, the Cal-CSIC does not have the resources to support the creation of
the playbook. Moreover, AI is a complex, ever-evolving topic. As a result, the staff will require AI
training to ensure they are equipped to create the playbook. This will also allow staff to consider
how the federal government is implementing requirements, standards, and best practices within
the Joint Cyber Defense Collaborative AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook.
Cal OES is requesting one (1.0) Associate Government Program Analyst to:
• Assist in the creation of the California AI Cybersecurity Collaboration Playbook.
• Maintain information on best practices and ensure best practices are incorporated into the
playbook.
• Track and provide updates on the development of the California AI Cybersecurity
Collaboration Playbook.
• Conduct outreach and coordination with entities involved in the development of the
playbook.
• Review federal requirements, standards and industry best practices, and use those
resources in the development of the playbook; and
• Collaborate with others to develop and implement the mandatory information sharing
mechanisms of potential threats and vulnerabilities.
Cal OES is requesting one (1.0) Information Technology Specialist II to:
• Serve as the expert in AI, ensuring the playbook created meets industry standards in the
sharing of threat reporting and AI collaboration.

• Evaluate current best practices and create new best practices for the state of California.
• Serve as project manager for the creation of the playbook, ensuring a robust plan is
developed that ensures cross-functional collaboration with network engineers and
developers to drive IT and business strategies in relation to the playbook.
• Conduct outreach and coordination with entities involved in the development of the
playbook, taking information from these sessions and transforming it into a unique and
forward leaning process that accounts for complex data systems architecture.
• Collaborate with internal and external partners to develop and implement the mandatory
information sharing mechanisms of potential threats and vulnerabilities, working with system
engineers as needed to implement tools to enhance the information sharing process.
C. Departmentwide and Statewide Considerations
Cal OES' mission is to protect lives and property, build capabilities, and support communities for a
resilient California. This proposal will specifically work toward the goals within the Cal OES Strategic
Plan:
Goal 1: Anticipate and enhance prevention and detection capabilities to protect our state
from all hazards and threats.
Goal 2: Strengthen California's ability to plan, prepare for, and provide resources to mitigate
the impacts of disasters, emergencies, crimes, and terrorist events.
Goal 3: Effectively respond to and recover from both human-caused and natural disasters.
Additionally, this proposal aligns with four of the ten goals set out in the 2021 – 2024 California
State Security Strategy (the 2025 California Homeland Security Strategy is pending approval):
Goal 1: Enhance Information Collection, Analysis, and Sharing, in Support of Public Safety
Operations across California.
Goal 2: Protect Critical Infrastructure and Key Resources from All Threats and Hazards.
Goal 3: Strengthen Security and Preparedness across Cyberspace.
Goal 4: Strengthen Communications Capabilities through Planning, Governance, Technology,
and Equipment.
D. Outcomes and Accountability
Cal-CSIC will comply with the statutory requirements and the successful completion of the
playbook by January 1, 2027.
E. Implementation Plan
Implementation will begin July 1, 2026. Cal OES will conduct advance recruitment to make
sure the positions are filled timely.
