# FedRAMP Marketplace

> **FedRAMP Terminology Update:** Effective immediately, "FedRAMP Authorization" is now called "FedRAMP Certification," and Impact Levels have been replaced with Classes A-D for package specifications. For complete details on these changes, visit [Initial Outcome from RFC-0020 FedRAMP Authorization Designations](/notices/0004/). **FedRAMP will provide previous Impact Levels in parentheses after classes until December 31, 2026 to help stakeholders transition to the new terminology. Beginning in January 2027, "Low", "Moderate", and "High" Impact Levels will be removed and full transition to the class structure will take effect.**
