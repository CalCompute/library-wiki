# Governor’s Innovation Fellows Program

![State employees talking through problems at tables](/img/wordpress/2025/09/fellows-cover-image-1024x538.webp)

The Governor’s Innovation Fellows are state employees solving real public sector challenges. The program brings them together to improve services for Californians.

The first and second cohorts include 21 employees. For 6 months, the Fellows will work on priority projects. They will make government more efficient, effective, and responsive to the public.

## What the Fellows do

* Spend 80% of their time on projects that improve efficiency, service delivery, and customer experience
* Get training and hands-on help from the Office of Data and Innovation, CalAcademy, and partners in the private sector and academia
* Work with executive sponsors and subject matter experts get measurable results through:
  + Applying product approaches
  + Using data
  + Conducting customer research

## Why it matters

Governor Newsom launched this program as part of a broader executive order. Its goal is to make state government efficient, engaged, and effective. We chose the Fellows for their initiative, creativity, and problem-solving skills.

The Innovation Fellows Program isn’t like a traditional leadership program. We designed it to be a delivery engine. It develops leaders while creating real improvements that help Californians.

## Looking ahead

The program will grow over time. New Fellows will join every 4 months. They will overlap with the earlier cohort so they can share knowledge and work together.

## Meet the Fellows

### Cohort 3

The [third cohort](https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/cohort-3/) started in June 2026. The 15 Fellows come from 7 agencies and 11 departments.

### Cohort 2

The [second cohort of Fellows](https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/cohort-2/) came from 11 agencies and 13 departments. They served from January 2026 to July 2026.

Read about some of their work:

* [AskCA case study](https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/cohort-2/helping-parents-understand-family-leave/)
* [Engaged California case study](https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/cohort-2/turning-engaged-california-ideas-into-future-state-projects/)

### Cohort 1

The [21 inaugural Fellows](https://innovation.ca.gov/our-work/governors-innovation-fellows-program/cohort-1) served from August 2025 to February 2026. They came from 9 agencies and 15 departments.
