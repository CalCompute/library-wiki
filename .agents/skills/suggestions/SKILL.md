# Suggestions

The rules in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md) apply to every step.

1. A visitor writes to team@californiacompute.org with the subject "Suggested reading". The site's **Suggest reading** button fills in that email.
2. A person adds a row to `wiki/analyses/Suggestions.md`: date received, the source or question, status `received`. Never the sender's name or address.
3. A consortium member reads the suggestion and decides. The person sets the status to `approved` or `declined`, with a short reason.
4. Only an approved source goes to Ingest. Only an approved question goes to Query.
